Market Outlook
9
AI technology is evolving rapidly beyond training to inference & agentic AI stage
As the volume of data generated by AI agents increases, demand is rising for a 
broad range of memory products to process and store such data efficiently
With increasing total volume of memory required across overall systems, the 
demand base is broadening for both DRAM and NAND
Memory
-efficiency technology is expected to improve the economics of AI services, 
expand the overall market, and create a virtuous cycle that further drives memory 
demand
Some signs of softening demand, including adjustments in 
set shipments and product portfolio changes, have 
emerged due to cost pressures from higher memory prices
However, robust server memory demand continues to 
offset this weakness and is driving overall market growth
As memory becomes increasingly critical in AI computing, demand for high-performance memory is surging 
while supply remains constrained, expect favorable pricing environment to continue for the time being

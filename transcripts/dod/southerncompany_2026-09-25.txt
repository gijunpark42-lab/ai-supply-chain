[DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY]
Georgia Power Co., Atlanta, Georgia, has been awarded a maximum $21,206,744 firm-fixed-price task order (SP0604-26-F-8001) issued against a ten-year contract (47PA04-25-D-0026) for energy conservation and demand side management services at Warner Robins Air Logistics Complex, Robins Air Force Base, Georgia. This was a sole-source acquisition using justification 10 U.S. Code 3204 (a)(1), as stated in Federal Acquisition Regulation 6.103-1. This is a twelve-year contract with no option periods. The performance completion date is Sept. 25, 2038. Using military service is Air Force. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 through 2038 defense working capital funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Energy, Fort Belvoir, Virginia.

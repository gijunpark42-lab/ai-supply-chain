[NAVY]
L3Harris Interstate Electronics Corp., Yorba Linda, California, is being awarded a $46,850,128 cost-plus-incentive-fee and cost-plus-fixed-fee contract (N0003027C2001) for services and support for flight test instrumentation systems for the Trident II Strategic Weapon System. This contract has optional line items which could increase the amount to $207,054,517, if exercised. Work will be performed in Yorba Linda, California (55%); Cape Canaveral, Florida (30%); Washington, D.C. (7%); Groton, Connecticut (3%); Bremerton, Washington (2%); Kings Bay, Georgia (2%); and Barrow-in-Furness, United Kingdom (1%). Work is expected to be completed on 30 September 2031. No funding will be obligated on this contract at time of award, and no funds will expire at the end of the current fiscal year, as it is being awarded subject to the availability of funds in fiscal 2027. This contract was awarded as a sole source acquisition pursuant to 10 U.S. Code 3204 (a)(1) and was previously synopsized on the System for Award Management (SAM.gov) online portal. Strategic Systems Programs, Washington, D.C., is the contracting activity.

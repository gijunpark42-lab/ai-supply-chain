[NAVY]
Northrop Grumman Corp., Charlottesville, Virginia, was awarded a $29,219,125 cost-plus-incentive-fee, cost-plus-fixed-fee, firm-fixed-price, and cost only contract for low-rate initial production units and maintenance assistance modules, engineering support, integration, and test of the AN/WSN-12 Inertial Navigation System and other direct costs, travel and material. This contract includes options which, if exercised, would bring the cumulative value of this contract to $103,629,832. This contract combines purchases for the Navy (27%); and the governments of Canada (42%), and Australia (31%), under the Foreign Military Sales (FMS) program. Work will be performed in Charlottesville, Virginia (90%); and Baltimore, Maryland (10%), and is expected to be completed by May 2030. If all options are exercised, work will continue through July 2032. FMS (Canada) funds in the amount of $12,271,012 (42%); FMS (Australia) funds in the amount of $9,203,260 (31%); fiscal 2026 other procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $4,677,100 (16%); and fiscal 2024 shipbuilding and conversion (Navy) funds in the amount of $3,067,753 (11%), were obligated at the time of award and will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. This contract was not competitively procured in accordance with 10 U.S. Code 3204(a)(1) (only one responsible source and no other supplies or services will satisfy agency requirements). Naval Sea Systems Command, Washington, D.C., is the contracting activity (N00024-26-C-5207). (Awarded Sept. 30, 2026).

[AIR FORCE]
Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Meadows, Illinois, has been awarded a $10,407,606 modification (P00189) to a previously awarded contract (FA8615-17-C-6047) for F-16 Integrated Viper Electronic Warfare Suite-Electronic Warfare radio frequency compatibility, diminishing manufacturing sources and material shortages effort. The modification brings the total cumulative face value of the contract from $1,971,177,431 to $1,981,585,037. Work will be performed at Rolling Meadows, Illinois, and is expected to be completed by March 31, 2029. Fiscal 2025 procurement funds in the amount of $10,407,606 are being obligated at the time of award. The Air Force Life Cycle Management Center, Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, Ohio, is the contracting activity.

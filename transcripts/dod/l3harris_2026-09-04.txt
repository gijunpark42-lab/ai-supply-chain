[NAVY]
L3Harris Technologies, Northampton, Massachusetts, is awarded a $139,012,362 cost-plus-fixed-fee, cost-plus-incentive-fee, and firm-fixed-price modification to previously awarded contract (N00024-20-C-6249) for production of new construction and in-service submarine low-profile photonics masts, spares and engineering support. Work will be performed in Northampton, Massachusetts, and is expected to be completed by September 2030. Fiscal 2024 shipbuilding and conversion (Navy) funds in the amount of $42,247,676 (30%); fiscal 2025 other procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $19,360,698 (14%); fiscal 2025 national sea-based deterrent funds in the amount of $18,876,901 (14%); fiscal 2025 shipbuilding and conversion (Navy) funds in the amount of $20,038,547 (14%); fiscal 2026 other procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $27,864,934 (20%); and fiscal 2026 national sea-based deterrent funds in the amount of $10,623,606 (8%), will be obligated at the time of award and will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. The Naval Sea Systems Command, Washington, D.C., is the contracting activity.

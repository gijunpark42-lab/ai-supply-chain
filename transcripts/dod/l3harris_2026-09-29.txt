L3 Technologies Inc., L-3 Telemetry & Radio Frequency Products, San Diego, California, is awarded a $10,754,610 modification (P00035) to a previously awarded firm-fixed-price contract (N0001921C0064). This modification exercises an option to procure an additional 176 Modernized Radios, including 38 for the Navy and 138 for Foreign Military Sales (FMS) customers. Work will be performed in San Diego, California, and is expected to be completed in November 2028. Fiscal 2025 weapons procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $2,322,018; and FMS funds in the amount of $8,432,593, will be obligated at the time of award. This modification was not competitively procured. Naval Air Systems Command, Patuxent River, Maryland, is the contracting activity.

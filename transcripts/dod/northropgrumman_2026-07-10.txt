Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Mission Systems Sector, Linthicum Heights, Maryland, is being awarded a $39,400,521 cost-plus-fixed-fee modification to a previously awarded contract (N00024-22-C-5520) to exercise an option for design agent engineering support of Surface Electronic Warfare Improvement Program Block Three systems. Work will be performed in Baltimore, Maryland, and is expected to be complete by August 2027. Fiscal 2026 shipbuilding and construction (Navy) funds in the amount of $6,000,000 will be obligated at time of award and will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. Naval Sea Systems Command, Washington, D.C., is the contracting activity.

[NAVY]
Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Aerospace Systems. Melbourne, Florida, is awarded a not-to-exceed $1,196,000,000 cost, no fee undefinitized contract for the production, delivery, and associated support for three E-2D Advanced Hawkeye Block II aircraft for the Navy. Work will be performed in Melbourne, Florida (29.88%); St. Augustine, Florida (17.68%); Liverpool, New York (13.85%); Indianapolis, Indiana (4.95%); Largo, Florida (3.66%); San Leandro, California (2.52%); Woodland Hills, California (2.42%); Aire-sur-l’Adour, France (2.09%); Rolling Meadows, Illinois (1.72%); Wimborne Minster, Dorset, United Kingdom (0.01%); and various locations within the continental U.S. (21.22%), and is expected to be completed in December 2031. Fiscal 2026 aircraft procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $583,394,000 will be obligated at the time of award. This contract was not competed in accordance with 10 U.S. Code 2304(c)(1). Naval Air Systems Command, Patuxent River, Maryland, is the contracting activity (N0001926C1017).

[ARMY]
Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., McLean, Virginia, was awarded a $27,888,512 modification (PZ0004) to contract W31P4Q-26-C-0011 to definitize an undefinitized contract action for engineering support, integrated product support, field service representative support, and training and training material support software products. One bid was solicited with one received. Work will be performed in McLean, Virginia, with an estimated completion date of Jan. 21, 2029. Fiscal 2026 aircraft procurement, Army funds in the amount of $9,317,073 were obligated at the time of the award. Army Contracting Command, Redstone Arsenal, Alabama, is the contracting activity.

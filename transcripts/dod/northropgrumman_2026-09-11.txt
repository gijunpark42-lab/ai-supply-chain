[ARMY]
Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Radford, Virginia, was awarded a $19,999,798 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract to procure non-standard ammunition. Two bids were solicited with two received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Oct. 1, 2027. Army Contracting Command, Rock Island Arsenal, Illinois, is the contracting activity (W519TC-26-F-A562).

[DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY]
Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, has been awarded a maximum $18,730,567 modification (P00024) to a delivery order (SPRTA1-16-F-0353) issued against a base contract (SPE4AX-16-D-9479) for the manufacture and production of B-2 hot trailing edges. This is a firm-fixed-price, indefinite-quantity contract. Location of performance is Utah, with a July 10, 2029, delivery order completion date. Using military service is Air Force. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 defense working capital funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Weapons Support, Tinker Air Force Base, Oklahoma.

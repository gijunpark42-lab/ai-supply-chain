[NAVY]
Honeywell Aerospace Technologies, Minneapolis, Minnesota, is being awarded $53,866,460 for a firm-fixed-price contract for the procurement of up to 1125 components over five years representing three different parts used in ring laser gyros that are components of and support the WSN-7 inertial navigation system used on a variety of Navy submarines and surface ships. This is a five-year base contract with no option periods. All work will be performed in Minneapolis, Minnesota. No funds are being obligated at time of award. Subsequent delivery orders under the resultant five-year contract will be issued by September 2031 and funded with appropriate fiscal year working capital funds (Navy) and those funds will not expire. One source was solicited for this sole-source requirement pursuant to the authority set forth in 10 U.S. Code 3204 (a)(1) and one offer was received. Naval Supply Systems Command Weapon Systems Support, Mechanicsburg, Pennsylvania, is the contracting activity (N00104-26-D-AZ01).

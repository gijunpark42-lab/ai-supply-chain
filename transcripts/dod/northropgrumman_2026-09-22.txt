[AIR FORCE]
Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Warner Robins, Georgia has been awarded a $11,492,322 cost plus fixed fee requirements contract modification (P00015) to a previously award contract (FA8524-20-D-0012) for engineering services to address Diminishing Manufacturing Sources for three components of the Receiver-Transmitter Processor applicable to the APN-241 Program. Work will be performed at Warner Robins, Georgia, and is expected to be completed by Sept. 25, 2027. No funds are being obligated at the time of the award. The Air Force Sustainment Center, Robins Air Force Base, Georgia, is the contracting activity. (Awarded Sept. 21, 2026)

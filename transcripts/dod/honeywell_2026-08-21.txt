[DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY]
Honeywell International Inc., Clearwater, Florida, has been awarded a maximum $9,123,852 firm-fixed-price contract for inertial navigation units. This was a sole-source acquisition using justification 10 U.S. Code 3204(a)(1), as stated in Federal Acquisition Regulation 6.302-1. This is a one-year five-month contract that includes an option to procure additional units. The performance completion date is Jan. 18, 2028. Using military service is Army. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 Army working capital funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Weapons Support, Warren, Michigan (SPRDL1-26-C-0118).

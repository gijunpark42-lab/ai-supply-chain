[NAVY]
Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Sunnyvale, California, was awarded $17,809,389 for a firm-fixed-price stand-alone contract for the manufacture of 157 ceramic ball rotating assemblies in support of Navy Virginia-class submarines. The contract does not include an option period. All work will be performed in Sunnyvale, California, and work is expected to be completed by August 2028. Fiscal 2026 working capital (Navy) funds in the contract's full amount of $17,809,389 will be obligated at time of award and those funds will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. One source was solicited for this sole-source requirement pursuant to the authority set forth in 10 U.S. Code 3204 (a)(1) and one offer was received. NAVSUP Weapon Systems Support, Mechanicsburg, Pennsylvania, is the contracting activity (N00104-26-C-YA55). (Awarded Sept. 23, 2026).

[DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY]
Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, has been awarded an estimated $14,179,813 firm-fixed-price contract for the manufacture and production of B-2 alarm controls. This was a sole-source acquisition using justification 10 U.S. Code 3204 (a)(1), as stated in Federal Acquisition Regulation 6.103-1. This is a three-year contract with no option periods. The performance completion date is Dec. 31, 2029. Using military service is Air Force. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 defense working capital funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Weapons Support, Tinker Air Force Base, Oklahoma (SPRTA1-26-C-0170).

[AIR FORCE]
Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Roy, Utah, has been awarded a $111,366,000 firm fixed price and cost- plus-fixed-fee modification (P00067) to previously awarded (FA8219-20-C-0006) for Ground Based Strategic Deterrent Engineering and Manufacturing Development and Early Production and Deployment. The modification brings the total cumulative face value of the contract to $13,473,182,364 from 13,361,816,364. Work will be performed in Warren, New Jersey and Kernersville, North Carolina, and is expected to be complete by Dec. 31, 2033. Fiscal 2025 Missile Procurement funds in the amount of $111,366,000 are being obligated at the time of award. The Air Force Nuclear Weapons Center, Hill Air Force Base, Utah is the contracting activity. (Awarded Sept. 24, 2026).

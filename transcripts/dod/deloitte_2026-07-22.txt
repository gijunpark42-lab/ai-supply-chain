[AIR FORCE]
Deloitte Consulting LLP, Arlington, Virginia, has been awarded a $20,527,492 firm-fixed-price modification (P00025) to a previously awarded contract (FA7014-24-F-0461) for technical support services. The modification brings the total cumulative face value of the contract to $56,372,569 from $35,845,078. Work will be performed in Washington, D.C., and is expected to be completed Sept. 29, 2029. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance funds in the amount of $7,079,222 are being obligated at the time of award. The Air Force District of Washington Contracting Directorate, Joint Base Andrews, Maryland, is the contracting activity.

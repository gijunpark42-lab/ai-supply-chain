[AIR FORCE]
Honeywell Aerospace, Clearwater, Florida, has been awarded a $25,608,698 cost-plus-fixed-fee contract for the compact strategic gyroscope-maturation project. This contract provides for radiation-hardened compact strategic gyroscopes. Work will be performed in Clearwater, Florida, and is expected to be completed by July 1, 2029. This contract was a competitive acquisition, and two offers were received. Fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation funds in the amount of $500,000 are being obligated at time of award. The Air Force Research Laboratory, Kirtland Air Force Base, New Mexico, is the contracting activity (FA9453-26-C-X026).

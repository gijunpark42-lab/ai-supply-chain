[ARMY]
Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Minneapolis, Minnesota, was awarded an $862,848,000 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract to manufacture, inspect, test, package and deliver M1156 precision guidance kits and variants. Bids were solicited via the internet with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Sept. 3, 2031. Army Contracting Command, Newark, New Jersey, is the contracting activity (W15QKN-26-D-A072).

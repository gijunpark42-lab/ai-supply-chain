[DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY]
The Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Melbourne, Florida, has been awarded a maximum $10,762,018 firm-fixed-price delivery order (SPRPA1-26-F-D601) issued against a five-year basic ordering agreement (SPRPA1-26-G-D601) for mission control displays. This was a sole-source acquisition using justification 10 U.S. Code 3204 (c)(1), as stated in Federal Acquisition Regulation 6.302-1. This is a three-year contract with no option periods. The delivery order end date is Feb. 9, 2029. Using customers are Navy and foreign military sales to France. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 through 2029 Navy working capital funds and foreign military sales funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Weapons Support, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.

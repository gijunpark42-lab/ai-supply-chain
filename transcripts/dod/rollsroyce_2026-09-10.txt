[AIR FORCE]
Rolls-Royce Corp., Indianapolis, Indiana, has been awarded a $43,203,798 modification (P00022) to a previously awarded contract (FA8107-21-D-0001) for an annual economic price adjustment. The modification brings the total cumulative face value of the contract to $3,037,212,324 from $2,994,008,526. Work will be performed at Indianapolis, Indiana, and is expected to be completed by September 23, 2038. No funding is being obligated at time of award. The Air Force Life Cycle Management Center Contracting, Tinker Air Force Base, Oklahoma, is the contracting activity.

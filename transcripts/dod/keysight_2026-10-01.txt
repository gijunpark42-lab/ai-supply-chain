[NAVY]
Keysight Technologies Inc., Colorado Springs, Colorado, was awarded an $11,376,546 firm-fixed-price, sole-source, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for the Microwave Radio Test Sets (MRTS). This contract provides calibration, repair, and other operations and maintenance support for the existing inventory of MRTS and for the procurement of new MRTS units. The ordering period will be a maximum of four years and is expected to be completed in September 2030. Work will be performed in Colorado Springs, Colorado. No funds will be obligated at the time of award. Funds will be obligated on individual task and delivery orders as they are issued. The requirement was procured in accordance with 10 U.S. Code 3204(a)(1) (as implemented by Federal Acquisition Regulation 6.302-1. Marine Corps Systems Command, Quantico, Virginia, is the contracting activity (M67854-26-D-5110).

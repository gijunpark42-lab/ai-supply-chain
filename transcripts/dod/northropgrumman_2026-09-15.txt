[ARMY | correction / update of an earlier announcement]
CORRECTION: The $19,999,798 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract (W519TC-26-F-A562) announced on Sept. 11, 2026, to Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Radford, Virginia, for non-standard ammunition, was not actually awarded.

[NAVY]
Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Woodland Hills, California, is awarded a $34,388,301 modification (P00005) to a firm-fixed-price indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract (N0001924D0123). This modification increases the ceiling of an existing contract for potential to procure additional UH-1Y and AH-1Z Helicopter Link-16 A-Kits, B-Kits, and associated Spare Parts. Specifically, the contract quantities are increased by (8) UH-1Y Link 16 A Kits, (9) AH-1Z Link 16 A Kits, (17) Link 16 B Kits, and Spare Parts including Systems Engineering/Program Management and Logistics Support. Work will be performed in Chicago, Illinois (23%), Woodland Hills, California (22%), Salt Lake City, Utah (20%), San Diego, California (9%), Rancho Cucamonga, California (8%), Huntsville, Alabama (8%), Dallas, Texas (5%), Scottsdale, Arizona (2%), Rochester, New York (1%), and Other Localities below 1% (2%), and is expected to be completed by March 2027. No funds will be obligated at the time of award; funds will be obligated on individual orders as they are issued. This contract was not competed. Naval Air Systems Command, Patuxent River, Maryland, is the contracting activity.

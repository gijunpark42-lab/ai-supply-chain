[MISSILE DEFENSE AGENCY]
Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Chandler, Arizona, is being awarded a noncompetitive, cost-plus-fixed-fee, cost reimbursable, and fixed-price-incentive contract. The total value of this contract is $508,490,719.00. Under this follow-on contract, the contractor will provide flight test support for the remaining Intermediate Range Ballistic Missile/Intercontinental Ballistic Missile targets and integrated logistics support for all government furnished equipment in support of Missile Defense System testing. The work will be performed in Chandler, Arizona; Corinne, Utah; and Elkton, Maryland. The performance period is from September 2026 through August 2035. One offer was solicited and one offer was received. Fiscal 2026 research, development, test, and evaluation funds in the amount of $8,786,477.00 are being obligated on this award. The Missile Defense Agency, Huntsville, Alabama, is the contracting activity (HQ0855-26-C-E001).

[NAVY]
Northrop Grumman Systems Corp.-Marine Systems, Sunnyvale, California, is awarded a $111,354,038 firm-fixed-price and cost-only, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for the ship service turbine generator maintenance, overhaul, repair, and long-lead time material in support of the in-service Virginia class nuclear-powered attack submarine fleet. This contract includes options which, if exercised, would bring the cumulative value to $253,293,127. Work will be performed in Sunnyvale, California (40%); Norfolk, Virginia (15%); Kittery, Maine (15%); Bremerton, Washington (15%); and Pearl Harbor, Hawaii (15%), and is expected to be completed by August 2031. If all options are exercised, work will continue through August 2034. No funds will be obligated at the time of the award; funds will be obligated on individual orders as they are issued. This contract was procured on a sole-source basis under the authority of 10 U.S. Code 3204(a)(1) only one responsible source and no other supplies or services will satisfy agency requirements. The Naval Sea Systems Command, Washington, D.C., is the contracting activity (N00024-26-D-4314).

[NAVY]
Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., San Diego, California, is awarded a $46,089,540 firm-fixed-price modification to a previously awarded contract (N00024-24-C-6400) to procure Joint Counter Radio-Controlled Improvised Explosive Device Electronic Warfare Increment One Block One systems, retrofit kits and spares. This modification also includes $4,199,955 in incremental funding for engineering support services and other direct costs. This contract involves Foreign Military Sales (FMS) to Australia. Work will be performed in Beaverton, Oregon (66%); and San Diego, California (34%), and is expected to be complete by December 2028. FMS (Australia) customer funds in the amount of $15,939,572 (32%); fiscal 2026 other procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $13,608,567 (27%); fiscal 2026 other procurement (Air Force) funds in the amount of $10,133,080 (20%); fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance (Air Force) funds in the amount of $3,807,104 (7%); fiscal 2025 other procurement (Air Force) funds in the amount of $2,790,248 (6%); fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance (Navy) funds in the amount of $2,174,345 (4%); fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation (Navy) funds in the amount of $1,370,014 (3%), and fiscal 2025 other procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $466,565 (1%), will be obligated at time of award, and funds in the amount of $5,981,449 will expire at the end of the current fiscal year. The Portfolio Acquisition Executive Maritime, Washington, D.C., is the contracting activity.

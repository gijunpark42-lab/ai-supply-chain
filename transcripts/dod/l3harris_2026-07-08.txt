[ARMY]
L3Harris Global Communications Inc., Rochester, New York, was awarded a $10,780,902 firm-fixed-price contract to procure of tactical communications equipment, hardware, and integration kits including GPS jamming land shield antennas, AN/PRC-167 50W vehicle accessory kits, AN/PRC-163 standard vehicle accessory kits, dismount soldier audio solutions, RF-7800I intercom systems, ruggedized tablets, and associated HC2 and CROWS software integration packages. One bid was solicited via the internet with one received. Work will be performed in Rochester, New York, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 28, 2027. Fiscal 2026 Foreign Military Sales (Slovakia) funds in the amount of $10,780,902 were obligated at the time of the award. Army Contracting Command, Aberdeen Proving Ground, Maryland, is the contracting activity (W91CRB-26-C-5057). (Awarded July 6, 2026)

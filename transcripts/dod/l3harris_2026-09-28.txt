[NAVY]
L3 Harris Technologies Inc., Rochester, New York, is awarded a $96,905,068 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract in support of Marine Corps Wideband Satellite-Expeditionary (MCWS-X). This contract provides for procurement of MCWS-X systems spares and associated services. Work will be performed in Rochester, New York, and is expected to be completed by September 2031. The maximum dollar value is $96,905,068. Funds will be obligated on individual task and delivery orders as they are issued. This contract was not competitively procured pursuant to Federal Acquisition Regulation 6.302-1. Portfolio Acquisition Executive Marine Corps, Quantico, Virginia, is the contracting activity (M67854-26-D-2001).

[NAVY]
L3Harris Fuzing and Ordnance Systems Inc., Cincinnati, Ohio, is awarded a $52,319,903 firm-fixed-price modification to previously-awarded contract N00174-26-C-1001 for proximity fuzes to upgrade the Advanced Precision Kill Weapon System. Work will be performed in Cincinnati, Ohio, and is expected to be completed by November 2027. Fiscal 2026 Defense-wide operations and maintenance funds in the amount of $44,801,033 (86%), fiscal 2025 Defense-wide operations and maintenance funds in the amount of $3,825,390 (7%); and fiscal 2026 procurement of ammunition (Army) funds in the amount of $3,693,480 (7%), will be obligated at time of award; of which $3,825,390 will expire at the end of the current fiscal year. The Naval Surface Warfare Center, Indian Head Division, Indian Head, Maryland, is the contracting activity.

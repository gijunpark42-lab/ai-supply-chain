[AIR FORCE]
L3Harris Technologies Inc., Colorado Springs, Colorado, has been awarded a $33,141,075 modification (P00380) to previously awarded contract FA8823-20-C-0004 for the Unified Messaging System effort. The modification brings the total cumulative face value of the contract to $1,527,155,327 from $1,494,014,252. Work will be performed in Colorado Springs, Colorado; and Vandenberg Space Force Base, California, and is expected to be completed by October 2028. Fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation funds in the amount of $2,419,579 are being obligated at the time of award. The Space Systems Command, Peterson Space Force Base, Colorado, is the contracting activity. (Awarded on Oct. 10, 2026).

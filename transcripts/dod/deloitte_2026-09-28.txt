Deloitte Consulting LLP, Arlington, Virginia, is awarded $61,890,804 for modification P00006 to increase the ceiling available to previously awarded, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity, firm-fixed-price contract N00189-24-D-Z004. This modification will provide business and technology management services in support of the Navy’s Office of Warfighting Advantage Digital Transformation Office. This modification will bring the estimated cumulative value of the total contract from a previously modified value of $97,556,463 to $159,447,267. The original contract included a five-year base ordering period that began in November 2023. There are no options periods. Work will be performed in Washington, D.C., and at other Navy facilities within the U.S., however the exact percentage of work at each site is unable to be determined at this time. No new funding will be obligated at time of contract modification. Appropriate fiscal year operations and maintenance (Navy) funds will fund individual task orders at the time of their issuance under the modified contract. This contract modification action is being executed on a non-competitive basis pursuant to Federal Acquisition Regulation. Naval Supply Systems Command Fleet Logistics Center Norfolk Contracting Department, Pentagon Directorate, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, is the contracting activity.

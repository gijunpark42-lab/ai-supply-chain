[AIR FORCE]
Eaton Mission Systems Division, Davenport, Iowa, has been awarded a $17,198,701 contract for Universal Water Activated Release System (UWARS). This contract provides UWARS units to support and enhance aircrew survivability during water landing. Work will be performed in Davenport, Iowa, and is expected to be completed by March 22, 2029. This contract involves foreign military sales to multiple countries. This contract was a sole source acquisition. Fiscal 2024 procurement funds in the amount of $2,884,062; fiscal 2025 procurement funds in the amount of $3,114,336; and Foreign Military Sales funds in the amount of $11,774,604, are being obligated at the time of award. The Air Force Lifecycle Management Center, Hill Air Force Base, Utah, is the contracting activity (FA8213-26-C-0010).

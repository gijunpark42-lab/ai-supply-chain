[NAVY]
Keysight Technologies Inc., Colorado Springs, Colorado, is awarded a $10,707,907 fixed-price indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for repair, maintenance, and calibration of signal generators, network analyzers, and oscilloscopes on a per-incident basis; purchase and renewal of maintenance and support agreements; on- and off-site technical support; on-site calibration services at naval facilities; and instrument-specific training in support of maintaining the accuracy and availability of specialized electronic test equipment used to support Navy research, development, test, and evaluation activities. Work will be performed in Colorado Springs, Colorado, and is expected to be completed in September 2031. No funds will be obligated at the time of award; funds will be obligated on individual orders as they are issued. This contract was not competitively procured. Naval Air Warfare Center Weapons Division, China Lake, California, is the contracting activity (N6893626D5012).

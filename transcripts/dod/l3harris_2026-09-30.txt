[NAVY]
L3 Technologies Inc., Camden, New Jersey, is being awarded a $26,945,335 firm-fixed-price and cost-plus-fixed-fee contract (N66001-26-C-0021) for the procurement, assembly, integration, configuration and shipment of communications equipment and components. This contract includes 15 options which, if exercised, would bring the overall potential value of this contract to an estimated $74,935,753. Work will be performed in Camden, New Jersey. Work is expected to begin in September 2026 and be completed by March 2030 if all options are exercised. Naval Information Warfare Systems Command (other procurement, Navy) funds in the amounts of $22,430,007; and shipbuilding and conversion, Navy funds in the amount of $4,515,328, will be obligated at the time of award. This contract was awarded as a sole-source acquisition under the statutory authority of 10 U.S. Code 4022, which allows for the award of a follow-on production contract or other transaction without competitive procedures. Naval Information Warfare Center Pacific, San Diego, California, is the contracting activity.

[AIR FORCE]
L3Harris, Greenville, Texas, was awarded a $461,908,308 firm-fixed-price definitization contracting action to provide commercial satellite communications connectivity for designated executive airlift aircraft. The contract supports authorized government personnel and is expected to be completed by Oct. 31, 2028. This contract was a sole-source acquisition. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance funds in the amount of $62,305,059 are obligated at the time of award. The Space Force Portfolio Acquisition Executive for Satellite Communications and Positioning, Navigation, and Timing, Los Angeles Air Force Base, California, is the contracting activity (FA254126CB014PZ004).

[ARMY]
Aerojet Rocketdyne Inc., Jupiter, Florida, was awarded a $9,567,725 cost-plus-fixed-fee contract to modernize and expand hypersonic test infrastructure. Bids were solicited via the internet with one received. Work will be performed in Jupiter, Florida, with an estimated completion date of Sept. 28, 2028. Fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation, Army funds in the amount of $9,567,725 were obligated at the time of the award. Army Contracting Command, Orlando, Florida, is the contracting activity (W900KK-26-C-A030).

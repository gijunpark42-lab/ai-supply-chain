Rolls-Royce Solutions America Inc., Novi, Michigan, was awarded a $39,123,120 modification (P00011) to contract W56HZV-23-C-0054 to procure Eitan power packs. The modification brings the total cumulative face value of the contract to $158,903,171. Work will be performed in Graniteville, South Carolina, with an estimated completion date of Sept. 11, 2027. Fiscal 2010 non-expiring Foreign Military Sales (Israel) funds in the amount of $39,123,120 were obligated at the time of the award. Army Contracting Command, Detroit Arsenal, Michigan, is the contracting activity.

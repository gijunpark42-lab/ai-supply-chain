Northrop Grumman Corp., Charlottesville, Virginia, is awarded a $18,121,584 firm-fixed-price modification to a previously awarded contract (N00024-23-C-5234) to exercise options for the manufacture of Navy and Foreign Military Sales (FMS) surface and submarine inertial sensor module configurations and associated support equipment in support of the IWS 6 Navigation program. This contract combines purchases for the Navy (23%); and the governments of Canada (44%); and Australia (33%), under the FMS program. Work will be performed in Charlottesville, Virginia (84%); Salt Lake City, Utah (14%); Woodland Hills, California (1%); and Baltimore, Maryland (1%), and is expected to be completed by February 2029. FMS (Canada) funds in the amount of $7,990,232 (44%); FMS (Australia) funds in the amount of $5,992,674 (33%); fiscal 2026 other procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $3,929,410 (22%); and fiscal 2025 other procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $209,268 (1%), will be obligated at the time of award and will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. Naval Sea Systems Command, Washington, D.C., is the contracting activity.

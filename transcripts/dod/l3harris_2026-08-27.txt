[AIR FORCE]
L3Harris Technologies Inc., Rochester, New York, has been awarded a $12,305,752 cost-plus-fixed-fee contract for advanced processing and exploitation software and reports. This contract provides for the Modality Independent Raster Image (MIRI) technology and demonstrates MIRI as an emerging capability within the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency PHENOM environment. Work will be performed at Rochester, New York and Herndon, Virginia, and is expected to be completed by Oct. 27, 2028. This contract was a competitive acquisition and two offers were received. Fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation funds in the amount of $2,000,000 are being obligated at the time of award. The Air Force Research Laboratory, Contracting Office, Rome New York, is the contracting activity (FA8750-26-C-B044).

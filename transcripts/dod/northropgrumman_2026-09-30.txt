[AIR FORCE]
Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Warner Robins, Georgia, has been awarded a $71,653,554 firm-fixed-price requirements contract for repair of the AN/APN-241 Low Power Color Radar. This contract provides for depot-level maintenance, repair, testing, packaging, handling, storage, transportation, and associated data support for AN/APN-241 radar components. Work will be performed at Robins Air Force Base, Georgia, and is expected to be completed by Sept. 29, 2031. This contract was a sole-source acquisition. No funds are being obligated at the time of award; funds will be obligated through subsequent delivery orders. The Air Force Sustainment Center, Robins Air Force Base, Georgia, is the contracting activity (FA8538-26-D-0002).

[DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY]
Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, has been awarded an estimated $49,528,809 cost-plus-fixed-fee modification (P00010) to a six-year firm-fixed-price contract (SPRTA1-23-C-0003) to extend the period of performance for the manufacture and repair of required tooling and production of B-2 rudder and elevon. This is a seven-year contract with no option periods. The performance completion date is March 27, 2030. Using military service is Air Force. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 defense working capital funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Weapons Support, Tinker Air Force Base, Oklahoma.

Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Melbourne, Florida, is awarded a $21,081,363 cost-plus-fixed-fee and firm-fixed-price order (N0001926F1043) against a previously issued basic ordering agreement (N0001925G0003). This order procures non-recurring engineering, including software development, modifications to the E-2D Avionics Flight Management Computer, systems engineering, integration, and test activities, to integrate the Second Generation Anti-jam Tactical Ultra High Frequency Radio (SATURN) for NATO capability into the E-2D Advanced Hawkeye aircraft. The order also procures the production and delivery of 94 SATURN modification kits. Work will be performed in Melbourne, Florida (99.1%); West Chester, Ohio (0.6%); Ronkonkoma, New York (0.2%); and Laval, Quebec, Canada (0.1%), and is expected to be completed in April 2028. Fiscal 2026 aircraft procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $21,081,363 will be obligated at the time of award. This order was not competed. Naval Air Systems Command, Patuxent River, Maryland, is the contracting activity.

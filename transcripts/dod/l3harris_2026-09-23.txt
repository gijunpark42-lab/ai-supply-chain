[NAVY]
L3Harris Technologies Inc., Salt Lake City, Utah, is awarded an $876,440,942 cost-plus-incentive-fee, undefinitized modification (P00021) to previously awarded contract (N0001924C0061). This modification adds scope to provide for the continued development of the Next Generation Jammer Low Band system, including non-recurring tooling and equipment and associated labor; delivery of system demonstration test article pods; additional cybersecurity and system security development; and product, logistics, test and evaluation, and software support for the Navy and Royal Australian Air Force (RAAF). Work will be performed in Salt Lake City, Utah (43%); Hollywood, Alabama (14.8%); Lansdale, Pennsylvania (8.1%); Clifton, New Jersey (8%); Boulder, Colorado (7.5%); Goleta, California (5.8%); Australia (3.4%); Carlsbad, California (3%); Edgewood, New York (2.6%); Waco, Texas (2.6%); Parsippany, New Jersey (1%); and Long Island, New York (0.2%), and is expected to be completed in fiscal 2031. Fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation (Navy) funds in the amount of $3,653,866; and RAAF cooperative funds in the amount of $23,044,630 will be obligated at the time of award. This modification was not competitively procured. Naval Air Systems Command, Patuxent River, Maryland, is the contracting activity.

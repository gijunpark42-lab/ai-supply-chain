Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Rolling Meadows, Illinois, was awarded a $4,838,487,318 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for full rate production of the Common Infrared Countermeasure. One bid was solicited with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Sept. 30, 2035. Army Contracting Command, Redstone Arsenal, Alabama, is the contracting activity (W58RGZ-26-D-0056).

[AIR FORCE]
Blue Origin LLC, Merritt Island, Florida, has been awarded an $11,707,762 firm-fixed-price modification (P00001) to a previously awarded contract (FA2385-25-C-B023) to define requirements for how Blue Origin systems and technologies could be used for the rocket cargo mission. The modification brings the total cumulative face value of the contract to $13,074,453 from $1,366,691. Work will be performed in Merritt Island, Florida; and Kent, Washington, and is expected to be completed by Oct. 29, 2027. Fiscal 2025 research, development, test and evaluation funds (Space Force) in the amount of $1,130,643; and fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation funds (Space Force) in the amount of $10,464,929, are being obligated at time of award. The Air Force Research Laboratory, Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, Ohio, is the contracting activity (FA2385-25-C-B023).

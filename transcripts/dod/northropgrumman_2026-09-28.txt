[AIR FORCE]
Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Meadows, Illinois, has been awarded a $36,987,335 modification (P00187) to previously awarded (FA8615-17-C-6047) for F-16 Integrated Viper Electronic Warfare Suite – Electronic Warfare Radio Frequency Compatibility, Big Iron effort. Work will be performed in Rolling Meadows, Illinois, and is expected to be completed by Dec. 31, 2029. Fiscal 2025 research, development, test and evaluation funds in the amount of $16,183,490 are being obligated at the time of award. Air Force Life Cycle Management Center, Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, Ohio, is the contracting activity.

[AIR FORCE]
Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Linthicum Heights, Maryland, has been awarded a $22,320,854 firm-fixed-price contract for F-35 radar sustainment. This contract provides for sustainment for the F-35 radar. Work will be performed at Linthicum Heights, Maryland; and Robins Air Force Base, Georgia, and is expected to be completed by March 31, 2032. This contract was a sole source acquisition. Fiscal 2026 working capital funds in the amount of $3,682,496 are being obligated at time of award. The Air Force Sustainment Center, Robins AFB, Georgia, is the contracting activity (FA8579-26-C-0013).

[NAVY]
Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Linthicum Heights, Maryland, is awarded a $19,849,996 firm-fixed-price job order (JO) against previously issued basic ordering agreement (BOA) (N0016425GJW77) for the procurement of test program sets and automated test equipment to support depot repair testing of AN/ZPY-3 multi-function active sonar shop replaceable assemblies in support of the PMA-262 Program. This JO does not include options. Work will be performed in Linthicum Heights, Maryland, and is expected to be completed by April 2030. Fiscal 2026 aircraft procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $7,035,383 (35%); and fiscal 2025 aircraft procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $12,814,613 (65%), will be obligated at time of award. The BOA and resulting JO were awarded on a sole-source basis in which there is only one responsible source, and no other supplies or services can satisfy agency requirements in accordance with 10 U.S. Code 3204(a)(1). Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane, Crane, Indiana, is the contract activity (N0016426FM026).

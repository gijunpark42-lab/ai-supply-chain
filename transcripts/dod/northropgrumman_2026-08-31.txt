[AIR FORCE]
Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Riverdale, Utah, has been awarded an $86,069,000 task order (FA8214-26-F-B017) under previously awarded ground subsystem support contract 2.0 FA8214-21-D-0002 for remote visual assessment (RVA) program support. This task order provides for program management, maintenance, sustaining engineering, and support services for the RVA program. Work will be performed at Hill Air Force Base, Utah; F.E. Warren AFB, Wyoming; Malmstrom AFB, Montana; and Minot AFB, North Dakota, and is expected to be completed by September 2031. This award is the result of a sole-source acquisition. Fiscal 2026 procurement funds in the amount of $15,240,085 are being obligated at the time of award. The Air Force Nuclear Weapons Center, Hill AFB, Utah, is the contracting activity (FA8214-26-F-B017).

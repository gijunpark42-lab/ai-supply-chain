Semiconductor Manufacturing International Corporation said second-quarter 2026 profit and revenue topped Wall Street expectations, helped by strong demand for AI-related chips, higher prices and tight factory utilization. The Shanghai-based chipmaker reported earnings per share of $0.06, above the $0.0372 forecast, and revenue of $3.01 billion, ahead of the $2.85 billion estimate. The stock was little changed in recent trading at $67.55, up 0.22% from the previous close, suggesting investors were weighing the strong quarter against rising depreciation and other cost pressures.

SMIC delivered what executives called an exceptional quarter, with gains across shipment volume, pricing, utilization and profitability. Revenue rose 20% sequentially, while shipment volume increased 14.4% as customers pulled in orders and demand for AI-related peripheral chips accelerated. The company said it also benefited from selective price increases in supply-constrained segments. According to InvestingPro, SMIC stands as a prominent player in the Semiconductors & Semiconductor Equipment industry, a position that has helped it capitalize on the current AI-driven demand surge.

Gross margin climbed to 25.3% from the prior quarter, and operating profit reached $534 million. Net profit attributable to shareholders was $479 million. For the first half of 2026, revenue reached $5.511 billion, up 23.7% year over year, while net profit totaled $677 million.

The results point to a semiconductor market that remains uneven. AI and data-center-related demand is strong, while smartphone and consumer electronics remain weak. SMIC said it is shifting capacity toward tighter markets, especially logic circuits for AI computing, BCD power management, image sensors, display drivers and specialty memory.

SMIC beat expectations on both major metrics. Earnings per share came in at $0.06, compared with a forecast of $0.0372, a surprise of 61.29%. Revenue was $3.01 billion versus the expected $2.85 billion, a 5.61% beat.

The size of the EPS beat was especially notable. In semiconductor manufacturing, profit can move sharply with utilization and pricing, and SMIC said both improved in the quarter. The company also benefited from a better product mix and higher average selling prices, which rose 5.7% sequentially to $991 per 8-inch equivalent wafer.

The result was stronger than a routine top-line beat. It reflected a quarter in which demand, pricing and factory efficiency all moved in the same direction. Management said the shipment increase came from AI-related demand and customer pull-ins, while pricing gains from earlier negotiations began to flow through the quarter.

The stock was trading at $67.55, up 0.22% from the previous close, after the report. That muted reaction suggests investors may be looking past the headline beat and focusing instead on whether the company can sustain its margin gains as depreciation rises. The modest price movement is consistent with the stock’s character&mdash;InvestingPro notes that SMIC generally trades with high price volatility, though the company has delivered strong returns over the last five years. The shares are trading well above the 52-week low of $49.32, but still below the 52-week high of $93.50. That places the stock in the middle of its yearly range, which may indicate that the market already expected a solid quarter after a strong run in AI-related semiconductor names.

No unusual trading volume was provided. Still, the limited price move stands out against the size of the earnings surprise, hinting that investors may be balancing the strong near-term demand picture against longer-term cost pressure and the risk that some orders were pulled forward into the quarter.

For the third quarter, SMIC said revenue should rise 2% to 4% sequentially, implying sales of about $3.066 billion to $3.126 billion. Gross margin is expected to improve to 26% to 28%, supported by pricing gains and high utilization.

Management said utilization should remain near 95%, including newly added capacity. The company also expects shipment volume to keep growing, though more moderately than in the second quarter, after some customer pull-ins boosted Q2 results.

Looking further ahead, SMIC said it will continue adding about 8,000 12-inch equivalent wafers of monthly capacity and will keep 5% of capacity reserved for research and development. The company also expects depreciation to rise about 30% year over year in 2026, reaching about $4.9 billion to $5.0 billion for the full year. Management said depreciation may peak in 2027, though the timing could change if capital spending plans are adjusted.

Co-Chief Executive Officer Zhao Haijun said the quarter showed broad-based strength. &ldquo;In the second quarter, the company’s business achieved growth in both wafer shipment and price,&rdquo; he said. &ldquo;Quarterly revenue surpassed $3 billion, with all key operating metrics recording substantial increases year-over-year and quarter-over-quarter.&rdquo;

He said shipment growth was driven by AI demand and customer pull-ins. &ldquo;The increase in shipment was mainly driven by surging demand for peripheral chips brought by AI as well as customers’ shipment pull-in,&rdquo; Zhao said.

On pricing, Zhao said SMIC is taking a measured approach. In Chinese, he noted that the company is &ldquo;never the first to raise prices, nor raises them the most aggressively,&rdquo; but maintains a reputation for negotiating reasonable prices with customers. That stance suggests SMIC is trying to balance margin gains with long-term customer relationships.

CFO Wu Junfeng said the 70.2% EBITDA margin was a high point for the company, but he also noted that some of the quarter&rsquo;s strength came from one-time items, including other income and fair value changes in associated companies.

Analysts focused on three main issues: where shipment growth came from, how long pricing strength can last, and how the company plans to manage rising depreciation.

Management said the 14.4% sequential shipment increase came from customer demand pull-ins and faster shipment of near-complete wafers, rather than from a large jump in new capacity. Executives also said AI-related demand is flowing through several categories, including computing, industrial and automotive products.

On pricing, SMIC said it has already implemented selective increases in supply-constrained segments such as AI, computing and industrial applications, but not in weaker smartphone and consumer categories. Management said further price increases could come later if shortages persist.

Analysts also pressed the company on the outlook for margins as depreciation rises. SMIC said gross margin should still improve in Q3 because pricing gains are expected to outweigh the higher depreciation burden. The company said it remains optimistic about industry trends, especially as AI infrastructure spending continues to support demand for mature and specialty semiconductor processes.

Dr. Zhao Haijun, Co-Chief Executive Officer, Semiconductor Manufacturing International Corporation (SMIC): 好的，胡建，谢谢你。专门讨论BCD的问题，或者是电源供应、电源管理这方面的问题。现在我们看到一件事情，就是说最近出现的新的未来产业，人工智能带动的一个产业，首先推动的是两头，一头是跟计算的标准逻辑有关的，另外一部分就是非常成熟的模拟电路有关的，尤其是8英寸的需求，它的增长幅度远远超过想象。反而中间的一截是慢慢地被溢出来推动的，比方说40纳米、28纳米，反而是被溢出来推动的。40纳米、28纳米没有任何东西可以直接进入跟算力和人工智能数据中心有关的部分。所以大家都在说，因为BCD的产能供不应求，就所有做BCD、做模拟电路的，现在基本上都在讲自己在大幅地成长。那我们现在看到这里有两点：第一点就是其实大家需要的产品类型非常的多，BCD模拟电路是一个长期经营的、慢慢积累的这样一个产品类型，所以没有任何一家能够把它完全都吃掉，你只能说你吃掉要求最高、价钱最好的那个部分，因为它量太大了。在国外的巨头公司，模拟电路动辄推出来有20万、30万个型号，然后这样就变成了有的型号它的单一需求量比较大，所以现在迅速上量的，包括用12吋来去做也可以。但是有一些需求量很小，而且未来10年要一直供应，中间还不能断的，这个就没办法立刻拿过去。几千个产品你不可能短时间拿过去的。还有一个，它需求量每个月只有100片，换成12吋，一个月你下线都不方便，对吧？所以就变成了有一些是8英寸，要牢牢地在那里。再加上8英寸的设备更成熟，12吋设备往往现在你建条产线，你要做65纳米、50纳米，可能有的设备相当于是22纳米，或者是更先进的，有一些是老型号，它搭配起来需要磨合的时间，不一定立刻就能取代现在8英寸的部分。所以现在我们看到它是有分割的，其中分割需求量很大的，要求48伏高压大电流的，基本都是在8英寸上面。然后有一些单一大宗的，现在8英寸没那么多产能，逐渐地向12吋来移动。但是不管怎么样，现在看到的这个需求可以一直看到来年年底都没问题。因为大家宣布建了一个数据中心，宣布要买GPU，也有业界的龙头公司现在已经在建设更先进的生产厂，芯片的生产厂，那些是不会被浪费的。那些生产厂每生产一片GPU或者是算力的ASIC，它一定会搭建相应的，刚才我说这些模拟电路、电源供应、光模块、数据管理、HBM、SSD，它都会在那个地方的。所以这个配套的趋势也可以肯定，将来它一定是这样的需求。所以我们看到BCD平台的需求和价格走势一直是坚挺的，一直是供不应求的。那刚才讲的不同的公司差异怎么样？差异现在看两点，第一个是电压，应该大家要求这个要做高压一点，这样可以节省很多导线，也可以提供更大的电流，从48伏一直变到1伏。做48伏的这个需求量是比较大的，供应的人也是比较少的，可靠性也比较少，因为你的耐压一定要耐压到80到100伏，你才可以工作在48伏，这需要长期的验证。那这个部分供应是比较少的，这是一个。另外一个，我们觉得这个就是要跟原来已经经营了好多年的大客户，已经有10来年的联系，一直工作，现在它突然爆发，你就可以突然供上去。如果这个客户现在再去转移，可能就慢一点。所以我们主要是看这两点就好了。

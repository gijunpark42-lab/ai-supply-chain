---
E2E Networks reported a sharp rise in revenue and profit for Q2 FY27, driven by Blackwell GPU deployment, stronger utilization and more long-term contracts, while its shares slipped 1.81% to $698 from the previous close of $710.9. Revenue rose 411% from a year earlier to INR 238 million, and EBITDA climbed to INR 1,750 million with a margin of 76.6%. Profit after tax reached INR 727 million, up from INR 439 million in the prior quarter. The company also said it expects the next batch of 1,024 B200 GPUs to go live in Q3 FY27.

Key Takeaways

Revenue jumped 411% year over year and 43% sequentially, reflecting strong demand for AI infrastructure.EBITDA margin reached 76.6%, while gross margin rose to 88%, above the company’s earlier 85% target.E2E signed a nearly INR 1,000 crore multi-year contract with a sovereign AI customer through June 2029.Management said the company is still in the early stages of its AI buildout and is investing in platforms, talent and product development.The company plans to deploy 1,024 more B200 GPUs in Q3 FY27 and is preparing inference products for launch.

Company Performance

E2E Networks said Q2 FY27 was another strong quarter, with growth across revenue, EBITDA and profit. The company’s results point to rising demand for GPU infrastructure used in training, inference and other AI workloads.

The quarter also showed a shift in the business mix. Management said most GPUs now run on top of its platform, rather than being sold as bare metal. That suggests the company is moving beyond simple compute rental and toward a more managed software-led model.

The company described its market as global and still early in development. It said the AI infrastructure opportunity is large enough for many players, even as competitors in India and abroad expand their GPU fleets.

Financial Highlights

Revenue: INR 238 million, up 411% year over year and 43% quarter over quarter.H1 FY27 revenue: INR 3,805 million, up 376% year over year.EBITDA: INR 1,750 million, with a 76.6% margin, up 45% quarter over quarter.EBITDA margin: up 141 basis points from Q1 FY27.Gross margin: 88%, above the company’s earlier 85% guidance level.Profit before tax: INR 971 million, up from INR 586 million in Q1 FY27.Profit after tax: INR 727 million, up from INR 439 million in Q1 FY27.H1 FY27 PAT: INR 1,176 million, compared with a loss of INR 163 million in H1 FY26.Diluted EPS: 3.4x, compared with 2.1x in Q1 FY27.Operating costs: INR 18 crore, reflecting higher spending on talent and expansion.

Earnings vs. Forecast

No forecast figures were provided for revenue or EPS, so a formal beat or miss cannot be measured. Even so, the reported numbers were strong by any standard.

Revenue growth of 411% year over year and EBITDA margin above 76% suggest a quarter that likely exceeded many market expectations. The company also delivered a sharp improvement in profitability, with PAT rising sequentially and H1 earnings moving from a loss a year earlier to a solid profit.

The scale of the improvement stands out even against E2E’s recent trend. The company has been growing quickly in prior quarters, but Q2 FY27 showed a larger jump in both revenue and margins, helped by Blackwell GPU deployment and better asset use.

Market Reaction

E2E Networks shares were down 1.81% at $698, compared with a previous close of $710.9. The stock remains near the upper end of its 52-week range of $183.36 to $765, sitting about 8.8% below the high and roughly 280% above the low.

The modest decline suggests investors did not react with strong enthusiasm, even after the company posted very fast growth. InvestingPro data shows the stock has delivered strong returns of 18% over the last month, 45% over three months, 92% over six months, and 281% over the past year, placing it near the top of its 52-week range. The move was not large enough to indicate a sharp negative reassessment, but it may reflect profit-taking after a strong run or caution around the company’s limited formal guidance.

No trading volume data was provided, so it is not possible to judge whether the move came with unusual activity.

Outlook & Guidance

Management did not give revenue or MRR guidance. Tarun Dua, managing director, said the company prefers to report what it has done rather than offer speculative forecasts.

The clearest near-term milestone is the planned deployment of 1,024 B200 GPUs in Q3 FY27. Management also said it will announce plans for B300 and Vera Rubin GPUs once those deployments are sufficiently solidified.

The company is also preparing to launch inference products, including Blaze and Endpoints, within weeks. Management said these tools are meant to help customers move from development to production workloads.

Longer term, E2E said it aims to maintain EBITDA margins above 70% and continue building its sovereign AI platform, TIR, along with Jarvislabs.

Executive Commentary

Tarun Dua said the quarter reflected continued execution and early-stage growth in a still-developing market. “This is still day zero of AI for us, as far as we can see,” he said.

He also highlighted the company’s long-term contract with a sovereign AI customer. “We signed a nearly INR 1,000 crore deal with a sovereign AI company for the Blackwell chip, which lasts through June 2029,” he said.

On strategy, Dua said the company is focused on technology rather than squeezing more short-term return from existing GPUs. “The long-term strategy is to make money by building more technology,” he said.

Chief Financial Officer Nitin Jain said the quarter’s performance was driven by the Blackwell GPU, better utilization and higher average realization across the fleet.

Risks and Challenges

No formal guidance: The company does not provide revenue or MRR forecasts, which can make future performance harder to model.CapEx timing: Results depend on when new GPUs are ordered, deployed and utilized.Margin volatility: Management said gross and EBITDA margins can move with capacity cycles and utilization patterns.- Competitive pressure: Rival firms in India and abroad are also expanding GPU capacity.

Valuation risk: InvestingPro analysis indicates the stock is currently overvalued based on multiple metrics. Investors monitoring valuation levels can explore the platform’s Most Overvalued stocks list for broader market context.

Q&A

Analysts focused heavily on GPU deployment, contract structure, margins and future capacity plans.

Questions centered on the next B200 batch, with management saying the 1,024 GPUs should go live in Q3 FY27. Analysts also asked about the INR 1,000 crore contract, which management said was converted from pay-as-you-go capacity into a multi-year arrangement.

Margin sustainability was another major topic. Management said the company’s medium- and long-term target remains 70%+ EBITDA margins, even after the quarter’s 88% gross margin.

Analysts also asked about international revenue, which management said made up about 20% to 25% of Q2 revenue, and IndiaAI Mission revenue, which CFO Nitin Jain said accounted for about 13% to 15%.

Other questions covered debt, funding flexibility, AMD compatibility, inference demand and the company’s platform strategy. Management said it is open to debt and other funding structures as long as cash flow supports repayment, and that its software should be compatible with future GPU architectures.

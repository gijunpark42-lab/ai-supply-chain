Mersen said first-half sales rose 3.9% on an organic basis to EUR 611 million, with EBITDA margin holding at 15.9% and operating income before non-recurring items reaching EUR 56.5 million. The French industrial group also raised its full-year 2026 outlook, pointing to stronger growth and margins ahead. The stock rose 1.73% to $35.2 in early trading, after closing at $34.6, leaving it well above its 52-week low but still below the year&rsquo;s high.

Mersen delivered a solid first half, with growth supported by its Electrical Power business and strong demand in data centers and semiconductors. The company said net income rose 5% year over year, or 10% at constant exchange rates, while non-recurring expenses remained low at EUR 1 million.

The results showed a mixed picture across end markets. Electrical Power posted very strong profitability, while Advanced Materials faced pressure from weak chemicals and a still-soft solar market. Even so, management said that without those two segments, Advanced Materials would have shown positive organic growth.

The company also highlighted its global footprint and its position in markets tied to electrification, artificial intelligence infrastructure and clean mobility. Chief Executive Salvador Lamas said Mersen is benefiting from &ldquo;powerful long-term trends&rdquo; and a broad presence across the electrical value chain.

No EPS or revenue consensus figures were provided for the period, so a direct comparison with analyst forecasts was not available.

Still, the reported numbers suggest a healthy operating performance. Organic sales growth of 3.9% and an EBITDA margin of 15.9% indicate that Mersen is holding profitability while navigating uneven demand. The company also said operating income improved 2% at constant exchange rates and EBITDA rose nearly 4%.

The quality of the result mattered as much as the headline figures. Electrical Power posted a historical EBIT margin of 14.9%, and management said that level is sustainable. That helped offset weakness in chemicals and solar, which kept Advanced Materials from contributing more strongly.

Mersen shares were up 1.73% at $35.2 in early trading, compared with $34.6 at the prior close. The move added about $0.60 a share.

The stock is now about 22.5% above its 52-week low of $20.43, but still roughly 22.4% below its 52-week high of $45.32. The reaction suggests investors viewed the report and outlook as constructive, but not transformative. With a market cap of $983 million and a P/E ratio of 62, the stock trades at a premium valuation despite recent volatility.

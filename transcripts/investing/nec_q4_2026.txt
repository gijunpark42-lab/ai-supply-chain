NEC Corporation reported strong financial results for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2026, with significant profitability improvements and solid cash generation. Despite revenue declines in some sectors, NEC achieved a record non-GAAP operating profit. However, the company’s stock fell by 3.91% following the earnings call, reflecting investor concerns over future revenue projections and market headwinds.

NEC’s non-GAAP operating profit reached a record high, growing by 27.6% year-over-year.The company’s BluStellar business showed a 30% increase in revenue, exceeding initial targets.Free cash flow increased by 121.5%, driven by strategic sales and real estate disposals.The stock price dropped 3.91% after the earnings call, despite strong profit growth.NEC’s FY 2027 revenue is forecasted to decline by 2.3%, reflecting peaking public sector projects.

Company PerformanceNEC Corporation demonstrated robust performance with a 9% year-over-year increase in adjusted revenue, excluding certain strategic adjustments. The company achieved a non-GAAP operating profit of 397.2 billion yen, marking a 27.6% increase from the previous year. Key drivers included IT services and the aerospace and defense segments, with BluStellar contributing significantly to profit growth.

The company’s valuation metrics present an attractive picture for investors. Trading at a P/E ratio of 24.09 with a PEG ratio of just 0.79, InvestingPro analysis indicates NEC is trading at a low P/E ratio relative to near-term earnings growth. The company maintains a market capitalization of approximately $37 billion and has delivered a remarkable 43.6% return over the past year.

For FY 2027, NEC forecasts a revenue of 3,500 billion yen, a 2.3% decrease from FY 2026, factoring in macroeconomic uncertainties and component risks. The company aims to achieve a non-GAAP operating profit of 420 billion yen, reflecting ongoing profitability expansion.

NEC’s executives highlighted the success of the BluStellar business, with plans to expand revenue to 840 billion yen in FY 2027. They emphasized continued focus on AI-driven solutions and productivity improvements to drive future growth.

Sterlite Technologies said first-quarter profit, revenue and margins all reached record levels, as demand from data centers and enterprise customers helped drive a sharp jump in sales and earnings. The company reported revenue of INR 1,910 crore, up 87% from a year earlier, EBITDA of INR 397 crore, up 184%, and profit after tax of INR 197 crore. Management also raised its full-year EBITDA margin target to 23% from 20%. The stock rose 2.94% to $565, near the top of its 52-week range. The strong performance aligns with InvestingPro analysis showing that analysts anticipate sales growth in the current year and expect net income to grow as well. The platform identifies Sterlite as a prominent player in the Communications Equipment industry, though investors should note the stock is trading at a high earnings multiple.

Sterlite Technologies said Q1 FY 2027 was its strongest quarter on record, with gains across revenue, EBITDA and profit. The company said the results reflected a shift in its business mix toward higher-value data center and enterprise work, while telecom and citizen networks remained a core part of the business.

The company&rsquo;s revenue growth was supported by strong demand in North America, which accounted for 54% of quarterly sales, up from 39% in FY 2026. Europe contributed 25%, while the rest of the world made up 22%. Management said the broader geographic mix reduces concentration risk and supports growth across several markets.

Sterlite also said it completed a qualified institutional placement of INR 1,500 crore, with proceeds mainly earmarked for debt reduction. The company ended the quarter with a net cash position of INR 483 crore and said it expects to remain net debt-free through FY 2027.

No EPS or revenue consensus figures were provided in the materials, so a direct comparison with analyst forecasts was not available. Even so, the reported numbers point to a clear operational beat in the broader sense, with revenue, EBITDA and PAT all setting records.

The scale of the improvement was notable. Revenue rose 87% from a year earlier, while EBITDA increased 184%. PAT reached INR 197 crore, which management said was the company&rsquo;s highest margin ever at 10.3% of revenue. That kind of growth is far above the company&rsquo;s recent historical trend and suggests the business is benefiting from both stronger demand and a better product mix.

Sterlite Technologies shares rose 2.94% to $565 from a previous close of $548.85. The stock is now trading close to the top of its 52-week range of $84.6 to $679.9, reflecting a strong rally over the past year. According to InvestingPro data, the stock has delivered high returns over the last year, with strong gains also recorded over the last three and six months. The company has been profitable over the last twelve months, supporting the recent price momentum.

The move suggests investors welcomed the record quarter and the higher margin outlook. At the same time, the gain was not extreme, which may indicate that some optimism was already priced in after the stock&rsquo;s sharp run. No unusual volume data was provided.

Management raised its full-year FY 2027 EBITDA margin target to 23% from 20%, saying the company had already reached the earlier goal in Q1. The company did not provide revenue guidance, but it pointed to a strong order book and a large execution pipeline for the rest of the year.

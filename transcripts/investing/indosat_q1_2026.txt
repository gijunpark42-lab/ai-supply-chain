PT Indosat Tbk reported a robust performance in Q1 2026, with revenue rising 12.1% year-over-year to IDR 15.2 trillion and EBITDA increasing by 12.9%. The company demonstrated effective cost management, resulting in an expanded EBITDA margin of 47.6%. Despite a slight quarter-on-quarter revenue decline due to seasonal factors, the underlying trend remains positive. The stock price remained stable in premarket trading at IDR 26.24.

Revenue increased by 12.1% year-over-year in Q1 2026.EBITDA margin expanded to 47.6%.ARPU grew by 15% year-over-year, reaching IDR 45,000.Significant CapEx investment with a 59.6% year-over-year increase.

Company PerformancePT Indosat Tbk showcased strong financial performance in the first quarter of 2026, driven by strategic initiatives and cost management. The revenue growth of 12.1% year-over-year highlights the company’s ability to capitalize on market opportunities. The EBITDA margin improvement to 47.6% reflects effective cost control measures. While revenue saw a slight quarter-on-quarter decline due to seasonality, the adjusted underlying trend remains positive.

Revenue: IDR 15.2 trillion (+12.1% YoY)EBITDA: +12.9% YoY, margin at 47.6%Normalized NPAT: +26.2% YoYCapEx: +59.6% YoY

Outlook & GuidanceThe company is committed to strategic CapEx investments aimed at medium and long-term growth. With 32% of its full-year CapEx guidance already deployed, PT Indosat Tbk is focused on expanding its network capacity to meet increasing demand. The ongoing spectrum auction in Indonesia is expected to further enhance the company’s 5G investment strategy.

CEO of PT Indosat Tbk stated, "Our strong financial results in Q1 2026 reflect our strategic focus on cost leadership and revenue diversification. The partnership with Google Gemini positions us as a leader in AI services in Indonesia."

Rising personnel costs due to increased performance-based compensation.Potential regulatory changes impacting the telecommunications sector.Competitive pressure in the AI and digital services market.Supply chain constraints affecting network infrastructure deployment.

Q&AAnalysts inquired about the impact of the Google Gemini partnership on ARPU growth, to which executives responded that early numbers are positive and expected to drive further ARPU increases. There were also questions regarding the company’s strategy for handling increased personnel costs, with management highlighting the focus on performance-based compensation to align with operational success.

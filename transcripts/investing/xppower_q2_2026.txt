Analysts focused on three main themes: semiconductor share gains, delivery timing and capacity expansion.

In semiconductors, management said growth came from both new business and market recovery, with wins across etch, deposition, ion implant and inspection. The company also said it gained share after a competitor had a business continuity issue earlier in the year.

On lead times, CFO Matt Beale said they were in the five- to six-month range and slightly longer in the second quarter. He said pricing improvements are still flowing through, helped by both tariff pass-through and underlying price increases.

Questions also centered on the new Malaysia plant and the Vietnam ramp. Management said the Malaysia facility is complete, but customer qualification will affect the pace of production increases. The company expects Vietnam to drive most of the growth in 2027 while it balances output across both sites.

Analysts also asked about industrial technology, where XP Power has its lowest market share but the largest addressable market. Management said it is expanding through distribution channels and a partner model in Europe, and believes it can maintain or grow share in that segment.

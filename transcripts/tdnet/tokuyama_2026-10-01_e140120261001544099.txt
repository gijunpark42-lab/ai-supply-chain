3. Recognition of  Extraordinary Income, Extraordinary Losses, and Future Outlook
Following completion of  this share transfer, Tokuyama expects to record an extraordinary income  of
approximately 30.5 billion yen  as income  on the transfer and an extraordinary loss of  approximately
12 billion yen as impairment loss  in the third quarter of  the fiscal year ending March 31, 2027. The
amount of  the income on the transfer  is based on the previously disclosed transfer price of  37.0
billion yen, factoring in the consolidated book value, related expenses, and other adjustments. Please
note that this amount is an estimate and may vary depending on the progress of  financial clo sing
procedures.
The Company is currently assessing the impact of  the recognition of  the aforementioned
extraordinary income  and e xtraordinary losses on its consolidated earnings forecast for the fiscal year
ending March 2027.  Should a revision to the earnings forecast be necessary, details will be promptly
disclosed separately.

Nidec Corporation to Transfer (Its Shares in) a Subsidiary
Nidec Corporation (TSE: 6594; OTC US: NJDCY) (“Nidec Corporation” or the “Company”) announced that it has reached
a resolution in the meeting of its Board of Directors held today to transfer all of its shares in Nidec Components Corporation
(hereinafter referred to as “Nidec Components”), a subsidiary of the Company, to TCG2603 Co., Ltd, a company indirectly
owned by an investment fund related to The Carlyle Group (hereinafter referred to as “Carlyle,” including its affiliate
companies and other related entities) (hereinafter referred to as the “Share Transfer”), as described below.
1. Reasons for the Share Transfer
Based on the “Re-Definition” declaration, a five-year (FY2026 – FY2030) management reform plan that it announced in
May 2026, the Company is promoting a business portfolio reform to restore its proper business management and improve its
profit-generating structure.  This plan upholds a basic policy of allocating the entire Nidec Group’s management resources
focusing on its core businesses and important high-growth areas.
Since its establishment as Copal Electronics Corporation in April 1967 to research, develop, and sell electronics components,
Nidec Components Corporation constantly grew as part of the Nidec Group’s electronic components business after Nidec
Corporation’s capital participation in the company in April 1998 and becoming a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company
in 2014.
Now, selling pressure sensors, switches, torque sensors, encoders, and other products for industrial equipment and other
applications, Nidec Components boasts a high degree of trust among its customers.  In particular, the company enjoys a
largest-level share of the global external sales market of polygon mirrors.
While mid-to-long-term growth is expected for the semiconductor manufacturing equipment and collaborative robot markets
where Nidec Components operates business, the rapid technological innovation and the escalating global competition require
companies, more than ever, to make prompt and large-scale capital and R&D investments and to make expeditious business
decisions.
Based on the aforementioned changes in the market environment, Nidec Corporation contemplated possible options for Nidec
Components, such as promoting an independent growth strategy under an external partner, to help the company best utilize
its technological capabilities and customer base and grow further as a company.
Consequently, the Company came to the conclusion that transferring Nidec Components to Carlyle, an investment firm with
global knowledge and network in addition to abundant management resources , would be the best option for Nidec
Components to further improve its corporate value, and for the Company to concentrate its management resources on
important growth areas and reform its business portfolio significantly.

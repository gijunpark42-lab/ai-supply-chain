(Cancellation of Disclosed Matter) Notice Regarding Cancellation of Acquisition of
DENSO CORPORATION’s Spark Plug and Exhaust Gas Sensor Businesses
Niterra Co., Ltd. (hereinafter the “Company”) hereby announces that it has resolved today to cancel the
acquisition (hereinafter the “Business Acquisition”) of the spark plug and exhaust gas sensor businesses
(hereinafter the “Target Businesses”) operated by DENSO CORPORATION (hereinafter “DENSO”), which
was announced in the press release titled “Announcement on the Acquisition of DENSO CORPORATION’s
Spark Plug Business and Exhaust Gas Sensor Business” dated September 1, 2025, and to enter into an
agreement regarding the termination of the business transfer agreement previously executed with DENSO, as
described below.
1. Reasons for Cancellation of the Business Acquisition and Reaching the Termination Agreement
While the Company has been preparing for the execution of the Business Acquisition, the Company
recently received a request from DENSO to terminate the business transfer agreement, citing recent changes
in the market environment surrounding DENSO and a review of its strategic policies regarding the Target
Businesses.
In order to fulfill its responsibility for supplying internal combustion engine-related products, the Company
engaged in continuous and sincere discussions to successfully complete the Business Acquisition. However,
prolonged negotiations and an extended acquisition process would not only increase uncertainty regarding
the Company's future business outlook, but also affect the formulation of integration plans required for a
smooth business transfer and synergy creation. Furthermore, from the perspective of resource allocation and
capital efficiency, the Company concluded that proceeding with the Business Acquisition is no longer
appropriate considering factors such as the reduction in anticipated acquisition benefits due to delays in
execution. Accordingly, the Company decided to accept the request from DENSO and terminate the
business transfer agreement.
2. Future Outlook
The earnings forecast for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2027, announced on April 30, 2026, incorporated
preparation costs for executing the Business Acquisition; however, the impact of this cancellation is
immaterial.
Please note that the "Mid-term Management Plan 2030," announced in November 2025, includes the
impact of this Business Acquisition. Regarding the potential review of the Mid-term Management Plan in
light of the cancellation, while no material changes to the numerical targets are anticipated under current
circumstances, the Company will re-examine its business plan, including capital allocation for growth
investments, and announce any updates in due course via its website (IR section) and other disclosure
channels.

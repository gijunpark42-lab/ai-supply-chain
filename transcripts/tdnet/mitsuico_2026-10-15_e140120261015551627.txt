Update on the Previously Announced Non-binding Proposal with Penske Corporation to Acquire Free-
float Shares of Penske Automotive Group, Inc.
Mitsui & Co., Ltd. (head office: Tokyo; President and CEO: Kenichi Hori) and its wholly owned subsidiary,
Mitsui & Co. (U.S.A.), Inc. (together, “Mitsui”), announced that Mitsui and Penske Corporation (“PC”), the latter
acting on behalf of itself and its wholly owned subsidiary, Penske Automotive Holdings Corp. (collectively, the
“PC-Mitsui Investors”), submitted a non-binding proposal (the “Proposal”) to acquire all of the free-float shares
of common stock of Penske Automotive Group, Inc. (NYSE: PAG) (“PAG”) on July 22, 2026 (US time).
With respect to the Proposal, following the recommendation of a special committee comprised of independent
and disinterested members of PAG’s Board of Directors, on October 14, 2026 (US time) PAG’s Board of
Directors (with directors affiliated with the PC-Mitsui Investors recusing themselves from the deliberations and
vote), approved the going-private merger involving PAG (the “Merger”). Today, Mitsui made a decision to
acquire, together with PC, all of the free-float shares of PAG. Accordingly, Motown Acquisition Corp. (“Buyer”),
a Delaware corporation which was established for the purpose of this transaction by PC and Mitsui & Co.,
Ltd., and PAG entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger (the “Merger Agreement”). In connection with
the acquisition of the shares to be acquired for cash in the Merger, Mitsui & Co., Ltd. and PC have committed
to provide equity financing to Buyer of up to US$1.6 billion (Approx. 240 billion yen) and US$600 million
(Approx. 90 billion yen), respectively. Following the completion of the Merger, Mitsui's and PC's ownership
interests in PAG are expected to increase from approximately 20.3% to approximately 36% and from
approximately 52.1% to approximately 64%, respectively.
This transaction was not included in the Medium-term Management Plan 2029 announced in May 2026. The
transaction is currently expected to be completed in the last quarter of FY March 2027, subject to receipt of
required approvals and satisfaction of other closing conditions. The impact on Mitsui's consolidated financial
results for FY March 2027 is expected to be limited. From FY March 2028 onward, Mitsui expects additional
earnings contributions beyond the assumptions of Medium-term Management Plan 2029, reflecting the
expected increase in our ownership interest.
1. Objective of the transaction
PAG is a diversified international transportation services company and one of the world’s premier
automotive and commercial truck retailers. PAG operates dealerships in the United States, the United
Kingdom, Canada, Germany, Italy, Australia and Japan and is one of the largest retailers of commercial
trucks in North America for Freightliner. PAG also distributes and retails commercial vehicles, diesel and
==== page 2 ====
gas engines, power systems and related parts and service principally in Australia and New Zealand.
Mitsui has supported PAG’s growth strategy since its initial investment in PAG in 2001, and over that time
PAG has succeeded in diversifying its business domains beyond passenger vehicle retail.
The automotive industry is experiencing a transformative period due to technological advancements. To
appropriately respond to these changes, PC and Mitsui aim to sustain current operational excellence
while innovating and growing PAG’s business. Mitsui will support PAG's business development and
create collaborative projects by leveraging its comprehensive capabilities and network in cross-functional
business domains.
2. Outline of the Merger
(1) Merger structure
The Merger will be implemented through the Merger of Buyer, a newly formed Delaware corporation
owned by PC and Mitsui & Co., Ltd. and established for purposes of the transaction, with and into
PAG, with PAG continuing as the surviving corporation. Pursuant to the Merger Agreement, shares
of PAG common stock held by the PC-Mitsui Investors will remain outstanding, while other
outstanding shares of PAG common stock (other than certain shares to be cancelled and shares in
respect of which appraisal rights have been properly exercised) will be converted into the right to
receive cash consideration as described below. Shares of Buyer will be converted into newly issued
shares of PAG common stock at the effective time of the Merger. Following completion, PAG will be
privately held by the PC-Mitsui Investors. This transaction is subject to customary closing conditions
specified in the Merger Agreement, including (a) approval at a shareholders’ meeting convened by
PAG by (i) the holders of a majority of the outstanding shares of PAG common stock entitled to vote
thereon and (ii) a majority of the votes cast by disinterested stockholders and (b) receipt of required
regulatory approvals. Completion of the Merger is not subject to a financing condition.

(3) Merger consideration
US$212 per share of PAG common stock entitled to receive cash consideration pursuant to the
Merger Agreement.
The merger consideration implies an aggregate equity value for PAG of approximately US$13.9 billion
(Approx. 2.1 trillion yen), calculated using the number of fully diluted shares outstanding of PAG
common stock as of September 30, 2026.
(4) Estimated number of shares of PAG to receive merger consideration
Approximately 18,285,048 shares of PAG common stock, based on the diluted outstanding share
count of 65,788,374 as of September 30, 2026, less the 34,181,121 shares held by PC and its
subsidiary and less the 13,322,205 shares held by Mitsui. The actual number of shares (or
equivalent) entitled to receive cash consideration will be determined in accordance with the Merger
Agreement at the effective time of the Merger, including with respect to the treatment of existing
awards under PAG’s equity plan.
(5) Total estimated transaction cost for the PC-Mitsui Investors
Approximately US$3.9 billion (Approx. 585 billion yen) (US$212 per PAG share ×18,285,048 PAG
shares (or equivalent) based on the estimated share count described in paragraph (4) above and
excluding amounts in respect of Company Performance Awards (defined in the Merger Agreement),
transaction fees, financing costs and other expenses).
(6) Source of funding
To finance the transaction Mitsui & Co., Ltd. and PC have entered into separate equity commitment

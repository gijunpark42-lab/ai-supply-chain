October 9, 2026
To whom it may concern:
Company Name: KJ005 Co., Ltd.

Notice Regarding Progress Towards Implementation of a Tender Offer for the Shares of Taiyo Holdings Co., Ltd.
(Stock Code: 4626)
KJ005 Co., Ltd. (“Tender Offeror”) had announced in the press release titled “Notice Regarding Plans to Commence a
Tender Offer for the Shares of Taiyo Holdings Co., Ltd. (Stock Code: 4626)” dated March 31, 2026 (the “March 31, 2026
Press Release”) that, with respect to a tender offer (the “Tender Offer”) for the common shares of Taiyo Holdings Co., Ltd.
(Stock Code: 4626, listed on the Prime Market of the Tokyo Stock Exchange, Inc.; the “Target”), subject to the satisfaction
or waiver by Tender Offeror of all conditions, including the completion of the procedures and steps required under (i)
domestic and foreign competition laws (in Japan, China, Taiwan, Germany , South Korea, Spain, Israel, Tunisia, and Vietnam)
and (ii) domestic and foreign laws and regulations regulating investments (in Japan and the United States), Tender Offeror
planned to promptly commence the Tender Offer, with the commencement aimed at around early October 2026.
Tender Offeror has been conducting the procedures and steps required under domestic and foreign competition laws and
domestic and foreign laws and regulations regulating investments for the purpose of implementing the Tender Offer (Note),
and the procedures and steps required under competition laws in Japan, Taiwan, Germany, Tunisia, and Vietnam and the
procedures and steps required under laws and regulations regulating investments in the United States have been completed
as of today . However, as of today , the procedures and steps required under competition laws in China and the procedures and
steps required under laws and regulations regulating investments in Japan have not yet been completed.
(Note) The March 31, 2026 Press Release stated that it was believed that advance procedures would be needed under
competition laws in Spain, Israel, and South Korea. However, after the announcement date of the March 31, 2026
Press Release, based on discussions with local law firms, Tender Offeror confirmed that it would not be necessary
to conduct procedures under competition laws in Spain and Israel, and it would be necessary to take post- closing
procedures, rather than advance procedures, under competition laws in Korea. Therefore, Tender Offeror has not
conducted advance procedures under competition laws in those countries.
Tender Offeror will commence the Tender Offer once those procedures and steps are completed or are reasonably expected
to be completed, and the conditions for commencing the Tender Offer are satisfied. As of today, Tender Offeror aims to
complete those procedures and steps in November 2026 and to commence the Tender Offer around late November 2026.
If the conditions for commencing the Tender Offer are satisfied and the Tender Offer is to be commenced, or if there is any
change in the anticipated timing of the commencement of the Tender Offer, Tender Offeror will promptly make an
announcement to that effect.

Notice Regarding the Mutual Termination of the Basic Memorandum of Understanding for Discussions
Toward the Business Integration Between Our Subsidiary and Kanadevia Corporation
With respect to the “Notice Concerning Commencement of Discussions Toward a Business Integration
between Our Subsidiary and Kanadevia Corporation,” announced on February 5, 2026, we hereby announce
that our subsidiary, Nippon Steel Engineering Co., Ltd., and Kanadevia Corporation resolved, at their
respective Board of Directors meetings held today, to terminate by mutual agreement the “Basic Memorandum
of Understanding on Discussions Toward the Business Integration” entered into between the two companies on
February 5, 2026, as set forth in the attached document. Accordingly, the two companies have ceased all
discussions and deliberations regarding the business integration.
This matter will have no impact on our consolidated financial results.

Notice Regarding the Mutual Termination of
the Basic Memorandum of Understanding for Discussions toward the Business Integration
between Kanadevia Corporation and Nippon Steel Engineering Co., Ltd.
Kanadevia Corporation and Nippon Steel Engineering Co., Ltd. (hereinafter collectively referred to as the
"Companies") hereby announce that the Companies resolved, at their respective Board of Directors meetings held
today, to terminate by mutual agreement the " Basic Memorandum of Understanding on Discussions toward the
Business Integration" (hereinafter referred to as the "MOU") entered into between the Companies on February 5,
2026, as described below.
1. Reasons for the Termination of the MOU
As announced in "Notice Concerning Commencement of Discussions Toward a Business Integration
Between Kanadevia Corporation and Nippon Steel Engineering Co., Ltd." dated February 5, 2026, the
Companies have engaged in sincere and earnest discussions to date  based on the belief that the integration
of the Companies and the creation of a new corporate group with an industry-leading management foundation
and advanced technological capabilities would contribute to enhancing corporate value and serving the
interests of stakeholders. However, the Companies have come to recognize that it would be difficult to reach
an agreement on the terms and conditions regarding the business integration, and accordingly, the Companies
resolved, at their respective Board of Directors meetings held today , to terminate the MOU by mutual
agreement. Consequently, the Companies will cease all discussions and deliberations regarding the business
integration.
2. Impact on Financial Results
The termination of the MOU and the cessation of discussions and deliberations regarding the business
integration will have no impact on the consolidated financial results of Kanadevia Corporation.

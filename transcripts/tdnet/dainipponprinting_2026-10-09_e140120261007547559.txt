Notification of the Absorption-type Merger (Simple Merger) of
a Wholly Owned Subsidiary
Dai Nippon Printing Co., Ltd. (“the Company”) hereby announces that it resolved, at its Board of
Directors meeting held today, to carry out an absorption-type merger (the "Merger") of
its wholly owned subsidiary DNP Digital Solutions Co., Ltd. ("DNP Digital Solutions") effective
April 1, 2027. The Company provides details below.
Because the merger is a simple, absorption-type merger targeting a wholly owned subsidiary
of the Company, some disclosure items and details have been omitted from this announcement.
1. Purpose of the Merger
In light of changes in the business environment resulting from factors including advances in
digital technology and the rapid spread of generative AI, the DNP Group is working to create
new value that makes use of digital technology, and to further strengthen the competitiveness
of its businesses.
DNP Digital Solutions handles the planning, design, development, maintenance, and
operational management of a wide range of information systems. As such, it has provided
technology-related support for business growth and digital transformation, not only for DNP’s
Smart Communication segment, but for the overall DNP Group.
The Company decided to implement the Merger based on its judgment that by integrating
its business divisions with its system development and operations division, it will be able to
more closely align business strategies with digital technologies, accelerate decision-making,
==== page 2 ====
optimize allocation of human resources, technologies and know-how, and transform
development and operational processes by using AI, thereby allowing DNP to enhance the
value it provides to customers and to boost the DNP Group’s business competitiveness.
2. Main points of the Merger
(1) Schedule for the Merger
Merger agreement approved by Board of Directors  October 9, 2026
(Scheduled) Merger agreement signed    February 18, 2027
(Scheduled) Effective date for merger    April 1, 2027
Note: For DNP, the Merger meets the requirements for a simple merger as stipulated in
Article 796, Paragraph 2 of the Companies Act. Therefore, the merger can be
executed without approval of the merger agreement by resolution at a general meeting
of shareholders.
(2) Method of the Merger
An absorption-type merger will be implemented, with DNP as the surviving company. DNP
Digital Solutions will be dissolved.
(3) Details of allocation related to the Merger
Since DNP Digital Solutions is a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, no shares will be
issued and no money or other assets will be delivered in connection with the Merger.
(4) Stock acquisition rights and bonds with stock acquisition rights associated with the Merger
Not applicable

Reason for the revision
Consolidated net sales are expected to exceed the previous forecast, reflecting strong semiconductor demand. In
addition, due to the increase in profit accompanying higher sales, operating profit, ordinary profit, and profit
attributable to owners of parent are all expected to exceed the previous forecasts.
Note: The above forecasts have been prepared based on information available as of the date of this announcement.
Actual results may differ from the forecast figures due to various factors that may arise in the future.

Reason for the revision
The Company had planned a year-end dividend of ¥20 per share for the fiscal year ending February 28, 2027, as
announced on April 9, 2026. However, as the Company now expects its financial results to exceed the previous forecast
as described in “1. Revision to Full-Year Consolidated Financial Forecast” above, the Company has decided to increase
the dividend by ¥8 and revise the year-end dividend to ¥28 per share.
The matter is scheduled to be submitted for approval at the 42nd Ordinary General Meeting of Shareholders to be
held in May 2027.

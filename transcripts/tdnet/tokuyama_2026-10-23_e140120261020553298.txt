Notice concerning Absorption-type Merger (Simplified Merger) of  Consolidated Subsidiary
With a resolution at a meeting of  Board of  Directors  held on October  23, 2026, Tokuyama Corporation
(hereinafter referred to as “ the Company,” “Tokuyama” or “we”) hereby announces that the  Company will
absorb Super Nano Design Co., Ltd (hereinafter referred to as “Super Nano Design”), the Company’s wholly
owned subsidiary, effective February 1, 2027.
Since the merger is a simplified merger of  a wholly owned subsidiary, certain disclosure items and details are
omitted.
1. Purpose of  Merger
Super Nano Design engages in the development of  synthesis recipes and provides custom synthesis services
for fine-particle materials.
In this instance, the decision has been made to complete an absorption merger with the aim of  improving
organizational efficiency.
2. Outline of Merger
(1) Schedule of Merger
･
Resolution of  the Company’s Board of  Directors relating to the merger October 23, 2026
･ Conclusion of merger agreement October 23, 2026 (planned)
･ Date of merger (effective date) February 1, 2027 (planned)
(Note) The merger is, for the Company, a simplified merger under the provisions of Paragraph 2 of Article
796 of the Company Act. Therefore, the Company will not hold a general meeting of shareholders to
carry out the merger.
(2) Method of Merger
It is an absorption-type merger in which the Company is the surviving company, and Super Nano Design will
be dissolved.

● Operating environment
• The semiconductor market continued to grow at a high rate, driven mainly
by demand for AI applications. The memory market continued to face tight
supply- and -demand situation against the backdrop of demand for AI data
centers, with contract prices for both DRAM and NAND on an upward trend.
• In display-related areas, a major Taiwanese manufacturer shifted its focus to
high-value-added panel fields. Japanese manufacturers are also withdrawing
from the large-sized LCD panel field and progressing restructuring.
● Market risks
• Continuation and escalation of the U.S.-China economic confrontation.
• Slowing demand for end products due to soaring memory prices.
• Progress in in-house production of semiconductors in China, fluctuations in
raw material and energy prices, and disruption in logistics.
● OOC
• Sales of our mainstay ArF resist raw materials and raw materials for cutting-
edge EUV resists increased significantly, and Group-wide sales also
increased significantly.
• Sales in the display materials group decreased. Sales of other groups
remained strong.

● Operating environment
• In the domestic cosmetics market, consumers’ tendency to be thrifty-minded
became more pronounced due to prolonged high prices, and sales volume
was sluggish.
• While inbound demand drove total consumer spending in Japan to a record
high, a structural shift from dependence on Chinese visitors progressed.
• Demand for related materials increased, supported by the strong
semiconductor market.
● Market risks
• For sales of cosmetics materials, risks of intensifying competition due to
improved quality and strengthened technology of local manufacturers in
China over the medium to long term, in addition to delayed market recovery
in China.
• Risks of price competition and inventory adjustment due to thrifty-
mindedness in the domestic cosmetics market and the expansion of discount
retail formats.
● OOC
• Sales in the cosmetics materials group remained strong, while sales in the
functional materials group increased significantly.
• Sales of high-purity specialty solvents at subsidiaries also remained strong.

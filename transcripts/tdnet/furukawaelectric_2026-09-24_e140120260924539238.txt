Notice Regarding Sale of Investment Securities, Expected
Recognition of Extraordinary Income, and Revision to
Consolidated Financial Forecasts for the First-half (Interim Period)
of the Fiscal Year Ending March 31, 2027
Furukawa Electric Co., Ltd. (the “Company”) hereby announces that it expects to recognize a
gain on sale of investment securities in the second quarter of the fiscal year ending March 31,
2027, as a result of the sale of a portion of its investment securities holdings. The Company has
also revised its consolidated financial forecasts for the first-half (interim period) of the fiscal year
ending March 31, 2027, which were announced on August 6, 2026, as follows.
1. Expected recognition of gain on sale of investment securities
The Company expects to recognize a gain on sale of investment securities as extraordinary
income in connection with the sale of a portion of its listed securities holdings.
2. Details of the sale of investment securities
(1) Reason for sale: Reduction of cross-shareholdings and securing funds for growth
investments
(2) Security to be sold: One listed security (UACJ Corporation) held by the Company
(3) Scheduled sale date: September 25, 2026 (planned)
(4) Sale amount (estimate): Approximately JPY 28.6 billion
(5) Gain on sale of investment securities (estimate): Approximately JPY 22.0 billion

(2) Reason for the revision
The gain on sale of investment securities described in Item 2(5) above was originally expected
to be recognized in or after the third quarter of the fiscal year ending March 31, 2027. However,
after comprehensively considering market conditions and share price trends, the Company
revised the timing of the sale, and the gain on sale of investment securities is now expected to
be recognized in the second quarter of the fiscal year ending March 31, 2027. Accordingly, the
Company has revised its consolidated financial forecasts for the first-half (interim period) of the
fiscal year ending March 31, 2027. There is no change to the full-year consolidated financial
forecasts.

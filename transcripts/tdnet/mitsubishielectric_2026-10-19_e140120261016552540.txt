Executive Summary
1. Executive Summary
 Mitsubishi Electric has agreed to transfer 50% of its shares in Mitsubishi Electric Mobility, which handles Mitsubishi
Electric’s automotive equipment business, to Lin Yin International Investment Co., Ltd., Hon Hai Group’s
investment management company (hereinafter referred to as Lin Yin).
 Mitsubishi Electric Mobility will contribute to developing EV platforms through three domains, where Mitsubishi
Electric Mobility can leverage its strengths.
 Mitsubishi Electric Mobility is expected to grow into a 1 trillion-yen company by strengthening and expanding its
key business through the strategic alliance with Hon Hai.
 The strategic alliance will strengthen Japan’s industrial base and support the sustainable growth of the
domestic automotive industry by providing high-quality EV platforms made in/by Japan.

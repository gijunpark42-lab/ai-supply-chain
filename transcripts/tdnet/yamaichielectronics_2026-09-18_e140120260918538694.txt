Notice Regarding Construction of Factory 4 in the Philippines
YAMAICHI ELECTRONICS CO., LTD. (the "Company") hereby announces that, at a meeting of
the Board of Directors held on September 18, 2026, the Board resolved to construct Factory 4
at Pricon Microelectronics, Inc. (Philippines; hereinafter, "Pricon Microelectronics"), a
consolidated subsidiary of the Company, as described below.
1. Purpose of Construction of Factory 4
The Yamaichi Electronics Group is expanding its global production capacity and strengthening
its stable supply system in anticipation of medium- to long-term demand growth in markets
including semiconductors, automotive, communications (including AI servers), and industrial
equipment.
At Pricon Microelectronics, securing additional production space has also become necessary to
accommodate future business expansion. Accordingly, the Company has decided to construct a
new Factory 4.
2. Overview of Factory 4
Item Details
Location Light Industry & Science Park IV (LISP IV),
Malvar, Batangas, Philippines
Structure Three-story building
Total floor area Approx. 30,000 m²
Main use Assembly of semiconductor test sockets, connectors, and other
products
Total investment Approx. JPY 6.5 billion
Start of construction February 2027 (planned)
Building completion and handover End of September 2028 (planned)
Start of operations Second half of FY2028 (planned)
Total investment includes the cost of the building, fixtures and equipment, and other expenses, but
excludes costs related to the relocation of existing factories and similar expenses.
3. Key Features of Factory 4
• Improved quality and safety through a layout optimized for production flow
• Further improvements in quality, cost, and delivery (QCD) through expanded in -house
production of components
• Improved productivity through equipment automation and robotics
• Use of renewable energy through the installation of solar power generation systems

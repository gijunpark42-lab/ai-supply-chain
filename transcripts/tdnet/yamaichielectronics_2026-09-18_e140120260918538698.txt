Yamaichi Electronics Co., Ltd. (the “Company”) hereby announces that, at its Board of
Directors meeting held today, it resolved to transfer a portion of the shares that the Company
holds in its consolidated subsidiary, Koshin Kogaku Co., Ltd. (“KKC”), to VS Technology
Corporation (“VST”), and entered into a share transfer agreement to that effect on the same
date, as set forth below.
KKC will continue to be a consolidated subsidiary of the Company following the transfer, and
there will be no change to the scope of consolidation.
1. Reason for the Share Transfer
Under its medium-term management plan for the fiscal years ending March 2027 through March 2029,
the Company's group has positioned the strengthening of its optical-related business — a reportable
segment to which KKC belongs — as a key priority, with a particular focus on expanding KKC's sales
platform in overseas markets.
KKC and VST already have a business relationship, with KKC's products used in filter s of VST's
machine vision lenses. This transfer will combine KKC's manufactur ing capabilities with VST's
established overseas sales network to expand sales of existing products and support joint development of
new products and customers, while the Company continues to hold 85% of KKC's shares and remains
involved in its management as a consolidated subsidiary.

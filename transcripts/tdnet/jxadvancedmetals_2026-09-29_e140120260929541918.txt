Notice Regarding Cooperation and Investment Agreement with Tronox to Conduct a Joint Study
for Entry into the Rare Earth Refining Business
JX Advanced Metals Corporation (President: Hayashi Yoichi; hereinafter the “Company”) hereby announces
that it has resolved today to commence a joint study with Tronox Holdings plc (Headquarters: Connecticut,
U.S.; Chief Executive Officer: John D. Romano; hereinafter “Tronox”) to evaluate the feasibility of
participating in Tronox’s planned rare earth business.
Based on the current concept, t hrough 2027, the two companies will conduct a feasibility assessment of the
separation, leaching, and refining of rare earth mineral concentrates and rare earth elements, with in Australia
and the United States among candidate  locations. The total cost of the joint study is expected to be
approximately USD 32 million, or approximately JPY 5.0 billion1), with each company expected to bear 50%
of the cost.
1. Background and Purpose
Rare earths are critical resources essential to a wide range of advanced industries, including semiconductors,
telecommunications equipment, and high-performance magnets. Their importance is expected to grow further
with advances in artificial intelligence, data centers, next-generation telecommunications, electrification, and
other fields. At the same time, the rare earth supply chain, including not only mining but also the extraction of
rare earth components from ores and their separation and refining into individua l elements, remains highly
dependent on certain countries and regions.
Under the “JX Advanced Metals Group Long- Term Vision 2040,” the JX Advanced Metals Group has
positioned the advanced materials sector, centered on semiconductor materials and information and
telecommunications materials, as a core pillar of its growth strategy. Rare earth elements are essential to the
manufacture and development of the Group’s advanced materials. The Group has therefore been working to
strengthen the rare earth supply chain, including through initiatives in the field of resource development. The
joint study with Tronox represents an effort to expand these initiatives into the processing stage and develop
them into an integrated approach linking raw materials to finished products.
Tronox is one of the world’s largest producers of titanium dioxide pigment. It is a vertically integrated chemical
company with operations spanning mineral resource extraction, mineral separation, and finished- product
manufacturing. Tronox owns mines and p roduction facilities in Australia, South Africa, the United States,
Europe, the Middle East, and other regions, and handles mineral resources containing rare earth elements
through its existing operations. Tronox is exploring an expansion into the rare earth business by leveraging its
existing business platform in Australia, including its mineral sands mines and mineral separation facilities.
The joint study will evaluate the technical feasibility, economic viability, proposed business structure, and
other aspects of a business concept involving the leaching, separation, and refining of rare earth and individual
elements in Australia, the United States and other locations, using rare earth mineral s sourced from Tronox
and third parties. At this stage, the concept contemplates total production of up to approximately 10,000 tonnes
per annum on a total rare earth oxide basis. The Company will leverage the technologies and expertise it has

2. Overview of the Joint Study
Under this agreement, the two companies will conduct a feasibility assessment of the leaching, separation, and
refining of rare earth and individual elements, with Australia, the United States, and other locations under
consideration, targeting completion by the end of 2027. The principal roles of each company in the joint study
are as follows:
-JX Advanced Metals
JX Advanced Metals will provide its technologies and expertise in nonferrous metals smelting, recycling, and
high-purity refining, as well as its expertise in the advanced materials sector.
-Tronox
Tronox will provide mineral resources containing rare earths, technologies and expertise in mining operations
and mineral separation, expertise in the management of raw materials and products containing radioactive
materials, and business platforms and infrastructure at candidate locations in Australia, the United States, and
other locations.

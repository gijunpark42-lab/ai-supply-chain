Completion of Execution of Partial Spin-off of Crasus Chemical (Petrochemical Business)
Resonac Holdings Corporation (the “Company”) today announced that the distribution of dividends in kind
representing the partial spin -off (the “Spin -off”) of Crasus Chemical Inc. (“Crasus  Chemical”), a wholly
owned subsidiary of the Company engaged in the petrochemical business, has become effective, and the
execution of the Spin-off has been completed.
As a result of the execution of the Spin -off, the Company now hold s 18.57% of the shares of common
stock of Crasus Chemical (“Crasus Chemical Shares”). Accordingly, Crasus  Chemical is no longer a
consolidated subsidiary of the Company and is no longer accounted for using the equity method, as the
Company is not expected to have significant influence over Crasus Chemical.

2. Proportion of Distributed Assets
As a result of the execution of the Spin-off, the proportion of distributed assets has been finalized as
follows, unchanged from the final estimate disclosed on September 28, 2026:
Proportion of distributed assets (Final): “0.085”
The proportion of distributed assets is a factor necessary for calculating, for Japanese tax purposes,
the respective tax bases of the Company’s common stock immediately after the Spin -off and the
Crasus Chemical Shares distributed as an in -kind dividend in connection with the Spin -off. For the
final confirmed proportion, please refer to the notice scheduled to be sent in early November 2026 to
the Company’s shareholders of record as of September 30, 2026, the record date for the in -kind
dividend.
3. Impact of the Spin-off on the Company's Consolidated Financial Results
Following the effectiveness of the in-kind dividend in connection with the Spin -off, the Company will
account for the loss of control over the Crasus Chemical segment in accordance with IFRS 10
“Consolidated Financial Statements.”
The financial impact of the Deconsolidation on the Company’s consolidated financial results is
currently under review. The Company will promptly disclose any matters requiring disclosure once
they have been determined.
For details of the accounting treatment in connection with the Spin-off, please refer to “Resolution for
Execution of Partial Spin-off of Crasus Chemical (Petrochemical Business)” dated August 25, 2026.

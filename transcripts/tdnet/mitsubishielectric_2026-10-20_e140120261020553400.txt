Mitsubishi Electric to Form Joint Venture with ABB for Low-Voltage Power
Distribution Business
TOKYO, October 20, 2026 – Mitsubishi Electric Corporation (TOKYO: 6503) announced today that it has
entered into joint venture agreement with a subsidiary of Switzerland-based ABB Ltd. (ABB). The company
will establish a wholly owned preparatory company (the “New Company”) and to implement an absorption-
type company split (the “Company Split”) to further grow and strengthen the competitiveness of power
distribution b usiness, including low -voltage circuit breakers, electromagnetic switches, measurement and
control equipment, uninterruptible power supply (UPS), etc., (the “Target Business”). Following the effective
date of the Company Split, 51% of the shares of the New Company will be transferred to ABB Corporation, a
Japanese subsidiary of ABB (the “Share Transfer”). Through a series of these transactions (the “Transactions”),
the New Company will become a joint venture company owned 51% by ABB Corporation and 49% by
Mitsubishi Electric.
Under Company Split, the New Company will succeed to all rights and obligations of Mitsubishi Electric
relating to the Target Business, including shares held by certain Mitsubishi Electric subsidiaries engaged in
the Target Business. In conjunction with the  Company Split, Mitsubishi Electric also plans to transfer the
relevant businesses currently operated by its subsidiaries to the New Company.
In conjunction with this share transfer, related sales operations currently conducted by overseas sales
subsidiaries are expected to be transferred to ABB’s local entities in the respective regions.
Since the New Company, a wholly owned subsidiary of Mitsubishi Electric, will be the succeeding company
in the Company Split, certain disclosure items and details have been omitted from this disclosure.
1. Summary of the Transactions
(1) Purpose of Transactions
The Target Business encompasses low-voltage circuit breakers, electromagnetic switches, measurement and
control equipment, and UPS for general industrial use  of 5kV A or less, and other products . Leveraging
technologies and sales networks developed over many years, the Target Business supports the stable supply
of electricity and energy to factories, buildings and infrastructure in Japan and overseas. In particular, it has
established a strong market position in Japan.

While stable demand is expected in the Japanese market, the demand for these products is expected to
increase further overseas as electrification expands, accompanied by intensifying competition. Under these
circumstances, Mitsubishi Electric has determined that teaming up with an appropriate strategic partner and
jointly managing the New Company through the Share Transfer will enable the Target Business to strengthen
its overall global competitiveness, including its product capabilities and ability to support solutions.
ABB holds a leading position in the power distribution and control field, and develops power distribution
and control solutions by leveraging its technologies and global sales network developed over many years.
By combining the technologies, sales networks, and other resources of both companies, Mitsubishi Electric
aims to further enhance its products and services and strengthen its global business development, as well as
improve the value provided to customers and achieve sustainable medium- to long-term business growth.
As a result of the Transaction s, the N ew Company will succeed to Mitsubishi Electric’s development,
manufacturing, sales, and services relat ing to low -voltage circuit breakers, electromagnetic switches,
measurement and control equipment, and UPS for general industry use of 5kV A or less. The New Company
will also succeed to sales businesses of certain high-voltage power distribution control equipment, relevant
service functions, and the development and manufacturing of smart meters. Mitsubishi Electric will take
appropriate measures to ensure a smooth transition of the businesses and maintain a continuous supply of
products to customers and suppliers in Japan and overseas.
Takayuki Tsuzuki, Mitsubishi Electric Corporation’s Senior Executive Officer, Group President, Factory
Automation Systems, said: “Mitsubishi Electric’s power distribution business has continued to improve its
technological and quality capabilities over the past 100 years. Currently, Mitsubishi Electric is well
positioned in many product fields in Japan, supporting a stable supply of electricity and energy essential to
society. Through this joint venture with ABB, Mitsubishi Electric will combine ABB’s global business
foundation with the technologies, quality, customer base and sales channels it has cultivated over many
years. By doing so, we aim to provide even higher value to our customers and further grow and develop our
power distribution product business.”
Giampiero Frisio, President of ABB’s Electrification Business Area, said: “Japan is one of the world’s most
important electrification markets, where Mitsubishi Electric’s low-voltage power distribution division have
served customers and built deep expertise over many decades. This joint venture brings together that strong
local foundation with ABB’s leading technologies and global reach, significantly strengthening our position
in Japan and across Asia-Pacific. For customers and employees, it means continuity, greater opportunities,
and access to a broader portfolio of low -voltage solutions. For ABB, this partnership brings together the
best of both companies in a long-term commitment to growth in the region.”

(1) Outline of Company Split
(a) Schedule of Company Split
Please refer to “ 1. Summary of the Transactions (2) Schedule for the establishment of New Company,

(b) Method of Company Split
This is an absorption-type company split in which Mitsubishi Electric is the splitting company and the
New Company is the succeeding company.
(c) Details of allotment in Company Split
As the Company Split is to be carried out by Mitsubishi Electric  with its wholly owned subsidiary, no
shares or other money will be delivered from the New Company to Mitsubishi Electric.
(d) Treatment of share options and bonds with share options in Company Split
Not applicable.
(e) Change in capital due to Company Split
There will be no change in Mitsubishi Electric’s capital due to the Company Split.
(f) Rights and obligations to be succeeded by succeeding company
The New Company will succeed to the assets, liabilities, contractual status, intellectual property rights,
and other rights and obligations of Mitsubishi Electric relating to the Target Business , to the extent
stipulated in the a bsorption-type company split agreement. Rights and obligations shared with other
businesses will not be transferred to the New Company or, depending on the circumstances, will be
handled separately pursuant to individual agreements.
(g) Prospect of performance of obligations
Mitsubishi Electric and the New Company are not expected to have any problem with the prospect of
performance of obligations after the effective date of the Company Split.

4. Future Outlook
(1) Future Outlook for New Company
After the Transactions, the New Company is expected to become a consolidated subsidiary of ABB and an
equity-method affiliate of Mitsubishi Electric. The Board of Directors of the New Company will consist of
a total of seven (7) members, including five (5) directors nominated by ABB and two (2) directors nominated
by Mitsubishi Electric. The New Company aims to enhance the competitiveness and growth of the Target
Business by leveraging Mitsubishi Electric’s customer, distribution, technology, and quality infrastructure
and ABB’s global technology, products, and sales network. The name, representative, capital, location, and
other details of the New Company after the Transactions will be announced as soon as they are finalized.
(2) Forecast of Impact on Mitsubishi Electric’s Financial Results
Mitsubishi Electric’s consolidated business forecast for the fiscal year ending in March 2027 announced on
July 31, 2026 does not include the impact of the Transactions . If any matters requiring disclosure arise in
the future, Mitsubishi Electric will promptly make an announcement. In connection with this share transfer,
the company expects to record an approximately 900 billion yen gain arising from the transfer under “Other
income (expenses)” in its consolidated financial results for the fiscal year ending on March 31, 2028.

Notice of Simplified Absorption-type Company Split for Transfer of
Automotive Equipment Business and Execution of Share Transfer
Agreement to Transfer 50% of Mitsubishi Electric Mobility’s Shares
TOKYO, October 16, 2026 – Mitsubishi Electric Corporation (Mitsubishi Electric) (TOKYO: 6503) announced
today that, as disclosed in the press release “Mitsubishi Electric Considering Alliance with Hon Hai Precision
Industry for Joint Operation of Automotive Equipment Business” dated April 24, 2026, Mitsubishi Electric signed
a memorandum of understanding (MoU) with Hon Hai Precision Industry Co., Ltd.(Hon Hai Precision
Industry)based in New Taipei City, to commence discussions about forming a strategic alliance (the “Alliance”)
to jointly operate Mitsubishi Electric’s automotive equipment business (“Target Business”), based on which the
companies held discussions leading to this agreement.
To implement the Alliance, Mitsubishi Electric will carry out a series of transactions (the “Transaction”),
including: (i) the succession by Mitsubishi Electric Mobility Corporation (Mitsubishi Electric Mobility), which
operates the Target Business, of certain rights and obligations held by Mitsubishi Electric concerning the Target
Business, through an absorption-type company split (“Company Split”); (ii) the transfer of 50% of the issued
shares of Mitsubishi Electric Mobility (“Share Transfer”), a subsidiary of Mitsubishi Electric that operates the
Target Business, to Lin Yin International Investment Co., Ltd. (Lin Yin), an investment management company
of Hon Hai Precision Industry; and (iii) the transfer or other reorganization of the rights and obligations relating
to the Target Business held by Mitsubishi Electric’s group companies in Japan and overseas to Mitsubishi
Electric Mobility or its subsidiaries (the “Intra-group Reorganization”).
The transfer price for the Share Transfer is expected to be JPY 115.0 billion. Under the Share Transfer
Agreement, the parties agreed on an enterprise value of JPY 320.0 billion for the Target Business and, after
taking into account changes in assets following execution of the agreement, calculated and agreed on an equity
value of JPY 230.0 billion. The transfer price above represents the amount corresponding to the 50% interest
that will be transferred based on the equity value.
The Transaction is scheduled to be implemented subject to receipt of approvals from relevant authorities under
applicable competition laws in Japan and overseas, as well as satisfaction of other applicable conditions. Since
the Company Split will be a simplified absorption-type company split in which Mitsubishi Electric Mobility,
a wholly owned subsidiary of Mitsubishi Electric, will be the successor company, certain disclosure items and
details have been omitted.
Details
1. Purpose of the Transaction
The Transaction will combine Mitsubishi Electric Mobility’s technological and developmental capabilities,
particularly in electric powertrains, software and vehicle control, with Hon Hai Precision Industry’s speed,
scale and other strengths in market deployment.
By providing high-quality electric-vehicle platforms (basic structures) developed in Japan, the companies
expect to contribute to the widespread adoption and advancement of next-generation mobility. The alliance

5. Outlook
As a result of the Share Transfer, Mitsubishi Electric Mobility will cease to be a consolidated subsidiary
of Mitsubishi Electric and in turn will become an equity-method affiliate from the third quarter of the fiscal
year ending on March 31, 2028.
In connection with the Share Transfer, Mitsubishi E lectric expects to record an impairment loss of
approximately JPY 100.0 billion under “Other profit (loss)” in its consolidated financial results for the
fiscal year ending on March 31, 2027. The company is currently reviewing its full -year consolidated
earnings forecast, including other factors, for the fiscal year ending on March 31, 2027, and plans to
announce the forecast on October 30, 2026 based on the results of that review.

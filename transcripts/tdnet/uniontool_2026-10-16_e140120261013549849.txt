Notice Regarding the Construction of a New Factory Building
and Acquisition of Factory Land
UNION TOOL CO. (the “Company”) hereby announces that, at a meeting held today, its Board of Directors
resolved to construct Mitsuke No. 4 Factory. The Board also resolved to proceed with preparations to apply for
approval of a Regional Economy Advancement Project Plan under the Regional Future Investment Promotion Act,
with a view to acquiring land for the construction of a new factory in Sekihara-machi, Nagaoka-shi, Niigata
Prefecture. Details are as follows.
The Company will continue to collaborate with the local community as it strengthens its production capabilities
and seeks to enhance its corporate value over the medium to long term.
1. Purpose of New Factory Construction and Land Acquisition
These initiatives are intended to increase production capacity for cutting tools, the Company’s core products,
and establish the production and development infrastructure needed to support future business expansion.
2. Overview of the Mitsuke No. 4 Factory Construction Plan
(1) Name: Mitsuke No. 4 Factory
(2) Location: Shinko-cho, Mitsuke-shi, Niigata Prefecture (A new building will be constructed on the same site
as the existing Mitsuke Factories Nos. 1-3.)
(3) Total Floor Area: Approximately 6,600 m² (two stories above ground)
(4) Scheduled Completion: February 2028
3. Overview of the Land Acquisition and New Factory Plan in Sekihara-machi, Nagaoka-shi
(1) Location: Sekihara-machi, Nagaoka-shi, Niigata Prefecture
(2) Land Area to Be Acquired: Approximately 150,000 m²
(3) Plan Description: The Company plans to construct a new factory building to establish a new production base.
(4) Building Size and Investment Amount: To be determined. (These are to be finalized following approval
of the Regional Economy Advancement Project Plan.)
(5) Scheduled Completion: 2030
4. Future Outlook
The Company expects the impact of this matter on its financial results for the current fiscal year to be minor.
The Company will promptly disclose any additional matters requiring disclosure as they arise.

1. Overview of Results of Operations
(1) Results of Operations for the Period under Review
During the nine months ended August 31, 2026, the Japanese economy showed moderate recovery against the
backdrop of the improvement in the employment and income environment and the effects of various policies. There
were signs of a pickup in personal consumption and capital investment, and production remained generally flat. In
addition, despite the improvement in corporate earnings, the outlook remains uncertain due to the impact of the situation
in the Middle East and natural disasters, fluctuations in th e financial and capital markets, and trends in raw material
and energy prices and foreign exchange rates.
Under these circumstances, we have been working on the medium -term management plan Progress & Development
2030 (P&D 2030) covering the period from the fiscal year ended November 30, 2024 to the fiscal year ending
November 30, 2030. Based on our Group management philosophy, P&D 2030 has set a management vision of
“providing value to the global market as a leading company in specialty acrylic acid esters.” Under this vision, we will
promote sustainable management that takes ESG into account, aiming to increase corporate value and achieve
sustainable growth.
In the Chemical Products Business, we worked to improve profit ratios mainly by integrating and closing products
and improving production efficiency, and proceeded with expanding sales of environmentally friendly products, such
as those derived from biomass. In the Electronic Materials Business, as the semiconductor -related market is expected
to grow over the medium to long term against the backdrop of expanding global AI-related demand, the Group worked
to accelerate the development of cutting-edge semiconductor materials and to develop new applications for photoresist
materials. In the Specialty Chemicals Business, the Group focused on strengthening the overseas expansion of cosmetic
raw materials and expanding sales of high-purity special solvents. We are also working to strengthen our overseas sales
system by acquiring new customers and cultivating new markets overseas through our overseas bases. Furthermore, in
anticipation of future growth in the semiconductor market, we are proceeding with the construc tion plan for new
facilities for cutting -edge semiconductor materials at the Sakata Plant, thereby working to expand future production
capacity and strengthen stable supply systems. In addition, through capital and business alliance with SAMBO
CHEMICAL IND. CO., LTD., we are strengthening our development capabilities and ability to propose solutions to
customers in the semiconductor and electronic materials sectors, by mutually leveraging high- purity technology and
development and manufacturing platforms that the companies possess.
As a result, for the nine months ended August 31, 2026, the Group recorded net sales of 32,293 million yen (up 21.0%
year on year), operating profit of 7,578 million yen (up 63.6% year on year), ordinary profit of 7,893 million yen (up
62.9% year on year), and profit attributable to owners of parent of 5,325 million yen (up 55.0% year on year).
Business results by segment are explained below (excluding inter-segment transactions).
1) Chemical Products Business
In the Chemical Products Business, in the acrylic acid ester group, sales of coating materials for automotive and other
uses remained strong, while sales of adhesive materials for optical and other uses remained solid. On the other hand,
sales of materials for inks, including inks for UV inkjet use, were flat. Sales in the methacrylate ester group remained
flat. As a result, net sales were 11,058 million yen (up 11.5% year on year) and segment profit was 2,461 million yen
(up 49.4% year on year) due to the optimization of selling prices and improved product composition.

2) Electronic Materials Business
In the Electronic Materials Business, in the semiconductor materials group, sales of raw materials for ArF resists,
which are the Company’s mainstay, and sales of raw materials for cutting-edge EUV resists increased significantly, and
Group-wide sales also increased significantly. Sales in the display materials group decreased. In addition, sales of other
groups remained strong. As a result, net sales were 15,570 million yen (up 27.0% year on year) and segment profit was
3,608 million yen (up 71.2% year on year).
3) Specialty Chemicals Business
In the Specialty Chemicals Business, sales in the cosmetics materials group remained strong, while sales in the
functional materials group increased significantly. In addition, sales of high -purity specialty solvents at subsidiaries
remained strong. As a r esult, net sales were 5,663 million yen (up 25.2% year on year) and segment profit was 1,577
million yen (up 78.1% year on year).
(2) Financial Position for the Period under Review
Total assets at the end of the third quarter of the current fiscal year increased by 4,570 million yen from the end of
the previous fiscal year to 68,284 million yen. This was mainly attributable to an increase of 584 million yen in cash
and deposits, an i ncrease of 1,234 million yen in accounts receivable -  trade, and contract assets, a decrease of 1,198
million yen in property, plant and equipment, and an increase of 2,345 million yen in investment securities due to the
rise in stock prices, etc. of shares held.
Liabilities at the end of the third quarter of the current fiscal year decreased by 1,037 million yen from the end of the
previous fiscal year to 12,139 million yen. This was mainly due to a decrease of 1,208 million yen in income taxes
payable, a decrease of 355 million yen in long-term borrowings, an increase of 767 million yen in deferred tax liabilities.
Net assets at the end of the third quarter of the current fiscal year increased by 5,608 million yen from the end of the
previous fiscal year to 56,144 million yen. This was mainly due to an increase of 3,637 million yen in retained earnings
and an increase of 1,600 million yen in valuation difference on available-for-sale securities.

2. Reason for the revision
In addition to rising prices of construction materials and facilities, the Company reviewed the details of facilities to
be installed, with the aim of expanding production capacity and improving high- purity technology, based on the latest
demand simulation. As a result, the planned amount of investment is expected to exceed the amount in the initial plan.
In addition, as a result of reviewing the construction schedule in line with the revision of the details of facilities to be
installed, the Company also changed the commencement date and completion date.
3. Background and Purpose of the Investment
The semiconductor market is expected to continue to grow, driven by the advancement of AI technology and high-
performance devices. Development of materials which support miniaturization and high- density integration as well as
establishment of a stable supply system have become even more significant.
The Company, according to the medium-term management plan Progress & Development 2030 (covering the period
from the fiscal year ended November 30, 2024 to the fiscal year ending November 30, 2030), has strengthened the
development and production system of cutting -edge semiconductor materials through the construction of a medium -
scale laboratory and the operation of manufacturing facilities, in which approximately 8.0 billion yen has been invested.
The Company aims to enhance production capacity and advance high- purity technology through this new facility
construction plan, thereby expanding its market share in cutting-edge semiconductor materials and promoting business
growth. The plan also aims to ensure business continuity by enabling dual-site production at the Kanazawa and Sakata
Plants, further promoting the establishment of a stable supply system.
4. Outlook
The new facility under this investment is scheduled to be completed in 2029, and therefore its impact on the
consolidated results for the fiscal year ending November 30, 2026 will be immaterial. Should any developments
requiring disclosure arise in the future, the Company will promptly make notification.

３. Reasons for the revision
For the second quarter (interim period), consolidated results are expected to show a decline
in sales revenue, primarily because the timing of orders for large water treatment systems
projects in the United States, which had been expected to be received in the first half, is
now expected to shift to the second half. In addition, profitability is expected to fall
below the beginning of period forecast due to factors including increased costs for large
water treatment systems projects already ordered in South Korea.
For the full-year consolidat ed earnings forecast, while inquiries in each region remain
strong and progress- based sales for large water treatment systems projects in the United
States are expected to be realized in the second half, the forecasts for sales revenue and
operating profit remain unchanged from the beginning of period estimates. However, following
a review of interest paid on borrowings and other factors, operating income and net income
attributable to Parent company shareholders are expected to fall below the initial forecasts.
Should we determine that revisions to the earnings forecast are necessary depending on future
developments, we will promptly disclose them.
(Note) The above forecasts have been prepared based on information available under the current
business environment. Actual results may differ from these forecasts due to various factors
in the future.

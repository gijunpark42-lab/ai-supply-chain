Notice of the Simplified Absorption-Type Company Split of a Consolidated Subsidiary
Dai Nippon Printing Co., Ltd. (the “Company”) hereby announces that it resolved, at a meeting of
its Board of Directors held today, to succeed to the business of the sales division of DNP Planning
Network Co., Ltd. (“DNP Planning Network”), a consolidated subsidiary of the Company, by way
of a simplified absorption-type company split (“the Absorption-type Company Split”) to take effect
on April 1, 2027. The Company provides details below.
Although DNP Planning Network is a consolidated subsidiary of the Company as of today, the
Company plans to make it a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company on or before the effective
date of the Absorption-type Company Split. Based on the premise that the Absorption-type
Company Split will constitute a simplified absorption-type company split involving a wholly owned
subsidiary of the Company as of its effective date, certain disclosure items and details have been
omitted from this notice.
1.  Purpose of the Absorption-type Company Split
The DNP Group provides a wide range of services aimed at helping client companies resolve
issues related to their marketing activities, from planning and production to the execution of
various promotional measures.
In recent years, greater diversification of consumers’ values and ways of accessing
information, along with advances in digital technologies, have made the marketing issues
confronting our client companies increasingly complex. In order to respond swiftly and flexibly
to these changes in the business environment, and to further strengthen the competitiveness
of the DNP Group’s marketing-related businesses, the Company has decided to restructure its
organization.

By means of the Absorption-type Company Split, the Company will succeed to the sales
division business of DNP Planning Network, a consolidated subsidiary of the Company. On
the same date, the Company will implement an absorption-type merger in which DNP
Communication Design Co., Ltd. will survive and DNP Planning Network will be dissolved.
Through this merger, the human resources, know-how and operational infrastructure of both
companies will be utilized on an integrated basis, thereby allowing marketing measures to be
supported by more sophisticated planning and production functions while improving
operational efficiency.

5. Status of the successor company after the Absorption-type Company Split
There will be no changes to the Company’s trade name, head office location, position and name
of representative, business activities (excluding the businesses subject to the Absorption-type
Company Split), capital, or fiscal year-end as a result of the Absorption-type Company Split.
6. Future outlook
Since the Absorption-type Company Split will be implemented between the Company and DNP
Planning Network, which is scheduled to become a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company
by the effective date of the Absorption-type Company Split, the impact on the Company’s
consolidated financial results will be insignificant.

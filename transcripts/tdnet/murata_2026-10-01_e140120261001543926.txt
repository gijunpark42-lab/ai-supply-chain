M
urata Manufacturing Co., Ltd. (“the Company”) hereby announces that its Board of Directors resolved today
(October 1, 2026) to conduct an absorption-type merger (“the Merger”) with Saitama Murata Manufacturing Co.,
Ltd. (“Saitama Murata”), a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, and to close Saitama Murata’s business site,
as described below.
A
s the Merger is an absorption-type merger involving a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, certain
disclosure items and details have been omitted.
１．
Purpose
Saitama Murata primarily engages in the development of components on behalf of the Company.
The Company has decided to absorb Saitama Murata, its wholly owned subsidiary, through the Merger, with the aim of
improving management efficiency through consolidation of management resources by integrating Saitama Murata’s
functions into the Company.
２．
Outline of the Merger
（1） Schedule of the Merger
Board Resolution Approving the Merger Agreement: October 1, 2026
Execution of the Merger Agreement: October 1, 2026
General Meeting of Shareholders to Approve the Merger Agreement: June 28, 2027 (planned)
Effective Date of the Merger: July 1, 2027 (planned)
Note: For Saitama Murata, the Merger qualifies as a short-form merger as stipulated in Paragraph 1, Article 784 of
the Companies Act, and therefore, it will be carried out without obtaining approval at Saitama Murata’s
general meeting of shareholders.
（
2） Method of the Merger
A
n absorption-type merger in which the Company will be the surviving company and Saitama Murata will be the
absorbed company. Saitama Murata will be dissolved.
（
3） Details of Allotments Concerning the Merger
A
s the Merger is with a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, no new shares will be issued, there will be no
increase in the Company’s capital, and no merger consideration or any other consideration will be paid.
（4） Handling of Share Acquisition Rights and Bonds with Share Acquisition Rights of the Absorbed Company
N
ot applicable.

４． S
ituation After the Merger
There will be no changes in the name, location, title and name of representative, description of business, amount
of capital, or fiscal year-end of the Company as a result of the Merger.
５． C
losure of Business Site and Transfer of Functions in Connection with the Merger
In connection with the Merger, Saitama Murata’s business site will be closed as of May 31, 2027, and functions
currently performed at the business site, including product development, will be transferred to the Company’s
headquarters and other business sites.
６． F
uture Outlook
As the Merger is an absorption-type merger with a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, the impact of the
Merger on the Company’s consolidated financial results is expected to be minimal. If any matters requiring disclosure
arise in the future, the Company will promptly disclose them.

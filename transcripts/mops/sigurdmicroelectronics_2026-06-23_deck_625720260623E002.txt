==== page 13 ====
Operational Outlook
13
 1Q 2026 consolidated revenue reached NT$5.29 billion, setting a record quarterly high for the 
Company.
 Although 2Q 2026 revenue growth may face short-term pressure from rising memory prices, 
demand remains strong across AI servers, ASIC, AI Connectivity, silicon photonics, networking 
ICs, and memory. With capacity expansion progressing steadily and new equipment gradually 
coming online, additional capacity is expected to support future revenue growth.
 Looking ahead to H2 2026, growth momentum is expected to be supported by AI servers, ASIC, 
AI Connectivity, SiPh, networking ICs, LEO satellites, memory, and silicon capacitors. Sigurd 
will continue to invest in advanced technologies, expand capacity, and enhance technical service 
capabilities to support customers’ volume ramp-up and drive medium- to long-term growth.

 In May 2026, the Board approved an additional capex of NT$2.822 billion, bringing full-year 
capex to NT$8.8 billion, up approximately 48% from the original plan, the group's capital 
expenditure is estimated to reach NT$ 18.6 billion. The additional investment aims to support 
increasing testing demand from AI servers, ASIC, AI Connectivity, silicon photonics, networking 
ICs, silicon capacitors, and 2nm flagship 5G Agentic AI chips. Sigurd will continue to expand 
facilities and advanced equipment while deepening its 3A Technology strategy: Advanced Test 
Technology, Automation, and AI Factory. These initiatives will strengthen R&D, engineering, 
manufacturing, and quality capabilities, supporting customers’ long-term growth and driving 
Sigurd’s medium- to long-term momentum.

==== page 19 ====
 Chungshing Plant III is planned as a high-tech facility with two basement floors and 
seven above-ground floors, covering a total floor area of 36,340 square meters, or 
approximately 10,993 ping.
 Construction is progressing steadily and is expected to be completed ahead of 
schedule in Q4 2026, with mass production planned for Q1 2027.The new plant will 
integrate the Company’s 3A Technology strategy—Advanced Test Technology, 
Automation, and AI Factory—and target high-growth applications such as AI, ASIC, 
AI Connectivity, and Automotive ICs. Once operational, it will further enhance 
advanced testing capabilities, capacity flexibility, and medium- to long-term growth 
momentum.

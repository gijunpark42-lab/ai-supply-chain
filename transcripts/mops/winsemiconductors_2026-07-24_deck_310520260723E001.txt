Revenue & Margin Trend 
Source: Company. 
 2Q26 consolidated revenue was NT$5,257 mn, up 15% QoQ and 39% YoY, in line with expectations. 
 Reflecting the net impact of a more favorable product mix and higher shipment volumes, partially 
offset by margin dilution during the initial ramp-up of new products, individual GM was 32.6% in 
2Q26, up 0.5 pp sequentially. Consolidated GM was 28.2%, also improving by 1.9 pp sequentially. 
With opex remaining well controlled, OPM reached 14.1%, up 4.8 pp compared to the previous 
quarter .

Year 2026 Guidance
10
In the first half of 2026, Chroma delivered consecutive quarters of record-high consolidated sales 
revenue. Total consolidated revenue in the first half reached NT$25 billion, marking a robust 91% 
YoY. The parent company revenue increased 92% YoY to NT$20 billion. Performance was 
supported by strong momentum from two key business units, Test Instruments & ATS and 
Semiconductor surging 136% YoY and 62% YoY , respectively.
Year 2026.2H Business Outlook:
✓ For second half of the year, the strong momentum remained. 
✓ The second half will be fueled mainly by semiconductor contributions, outperforming the first 
half with drivers of SLT from AI / HPC / ASIC and Photonics from optical communication / CPO. 
✓ The sales revenues of Test Instruments & ATS for power testing continuously contributes from AI 
server power (included HVDC) and ESS markets.

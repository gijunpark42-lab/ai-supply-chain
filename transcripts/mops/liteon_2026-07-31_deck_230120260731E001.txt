【2Q26】
• Q2 sales of NT$52.7 billion, up 30% YoY ,21% QoQ; the Cloud & AIoT delivered over 70% yearly growth.
• Gross Profit of NT$14.3 billion, up 60% YoY , Gross Margin were 27.2%, up 5.1 ppt YoY; Operating Profit of NT$82 billion, up 120% 
YoY , Operating Margin were 15.6%, up 6.3 ppt YoY , resulting from synergies of strong demand for AI Datacenter, partial high-end 
product shipment deferred from 1Q, enhanced high-value business, economies of scale from global capacity expansion, and 
operational efficiency improvements through smart manufacturing.
• Q2 Net profits reached NT$7.1 billion and EPS of NT$3.14, up 126% YoY.
【1H26】
• 1H26 consolidated sales reached NT$96.1 billion, up 25% YoY; Gross Profit of NT$23.8 billion, up 38% YoY , Gross Margin were
24.7%, up 2.4 ppt YoY;
• Operating Profit of NT$12.3 billion, up 66% YoY , Operating Margin were 12.8%, up 3.2 ppt YoY; Net profits reached NT$10.9 billion;
EPS of NT$4.8, up 66% YoY ,reaching a record high level.
【Core businesses】
• Thanks to increased shipments of cloud computing products for AI server power management and energy storage solutions, cloud-
related business has achieved over 100% yearly growth.
• Core application in Opto-semiconductors enhanced, driven by advanced visible LED in cloud computing and invisible LED.
• In IT&CE, shipments of high-end and LEO satellite power supplies increased yearly.
【Board Resolutions】
• BOD approved the cash dividend of NT$2.5 per share in the second quarter of 2026.

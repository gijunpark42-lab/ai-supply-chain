Integrity, Service, Quality, Innovation 12
Memory Foundry
DRAM:
 Inference are replacing training as the primary consumer of computational resources, creating a seemingly 
"bottomless pit" of demand.
 Recently, the supply of DRAM from disassembled boards has been abundant, leading to a weakening of spot prices 
for DDR3, which is a short-term effect.
 Niche DRAM wafer foundry start prices remain unaffected and continue to be high.
 DRAM 1P process development is progressing smoothly, the PDK will be completed before the end of the year, and 
pilot production is scheduled for Q2 2027.
Flash:
 Due to the shortage of MLC NAND, the demand for large-capacity SLC is expected to further increase. PSMC has 
shifted its SLC foundry platform to the 24nm process.
 Leading manufacturers are shifting their focus to 3D TLC or QLC NAND. PSMC's MLC NAND is expected to have 
new products by the end of 2026.
 AI servers are driving a 3-5 times increase in the content per box for NOR Flash compared to traditional servers, with 
promising prospects for the future.
 PSMC benefits from geopolitical factors and is the only foundry for NOR Flash products outside of China.
==== page 14 ====
Integrity, Service, Quality, Innovation 13
Logic Foundry
1. Wafer Start ASP increased by 8~10% for Q3 eight-inch and twelve-inch logic foundry services, 
2. Power management IC:
 AI server and automotive applications continue to see strong demand.
 55nm BCD production has begun, and customers require a three-year contract to ensure a stable supply.
 All wafer foundries are fully loaded in 2026, which will affect the supply of PMICs for mobile phones. However, 
major mobile phone manufacturers still have a strong demand for production capacity in 2027.
3. 80% of the MOSFET/IGBT capacity have been secured with Long-Term Agreements (LTAs) for 2027; the GaN
product line has started small-volume wafer production, waiting for final customer validation.
4. The demand for TDDI in automotive applications remains strong, while Taiwanese manufacturers are reducing 
production, and the situation of capacity shortages and extended delivery times has not improved.
5. Insufficient capacity; a further price increase of 10~20% is expected in 2027, and the price and volume will be linked 
to the LTA in 2027.
==== page 15 ====
Integrity, Service, Quality, Innovation 14
3D AI Foundry
1. IPD:
 The visibility of IPD requirements has now extended to 2028.
 8-inch and 12-inch products are already in mass production (1.2V~100V, 10nf~2300nf), and a new 
4500nf product will be introduced next year.
2. Interposer:
 CoWoS orders overflow, leading to strong demand for interposers.
 The production line's relocation from the Tonglo to Hsinchu caused temporary disruptions, but we 
expect to return to full capacity by the end of the year.
3. Wafer-on-Wafer:
 The WoW production line has been moved from the Tonglo to Hsinchu. Currently, production has 
resumed, and we are conducting risk run. Mass production will officially begin in the first half of 2027.
4. PWF:
 Continuously working closely with Micron, and we expect to complete the pilot line by the end of 2026, 
with mass production expected to begin in the fourth quarter of 2027.

Integrity, Service, Quality, Innovation 15
Future Prospect
 3D AI Foundry's revenue momentum has gradually recovered since Q4 and will 
increase quarter by quarter starting in 2027. PWF will be added in 2028.
 Memory foundry: ASP for finished products still 30% or more lower than the 
wafer start ASP. Q4 company-wide revenue is still expected to grow by double 
digits. Revenue and profits are both promising in 2027.
 The new Fab plan focuses on 3D AI foundry, and will jointly invest in new 
production capacity with strategic customers.

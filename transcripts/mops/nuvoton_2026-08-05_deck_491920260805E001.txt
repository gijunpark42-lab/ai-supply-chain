❖ Nuvoton’s cryptographic engine for its cloud computing chips—including the NCT6 (eSIO, eBMC), NCT9 (DMC),
and NPCM4 (SMC) families—obtained the NIST CAVP (Cryptographic Algorithm Validation Program) validation
❖ Demonstrated our post-quantum cryptography (PQC) capability via the NuMicro® M2354, integrating TrustZone®
hardware security architecture and PSA Certified / SESIP Level 3 security capabilities to balance performance,
power,and security on resource-constrained MCU platforms
❖ Launched NuMaker-GestureAI-M55M1 module combining AI and vision to give devices easy gesture control for
smart homes, industrial controls, touchless systems, and security, further accelerating the deployment of
Endpoint AI applications
❖ Announced a collaboration with Qualcomm Technologies to advance tethered XR smart glasses by integrating
XR platform and low-power SoC technologies, accelerating the growth of the enterprise and industrial XR
ecosystem

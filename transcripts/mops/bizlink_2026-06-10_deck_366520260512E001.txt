III. Corporate Highlights: 
First Quarter 2026
AI Infrastructure Scaling Is Becoming Increasingly Complex
As AI infrastructure scales, deployment requirements are increasingly shifting beyond compute alone toward interoperability, power density, 
validation depth, and system-level infrastructure readiness.
◼ Platform transitions are becoming more overlapping and less sequential across infrastructure generations
◼ Rack-level requirements continue increasing across power, cooling, and interconnect architectures
◼ Deployment execution increasingly depends on validation capability, interoperability, and infrastructure readiness
Why This Supports BizLink’s Long-Term Positioning
As infrastructure architectures diversify and system complexity rises, customers increasingly require partners capable of supporting 
multiple connectivity, power, and integration layers across evolving deployment environments.
◼ Participation across copper interconnects, optical connectivity, and power infrastructure layers
◼ Global engineering and manufacturing footprint supporting deployment flexibility and operational resilience
◼ Increasing customer engagement across validation, integration, and system-level execution activities

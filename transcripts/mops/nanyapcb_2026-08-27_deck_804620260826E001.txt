Future Product Development  
Continue to Expand High Value Products  
9 
 ABF Substrate 
As CSPs increased their data center capital expenditures, the Company collaborated 
with customers to develop large-area, high-layer-count substrates for next-generation AI 
server ASICs, CPUs, and high-speed switch chips. Additionally, optimistic about the 
growth of inference AI, the Company will mass-produce substrate chips for high-end AI 
PCs, thereby increasing the proportion of high-value-added products. 
 
 BT Substrate  
On top of developing substrates for next-gen mobile SiP mainboards, smart glasses, 5G 
base stations, and automotive signal switches, we’ve also partnered with customers to 
work on substrates for next-gen servers and power IC chips. This comes as AI chips 
demand power management modules with higher power conversion efficiency, and it’s 
going to help drive our overall profitability. 
 
 Conventional PCB 
Driven by demand for high-end PCBs from data center servers and switches, the 
Company will develop and mass-produce products to capture related business 
opportunities, such as server mainboards, server secondary boards, and high-layer 
switch boards. Additionally, targeting opportunities across various edge AI applications, 
the Company will mass-produce high-end PC and LED circuit boards to further 
diversify its product portfolio.

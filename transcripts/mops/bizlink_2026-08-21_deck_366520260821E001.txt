I. Corporate Highlights: 
Second Quarter 2026
Architecture Is Evolving — And So Are We
◼ Higher power, bandwidth and system complexity are increasing engineering requirements across AI infrastructure.
◼ Our participation is expanding from individual components toward higher-value, more integrated solutions.
◼ While today’s platforms contribute to growth, we are already working on next-generation and, in selected areas, N+2 solutions.
Being In Today Helps Us Stay In Tomorrow
◼ Early customer engagement matters — engineering involvement begins well before volume deployment.
◼ Experience compounds across generations — qualification, manufacturing know-how and system knowledge carry forward.
◼ Capability expansion is deliberate — we invest organically and through M&A in capabilities that can be applied across customers,
products and technology cycles.

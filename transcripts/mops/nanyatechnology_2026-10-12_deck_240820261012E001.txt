DRAM Market Outlook (Q4'26)
⚫ AI computation (GPU, TPU, CPU & ASIC) dominate DRAM demand growth
⚫ Memory supply to smartphones, PCs, and general consumers, suppressed 
⚫ Suppliers’ long-term capacity planned based on LTA commitments
Market
Supply
Demand
⚫ New fab construction, equipment delivery and process-node introduction all require 
longer lead time
⚫ LTAs enable efficient product and capacity planning, supporting market stability
⚫ Lean supply-side inventory
AI driven:
⚫ Expanding AI applications are driving memory demand for higher-content, higher-
bandwidth, and customized architectures
⚫ AI drives DRAM demand beyond HBM and LPDDR5, across networking, storage, and 
system management
General applications:  
⚫ Supply shortage and higher ASP, result in down-specification or extended lifecycles 
⚫ Recycled DRAM partially supports low-end market

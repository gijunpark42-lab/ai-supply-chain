ESG Sustainability 
InWin aligns its operations with the United Nations Sustainable Development 
Goals (SDGs), integrating environmental, social, an d corporate governance 
principles into its corporate culture and strategie s, with a focus on achieving 
both economic benefits (EPS) and sustainable develo pment (ESG). By leveraging 
internal and external resources, the company has cr afted various short, medium, 
and long-term strategies and management guidelines,  driving sustainable 
practices across its business system and value chai n. 
Key strategies encompass: 
1.Reducing Carbon Emissions and Environmental Impac ts: InWin uses clean 
energy technologies to improve production efficienc y and environmental 
performance. 
2.Promoting Labor Rights and Community Development:  InWin prioritizes 
positive employee relations, welfare, and local com munity support. 
3.Enhancing Corporate Governance and Transparency: InWin focuses on 
strengthening governance, refining controls, and im proving transparency. 
These strategies enable InWin to adapt to environmen tal changes, enhance 
competitiveness, and uphold its corporate reputatio n, furthering sustainable 
development. Collaboration with stakeholders/shareh olders, customers, 
employees, and suppliers is essential for achieving  mutual benefits and 
fostering sustainable progress.

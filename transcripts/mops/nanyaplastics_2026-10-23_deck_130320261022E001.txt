Financial Highlights
EPS (IFRS)
(NT$ dollar)
 EPS in 3Q26 increased QoQ due to:
With electronic materials facing severe supply shortages, Nan Ya not only leveraged its economies of
scale and cost controls, but also addressed industry demand by developing next-generation materials
ahead of time, advancing product certification and commercial ramp-up schedules, and implementing
timely price increases, driving electronic materials operating profit up 96.1% QoQ with a 30.9% net
margin. In terms of equity-method investments, sustained memory price gains enabled Nanya
Technology Corp. to contribute substantial investment returns, lifting 3Q26 EPS to NT$4.90, setting a
new single-quarter record.
 EPS in 1Q-3Q26 increased YoYdue to:
In terms of core operations, electronic materials benefited from capex expansion and supply
shortages, driving price and volume growth in baseline materials; meanwhile, leveraging vertical
R&D, Nan Ya accelerated qualification and ramp-up of advanced ultra-high-speed, ultra-low-loss
materials, further enhancing product profitability; other segments also returned to profit amid market
recovery. As for equity-method investments, Nanya Technology Corp. turned profitable, contributing
substantial earnings. Driven by strong core and investment results, 1Q-3Q26 EPS reached a record
NT$10.07, exceeding one full capital stock.

Financial Highlights
Consolidated Revenue (IFRS)
(NT$ billion)
6
 Revenue in 3Q26 increased QoQ due to:
As the industry rapidly expands AI computing power to build hyperscale infrastructure, demand for
materials has surged and prices have risen month after month; furthermore, high-speed transmission
and ultra-low-loss requirements have driven rapid specification upgrades. The capacity utilization
across electronic materials product lines of Na Ya is approaching full load, and by actively shifting
capacity toward high-end materials to continually optimize the product mix, the single-quarter
revenue share of electronic materials has reached to 58.1%. For chemical products, ethylene glycol
prices hit multi-year highs as unrestored Middle Eastern capacity constrained Far East arrivals,
further boosting revenue.
 Revenue in 1Q-3Q26 increased YoYdue to:
Throughout 2026, rapid capex expansion across AI industries has widened electronic materials supply
shortages across the board; price hikes have spread from high-end to baseline specifications, driving
sustained revenue growth in general-purpose materials, while mid-to-high-end materials have
progressively completed qualification and entered mass production, comprehensively optimizing
electronic materials’ pricing, volume, and product mix. In addition, affected by geopolitical conflicts
in the Middle East, chemical product prices rose alongside feedstock costs through 2Q-3Q26,
significantly improving operating performance and driving overall revenue growth.

 Operating profit in 3Q26 increased QoQ due to:
The primary driver of overall operating profit is electronic materials; driven by the extremely rapid
surge in AI demand, PCB-related materials are shifting toward higher layer counts as well as more
complex formulations and manufacturing processes, widening the supply shortage, lifting product
prices month after month, and substantially boosting electronic materials’ profits.
 Operating profit in 1Q-3Q26 increased YoYdue to:
Operating profit across all four major segments grew YoY; among them, in addition to leveraging
existing scale and volume, electronic materials actively expanded and integrated its upstream-to-
downstream collaborative R&D capabilities to continuously penetrate AI supply chains and optimize
the product mix, surging 995.1% YoY compared to 2025; for chemical product, higher selling prices
and expanding spreads improved operating performance; polyester product benefited from easing
low-price dumping while continuing to develop optical and electronic films, returning to overall
profitability.

 Driven by the rise of AI and high-performance computing, market demand for ultra-high-speed
transmission and ultra-low-loss materials has surged, while equipment suppliers also face shortages,
widening the electronic materials supply gap and driving continuous price increases.
 In addition to leveraging its scale and capacity to capture general-specification orders, Nan Ya
operates fully integrated production lines across the PCB value chain. Through collaborative R&D in
cutting-edge materials, the company has developed Low Dk and Low CTE glass fibers, HVLP copper
foils, proprietary hydrocarbon resins, and ultra-low-loss CCL, efficiently accelerating qualification and
mass production for mid-to-high-end products.
 Driven by simultaneous gains in price and volume alongside ongoing product mix optimization,
electronic materials' single-quarter revenue contribution climbed from 46.6% in 3Q25 to 58.1% in
3Q26, relatively diluting the shares of chemical, polyester, and plastics segments, and solidifying
electronic materials as the primary engine driving overall performance.

4Q26 Outlook
11
Nan Ya delivered record-high performance in the first nine months of 2026, and 4Q consolidated
revenue is poised to climb further, driven primarily by robust demand across electronic
materials applied in high-tech fields; overall performance will outpace 3Q, fulfilling early-year
expectations that 2026 would be an outstanding year.
 Electronic Materials
In addition to optimizing and restarting production lines to quickly boost output, fully
leverage capacity advantages, meet internal vertical integration needs, and stabilize supply to
downstream customers, high-end materials will enter a phase of concurrent shipments and
qualifications starting in 4Q, driving sustained revenue growth for electronic materials.
 Chemicals and Polyesters
Downstream demand has yet to show a noticeable recovery, and with 4Q coinciding with the
Golden Week holidays and year-end inventory counts, customers are primarily placing
short-term orders.
 Polyesters
Upstream raw material prices for fiber products remain elevated, but market sentiment is
subdued; to avoid accumulating high-cost inventory, customers are ordering conservatively,
compounded by holiday shutdowns in the U.S. during Thanksgiving and Christmas,
impacting overall shipment volumes.
Polyester films benefit from expanding AI server and electronics demand, supporting steady
sales growth across the company's transformed electronic and optical applications.
 Plastics
Accelerated year-end budget execution for public infrastructure alongside construction rush
in the housing market have driven increased consumption of building materials.

Advantages of UV curable materials:UV curable technology, characterized by high efficiency, energy-saving, environmental friendliness, and cost-effectiveness, meets the operational needs of manufacturing industries—such as consumer electronics, renewable energy, 3D printing, PCB, cosmetic packaging, printing, optical fiber, and AI supply chain —by enabling automation and cleaner production.Increasing demand:With more breakthrough in UV curable technology and the growing demands for material performance in the downstream market, the scale of the UV curing materials industry continues to expand.Carbon emission reducing:As COP30 drives stricter global carbon reduction targets with updated commitments from over 120 parties, UV-curable materials offer a vital solution. Thanks to their energy-saving and eco-friendly properties, these materials directly support long-term corporate sustainability.Steady market growth:Steady growth in the UV-curable materials industry: The global UV monomer market is projected to register a Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) of 5.9% from 2025 to 2035.[cited from WiseGuyReports]

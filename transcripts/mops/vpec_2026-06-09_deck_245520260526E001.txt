==== page 3 ====
3
Through the organic metal chemical vapor deposition method, the 
semiconductor film is grown on the substrate, and the epitaxial 
layer is accurately controlled through the real-time monitoring of 
the machine to complete the production of epitaxial wafers for 
different products such as GaAs 、 InP and GaN.
The epitaxial layer is heated by MOCVD in the cavity of the 
substrate, and an atomic layer is stacked layer by layer to form an 
epitaxial layer.

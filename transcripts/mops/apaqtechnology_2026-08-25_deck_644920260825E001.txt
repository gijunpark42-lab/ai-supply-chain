2026 H1 Prospect 
◼ Macro Environment & Supply Chain Dynamics:
Surging prices for memory and storage, coupled with rising cost pressures, have led to a contraction in PC shipments.
While Server supply remains prioritized and less affected, component shortages and uncoordinated kit readiness continue to cause
fluctuations in demand.
◼ Notebook (NB) Strategy:
Slowing overall NB market demand has impacted Vchip sales; however, the company successfully sustains NB revenue growth by scaling 
up CAP output and accelerating the adoption of high-spec CAP .
◼ Server Growth Engine:
Sustained expansion in overall Server demand drives revenue growth, supported by aggressive qualification and design-ins of high-spec 
CAP , Vchip, and Hybrid capacitors to capture more market share.
◼ Capacity Expansion & Yield Optimization:
Accelerating yield improvements for high-spec CAP while expanding monthly capacity targets to 80 million units by November 2026 and 
100 million units by January 2027.
This expansion aims to aggressively penetrate Server, NB, VGA, and Switch markets, boosting CAP revenue, gross margins, and overall 
operational efficiency.
◼ Future Outlook:
As high-spec CAP capacity comes online in H2, shipments to Server and NB segments will scale up. Concurrently, Vchip and Hybrid 
shipments will rise to meet strong Server demand.
This momentum is projected to drive continuous revenue growth—achieving consecutive record-breaking quarters—alongside steady 
gross margin expansion.

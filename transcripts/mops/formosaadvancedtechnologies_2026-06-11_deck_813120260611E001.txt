Research &Development Plan©2026 Formosa Advanced Technologies Co.
==== page 12 ====
Development and Pilot Production of Through-Silicon Via (TSV) Technology, Multi-Layer Stacked Chips, and Wafer Bump ProcessesThe company is actively developing Bump and Redistribution Layer (RDL) technologies. New equipment is expected to be installed and qualified in Q2, with initial customer validation completed in Q4. Combined with the 3D TSV process, the company plans to provide customers with a full turnkey service covering Bump, RDL, and overmold packaging by Q1 2027.12The company has successfully developed 3D Through-Silicon Via (3D TSV) copper-revealed process (BVR) and 2-die stacked overmold packaging technology. Pilot production is expected to begin in the third quarter. In addition, 4-die stacked overmold packaging technology is currently under verification and is expected to complete development in the fourth quarter. The technology will be applied to high-capacity, high-speed DDR5 R-DIMM server memory modules.

In response to growing demand for memory packaging and testing services, the company has already increased output from existing facilities by upgrading bottleneck equipment. In the long term, it plans to replace aging equipment and further expand production capacity within current factory sites.1As demand for advanced packaging accelerates, the utilities and cleanroom construction of the new Plant 5 were completed in Q2 2026. Installation and qualification of advanced packaging equipment are currently underway, with pilot production expected to begin in Q4.
Business Overview
14©2026 Formosa Advanced Technologies Co.                                                                                      With the rapid development of AI driving growth across the memory industry, the company will continue adjusting capital expenditures and expanding production capacity according to customer demand, meeting outsourcing needs for IC packaging and testing, memory modules, and advanced packaging services.32
==== page 15 ====
The development of AI is reshaping the supply-demand structure of the memory industry. Strong demand for HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) has caused major international DRAM manufacturers to prioritize HBM production, resulting in a shortage of conventional DRAM supply and creating growth opportunities for Taiwanese DDR4 manufacturers.The sharp increase in DRAM prices has driven up the selling prices of DRAM module products, leading to weaker end-market consumer demand. However, demand for server and industrial-control memory modules remains strong, and the memory module outsourcing business continues to operate steadily.Memory prices remain at relatively high levels. Taiwanese manufacturers are actively expanding DDR4 production capacity and increasing output. Packaging and testing outsourcing demand is also growing simultaneously. Together with the rapid rise in advanced packaging demand, the overall outlook for the memory packaging and testing industry remains positive.

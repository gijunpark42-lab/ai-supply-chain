Growth Driven by AI & Silicon Photonics Testing Demand
1616
 Continued Growth in Operating Performance
Consolidated revenue reached NT$17.35 billion in Jan–Sep 2026, up 21.2% YoY, marking a record high for the same period. 
Monthly revenue exceeded NT$2.0 billion in both July-Sep, reflecting sustained business momentum.
 Growth Driven by AI and Advanced Testing Demand
In 2H26, despite cost pressures from rising memory prices in certain end applications, testing demand continues to strengthen 
for AI servers, CPUs/GPUs, ASICs, AI Connectivity, Silicon Photonics, 2nm high-end smartphone chips, LEO satellites, high-
speed networking, and silicon capacitors.
 New Capacity to Support Mid- to Long-Term Growth
Hukou Plant II commenced mass production in July 2026 and continues to ramp up. Zhongxing Plant III is scheduled for 
completion in 4Q26, with mass production planned for 1Q27. As new equipment and capacity come online, Sigurd will further 
enhance its advanced testing capabilities and capacity flexibility.
 Early Capacity Reservations Improve Demand Visibility
Some customers have secured testing capacity in advance through capacity reservation agreements and dedicated tester 
reservations, supporting upcoming new product introductions and mass production requirements while improving mid- to 
long-term visibility for orders and capacity demand.

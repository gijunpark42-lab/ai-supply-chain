Through these strategic allocations of funds, we will strive to maximize corporate value while balancing
investment in growth and shareholder returns. In order to prevent excessive expansion of capital adequacy
due to profit growth, we will strive for sustainable capital efficiency by firmly controlling the capital adequacy
ratio at around 55%, while utilizing agile allocation.

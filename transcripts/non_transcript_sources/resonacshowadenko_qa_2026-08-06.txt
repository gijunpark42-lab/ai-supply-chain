Summary of Questions and Answers at a Briefing on Resonac Holdings,  
2nd Quarter, 2026 Financial Results     
17:00 – 18:00, Thursday, Aug. 6, 2026 
Briefer: Hideki Somemiya, Director & Managing Corporate Officer, CFO 
*All contents are based on the information available as of the date and time of the briefing.
  
[Semiconductor and Electronic Materials Segment] 
Q. Following the upward revision of the FY2026 
forecast, could you provide a breakdown of the 
revenue outlook for each subsegment in 2026 2H? 
A. Compared with 2026 1H, all subsegments are expected 
to achieve revenue growth in 2026 2H. In terms of revenue 
growth rates, the order is expected to be Back-end Process 
Materials, Device Solutions, and Front-end Process 
Materials. 
 
Q. Following the upward revision of the FY2026 
forecast, what proportion of revenue do you expect AI-
related products to account for? 
A. In the FY2026 forecast announced at the time of our 
FY2025 financial results announcement, AI-related products 
were expected to account for approximately 30% of 
revenue in the Back-end Materials subsegment. Following 
the upward revision of the forecast, this proportion is now 
expected to exceed 30% of revenue in the subsegment. 
 
Q A press release announced a 30% price increase for 
copper-clad laminates (CCL) on January 16, 2026. Has 
the impact of this price increase already been reflected 
in earnings? Also, could you comment on the current 
status of negotiations? 
A. As stated in the press release, we negotiated price 
increases with customers. The impact of the price increase 
has been partially reflected since 2026 Q2. As we expect 
further revenue contribution from the price increase in 2026 
2H and beyond, our FY2026 forecast incorporates this 
effect. 
 
Q. A press release regarding additional investment in 
HD media was announced on July 28, 2026, which also 
referred to price revisions. Could you comment on the 
current status of HD media price negotiations?
 
A. Demand for HD media is currently very strong, which led 
us to decide on capacity expansions. We had been 
negotiating price revisions with customers and have recently 
reached agreements. We expect the revenue contribution 
from the price increase to be reflected from 2026 2H. 
 
Q With regard to technological changes in 
semiconductor materials, I believe there are various 
risks for the Company, such as NCF being replaced by 
hybrid bonding. How do you view these risks? 
A. We assess various scenarios associated with emerging 
technologies internally. At the same time, we believe that 
the mass production of HBM utilizing hybrid bonding is still 
some way off. Taking these risks into account, we continue 
to advance the development of semiconductor materials so 
that we can respond appropriately to a wide range of 
technological developments. 
 
[Chemicals Segment] 
Q. Could you comment on the performance of the 
Graphite subsegment? Did it return to be profitable in 
2026 Q2? Chemicals segment is expected to deliver a 
significant profit increase from 2026 1H to 2026 2H. Is it 
correct to assume that this is mainly attributable to 
improved profitability in the graphite electrode 
business? 
A. The Graphite subsegment posted a loss in 2026 Q1 due 
to weak sales volumes. However, its performance improved 
in 2026 Q2, resulting in a near break-even outcome. While 
the Chemicals segment is expected to achieve higher profit 
in 2026 2H than in 2026 1H, this is primarily attributable to 
the seasonality of Basic Chemicals. Many chemical 
products undergo scheduled maintenance in 1H, while 
demand for products such as carbon dioxide is stronger in 
2H. As a result, profit tends to increase toward the 2H of the 
year.

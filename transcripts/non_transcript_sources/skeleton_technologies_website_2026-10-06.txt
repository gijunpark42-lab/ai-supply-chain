 Supercapacitor power systems for AI data center power infrastructure

 Supercapacitors are a high-power energy storage technology capable of responding to dynamic electrical loads in microseconds. With extremely high power density, ultra-fast charging and discharging, exceptional reliability and safety, and operational lifetimes of up to hundreds of millions of charge-discharge cycles, they are increasingly becoming a critical technology for next-generation AI infrastructure and mission-critical power systems.

 
 Recent advances in nanomaterials and power electronics have significantly expanded the capabilities of supercapacitor technology. Skeleton’s patented Curved Graphene material enables industry-leading power and energy performance, supporting applications ranging from rack-level AI power stabilization to grid-scale infrastructure support.

 
 Today, Skeleton's power systems are helping improve energy efficiency, power quality, and infrastructure utilization across AI data centers, grid infrastructure, mobility, and defense applications.

owners of the parent of JPY160 billion, with a profit ratio of 7.7%.

Business, primarily due to market expansion of products for AI data centers and fluctuations in the foreign 
exchange rates, and for the Solutions Business, due to improved profitability in each business as well as to 
fluctuations in the foreign exchange rates.

TOYOBO Co., Ltd. Q&A Summary for the Presentation Regarding 
Financial Results for Q1 FY 3/27 
 
Date and Time: Friday, August 7, 2026 at 10:00 - 11:00 a.m. (Japan Standard Time) 
Place: WEB Conference 
Presenter: Hidekazu Takahashi, CFO 
 
Future earnings forecasts and other forward-looking statements in this release are based 
on data currently available to manage and cannot be guaranteed by the Company. Actual 
business performance, etc. may differ from the forecasts due to various factors. 
 
 
Q: What is the status of passing on costs, and what is the outlook for the future? 
A: Price revisions, particularly in Packaging film, are generally advancing. For the rises in raw material 
and fuel costs stemming from the situation in the Middle East, we intend to continue passing on 
these costs. On the other hand, there are fields where price revisions are difficult, such as in Life 
Science. 
 
Q: What is the reason for taking a cautious view of Films in the full-year plan? 
A: We expect that Packaging film will be affected by food price hikes, and shipments for food-related 
applications will slow over the H2 of the year. Furthermore, because of the rising raw material and 
fuel prices stemming from the situation in the Middle East, as a certain amount of time is required 
from production to sales, the impact is limited during Q1. However, this is expected to be reflected 
in a rise in cost of sales over the H2 of the fiscal year. Additionally, we have made cautious 
assumptions considering uncertainties in the external environment, such as trends in raw material 
and fuel prices. 
 
Q: How do you view the volume impact resulting from the situation in the Middle East? 
A: While Q1 saw an impact on net sales to the Middle East, going forward, we anticipate gradual 
normalization. However, as recovery speed will vary depending on the business, such as in reverse 
osmosis (RO) membranes for seawater desalination, we are focusing on the status of each business. 
 
Q: What is the status of Films for SRF and film for MLCC applications? 
A: Demand for SRF and MLCC applications is remaining steady. The full production and full sales 
situation are continuing for SRF, and volume is increasing for MLCC use due to the expansion of 
the Utsunomiya Plant. The high-performance film field is expected to continue growing.

Q: What is the outlook for the recovery of Life Science? 
A: The new plant for dialysis membranes has begun actual production, and is working towards 
enhancing production efficiency, such as yield improvements. In contract manufacturing business 
of pharmaceuticals, while depreciation burdens from facility upgrades are taking the lead, 
experimentation and trial manufacturing aimed at acquiring new contracting projects are advancing. 
Biotechnology sales are growing steadily due to the expansion of incubation facilities. We are 
expecting improved profitability over the H2 of the fiscal year due to volume increases. 
 
Q: What is the H2 outlook for Environmental and Functional Materials? 
A: Current demand remains generally steady. Compared to previous years, in H2 of the previous fiscal 
year, the impact of seasonal and other factors strongly increased revenue. However, such factors are 
not expected to have the same impact for this fiscal year. Furthermore, going forward, we are also 
factoring in the gradual manifestation of the impact of increased power and raw material costs. 
 
Q: What is the status of the structural reforms for the Airbag fabric business? 
A: We are advancing with the transfer of our China site, dissolution of our North American site, and 
consolidation into our Thai sites, and business structure reforms are progressing as planned. Effects 
of productivity improvements and expanded sales are also materializing, and we expect a return to 
profitability during H2.

3. Comments regarding this FAQ 
(1) What were the key drivers behind the recovery in orders in FY2026?  
 Demand for mask inspection systems recovered as production capacity for leading -edge 
nodes (2nm and 3nm equivalent) expanded.  
(2) What supports your forecast of record-high sales and profits in FY2027? 
 With the  recovery in orders in FY2026, we expect sales of mask inspection systems to 
increase, primarily driven by ACTIS and MATRICS. 
(3) What impact could rising raw material and memory component costs have on your 
business? 
 Through negotiations with customers, we have generally been able to pass on higher costs 
through pricing adjustments.  As a result, the impact on our business has been limited.

(Note) Product categorization 
 Semiconductor-related products  
 Mask inspection: For EUV masks (ACTIS, MATRICS X8/9ULTRA, BASIC, MZ, PELMIS, etc.) 
 For DUV masks (MATRICS Series other than X8/9ULTRA, MPM) 
 Blank inspection:  For EUV blanks (ABICS) 
 For various types of blanks and substrates (MAGICS Series) 
 Wafer inspection： For various types of wafers  (SICA, CIRIUS, LX, CIEL, EZ, VIANCA, BGM, 
 BIM, etc.) 
 Other products  
 FPD: FPD mask or mask blank inspection (CLIOS, LBIS, etc.) 
 Microscopes and others: Microscopes, battery-related products (OPTELICS, ECCS, etc.)

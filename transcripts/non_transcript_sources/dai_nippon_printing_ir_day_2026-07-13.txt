                                                                                                                                                  5
Nakamura: I am Senior Managing Director Nakamura. Now, I will explain the sources of our competitive
advantage.
I would like to explain our advanced printing technology, which is the source of our strength. Printing
technology is a technology that imparts functionality and information to the surfaces of objects, thereby
creating new value.
DNP's printing, "INSATSU" in Japanese is more than just printing in English. We have taken on challenges in
various fields--both individually and by combining our core technologies--and have created unique value and
products. That is the P&I Solution represented by this Technology Tree.
The "IN" on the left represents technical fields that create value, such as project planning and design,
information processing, and microfabrication. On the other hand, "SATSU" on the right refers to the technical
field of precision coating and postprocessing--technologies that enable high-precision reproduction and mass
production. Furthermore, adjacent technologies such as materials development, assessment and analysis
underpin these technologies.
These technologies will form the foundation--much like the roots and branches of the Technology Tree--that
will continue to interact and evolve. Furthermore, the fact that this technological foundation supports our
diverse businesses and continuous innovation is the source of our competitive strength.

                                                                                                                                                  6
Our business divisions are at the heart of our research and development. Starting with the business divisions,
we gather challenges, needs, and roadmaps directly from customers, major brand users, and the market, and
share them company-wide.
Research and development will begin, with tasks divided between the headquarters R&D divisions and the
business division's development department. The headquarters R&D divisions are responsible for the "0 to
10" phase of the innovation process--from exploration through commercialization to mass production--after
which the project is transferred to the business division to expand the business into the "10 to 100" phase, or
product development.
Our strengths lie in our ability to identify challenges from a customer-centric perspective and drive an
integrated process from technology development through commercialization and expansion, as well as in our
company-wide system for strategy development and overall management, which ensures that our R&D
investments are reliably converted into business value.

                                                                                                                                                  7

will further refine the "IN" and "SATSU" techniques I explained earlier, developing them into differentiated
technologies that our competitors cannot easily replicate.
At the same time, we are not only refining these differentiating technologies into even higher-quality products,
but also actively working to acquire new core technologies with an eye toward the next generation.
By continuing to pursue open innovation together with our customers and partners, we will create new value,
gain a competitive advantage, and accelerate the creation of value and new businesses that will serve as the
pillars of our future growth.

                                                                                                                                                  8

on these focus areas. As the globalization of markets and customers accelerates, R&D must also be conducted
on a global scale.
As part of our specific initiatives, we have opened two new overseas R&D sites. First, in September 2025, we
opened our first overseas R&D site in Eindhoven, the Netherlands, followed by our second site in Hyderabad,
India, in April 2026.
Through open innovation with local research institutions and universities, we aim to acquire cutting-edge
technologies and commercialize them in growing markets.

                                                                                                                                                  9
Our strength lies in the fact that we develop everything in-house--from material design to production
processes and manufacturing equipment--and handle both manufacturing and maintenance internally. This
prevents the leakage of know-how, and by deliberately keeping the technology as trade secrets rather than
patenting it, the Company has established high barriers to entry for competitors.
This supports the competitiveness of our product lineup--which boasts the top global market share in areas
such as battery pouches for lithium-ion batteries, metal masks for OLED displays, optical films for displays,
and dye-sublimation thermal transfer media for photo prints.
In addition to these foundations of manufacturing--people, machines, methods, and materials--evaluation
and analysis aimed at elucidating mechanisms are also critical core technologies, and we are strengthening
our capabilities in this area.

and products. This integrated evaluation and analysis capability contributes to accelerating new product
development, acquiring intellectual property, and collaborating with external partners on joint development
and commercialization, and will further accelerate and expand our value creation.

                                                                                                                                                11

Through close collaboration among our business, R&D, and IP divisions, we are vigorously advancing our
global intellectual property strategy while maintaining a solid domestic patent foundation. Currently, our

new value by combining our technological strengths.
Furthermore, our robust R&D framework will continuously support this value creation; by developing our own

As a result, while operating profit for FY2025 was JPY101 billion, the plan for FY2028 targets JPY130 billion,

First, here is an overview of our business. For approximately 150 years, DNP has been engaged in businesses

have earned a high level of trust and confidence from society and our client companies.

Leveraging our strengths such as superior product development and manufacturing capabilities--honed
through the production of market-leading smart cards, holograms, and direct mail--along with high-quality

business model that combines manufacturing with services across the fields of authentication, security,
payment, BPO or Business Process Outsourcing, and IoT or Internet of Things.

Furthermore, in July of last year, we made Rubicon SEZC a consolidated subsidiary and are accelerating its
global business by targeting emerging markets in the Government ID solutions.

Next, let's look at our strengths in the information security business. The first point is "the high reliability and
customer base cultivated in public infrastructure." Our extensive track record to date is proof of that.
Leveraging our proprietary security technologies and robust security framework, we have earned a high level
of trust by providing products and services over the long term, particularly to social infrastructure
companies--such as those in the financial industry and payment service providers--that are subject to strict
personal information management requirements.

The second point is "the integrated security model combining manufacturing, services, and operations." We
possess the rare capability to consistently provide high-quality, one-stop value--ranging from physical
security of smart cards and other devices to digital services in the payment and authentication sectors, as well
as the operations supporting them.

The third point is "the global value creation through strong collaboration with overseas partner companies."
In addition to our solid business foundation in Japan, we strategically partner with overseas companies such
as MK Smart and Rubicon to provide a wide range of optimal solutions on a global scale.

                                                                                                                                                26

change due to the emergence of new risks resulting from the evolution of digital technologies, as well as the
materialization of geopolitical risks. However, we view this change itself as an opportunity for growth.

robust cybersecurity and cyber resilience are required throughout the entire supply chain.
Moreover, as society becomes increasingly digitized and its structure grows more diverse and complex, the

system of government-issued IDs.
Furthermore, society is moving even more toward a cashless system with the aim of reducing costs associated
with cash and improving economic efficiency through the use of data. Against the backdrop of this demand,
the information security market is expected to experience medium- to long-term growth globally.
As shown in the graph on the right, the domestic digital ID and authentication solutions market is also
expected to grow at a high CAGR of 7.8%.
In addition, the number of Government ID holders worldwide is steadily increasing. The DNP Group has
identified the information security sector, which is expected to grow, as one of its key focus areas.

Q: How should we assess your competitive position relative to other companies?
     Given that many package manufacturers have recently been conducting
     research and development on glass cores, could you explain your strengths once
     again?

A: We have three strengths. First, glass cores are produced by filling holes formed in
     glass with copper. It is said that the presence of bubble-like voids can increase
     electrical resistance and cause defects, and we are able to produce glass cores in
     which the holes are completely filled without voids. Second, our manufacturing
     process enables us to form smaller holes relative to the substrate thickness, as
     measured by the aspect ratio, arrange them more closely together, and achieve a
     higher hole density. Third, while not unique to us as some competitors have also
     operated display businesses, we have many years of experience handling thin glass
     substrates measuring up to three meters square through our color filter business,
     giving us extensive expertise in glass handling.

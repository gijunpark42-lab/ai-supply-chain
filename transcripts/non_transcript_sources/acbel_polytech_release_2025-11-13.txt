Acbel Achieves Decade-High Q3 Operating Profit, Targeting Megawatt-Scale AI Data Centers
Acbel Polytech Inc. (6282) announced its strong third-quarter financial results today. The company reported its highest operating profit in a decade, driven by robust demand for its next-generation AI and data center power solutions.
Financial Highlights
- Consolidated Revenue: NT$8.07 billion (up 10% QoQ).
- Operating Profit: NT$280 million (surging 165% QoQ), marking a ten-year high.
- Net Profit After Tax: NT$250 million (best performance since Q4 2022).
- EPS: NT$0.3.
Growth Drivers and Strategic Focus
Acbel's strong Q3 performance was primarily attributed to the stable growth in shipments of AI server and data center power solutions, alongside increased demand for telecom power.
Jonathan Chen, CFO of Acbel, pointed out that the AI wave is fundamentally reshaping data center energy architecture. To seize future megawatt-scale AI opportunities, Acbel is building total solutions using three core pillars:
1. Technology Upgrade: Advancing power technology across server, rack, and cabinet levels to meet high-power-density demands. (e.g., Mass production of 33kW rack systems supporting platforms like the NVIDIA GB300 NVL72).
2. System Integration: Offering a full product portfolio from power shelves to rack-level solutions for flexible, scalable architecture upgrades for clients.
3. Strategic Alliance: Collaborating with global partners (spanning power modules, system management, and thermal design) to accelerate development and enhance competitiveness in AI infrastructure.
Future Outlook: Transition to Architecture Integration
Looking forward, Acbel will continue to focus on AI data center development. Through its three core strategies, the company is committed to a gradual transformation from a high-efficiency power supplier into AI data center energy solutions integrator, driving the business toward higher added value.

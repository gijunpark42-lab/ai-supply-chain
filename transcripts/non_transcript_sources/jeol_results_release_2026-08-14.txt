2. Changes to Reporting Segments 
 
The Group’s reporting segments were previously classified into three segments: the Scientific and Measurement Instruments Business, the Industrial Equipment Business, and the 
Medical Equipment Business. However, following the transfer of the Medical Equipment Business at the beginning of the current consolidated fiscal year, the Medical Equipment 
Business has been excluded from the reporting segments. Accordingly, the Group’s reporting segments have been changed from three segments to two segments: the Scientific and 
Measurement Instruments Business and the Industrial Equipment Business.

Acbel Delivers Record Q1 Revenue; Scales Strategic Layout in AI Data Center Power Solutions
Acbel Polytech Inc. (6282.TW), a global leader in power supply solutions, today announced its consolidated financial results for the first quarter of 2026. The company reported record-breaking first-quarter revenue of NT$8.58 billion, representing a 20.5% increase year-over-year (YoY), while seeing a sequential decrease of 6.7% compared to the previous quarter.
Reflecting shifts in the product mix, the gross margin for the quarter was 24.4%. Net income after tax stood at NT$231 million, with earnings per share (EPS) of NT$0.27.
Acbel noted that Q1 performance was primarily influenced by the evolving product portfolio and the shipment schedules of specific high-power products. Currently, key initiatives in AI data centers and high-power power supplies are progressing steadily. Validation for the next generation of power products and related solutions continues to expand across customer segments, with development and implementation timelines tracking in line with internal expectations.
“The infrastructure upgrade within AI data centers represents a long-term structural trend in the industry,” said Jerry Hsu, Chairman and President of Acbel. “In recent years, Acbel has consistently deepened its expertise in high-power electronics and system integration. This year, we continue to leverage the collective strengths of the Kinpo Group, our subsidiary OmniOn Power, and our strategic industry partners. Together, we are accelerating the development and market adoption of AI data center power solutions, solidifying Acbel’s pivotal role within the AI infrastructure value chain.”
Since the beginning of the year, Acbel has collaborated with group affiliates, OmniOn Power, and industry partners to showcase advanced AI data center power solutions at major international forums, including NVIDIA GTC and OCP EMEA. These efforts underscore the company’s commitment to expanding its global market presence and fostering deeper partnerships.
On the manufacturing front, Acbel continues to integrate Kinpo Group’s global production and supply chain resources to accelerate the commercialization and market entry of high-power supplies and HVDC (High Voltage Direct Current) solutions. Looking ahead, Acbel remains focused on the steady advancement of its product roadmap and solutions, positioning the company to capture long-term growth opportunities within the AI infrastructure sector.

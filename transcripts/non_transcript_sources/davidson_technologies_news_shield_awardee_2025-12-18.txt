Davidson Selected as Awardee for MDA’s SHIELD Contract Vehicle
Davidson has been selected as one of the 1,014 initial awardees under the Missile Defense Agency’s (MDA) SHIELD contract, formally known as Scalable Homeland Innovative Enterprise Layered Defense. As a holder of this Indefinite Delivery, Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) vehicle, Davidson is eligible to compete for future task orders that support the advancement of integrated missile defense technologies and capabilities.
While no task orders have yet been awarded, SHIELD is structured to enable rapid, scalable support across a broad range of critical national defense functions. Davidson’s expertise aligns with multiple areas of SHIELD’s scope of work, including:
Modeling, Simulation &
Systems Engineering
Cybersecurity
These capabilities reflect Davidson’s technical depth and past performance across programs supporting the Department of Defense and broader national security enterprise.
“This is a strategically important contract vehicle that aligns directly with Davidson’s mission to support the warfighter through purposeful innovation and mission-driven execution,” said Dale Moore, President and CEO of Davidson. “We recognize SHIELD is highly competitive. Our focus is on preparing to deliver meaningful solutions in support of MDA’s evolving priorities.”
SHIELD covers a wide range of technical scope areas related to missile defense, from research and development to experimentation, prototyping, system integration, fielding, and sustainment. Future task orders may be issued to address emerging threats, leverage disruptive technologies, and enhance system interoperability across domains.
Davidson’s inclusion in SHIELD strengthens its ability to contribute to these efforts through the lens of its core values:
Purposeful Innovation – Applying domain expertise to mission-aligned solutions
Mission Ownership – Treating every objective as our own
Technical Excellence – Holding a high standard of precision and scalability
Warfighter Always – Prioritizing operational optimization and advantage
This marks the latest step in Davidson’s ongoing strategic growth, building on previous multi-award IDIQ selections that expand our impact across the defense sector. It reflects our enduring commitment to deliver with urgency, innovate with intent, and strengthen those who serve on the front lines of national defense.

Q.
Why are you focusing your investments on Vietnam?
A.
Due to increasing production demand in Southeast Asia. We entered Vietnam about 15 years ago and have continued to invest there.
Q.
What will Vietnam’s sales ratio be going forward?
A.
Currently around 60%, and we expect it to increase further.
Q.

The current rise in raw material prices has been incorporated into our forecast.

How certain are orders from customers in growing areas such as AI and satellite communications?
A.
Our mid-term management plan is based on customer forecasts and other factors.

Our large production capacity for HDI PCBs has helped us capture new demand.

PCB spec. of 400G+ Optical Transceivers  
Optical transceivers are experiencing significant market growth driven by the rapidly increasing 
demand for high-bandwidth networking required by AI computing. Demand for 400G and above 
optical transceivers is accelerating, further driving demand for high-end PCB solutions. 
Compeq has been deeply engaged in mSAP substrate-like PCB for nearly a decade. We has 
continuously invested in fine line/space processes and high-speed material technologies, while 
actively expanding the high-speed networking market.

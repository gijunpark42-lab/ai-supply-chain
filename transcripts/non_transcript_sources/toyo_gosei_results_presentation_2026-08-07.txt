FY2026 Q1 Financial Highlights and Full-Year Outlook
FY2026 Q1 
Results (YoY)
FY2026 Full-Year 
Earnings 
Forecast
• Net sales increased 32% YoY to a quarterly record high of ¥12,086 million,
driven by higher sales of advanced semiconductor materials.
• Operating profit reached a quarterly record high of ¥1,932 million,
4.9 times the prior-year level, supported by higher sales and a temporary ¥0.51 billion benefit related 
to increased production in the previous fiscal year, as cost variances embedded in inventories at the 
end of the previous fiscal year were recognized in cost of sales.
• Full-year net sales are forecast at ¥50.0 billion (+5% vs. the May forecast, +19% YoY), and 
operating profit at ¥5.8 billion (+16% vs. the May forecast, +58% YoY), with net sales and all 
profit measures expected to reach record highs.
FY2026 H1/H2 
Outlook
• For H1, net sales are forecast at ¥24.5 billion, while the operating profit forecast has been revised upward to ¥3.5 
billion, factoring in an expected ¥0.4 billion favorable inventory valuation effect in Q2 due to higher raw material and 
fuel prices.
• For H2, the net sales forecast has been revised upward to ¥25.5 billion, reflecting expected continued sales growth.
Meanwhile, the H2 operating profit forecast remains unchanged at ¥2.3 billion, factoring in the non-recurrence of 
¥0.9 billion in temporary profit benefits recognized in H1, the impact of higher raw material and fuel prices 
beginning to be reflected in earnings, and increased fixed costs to support higher production.

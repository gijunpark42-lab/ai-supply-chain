Development of TSV, Multi-Layer Chip Stacking and Wafer Bump Processes：Wafer Bump & RDL In 2026,development will focus on Wafer Bump and RDL processes.Equipment relocation and setup are planned for Q2, followed by equipment installation and initial process trials in Q3, and pilot-run validation in Q4. The program will expand the foundry/OSAT product portfolio, targeting 20K↑Bumps/Die for full-flow wafer bump production. It will also extend from existing memory FCCSP products to high-pin-count ASIC and FCBGA logic packaging.123D TSV & Chip Stacking Successfully developed 3D TSV, BVR, and 2-layer chip stacking processes. Product validation is expected in Q4 based on customer development needs. Development of 4-layer chip stacking is also underway, with equipment installation and process trials planned for Q2 next year. The products are expected to be used in high-capacity, high-speed DDR5 R-DIMM server modules.

The continued expansion of AI application has driven a significant increase in demand for memory market. The demand for HBM and RDIMM from AI servers and general servers has squeezed out the supply of normal consumer memory products.Due to rising price of DRAM IC, demand for memory module in End-consumer spot market has slowed, but demand for server and industrial control memory module remain stable.The supply-demand imbalance in memory industry has driven up memory product price continuously, prompting Taiwanese manufacturers to actively increase output and expand capacity, which will simultaneously boost demand for assembly and test OEM.

Our leadership team

# Visionaries at the forefront of digital transformation

At QTS, our Powered by People™ culture drives our innovation, world-class customer service and unparalleled operational maturity.

Meet our team

 Thomas A. “Tag” Greason  

###### Co-Chief Executive Officer

Tag Greason is the Co-Chief Executive Officer of QTS, a digital infrastructure leader in designing, building and operating sustainable data centers with 75 data centers in operation or under development across the United States and Europe. QTS provides mission critical infrastructure solutions to Fortune 1000 enterprises, government agencies and the largest technology companies in the world, with an annual leasing revenue of more than $3 billion and total contracted power capacity eclipsing 3 gigawatts.

In collaboration with Co-Chief Executive Officer David Robey, Tag oversees all aspects of QTS in service to its people, customers and communities. He brings 25 years of industry experience driving business growth and value creation and developing deep customer relationships. Tag is recognized for his business acumen, strategic mindset and visionary leadership.

Tag joined QTS in 2011 and most recently served as Chief Growth Officer, leading all external activities, customer engagement and European expansion. Prior to QTS, he was the Executive Vice President for Current Analysis, Inc. and Vice President of Global Services at Savvis Communications, Inc.

He has a storied background in public service. Tag served in both the United States Army and the Virginia Army National Guard. In 2009, Tag was elected to the Virginia House of Delegates and served in the role for eight years. During his tenure, he was appointed to the House committees on Appropriations, Education, Finance, and Science and Technology and was selected as one of eight conferees responsible for negotiating the Commonwealth’s budget. In this role, Tag consistently balanced the state’s budget with a focus on both strategic growth, contingency planning, and problem solving. In July 2026, Tag was appointed to the U.S. Department of Energy’s Secretary of Energy Advisory Board (SEAB) where he provides strategic guidance to the Secretary of Energy on issues shaping the future of American energy, science, technology and innovation. In this role, Tag contributes industry expertise to help advance and strengthen U.S. energy leadership.

As a veteran, he is committed to championing military support organizations, including Stop Soldier Suicide. Tag serves as the Vice Chair of E3: Elevate Early Education, an organization that creates innovative initiatives to give children access to high-quality education for their first eight years. He is also dedicated to advocating for kids with autism spectrum disorder.

He earned an M.B.A. from George Mason University and a Bachelor of Science degree in systems engineering from the United States Military Academy at West Point.

 David Robey  

###### Co-Chief Executive Officer

David Robey is the Co-Chief Executive Officer of QTS, a digital infrastructure leader in designing, building and operating sustainable data centers with 75 data centers in operation or under development across the United States and Europe. QTS provides mission critical infrastructure solutions to Fortune 1000 enterprises, government agencies, and the largest technology companies in the world, with an annual leasing revenue of more than $3 billion and total contracted power capacity eclipsing 3 gigawatts.

In collaboration with Co-Chief Executive Officer Tag Greason, David oversees all aspects of QTS in service to its people, customers and communities. He brings 30 years of industry experience in critical environment engineering, facilities and operations management, and technology development. He is recognized for his authentic servant leadership, genuine care for people and customer service, and unwavering commitment to quality and excellence.

David joined QTS in 2010 and most recently served as Chief Operating Officer, overseeing all aspects of development and facility operations, supporting customer delivery while maintaining operational excellence. Prior to QTS, David held multiple technology leadership roles at Infineon/Qimonda, a global semiconductor manufacturing company.

David is committed to upholding and living out QTS’s core values, including our focus on faith, family and community. He is passionate about causes dedicated to helping children and veterans and their families. This commitment is consistent with QTS’s culture of service, which emphasizes making a meaningful impact in the communities we serve.

David holds a Bachelor of Science degree in physics from Missouri State University.

 Lane Anderson  

###### Chief Delivery Officer

Lane Anderson joined QTS in 2018 and serves as the company’s Chief Delivery Officer, where he is responsible for delivering complex data center projects with safety, quality, speed and accountability to meet rapidly growing customer demand. During his tenure, Lane has led the teams and initiatives that fundamentally transformed how QTS procures, designs and constructs data centers, helping the company evolve from delivering individual buildings to developing and bringing online multiple gigawatt-scale campuses.Lane brings more than 30 years of experience in the engineering,construction and data center and MEP intensive development industries.

Lane earned a Bachelor of Science degree in Mechanical Engineering from Texas A&M University.

 Robin Anselmi  

###### Chief Leadership, Culture & Strategy Officer

Robin joined QTS in 2026 and serves as the company’s Chief Leadership, Culture & Strategy Officer. She connects QTS’s strategy, people and execution. She partners with leaders across the enterprise to advance organizational effectiveness, strengthen leadership and shape culture, ensuring alignment around the company’s highest strategic priorities.

Robin brings more than 20 years of experience helping leaders and organizations navigate growth, change and transformation. Prior to QTS, she served as Chief Executive Officer of Conversant, where she worked with executives and teams across a wide range of industries to strengthen leadership effectiveness, align culture with strategy and build high-performing organizations. Her career spans engineering, business operations, consulting and executive coaching.

Robin earned a Master of Business Administration from the University of North Carolina Wilmington and a Bachelor of Science degree in Ceramic Engineering from Rutgers University.

 Barry Clegg  

###### Chief Safety & Infrastructure Officer

Barry Clegg joined QTS in June 2024 and serves as Chief Safety & Infrastructure Officer. Barry oversees Global Utilities Development & Operations, Global Environmental, Health and Safety (EHS), Global Quality and U.S. Procurement. He drives organizational and operational resiliency in support of Property Development’s growth and continuous improvement in QTS’s safety culture. Before his current role, Barry served as Chief Development Officer, responsible for all pre-development, development and substation activities.

Prior to QTS, Barry was President of Operations for Black & Veatch, focused on global operations, strategic priorities in the market sector and solutions leadership. During his tenure at Black & Veatch, Barry also served as project director and in various management roles within development.

Barry earned a Master’s degree from Kansas State University and a Bachelor’s degree from MidAmerica Nazarene University.

 Stephen Douglas  

###### Chief Financial Officer

Stephen Douglas joined QTS in 2015 and serves as Chief Financial Officer. He leads the company’s financial and capital markets strategy, supporting its rapid growth and long-term value creation. Stephen brings more than 20 years of experience in financial services and capital markets.

Prior to QTS, Stephen served as Equity Research Vice President at Bank of America focused specifically on the digital infrastructure sector.

Stephen earned a Bachelor of Science degree in Business Administration from the University of Richmond.

 Chris Gandolfo  

###### Chief Commercial Officer

Chris Gandolfo joined QTS in 2026 and serves as the company’s Chief Commercial Officer. Chris aligns strategy, sales and go‑to‑market execution to drive sustainable growth and long‑term customer value and oversees the Sales and Brand, Marketing & Communications teams.

He has more than 30 years of experience in sales leadership, with deep expertise across enterprise technology, cloud infrastructure and AI-driven platforms. Prior to QTS, Chris served as Executive Vice President at Oracle, where he led North America Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) Sales and Global AI Infrastructure, partnering with organizations to adopt and scale critical cloud and AI capabilities while strengthening go-to-market strategy across regions and industries.

Chris earned a Master of Business Administration from the UCLA Anderson School of Management and a Bachelor of Science degree in Economics from Vanderbilt University.

 Adam Haubenreich  

###### Chief Legal & Risk Officer

Adam Haubenreich joined QTS in 2023 with a focus on building the legal team to meet the growing needs of customers and internal clients. Since then, Adam has also taken on leadership of other QTS teams that focus on risk mitigation, physical and cyber security and lease delivery. Adam’s teams are responsible for critical governance and compliance initiatives. His many years of experience include complex corporate transactions and financing, including mergers and acquisitions.

Prior to QTS, Adam served as the executive vice president and general counsel of Custom Truck One Source, Inc. which specialized in heavy equipment solutions for customers in various industries. He also previously served as a corporate partner at the multinational firm, Baker Botts, LLP, where he worked with private equity and public companies covering the power, oil and gas, and telecommunications industries.

Adam earned his J.D. from Harvard Law School and a Bachelor of Arts from the University of Notre Dame.

 Brian Herlihy  

###### Chief Development Officer

Brian Herlihy joined QTS in 2025 and serves as the company’s Chief Development Officer. Brian leads an integrated organization that unifies our development, design, safety and energy infrastructure capabilities. He is also responsible for the commercial aspects of utility relationships, including business development and realization of new utility innovation projects. His commercial team supports contract negotiations and solutions for new projects in the U.S.

Prior to QTS, Brian founded and served as CEO for four platforms in the past two decades including Black Rhino Group in Johannesburg, South Africa and SEACOM, which was Africa’s first submarine fiber optic cable to connect to Europe and Asia. As an energy and infrastructure developer executive, he brings a deep understanding of project economics and structuring of complex financing for energy and infrastructure assets.

Brian was named a Young Global Leader by the World Economic Forum in 2012. Brian earned a Master of Science degree in Development Economics from the London School of Economics, a Bachelor of Arts degree in Economics and a Bachelor of Arts degree in Philosophy from Boston College.

 Ryan Hunter  

###### Chief Operations Officer

Ryan Hunter joined QTS in 2017 and serves as Chief Operations Officer. Ryan oversees all Development, Design, Engineering and Facility Operations for the U.S. and Europe. In addition, he is responsible for Europe Pre-Development and Procurement. Since 2023, Ryan served as EVP, Facilities Operations, driving all infrastructure and operations of QTS facilities and ensuring reliability, efficiency and safety, and before that he was VP, Facilities Operations.

Ryan brings 25 years of experience in mission critical operations to his role at QTS. Prior to QTS, Ryan was Director, Facilities Support at vXchnge where he was responsible for the maintenance, operations and overall management of the company’s facilities. He also served in facilities management roles at Equinix and Switch and Data.

Ryan served as Leading Petty Officer, Reactor Electrical Division in the United States Navy for eight years and completed Naval Nuclear Power School as an Operator and Electrician during his service.

 Madison Williams  

###### Chief Investment Officer

Madison Williams joined QTS in 2013 and serves as Chief Investment Officer. Madison has more than 20 years of real estate investment experience. Madison oversees the U.S. & European Investment, Real Estate and Site selection teams, along with U.S. Pre-Development and Development Capital management.  Theo Yedinsky  

###### Chief External Affairs Officer

Theo Yedinsky joined QTS in 2026 and serves as the company’s Chief External Affairs Officer. He leads the Government Relations, Economic Development, Community Engagement and Philanthropy teams under a unified strategy to support QTS’s expansion and strengthen long-term impact.

Prior to QTS, Theo served as Vice President of Public Policy at Airbnb where he oversaw global regulatory strategy, government relations, advocacy campaigns and policy development. He has more than 20 years of experience and brings a deep expertise leading public policy, regulatory and communications efforts.

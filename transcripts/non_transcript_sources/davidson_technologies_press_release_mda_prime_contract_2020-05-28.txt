MDA Prime Contract – May, 2020
Davidson Technologies leverages intellectual property to support MDA May 28, 2020, Huntsville, AL – Davidson Technologies announces the award of a contract modification to a multi-year prime contract that will continue efforts toward providing a cyber-secure information technology infrastructure, uniquely developed by Davidson Technologies, in support of the Missile Defense Agency at Redstone Arsenal in Huntsville, Alabama.
Maj. Gen. John W. Holly, (Ret.), president of Davidson Technologies, said, “This is a great step forward for Davidson. Providing a product to MDA that originated with a Davidson IRAD investment is a fantastic outcome for all involved. We look forward to supporting MDA and its mission to defend our country.”

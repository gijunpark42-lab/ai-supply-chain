In the T&M business, data center-related demand, such as in the optical transceiver market,
remained strong due to the spread of AI, and customer investment, which had been deferred due to
US tariff policies in the previous year, also recovered. As a result, revenue increased 34% YoY to 19.4
billion yen, and operating profit increased 149% YoY to 3.2 billion yen (operating margin of 16.3%),
representing an increase in both revenue and profit.
In the PQA business, demand remained strong due to growing interest in quality inspections and
demand for capital investment aimed at automation and manpower savings in the quality assurance
process in the food market. In North America in particular, we captured demand for X-ray inspection
systems, mainly in the meat market. As a result, revenue increased 16% YoY to 7.9 billion yen, and
operating profit increased 36% YoY to 0.8 billion yen (operating margin of 9.8%), representing an
increase in both revenue and profit.
In the Environmental Measurement business, in addition to DEWETRON GmbH's contribution to
consolidated results, demand for DEWETRON GmbH's data acquisition systems remained solid. As a
result, revenue increased 323% YoY to 3.6 billion yen, and operating profit increased 0.6 billion yen
YoY to 0.2 billion yen (operating margin of 5.5%), representing an increase in both revenue and
profit.
In other businesses, revenue increased 18% YoY to 1.7 billion yen, and operating profit increased
61% YoY to 0.6 billion yen (operating margin of 35.4%), representing an increase in both revenue
and profit.

In the wired market within the network infrastructure segment of the Test and Measurement
Business, the establishment of new data centers and the rapid expansion of data center capacity
driven by generative AI, which emerged in 2023, are driving demand for test instruments.
In data centers for generative AI, the upgrade to 800GE networks is gaining full steam, and
optical transceiver manufacturers are ramping up production of 800GE optical transceivers.
Progress is also being made in the development of 1.6TE optical transceivers for the purpose of
further increasing speed and capacity, and efforts have begun with a view to mass production. In
addition, preparations have begun for the next wave of 3.2T optical transceiver development.
Meanwhile, network equipment manufacturers are accelerating the development of PCIe Gen7

high-speed buses, and activities toward PCIe Gen8 standardization are also gaining momentum.
In addition, we believe that moves toward further network sophistication, including fully
optical networks, will support strong demand for network-related test instruments.
In the mobile market, In the area of 5G utilization, research and development for in the
automotive field has been advancing, and demand is expected for the development of Wi-Fi 7

as well as for the development of Non-Terrestrial Networks (NTN: Non-Terrestrial Network).
The 3GPP, an international standards organization, is deliberating the standardization of Release
20 with the goal of launching commercial 6G services in 2030. In conjunction with this, we expect
to see full-fledged demand for 6G test instruments beginning around 2027. Furthermore, we are
starting to see moves to utilize the FR3 (7GHz-15GHz) band newly defined by WRC-23 in order to
provide Pre-6G service at the Olympic Games in Los Angeles in 2028. Due to this initiative,
development demand for FR3-related technologies is expected to grow earlier than initially
forecast.
We aim to seize these business opportunities and expand sales in the Test and Measurement
Business.

Vie BOARD, a Joint Venture by DOCOMO and DatViet Group, Launches DOOH Operations in Ho Chi Minh City
DOCOMO Enters Overseas DOOH Market for the First Time
[image: Print]
Tweet
TOKYO, JAPAN, April 27, 2026 --- NTT DOCOMO, INC. announced today that Vie BOARD CORPORATION (Vie BOARD), * 1 a joint venture established by DOCOMO and Vietnam's DATVIETVAC GROUP HOLDINGS and DATVIETOOH CORPORATION, will begin operating its own digital signage network in Ho Chi Minh City on May 1. * 2 The company will install digital signage across the city and launch Digital Out-of-Home (DOOH) advertising services.
DOCOMO will support the project by providing estimated audience data, such as viewer numbers and demographic attributes, calculated using its proprietary DOCOMO Data Science technology.
This marks DOCOMO's first entry into the overseas DOOH business, and it is also the first time Vie BOARD has operated its own digital signage assets.
To expand DOOH services internationally, DOCOMO has developed DOCOMO Data Science, a technology that uses overseas GPS data * 3 to accurately estimate audience size and demographics, such as gender, age and interests, enabling targeted delivery and effectiveness measurement. In August 2024, DOCOMO and DatViet Group established Vie BOARD to promote DOOH business development in Vietnam.
Vie BOARD has made a proposal to the government offices of Ho Chi Minh City for using digital signage within the city for cultural promotion and will begin installing its signage on May 1. The first 10 screens will be placed inside Ho Chi Minh City Metro Line 1 stations, with DOOH operations launching concurrently.
DOCOMO will leverage DOCOMO Data Science and its proprietary algorithms to analyze DOOH viewer numbers and user attributes, and to analyze internationally standardized audience measurement data compliant with global OOH measurement guidelines. * 4 Based on the estimated data and analysis provided through DOCOMO Data Science, Vie BOARD will enable more impactful DOOH advertising than conventional DOOH.
The first 10 digital signages will be installed in four of the busiest stations among the 14 total stations on Ho Chi Minh City Metro Line 1, which began service in December 2024. The digital signage is being installed in areas with the highest expected foot traffic within each station, such as near the ticket gates, and will contribute to increasing brand awareness for advertisers.

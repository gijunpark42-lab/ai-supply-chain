visible in the numbers.

in 2H, additional price increases, and the effects of new products. 
Turning to each business area, in the Multipurpose Analytical Instruments category, the timing of the recovery 
in revenue was about one Q later than we had initially expected at the beginning of the year. However, in 2Q, 
we began to see a recovery trend in revenue, which has now returned to nearly the same level as last year. 
As a result, for 1H overall, orders grew 5% YoY. 2Q orders were particularly strong, increasing by more than 
20% in Japan, while the U S market recovered significantly, with orders growing by more than 50% YoY. The 
order outlook for 3Q also remains strong, and the recovery trend in Multipurpose Analytica Instruments has

We have been pursuing a shift from academia to industry for some time. Although it has taken a little longer 
than expected, we believe this shift has now begun to be reflected in our order figures. Based on this, we 
expect substantial revenue growth in 2H and into next year.

Ogata [A]: In terms of applications, for example, in memory application, T-SAXS and OCD can both be used 
for measurement of deep-hole structures, but each has its own advantages/disadvantages. 
T-SAXS offers high accuracy and can accurately measure deep-hole structures, but its throughput is slow. OCD, 
on the other hand, has high throughput but has some limitations. For example, data from one system can be 
passed to the other, or the data can be analyzed jointly.  The methodology will become more sophisticated 
over time, but in this way, the performance of both systems improves and the accuracy of the results increases. 
This would make it possible to introduce both T -SAXS and OCD, or hybrid metrology, into mass-production 
lines. Until now, T -SAXS has been used in development. It has also been used by WFE manufacturers in  
development of etching equipment. However, it has not been installed in large numbers on mass-production 
lines. 
We are therefore developing hybrid metrology with the expectation that it will enable T -SAXS to be applied 
to mass-production lines and thereby increase the number of systems installed.

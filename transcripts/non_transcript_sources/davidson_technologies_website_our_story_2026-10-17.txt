Our Story
Innovate with Purpose. Deliver with Speed. Empower the Warfighter.
For 30 years, Davidson has delivered advanced engineering, digital, and AI-enabled solutions that empower the warfighter.
Our team of experts and innovators remains dedicated to safeguarding missions and shaping the future of defense.
[image: Abstract vertical light streaks on dark background.]
History
Founded in 1996 in Huntsville, Alabama, Team Davidson built its legacy supporting MDA, the U.S. Army and U.S. Air Force with engineering and modeling & simulation expertise.
Today, we infuse quantum, AI, and digital transformation into our DNA—developing breakthrough technologies that defend our nation and honor our history of mission-focused excellence.

Chapter 1. Letter to Shareholders

Dear Ladies and Gentlemen:

Thank you for attending the 2026 Annual General Meeting of STL Technology Co., Ltd. (hereinafter
referred to as "the Company") in the midst of your busy schedule. Recalling 2025, the Company's
consolidated operating revenue, net, was NT$ 2,451,377 thousand. The net profit after tax was NT$
203,562 thousand, with the earnings per share at NT$ 3.13. The 2025 business plan implementation
results and the business plan for this year are stated as follows:

I. 2025 Business Report

 (I) Business plan implementation results

       In 2025, the Company's net operating revenue was NT$ 2,451,377 thousand, growing by 23%
       from that amounting to NT$ 1,995,268 thousand in 2024; the net profit after tax was NT$
       203,562 thousand, growing by 26% from that amounting to NT$ 161,498 thousand in 2024.
       The growth in operating revenue and profit primarily resulted from the continued high growth
       of the AI industry that boosted the demand for battery backup systems in AI computing power
       centers.

       The Company's product sales continued to focus on lithium-ion battery modules for
       applications in three major fields: battery backup unit/ uninterruptible power system (BBU/
       UPS), power tools (PT) and light electric vehicles (LEV). The sale territories were primarily
       markets in Europe and the United States. Direct and indirect Export sales accounted for around
       99% of the operating revenue, net.

 (II) Budget implementation status: The Company didn't publish any annual financial forecast in
       2025.

 (III) Analysis on financial revenue and expenditure, and profitability

       The Company's net cash inflow from operating activities was NT$ 266,974 thousand in 2025,
       increasing by NT$ 161,128 thousand from 2024, primarily as a result of the increase in the
       accounts receivable of the current year. The net cash outflow from investing activities was NT$
       66,909 thousand in 2025, decreasing by NT$ 37,005 thousand from 2024, primarily due to the
       fact that there was no acquisition of financial assets at fair value through other comprehensive
       income in 2025. The net cash outflow from financing activities was NT$ 135,684 thousand in
       2025, decreasing by NT$ 79,327 thousand from 2024, primarily due to the increase in
       distributed cash dividends. The financial ratios for 2025 all show an increase from 2024,
       primarily as a result of the increase in the net profit for the year.

 (IV) R&D performance

       The Company continued to invest capital in the R&D of core technologies in 2025. The R&D
       expenditure for the whole year accounted for 4% of the annual operating revenue. The primary
       development projects include:

       1. Lithium-ion battery backup modules for AI computing power centers.

       2. Lithium-ion battery backup modules for server racks.

       3. Lithium-ion battery backup modules for server motherboards.

       4. Next-generation high-power-density lithium-ion battery modules for power tools.

       5. Lithium-ion battery modules for UAVs.

II. Overview of the 2026 Business Plan:

 (I) Business policy

       In 2026, geopolitical tensions will continue to impact the world. Nevertheless, with
       adjustments to global supply chains and corporate strategies, political and economic
       uncertainties are gradually becoming the new normal, and their direct impact on business
       operations will also be lessened.

       The Company has long focused on and been devoted to three major fields: lithium-ion battery
       backup system modules for AI computing power centers and servers, lithium-ion battery
       modules for power tools, and lithium-ion battery modules for lightweight electric vehicles.
       Meanwhile, the Company has also been pursuing a balanced revenue ratio in these fields to
       reduce the risk of excessive concentration in the market.

       However, in response to the continued high growth of the AI industry, the Company will further
       expand the deployment in the AI industry. With our robust risk management mechanism, we
       will closely monitor market changes to ensure the stability of supply chains and meet customer
       demands, so that we can maintain the agility and flexibility to adapt to market changes while
       striving for exceptional growth.

 (II) Anticipated sales volume and the basis thereof

       The Company focuses on the R&D and manufacturing of high power, multi-series parallel
       lithium-ion battery modules, which are mainly used in the fields of lithium-ion battery backup
       system modules for AI computing power centers, lithium-ion battery modules for power tools,
       and lithium-ion battery modules for lightweight electric vehicles. Among the target industries,
       the battery backup systems industry is expected to show impressive growth in the next few
       years due to the changes in the demand of industrial development. Our market growth strategies
       include vertical development and horizontal expansion. In addition to developing existing
       customer relationships based on our existing product portfolio, the Company will also actively
       develop new customers for relevant products to maintain the overall growth momentum.

 (III) Important production and sales policy

       In response to the external competition and environment, legal environment, and overall
       business environment, the Company prepares the production and sales strategies as follows:

       1. For production, the Company will implement the assembly of new lithium-ion batteries

             proactively and increase the processes of automated production. Meanwhile, we will be
             well-prepared to meet the customer's requirement for off-site production.
       2. For sales, the Company still aims at the target industries, strives for new customers'
             certification, and increases the existing customers' products in depth and width.
       3. For R&D, the Company will continue to invest resources in the target industries and
             emerging fields, deepen technology related to battery assembly, and establish design
             capabilities in line with the future industry safety specifications.
       4. The Company continues to set up the ESG indicators critical to an enterprise's sustainable
             development.
 (IV) The Company's future strategies, and the effects of external competition, legal environment
       and overall business environment
       As a company that expects itself to become a global citizen, the Company complies with the
       regulations related to any business activities, and is also committed to making contributions to
       the development of the green industry. Given the energy-conservation and carbon-reduction
       trend prevailing all over the world, as a part of the green economy, the Company can benefit
       from the trend undoubtedly. Meanwhile, in the process of production and manufacturing, the
       Company is considered a low-energy-consumption and low-pollution industry. Therefore, the
       negative environmental factors, such as power shortage, water shortage and pollution, would
       pose relatively minor impacts on the Company. Given this, the Company may be better at
       responding to the changes in external environment and laws & regulations. Still, the Company
       will keep making responses to current and future ESG-related requirements in an active manner.
       Finally, we thank you for your long-term support and encouragement to the Company, and
       hereby extend our sincerest appreciation to all of you on behalf of the Company.

We wish all of you good health and good luck.

(II) Overview of industry

1. Current status and development of the industries:

      (1) Global lithium-ion battery market
            In terms of the global battery industry scale, the batteries may be divided into primary
            batteries and secondary batteries. The primary batteries refer to non-rechargeable
            batteries. Such batteries are usually applied to disposable electronic products, such as
            alarm clocks and remote controls, etc. Secondary batteries, also known as rechargeable
            batteries, can be recharged through reverse electrochemical reaction, and release power if
            necessary. Such batteries are usually applied to various reusable electronic devices, such
            as laptops, smartphones and electric vehicles. Given the rapid growth of markets
            including electric vehicles, the scale of the secondary battery business is rapidly
            expanding and, therefore, becomes an important part of the battery industry.
            In terms of the raw materials as applied, the secondary batteries can be generally
            categorized into lead-acid batteries, NiMH batteries, NiCd batteries, and lithium-ion
            secondary batteries. Among other things, the lead-acid batteries still account for the
            largest market share. However, the market as they are replaced by lithium-ion batteries
            gradually in recent years. NiCd batteries would cause a serious environmental protection
            problem from cadmium pollution, so they have been abandoned by almost all portable
            products. Though NiMH batteries have no environmental protection problems, they
            deliver disadvantages such as low energy density and low charge and discharge
            frequency, and, therefore, are being replaced by lithium-ion batteries in the application of
            portable products. Following the evolution and development of portable electronic
            products, lithium-ion batteries have become the mainstream product in the field of
            secondary batteries in recent years, in consideration of the advantages, such as high
            capacity, small size and light weight. Meanwhile, given the impact posed by
            environmental protection and energy sustainability issues, the demand in the electric
            vehicles and energy storage industries will increase step by step. In order to reduce the
            battery costs, all of the manufacturers are racking their brains on the formulation of
            battery materials.
            In November 2025, production and sales in the power battery market remained booming.
            Despite the start of the off-season, the demand was bolstered by auto makers accelerating
            battery installations. In addition, the cost of power battery materials rose significantly due
            to recent price increases in cathode materials, electrolytes, and copper/aluminum foils,
            leading to a rebound in the price of power battery cells, particularly for ternary battery
            cells containing cobalt.
            According to TrendForce, the average prices of ternary battery cells all increased in
            November 2025. Prismatic ternary cells reached CNY 0.48/Wh (a month-over-month
            increase of 6.7%) while pouch ternary cells averaged CNY 0.50/Wh (a month-over-month
            increase of 4.2%). The price of prismatic LFP cells remained relatively stable at CNY

                                                                       56
0.34/Wh, unchanged from October. With auto makers completing most of their year-end
stocking ahead of schedule, demand for power batteries was expected to ease slightly.
However, given continued material inflation, the cell prices were still forecast to rise
modestly in December 2025. The price of energy storage battery cells is currently stable,
with further price increase anticipated.
The demand for energy storage battery cells in the second half of 2025 actually exceeded
expectations, and the prices remained stable in November 2025. Based on analysis of the
orders, it seems that leading manufacturers are gradually raising their shipment targets for
2026, so it seems certain that the rapid growth in energy storage battery demand will
continue next year. Furthermore, as leading companies have made mass shipments of
500+Ah large-capacity energy storage cells in the second half of 2025 (e.g. CATL has
completed shipments of 587Ah energy storage cells at the GWh level), the overall
shipment proportion of 500+Ah large-capacity energy storage cells is projected to
increase progressively in the industry in 2026.
As indicated by TrendForce, the power and energy storage markets gained momentum in
the second half of 2025. In particular, the energy storage sector expanded rapidly, driving
significant increases in the prices of non-ferrous metals related to lithium batteries, such
as lithium, cobalt and copper. For example, due to the adjustments to the cobalt export
policy of the Democratic Republic of the Congo, cobalt prices have risen steadily since
March 2025 and increased by over 140% so far compared to the year's low point; the
price of ternary cathode materials has also climbed accordingly.
Other battery materials also experienced supply shortages and sharp price rises in the
second half of the year because of the strong demand of the peak season. The most
significant price rises were seen in electrolytes and materials related thereto, especially
lithium hexafluorophosphate (LiPF6) and vinylene carbonate (VC), with their prices
soaring by over 230% and 260%, respectively, from July to November 2025.
In 2026, the demand for power and energy storage batteries is expected to remain strong.
Major battery material suppliers--including those of cathode and anode materials,
separators, and electrolytes--have given notice of price adjustments, and some price
negotiations with downstream customers have been concluded. The cost is very likely to
be higher for new orders in 2026. Based on this, TrendForce forecasts a slight increase in
the price of power battery cells next year.

In 2024, the Asia Pacific region dominated the global lithium-ion battery market with its

3. Product development trends

      (1) Extensive application scope of lithium-ion battery module products
            The lithium-ion battery has the characteristics of high energy density, fair cycle function
            and low pollution. The application fields of lithium-ion battery modules are expanding,
            including the emerging trend for replacement of lead-acid batteries and NiMH batteries.
            The scope of application covers automobiles, motorcycles, electric scooters, agricultural
            and construction machine, industrial machine, UPS system, wind power and solar power
            energy storage systems equipment, etc. It will become the most promising product in the
            market.

      (2) Improvement of customized service ability
            Unlike the standardized product design for 3C products, in the Non-IT field, there is no
            standardized design applicable among the downstream dealers. Therefore, the service

                                                                       63
      skills that lithium-ion battery module suppliers need to improve immediately are how to
      provide special designs with respect to the different models requested by various
      customers and various application products, and to provide sufficient standardized spare
      parts, flexible production and after-sale service that satisfy consumers' expectation,
      subject to the highly differentiated market demand for the downstream end products,
      which also raise the entry barriers to the industry.
(3) Improvement of product safety
      In order to stabilize the lithium-ion battery module product safety, various manufacturers
      have successively improved the battery safety. Therefore, solid-state battery materials are
      aimed as one important research direction. Unlike the existing commercial lithium-ion
      batteries which apply the flammable organic liquid as electrolyte, the solid-state batteries
      use lithium ceramic compounds to replace traditional electrolytes, in order to avoid the
      danger, such as explosion caused by punctures, and also the leakage problem, and ensure
      the battery safety. Notwithstanding, the technical threshold for making solid-state
      batteries appears to be higher, and some breakthrough measures are still needed in terms
      of cost, electrical performance and life cycle. The method commonly applied at present
      refers to the semi-solid strategy that combines colloidal and solid states. The battery pack
      design delivers the protection functions to strengthen and protect the component design
      and also related positions in the battery pack, in order to achieve the purpose for
      comprehensive protection. Therefore, battery module manufacturers shall respond to the
      evolution of the secondary battery technology, improve their own R&D technology and
      process capabilities, and also develop the high-performance battery packs that take into
      account safety and environmental protection at the same time, in order to satisfy the
      market demand.
(4) Replaceability of products
      In consideration of the restrictions on CO2 emissions in the world, use of cleaner and
      energy-conservation energy is inevitable. Therefore, the application of lithium-ion
      batteries to power batteries and energy storage systems equipment in the Non-IT field
      may be identified as an emerging industry which various industrial countries are eager to
      invest in. The factors, such as increasingly rigid environmental protection laws and
      regulations applicable in various countries, policy subsidy, increasingly well-founded
      energy infrastructure, tax credit and laws banning fuel and diesel vehicles, drive the
      increasing sales of electric vehicles and motorcycles. Taiwan's government also makes
      every endeavor to guide the promotion of the green industry in line with the globalized
      trend for energy conservation and carbon reduction. Taiwan has planned the policy target
      to achieve 20% of the power generated by renewable energy in 2025, but the intermittent
      and uncontrollable/unpredictable factors remain the important variables for the
      development. Therefore, application of energy storage systems modulation is considered
      one of the methods to solve the problem about the instability of renewable energy
      quickly, as the energy storage systems can help stabilize wind power or solar power,
      making it easier to integrate with the power grid or household power consumption, and
      accelerate decentralized or centralized energy development, and also increase the stability
      of the energy storage systems and deepen the demand for lithium-ion batteries. The
      application of the Company's lithium-ion battery modules constitutes an important part of
      the development of green industry. The lithium-ion batteries deliver the advantages of
      high energy density, and the price thereof is gradually accepted by consumers due to the
      effect produced by mass production. They may be applied to the battery market demand
      for the different industries including, hand-held power tools, energy storage systems and
      electric vehicles extensively. Therefore, they have become the mainstream in the power
      secondary battery market. Besides this, the lithium-ion batteries are expected to become
      the mainstream in the market relying on the excellent performance. However, due the
      factors including heavy metal pollution, heavy weight and low power density, the lead-

                  Energy conservation and carbon reduction, development of new energy and

                  emerged accordingly and the battery technology related to the green industry became
                  the highest-profile industry. In recent years, the wind power and solar power
                  generation technology has become matured increasingly in the world. Meanwhile, the

                  to create more business opportunities for the secondary battery industry in the future.

            B. Concept about alternative energy and emerging electric vehicles

                  In recent years, the concept about green and alternative energy has become more and
                  more popular. Europe and China are using their best effort to develop the electric
                  vehicle industry, in order to improve the energy problem and alternative energy for
                  sustainable development. Benefiting from the development and growth of the electric
                  vehicle industry, the demand for battery materials needed by electric vehicles is
                  increasingly expanding. The governments of various countries have successively
                  announced the schedule for termination of fuel vehicles. New energy vehicles, such
                  as electric vehicles, have been agreed commonly by the world for the purpose of

      exit the market successively in the next three decades, and new energy vehicles, such
      as electric vehicles, are the global consensus and drive the new trend for long-term
      development in the world. Among other things, China will be the largest electric
      vehicle market in the world. The government of China has identified the new energy
      vehicles as the strategic emerging industry, and is researching and preparing the
      schedule to ban the sale of traditional gasoline and diesel vehicles, in order to move
      toward the electric vehicle industry, which is more eco-friendly.

      The internal cells in the Company's lithium-ion battery modules consist of multi-series
      parallel connected application requirements, the product specifications are diversified.
      The Company is a manufacturer engaged in production of medium- and high-power
      lithium-ion battery modules. In consideration of the higher technical threshold for the
      power management system's integration abilities required by the medium- and high-
      power batteries, in order to maintain the stability of product charging and discharging,

      testing equipment. The Company can also provide customers with complete
      customized solutions, including lithium-ion battery module certification, circuit

      competitiveness and values reside.

(2) Negative factors and responsive measures

      A. The raw materials of the battery are concentrated in Japan and Korea.

            The main raw materials needed by the Company are lithium-ion and lithium polymer
            battery cells. Notwithstanding, the battery cells rely on the suppliers based in Japan
            and Korea, while only few manufacturers are engaged in production of the same in
            Taiwan, with lower output volume. Therefore, most of the raw materials still need to
            be imported from foreign countries and, therefore, are affected by factors such as
            delivery date, price and sufficient supply of raw materials and supplies. Besides this,
            in consideration of the rapid growth of lithium-ion battery energy storage systems
            application fields (servers and data centers) in recent years. As a result, the supply will
            become tight and, coupled with the biggest issue of cost competitiveness of Chinese
            manufaturers faced by Japanese and Korean manufacturers, pose a certain degree of
            risk to operations.

            Responsive measures

            (A) The Company's procurement policy for battery cells is decided subject to the
                 shipment in the year and also in reference to the forecast provided by major
                 customers, plus the Company's own estimates. Then, the estimated purchase
                 quantity will be notified to the battery cell manufacturers.

            (B) The Company's main customers are all world-renowned manufacturers with great
                 demand for battery cells. Therefore, if the Company negotiates with battery cell
                 manufacturers for the quantity relying on their group resources, they may
                 guarantee the supply of goods even if there is a lack of supply in the battery cell
                 market, and also ensure that the shipment of the Company's battery modules to
                 customers would not be affected by the short supply of battery cells.

      B. Insufficient sources of R&D personnel

            Responsive measures

            (A) Exercise the abundant experience accumulated by the Company R&D design team
                 in the lithium-ion battery cell field for more than one decade, lead new employees
                 to integrate into the team's operation in a short time, and extensively recruit
                 foreign talents.

            (B) The Company will train professionals through the industry- academia technical
                 exchanges, and provide the Company with steady human resources.

      C. Product characteristics reside in HMLV production, increasing the difficulty and risk
            over inventories.

            Considering that the Non-IT lithium-ion battery module industry in which the
            Company is engaged involves multiple product types, batch production and multiple
            standards, including EU standards, US standards, and Japanese standards, the
            customers require quality strictly. The Non-IT lithium-ion battery module industry's
            production process usually needs to be completed in multiple steps. In order to ensure
            the product quality, customers are used to tracking the source of raw materials and
            production process. The Company shall not alter the raw materials and supplies
            already certified by customers, but need to prepare the materials based on the forecast

                                                                71
                  provided by customers. In recent years, due to the short supply of active components
                  and battery cells, the Company has prepared materials in advance. Therefore, the
                  Company's risk over inventory control has increased.
                  Responsive measures
                  (A) The Company regularly contacts customers, and decides the time to place the

                       order for raw materials and spare parts, and the quantity thereof, based on the
                       order forecast provided by customers. The Company will verify the orders placed
                       by customers voluntarily and check the changes in stock regularly, in order to
                       maintain the optimal stock and reduce the possibility of overstock.
                  (B) The Company secures a stable supply of battery cells with the assistance of
                       customers, and ensures that they may receive the battery modules successfully
                       without experiencing supply shortage.
                  (C) If the supply of some important raw materials becomes tight due to the emerging
                       electric vehicle market, the Company will also prepare materials in advance per
                       the request of customers, and sign an agreement with customers to promise the
                       repurchase of the residual materials for certain products, in an attempt to transfer
                       the risk over inventory control.

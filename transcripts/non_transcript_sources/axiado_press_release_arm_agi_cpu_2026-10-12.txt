Axiado Collaborates with Arm to Deliver AI-Driven Security and Platform Management for the Arm AGI CPU Platform
Axiado DC-SCM integration brings hardware-anchored security and intelligent management to Arm’s first production silicon for agentic AI; demonstration at OCP Global Summit 2026
SAN JOSE, Calif. – October 12, 2026 – Axiado Corporation, a leader in AI-driven platform security and intelligent infrastructure management, today announced a collaboration with Arm to integrate Axiado’s Data Center Secure Control Module (DC-SCM) cards into the Arm® AGI CPU server platform. The companies will showcase the integration at the OCP Global Summit, October 12-15, 2026, at the San Jose Convention Center.
Built on Arm Neoverse® cores and designed for agentic AI infrastructure, the Arm AGI CPU is the company’s first data center processor and production silicon in its 35-year history.
A new class of AI server platform needs a new class of management and security architecture. As AI data centers scale, the baseboard management and root-of-trust functions that sit beneath the host CPU have become critical to uptime, fleet operations, and protection against firmware, supply chain, and runtime attacks. Axiado’s Trusted Control/Compute Unit (TCU) combines BMC, hardware root of trust, and inference acceleration in a single chip, giving operators hardware-rooted security, real-time control, platform management, and on-chip AI from the first instruction at power-on.
Under the collaboration, Arm and Axiado engineering teams are integrating Axiado’s OCP-compliant DC-SCM cards, powered by the Axiado TCU, with the Arm AGI CPU platform. The integration is based on industry standards and protocols such as OpenBMC, OCP DC-SCM and DC-SCI, and Redfish, enabling rapid deployment of Axiado’s technology on this new platform.
“Agentic AI is creating a new class of always-on compute demand, requiring infrastructure that can increase performance while strengthening security throughout the system. Our collaboration with Axiado brings intelligent platform management and strengthens the hardware-rooted security on the Arm AGI CPU platform and helping customers build secure, scalable infrastructure,” said Eddie Ramirez, Vice President of Go-to-Market, Cloud AI at Arm.
“Arm’s move into data center silicon with the Arm AGI CPU is a significant moment for the industry, and an innovative new platform deserves an equally innovative approach to management and security,” said Gopi Sirineni, Founder, President, and CEO of Axiado. “Our TCU-based DC-SCM brings intelligent platform management, hardware root of trust, and advanced AI-driven security together on a single module. Working closely with Arm’s engineering team, we’re making these capabilities available to Arm AGI CPU customers as they build out the next generation of AI infrastructure.”
Demonstration at OCP Global Summit 2026
Axiado will show a video demonstration of its DC-SCM integration with the Arm AGI CPU server platform at booth #A1 during the OCP Global Summit. Live demonstrations will be available by appointment at Axiado’s San Jose headquarters. To schedule a meeting or live demo, contact sales@axiado.com.
About Axiado
Axiado is a semiconductor company adding platform intelligence to AI infrastructure. Axiado’s Trusted Control/Compute Unit (TCU) brings intelligence into the platform itself: a single SoC combining hardware-rooted security, real-time control, platform management, and on-chip AI. Delivered as Secure Control Modules in OCP standard form factors, Axiado silicon senses, decides, and acts on the platform in real time, making AI infrastructure more efficient, more secure, and more reliable. Founded in 2019, Axiado is headquartered in San Jose, California, with engineering centers in Taiwan, India, and Vietnam. Learn more at axiado.com.

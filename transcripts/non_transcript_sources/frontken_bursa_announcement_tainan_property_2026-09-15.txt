FRONTKEN CORPORATION BERHAD (“FCB” OR “THE COMPANY”)

ACQUISITION OF AN INDUSTRIAL LAND AND THE FACTORY BUILDINGS ERECTED 
THEREON BY ARES GREEN TECHNOLOGY CORPORATION ("AGTC"), A MAJOR 
SUBSIDIARY OF FCB, FROM GINWIN TECHNOLOGY CO., LTD.  (“VENDOR”) FOR A 
TOTAL CASH CONSIDERATION OF NTD 920,000,000 OR APPROXIMATELY RM 
118,161,120.  
 
1. INTRODUCTION 
 
The Board of Directors of FCB  wishes to announce that its major subsidiary, AGTC, 
had on 15 September 2026, successfully bidded for an industrial land together with the 
existing factory buildings  erected thereon (the “Property”) located in Tainan, Taiwan, 
for a total cash consideration of NTD 920,000,000 or approximately RM 118,161,120 
(“Purchase Consideration”) ("Acquisition"). 
 
2. DETAILS OF THE ACQUISITION 
 
2.1 Description of the Property  
 
The Property has a land area of approximately 16,172.00 m² and comprises of seven 
existing factory building blocks with an aggregate floor area of approximately 12,611.07 
m². The Property is located in the Tainan Xinying Industrial Zone, Taiwan, 
approximately 950 metres from AGTC’s existing P1 facility.  
 
The Property is classified as Type B Industrial Land with a b uilding coverage ratio of 
70% and a f loor area ratio of 210%. The Property also i ncludes a solar photovoltaic 
system with a capacity of 1,245.92 kWp. 
 
Based on the environmental soil testing conducted by the Vendor across 28 sampling 
points at depths ranging from 30 cm to 330 cm, the test results were reported to be 
within the applicable statutory standards.  
 
2.2 Details of the Vendor 
 
The vendor is Ginwin Technology Co., Ltd., a company incorporated in Taiwan. 
 
2.3 Basis and Justification of the Purchase Consideration 
 
The Property was offered for sale by way of public tender at a reserve price of NTD 
820,000,000 or approximately RM105,317,520. The tender closed on 14 September 
2026 and AGTC was awarded the tender on 15 September 2026 at a purchase 
consideration of NTD 920,000,000 or approximately RM118,161,120.  
 
 2 
The Purchase Consideration was arrived at after taking into consideration, among 
others, the location and size of the Property, the existing factory buildings and facilities, 
prevailing market conditions and the strategic proximity of the Property to AGTC’ s 
existing P1 facility.  
 
2.4 Salient Payment Terms 
 
The Purchase Consideration is to be satisfied entirely in cash, with an initial deposit of 
NTD 82,000,000 or approximately RM 10,531,752 payable upon the successful award 
of the tender . The remaining balance of NTD 838,000,000  or approximately RM 
107,629,368 will be paid in three (3) instalments in accordance with standard 
commercial land transaction practices in Taiwan. 
 
2.5 Liabilities to be Assumed 
 
There are no liabilities, contingent liabilities, or financial guarantees to be assumed by 
FCB or AGTC arising from the Acquisition. 
 
2.6 Source of Funding 
 
The Purchase Consideration will be fully satisfied through AGTC’s internally generated 
funds. 
 
3. RATIONALE AND PROSPECTS 
 
The Acquisition is in line with the Company’s  long-term strategy to support the 
expansion of AGTC’s operations and future capacity requirements. The Property is 
strategically located approximately 950 metres from AGTC’s existing P1 facility and will 
provide additional space for establishment of future production lines to meet 
anticipated market demand.  
 
The Acquisition is expected to provide AGTC with greater flexibility in planning for its 
expected future operational requirements and optimising the use of the Property in line 
with its business requirements as guided by its customers. Given the limited availability 
of suitable industrial land within close proximity to AGTC’s existing P1 facility, the 
Acquisition is expected to support AGTC’s continued business growth over the medium 
to long term.  
 
4. RISK FACTORS 
 
4.1 Non-Completion and Administrative Risk 
 
The completion of the Acquisition is subject to the fulfilment of all terms and conditions 
stipulated in the SPA including the completion of the requisite property title transfer and 
registration formalities in Taiwan. 
 
  
 3 
4.2 Operational Integration Risk  
 
The establishment of future production lines will be  subject to AGTC’s engineering 
setup requirements and the ability of its management to execute its business 
expansion plans.  
 
5. FINANCIAL EFFECTS 
 
5.1  Share Capital and Substantial Shareholders' Shareholdings  
 
The Acquisition will not have any material effect on FCB's issued share capital or 
substantial shareholders' shareholdings, as the Purchase Consideration will be 
satisfied entirely in cash and does not involve any issuance of new ordinary shares in 
FCB. 
 
5.2  Net Assets ("NA") per share and Gearing  
 
The Acquisition will be satisfied via internal ly generated funds and will not have any 
material impact on the consolidated NA, NA per share and gearing of FCB for the 
financial year ending 31 December 2026. 
 
5.3  Earnings Per Share ("EPS")  
 
The Acquisition is not expected to have any material effect on the consolidated 
earnings and EPS of the Group for the financial year ending 31 December 2026. Over 
the longer term, subject to the prevailing market conditions and the successful 
establishment and commencement of the additional production lines, the Acquisition 
is expected to contribute positively to the Company’s future earnings and EPS. 
 
6. HIGHEST PERCENTAGE RATIO 
 
The highest percentage ratio applicable to the Acquisition pursuant to Paragraph 
10.02(g) of the Main Market Listing Requirements ("MMLR")  of Bursa Malaysia 
Securities Berhad is 11.40%, calculated based on the Purchase Consideration 
compared to FCB's audited consolidated NA as at 31 December 2025.  
 
7. APPROVALS REQUIRED 
 
The Proposed Acquisition is not subject to the approval of the shareholders of the 
Company or any other relevant authorities/parties. 
 
8. DIRECTORS' AND SUBSTANTIAL SHAREHOLDERS' INTERESTS 
  
Insofar as the Board is aware, none of the Directors, substantial shareholders of FCB, 
or persons connected with them have any interest, direct or indirect, in the Acquisition.

The Board of Directors of FCB, having evaluated all aspects of the Acquisition

interest of the Company and its shareholders.

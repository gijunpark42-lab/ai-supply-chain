ArianeGroup: the Space and Defence Group

ArianeGroup includes ArianeGroup itself, consisting of a holding company and two national subsidiaries – ArianeGroup SAS in France and ArianeGroup GmbH in Germany – as well as six specialist space and defence subsidiaries. ArianeGroup is a French–German industrial group and a leading player in the field of European space launchers and defence systems. In addition to designing and delivering complete systems, ArianeGroup also directly supplies world-class products and equipment for space applications and launchers, and for defence and industry, together with a range of high value-added services. ArianeGroup is also a majority shareholder in subsidiaries specialising in space launch vehicles, launch services, and space and defence equipment and services. Our subsidiaries all have unique expertise and exceptional technological prowess in their respective fields and deliver state-of-the-art solutions for their customers.

100% Arianespace Participation SA

Arianespace has the dual purpose of guaranteeing independent access to space for Europe, and providing the global commercial market with competitive space transportation services. The company delivers customer-tailored solutions, providing access to all orbits for all missions and space applications. Arianespace operates both the two-booster and the four-booster versions of Ariane 6, and the Vega-C launcher from Europe’s Spaceport in Kourou, French Guiana. To serve its customer base worldwide, Arianespace has subsidiaries in Washington and Singapore, and an office in Tokyo.

MaiaSpace offers space mobility solutions using the Maia launcher. The company is designing, developing and testing Maia under an accelerated Agile model approach. A focus on sustainability is a hallmark right from the drawing board via a systematic life cycle analysis and an integrated eco-design approach. MaiaSpace aims to be operating the first European reusable and configurable mini-launcher by 2026, addressing current and future needs. Its range of launch and in-orbit services are precision-matched to customer requirements for low Earth orbit, sun-synchronous orbit and higher energy orbits.

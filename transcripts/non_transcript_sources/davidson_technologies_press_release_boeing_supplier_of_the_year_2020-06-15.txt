Supplier of the Year – June, 2020
June 15, 2020, Huntsville, AL – Davidson Technologies is pleased to announce that we have been named 2019 Boeing Supplier of the Year in the Support and Services category. The Boeing Supplier of the Year is one of the company’s official programs to recognize suppliers who achieve the high-performance standards necessary to meet customer expectations and remain competitive in the global economy.
“We are honored to receive such a prestigious award. We will continue to strive for excellence in supporting every aspect of our enduring relationship with Boeing.” – Maj. Gen. John W. Holly, (Ret.), President of Davidson Technologies.

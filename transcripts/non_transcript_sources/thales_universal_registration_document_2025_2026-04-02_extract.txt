[page 29] 2.1.1.3.1 General overview A joint venture between Thales (67%) and Leonardo (33%), Thales Alenia Space is a space manufacturer that provides high-tech solutions for telecommunications, navigation, Earth observation including environmental monitoring, space exploration, science and orbital infrastructures. Together with Telespazio, Thales Alenia Space forms the Space Alliance, which proposes a complete range of solutions including services. Thales Alenia Space offers solutions to commercial, institutional and defence markets.

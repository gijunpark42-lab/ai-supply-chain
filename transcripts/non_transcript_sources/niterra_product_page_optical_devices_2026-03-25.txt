NTK CERAMIC delivers packages featuring exceptional airtightness, heat dissipation, and dimensional stability to enable higher power output and longer device life. Our robust structures resist heat, impact, and environmental changes, making them the preferred choice for mission-critical applications. Package Lineup LCC (Leadless Chip Carrier) Applications and Key Fields LEDs and LDs are indispensable in diverse fields, including automotive, displays, medical, and optical communications. With the advancement of AI and autonomous driving, devices now require even greater miniaturization, higher power output, and enhanced efficiency. NTK CERAMIC meets these demands with packages featuring superior heat dissipation and environmental resistance. We provide technologies that balance long-term stable operation with high optical precision, even under the most demanding conditions. Key Applications LEDs Laser Diodes (LD) Product Features and Strengths 1. Reliability & Thermal Management Ceramic packages are essential for the long-term performance of LEDs and LDs. By matching the Coefficient of Thermal Expansion (CTE) and ensuring high airtightness, we minimize stress and prevent corrosion. Unlike resin packages prone to thermal deformation, our high-rigidity ceramics guarantee precise optical alignment, providing the ideal solution for high-power, compact devices. 2. Advanced Heat Dissipation Leveraging decades of expertise, NTK CERAMIC delivers proprietary material solutions that exceed industry standards. For LEDs: Our thick-film copper electrode structures significantly improve thermal conductivity compared to conventional alumina/CuW designs. For LDs: Our LCC packages with copper heat sinks have successfully replaced standard AlN solutions. These technologies achieve both higher power output and exceptional reliability. 3. High Precision & Custom Design The superior dimensional stability of ceramics prevents optical misalignment. We support a wide range of custom geometries, including LCC and butterfly types. From the design phase, we optimize thermal resistance, light distribution, and mounting structures to deliver high-precision packaging that meets the stringent requirements of the optical industry. Initiatives for the Next Generation As optical devices evolve, demands for miniaturization, better heat dissipation, and cost reduction are rising simultaneously. NTK CERAMIC is developing innovative hybrid structures that integrate the strengths of both ceramics and metals, focusing on practical solutions for high-power LEDs and LDs that require superior flatness and thermal management. By accelerating the development of these high-performance packages, we aim to enhance our customers' product performance. We remain committed to leading the way in next-generation optical packaging through continuous technical innovation.

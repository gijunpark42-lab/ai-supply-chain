May 7, 2026 
KANTO DENKA KOGYO Co., Ltd. 
 
Notice of Adoption of KSG®-5 
 
KSG-5, produced at the Shibukawa Plant (Gunma Prefecture) of KANTO DENKA KOGYO 
Co., Ltd., was selected through the joint development of a low-environmental-impact etching 
gas with Samsung Semiconductor in Korea, and has been adopted since 2025 as “G3”, a CF₄ 
replacement gas for the company. 
KSG-5 has a global warming potential (GWP) of less than 1, while providing etching 
performance equivalent to CF₄ (GWP 7,380) and CHF₃ (GWP 14,600) for silicon-containing 
films. It also has the ability to suppress etching of mask materials, contributing to improved 
productivity. 
We will continue our development of products that combine both high performance for 
semiconductor manufacturing processes and low environmental impact, thereby 
contributing to the realization of a sustainable society. 
 
KSG® Series 
The KSG® series under development by our company was created with the concept of 
addressing customer challenges and achieving a sustainable society. 
To respond to technology trends in the semiconductor industry and evolving customer needs, 
we are developing new materials by combining our core technologies—chemical synthesis and 
materials evaluation—with materials informatics. 
KSG®, developed through our proprietary technology, achieves both high performance for 
semiconductor manufacturing processes and low environmental impact.

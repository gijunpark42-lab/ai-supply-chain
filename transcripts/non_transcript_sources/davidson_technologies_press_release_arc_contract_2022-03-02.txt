Stay Lifted: DTechLogic to Begin Work on Missile Defense Agency’s ARC Contract
March 2, 2022 Huntsville, AL – Davidson is pleased to announce a 30-month stay-of-performance on the MDA
Advanced Research Center (ARC) Contract has been officially lifted. DTechLogic LLC., a Huntsville-based
joint venture between Davidson Technologies, Inc. and nLogic, LLC. will proceed with work on the contract
immediately. Subcontractors include ASRC Federal, Qualis Corporation, and Technology Service Corporation
(TSC). This contract, with a total value of $146M will operate with a five-year base and 3 option years.
“We are pleased to support this critical asset for MDA,” says Davidson’s President, CEO & Chairman, MG
John W. Holly (USA, Ret.). “We intend to provide the highest quality performance to ensure the ARC can
directly support MDA’s mission.”
“We are pleased to have formed a true partnership with Davidson,” says nLogic CEO, Tim Thornton. Thornton
worked at Davidson for 6 years under the mentorship of Drs. Julian and Dorothy Davidson before starting
nLogic. “Davidson awarded us our first contract when we started the company and this is a special win for
nLogic,” Thornton says.

Executive Summary
FY2026 H1 results
Impact of the 
situation in the 
Middle East on 
business operations
FY2026 forecasts
− The Group has no production or sales sites in the region, and sales exposure is minimal, accounting 
for less than 0.1% of consolidated net sales in the previous fiscal year. Accordingly, the direct 
business impact is limited.
− Continue to monitor the impact on orders already received of supply constraints and rising costs for 
certain materials resulting from higher crude oil prices and disruptions in international logistics, as 
well as trends in customers' investment plans amid increasing uncertainty in the global economy.
− Supported by the continued strong business environment, particularly in cleanroom systems, we 
revised upward our full-year earnings forecast for orders, sales, and all profit items. All of these are 
expected to reach new record highs. The Company has also increased the annual dividend forecast to 
¥90 per share to further enhance shareholder returns.
− Orders, sales, operating income, ordinary income, and net income attributable to shareholders of the 
parent company reached new record highs for the first half of the fiscal year. Sales and all profits 
exceeded the upwardly revised forecast announced in May.
− Orders remained strong, driven primarily by cleanroom systems for semiconductor production lines, 
and order backlogs exceeded JPY 740 billion, reaching a new record high.
− Operating margin improved, driven by higher sales as well as progress in production efficiency and 
cost reduction efforts.

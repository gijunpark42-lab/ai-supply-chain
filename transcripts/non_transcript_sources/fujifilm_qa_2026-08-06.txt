Higuchi: This is Higuchi. I will explain the consolidated results of FUJIFILM Holdings Corporation for Q1 of the 
fiscal year ending March 2027. 
Revenue for Q1 was JPY826.5 billion, operating income was JPY51.2 billion, and net income attributable to 
FUJIFILM Holdings was JPY37.4 billion. 
Revenue reached a record high for Q1. Growth was driven by strong performance in the Healthcare segment, 
particularly endoscopes, as well as solid sales in the Electronics segment, including Semiconductor Materials 
such as CMP slurry, and data tapes. Foreign exchange effects also contributed to revenue growth. 
Operating income increased in the E lectronics and I maging segments. However, overall operating income 
declined, reflecting higher fixed costs in the B io CDMO business, one-time expenses associated with 
strengthening the organizational structure in the Business Innovation segment, and higher raw material costs. 
I will now explain our full-year consolidated forecasts. 
We forecast revenue of JPY3,560 .0 billion, reflecting strong sales in the E lectronics segment and favorable 
foreign exchange effects. 
Our operating income forecast remains unchanged. While we expect higher profit in the Electronics segment, 
this is expected to be offset by a slower profit ramp -up in the B io CDMO business and the impact of higher 
semiconductor memory prices. 
We continue to forecast record highs in revenue, operating income, and net income attributable to FUJIFILM 
Holdings.

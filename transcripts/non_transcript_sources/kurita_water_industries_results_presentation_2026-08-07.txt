Summary
YoY change rate  Orders: +77.1%, Net Sales: +14.3%, Business Profit: +7.8%1Q Results
⚫ Order intake progressed at a high level, supported by the acquisition of large facility projects in East Asia, and  
the United States.
⚫ Net sales increased due to solid performance in facilities and recurring contract-based services, while business 
profit declined due to additional costs incurred in a specific facility project.
Topics
⚫ FY 03/2027 business forecast revised to reflect the gain recognized upon completion of the share transfer of Pentagon 
Technologies Group, Inc. in 1Q.
⚫ Share repurchase program implemented pursuant to the Board resolution on May 14, 2026 (up to 5 million shares and ¥35.0 
billion). As of July 31, 1.85 million shares have been repurchased for ¥16.1 billion.

Q&A for Fiscal 2026 First Quarter Results Briefing 
Three months ended June 30, 2026 
 
Q: Regarding the status of FC-BGAs in the Electronics segment, when looking at the operating profit margin 
for the Electronics segment, I see that it has risen significantly QoQ. I would appreciate it if you could explain 
the reasons behind this, including whether there were one -time factors. Also, regarding the recognition of 
advanced semiconductor packaging costs, you mentioned there was some revision of the accounting 
treatment applied this time. How much of that was actually recorded in Q1? 
A: First, regarding FC -BGAs, we launched a new production line last fiscal year  and expected to see gradual 
improvements to takt time. However, these improvements are progressing faster than anticipated, and we 
have already achieved near -full capacity in Q1.  As a result, we have been able to secure AI-switch-related 
business, which has led to an improvement in our profit margins. 
Regarding development expenses  for advanced semiconductor packaging, there are two reasons why we 
forecast a decrease for the current period. 
One reason involves the portion outsourced to universities, which is funded by a grant from NEDO. Initially, 
we had budgeted this as an operating expense. However, the direct payment of the NEDO subsidy to the 
universities reduces our operating expenses by  that amount, resulting in an increase in operating profit of 
JPY1.7 billion per year. 
Another factor is the reduction in depreciation expenses. Due to the notification of grant approval of the 
NEDO subsidy being later than expected, installing equipment is now expected to be delayed by about half a 
year. While we will be outsourcing that portion of the work  so there will be no delay in development , as 
depreciation expenses will be delayed, expenses will be JPY1.2 billion lower than originally planned.  
On an annual basis, this will result in a combined reduction of JPY2.9  billion in depreciation expenses and in 
outsourcing expenses. The combined impact on Q1 profit was an increase in profits of JPY1 billion. 
Q: Regarding FC-BGAs, comparing also to past data, I believe the profit margin was high. Can this level of profit 
margin be maintained from Q2 onward? Also, regarding depreciation expenses, since depreciation will shift 
to a later period, can we assume that depreciation will be recognized as planned from FY2027 onward? 
A: I believe we can maintain our profit margin . As for  depreciation, as it  will begin to accrue as scheduled 
starting in H2, that depreciation will be recognized as planned in the next fiscal year and beyond.

Q: The Living & Industry segment profit level for Q1 was high. I would like to know whether there are generally 
no one-time factors involved, and whether it will be possible to maintain relatively high profit margins going 
forward. Looking at YoY figures, the operating profit margins were at the same level on a GAAP basis. However, 
last fiscal year's results show that margins peaked at that level and then declined slightly. I would like to ask 
whether this level can be maintained for the current fiscal year. 
A: The valuation gains on inventory are temporary in nature. They will turn into losses at some point.  On 
another note, market conditions were somewhat challenging in the last fiscal year  in the North American 
packaging market, but as we are currently seeing recovery in the market, I believe we can continue to achieve 
the JPY6 billion from the American packaging business seen in this quarter. 
Also, while the other JPY2 billion may be partly due to orders being brought forward, the effects of our 
investments—primarily overseas—are clearly paying off, and as we’re also beginning to see some synergies 
emerging within that, we expect this trend to continue for the most part. We do, however, need to somewhat 
further assess the impact of those brought-forward orders. 
Q: Regarding the inventory valuation, you mentioned that there would be a JPY2 billion gain, which would be 
offset as the situation stabilizes. Is it possible that this won’t occur during th e current fiscal year, depending 
on the situation in the Middle East?

what will happen in the future, so it’s hard to make a definitive judgment at this point. Depending on the 
circumstances, as you say, it may not occur this fiscal year. It will occur at some point, though.

Q: Regarding the FC -BGA segment that is now operating at nearly full capacity, consider ing the upside 
potential going forward, is there still room for sales growth ? A lso, can we  assume that by improving the  
product mix, we could also see higher profitability? 
A: We have reached full capacity in Q1. As there will be no capacity expansion this fiscal year, the next period 
that we will be looking to grow sales will be in 2027, when our new production line in Singapore comes online.  
We’ve already made significant progress in improving the mix, so while there may be some further 
improvements down the line, we plan to proceed with the current mix from Q2 onward. While there will be 
some impact from the introduction of AI ASICs and similar factors, we generally expect that, in Q2 and beyond, 
levels will remain at the Q1 level.

has generally brought increases in unit prices for your company, on a like-for-like basis for the same products, 
have price adjustments or increases occurred or are they likely to occur in the future? 
A: I believe that, in principle, we are operating effectively based on market prices . O f course  there are 
discussions on the matter, but we have not yet incorporated any further price increases into our plan.

A: That is correct.

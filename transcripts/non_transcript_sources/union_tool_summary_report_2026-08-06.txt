Financial Performance 
 
(1) Consolidated Financial Results for the First Half of Fiscal Y ear Ending December 31, 2026   
During the first half of the fiscal year ending December 31, 2026, the Japanese economy remained on a gradual 
recovery path. However, the outlook remained uncertain due to concerns over the potential impact of heightened 
tensions in the Middle East. In addition, crude oil, energy, and raw material prices increased, while uncertainty over 
logistics conditions continued. As a result,  concerns remained that higher import costs could weigh on corporate 
earnings and the real income of households. In the electronics industry, demand for data centers, particularly for AI 
servers, increased significantly, driven by expanded AI infrastructure investment by hyperscalers. Demand for 
industrial equipment, such as factory automation equipment and semiconductor manufacturing equipment, also 
remained firm, supported by the expansion of such investment. 
Against this backdrop, demand for our Group’s high- quality products remained strong. To meet this growing 
demand, we flexibly strengthened our production system and expanded  supply capacity by optimizing the Group’s 
production structure. As a result, net sales and operating profit increased significantly from the same period of the 
previous fiscal year. In addition to higher sales of high- quality products, lower production costs resulting from 
expanded production capacity and higher utilization rates contributed to stronger earnings and high profit margins. 
Net sales for the first half of FY12/2026 were ¥27,055 million (YoY +48.3%). Operating profit was ¥7,595 million 
(YoY +81.4%), ordinary profit ¥8,034 million (YoY +109.4%), and profit attributable to owners of parent  ¥5,962 
million (YoY +108.7%). Both net sales and operating profit reached record highs for the first half of a fiscal year. 
In Japan, strong demand for high- quality products continued, supported by growing demand in generative 
AI-related markets. To meet this demand, we expanded production capacity by  launching a satellite plant and 
reviewing factory layouts. As a result, net sales (including intersegment transaction amount) were  ¥17,287 million 
(YoY +41.5%). Operating profit was ¥4,477 million (YoY +101.1％). 
In Asia excluding Japan, demand for package  substrates for data center servers and high -layer-count PCBs 
continued to grow above the previous year’s level, mainly in China and Taiwan. In addition to expanding 
production capacity at local production sites, utilization rates improved through stronger  coordination among sites. 
As a result,  net sales  were ¥ 17,626 million (YoY + 63.2%), and the operating profit wa s ¥3,048 million (YoY 
+131.5%). 
In North America, net sales were ¥ 1,295 million (YoY +40.3%), and operating profit amounted to ¥74 million 
(YoY +19.6%). In Europe, net sales and operating profit were ¥ 1,471 million (YoY +16.6%) and ¥56 million (YoY 
-16.6%), respectively.

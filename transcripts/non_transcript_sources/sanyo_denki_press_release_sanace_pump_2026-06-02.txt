SANYO DENKI Launches 
San Ace Pump
 for Liquid Cooling Systems with High Flow, Low Power Consumption, and Long Life

June 2, 2026

SANYO DENKI CO., LTD. has launched the 
San Ace Pump
, a liquid cooling system pump that delivers high flow, low power consumption, and long life.
The product helps cool data center and AI infrastructure applications with increasingly demanding thermal requirements.

Features

1. High Flow and High Head
(1)

The product delivers a maximum flow rate of 160 L/min and a maximum head of 32 m (H
2
O). It helps increase the cooling capacity of liquid cooling systems used in data centers.

2. High Efficiency

The product maintains high-flow performance with a power consumption of just 648 W at maximum flow. This contributes to improved energy efficiency in data centers.

3. Long Service Life

The product offers an expected life of 70,000 hours (approximately eight years).
(2)
 This helps reduce maintenance costs and supports reliable, continuous operation of liquid cooling systems.

(1) Head is the total height through which a pump can move a liquid, including both suction lift and discharge lift.

(2) Based on operation with water at rated voltage under continuous operation at maximum flow, with ambient and fluid temperatures of 45°C.

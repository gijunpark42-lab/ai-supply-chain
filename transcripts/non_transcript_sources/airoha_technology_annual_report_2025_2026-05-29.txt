I. Letter to shareholders

Dear Shareholders,

In 2025, the global macroeconomic environment faced challenges from tariff and exchange rate
fluctuations. Nevertheless, Airoha Technology achieved full-year operating revenue of NT$20.926
billion through customer expansion and product portfolio optimization, representing a 9.4% increase
compared to operating revenue of NT$19.122 billion in the prior year. In terms of profitability, net
profit after tax for 2025 reached NT$2.825 billion, an 8.2% increase from NT$2.611 billion in the
prior year. EPS were NT$17.32, up 7.6% from NT$16.10 in the prior year, reflecting steady overall
profit growth.

Airoha Technology has ventured into two major fields: "Wireline Infrastructure of Fixed Broadband
Network" and "Wireless Edge AI (Edge AI)." To enhance competitive barriers, the Company is
committed to the research and development of high-value chips and the deployment of advanced
technologies. Through years of accumulated technical expertise and global expansion strategies,
Airoha Technology aims to establish a more diverse global customer portfolio.

In the area of Wireline Infrastructure of Fixed Broadband Network, MediaTek's Wi-Fi 7 network
communication chips and Airoha Technology's 10G-PON broadband chips have been adopted by
multiple tier-one and tier-two global operators over recent years and entered mass production in 2025.
Concurrently, at the Network X 2025 exhibition in Paris, France, Airoha Technology collaborated
with MediaTek to showcase technological advances in integrating AI applications into fiber optic
gateways. In Ethernet products, the synergies from group resource integration have become
increasingly evident. Airoha Technology focuses on 1G/2.5G physical layer chips and 1G switch
single-chip solutions with cloud management capabilities, continuing to advance product upgrades
while combining IC Plus Corp.'s power over Ethernet technology to increase market share.
Additionally, the 10G physical layer chip developed in collaboration with Aeonsemi has commenced
shipments. In optical communication products for AI-related applications, Airoha Technology
supplies single-lane 50G transmission rate PAM4-DSP chips supporting speeds up to 400G, serving
multiple global cloud service providers. Furthermore, the single-lane 100G transmission rate PAM4-
DSP chip supporting speeds up to 800G officially entered the tape-out stage in Q4 2025, as the
Company continues to expand its position in higher-speed transmission technologies.

In Wireless Edge AI, Airoha Technology holds a leading position in the Bluetooth audio, with
technology development closely tracking the evolution of Bluetooth standards toward the 6.0
specification. The Company specializes in high transmission stability, low latency, and low power

consumption, with applications extending from gaming headsets and business headsets to emerging
end-use applications such as hearing aids and assistive listening devices. Notably, in 2025, the
Company's hearing aid/assistive listening products were launched in collaboration with five of the
world's top hearing aid brands as over-the-counter (OTC) hearing products. In satellite positioning,
the Company's high-precision inertial navigation fusion algorithms, combining sensor and satellite
positioning technologies and leveraging AI machine learning to enhance positioning accuracy,
maintain close collaboration with global brand customers in the wearable device space, while also
continuing to develop markets in low-earth orbit satellites, fleet management, industrial applications,
and automotive.
Looking ahead, Airoha Technology will focus on product portfolio optimization and technology
platform upgrades to deliver competitive products and serve as a reliable and trusted partner for its
customers. The Company will also effectively implement supply chain management, strategically
allocate human resources and capital to optimize operational efficiency, pursue sustainable
development and deepen corporate governance, and maintain steady growth to create shareholder
value.

        1.1.5 Diversity and Independence of the Board of Directors:

               (1)Diversity of the Board of Directors:
                  The Company has established a diversity policy for its Board of Directors.
                  According to this policy, the composition of the Board should reflect diversity
                  by selecting members with varied backgrounds and perspectives that align with
                  the Company's operations, business model, and development needs.
                  Consideration factors include, but are not limited to, gender, age, nationality,
                  culture, educational background, professional background (such as law,
                  accounting, industry, finance, marketing, or technology), professional skills, and
                  industry experience. Additionally, the criteria for nominating Board members
                  require that candidates possess a reputation for integrity and honesty,
                  demonstrate outstanding achievements, experience, and recognition in their
                  respective professional fields, commit to dedicating sufficient time to oversee
                  the Company's business, and possess the ability to assist in management.
                  Furthermore, they should contribute to the Company's success. For independent
                  directors, their qualifications must comply with legal requirements.
                  A. The Company prioritizes the diverse backgrounds of Board of Director. Our
                      specific management objectives encompass a broad range of cross-
                      disciplinary and complementary experiences and professional skills. The
                      Board of Directors should include at least one member with expertise in
                      accounting or finance, as well as at least one member with experience in
                      business management, in alignment with our management objectives.
                  B. The current board of directors of the Company consists of seven directors,
                      including one director employed by the Company (14%). Regarding the age
                      of directors, there are three directors aged between 51 and 60, and four aged
                      between 61 and 70. Their industry experiences cover various fields in the

(2)Independence of the Board:
   A. The Company has 7 directors, of which 4 are independent directors,
       accounting for 57% of the total board seats. To enhance the independence and
       operational efficiency of the Board of Directors, the Company has formulated
       the "Rules Governing the Scope of Powers of Independent Directors", in
       accordance with which the independent directors perform their duties. During
       their tenure of office, they have no relationship with management or related
       parties of the Company that would harm the interests of the Company or make
       unfair judgments. All four independent directors can independently and
       effectively supervise the operation of the Board of Directors.
   B. All directors of the Company have no spouses or relatives within the second
       degree of kinship with each other, and if the matters at the Board Meeting
       involve directors' interests, the directors shall avoid the discussion and vote
       of the bill to ensure that the Board of Directors can perform its duties
       independently and objectively.

1.3.1 Dividend Policy under the Articles of Association

       If there is any surplus in the Company's annual accounts, the Company shall first
       raise taxes to make up for the accumulated losses, and then list 10% as the statutory
       surplus reserve, unless the accumulated statutory surplus reserve has reached the
       total capital of the Company. Appropriation or reversal of the special reserve in
       accordance with the law or the regulations of the competent authority; the
       remaining balance is added to the accumulated undistributed surplus as dividends
       to shareholders, and the Board of Directors shall prepare a proposal for the
       distribution of earnings and submit it to the shareholders for approval.

       The dividend policy of the Company is formulated in conjunction with the current
       and future development plans, taking into account the investment environment,
       capital needs and domestic and foreign competition conditions, as well as the
       interests of shareholders and other factors, for which no less than 10% of the
       earnings available for distribution for the current year shall be allocated as a
       shareholders' dividend each year, but such dividend shall not be distributed under
       special circumstances or unless the accumulated earnings available for distribution
       is less than 10% of the paid-in capital; the shareholders' dividend shall be
       distributed in shares or cash, of which the cash dividend is no less than 10% of the
       total dividend.

       After the Company's public offering, dividends and bonuses distributed in cash, or
       legal reserve and capital surplus distributed in cash based on the original
       shareholding of the shareholders shall be adopted by the resolution of the Board of
       Directors attended by more than two-thirds of the Directors and agreed by the
       majority of Directors present, and shall be reported to the Shareholders' Meeting.

                   Airoha Technology is an IC design company focused on two major fields -
                   "Wireline Infrastructure of Fixed Broadband Network" and "Wireless Edge AI."

                   communications, and wireless communications application fields, respectively:

                   A. Overview of IC design industry

                      Integrated circuits (ICs) are ubiquitous in daily life. Common types of ICs
                      include CPUs, touch ICs, networking ICs, DRAM, and mobile SoCs. IC design,
                      as the name implies, primarily involves designing the functions, code,
                      components, and circuitry between components on each chip. Driven by the
                      significant surge in demand for artificial intelligence (AI) and high-performance
                      computing (HPC), the global semiconductor industry experienced strong growth
                      in 2025. According to forecasts by the World Semiconductor Trade Statistics
                      (WSTS) organization, the global semiconductor market was estimated at
                      US$772.2 billion in 2025, representing a year-over-year growth rate of 22.5%.

   2026, representing a growth rate of 26.3%.

B. Wireline Infrastructure of Fixed Broadband Network market overview

     The Company's wireline communications chips are widely used in artificial
     intelligence (AI) infrastructure, broadband networking (fiber optic), and
     Ethernet, among other fields. Driven by the rapid development of AI and cloud
     services markets, global data center data transmission demand has surged
     significantly. With cloud service providers (CSPs) accelerating the deployment
     of AI servers and large-scale data centers, demand for high-speed transmission
     and optical communications equipment has risen markedly, driving a strong
     growth trend in the optical communications industry.

     The networking market, driven by artificial intelligence (AI) infrastructure and
     telecom operators' demand for networking equipment upgrades, governments
     around the world are actively investing in networking infrastructure. Coupled
     with the continued push by global medium-to-large-scale operators to upgrade
     to 10G-PON standards, the overall broadband market has exhibited steady
     growth. Furthermore, as low-earth orbit satellite deployment enters an
     expansion phase, demand for ground receiving stations and terminal equipment
     is emerging, concurrently driving growth in the Ethernet chip market.

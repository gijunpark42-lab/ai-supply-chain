D-CiPHER Contract – June, 2019
June 12, 2019 Huntsville, AL – Davidson Technologies Inc., a Huntsville-based Woman-Owned small business, announces the award of the Developmental – Cyber Integration Penetration Hardening Execution Range (D-CiPHER) prime contract in support of the Missile Defense Agency (MDA). D-CiPHER delivers a simulated rapid-prototyping environment that allows for high-fidelity Cybersecurity training, critical asset security evaluations, and realistic threat modeling through event scenario creation that unveils hidden security vulnerabilities that would otherwise remain dormant.
Davidson Technologies Inc. is an employee-focused, award-winning, women-owned small business dedicated to strengthening our nation’s defense through programs that solve our customer’s toughest problems within the Missiles, Aerospace, Cyber, and Intelligence domains of expertise. We seek to build partnerships with government, industry, and academia to advance the state-of-the-art and redefine what is possible.

Customer Loyalty
[image: More Rewards]
More Rewards is one of Western Canada’s favorite loyalty programs with over 3.5 million members allowing customers to earn points through many partners including Save-On-Foods, RBC, Panago Pizza, the Jim Pattison Auto Group and many more. Customers can redeem for a wide variety of great products including groceries, gift cards, travel and more. More Rewards drives customers data and insights to enable digital engagement with personal offers which drive profitable sales growth for Pattison Food Group and our partners.

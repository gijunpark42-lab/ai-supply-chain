ABOUT US

AI is reshaping every industry, every economy, and every human endeavor. Nscale is building the advanced infrastructure, systems and solutions that enables practitioners, enterprises, and governments across the globe to create, deploy, and scale their most transformative AI systems.

We’re builders shaping the future of AI—and how we build defines us.

We push boundaries without fear, creating products that move the world forward.

We take full responsibility for our work, holding the bar high and delivering with urgency.

We value speed paired with openness to learn—no egos, only progress.

We operate transparently, earning trust through clear communication and consistent delivery.

OUR TEAM

## Our leadership team

Nscale is led by proven founders, engineers, strategists and builders with a track record of building and scaling successful technology ventures. With deep expertise across HPC, advanced data center design, and AI infrastructure—and the commercial acumen to bring breakthrough technologies to market—they are building the scalable, sustainable AI backbone that the world’s most ambitious builders need to shape the future.

![Image 2](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a82f7ca23e97c6371bbf5c9/6abe50fc151fd3f96e030909_Josh%20Payne-headshot.avif)

### Josh Payne

CEO and Founder

Josh is the CEO and Founder of Nscale, with a proven record of turning opportunities into high-growth businesses. An entrepreneur from an early age, he has built companies across recruitment, capital markets, and supercomputing infrastructure, consistently anticipating market shifts from GPUs to AI. With deep experience in energy, data centres, and hardware supply chains, Josh combines commercial discipline with visionary execution.

![Image 3](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a82f7ca23e97c6371bbf5c9/6abe5087599bf13c15867537_Alice%20Takhtajan-headshot.avif)

### Alice Takhtajan

Chief Financial Officer

Alice brings 25+ years of experience at JP Morgan, having led 150+ equity transactions in the Tech sector, working with venture, financial sponsors and corporate clients, raising both public and private capital

![Image 4](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a82f7ca23e97c6371bbf5c9/6ac2a7ee1029948848f9be23_Justin%20Osofsky_Nscale%20Headshot%20(1)%20Large.avif)

### Justin Osofsky

Chief Operating Officer

Justin Osofsky is Chief Operating Officer at Nscale, where he oversees global operations, including the company's People, EMEA, APAC, Business Development, Communications, Marketing, Global Policy, and M&A organisations. Before Nscale, he spent 18 years at Meta, most recently as Chief Partnerships Officer. Previously, he served as Chief Operating Officer of Instagram, scaling the business as it grew past 2 billion monthly users, as well as Vice President of Global Operations and Head of Small and Medium Business. Justin holds an MBA from Harvard Business School, a J.D. from Harvard Law School, and an A.B. from Harvard University.

![Image 5](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a82f7ca23e97c6371bbf5c9/6abe5158599bf13c15876334_Nidhi%20Chappell-headshot.avif)

### Nidhi Chappell

President AI Infrastructure

Seasoned AI Infrastructure expert with deep experience in building and scaling large-scale AI infrastructure. Nidhi is the Former Head of AI Infrastructure at Microsoft where she managed 2m GPUs and a former Senior Director of Data Centres at Intel

![Image 6](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a82f7ca23e97c6371bbf5c9/6abe51db82b5ccc1859ed56d_Sam%20Huckaby-headshot.avif)

### Sam Huckaby

President of Data Centers

Sam Huckaby is President of Data Centers at Nscale, with extensive experience in large-scale data center construction, capital deployment, and infrastructure delivery. He joins from Oracle, where as Senior Vice President he led global data center build and operations for Oracle Cloud Infrastructure. Prior to Oracle, Sam held senior roles at Vantage Data Centers, including COO for North America and Chief Development Officer in EMEA, delivering hyperscale campuses to the highest standards of uptime and energy efficiency.

![Image 7](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a82f7ca23e97c6371bbf5c9/6abe5180bc96eee40de8fac0_Philippe%20Sachs-headshot.avif)

### Philippe Sachs

President EMEA

Philippe's 25 years of experience span banking where he was focused on sovereigns, most recently setting up Standard Chartered Bank's Global Public Sector Business, as CEO of an educational company with over 500 employees in the UK, and a blockchain and deep tech venture capitalist, where he led the early investment rounds in Nscale.

![Image 8](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a82f7ca23e97c6371bbf5c9/6abe50ea0236a97368c76398_Jing%20Yin-headshot.avif)

### Jing Yin

President APAC

Jing is a senior technology executive with leadership roles across Alibaba Cloud, Ant Group, and Lazada where he oversaw major cloud, payments, and e-commerce initiatives across Asia. He brings deep expertise in global operations, supply chain, and commercial strategy

![Image 9](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a82f7ca23e97c6371bbf5c9/6abe506ddfef91a5a75c9308_Adam%20Rosman-headshot.avif)

### Adam Rosman

Chief Legal Officer

Adam Rosman is Chief Legal Officer at Nscale, where he leads the company's legal, regulatory, and governance functions. He brings more than 30 years of legal and operating experience across payments, financial services, and regulated industries. Most recently, Adam was Chief Legal and Administrative Officer at Fiserv. Before Fiserv, Rosman was Executive Vice President and General Counsel of OneMain Financial and, before that, at First Data, where he played a central role in its merger with Fiserv. He began his career in public service as a federal prosecutor in Washington, D.C., and as Deputy Staff Secretary to President Bill Clinton.

![Image 10](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a82f7ca23e97c6371bbf5c9/6abe5195a0c0233130ea53c4_Phoebee%20Gahan-headshot.avif)

### Phoebee Gahan

General Counsel

Phoebee Gahan is General Counsel at Nscale, where she oversees corporate law, capital markets, and complex commercial contracting across the fast-evolving AI and data infrastructure sector. She brings a unique mix of experience spanning M&A, finance, technology, and corporate governance, with a career that bridges global law firms, financial institutions, private equity, and advisory roles with startups and scale-ups

![Image 11](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a82f7ca23e97c6371bbf5c9/6abe5200590c8cc4df4018c0_Tom%20Burke-headshot.avif)

### Tom Burke

Chief Revenue Officer

Tom Burke is Chief Revenue Officer at Nscale, with over 15 years of experience leading global enterprise sales in data and AI. He previously served as CRO at Applied Digital (NASDAQ: APLD) and spent more than 15 years at IBM, most recently as the Vice President and Head of Global Sales for Data & AI. Earlier at IBM, he served as Chief of Staff for the Data & AI division, advising the General Manager and executive team on global strategy and operations. Tom brings a proven record of scaling international sales organisations, forging strategic partnerships, and driving growth across fast-moving technology markets

![Image 12](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a82f7ca23e97c6371bbf5c9/6abe50c9c4bb551278266a6f_David%20Power-headshot.avif)

### David Power

Chief Technology Officer

David Power is Chief Technology Officer at Nscale, with over 20 years of experience in high-performance computing (HPC). His expertise spans the full stack — from hardware and data centre design to workload orchestration. He has led organisations integrating supercomputers with cloud technology, designed and delivered supercomputing systems, and began his career developing HPC clusters, workload schedulers, and monitoring tools to support research in distributed genetic algorithms. David also served for a decade on the advisory board of the Irish Supercomputing List, advancing HPC adoption and standards.

![Image 13](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a82f7ca23e97c6371bbf5c9/6abe513f112a665aa5c22d6e_Naeem%20Hatimi-headshot.avif)

### Naeem Hatimi

Chief People Officer

Seasoned global People & Culture executive with deep experience scaling high-growth technology companies and leading organisational transformation across multiple markets including: SharkNinja, Netflix, Vodafone, and Microsoft

![Image 14](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a82f7ca23e97c6371bbf5c9/6abe51ac19a98c03b3c3e78d_Ron%20Huisman-headshot.avif)

### Ron Huisman

Chief Accounting & Financial Ops Officer

Ron Huisman is CAO at Nscale, where he leads financial operations and accounting. With over 25 years in finance, capital markets, and M&A, he brings deep expertise in scaling infrastructure-led businesses in fast-paced technology sectors. He previously held senior finance roles at Liberty Global, where he supported major investment and transformation programs, and served as CFO at AtlasEdge Data Centres, managing large-scale infrastructure across Europe.

![Image 15](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a82f7ca23e97c6371bbf5c9/6abe636aadfbe31876591860_Daniel%20Bathurst-headshot.avif)

### Daniel Bathurst

Chief Product Officer

Dan Bathurst is Chief Product Officer at Nscale, where he leads product and marketing across the company’s AI infrastructure stack. With over a decade of experience launching platforms across cloud, finance, AI, and Web3, he has helped shape Nscale’s GPU cloud services, built its distinctive brand, and driven partnerships with enterprise customers - with a focus on performance, scalability, and developer experience.

![Image 16](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a82f7ca23e97c6371bbf5c9/6abe50b216f69f3f9f4a9f46_Daniel%20Shapiro-headshot.avif)

### Daniel Shapiro

Chief Power Officer

Daniel serves as Chief Power Officer for Nscale, where he focuses on advancing Nscale's vertical integration strategy from energy and power to compute infrastructure.

![Image 17](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a82f7ca23e97c6371bbf5c9/6abe5121dfef91a5a75d2124_Lauren%20Hurwitz-headshot.avif)

### Lauren Hurwitz

Chief Digital & Information Officer

Lauren R Hurwitz is the Chief Digital & Information Officer, developing the operating systems, procedures, and cross-functional coordination needed to sustain operational agility and execution velocity at Nscale. Previously, she spent ten years at Palantir Technologies, where she scaled mission-critical operations across worldwide government and enterprise sectors as Head of Worldwide Public Sector.

![Image 18](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a82f7ca23e97c6371bbf5c9/6abe51c312a2881e6c6d37c0_Sagar%20Rawal-headshot.avif)

### Sagar Rawal

Chief Infrastructure Officer

Former Head of Infrastructure at Microsoft, Sagar brings a wealth of leadership experience to his role as Nscale’s Chief Infrastructure Officer. Prior to Microsoft, he led compute product management at OCI, where he focused on delivering flexible, scalable, and highly performant infrastructure. He previously held senior roles at NVIDIA and AWS

![Image 19](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a82f7ca23e97c6371bbf5c9/6abe510e7101bedae569e94e_Latha%20Maripuri-headshot.avif)

### Latha Maripuri

Chief Information Security Officer

Latha serves as CISO for Nscale where she oversees cyber, physical and supply chain security. A globally recognized engineering, product and security expert, she previously held executive roles at Uber, News Corp and IBM.

![Image 20](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a82f7ca23e97c6371bbf5c9/6abe51f17101bedae56a9354_Sam%20Sturgess-headshot.avif)

### Sam Sturgess

SVP, Strategy and Corporate Development

Sam Sturgess is SVP of Strategy & Corporate Development at Nscale, where he leads fundraising, corporate strategy, M&A, and high-impact strategic initiatives. He brings diverse experience across the AI and deep tech ecosystem, with a proven track record in driving corporate and commercial strategy, raising capital, and scaling technology ventures.

![Image 21](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a82f7ca23e97c6371bbf5c9/6abe516dc2f89d1ee1359066_Osvaldo%20Morales-headshot.avif)

### Osvaldo Morales

Chief Engineering Officer

Osvaldo is the Chief Engineering Officer Global DCI. He has a proven track record of data centre delivery having delivered 10GW+ across 200+ projects. Previously held senior leadership roles at Microsoft, AWS, Amazon, NVIDIA, and Digital Realty; and advisory roles at Partners Group and SoftBank.

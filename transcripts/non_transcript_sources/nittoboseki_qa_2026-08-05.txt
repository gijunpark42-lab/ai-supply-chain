Nittobo 
 
Main Questions and Answers at Financial Results Briefing  
For the First Quarter of the Fiscal Year Ending March 31, 2027 
（Held on August 5, 2026） 
 
 
Q: Regarding the upward revision of the full -year earnings forecast for FY2026: 
The improvement in the "Others" item compared to the operating profit forecast 
from May is significant. Could you break down the reasons behind this? 
A: First, the main factor is the effect of price revisions for commodity products—
primarily in the Insulation Materials—which was not factored into the initial plan. 
It also reflects the impact of expanded sales efforts in the Composite Materials, 
as well as the mitigation of risks related to production and sales decreases 
associated with supply chain disruptions due to the situation in the Middle East. 
 
Q: Regarding T -glass, we understand that demand for both thick and thin 
products growing stronger, and supply-demand conditions remain tight. If the 
capacity expansion plan running through 2028 is insufficient, are you 
considering additional capital investment? Also, if you make additional 
investments, will you implement price revisions alongside them? 
A: Demand for T-glass remains extremely strong. We are currently executing our 
capacity expansion plan through 2028 as scheduled, and as long as demand 
continues, we will prioritize allocating resources to T-glass. There are several 
ways to expand capacity—such as product line replacement or making major 
investments—and we are deliberating our future approach while considering 
medium- to long-term demand outlooks. If the opportunity is significant, we will 
not hesitate to execute capital investments. As for price revisions, they are not 
directly tied to investment decisions; rather, we will consider them at the timing 
when we are able to increase supply volume as a result of the investments. 
 
Q: How do you view the fact that semiconductor package manufacturers are 
starting to adopt low -CTE glass from sources other than Nittobo, and that 
competitors are increasing their production capacity? Please tell us about the 
competitive environment for T-glass. 
A: While T-glass continues to see persistent, strong demand, the effects of our 
capacity increases have not yet fully materialized, so we recognize that 
customers are considering the use of low -CTE glass from other suppliers. 
However, our competitive advantage in T-glass remains unchanged regarding 
the high quality required for semiconductor package substrates, and our 
primary goal right now is to steadily boost our supply capacity to maintain our 
market share. 
 
Q: Supply and demand for low -dielectric glass seem to be tightening. What is 
your outlook on the sales growth rate for NER-glass this fiscal year? Also, price 
hikes for low -dielectric glass cloth appear to be occurring within the supply 
chain; what is your stance on price revisions? 
A: Demand for NER -glass for AI servers is growing rapidly, and we plan to 
significantly increase sales volume this fiscal year compared to the previous

The Proposed Revised KBH Purchase Annual Cap and the Proposed KBH
Purchase Annual Caps under the New Materials Purchase Framework Agreement
were determined with reference to the internal projection of the purchases having
regard to (i) the historical amount of purchase of Chemicals for the production of
laminates by the Group from the KBH Group; (ii) the anticipated growth in demand
for Chemicals; (iii) the anticipated increase in market price of Chemicals; and (iv)
inflation.
The purchase from the KBH Group for the year ending 31 December 2026 are
estimated to be approximately HKD939,252,000 by annualising the sales amount for
the eight months ended 31 August 2026. The increase in purchase from the KBH
Group during the year ending 31 December 2026 was mainly due to the increase in
the production volume of the laminates and related upstream materials products
required more quantity of the chemicals used in the production and also the increase
in the market selling price of the chemical products purchased from the KBH Group.
The Proposed Revised KBH Purchase Annual Cap and the Proposed KBH
Purchase Annual Caps under the New Materials Purchase Framework Agreement
are calculated based on: (i) for the Proposed Revised KBH Purchase Annual Cap
for the year ending 31 December 2026, the estimated figure by straight-line
multiplication from the actual transactions during the eight months ended 31
August 2026 plus a buffer of 25%; and (ii) for the Proposed KBH Purchase Annual
Caps for the years ending 31 December 2027 and 2028, an annual growth rate of
20%.
The 25% buffer takes into account: (i) upward commodity price trends during
the eight months ended 31 August 2026, in particular, the crude oil price (a key
upstream cost driver for Chemicals) increased by approximately 32.5% between
January and August 2026 and the purchasing price index for raw chemicals
published by the National Bureau of Statistics of China increased by approximately
11.5% in mid-2026, indicating continued upward pressure on Chemical prices for
the remaining four months of 2026; (ii) the fact that the average purchase price of
Chemicals in September 2026 was higher than the average purchase price for the
eight months ended 31 August 2026, such that the straight-line annualised figure for
the eight months ended 31 August 2026 may understate the expected transaction
amounts for the year ending 31 December 2026; and (iii) volume growth from
increased laminate production, which requires a corresponding increase in the
quantity of Chemicals to be purchased.
The 20% annual growth rate reflects: (i) projected volume growth of
approximately 7.5% to 10%, having regard to the expected increase in the Group’s
laminate production capacity of approximately 10%; (ii) anticipated commodity
price increases of approximately 8% to 10%, having regard to the 11.5% increase in
the purchasing price index for raw chemicals in mid-2026; and (iii) general cost
inflation of approximately 2% to 3%.
Having considered the above, the Directors (including the INEDs) are of the
view that the Proposed Revised KBH Purchase Annual Cap and the Proposed KBH
Purchase Annual Caps under the New Materials Purchase Framework Agreement
are fair and reasonable.

The Proposed Revised KBH Supply Annual Cap and the Proposed KBH
Supply Annual Caps under the New Supply Framework Agreement were
determined with reference to the internal projection of the supplies to be made
having regard to (i) the historical amount of the sales of laminates and related
upstream materials by the Group to the KBH Group; (ii) the anticipated demand of
such materials for the four months ending 31 December 2026 and the years ending
31 December 2027 and 2028; (iii) the anticipated increase in market price of such
materials; (iv) the expected increase in the production capacity of PCBs of the KBH
Group; and (v) inflation.
The sales to the KBH Group for the year ending 31 December 2026 are
estimated to be approximately HKD5,553,251,000 by annualising the sales amount
for the eight months ended 31 August 2026. The significant increase in sales to the
KBH Group during the year ending 31 December 2026 was mainly due to the
significant increase in the market selling price of the laminates and related upstream
materials products supplied to KBH Group.
Among the goods to be supplied to KBH Group, laminates and related
upstream materials are mainly produced from copper. The price fluctuations in
copper would have significant impact on the selling price of the goods, which in
turn would affect the Proposed Annual Caps. Based on the historical copper price
quoted on the London Metal Exchange (https://www.lme.com/) in US Dollar
(“USD”) per tonne, the copper price exhibited an aggressive upward trend between
January and August 2026, surging by over 11.5% from around USD13,000 per tonne
early in the year to record highs above USD14,500 per tonne by August. By early
August, the copper price crested at an all-time peak of USD14,533 per tonne. Such
significant price surge was driven by the combination effect of massive warehouse
stockpiling in response to US tariff delays and strong physical demand from
artificial intelligence infrastructure, such as AI data center expansions and power
grid builds. AI data centers require thick copper wiring to supply massive electrical
power and copper plumbing systems to keep the hardware from overheating. This
surging structural demand pushes market prices higher and drives severe cost
inflation across the entire electronic supply chain from raw laminates up to finished
PCBs.
The Company anticipates higher demand for laminates and related upstream
materials from KBH Group, as KBH Group expands its PCB production capacity.
The current total production capacity of PCBs of KBH Group as at 30 June 2026 was
20 million square feet per month. With an aim of increasing the market penetration
of PCBs, KBH Group has brought in 600,000 square feet of additional monthly
capacity for multi-layered PCBs in Vietnam starting from the fourth quarter of the
year 2026. With the new capacity, it is expected that the production capacity for
PCBs of KBH Group will be increased by around 3% from the year ended 31
December 2025.

Supply Annual Caps under the New Supply Framework Agreement are calculated
based on: (i) for the Proposed Revised KBH Supply Annual Cap for the year ending
31 December 2026, the estimated figure by straight-line multiplication from the

Between January and August 2026, the copper price exhibited an aggressive upward
trend, surging by over 11.5% from around USD13,000 per tonne early in the year to record
highs above USD14,500 per tonne by August. By early August, the copper price crested at
an all-time peak of USD14,533 per tonne. Such significant price surge was driven by the
combination effect of massive warehouse stockpiling in response to US tariff delays and
strong physical demand from artificial intelligence infrastructure, such as AI data center
expansions and power grid builds. AI data centers require thick copper wiring to supply
massive electrical power and copper plumbing systems to keep the hardware from
overheating. This surging structural demand pushes market prices higher and drives
severe cost inflation across the entire electronic supply chain from raw laminates up to
finished PCBs.

We have discussed with Management and reviewed the calculation of the Proposed
Revised KBH Supply Annual Cap, which was derived from the estimated figure by
straight-line multiplication from the actual transactions during the eight months ended 31
August 2026 of approximately HKD5,553.3 million with a buffer of 25%. The 25% buffer
takes into account: (i) upward commodity price trends during the eight months ended 31
August 2026; (ii) the fact that the average selling price of laminates and related upstream
materials in September 2026 was higher than the average selling price for the eight months
ended 31 August 2026; and (iii) the commissioning of 600,000 square feet per month of
additional PCBs production capacity by the KBH Group in Vietnam in the fourth quarter
of FY2026.
Having reviewed the underlying factors, we consider the inclusion of a 25% buffer
in determining the Proposed Revised KBH Supply Annual Cap for FY2026 is justified
based on:
(i) copper prices exhibited significant volatility, surging by approximately 11.5%
between January 2026 and August 2026. Management anticipates that the
selling prices of laminates and related upstream materials will maintain an
upward trend as expanding demand from electric vehicle fleets, renewable
energy grid upgrades, and power-hungry AI data center builds continues to
absorb virtually all incremental market supply;
(ii) the average selling prices of laminates and related upstream materials in
September 2026 outpaced the average for the eight months ended 31 August
2026. This upward trend confirms that a standard straight-line annualization
based solely on the first eight months’ actual transactions would understate
second-half financial performance; and
(iii) the scheduled commissioning of 600,000 square feet per month of new PCBs
production capacity by KBH Group in Vietnam during the fourth quarter of
FY2026 will directly amplify downstream purchase volume for the Group’s
products.
Therefore, we are of the view that a 25% buffer is a prudent provision that caters to
both price volatility and volume growth, ensuring the Group can maintain production
without exceeding its annual caps for the remainder of FY2026 and we consider such
estimation to be fair and reasonable.

by the KBH Group for the next two years, we have reviewed the latest annual report of
KBH Group and the expansion plan on the production capacity of PCBs of KBH Group
provided by the Group. In order to facilitate the manufacturing and sale of PCBs of the
KBH Group, the KBH Group requires laminates and related upstream materials for its

Framework Agreement thus is relevant to justify the fairness and reasonableness of the
proposed annual caps for the two years ending 31 December 2028. According to the latest
annual report of KBH Group, KBH Group has been actively developing
higher-layer-count and advanced high-density interconnect (“ HDI”) PCBs to meet global
demand driven by ongoing digital and smart transformation. Therefore, the KBH Group is
significantly scaling its advanced multi-layered PCBs production capacity through three
major expansion projects. In Vietnam, Phase I of the Gia Binh facility will be put into
production in the fourth quarter of FY2026 with new capacity of 600,000 square feet per

Thailand, Phase I will contribute another 600,000 square feet per month in FY2027 toward
a full capacity of 1.2 million. Finally, an expansion in Kaiping City, Guangdong, will be
completed by the end of FY2027, adding 300,000 square feet per month of specialized
multilayer and HDI PCBs for AI and advanced electronics. Phase I of these three sites will
deliver 1.5 million square feet of immediate new monthly capacity by the end of FY2027.
With the new capacity, it is expected that the annual production capacity for PCBs of KBH
Group will be increased by around 7.5%.
Amid accelerating digitalisation and the growing adoption of smart technologies,
domestic demand for high-end PCBs in China has been continuing to experience strong
structural growth, particularly for advanced HDI and flexible PCBs. According to the data
released from the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology for the first half of
FY2026, the output of integrated circuits in China serving as a direct proxy for carrier
board and PCB usage expanded by approximately 23.1% year-on-year to 279.8 billion
units. Looking ahead through FY2027, PCBs demand will be further amplified by the
rapid rollout of 6G-Advanced networks and smart automotive applications.
Next-generation electric vehicles need centralized controllers, efficient power modules,
and advanced driver-assistance systems (“ ADAS ”), while 6G networks depend on fast,
high-frequency circuits. Together, these high-tech needs will drive steady, large-scale
domestic demand for advanced HDI and multilayer PCBs across China. KBH Group is
actively developing new manufacturing technologies for 6G wireless communications, AI
servers, low-earth orbit (“ LEO ”) satellites, 4D imaging mmWave radar, automotive
high-end common control unit components, new energy vehicle high voltage fast chargers
and intelligent robots to diversify a higher-value-added product portfolio. This will
progressively expand its capacities for advanced multi-layered PCBs and thus the demand
of goods from the Group is expected to be stable in growth.

general cost inflation of approximately 2% to 3%.

annual growth rate is consistent with the Company’s projections. When compared to the

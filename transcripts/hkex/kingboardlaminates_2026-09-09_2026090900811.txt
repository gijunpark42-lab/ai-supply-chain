BACKGROUND
Reference is made to the joint announcement of KBH and KBL dated 31 October 2023 in
relation to the Existing Agreements. The Existing Agreements were entered into for terms
from 1 January 2024 to 31 December 2026. Taking into account the business needs of
KBH and KBL, on the date of this announcement, Hallgain and, as the case may be, KBH
and KBL entered into three new framework  agreements, namely the New KBH Purchase
Framework Agreement, the New KBL Supply Framework Agreement and the New KBL
Purchase Framework Agreement to revise the applicable  annual caps for the year ending
31 December 2026 (where applicable) and renew the transactions contemplated under the
Existing Agreements. Each of the New Agreements will expire on 31 December 2028.
Save for (i) the Proposed Revised Annual Caps for the year ending 31 December 2026;
(ii) the Proposed Annual Caps for the years ending 31 December 2027 and 2028; and (iii)
the terms of the New Agreements, the New Agreements are substantially the same as the
Existing Agreements.
KBH
As at the date of  this announcement, Hallgain owns approximately 31.75% of the issued
share capital of KBH and is therefore a connected person of KBH. KBH, directly or
indirectly, owns over 50% of the issued share capital of KBL and therefore KBL is a non-
wholly owned subsidiary of KBH. Accordingly, the transactions contemplated under the
New Agreements constitute continuing connected transactions of KBH under Chapter 14A
of the Listing Rules.

1. NEW KBH PURCHASE FRAMEWORK AGREEMENT
Background
On the date of this announcement, KBH  entered into a purchase framework agreement
with Hallgain (the “ New KBH Purchase Framework Agreement ”) in relation to
the purchase of materials including copper balls and drill bits and machineries for the
production of PCBs from the Hallgain Group by the KBH Group to revise the annual cap
for the year ending 31 December 2026 and renew the transactions contemplated under
the Existing KBH Purchase Framework Agreement. Details of the New KBH Purchase
Framework Agreement are set out below:
Date: 9 September 2026
Parties: (1) Hallgain
(2) KBH
Nature of transaction: Pursuant to the New KBH Purchase Framework Agreement,
the KBH Group agrees to purchase materials including
copper balls and drill bits and machineries for the production
of PCBs from the Hallgain Group.
The amount of materials or machineries to be purchased
is not fixed but is to be determined and agreed between
the parties from time to time. The KBH Group will not be
obligated to purchase a minimum amount of materials or
machineries from the Hallgain Group and the Hallgain Group
will not be obligated to sell any set quantity of materials or
machineries to the KBH Group during the term of the New
KBH Purchase Framework Agreement.

The actual quantity, specification and price (with reference
to the prevailing market price) of materials including
copper balls and drill bits and machineries under the New
KBH Purchase Framework Agreement will be subject to
the individual orders placed by the KBH Group with the
Hallgain Group.
Term: From 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2028, both days
inclusive.
Consideration: The price at which the materials or machineries are to be
purchased will be at a price which is not less favourable
to the KBH Group than the price at which the KBH Group
purchases, or would be able to purchase similar materials or
machineries from independent third parties having regard
to the quantity and other conditions of the purchase. To

or machineries (based on similar amount and similar
specifications). In particular, where appropriate, the relevant
purchasing department of the KBH Group will obtain
quotations from different suppliers (both the Hallgain Group
and the independent third party suppliers) and monitor the
movement of market price from time to time.
The Hallgain Group will grant a credit period of 60 days to
the KBH Group. The consideration will be settled in cash.

REASONS FOR AND BENEFITS OF ENTERING INTO THE NEW AGREEMENTS
The KBH Group is engaged in, among other things, the manufacturing and sale of PCBs,
which require materials such as copper balls and drill bits and specific machineries as
components for its production. The KBL Group is engaged in, among other things, the
manufacturing and sale of laminates and related upstream materials, which require specific
machineries as a component for its production. The Hallgain Group is engaged in, among
other things, the manufacturing and sale of copper balls, drill bits and machineries.
Pursuant to the New KBH Purchase Framework Agreement, the Hallgain Group shall produce
and supply materials including copper balls and drill bits and machineries to the KBH Group.
In order to satisfy the demand of the KBH Group for materials such as copper balls, the
Hallgain Group shall purchase copper for the production of copper balls for its sale to the
KBH Group. The provision of copper and laminates by the KBL Group to the Hallgain Group
under the New KBL Supply Framework Agreement not only facilitates the KBL Group to
distribute and sell its copper and laminates, thereby increasing the sales and revenue of the
KBL Group, it also secures a steady supply of copper and laminates to the Hallgain Group.
A reliable supply of materials such as copper balls and drill bits by the Hallgain Group to the
KBH Group can also facilitate the KBH Group’s production of PCBs. The KBH Group and
the KBL Group consider that the Hallgain Group is a reliable business co-operation partner
and such co-operation is beneficial to the business of the KBH Group and the KBL Group,
respectively.
The KBH Group anticipates that machineries for the manufacture of PCBs will continue to
be required for the KBH Group to operate its business. The KBH Directors consider Hallgain
Group to be a reputable manufacturer of copper balls, drill bits and machineries. Taking into
account a range of factors including the reduction in transportation costs, the quality and the
price of the machineries manufactured by the Hallgain Group for the production of PCBs,
the KBH Group considers that the purchase of machineries under the New KBH Purchase
Framework Agreement will facilitate its business plan and will improve the competitiveness
and the quality of the PCBs manufactured by the KBH Group.
The KBL Group anticipates that machineries for  the manufacture of laminates will continue
to be required for the KBL Group to operate its business. The KBL Directors consider
Hallgain Group to be a reputable manufacturer of machineries. Taking into account a range
of factors including the reduction in transportation costs, the quality and the price of the
machineries manufactured by the Hallgain Group for the production of laminates, the KBL
Group considers that the purchase of machineries  under the New KBL Purchase Framework
Agreement will facilitate its business plan and will improve the competitiveness and the
quality of the laminates manufactured by the KBL Group.

The supply of copper and laminates and purchases of copper balls, drill bits and machineries
contemplated under the respective agreements are to be of a recurrent revenue nature occurring
on a regular and continuing basis in the ordinary and usual course of business of the KBH
Group and the KBL Group, respectively. The agreements provide a framework for the supply
of copper and laminates by the KBL Group to the Hallgain Group from time to time on a non-
exclusive basis and the purchase of materials such as copper balls, drill bits and machineries
by the KBH Group and the KBL Group from the Hallgain Group respectively from time to
time on a non-exclusive basis and regulate the future possible business relationship among the
KBH Group, the KBL Group and the Hallgain Group in relation to the transactions under the
agreements.
In negotiating and determining the pricing terms of each of the subject transactions under the
New Agreements, in order to ensure that the pricing terms are on normal commercial terms
and in the interests of KBH or KBL (as the case may be) and their respective independent
shareholders as a whole, the general principle is that the KBH Group and the KBL Group (as
the case may be) shall consider, where appropriate, the prices offered by independent third
party suppliers or to independent third party customers (as the case may be) of similar product
(based on similar amount and similar specifications) in arriving at the pricing terms with the
Hallgain Group after arm’s length negotiations. In particular, as part of the internal control
procedures, the relevant purchasing department and sales department (as the case may be)
and/or the director-in-charge of the relevant operating subsidiary, shall, where appropriate,
obtain quotations from independent third party suppliers or compare prices offered to
independent third party customers (as the case may be) in determining the pricing terms with
the Hallgain Group from time to time. The historical price of such comparable transaction
will be taken into account. As such, the terms offered to the Hallgain Group in respect of
the subject transactions under the New Agreements will be comparable to market terms, or
not more favourable to, the Hallgain Group. In the absence of any comparable transaction,
KBH or KBL (as the case may be) will conduct market research on the open market where
applicable. Such market research comprises the sales/purchase department’s coming into
contact with and initiating business enquiries with potential buyers/suppliers in relation to the
subject transactions contemplated under the New Agreements. Such potential buyers/suppliers
(as the case may be) are independent third parties to the Group and are market players which
have been KBH or KBL’s customers/suppliers (as the case may be). The prices and terms
acceptable to potential suppliers/buyers are recorded and at least two summary reports of such
open market research are submitted to the head of the sales/purchase department and/or the
director-in-charge of the relevant operating subsidiaries for their information and reference in
considering the orders with Hallgain Group under the New Agreements.

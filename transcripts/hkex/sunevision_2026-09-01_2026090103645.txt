REVISION OF THE ANNUAL CAPS FOR CONTINUING CONNECTED TRANSACTIONS
Reference is made to the announcement of the Company dated 7 May 2026 in relation to, among
other things, the continuing connected transactions contemplated under the Property Management
Arrangement by SHKP Group to the Group for a period from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2029 (both days
inclusive).
In light of recent developments in the operations of the Group’s data centres and their operational
requirements, the Group expects to extend and increase the scope and amount of cleaning and sanitary
services required under the Extra Management Services. The transaction amounts contemplated under
the Property Management Arrangement are expected to increase and the existing annual caps may no
longer be sufficient to cover the relevant transaction amounts. Accordingly, on 1 September 2026,
the Company and SHKP entered into the supplemental agreement (the “Supplemental Agreement”),
pursuant to which the parties agreed to revise the annual caps for the transactions under the Property
Management Arrangement for each of the three financial years ending 30 June 2027, 30 June 2028
and 30 June 2029 (the “Revised Annual Caps”). Save for the Revised Annual Caps, the other terms
and conditions of the Property Management Agreement remain unchanged and are in full force and
effect.

LISTING RULES IMPLICATIONS
As at the date of this announcement, SHKP is a substantial Shareholder indirectly holding an
aggregate of 1,730,722,500 Shares, representing approximately 73.24% of the entire issued share
capital of the Company. As such, SHKP and its associates are connected persons of the Company
under the Listing Rules for so long as SHKP remains a substantial Shareholder.
Pursuant to Rule 14A.54(1) of the Listing Rules, as the Company proposes to revise the original
annual caps for the Property Management Arrangement, it will have to re-comply with the
applicable provisions of Chapter 14A of the Listing Rules. It is anticipated that one or more of the
applicable percentage ratios set out in Rule 14.07 of the Listing Rules in respect of the transactions
contemplated under the Property Management Arrangement (based on the Revised Annual Caps), on
an annual basis, will be more than 0.1% but will be less than 5%. Hence, the transactions under the
Property Management Arrangement will constitute continuing connected transactions of the Company
under Chapter 14A of the Listing Rules that are subject to the reporting, announcement and annual
review requirements, and are exempt from the circular (including independent financial advice) and
the Independent Shareholders’ Approval requirements under Chapter 14A of the Listing Rules.
INTRODUCTION

PROPERTY MANAGEMENT ARRANGEMENT
Members of the SHKP Group, as the building manager appointed by the owners of the relevant buildings
in accordance with the terms of the relevant deeds of mutual covenant and management agreements,
have provided and will continue to provide the Building Management Services in relation to certain
properties of the Group including those situated at Millennium City 1 in Kwun Tong, Kowloon, Hong
Kong. The relevant members of the Group pay management fees to the relevant building manager on the
same basis as the other owners of the relevant buildings in accordance with the relevant deeds of mutual
covenant and management agreements.

PROPERTY MANAGEMENT AGREEMENT AND SUPPLEMENTAL AGREEMENT
Property Management Agreement
On 7 May 2026, the Company and SHKP entered into the Property Management Agreement whereby
SHKP agreed to procure relevant members of the SHKP Group to provide Extra Management Services
to members of the Group for a period from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2029 (both days inclusive).
This agreement is a master agreement which sets out the principles upon which detailed terms are to
be determined in respect of the provision of the Extra Management Services. Relevant members of the
Group and of the SHKP Group have entered into and will enter into separate and definitive agreements
with detailed terms for the services required from time to time pursuant to such arrangement. The
said principles include that the Extra Management Services to be provided shall be negotiated on an
arm’s length basis and on normal commercial terms, and the amount of service fees payable by the
Group to the SHKP Group shall be at rates comparable to those charged by other third-party service
providers taking into account the type and nature, scale, frequency and duration of the services involved.
Depending on the type, nature and scale of the services required, the Group may invite tenders from
potential service providers (which include members of the SHKP Group) and select the bidder who
offers the most attractive terms. The Property Management Agreement is in essence of substantially the
same terms as those under the previous agreement for Extra Management Services entered into by the
parties on 3 May 2023, which expired on 30 June 2026.

Subject Matter
In light of recent developments in the operations of the Group’s data centres and their operational
requirements which are more likely to be met by members of SHKP Group, the Group expects to
extend and increase the scope and amount of cleaning and sanitary services required under the Extra
Management Services. As a result, the transaction amounts contemplated under the Property Management
Agreement are expected to increase and the existing annual caps may no longer be sufficient to cover
the relevant transaction amounts. Accordingly, on 1 September 2026, the Company and SHKP entered
into the Supplemental Agreement, pursuant to which the parties agreed to revise the annual caps for the
transactions under the Property Management Arrangement for each of the three financial years ending
30 June 2027, 30 June 2028 and 30 June 2029. Save for the Revised Annual Caps, the other terms and
conditions of the Property Management Agreement remain unchanged and are in full force and effect.

It is anticipated that, on average (for each of the three financial years ending 30 June 2029),
approximately 11% of the total fees expected to be payable under the Property Management

attributable to the Extra Management Services.
The Revised Annual Caps are determined based on arm’s length negotiations between the Company
and SHKP, with reference to (i) the management fees in respect of the Building Management Services
(which are to be fixed by the owners of the buildings concerned in accordance with the respective
deeds of mutual covenant and management agreements); (ii) the expected increase in the level of the
Extra Management Services required; and (iii) the anticipated extension of the scope and increase in
the demand for cleaning and sanitary services to data centres of the Group to be provided by members
of the SHKP Group, thereby resulting in an increase in the transaction amounts contemplated under the
Property Management Arrangement.
As seen from above, from 1 July 2026 to 31 July 2026, the actual transaction amounts under the
Property Management Arrangement have not exceeded the original annual cap for the financial year
ending 30 June 2027.
The Property Management Arrangement has been and will be conducted in the ordinary and usual
course of business of the Group. The Directors (including the independent non-executive Directors)
confirm that (i) the transactions in relation to the provision of the Building Management Services have
been and will continue to be on the same basis as those payable by the other owners of the relevant
buildings; and (ii) the transactions in relation to the provision of the Extra Management Services have
been and will continue to be negotiated on an arm’s length basis and on normal commercial terms, and
the service fees payable to the SHKP Group have been and will continue to be at rates comparable to
the rates chargeable by other third-party service providers taking into account the type and nature, scale,
frequency and duration of the services involved.

REASONS FOR AND BENEFITS OF ENTERING INTO OF THE SUPPLEMENTAL
AGREEMENT
The Company considers that the entering into of the Supplemental Agreement to revise the existing
annual caps for the Property Management Arrangement is prudent and necessary to cater for the
increase in scope and amount of cleaning and sanitary services required under the Extra Management
Services, and to ensure that satisfactory service quality is consistently delivered and maintained at the
Group’s data centres. Members of the SHKP Group have extensive experience in property and facilities
management and are familiar with the operational, security and hygiene requirements of the Group’s
data centre facilities. Through their long-standing business relationship with the Group, members of
the SHKP Group have demonstrated satisfactory service quality, reliability and responsiveness. The
continued engagement of members of the SHKP Group enables the Group to maintain consistent service
quality, operational reliability and uninterrupted service support for the relevant data centre facilities. In
particular, the Group has contracted with the SHKP Group for the Property Management Arrangement
because the Group and the SHKP Group are able to meet the respective requirements of each other.
For the aforesaid reasons and also taking into account, among other things, the experience of and
relationship between the Group and the SHKP Group developed through transactions undertaken in the
past, the Directors (including the independent non-executive Directors) consider that the entering into
of the Property Management Arrangement (including the Supplemental Agreement and the Revised
Annual Caps) is in the commercial interest of the Group. The Directors (including the independent
non-executive Directors) consider that the transactions under the Property Management Arrangement
(including the Revised Annual Caps) have been and will be conducted in the ordinary and usual course
of business of the Group and on an arm’s length basis, on normal commercial terms or on terms no less
favourable to the Group than those available from or to independent third parties (as the case may be),
and are fair and reasonable and in the interests of the Shareholders as a whole.
INTERNAL CONTROLS
According to the Group’s internal control procedures, the Group is required to solicit quotations/tenders
for similar products and services from at least two independent third parties (depending on the estimated
contract value) from its approved contractor list, where possible. The Group will select the contractor
which offers the most attractive terms taking into account all relevant factors including but not limited
to the price, quality of work and schedule of completion. For Building Management Services under the
Property Management Arrangement, however, the selection of building management service providers is
performed by the owners’ corporations of the relevant properties and not by the Group.
The Group’s internal audit department regularly conducts sample checking of the transactions under the
Property Management Arrangement to ensure the efficiency and effectiveness of the relevant internal
control procedures.

The above methods and procedures allow the Group’s management to ensure that the service fees
charged by or to be paid by the Group to the SHKP Group under the Property Management Arrangement
are no less favourable than the respective similar transactions conducted between the Group and
independent third parties.
The finance department of the Group monitors the transaction amounts under the Property Management
Arrangement on a monthly basis and the internal audit department of the Group reviews them regularly
to ensure that timely alert will be made to the management of the Group whenever the aggregate
transactions amount is likely to exceed the Revised Annual Caps.
The Company will provide information and supporting documents to the independent non-executive
Directors and its auditors for them to conduct an annual review of the transactions under the
Property Management Arrangement. The independent non-executive Directors will provide an annual
confirmation to the Board as to whether such transactions have been entered into in the ordinary and
usual course of business of the Group, are on normal commercial terms and are in accordance with
the Property Management Agreement (as revised by the Supplemental Agreement) on terms that are
fair and reasonable and in the interests of the Shareholders as a whole. The auditors will report to the
Board annually as to whether anything has come to their attention that causes them to believe that the
transactions under the Property Management Arrangement have not been approved by the Board, are
not in accordance with the pricing policies of the Group or are not entered into in accordance with
the Property Management Agreement (as revised by the Supplemental Agreement) governing the
transactions in all material respects, or have exceeded the Revised Annual Caps.

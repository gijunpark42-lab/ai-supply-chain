Reference is made to the announcements of the Company dated (i) 31 August 2025, 31 December
2025, 22 January 2026, and 11 September 2026 in relation to the Proposed Acquisition; and
(ii) 30 December 2025 in relation to, amongst others, the 2026 Huahong Group Framework
Agreement.
Pursuant to the 2026 Huahong Group Framework Agreement, the Existing Sales Annual Cap for
the year ending 31 December 2026 is US$90,000,000.
Following Completion of the Proposed Acquisition, Huali Micro has become a wholly-owned
subsidiary of the Company and any continuing transactions between Huali Micro and the
Huahong Group Companies will constitute continuing connected transactions of the Company
under Chapter 14A of the Listing Rules. Accordingly, the Board is of the view that the Existing
Sales Annual Cap under the 2026 Huahong Group Framework Agreement will not be sufficient
to meet the expected sales transaction amount for the year ending 31 December 2026 under the
2026 Huahong Group Framework Agreement. Therefore, the Company intends to increase the
Existing Sales Annual Cap to US$120,000,000 (the “Revised Sales Annual Cap ”).
The Revised Sales Annual Cap is determined principally by reference to, (i) the historical
amount of sales between Huali Micro and Huahong Group Companies; (ii) the amount of the
Existing Sales Annual Cap utilized for the period from 1 January 2026 to 30 June 2026; (iii) the
expected amount of sales between the Group and Huahong Group Companies for the period from
the date of Completion to 31 December 2026; (iv) a reasonable buffer to accommodate potential
fluctuations in transaction volumes, product mix, market prices and foreign exchange rates; and
(v) the general market conditions of the semiconductor market.

1. PROPOSED REVISION OF EXISTING ANNUAL CAPS UNDER THE 2026
HUAHONG GROUP FRAMEWORK AGREEMENT
1.1 Background
Reference is made to the announcements of the Company dated 30 December 2025 in relation
to, amongst others, the 2026 Huahong Group Framework Agreement between the Company
(on behalf of the Group) and Huahong Group (on behalf of the Huahong Group Companies)
for a term of one year from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026 (both dates inclusive),
which regulates, among others, (i) the sale by the Group of semiconductor products, including
wafers and integrated circuits, to the Huahong Group Companies; and (ii) the purchase by the
Group of semiconductor products, including wafers, chemicals and other commodities, from
the Huahong Group Companies.
Further reference is made to the announcements of the Company dated 31 August 2025,
31 December 2025, 22 January 2026, and 11 September 2026 in relation to the Proposed
Acquisition. Following Completion of the Proposed Acquisition, Huali Micro has become
a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company and any continuing transactions between Huali
Micro and the Huahong Group Companies will constitute continuing connected transactions
of the Company under Chapter 14A of the Listing Rules, which will lead to an increase in the
annual transaction amount between the Group and the Huahong Group Companies.
Specifically, the Board is of the view that the Existing Sales Annual Cap under the 2026
Huahong Group Framework Agreement will not be sufficient to meet the expected sales
transaction amount for the year ending 31 December 2026 under the 2026 Huahong Group
Framework Agreement, and such cap should be revised upward accordingly.
Save for the revision of the Existing Sales Annual Cap as described below, the terms of the
2026 Huahong Group Framework Agreement have not been changed or modified in any
aspect and the major terms thereof were set out in the section headed “1.1 Principal terms of
the 2026 Huahong Group Framework Agreement ” in the announcement of the Company dated
30 December 2025.
1.2 The Revised Sales Annual Cap
The Company proposes that the Existing Sales Annual Cap under the 2026 Huahong Group
Framework Agreement be revised upward to US$120,000,000. Following the revision, the
annual caps under the 2026 Huahong Group Framework Agreement are as follows:

1.4 Reasons for and benefits of revising the Existing Sales Annual Cap
Revising the Existing Sales Annual Cap will enable Huali Micro to maximize its production
capacity and at the same time minimise the impact of the Proposed Acquisition on Huali
Micro ’s operations and financial performance. Further, as per the transaction amount of
Huali Micro ’s sales to Huahong Group Companies as disclosed above, the transaction amount
for the six months ended 30 June 2026 was approximately US$43,000,000, indicating a
significant increase compared with the annual transaction amount for the year ended 31
December 2025, indicating strong demand and performance. Following the Completion of
the Proposed Acquisition, Huali Micro is expected to be fully integrated into the Group. Its
customers, product portfolio, process platforms, production capacity and business transactions
are expected to be integrated with those of the Group. The revision of the Existing Sales
Annual Cap, together with the integration as aforementioned, is expected to enhance Huali
Micro ’s operating performance and thus maximize the interests of the Shareholders.
The Directors (including the independent non-executive Directors) are of the view that (i) the
terms of the 2026 Huahong Group Framework Agreement and the Revised Sales Annual Cap
are fair and reasonable; (ii) the terms of the 2026 Huahong Group Framework Agreement are
on normal commercial terms or better and the transactions thereunder are and will continue
to be conducted in the ordinary and usual course of business of the Group; and (iii) the terms
of the 2026 Huahong Group Framework Agreement and the Revised Sales Annual Cap are in
the interest of the Company and its Shareholders as a whole.

2.5 Reasons for and benefits of entering into the Equipment Lease
The Equipment Lease is an existing agreement entered into by Huali Micro prior to the
Completion of the Proposed Acquisition. Upon Completion, the Company not only takes over
Huali Micro, but also intends to take on the business transactions previously entered into by
Huali Micro, including the Equipment Lease, with a view to minimizing disruption to Huali
Micro ’s existing operations and business transactions with the Huahong Group Companies.
Further, the Company considers that the continuance of the Equipment Lease is in the
interests of the Group and its Shareholders as a whole and is commercially justifiable.
Given that the relevant equipment has been specifically configured for current operations,
maintaining the existing leasing arrangement represents the most cost-effective means of
continuing to utilise the relevant equipment and facilities.

4. INFORMATION ABOUT THE PARTIES
4.1 The Company and the Group
The Company primarily focuses on embedded non-volatile memory, power discrete, analog &
power management, logic & RF and other specialty technology manufacturing platforms.
4.2 Huahong Group
Huahong Group is a high-tech industry group which primarily focuses on IC manufacturing,
research and development of advanced IC manufacturing process, IC systems integration
and application services, sale of electronic components and overseas venture capital
investment. For details of the transactions which are to be governed by the 2026 Huahong
Group Framework Agreement, please refer to the announcements of the Company dated 30
December 2025.
4.3 Huali Micro
Huali Micro ’s principal business is developing and operating a 300mm wafer fab. Upon
Completion of the Proposed Acquisition, Huali Micro has become a wholly-owned subsidiary
of the Company.
4.4 Huali MC
Huali MC is a subsidiary of Huahong Group and is primarily engaged in the manufacture and
sale of 300mm wafer.

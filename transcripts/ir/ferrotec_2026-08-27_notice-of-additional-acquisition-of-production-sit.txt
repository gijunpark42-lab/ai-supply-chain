August 27, 2026

Notice of Additional Acquisition of Production Site in Kawakita-machi, Ishikawa Prefecture

Ferrotec Corporation (Headquartered in Chuo-ku, Tokyo; Representative Director: He Xian Han; hereinafter “the

Company”) announces that, at the meeting of the Board of Directors held on August 17, 2026, the Company resolved to

additionally acquire a parcel of land (owner: Takakuwa Kinko Co., Ltd.; hereinafter “the Land”) adjacent to “Ishikawa Factory

No. 3,” the Company’s existing production base located within the industrial park in the eastern district of Kawakita-machi,

Nomi-gun, Ishikawa Prefecture. Details are as follows.

Please note that this release has been made today to coincide with the approval by Kawakita-machi and the town’s own

external announcement of this matter.

1. Background and Objective of the Acquisition

As part of its “Return to Japan” strategy, the Company began operation of Ishikawa Factory No. 3 within the same industrial

park in July 2025, aiming to strengthen its production capacity for products related to semiconductor manufacturing equipment

(fine ceramics). However, amid continued growth in demand, the Company’s bases in China and Malaysia — including metal

processing operations — are already operating at full capacity, and securing sufficient production capacity to meet domestic

demand has become a key challenge.

In light of this situation, the Company has positioned the construction of a new domestic factory for ceramics and metal

processing as an important management priority. In addition, following the Cabinet’s decision on July 21, 2026 to adopt the

government’s “Regional Future Strategy,” which positions the Hokuriku region as a hub for the AI and semiconductor industries

following Kyushu and Hokkaido, the Company determined that this presents a good opportunity to strengthen its production

base in the region, and accordingly decided to acquire the Land.

The Land to be acquired totals 5,090.09 tsubo (16,826.74 m²) and is adjacent to Ishikawa Factory No. 3. By operating it

together with the Company’s existing site, the Company expects to make effective use of the remaining unused building-to-

land ratio and floor-area ratio on the Ishikawa Factory No. 3 site.

Please note that resale of the Land was originally restricted for a 10-year period under the terms of sale set by the Kawakita-

machi, Land Development Corporation. However, with the understanding and cooperation of the seller, Takakuwa Kinko Co.,

Ltd., and of Kawakita-machi, this restriction is expected to be lifted solely with respect to the transfer to the Company.

The Company will begin studying the construction of a “Factory No. 4” utilizing the Land, together with a broader expansion

of investment. Once a specific business plan for Factory No. 4 (investment amount, total floor area, timing of the start of

operation, etc.) has been finalized, the Company will make a further announcement.

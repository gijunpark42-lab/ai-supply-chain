Unisys Named to TIME's 2026 List of India's Best Employers

September 22, 2026 at 9:00 AM EDT

Company climbs more than 60 spots on TIME's annual ranking of leading employers in India

BENGALURU, India and BLUE BELL, Pa , Sept. 22, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Unisys (NYSE: UIS) has been named to TIME's 2026 list of 500 India's Best Employers . Ranking No. 176 among 500 organizations, Unisys climbed more than 60 spots from No. 241 in 2025 , earning recognition on the list for a second consecutive year. The annual ranking recognizes organizations that earn strong recommendations from employees across India .

The recognition reflects employee feedback and highlights the company's continued focus on professional development, career growth and the overall associate experience.

"Advancing more than 60 positions in this year's rankings and earning recognition for a second consecutive year reflects the feedback of our associates and the culture they shape every day," said Mike Thomson , chief executive officer and president, Unisys. "We remain committed to creating opportunities for our associates to grow their careers, develop new skills and make a meaningful impact for our clients."

TIME's India's Best Employers list is presented by TIME and Statista Inc. , a statistics portal and industry ranking provider. For 2026, organizations were chosen based on survey data collected in 2025 and 2026 from approximately 760,000 employees working at companies with at least 500 employees in India . The top 500 employers were then selected based on their results across the evaluation criteria.

This announcement follows a series of recent recognitions, including TIME's America's Best Companies 2026 list, The Economist's 2026 Global Top 100 Most Loved Workplaces ® list and Forbes' 2026 list of America's Best Midsize Employers .

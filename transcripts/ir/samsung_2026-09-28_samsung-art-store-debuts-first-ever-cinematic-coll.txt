Samsung Art Store Debuts First-Ever Cinematic Collection for Samsung TVs

on September 28, 2026

AUDIO Play

Share open

Samsung Art Store 1 has announced its first-ever cinematic collection, featuring 38 stills from acclaimed films produced by studio A24. Available on supported Samsung Art TVs worldwide, the collection allows subscribers to revisit memorable moments and discover new favorites at home.

▲ A still from “Midsommar,” courtesy of A24

A24 is a global entertainment company that produces and distributes films, TV series, documentaries, music, and consumer products. Notable films include “Everything Everywhere All at Once,” “Moonlight,” “Past Lives,” and “Midsommar.”

▲ A still from “I Saw the TV Glow,” courtesy of A24

“Our collaboration with A24 expands the horizons of Samsung Art Store by bringing powerful cinematic storytelling to TV screens,” said Heeyeong Ahn, Vice President of the Visual Display (VD) Business at Samsung Electronics. “We look forward to seeing these iconic film moments inspire audiences around the world and offer a new way to engage with the stories they love.”

Designed to serve as modern digital canvases, Samsung Art TVs display artwork with vibrant color and rich detail. The Frame, along with The Frame Pro — whose debut model last year was named one of TIME’s “Best Inventions” — blend seamlessly into home décor like picture frames. The Frame lineup comes in a range of sizes, with customizable bezel options to help transform any space into a gallery.

Samsung further expanded its Art TV lineup 2 in 2026, offering Samsung Art Store to its Micro RGB and Neo QLED series. Samsung Art Store is now available on select Samsung OLED TVs 3 for the first time, including the flagship S95H.

Samsung Art Store is the No. 1 TV art subscription service globally, reaching over 117 countries. Subscribers can access more than 5,000 artworks from over 800 artists, including pieces from collaborations with institutions such as The Metropolitan Museum of Art, MoMA, and Musée d’Orsay.

Samsung Art Store requires a paid subscription, and its pricing, supported TV models, and available artworks and collections may vary and are subject to change by country and region. ↩︎

Samsung Art Store is available on select Samsung TVs: Compatible 2026 models include R95H, S95H, QN80H, QN70H, M80H, The Frame, and The Frame Pro. Compatible 2025 models include Neo QLED 8K, Neo QLED 4K, The Frame, The Frame Pro, Q8F, and Q7F. ↩︎

Samsung Art Store is available on select OLED models including S95H (global) and S99H (Europe). ↩︎

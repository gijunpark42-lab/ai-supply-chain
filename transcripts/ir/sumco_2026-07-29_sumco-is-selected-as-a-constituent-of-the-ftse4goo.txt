July 29, 2026

SUMCO CORPORATION

SUMCO is selected as a constituent of the FTSE4Good Index

SUMCO has been continuously selected as a component of the FTSE4Good Index, an environmental, social

and governance (ESG) index of FTSE Russell in the UK ’s. *1

The FTSE4Good Index has become a major investment selection criterion for investors as a leading index

measuring the performance  of companies that excel in ESG activities. 1,211 companies from developed

countries around the world (including 296 Japanese companies)  have been selected for the FTSE4Good Index

as of June 30th, 2026. *2

In addition, the Company was selected for inclusion in the “FTSE JPX Blossom Japan Index” and the “FTSE

JPX Blossom Japan Sector Relative Index” as in the previous year. *3

SUMCO will continue to engage in ESG activities with the aim of contributing to society as a good corporate

citizen through the supply of silicon wafers, an essential substrate material for semiconductor devices, and

contributing to the development of indu stry and improvement of living standards.

*1 FTSE Russell is a wholly owned subsidiary of the London Stock Exchange Group and is engaged in

designing and maintaining investment indices worldwide.

*2 https://www.ftserussell.com/products/indices/ftse4good

*3 https://www.ftserussell.com/products/indices/blossom -japan

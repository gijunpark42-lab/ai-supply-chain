Belimo Strengthens Its Hinwil, Zurich Site with New Production and Logistics Building

Hinwil (Switzerland), August 25, 2026 – With the official opening of its new production and logistics building, Belimo is making a strategic investment in the future of its Hinwil site.

The new building creates additional capacity for further growth, strengthens global delivery capability, and demonstrates how innovative building technology and sustainability can successfully work together.

Nanya Technology July 2026 Revenue NT$ 43,868 Million

August 4, 2026 – Nanya Technology Corporation (Ticker: 2408) today announced its unaudited consolidated net sales revenue of NT$ 43,868 million for July 2026, representing a 49.27% (Note) increase month-over-month, 719.61% increase year-over-year. Year-to-date consolidated sales revenue was NT$ 175,504 million, 660.87% increase year-over-year. For details, please log on www.nanya.com (Investor relations/ Financials/ Monthly Consolidated Revenue), or Taiwan Stock Exchange website https://emops.twse.com.tw/server-java/t58query.

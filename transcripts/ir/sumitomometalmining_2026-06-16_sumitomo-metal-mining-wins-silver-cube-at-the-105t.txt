June 16, 2026

Sumitomo Metal Mining Co., Ltd.

Tokyo, Japan - Sumitomo Metal Mining Co., Ltd. (TSE: 5713) announces that HOPE WEAR, a concept model of the company's solar-smart material technology SOLAMENT™, has won a Silver Cube in the Product Design category at the 105th ADC Annual Awards. Founded in 1920, the ADC Annual Awards is the world's longest continuously running awards program recognizing global excellence in craft and innovation in all forms of advertising and design.

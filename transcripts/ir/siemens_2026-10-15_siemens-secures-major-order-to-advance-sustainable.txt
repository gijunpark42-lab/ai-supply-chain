Press Release 15 October 2026 Siemens Mobility Munich

World’s largest battery train order: Siemens accelerates decarbonization

of rail transport in Lower Saxony

Lower Saxony Transport Authority (LNVG) and Greater Braunschweig Regional Association (RGB) award contract for 113 three-car Mireo Plus B battery trains, maintenance, and depots valued at over two billion euros

83 vehicles for Lower Saxony Transport Authority and an additional 30 trains for Greater Braunschweig Regional Association

Two new maintenance facilities in Lower Saxony, including long-term digital maintenance over 30 years and up to 100 future-proof jobs

Option for the procurement of an additional 132 trains

Siemens Mobility has

received a major order from the Landesnahverkehrsgesellschaft Niedersachsen

(LNVG) and the Regionalverband Großraum Braunschweig (RGB) for battery-powered

regional trains. The order includes 113 three-car Mireo Plus B trains, an

extensive 30-year service package, and the construction of two Rail Service

Centers in Lower Saxony. The contract also includes options for up to 132

additional rail vehicles. The trains will enter passenger service starting at

the end of 2031.

Michael Peter, President, Siemens Mobility: “With the world’s largest order for battery-powered trains, we are working alongside our partners in Lower Saxony to drive forward the decarbonisation of rail transport and demonstrate that sustainable mobility is already economically viable and scalable today. Our Mireo Plus B battery-powered trains offer emission-free operation, high availability and first-class passenger comfort. In combination with our digital service and maintenance concept, Railigent X, we are laying the foundations for efficient and reliable fleet operation throughout the vehicles’ 30-year life cycle. At the same time, we are investing in service and maintenance capacity as well as skilled local jobs. In this way, we are creating long-term added value for passengers, transport authorities and the region.”

Solidigm to Discontinue Review of Potential IPO, Reaffirming Commitment to Long-Term Growth and Shareholder Value

▪ Solidigm has explored various funding options, including a potential IPO, to finance a new fab in the U.S. to meet growing AI memory demand.

▪ While an IPO was expected to ease investment burdens and expand capacity for shareholder returns, the review has been discontinued in light of market conditions and share price trends.

▪ The need to invest in Solidigm’s growth remains unchanged, and alternatives to enhance shareholder value will continue to be explored.

Solidigm will halt its review of potential pre-IPO and IPO options.

This decision does not reflect any change in Solidigm’s growth prospects or investment needs. Rather, in light of recent capital market conditions and share price trends, Solidigm determined that it was necessary to reassess whether an IPO would be the most beneficial option for SK hynix and its existing shareholders at this time.

An IPO Was Considered as a Means of Enhancing Shareholder Value Through Growth

Growing memory demand in the AI era presents new growth opportunities for Solidigm. However, its existing production facilities have limited capacity to meet rising demand, making it necessary for U.S.-based Solidigm to secure additional production facilities in the U.S.

To secure the funding needed for Solidigm’s growth and ensure efficient capital allocation, SK hynix has been evaluating various options, including direct investment in Solidigm and raising external capital through an IPO.

Raising external capital through an IPO could increase SK hynix’s consolidated cash flow, creating greater capacity to fund facility investments while also supporting shareholder returns through dividends, share repurchases, and share cancellations.

Furthermore, if Solidigm were to achieve an appropriate valuation through an IPO, the market could recognize its growth potential more fully—a potential that is not adequately reflected in SK hynix’s current corporate value.

Ultimately, the IPO review was an effort to identify options that would secure funding for Solidigm’s growth while enhancing the economic value of SK hynix’s existing shareholders’ holdings.

IPO Review to Be Discontinued in Response to Financial Market Conditions; Alternatives to Support Growth to Remain Under Consideration

While SK hynix and Solidigm continue to deliver solid business performance, financial market volatility has increased, contributing to a broader correction in semiconductor stocks.

After comprehensively assessing these business conditions, Solidigm has decided to discontinue its review of potential pre-IPO and IPO options.

Expanding Solidigm’s production capacity and strengthening its competitiveness remain important priorities.

SK hynix will also continue to assess investment timing, scale, and financing methods while exploring various options to support Solidigm’s growth and enhance value for existing shareholders.

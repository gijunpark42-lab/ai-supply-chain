TAIPEI, Taiwan, August 24, 2026 — ASUS Republic of Gamers (ROG) today announced the ROG Flux Charger Dock, a premium all-in-one charging and connectivity hub for gamers and creators. With 260 watts of total power output, it easily turns a laptop into a full gaming setup with an external monitor, mouse, and keyboard. A built-in 1.9-inch LCD screen and control knob let users view critical information and adjust settings on the fly. The dock features extensive connectivity options, including USB-C® with 140W charging and 10Gbps transfer speeds, as well as HDMI® 2.1 to support up to 4K@144Hz visuals. Aura Sync compatibility and bold ROG design elements help set it apart from other devices in its class.

Unmatched power

Engineered to deliver 260W of continuous power, the ROG Flux Charger Dock can easily run any accessory in a gamer’s arsenal. The dock can charge a laptop at up to 140W, with capacity to spare for a gaming handheld, phone, headset, keyboard, and more. The single-cable design allows for neater setups and greater convenience.

The ROG Flux Charger Dock has five distinct charging modes that allow users to adapt the device to suit their unique needs. Silent Mode keeps noise levels low for late-night web browsing, while Priority Mode ensures that 140W is always delivered to the main device via USB-C Power Delivery. Dual Laptop Mode offers 100W charging to two devices simultaneously. Meanwhile, Smart Mode automatically allocates power as the situation changes. Lastly, Fan Turbo Mode runs the dock at full power and sets the cooling fan’s RPM to maximum to keep internal components as cool as possible.

Convenient control

A 1.9-inch LCD screen on the front face of the ROG Flux Charger Dock displays vital system information, displaying stats such as real-time charging rates and file transfer speeds. A physical knob lets users adjust operating modes and settings without any additional software.

For those who care about the look and feel of their setup, the ROG Flux Charger Dock offers a range of customization options, including RGB lighting effects, ROG screensaver themes, and the ability to upload custom animations.

Seamless connectivity

The ROG Flux Charger Dock offers extensive connectivity options to support a wide range of accessories and peripherals. The front panel includes two USB-C ports, each offering 100W charging and 10Gbps data transfer rates, with two additional USB Type-A ports providing 12W output and 480Mbps transfers. The rear of the dock features one 140W USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-C®, a full-size HDMI 2.1, and an AC port.

The HDMI 2.1 port supports up to 4K resolution at 144Hz refresh rates, or 8K resolution at 30Hz, giving gamers and creators flexibility in their setup. With variable refresh rate (VRR) technology, the ROG Flux Charger Dock allows for smooth, tear-free gaming visuals.

Classic ROG aesthetics

The ROG Flux Charger Dock has a sleek black chassis with classic ROG Slash design elements, including an RGB-backlit ROG Fearless Eye that’s displayed prominently on the side of the chassis. Full Aura Sync RGB support allows gamers to synchronize lighting across their entire setup for a cohesive look.

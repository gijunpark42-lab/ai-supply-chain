Solaris Energy Infrastructure, Inc. (NYSE:SEI) (“Solaris” or the “Company”), today announced updated Adjusted EBITDA guidance ranges for the third and fourth quarters of 2026 and an initial guidance range for the first quarter of 2027. The Company’s new expectations reflect stronger contributions from both its core power services offerings and better-than-expected performance from its recently acquired businesses.

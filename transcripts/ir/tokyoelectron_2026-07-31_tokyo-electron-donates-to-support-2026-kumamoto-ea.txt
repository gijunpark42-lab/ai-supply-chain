Tokyo Electron Donates to Support 2026 Kumamoto Earthquake Relief Efforts

Tokyo Electron (TEL; Head Office: Minato-ku, Tokyo; President: Toshiki Kawai) has decided to donate 100 million yen to Kumamoto Prefecture to aid relief and recovery efforts from the 2026 Kumamoto Earthquake occurred on July 28, 2026.

We extend our deepest sympathies and condolences for the victims of this disaster.

We sincerely hope that the afflicted areas can realize a quick recovery.

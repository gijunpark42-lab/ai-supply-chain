Lasertec Achieves Platinum Status in RBA VAP Audit

2026.06.03

The RBA is an international organization dedicated to promoting responsible business practices in global supply chains. The RBA Code of Conduct establishes standards relating to labor, health and safety, the environment, ethics, and management systems.

VAP audits are conducted by RBA-approved third-party organizations to evaluate a company’s compliance with the RBA Code of Conduct. The audit found that Lasertec’s business operations are appropriately managed and executed across all evaluated areas. As a result, Lasertec achieved a perfect score of 200 points and was awarded Platinum Status, the highest level of recognition.

Guided by its corporate philosophy, “Inventing for your success, inventing for the future”, Lasertec will continue to promote responsible business practices and contribute to the realization of a sustainable society.

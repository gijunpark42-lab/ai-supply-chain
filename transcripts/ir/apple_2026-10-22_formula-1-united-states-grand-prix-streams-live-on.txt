Formula 1® United States Grand Prix™ streams live on Apple TV this weekend - Apple

opens in new window

UPDATE

October 22, 2026

Formula 1® returns to the U.S. this weekend with the United States Grand Prix™, streaming live on Apple TV

First Formula 1 race to be broadcast in Dolby Atmos will deliver a more immersive viewing experience for fans

This weekend, the United States Grand Prix streams live on Apple TV, delivering a premium viewing experience with comprehensive coverage and analysis.

Formula 1® returns to the U.S. this weekend for the FORMULA 1® MSC CRUISES UNITED STATES GRAND PRIX™ 2026 — streaming in the U.S. exclusively on Apple TV, with every session live and on demand. 1

The championship battle intensifies as F1 enters the final quarter of the season. Watch Mercedes’s Kimi Antonelli chase history at the United States Grand Prix, as the Italian has a chance to clinch his first Drivers’ Championship and become the youngest to win the title. The stakes are high as teams arrive at Circuit of The Americas in Austin, Texas, for a pivotal weekend in the championship race.

The Ultimate Destination for F1 Fans in the U.S.

Apple TV is the U.S. home of F1 — delivering comprehensive coverage and analysis, powerful and customizable viewing features, and seamless integration across the Apple ecosystem to Apple TV subscribers.

United States Grand Prix coverage will feature English and Spanish commentary and access to up to 30 additional live feeds across all sessions, including Driver Tracker for a bird’s-eye view of the race, session timing and data feeds, a mixed onboard feed that automatically switches between cameras as the action unfolds, and a Podium feed that dynamically follows the drivers running in P1, P2, and P3 throughout. 2

Viewers can also get closer to the action with Multiview, a feature that brings together up to four live feeds on a single screen for an even more immersive race-day experience. And now Multiview is available on supported smart TVs in addition to Apple devices and streaming players — so more fans than ever can follow every angle and won’t miss a moment.

F1 on Apple TV Now Available in Dolby Atmos and Dolby Vision

Starting with the United States Grand Prix, Dolby Atmos® will come to F1 on Apple TV, joining Dolby Vision® to give U.S. subscribers an even more immersive race-weekend experience. Dolby Atmos surrounds fans with the sounds of race weekend, from team radio and commentary to roaring engines, on-track battles, and pit-lane intensity. Paired with Dolby Vision’s rich colors and lifelike clarity, the combination delivers exceptional sound and spectacular visuals that put fans at the heart of the action.

F1 in Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos is available through the Apple TV app on supported Apple devices and compatible Dolby-enabled TVs, soundbars, and home theater systems.

Original F1 Programming on Apple TV

Apple TV brings fans closer to the action with original programming, delivering fresh expert perspectives to race weekend and sharing deeper insights into the stories shaping F1 — from every curve, overtake, and podium.

Circuits in Focus — the dynamic track preview series featuring 2016 F1 World Champion Nico Rosberg and content creator, record-breaking car builder, and driver Emelia Hartford — returns to Apple TV for the Formula 1 United States Grand Prix. Ahead of race weekend, Rosberg and Hartford take viewers inside one of F1’s most exciting circuits, breaking down the technical challenges, race strategies, and split-second decisions that could shape the fight for the podium.

Using EA SPORTS™ F1® 25, the official video game of the FIA Formula One World Championship™, Rosberg and Hartford put fans in the driver’s seat as they navigate Circuit of The Americas’ signature corners, dramatic elevation changes, and high-speed straights, revealing the key overtaking opportunities and strategic moments that could make all the difference on race day.

Full Throttle returns with an adrenaline-fueled episode that combines thrilling action, adventure, and unexpected moments. Hosted by content creator and engineer Christina Roki, whose personality and passion for all things cars set the tone, this energetic segment pairs her with F1 driver Yuki Tsunoda as he puts his driving skills to the test on the new off-road track at Circuit of The Americas. Packed with excitement and plenty of surprises, Full Throttle delivers a fresh, fun, and engaging take on the world of racing.

Fans can follow the race after it wraps with The POV, a social series that features former Red Bull Racing senior technician Calum Nicholas and Roki reacting to key moments from the weekend. Whether highlighting decisive overtakes, strategy calls, or unexpected turning points, the series’ coverage blends technical expertise with a fan-first perspective.

Together, these shows make it easy for audiences to engage with F1 on and off the track.

Race Weekend Comes to Apple Domain NORTHSIDE

In celebration of the United States Grand Prix, Apple TV is bringing the thrill of race weekend to Apple Domain NORTHSIDE, in Austin, with exclusive programming and interactive sessions.

On Thursday, October 22, special Today at Apple sessions inspired by the Haas F1 Team invite customers to capture portraits of official driver suits and helmets on iPhone 18 Pro, or design custom car liveries on iPad Pro with Apple Pencil Pro. Outside on the lawn, attendees can view Haas’s special-edition livery up close and join a live Q&A with Haas team principal Ayao Komatsu, F1 drivers Esteban Ocon and Ollie Bearman, and American F1 Academy driver Kaylee Countryman. Fans can register to reserve a spot for the F1-themed Today at Apple sessions.

The action culminates on Sunday, October 25, when fans can catch the Apple TV broadcast of the United States Grand Prix streamed live at the store.

Follow Race Weekend with Apple Sports

Fans can keep up with their favorite teams and drivers all season long with the free Apple Sports app for iPhone. Designed for speed and simplicity, the app puts favorite leagues and teams front and center. F1 fans get real-time leaderboards, drivers’ and teams’ standings, and live updates for every qualifying, Sprint, and race session — all with quick access to the live race page on Apple TV.

With Live Activities, 3 Apple Sports delivers real-time updates to the iPhone Lock Screen and Apple Watch so fans never miss a moment. Game Card Sharing lets fans share their excitement for any Grand Prix. Fans can also follow F1 across Apple TV, Apple Sports, and Apple News with My Sports, which syncs users’ favorites across supported apps.

Explore the United States Grand Prix in Austin with Apple Maps

F1 fans can use Apple Maps to explore the Grand Prix in immersive detail, including a comprehensive circuit map with turn numbers, grandstands, and specially designed 3D landmarks like the F1 pit building, COTA Tower, and COTALAND Theme Park. For fans attending in person, Maps makes it easy to get around the Grand Prix, with useful information about grandstands, entry gates, rideshare locations, road closures, pedestrian walkways, and more.

Additionally, Apple Maps users can better navigate Austin with the new Detailed City Experience, which offers more in-depth road coverage, improved directions, and immersive 3D landmarks, including Q2 Stadium, Sixth Street, and Moody Center. Locals and visitors can also discover some of the city’s best places to eat, drink, and shop with country singer-songwriter Dylan Gossett’s Austin Spots guide.

Exclusive Playlists and DJ Mixes on Apple Music

Apple Music subscribers can get into race weekend mode for the United States Grand Prix with an exclusive new DJ mix from producer, songwriter, and GRAMMY Award winner Diplo. Racing Bulls driver Liam Lawson will also debut his personal driver playlist, giving listeners a glimpse into the songs that fuel his focus on and off the track. Subscribers can explore Apple Music’s curated collection celebrating the pinnacle of motorsport with more driver playlists, exclusive DJ mixes, and a refreshed Formula 1: Sounds of the Circuit playlist capturing the energy of race weekend and beyond.

In-Depth Coverage and a Glimpse into Race Weekend with Apple News

Fans can stay up to date on race weekend in Apple News with notifications and personalized coverage from leading publishers, and can track their favorite drivers and teams for live results, standings, and highlights. Users can also view the race directly within the News app through a special United States Grand Prix feature and easily jump to the Apple TV app for the full F1 streaming experience.

Experience the United States Grand Prix Live in IMAX

Fans across the country can also experience the United States Grand Prix live in IMAX, with special screenings at more than 50 locations nationwide. See every turn and straightaway come to life on IMAX’s expansive screens, with precision surround sound that puts fans closer to every engine roar. In the heart of Austin, fans can catch the action at a special local screening at the IMAX Theatre at the Bullock Texas State History Museum.

More information and tickets are available at imax.com/f1-live-in-imax .

Tubi Creator-Hosted Live Altcast

Tubi Original The Fast Lane returns for its second live F1 altcast of the 2026 season, streaming free on Tubi for the United States Grand Prix in Austin. Hosted by YouTuber Michelle Khare ( Challenge Accepted ) and former racing champion and motorsports personality Justin Bell ( Drive to Wynn ), the creator-led altcast offers fans an alternative way to experience the action of the Grand Prix from Circuit of The Americas. Blending race insights, real-time driver tracking, and onboard footage with entertaining, culture-forward commentary, Tubi’s The Fast Lane delivers a fresh and accessible complement to the live race coverage on Apple TV.

How to Watch

F1 on Apple TV is available in the U.S. on the Apple TV app on iPhone; iPad; Apple TV 4K; Apple Vision Pro; Mac; popular smart TVs from Samsung, LG, Sony, VIZIO, TCL, and others; Roku and Amazon Fire TV devices; Chromecast with Google TV; PlayStation and Xbox gaming consoles; and at tv.apple.com , for $14.99 per month with a seven-day free trial for new subscribers. For a limited time, eligible customers who purchase and activate a new iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, or Mac can enjoy three months of Apple TV for free. Visit apple.com/tvpr and see the full list of supported devices .

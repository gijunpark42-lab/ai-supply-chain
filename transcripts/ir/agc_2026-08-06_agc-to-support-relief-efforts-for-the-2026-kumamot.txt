AGC to Support Relief Efforts for the 2026 Kumamoto Earthquake ｜ News ｜ AGC

AGC (AGC Inc., Headquarters: Tokyo; President: Yoshinori Hirai) has decided to donate 5 million JPY through the Japanese Red Cross Society to support those affected by the 2026 Kumamoto Earthquake. AGC will also provide approximately 7,500 liters of drinking water as emergency relief supplies.

We would like to express our deepest condolences to those who lost their lives and our heartfelt sympathies to all those affected by the 2026 Kumamoto Earthquake. We sincerely hope for the earliest possible recovery and reconstruction of the affected areas.

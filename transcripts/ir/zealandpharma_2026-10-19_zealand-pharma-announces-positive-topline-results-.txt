Zealand Pharma announces positive topline results from the multiple ascending dose part of Phase 1 trial with Kv1.3 ion channel blocker ZP9830

ZP9830 is a novel Kv1.3 ion channel blocker with pipeline-in-a-product opportunity across a broad range of cell-mediated autoimmune diseases

Multiple doses of ZP9830 were well tolerated with no serious or severe adverse events or dose-limiting safety findings

Development program is progressing rapidly with a Phase 1b trial in people with plaque psoriasis recently initiated, and a Phase 2 trial in an additional autoimmune disease planned for initiation in H1 2027

Copenhagen, Denmark, October 19, 2026 – Zealand Pharma A/S (Nasdaq: ZEAL) (CVR-no. 20045078), a biotechnology company transforming the future of metabolic health, today announced positive Phase 1 multiple ascending dose (MAD) topline results for the company’s novel Kv1.3 ion channel blocker, ZP9830. The MAD part of the first-in-human, single-center, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 1 trial was designed to evaluate the safety, tolerability, and pharmacokinetics (PK) of multiple doses of ZP9830 in 32 healthy male and female participants.

Multiple doses of ZP9830 were found to be safe and well tolerated in this trial with a treatment duration of 4 weeks and a safety follow-up period until day 41. No dose-limiting safety findings or other significant safety concerns were observed. All treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs) in participants receiving ZP9830 were non-serious and mild in severity. No clinically relevant safety findings were observed in vital signs, 12-lead electrocardiogram (ECG) recordings, physical examinations, or safety laboratory parameters. PK parameters support continued development in trials of longer duration.

" These results represent an encouraging milestone for ZP9830. In this multiple ascending dose part of the Phase 1 trial, ZP9830 was well tolerated and no dose limiting safety findings were observed. The findings support continued development in studies of longer duration, " said David Kendall, MD, Chief Medical Officer of Zealand Pharma. " This brings us one step closer to our goal of delivering a novel immunological treatment to patients. We are excited to investigate the potential of this novel asset across multiple autoimmune and inflammatory diseases. Our Phase 1b trial in plaque psoriasis is already under way, and the data announced today give us the confidence to expand the development program and explore an additional autoimmune disease in 2027.”

Zealand Pharma initiated a Phase 1b trial with efficacy endpoints, investigating ZP9830 in people with plaque psoriasis. The trial was initiated on the back of positive topline results from the single ascending dose (SAD) part of the combined SAD/MAD Phase 1 clinical trial with ZP9830 announced earlier this year.

About the Phase 1 trial

The first-in-human single ascending dose (SAD) /multiple ascending dose (MAD) Phase 1a trial (ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT06682975) was conducted in healthy male/male and female participants to investigate the effects of single or multiple ascending doses of ZP9830 administered subcutaneously, as well as a single dose intravenously at one dose level. The main objectives were to assess safety, tolerability, and pharmacokinetics, with proof of mechanism as an additional exploratory objective. The SAD part of the trial included a suitable pharmacological model to explore immunomodulatory activity and provide early confirmation that ZP9830 affects immune cell biology as intended. The results from the SAD part of the trial were reported earlier this year. The MAD part of this Phase 1 trial is now completed. The trial continues to evaluate safety, tolerability, and pharmacokinetics of an additional single dose of ZP9830.

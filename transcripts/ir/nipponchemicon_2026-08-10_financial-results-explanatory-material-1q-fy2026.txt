Main Points

◇ Net sales (market trends)

◆ICT and AI server market

Sales related to PC and gaming devices were impacted by the

difficulty in obtaining memory and other semiconductors but sales of

AI and general-purpose servers for data centers were favorable.

◆Automotive electronics market

Global automobile sales numbers were weak due to a slowdown in

domestic demand in China but sales of xEV increased in European

markets.

◆Industrial equipment market

Demand grew on semiconductor and data center-related

investments. Demand also increased rapidly as the market moved

past a long period of inventory adjustments.

◇ Operating profit

Despite the impact of soaring material prices, profit increased

significantly on increased sales, particularly for AI servers and other

high-added-value products, and improvements to our product mix.

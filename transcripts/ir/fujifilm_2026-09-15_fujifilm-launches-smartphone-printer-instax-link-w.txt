Fujifilm Launches Smartphone Printer “instax Link WIDE 2™”

News Release September 15, 2026

Fujifilm Launches Smartphone Printer “instax Link WIDE 2 ™ ”

Mix More Memories into One Large instax ™ Print

Featuring the New “Photo Mixer ™ ” Function for Easy Creation of Scrapbook-Like instax ™ Prints

TOKYO, September 15, 2026 – FUJIFILM Corporation announces the launch of the instax ™ smartphone printer “instax Link WIDE 2 ™ ” (“Link WIDE 2”), which enables users to print images stored on their smartphones and will be available on October 9, 2026, in Japan.

Key visual for the “instax Link WIDE 2 ™ ”

Examples of collages created with the “Photo Mixer ™ ” function

The Link WIDE 2 is a WIDE format smartphone printer that produces instax ™ prints twice the size of mini-format prints. Following the globally successful instax Link WIDE ™ , known for its ability to produce large and vivid instax ™ prints of group photos and landscapes, the Link WIDE 2 features enhanced design and upgraded functionality.

The dedicated app *1 now features the new “Photo Mixer ™ ” function, which automatically creates and suggests collage designs by combining multiple images. Users can easily create scrapbook-like instax ™ prints using their favorite photos of places, people, and memorable moments stored on their smartphones. Making full use of the large WIDE format print area, the function allows users to bring together highlights from trips, events, and other special occasions that could not previously fit into a single print, creating a memorable keepsake that captures the highlights of an entire experience in a single print.

The new body design features a two-tone color scheme that combines a soft light color with a sophisticated dark color. Available in two color options, “Duo Beige” and “Duo Blue,” the printer can be displayed with either the light-colored side or the dark-colored side facing outward, allowing users to change the appearance of the printer to match their mood or room décor.

Fujifilm will continue to expand the world of the instax ™ instant photo system by proposing new ways to enjoy photography through instax ™ prints.

2. Main Features

(1) New “Photo Mixer ™ ” Function Creates Scrapbook-like Collages with Ease

The new “Photo Mixer ™ ” function automatically combines favorite scenes, people, and items from multiple images to create scrapbook-like collages. The app generates and suggests multiple collage options based on images selected by the user. Once a preferred collage is chosen, the app automatically adjusts image size, angle, layout, text placement, and font style to refine the design, creating a collage tailored to the user's preferences.

Suggested collages can also be edited before printing. In addition to adjusting the size and placement of photos, users can add text and stamps to further customize the design, creating a one-of-a-kind collage tailored to their preferences.

In addition, the app features a cutout function powered by image analysis technology. The function automatically extracts people and objects from photos, allowing users to incorporate highlights of memorable moments into collages. By incorporating cutout images, users can create scrapbook-like collages that emphasize favorite people and objects, resulting in more dynamic and personalized instax ™ prints.

(2) WIDE Format for Versatile Photographic Expression

The WIDE format, which is twice the size of the mini format, offers versatile photographic expression, making it ideal for group shots, landscapes, and other scenes that benefit from a larger print area. (Film size: 86 mm × 108 mm)

Compatible with the WIDE format, the largest format in the instax ™ lineup

(3) Stylish Body Design that Blends Naturally into Interior Spaces

The Link WIDE 2 features a two-tone body design that combines a soft light color with a sophisticated dark color. Available in two color options, “Duo Beige” and “Duo Blue,” the new design blends naturally into living spaces and can be displayed elegantly on its own without requiring an additional stand.

The instax ™ logo positioned at the lower right of the body features soft illumination inspired by indirect lighting, adding a subtle accent to interior spaces. During printing, the logo gently glows with a gradient of pale pink and blue, creating a sense of excitement and anticipation as instax ™ prints come to life

Body design that also complements interior spaces

(4) Seamless Connectivity with Multiple Devices for Easy Printing

In addition to smartphones, tablets, and PCs, the Link WIDE 2 can also connect with Fujifilm’s X Series digital cameras and the palm-sized digital camera “instax Pal 2 ™ ”. The expanded connectivity allows users to print a wider variety of content in the instax ™ format, including digital artwork and game screenshots stored on a PC, as well as photos captured with cameras.

Supports “Direct Print,” enabling direct printing from the “instax Pal 2 ™ ” to the printer

Printer driver for Bluetooth connectivity with PCs, released in 2024

Supports “Direct Print,” enabling direct printing from compatible cameras to the printer

Compared to the instax Link WIDE ™ , the Link WIDE 2 features faster Bluetooth ®*2 communication, reducing image transfer time for a quicker and more seamless printing experience.

In addition, when connecting with a Google Pixel device, the Link WIDE 2 supports Google Fast Pair Service *3 , which simplifies the initial setup process between the printer and the dedicated app. When the printer is powered on, setup notifications for both the printer and app are automatically displayed on the smartphone *4 , allowing users to connect easily by following the on-screen instructions.

*4 The printer and app setup notification will not be displayed if the initial setup between the dedicated app and the smartphone printer has already been completed.

3. Promotion of “instax Link WIDE 2 ™ ”

The Link WIDE 2 ™ not only allows users to print images stored on their smartphones as instax ™ prints, but also features the new “Photo Mixer ™ ” function, which brings multiple memories together into one large instax ™ print. Users can enjoy instax ™ prints in a variety of ways, such as displaying them on a shelf or preserving travel memories in a postcard-like format. The promotional tagline,“get more in the mix,” expresses the appeal of bringing multiple memories together in a single instax ™ print and revisiting those moments all at once. The tagline will be used online, on social media and at stores to communicate the appeal of the Link WIDE 2 ™ to as many people as possible.

About the instax ™ Smartphone Printer Lineup

Fujifilm offers the Link series of instax ™ smartphone printers, which allow users to easily create high-quality instax ™ prints from images stored on their smartphones. Since the Link series enables users to print smartphone photos anytime and anywhere through the dedicated app via Bluetooth ® connection, it has been widely embraced by users of all generations around the world. The lineup includes the “instax Link WIDE ™ ,” compatible with the WIDE format for larger prints; the “instax SQUARE Link ™ ,” which produces prints in the popular square format; and the “instax mini Link ™ ” series, compatible with the card-sized mini format. Through this diverse lineup, Fujifilm continues to expand the ways people can enjoy photography and instax ™ prints.

About the instax ™ brand

Since its original launch in 1998, the instax ™ series of instant cameras has embraced trends and evolving technologies with time and expanded its lineup to include not only analog instant cameras, but also hybrid instant cameras using digital technology, smartphone printers and palm-sized digital camera. The instax ™ continues to offer diverse ways to enjoy photography, broadening the user base in more than 100 countries.

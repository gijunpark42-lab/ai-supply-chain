JX Advanced Metals Corporation

Notice Regarding Inclusion in the Nikkei Stock Average (Nikkei 225)

JX Advanced Metals Corporation (President: Hayashi Yoichi; hereinafter "the Company") hereby announces that it has been selected for inclusion in the Nikkei Stock Average (Nikkei 225). The Company's stock is scheduled to be included in the calculation of the Nikkei 225 effective October 1, 2026.

For further details regarding this matter, please refer to the announcement issued by Nikkei Inc.

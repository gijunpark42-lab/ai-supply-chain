Winbond Announces Monthly Revenue for August

Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Sep. 04, 2026 – Winbond Electronics Corporation (TWSE: 2344) today announced its unaudited revenue for August 2026.

On a consolidated basis, including Winbond Electronics Corp., Nuvoton Technology Corp., and other subsidiaries, revenue in August 2026 was NT$27.309 billion, representing a 2.00% MoM increase and a 289.43% YoY increase. Consolidated revenue for January through August 2026 totaled NT$152.18 billion, an increase of 177.39% compared to the same period in 2025.

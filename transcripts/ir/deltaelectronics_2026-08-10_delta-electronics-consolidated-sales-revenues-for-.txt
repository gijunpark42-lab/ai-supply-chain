Delta Electronics’ Consolidated Sales Revenues

for July 2026 Totaled NT$67,073 Million

TAIPEI, August 10, 2026 – Delta Electronics, Inc. today announced its consolidated sales revenues for July 2026 totaled NT$67,073 million, a 47.7 percent increase as compared to NT$45,397 million for July 2025 and a 2.2 percent increase as compared to NT$65,603 million for June 2026. The Company's cumulative consolidated sales revenues from January to July in 2026 were NT$409,682 million, a 42.1 percent increase as compared to NT$288,352 million for the same period in 2025.

Percentage of sales for Delta’s four main business categories for July 2026:

Power Electronics

57%

Mobility

4%

Automation

7%

Infrastructure

32%

Others

0%

The figures have not been audited.

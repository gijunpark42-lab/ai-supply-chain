FSP Unveils the World's First 80 PLUS 230V Ruby-Certified ATX Power Supply, Leading the Green Revolution in Next-Gen AI Computing

2026-09-22

[Taipei, Sep. 22 nd , 2026] As the global wave of artificial intelligence (AI) accelerates, the surge in large language model training and edge-side inference has driven an exponential demand for computational power, bringing unprecedented challenges in energy consumption. A global trailblazer in power supply architectures, FSP Group today officially announced a groundbreaking innovation: the world’s first 850W ATX power supply certified with 80 PLUS 230V Ruby—the industry’s highest energy efficiency benchmark. In response to the EU ErP Lot 9 regulation mandating a minimum of Titanium efficiency for data center power supplies, this product leaps past the threshold with revolutionary Ruby-grade efficiency. It sets a new standard for energy efficiency and carbon reduction, establishing an exceptional benchmark for high-performance AI workstations and edge computing nodes.

Pushing R&D Boundaries: The World’s First 80 PLUS Ruby Certification

Under extreme computational workloads, thermal dissipation and energy conversion efficiency are paramount to system stability. Drawing on decades of deep expertise in high-frequency power electronics and architectural optimization, FSP broke through conventional topology constraints. By maximizing conversion efficiency under 230V input conditions, FSP became the first to secure the prestigious 80 PLUS 230V Ruby certification.

This milestone demonstrates that the unit outperforms traditional Titanium-grade efficiency across all load profiles, slashing heat dissipation losses to unprecedented lows. Fully compliant with EU Ecodesign directives for next-gen data center power architectures, it empowers enterprise clients to significantly lower Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) metrics, cut cooling energy overhead, and reduce total cost of ownership (TCO)—delivering an ideal low-carbon, high-compute solution for hyperscale cloud providers and enterprise infrastructure.

Engineered for High-Density Computing: Superior 850W AC-to-ATX Architecture

Tailored for demanding computing environments, this 850W power supply features a standard AC-to-ATX output design. It delivers robust peak-power tolerance and rapid dynamic load response, effortlessly handling instantaneous power spikes generated by modern high-end GPUs and accelerator cards during massive neural network training runs.

To meet the structural constraints of high-density server chassis and compact AI workstations, the unit is equipped with an industrial-grade 80 mm high-static-pressure cooling fan. Leveraging intelligent thermal control and fluid-dynamic airflow channeling, the 80 mm fan delivers concentrated, high-volume airflow within tight physical dimensions. Even under sustained full-load conditions, it ensures uniform heat dissipation, acoustic quietness, and extended operational lifespans (MTBF) for critical 24/7 workloads.

Powering AI Together: FSP and Partners Driving an Intelligent Future

As generative AI and intelligent IoT transform industries worldwide, standard hardware supply alone can no longer satisfy fast-evolving market needs. FSP remains dedicated to its core brand vision “Powering AI Together” moving beyond vendor roles to co-develop solutions from early architectural stages alongside global AI chipmakers, system integrators (SI), and industry innovators.

From power architecture definition and thermal fluid simulations to compliance with international green directives, FSP leverages agile customization and robust manufacturing delivery to resolve power and thermal bottlenecks rapidly. The debut of the world’s first 80 PLUS 230V Ruby-certified power supply reaffirms FSP’s technical development capabilities and unwavering commitment to global partners. Moving forward, FSP will continue expanding its high-efficiency, high-density, and compliant green power portfolio, working side by side with industry leaders to power a sustainable global AI ecosystem.

Key Product Highlights:

Industry-Leading Efficiency: World's first 80 PLUS® 230V Ruby-certified ATX PSU, compliant with stringent EU ErP Lot 9 standards for online data storage products.

Rated Power: 850W output engineered for high dynamic load capacity and transient peak endurance.

Form Factor & Output: Standard AC-to-ATX output, fully compatible with high-density server environments.

Thermal Solution: Industrial-grade 80 mm smart thermal-controlled fan balancing form factor, static pressure, and long-term reliability.

Target Applications: Next-generation data centers, edge computing nodes, and high-performance AI workstations.

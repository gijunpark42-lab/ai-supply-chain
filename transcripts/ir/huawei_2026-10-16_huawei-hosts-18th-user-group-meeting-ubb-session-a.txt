Huawei Hosts 18th User Group Meeting UBB Session Alongside UBBF

Summary:

The UBB session of the 18th Huawei User Group Meeting(HUGM) was held alongside Huawei UBBF, where Huawei held in-depth discussions with more than 40 global customers.

Remaining dedicated to joint exploration and continuous innovation with its customers, Huawei continues to build leading products and professional services. By advancing the intelligent upgrade of IP networks, optical networks, and end-to-end professional services with an unyielding focus on quality, Huawei empowers customers to succeed with quality for a better future.

[Vienna, Austria, October 16, 2026] The UBB session of the 18th Huawei User Group Meeting (HUGM) was held alongside Network X and Huawei's Ultra Broadband Forum (UBBF). The session gathered over 40 global UBB customers and industry experts for in-depth exchanges, interactive discussions, and roundtables designed to elicit real-world business requirements and feedback, and to share best practices across regions.

To establish all-round competitiveness and commercial advantages, telecom operators require highly efficient, high-quality UBB networks. This need becomes even more critical in the intelligence era: without robust, resilient, and high-quality network connectivity, computing power and data centers risk becoming isolated islands, affecting individual, home, and enterprise users alike. To address these demands, Huawei is committed to collaborating with global customers throughout the entire lifecycle of network infrastructure—spanning planning, construction, O&M, optimization, and operations—to deliver efficient, resilient, and ubiquitous high-quality broadband networks with an ultimate user experience.

HUGM 2026 UBB Session: Listening · Collaborating · Improving · Developing

Network Traffic Visibility, Management, and Governance for Precise QoE Issue Analysis and Differentiated Service Operations

Leveraging core backbone and access network elements such as Internet gateway (IGW) and broadband network gateway (BNG), Huawei enables full-spectrum visibility into network traffic flows and service awareness. This establishes a solid data foundation for refined bandwidth governance and monetization. By orchestrating key network elements for real-time policy adjustment and dynamic quality of service (QoS) guarantees, Huawei enables telecom operators to deliver high-value service acceleration and differentiated offerings, accelerating their strategic transition from "monetizing bandwidth" to "monetizing experience." Furthermore, an integrated analysis mechanism centered on quality of experience (QoE) enables the root causes for network faults and experience bottlenecks to be identified within seconds, driving a complete shift toward proactive and agile network operations and governance.

Proactive Risk Identification and Rapid Fault Recovery to Enhance Network Reliability and Ensure Zero Service Disruption During Fiber Cuts

Powered by dynamic chirp pulse analysis technology, Huawei's industry-first integrated sensing and communication solution accurately detects optical cable hazards, including third-party construction and co-trenching risks, without disrupting live network services. With AI-enhanced wavelength switched optical network (WSON) technology, millisecond-level service protection is achieved, reducing service downtime by 90% and lowering overall network protection costs by 30%. In addition, the OTNSpirit agent driven by O&M intent recognition, along with professional optimization approaches, rapidly identifies fault root causes and performs precise diagnostics. This cuts fault location time by 90% and reduces overall O&M costs by 40%, delivering a comprehensive boost to network reliability, responsiveness, and operational efficiency.

Visualized and Manageable Optical Cable and FTTx O&M for Precise Cut Location and Rapid Repair

To address customer pain points regarding the lack of proactive optical cable O&M capabilities, Huawei has created an end-to-end FTTx digital twin network (DTN) foundation covering services, networks, and optical cables. By integrating AI agents, Huawei delivers a comprehensive solution for optical cable hazard monitoring, identification, location, and closed-loop resolution. The solution provides end-to-end visualization and management of optical cable faults while enabling minute-level impact assessments. Combined with precise fiber breakpoint location and intelligent repair navigation, the mean time to repair (MTTR) is reduced by 25%, and the rate of major network incidents is lowered by 20%, ensuring consistent delivery of high-quality broadband connections.

Green CO: Driving Low-Carbon Operations and Optimal Benefits via Digital and Intelligent Planning and Modernization to Create Strategic Assets

Utilizing a cross-domain green CO planning platform alongside intelligent multi-objective optimization algorithms for site selection, Huawei's solution accurately targets high-value areas with over 90% site selection accuracy, over 95% service fulfillment, and 99.999% service availability. This enables optimal modernization pathways for green COs, achieving an optimal balance across carbon reduction, network resilience, and economic efficiency—effectively transforming existing COs into valuable strategic assets for the intelligence era.

Huawei remains dedicated to joint exploration and continuous innovation with global customers. By advancing the intelligent upgrade of IP networks, optical networks, and end-to-end professional services with an unyielding focus on quality, Huawei empowers customers to succeed with quality for a better future.

FAQs:

Q1: What is the background and purpose of the HUGM?

Hosted by Huawei, the HUGM focuses on quality improvement for ICT products and services in the carrier business domain. Since its inception in 2009, the HUGM has consistently listened to the voice of global customers, addressing their most pressing concerns to drive product quality enhancements. Through exceptional products and professional services, the HUGM continues to create value for customers.

Q2: What other activities does the HUGM include besides the UBB session?

MBB Session: Scheduled to be held alongside MWC Barcelona in March 2027, this session will feature in-depth discussions on the intelligent upgrades of wireless networks, cloud core networks, and end-to-end services. Global customers are welcome to register and participate.

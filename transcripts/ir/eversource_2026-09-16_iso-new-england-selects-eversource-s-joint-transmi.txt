ISO New England selects Eversource’s joint transmission project as preferred long-term solution to enhance reliability and affordability

September 16, 2026

PDF Version

Project would expand transmission capacity between Maine and New Hampshire, improve reliability, and deliver significant regional economic benefits

MANCHESTER, N.H., Sept. 16, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Eversource today announced ISO New England’s (ISO-NE) selection of the company’s joint Transmission Initiative Down East (TIDE) project as the preferred longer-term transmission solution to meet regional needs outlined by ISO-NE in its competitive transmission solicitation process. The proposed TIDE project will enhance reliability for customers and enable new energy supply sources in northern New England – easing congestion on the regional transmission system and helping to address affordability.

Through ISO-NE’s competitive bid process, TIDE emerged as the strongest overall and most cost-effective solution to meet the region’s energy needs with significant customer benefits – including expanded system capacity, reduced congestion costs, enhanced reliability and the ability to integrate to the regional grid up to 1,200 megawatts of future onshore wind generation in northern New England. The proposed project still requires extensive engineering, environmental, permitting and regulatory reviews, with construction anticipated to begin in 2029 pending all state and regulatory approvals.

“This selection is an important step toward delivering the modern, reliable transmission infrastructure New England needs while enabling new supply resources that are so critical to addressing our region’s longstanding energy affordability challenges,” said Bill Quinlan, President of Transmission at Eversource. “Transmission solutions like this project can help improve affordability for customers across New Hampshire and all of New England by enabling greater supply capacity and easing congestion costs while also ensuring customers have the highly reliable service they demand. We look forward to further engagement with our communities, ISO New England and other stakeholders to bring these benefits to the region.”

According to ISO New England’s analysis, TIDE is projected to provide substantial regional benefits at a more than 2-to-1 cost-benefit ratio, including:

$1.42 billion in production cost and congestion savings

$1.30 billion in avoided capital investment

$694 million in avoided transmission investment, which ultimately equates to significant savings for customers

Eversource’s portion of the TIDE project will primarily involve upgrades and replacement of infrastructure entirely within existing rights-of-way in New Hampshire, with additional work in Maine and Massachusetts. In New Hampshire, the project includes a new 18-mile, 345 kilovolt (kV) line, a rebuild of one of the existing 345 kV lines from a two-pole, H-frame design to a single-pole, reconductoring one of the existing 345 kV lines and installation of equipment to help regulate voltage and power quality. The project is designed to maximize the use of established transmission rights-of-way and existing infrastructure, which minimizes environmental and community impacts while efficiently delivering capacity, strengthening the grid, and relieving energy bottlenecks for the entire region.

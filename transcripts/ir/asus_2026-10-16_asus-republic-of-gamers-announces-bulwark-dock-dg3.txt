ASUS Republic of Gamers Announces Bulwark Dock DG310

October 16, 2026

・

Press Release

Perfect accessory for ROG Xbox Ally family turns the handheld into a desktop or console with a single cable

Classic ROG styling: Black finish with RGB accents complement ROG gear, while 6-in-1 connectivity ensures compatibility with a wide range of peripherals

Built for TV gaming: HDMI® 2.1 output supports 4K@144Hz, variable refresh rate, auto low-latency mode, and HDR for the ultimate couch gaming experience

One-touch Bluetooth® pairing: Instantly connect to wireless peripherals with the press of a button

TAIPEI, Taiwan, October 15, 2026 — ASUS Republic of Gamers (ROG) today announced the ROG Bulwark Dock DG310, the perfect companion for the ROG XBOX Ally family of gaming handhelds. Offering 6-in-1 connectivity and distinctive ROG design cues that blends easily into existing setups, the Bulwark Dock can instantly transform an ROG Xbox Ally into a full desktop or living room battlestation with just a single USB-C® connection. HDMI® 2.1 support provides the dock with variable refresh rate (VRR), auto low-latency mode (ALLM), and high dynamic range (HDR) technologies. With seamless Bluetooth® pairing for wireless peripherals, the Bulwark Dock makes it easy to connect and get straight into the game.

Classic ROG styling

Designed to be a must-have gaming accessory for any ROG XBOX Ally gaming handheld, the Bulwark Dock sports the classic ROG aesthetic and includes an ergonomic stand that ensures unimpeded airflow for the handheld when docked. A fixed 60cm USB-C cable with a 90° connector provides a clean single-cable connection to the handheld for a clutter-free setup. Built-in 6-in-1 connectivity turns the ROG XBOX Ally into the heart of a full desktop or gaming console, boasting up to 80-watt power delivery (when using a 100-watt charger) via USB-C, HDMI 2.1, 1Gb Ethernet port for wired networking, and three USB Type-A 3.2 Gen1 ports for a mouse, keyboard, or controller. Four selectable RGB lighting modes enable gamers to create a unified gaming setup and set the mood for different games.

Built for TV gaming

The Bulwark Dock’s HDMI 2.1 port supports the latest features in both high-end gaming displays and the latest smart TVs. VRR adjusts the refresh rate of the display to match the game’s framerate to reduce screen tearing and stuttering for smoother gaming experiences. An Ally connected to the Bulwark Dock will also be recognized by supported TVs as an XBOX, automatically switching to the TV’s low-lag Game mode for more responsive gameplay. In addition, 4K@144Hz support enables gamers to play games in native resolution on a 4K TV, while HDR ensures supported games are able to offer brighter visuals for detailed highlights and incredible color on the latest displays.

One-touch Bluetooth pairing

The Bulwark Dock’s 6-in-1 connectivity via USB-C offers near-limitless flexibility across most setups, with one-touch Bluetooth pairing to support a wide range of wireless accessories. With a press of a button, gamers can pair their headsets, controllers, and other peripherals to their ROG XBOX Ally directly from the dock, without complicated menus.

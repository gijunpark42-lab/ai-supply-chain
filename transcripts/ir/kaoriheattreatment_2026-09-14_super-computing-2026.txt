KAORI will be exhibiting at SC26 – The International Conference for High Performance Computing, Networking, Storage and Analysis , taking place November 17–19, 2026 , at McCormick Place in Chicago.

As AI and high-performance computing continue to drive higher rack power densities, liquid cooling is becoming an increasingly critical part of next-generation data center thermal management.

At SC26, KAORI will showcase high-performance heat exchangers engineered for In-Row CDUs and In-Rack CDUs , along with heat exchanger solutions designed for commonly deployed cooling architectures across both White Space and Gray Space environments.

With efficient, compact, and flexible heat exchanger designs, KAORI supports CDU manufacturers, cooling solution providers, and data center infrastructure developers in addressing a wide range of capacity, footprint, and system architecture requirements.

Visit us at Booth #7110 and discover how KAORI heat exchanger technology can support the next generation of AI and HPC liquid cooling infrastructure.

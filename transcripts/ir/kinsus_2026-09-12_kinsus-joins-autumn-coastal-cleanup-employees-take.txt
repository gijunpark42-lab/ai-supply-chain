Kinsus Joins Autumn Coastal Cleanup, Employees Take Action to Protect the Marine Environment

Kinsus Interconnect Technology continues to put its commitment to sustainable development and environmental protection into action. In support of the Hsinchu City Government’s 2026 Autumn Coastal Cleanup Campaign, 36 Kinsus employees took part in a coastal cleanup initiative, removing a total of 132.02 kilograms of marine debris. Through this hands-on effort, Kinsus demonstrated its commitment to marine ecosystem conservation, environmental sustainability, and community engagement.

The cleanup brought together government agencies, businesses, civic organizations, and community volunteers. Kinsus employees worked collaboratively to collect, sort, and transport waste along the coastline, including larger discarded items such as used tires. Beyond improving the coastal environment, the activity also helped participants gain a deeper understanding of marine pollution, waste reduction at source, and resource circularity.

Kinsus has long integrated environmental sustainability into its business operations and continues to advance energy conservation and carbon reduction, resource circularity, environmental protection, and employee sustainability education. Through employee participation in this coastal cleanup, Kinsus extended its environmental commitment beyond business operations to the broader community and natural environment, while strengthening teamwork and sustainability awareness among employees.

Looking ahead, Kinsus will continue to combine corporate resources with employee participation to deepen environmental protection and community engagement. Through a wide range of sustainability initiatives, the Company will respond to stakeholder expectations, create positive social impact, and contribute to cleaner oceans and a more sustainable environment.

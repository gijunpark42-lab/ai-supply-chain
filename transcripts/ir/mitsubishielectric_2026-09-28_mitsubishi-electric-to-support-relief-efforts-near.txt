TOKYO, September 28, 2026 – Mitsubishi Electric Corporation (TOKYO: 6503) announced today that the Mitsubishi Electric Group will donate a total of 5 million yen (approximately US$ 32,000) to aid victims of flooding and debris flows that occurred near the Nepal–China border in August.

The Mitsubishi Electric Group expresses its deepest sympathies to all those affected by the disaster and extends its very sincerest wishes for a speedy recovery. The Group will continue its efforts to support those affected by the disaster.

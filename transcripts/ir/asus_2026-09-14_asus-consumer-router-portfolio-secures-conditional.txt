ASUS Consumer Router Portfolio Secures Conditional FCC Approval

September 14, 2026

・

Press Release

Uninterrupted availability for United States supply is cleared by the Federal Communications Commission to lead the transition to the WiFi 8 era

Smooth advancement of next-generation WiFi 8 technology may now proceed

ASUS has achieved leading supply chain resilience & logistics compliance

TAIPEI, Taiwan, September 14, 2026 — ASUS today announce d that it has proactively secured Conditional Approval from the Federal Communications Commission across its entire consumer router portfolio, addressing market uncertainties and establishing a seamless, uninterrupted supply chain in the United States as the company accelerates the launch of next-generation WiFi 8 technology.

By proactively securing FCC Conditional Approval across its entire retail networking portfolio, ASUS has set a benchmark for business continuity. This pivotal authorization communicates to retail partners, national distributors, and e-commerce channels that the current inventory and future product playbooks of ASUS remain cleared for uninterrupted import, marketing, and sales in the United States.

While evolving US regulatory frameworks can create inventory vulnerabilities and potential customer service issues elsewhere in the market, the comprehensive compliance now achieved by ASUS enables peace of mind for buyers. Retailers can move forward with predictability, knowing that their store shelves can remain consistently stocked with fully authorized ASUS networking devices backed by long-term cloud security infrastructure and continuous software updates.

Rooted in a 35-year legacy of trusted engineering in the United States, ASUS remains committed to supporting the long-term evolution of the retail networking ecosystem while delivering full compliance with current regulatory frameworks. With this approval in place, ASUS intends to focus its ongoing efforts on promoting and advancing the development of next-generation Wi-Fi 8 technology standards. ASUS continues to develop internal research timelines, working closely with international technology partners to develop secure and compliant networking solutions. This responsible approach enables ASUS to remain a steady, reliable contributor to the future of wireless connectivity, fostering the technological progression of high-performance networks at a steady and fully compliant pace.

As a globally recognized technology leader, ASUS anchors its compliance architecture upon a foundation of operational transparency and an unrivaled worldwide manufacturing footprint. By maintaining a proactive, resilient engineering model, ASUS offers retail partners leading supply chain stability and business continuity for its networking devices. This agile and highly diversified global logistics network across strategic international manufacturing hubs also enables robust, uninterrupted production logic that satisfies the highest standard of international import and equipment authorization frameworks. This deep operational resilience avoids the complex regulatory transitions and logistics uncertainties occurring elsewhere in the US market, establishing ASUS as a stable, reliable, and entirely transparent partner for long-term commercial networking device collaboration.

LITEON Technology Reports Consolidated September Sales of NT$18.8 Billion, Up 22% Y-o-Y

Summary

LITEON’s consolidated revenue for September reached NT$18.8 billion, representing a 22% Y-o-Y increase. Strong demand for AI and cloud computing high-end server power systems, high efficiency backup battery systems (BBU), and advanced opto-electronic semiconductors drove solid revenue growth on yearly basis. The cumulative sales for January to September totaled NT$153.6 billion, up 26% Y-o-Y.

LITEON Technology (2301-tw) today reported its September consolidated revenue of NT$18.8 billion, up 22% Y-o-Y. Revenue growth was mainly driven by high-end server power systems for cloud and AI applications, high‑efficiency backup battery units (BBU), and advanced opto-electronic semiconductors. The cumulative sales for January to September totaled NT$153.6 billion, up 26% Y-o-Y.

Cloud & AIoT Business contributed a 59% share of total sales, of which, benefiting from the delivery growth for power management systems of high-end datacenter server and cloud computing, Cloud & AIoT business posted a yearly growth over 50%.

Opto-electronics Business contributed a 14% share of sales, of which, LITEON’s photocouplers used in industrial automation, new energy, and AI power applications; advanced visible LED indicator featuring high integration and miniaturization for AI servers and switches continued to see solid demand, driving 10% Y-o-Y revenue growth for the segment.

Information Technology & Consumer Electronics Business accounted for 27% of sales, of which, with the ongoing diversified application markets, demand for LEO satellite power systems and advanced power supplies continue to grow steadily.

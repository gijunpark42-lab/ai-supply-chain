Kioxia Selected for Inclusion in Three FTSE Russell ESG Investment Indices for the First Time

July 31, 2026

Kioxia Holdings Corporation

Kioxia Holdings Corporation, a world leader in memory solutions, today announced that it has been selected for the first time as a constituent of three of FTSE Russell's leading ESG investment indices: the FTSE4Good Index Series, the FTSE JPX Blossom Japan Index, and the FTSE JPX Blossom Japan Sector Relative Index.

These indices were developed by FTSE Russell, an index provider owned by London Stock Exchange Group, to measure and evaluate companies’ environmental, social and governance (ESG) practices and performance. They are used in the creation and evaluation of sustainable investment products. The FTSE4Good Index Series covers companies worldwide, while the FTSE JPX Blossom Japan Index and the FTSE JPX Blossom Japan Sector Relative Index focus on Japanese companies.

Kioxia regards sustainability as one of its highest management priorities. Its inclusion by FTSE Russell as a constituent of these indices underscores the importance of the Group's comprehensive ESG initiatives, including those aimed at mitigating climate change, building a sustainable supply chain, and respecting human rights.

With this latest inclusion, the Company now features in four ESG investment indices for Japanese equities adopted by Japan's Government Pension Investment Fund (GPIF): the FTSE JPX Blossom Japan Index, the FTSE JPX Blossom Japan Sector Relative Index, the S&P/JPX Carbon Efficient Index, which evaluates companies' environmental initiatives, and the Morningstar Japan ex-REIT Gender Diversity Tilt Index, which assesses companies' efforts to promote gender equality.

Guided by its mission of “Uplifting the world with ‘memory,’” Kioxia will continue to leverage its memory technologies to support the evolution of a digital society, while further strengthening its sustainability initiatives to address social issues and help build a more sustainable future.

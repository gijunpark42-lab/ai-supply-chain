Nuvoton Introduces I3810YYI Digital ChipCorder ® with Integrated 3W Class-D Amplifier for High-Performance Audio Playback

Hsinchu, T aiwan – September 22 , 2026 – As voice prompts, status notifications, and audible alerts become increasingly important in embedded systems, developers need audio solutions that deliver both clear sound and sufficient output power while keeping system designs compact and efficient. Nuvoton Technology, a leading global semiconductor provider, introduces the I3810YYI Digital ChipCorder ® , a high-performance audio playback device featuring an integrated 3W Class-D amplifier, designed to simplify the implementation of high-quality voice and audio functions across a wide range of embedded applications.

The I3810YYI combines digital audio decompression, advanced memory management, a fully digital audio signal path, and multi-channel playback capabilities in a highly integrated solution. With flexible audio sources and control interfaces, it enables developers to implement voice prompts, notifications, alerts, and sound effects with reduced system complexity.

Integrated 3W Class-D Amplifier for Powerful Audio Output

A key highlight of the I3810YYI is its integrated high-efficiency, filter-free Class-D amplifier, capable of directly driving a speaker. At a 5V supply, the device delivers up to 3W into a 4Ω speaker and 1.7W into an 8Ω speaker, providing strong audio output without requiring a separate power amplifier in many embedded audio designs. By integrating the power amplifier into the device, the I3810YYI helps reduce external component requirements and simplifies the overall audio signal chain. It also provides 8-bit digital volume control, PGA (Programmable Gain Amplifier) gain adjustment, and attenuation settings, giving developers additional flexibility to optimize audio output for different speaker configurations and product designs.

High-Quality Audio Playback up to 48 kHz

Beyond its high-power audio output, the I3810YYI supports audio sampling rates of up to 48 kHz, enabling clear and high-quality playback for voice prompts, notifications, alerts, and sound effects. The device supports multiple audio compression formats, including µ-Law, Differential µ-Law, PCM, Enhanced ADPCM, and variable-bit-rate optimized compression, allowing developers to balance audio quality and storage efficiency according to application requirements. The internal master sample rate can operate at either 32 kHz or 48 kHz, providing additional flexibility for different audio content and system requirements.

Three-Channel Playback and Mixing for Flexible Audio Design

The I3810YYI features three independent audio playback channels, each with its own memory management and decompression resources. Any or all three channels can be mixed together, while each channel can be independently controlled. The channels may also operate with different audio sample rates, allowing developers to combine multiple audio streams within a single system. This architecture provides greater flexibility for applications requiring combinations of voice prompts, notifications, and sound effects.

Up to 2 Gbit External SPI Flash for Scalable Audio Storage

To support applications requiring extensive voice or audio content, the I3810YYI provides a 28-bit external Flash address space, supporting SPI Flash capacities of up to 2 Gbit. Audio can be played directly from external SPI Flash, while the device also supports audio input through I²S or direct digital audio streaming from an MCU through the SPI interface. This flexible architecture allows developers to select the most appropriate audio source and storage configuration for their application.

The I3810YYI also supports multiple audio playback and data streaming paths, including Flash-to-PWM, SPI / I²C-to-PWM, I²S-to-PWM, Flash-to-I²S, and Flash-to-SPI / I²C, providing additional flexibility for system integration.

For developers seeking to add stronger audio output without significantly increasing system complexity, the I3810YYI provides a compact and flexible solution that combines voice playback, audio processing, multi-channel mixing, and 3W speaker-driving capability in a single device.

AbCellera Announces Presentation of Phase 1/2 Data for ABCL635 at TMS 2026 Annual Meeting

October 8, 2026

VANCOUVER, British Columbia--(BUSINESS WIRE)--

AbCellera (Nasdaq: ABCL) today announced an upcoming poster presentation of Phase 1/2 data on ABCL635 at The Menopause Society’s (TMS) 2026 Annual Meeting, to be held October 20 to 24 at the Hilton Bayfront in San Diego, California.

Details on AbCellera’s poster presentation at TMS are as follows:

Title: Safety, pharmacokinetics, pharmacodynamics, and preliminary efficacy of ABCL635, an NK3R monoclonal antibody antagonist for the treatment of vasomotor symptoms: a Phase 1/2 study

Poster ID: P-92

Date and Time: Thursday, October 22 from 6:15 p.m. to 7:15 p.m. PDT

Location: Indigo Exhibit Hall (Level 2), Hilton Bayfront

AbCellera will present safety, pharmacokinetics, pharmacodynamics, and efficacy data from its Phase 1/2 trial. This includes new safety data from the Phase 1 multiple ascending dose study, as well as new patient-reported outcome data from the Phase 2 study.

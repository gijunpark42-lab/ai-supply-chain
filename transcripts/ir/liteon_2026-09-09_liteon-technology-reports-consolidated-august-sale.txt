LITEON Technology Reports Consolidated August Sales of NT$19.6 Billion, Up 3% M-o-M and 25% Y-o-Y

Summary

LITEON’s consolidated revenue for August reached NT$19.6 billion, representing a 3% M-o-M and 25% Y-o-Y increase. Strong demand for AI and cloud computing high-end server power systems, high efficiency backup battery systems (BBU), and opto-electronic semiconductors drove solid revenue growth on both a monthly and yearly basis. The cumulative sales for January to August totaled NT$134.7 billion, up 27% Y-o-Y.

LITEON Technology (2301-tw) today reported its August consolidated revenue of NT$19.6 billion, up 3% M-o-M and 25% Y-o-Y. Revenue growth was mainly driven by high-end server power systems for cloud and AI applications, high‑efficiency backup battery units (BBU), and opto-electronic semiconductors. The cumulative sales for January to August totaled NT$134.7 billion, up 27% Y-o-Y.

Cloud & AIoT Business contributed a 60% share of total sales, of which, benefiting from the delivery growth for power management systems of high-end datacenter server and cloud computing, Cloud & AIoT business posted a yearly growth over 40%.

Opto-electronics Business contributed a 14% share of sales, of which, LITEON’s photocouplers used in industrial automation, new energy, and AI power applications; infrared applications for invisible LED; advanced visible LED indicator featuring high integration and miniaturization for AI servers and switches continued to see solid demand, contributing to nearly 20% Y-o-Y revenue growth for the segment.

Information Technology & Consumer Electronics Business accounted for 26% of sales, of which, with the ongoing optimized product mix and diversified application markets, steady shipment growth of LEO satellite power systems and advanced power supplies drove the segment’s revenue to increase 5% M-o-M.

Introducing HPE ProLiant Gen13: Reliability redefined for AI-era workloads

October 7, 2026 | Krista Satterthwaite, SVP & GM of Compute, HPE

Blog Post Security AI Compute Exec Authored

Four new servers combine workload-focused performance with automated protection and simpler lifecycle management

In this article

Reliability is the top reason customers choose HPE ProLiant and a key differentiator for 81%

HPE ProLiant Gen13 gives customers more choice in how they support demanding applications, scale AI adoption, and modernize their infrastructure

Three industry-first advances raise the bar for server security and management

HPE Compute Ops Management and HPE Tech Care help reduce downtime, simplify management, and resolve issues up to 2x faster

As organizations scale AI, infrastructure supports more of the work that keeps the business moving – from financial models and manufacturing simulations to AI applications. These environments are more complex to protect and manage. When systems go down, critical work can slow or stop.

That is why reliability today must extend beyond consistent performance. It must include built-in protection, simpler management, and faster support throughout the system lifecycle.

This holistic definition of reliability shaped the next evolution of HPE ProLiant. Today, we are announcing HPE ProLiant Gen13 Servers, designed to bring these capabilities together for the next era of enterprise and AI workloads. With HPE ProLiant Gen13, we’re making stronger, automated protection part of everyday operations while delivering the dependable performance customers expect from HPE ProLiant servers.

81% of HPE customers cite HPE ProLiant’s reliable performance as a key differentiator from other server vendors.

For more than 30 years, reliability has been central to the HPE ProLiant experience. And for four consecutive years, customers have ranked it as the leading reason they purchase HPE ProLiant servers. In fact, 81% of HPE customers cite HPE ProLiant’s reliable performance as a key differentiator from other server vendors. 1 We’re also seeing customers turn to HPE after facing reliability challenges with other providers.

Every new HPE ProLiant generation undergoes more than 1,000 tests and 10,000 hours of validation before it reaches customers. That rigor helps organizations maintain the consistent performance, availability, and resilience their business-critical workloads demand.

Our commitment to reliability is reflected in the four servers we’re introducing today. Powered by 6 th Gen AMD EPYC 9006 Series Server Processors, they combine industry-first advances, performance built for demanding workloads like AI, and intelligent operations that reduce manual effort.

That matters to the people responsible for keeping their businesses productive and protected.

Four new servers built for the work ahead

The first four ProLiant Gen13 servers give customers more ways to address demanding workloads, accelerate AI adoption, and optimize their data center footprint through AI-ready platforms with distinct architectures.

The HPE ProLiant DL525 Gen13 and HPE ProLiant DL585a Gen13 are standard rack servers for customers seeking the latest performance within conventional data center power and cooling.

The HPE ProLiant DL525 Gen13 Server is ideal for performance and memory-intensive workloads such as modeling and simulation, electronic design automation (EDA), AI inferencing, and virtualization. It combines up to 256 cores, expanded memory bandwidth, and next-generation connectivity in a compact, single-socket platform.

The HPE ProLiant DL585a Gen13 Server delivers substantial accelerator capacity for organizations ready to put AI into production to run inference and visualization capabilities. With support for up to eight GPUs, customers have the flexibility to scale, especially in areas such as agentic AI, inferencing, fine tuning, and small model training.

The HPE ProLiant XD245 Server and HPE ProLiant XD285 Server are designed for customers that need greater rack-level scale, density, and thermal flexibility.

Both systems are compliant with Open Compute Project (OCP) guidelines and engineered to run large datasets at extreme scale, including large-scale modeling and simulation for scientific discovery or product design.

The HPE ProLiant XD245 Server uses direct liquid cooling to remove heat efficiently and support more computing power in a smaller footprint, while the HPE ProLiant XD285 Server is air-cooled, giving customers another path to greater rack-level power and compute density when direct liquid cooling is not the right fit for their environment.

All four servers bring workload-focused performance together with the security, management, and support experience customers expect from HPE ProLiant.

As AI, modeling, and other compute-intensive workloads move into production, starting with a trusted, secure foundation is a critical way to reduce risk.

Setting a new standard for server security and management

Customers are processing some of their most valuable intellectual property, and often using that data in new, highly sensitive ways. As AI, modeling, and other compute-intensive workloads move into production, starting with a trusted, secure foundation is a critical way to reduce risk.

HPE ProLiant Gen13 introduces three industry firsts:

Automatic data protection from first boot. Self-encrypting drives encrypt the data, while HPE iLO 8 automatically provisions and manages the keys that secure it. This removes the separate configuration required today, protecting data at rest from the moment the server powers on.

Two-person approval for high-impact actions. Multi-party authorization implements a two-person approval workflow for actions such as key provisioning and secure erase, reducing risk from human error or malicious activity.

Post-quantum protection beyond firmware. PQC-enabled server protection extends into critical communications between system components, reducing exposure to vulnerabilities that could compromise critical workloads.

The value is practical: fewer protection gaps, stronger controls, and less dependence on manual configuration.

Keeping infrastructure productive throughout its lifecycle

Deployment is only the beginning of the HPE ProLiant experience. Throughout a server’s lifecycle, teams must keep systems secure, current, and productive – without adding manual effort.

HPE Compute Ops Management helps simplify that work. Its agentic AI-powered intelligence is designed to assess advisories against a customer’s environment, identify affected servers, and guide remediation. A Forrester study found that organizations using HPE Compute Ops Management had an 80% reduction in unplanned downtime, spent 50% less time managing servers, and achieved a 209% return on investment. 2

Integrated support workflows connect alerts, diagnostics, and case information with HPE experts through HPE Tech Care Service. By reducing repeated investigation and handoffs, teams can resolve issues up to 2x faster than with third party support maintainers.

That combination of rigorous engineering, intelligent management, and expert support is central to why customers continue to choose HPE ProLiant.

The next chapter of HPE ProLiant

The four servers announced today give customers more choice in how they support demanding applications, scale AI adoption, and modernize their infrastructure. And this is only the beginning. We’ll expand the HPE ProLiant Gen13 portfolio later this year, bringing these advantages to even more workloads and operating environments. This broader portfolio will help customers match the right system to their specific performance, deployment, and business needs.

To learn more about HPE ProLiant Gen13 and explore the new servers announced today, visit: HPE ProLiant Compute | HPE.

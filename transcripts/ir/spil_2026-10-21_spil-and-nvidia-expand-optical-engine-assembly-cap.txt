SPIL and NVIDIA Expand Optical Engine Assembly Capacity for Co-Packaged Optics (CPO) in Taiwan

Agreement accelerates high‑volume production to meet global demand for next‑generation AI networking systems

TAIPEI, Taiwan — October 21, 2026

Following NVIDIA's announcement, Siliconware Precision Industries Co., Ltd. (SPIL), a subsidiary of ASE Technology Holding Co., Ltd., today announced a new agreement to expand optical engine assembly capacity in Taiwan with NVIDIA. The expansion enables higher‑volume manufacturing of advanced optical engines that support the bandwidth and power‑efficiency requirements of AI factories. The companies started low volume production in Q3’26 and expect to ramp production by 2027 by 15X current levels with additional doubling by Q4 2028.

Expanding capacity to meet accelerating AI networking demand

The partnership focuses on scaling the assembly of optical engines, used in CPO products—critical components that deliver high‑throughput, low‑latency connectivity for AI clusters. The expanded Taiwan operations will increase SPIL’s optical‑engine assembly throughput and shorten lead times. The agreement builds on a multi‑year collaboration between SPIL and NVIDIA and responds to rapidly rising demand for AI‑optimized networking systems.

Joint engineering and manufacturing integration

Under the agreement, SPIL will buildout additional high‑precision optical‑assembly lines and collaborate with NVIDIA’s engineering teams to optimize manufacturing yield and performance. The companies expect the expanded operations to support broader adoption of CPO systems across cloud, enterprise, and telecommunications markets.

In addition, SPIL will also perform testing on NVIDIA’s co‑packaged optics (CPO) modules and support assembly for future silicon‑photonics products. SPIL’s established capabilities in wafer bumping, chip‑level packaging, and CPO module testing strengthen the manufacturing pipeline for CPO products as NVIDIA scales production of its next‑generation photonics engines.

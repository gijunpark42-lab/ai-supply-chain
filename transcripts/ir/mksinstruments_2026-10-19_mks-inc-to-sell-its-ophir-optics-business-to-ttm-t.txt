MKS Inc. to Sell Its Ophir® Optics Business to TTM Technologies, Inc.

PDF Version

ANDOVER, Mass. , Oct. 19, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MKS Inc. (NASDAQ: MKSI), a global provider of enabling technologies that transform our world, today announced that it has entered into a definitive agreement to sell its Ophir® Optics business to TTM Technologies, Inc. (“TTM”) in an all-cash transaction for $650 million , subject to customary closing adjustments. The purchase price represents an estimated multiple of over 18x projected FY 2026 Adjusted EBITDA for the Ophir Optics business.

The Ophir Optics business provides high-precision infrared thermal imaging and laser optics solutions to customers in MKS’ Specialty Industrial market, serving defense, security and industrial applications.

TTM is a leading global manufacturer of technology products, including mission systems, radio frequency (“RF”) components, RF microwave/microelectronic assemblies, and technologically advanced interconnect products, including printed circuit boards and substrates.

“The Ophir Optics business highlights how we can apply our broad technology capabilities to serve attractive opportunities outside our core Semiconductor and Electronics & Packaging markets. This transaction represents an opportunity for MKS to capitalize on the value we’ve created with the Ophir Optics business and further strengthen our balance sheet,” said John T.C. Lee , President and Chief Executive Officer, MKS Inc. “We believe TTM is well positioned to support this business in its next phase of growth.”

This transaction does not include the MKS Ophir Instruments laser characterization business, which operates separately from Ophir Optics, or the sale of the Ophir brand. Ophir Instruments provides laser power measurement and beam profiler solutions core to our photonics solutions portfolio, and there will be no impact on the products, services, and support MKS provides to its Ophir Instruments customers.

The transaction is expected to close in the second quarter of 2027, and is subject to customary closing conditions, including certain regulatory approvals and certain governmental consents and permits.

J.P. Morgan Securities LLC is serving as exclusive financial advisor and Herzog Fox & Neeman is serving as lead legal advisor to MKS in connection with this transaction.

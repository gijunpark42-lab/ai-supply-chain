Rehlko Presents the Next-Generation Command Pro® 747 at Equip Expo 2026

October, 2026 Email Print

Designed for professional turf applications, the next-generation Command PRO ® 747 combines dependable commercial performance, simplified maintenance and installation flexibility in a compact, OEM-ready platform.

MILWAUKEE, Wis. – October 15, 2026 – Rehlko, a global industrial energy resilience platform providing custom-engineered, integrated power solutions for mission-critical infrastructure worldwide, showcases the new Command PRO® 747, the latest addition to its next-generation Command PRO® family of commercial gasoline engines. Making its debut at Equip Exposition 2026, the Command PRO ® 747 is designed for professional turf applications, combining dependable performance, simplified ownership and installation flexibility in a compact platform.

Available from 23 to 25 hp, the Command PRO ® 747 is designed to simplify equipment integration while delivering the reliability and durability professionals expect from Rehlko commercial engines. Its compact, OEM-ready design provides installation flexibility across a wide range of commercial turf equipment.

“This launch reflects our ongoing commitment to innovation and to delivering the high-performance power solutions our customers need to succeed,” said Eric Fontaine, President of the Engines business at Rehlko. “Professional operators need equipment they can count on every day. The next-generation 747 is designed to deliver dependable performance, simplified ownership and the confidence to keep working.”

Built around the needs of landscapers, fleet operators and OEM partners, the Command PRO ® 747 incorporates robust construction and extended maintenance intervals designed to help reduce downtime and simplify service throughout the working day.

By combining extended maintenance intervals with a compact, installation-friendly design, the Command PRO ® 747 can help support lower total cost of ownership while giving OEMs greater flexibility when integrating commercial power into their equipment.

As part of the next-generation Command PRO ® family, the Command PRO ® 747 brings a practical approach to commercial power, focusing on the needs of professionals who rely on their equipment every day. The new platform reflects Rehlko’s continued commitment to developing purpose-built solutions that help professionals stay productive.

Rehlko will showcase its latest commercial engine solutions at Equip Exposition 2026 in Louisville, Kentucky, giving customers and industry professionals the opportunity to experience the Command PRO ® engine family firsthand.

Visitors can discover Rehlko’s commercial engine portfolio across two dedicated experiences:

Indoor Exhibit Facility – Booth 7176: where Rehlko will present its latest engine technologies, innovations, and commercial power solutions.

Outdoor Demo Area – Booth 6046D: where visitors can see Rehlko-powered machines in action and experience the performance, durability, and productivity benefits of the Command PRO ® engine family.

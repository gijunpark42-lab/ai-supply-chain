- Shipment marks entry into the U.S. power and AI infrastructure market

- Ceremony was held at Doosan Enerbility's Changwon headquarters with SpaceXAI project leadership in attendance

Doosan Enerbility announced that it had completed the manufacturing of its 380MW gas turbine (Model DGT6-300H S2) for the U.S. company SpaceXAI, and had recently held a ceremony to celebrate the shipment of the product.

The shipment ceremony was held at Doosan Enerbility's gas turbine assembly shop in Changwon. Over 70 key officials were in attendance, including key project and business leaders from SpaceXAI and Doosan, among which were Seungwoo Sohn, CEO of Doosan Enerbility's Power Services Business Group.

During the visit, the SpaceXAI delegation was provided with a tour of the gas turbine shop from where they observed the shipment process. Representatives from both companies also held discussions on future collaboration opportunities.

The gas turbine will be deployed as part of SpaceXAI's expanding infrastructure investments in the United States. Having satisfied the strict technical requirements of the global tech leader, Doosan Enerbility will be working with SpaceXAI to support the company's energy future.

“This shipment proves that the quality and technology of our homegrown gas turbines are recognized in the global AI supply chain,” said Seungwoo Sohn, CEO of Doosan Enerbility's Power Services Business Group. “We will now accelerate our expansion into North America and the wider global gas turbine market.”

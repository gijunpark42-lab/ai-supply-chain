Lenovo Introduces Its First Googlebook, Delivering Premium AI Experiences in a Sleek, Lightweight Design

Powered by Intel Core Ultra 5 processors, Lenovo Googlebook 15 combines built-in Gemini Intelligence with Lenovo’s trusted design and years of Chromebook expertise.

28 September 2026

Up to 47 TOPS helps users leverage Gemini Intelligence to create, stay organized, and work more efficiently throughout the day.

A vibrant 15-inch 2.8K OLED display, responsive haptic touchpad, quad-speaker system with Dolby Atmos®, and up to 12.5 hours of battery life¹ deliver an exceptional viewing and listening experience alongside all-day productivity wherever users go.

September 28, 2026 – Lenovo today introduced the Lenovo Googlebook 15, its first Googlebook, expanding the company’s laptop portfolio by combining Google’s computing experiences with Lenovo’s signature design, engineering, and user-focused innovation.

Powered by Intel Core Ultra 5 processors with built-in Gemini Intelligence, up to 32GB of memory² and up to 512GB of storage², the Lenovo Googlebook 15 helps users stay productive and connected – no matter where the day takes them.

Designed for professionals, creators and business owners, the Lenovo Googlebook 15 delivers up to 47 TOPS of performance while weighing under 3 lbs., making it the lightest 15-inch device in Lenovo’s portfolio.

“The Lenovo Googlebook 15 represents the next step in Lenovo’s collaboration with Google and our commitment to making powerful intelligent experiences accessible to more people,” said Benny Zhang, Executive Director and General Manager of Chromebooks in Lenovo’s Intelligent Devices Group. “By combining Google’s intelligence capabilities with Lenovo’s expertise in bringing devices to life through thoughtful design and engineering, we are expanding our portfolio with a new option designed for today’s connected lifestyles.”

See Every Detail – Feel Every Moment

The Lenovo Googlebook 15 features a stunning 15-inch 2.8K OLED touchscreen display with a 120Hz refresh rate 3 and 100% DCI-P3 color coverage, making everything from creative projects to everyday browsing look sharper and more vibrant.

A responsive haptic touchpad provides precise tactile feedback for smoother navigation, while the quad-speaker system with Dolby Atmos® delivers rich, room-filling audio for music, movies, and video calls. Support for intuitive pinch, zoom, and precision scrolling further enhances the premium, immersive user experience.

Lightweight by Design. Built to Last.

With its lightweight design, durability to help withstand everyday bumps and wear, and up to 12.5 hours of battery life, 1 the Lenovo Googlebook 15 combines portability with resilience, giving users the freedom to move seamlessly between work, creativity, and downtime.

Thoughtful design details elevate the experience even further. The signature Glowbar lets users check battery and charging status, even when the lid is closed, while a premium anti-fingerprint-coated metal chassis feels smooth to the touch. An integrated fingerprint reader and facial authentication enable fast, protected sign-in, and a built-in camera shutter gives users added privacy.

Smarter Productivity, Built In

Gemini Intelligence 4 helps individuals get from idea to action in seconds. With Magic Pointer, 5 the cursor just got its first glow-up in years to let individuals do more than simply point and click. Wiggle the cursor to activate Gemini and turn two images into a poster or add a date to the calendar instantly. The Lenovo Googlebook 15 also stays seamlessly in sync with Android™ devices, making it easy to switch between devices and access apps and files directly from a connected phone.

With Rambler 6 , voice-to-text becomes smarter and more natural. Users can press the dictation key on their Googlebook to automatically remove filler words and add formatting, transforming their stream of consciousness into polished text. Whether brainstorming ideas, drafting emails, or coding, users can speak naturally while Rambler keeps up, even when they switch languages mid-sentence.

The device also includes 12 months of Google AI Pro 7 , giving users expanded access to powerful features in the Gemini App, 5 TB of cloud storage to keep photos and files backed up, and subscriptions to apps like Adobe Photoshop, Premiere, and CapCut.

Designed specifically for Googlebook, Lenovo AI Mouse makes everyday AI interactions faster and more intuitive. Users can instantly access Gemini with a click, launch contextual actions with Magic Pointer, and personalize controls to fit the way they work.

By combining Google’s latest software and AI capabilities with thoughtful design, premium audiovisual experiences, and all-day productivity 1 , the Lenovo Googlebook 15 delivers a smarter, more intuitive computing experience that adapts to the way people work, create, learn, and stay connected.

Pricing & Availability 8

The Lenovo Googlebook 15 will be available starting October 4 th , 2026 starting at $1,099.99.

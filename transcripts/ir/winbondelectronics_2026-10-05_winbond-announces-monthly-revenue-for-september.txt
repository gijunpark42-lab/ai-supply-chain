Winbond Announces Monthly Revenue for September

Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Oct. 05, 2026 – Winbond Electronics Corporation (TWSE: 2344) today announced its unaudited revenue for September 2026.

On a consolidated basis, including Winbond Electronics Corp., Nuvoton Technology Corp., and other subsidiaries, revenue in September 2026 was NT$28.249 billion, representing a 3.44% MoM increase and a 256.67% YoY increase. Consolidated revenue for January through September 2026 totaled NT$180.43 billion, an increase of 187.39% compared to the same period in 2025.

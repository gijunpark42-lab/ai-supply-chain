ASUS Republic of Gamers Announces ROG Archer Suitcase 20

August 24, 2026

・

Press Release

Carry-on suitcase with quick laptop access, silent 360° wheels, and durable protection for essential gear make travel easier for gamers, creators, and professionals

Quick-access front compartment stores up to a 16" laptop and other gaming essentials

Hinomoto Miraclent 360° Silent Wheels support smooth, quiet movement across any surface

Polycarbonate hardshell and TSA-compliant lock ensure safe travels anywhere

TAIPEI, Taiwan, August 24, 2026 — ASUS Republic of Gamers (ROG) today announced the ROG Archer Suitcase 20, a 20-inch carry-on suitcase designed for gamers, creators, and professionals. As the first suitcase in the Archer series, it combines classic ROG style with practicality, quiet mobility, and reliable protection. The dedicated front compartment gives quick access to up to a 16-inch laptop during security checks or in transit, while the main compartment safely stores clothing and other gear. The ROG Archer Suitcase 20 also features TSA-compliant locks and a durable hardshell construction.

Premium ROG design with smart organization

The ROG Archer Suitcase 20 stands out with its black finish and distinctive ROG styling. Subtle design details, including the ROG Fearless Eye, give it a premium look.

The quick-access front compartment is built for convenience during travel, providing space for up to a 16-inch laptop along with dedicated pockets for a power adapter, mouse, and keyboard, as well as a dedicated strap for a headset. This section is double-padded to keep all gear safe. Inside the main clamshell compartment, a zippered divider and a Y-shaped compression strap secures clothing , toiletries, and other belongings.

Smooth, quiet travel

The ROG Archer Suitcase 20 uses four Hinomoto Miraclent 360° Silent Wheels for smooth, low-noise movement across a range of surfaces. These premium wheels glide effortlessly, enabling users to easily handle the suitcase through busy terminals. The wheel hubs are engineered to keep the ROG Fearless Eye logo upright while the suitcase moves, adding a thoughtful design detail. For convenience, the upper side studs also work as built-in hooks for small bags or umbrellas while the suitcase stands upright.

Durable construction and dependable security

The ROG Archer Suitcase 20 features TSA-compliant locks to secure both the main and quick-access front compartments through a single mechanism, while its 100% Covestro Makrolon polycarbonate hardshell resists bumps and impacts during travel. The interior includes double-padded foam protection to help safeguard devices and other packed items.

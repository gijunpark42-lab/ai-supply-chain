Qnity Joins PHLX Semiconductor Sector Index (SOX)

September 21, 2026 4:05pm EDT

WILMINGTON, Del.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--

Qnity Electronics, Inc. (“Qnity”) (NYSE: Q), a premier technology solutions leader across the semiconductor value chain, today announced that it has been added to the PHLX Semiconductor Sector Index (SOX).

“Joining the PHLX Semiconductor Sector Index marks another important milestone for Qnity as a public company,” said Jon Kemp, Chief Executive Officer of Qnity. “Our inclusion reflects the scale and relevance of our business across the semiconductor value chain and the important role our technologies play in enabling AI, high-performance computing and advanced connectivity.”

The PHLX Semiconductor Sector Index is a modified market capitalization-weighted index designed to measure and track the performance of the 30 largest eligible semiconductor companies listed in the US.

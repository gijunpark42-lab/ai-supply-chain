Hon Hai Precision Industry (Foxconn) and Mitsubishi Electric Reach Final Agreement on Strategic Alliance for Joint Operation of Automotive Equipment Business

3+3+3

Will leverage “Made in/by

Japan” brand to support sustainable growth of Japanese automotive industry

16 October 2026, Taipei, Taiwan, Tokyo

Japan – Lin Yin International Investment Co., Ltd. (Lin Yin), an investment

management subsidiary of Hon Hai Precision Industry Co., Ltd. (Hon Hai

Precision Industry) (Foxconn), and Mitsubishi Electric Corporation (Mitsubishi

Electric) announced today that they have formalized a strategic alliance for the

joint operation of an automotive equipment business. Through the alliance, the

companies aim to develop next-generation mobility and thereby strengthen the

sustainable growth of the Japanese automotive industry.

Under the

agreement, Mitsubishi Electric will transfer to Lin Yin 50% of its stake in

Mitsubishi Electric Mobility Co., Ltd., which handles Mitsubishi Electric’s

automotive equipment business, after which the two co-owners will jointly

operate Mitsubishi Electric Mobility. The share transfer price is expected to

be JPY 115.0 billion. The share transfer agreement establishes an enterprise

value of JPY 320.0 billion and an equity value of 230.0 billion for the target

business. The transfer price corresponds to a 50% stake to be transferred based

on the equity value. Subject to the satisfaction of contractual conditions,

including obtaining required approvals and clearances from relevant authorities

under competition laws and other applicable laws and regulations, the share transfer

is expected to be completed on October 1, 2027.

The

joint-operating arrangement will combine Mitsubishi Electric Mobility’s

technological and developmental capabilities, particularly in electric

powertrains, software and vehicle control, with Hon Hai Precision Industry’s

speed, scale and other strengths in market deployment.

By providing

high-quality electric-vehicle platforms (basic structures) developed in Japan,

the companies expect to contribute to the widespread adoption and advancement

of next-generation mobility. The alliance will translate advanced technologies

and quality achieved through high-level Japanese manufacturing into new value

for mobility. In turn, this is expected to strengthen Japan’s industrial base

and support the sustainable growth of the domestic automotive industry. In

addition, over the medium to long term, the companies aim to expand Japanese

technology and quality to global markets as well as further enhance the value

of “Made in/by Japan” and demonstrate new possibilities for Japanese

manufacturing to the world.

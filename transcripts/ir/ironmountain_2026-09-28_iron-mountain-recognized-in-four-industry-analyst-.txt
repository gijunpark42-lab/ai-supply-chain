Iron Mountain Recognized in Four Industry Analyst Reports with First-Time Placements

September 28, 2026

Iron Mountain InSight ® DXP adoption accelerates as enterprises look to turn unstructured assets into AI-ready data

PORTSMOUTH, N.H.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--

Iron Mountain (NYSE: IRM), a global leader in information management services, today announced its first-time inclusions in four analyst reports from Gartner, IDC, and Forrester. For Iron Mountain, these recognitions demonstrate the company’s market momentum and highlight the innovation of its AI-powered digital capabilities as enterprises seek trusted data foundations for artificial intelligence and enterprise automation.

At the core of this momentum is Iron Mountain InSight ® DXP, an AI-powered intelligent content platform that connects trusted information with governed automation across enterprise workflows. InSight DXP bridges digital and physical worlds, turning passive assets into active, actionable intelligence. By embedding proactive governance into enterprise workflows, the platform provides the trusted data foundation organizations need to deploy specialized AI agents, accelerate decision-making, and automate complex processes at scale.

The analyst reports evaluate Iron Mountain’s solutions across intelligent document processing, content services, mailroom automation, and document analytics:

2026 Gartner ® Magic Quadrant™ for Intelligent Document Processing : Named a Challenger in the 2026 Gartner ® Magic Quadrant™ for Intelligent Document Processing (IDP), Iron Mountain was recognized for its ability to execute and completeness of vision. Gartner states that “Beyond optical character recognition (OCR), IDP uses composite AI — multiple AI techniques, such as machine learning, large language models (LLMs) and knowledge graphs — to automatically extract data despite novel variations in document type and format.”

IDC MarketScape: Worldwide Intelligent Content Services (ICS) 2026 Vendor Assessment : Positioned as a Major Player , “The platform converts static corporate repositories into governed, active intelligence by managing the full information lifecycle: capture physical-to-digital twin lineage tracking, automated workflows, multi-jurisdictional retention, and AI-driven insight generation. It is built on Iron Mountain's compliance track record and holds FedRAMP High certification with HITRUST r2 certifications in progress — reducing regulatory exposure across jurisdictions.”

IDC MarketScape: Worldwide Mailroom Solutions and Services 2026 Vendor Assessment (doc # US52993325, July 2026) : Positioned as a Leader , “Iron Mountain combines an extensive physical mailroom infrastructure with a proprietary, cloud-native intelligent document processing (IDP) platform featuring agentic AI routing agents and LLM-based content understanding. With an impressive customer list that includes many Fortune 1000 companies, Iron Mountain offers a compelling combination of global operational scale, advanced AI automation, and enterprise-grade compliance certifications.”

The Forrester Wave™: Document Mining and Analytics Platforms, Q2 2026 : Recognized as a Strong Performer , Iron Mountain received the highest possible scores in the agentic AI functionality, document labeling/annotation, UI and other techniques for accuracy verification, and platform breadth/supported use cases criteria. Iron Mountain believes this highlights InSight DXP's strength in pairing automation with AI agents, LLM-driven rule generation, and enterprise-grade human-in-the-loop (HITL) quality controls.

“Organizations are embedding AI agents into enterprise workflows, but four gaps stand in the way of trusted outcomes: fragmented information, missing business context, inconsistent governance, and limited explainability,” said Narasimha Goli, Chief Technology Officer at Iron Mountain. “Success requires trusted, contextual information, strong governance, and an enterprise harness that defines what agents can access, how they act, and when people must review their decisions. For us, the first-time inclusion in these reports reinforces our strategy of helping customers unlock their dark data and put it to work through trusted automation, with the oversight and evidence needed to support measurable business outcomes.”

These recognitions follow recent customer momentum, including Iron Mountain’s accelerated expansion in the Middle East with the Social Development Bank in Saudi Arabia . The bank is deploying InSight DXP to modernize and automate its small and medium enterprise (SME) loan operations with AI-powered intelligent document processing.

⚫ Net sales increased by 26% year on year

<Reasons>  Continued yen depreciation and higher sales volume of PKG and rigid products.

Foreign exchange    The depreciation of the yen had a positive impact on business results.

Effects:                                 Average exchange rate during the first quarter:

PKG products:       Continued demand growth driven by AI adoption,

sales volume increased, mainly for dry film products for memory applications.

Rigid products:      Sales volume increased, mainly for automotive and high-density interconnect

(HDI) applications.

Sales volume of display (white dry film) products decreased.

Price revision:         In response to rising raw material and other expenses, prices of certain

products were revised for shipments from June onward.

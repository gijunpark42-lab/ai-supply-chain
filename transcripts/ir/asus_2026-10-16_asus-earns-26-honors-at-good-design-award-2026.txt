ASUS Earns 26 Honors at Good Design Award 2026

October 16, 2026

・

Press Release

ASUS leads the ICT Equipment category and stands out as the most-awarded Taiwanese company

ASUS earns 26 honors at the Good Design Award 2026, enabling the winning products to bear the prestigious G Mark symbol

ASUS Oxiis Intelligent Bike Booster and Ceraluminum™ are selected for the Good Design Award Judge’s Selection – My Favorite Design 2026

ASUS leads the ICT Equipment category and is recognized as the most-awarded Taiwanese company at this year’s awards

TAIPEI, Taiwan, October 15, 2026 — ASUS today announced it has received 26 accolades at the Good Design Award 2026. This achievement enables the winning products to carry the prestigious G Mark symbol, a recognition bestowed on high-quality, well-designed solutions. ASUS was recognized across PCs, peripherals, displays, components, personal mobility, and materials, underscoring the company’s continued commitment to design excellence, innovation, and user-centric technology.

"Earning 26 Good Design Awards is a meaningful recognition of the creativity, empathy, and rigor behind ASUS Design Thinking,” said Mitch Yang, ASUS Chief Design Officer. “Across AI PCs, creator tools, new materials, and mobility concepts, our teams are exploring how technology can become more intuitive, human-centered, and inspiring. This honor encourages us to keep pushing design and innovation forward to create incredible experiences that make a meaningful difference in people’s lives."

ASUS Leads Taiwan and the ICT Equipment Category

ASUS stood out as the most-awarded Taiwanese company at the Good Design Award 2026 and led the ICT Equipment category, reflecting the strength and breadth of its design capabilities. This year’s winning entries span premium consumer laptops, commercial PCs, creator devices, gaming products, displays, accessories, workstations, and emerging mobility solutions.

The recognition highlights ASUS Design Thinking, which focuses on understanding real user needs and translating them into products that balance performance, usability, aesthetics, and sustainability. From next-generation AI PCs and creator tools to innovative materials and mobility concepts, the awarded products demonstrate how ASUS continues to create technology that supports more intuitive, productive, and inspiring experiences.

With 26 awards, ASUS continues to demonstrate design leadership across a broad product portfolio.

Two entries selected as judges’ favorite designs

Among this year’s winners, ASUS Oxiis Intelligent Bike Booster and Ceraluminum™ were selected for the Good Design Award Judge’s Selection – My Favorite Design 2026. The distinction recognizes entries that particularly impressed the judges and reflects the ASUS drive to explore new forms of innovation beyond conventional product categories.

The two products, along with the other winners, will be on exhibition from 10/31-11/13 at the Good Design Marunouchi Shin Kokusai Building, 1st Floor, 4-1, Marunouchi 3-chome, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo.

Good Design Award 2026

The Good Design Award, established in 1957 and organized by the Japan Institute of Design Promotion, is one of Japan’s most prestigious design awards and recognized as one of the world’s top four design awards. Now in its 69th year, the program selects winners through a comprehensive, multifaceted evaluation by a panel of experts reviewing thousands of global entries. For more information, please visit: https://www.g-mark.org/en .

ASUS Recognized Among Newsweek’s World’s Most Trustworthy Companies for the Third Consecutive Year

September 11, 2026

Press Release

The recognition highlights the company's commitment to earning trust across customers, employees, and investors

ASUS named for the third time by Newsweek as one of the World’s Most Trustworthy Companies, reflecting strong stakeholder confidence

The ranking aligns with the company's commitment to a people-first culture built on a design-thinking approach and transparent supply chain practices

ASUS validated for its company values, management, and long-term potential driven by its Ubiquitous AI. Incredible Possibilities. strategy

TAIPEI, Taiwan, September 11, 2026 — ASUS today announced its inclusion among Newsweek’s World’s Most Trustworthy Companies in 2026, for the third consecutive year. This recognition is awarded to companies that have gained the trust of consumers, investors, and employees. It also underscores the company's commitment to reliability and excellence in the Technology Hardware industry.

The Newsweek ranking acknowledges companies that have earned customer confidence in their products, services, advertising, communication, and complaint handling; investor confidence in company values, management, and long-term potential; and employee satisfaction with fair treatment, compensation, workplace appeal, and career development. This aligns with the ASUS commitment to a people-first culture built on a design-thinking approach and transparent supply chain practices.

"We are honored that ASUS has once again been recognized by Newsweek as one of the World’s Most Trustworthy Companies,” said S.Y. Hsu, ASUS Co-CEO. “This recognition reinforces the importance of earning confidence and creating long-term value for customers, employees, and investors through our Ubiquitous AI. Incredible Possibilities strategy as we deliver comprehensive AI solutions across Infrastructure, Physical AI, AI PCs and devices."

"We are proud to receive this recognition from Newsweek, which underscores our commitment to responsible innovation and sustainable growth,” said Samson Hu, ASUS Co-CEO. “The company’s approach combines user-focused innovation with responsible business practices, including efforts to support a more sustainable future by designing products that balance high performance with environmental responsibility, using governance and initiatives such as our AI x ESG Platform ."

Building trust through Ubiquitous AI. Incredible Possibilities

ASUS is committed to fostering trust through its Ubiquitous AI. Incredible Possibilities. strategy, aiming to make AI accessible to everyone, everywhere. This involves delivering comprehensive AI solutions that span public and private clouds, general and personal intelligence, and seamless cloud-edge collaboration.

ASUS achieves this by designing high-performance products with environmental responsibility in mind. The company builds lasting stakeholder trust by combining innovation, AI leadership, and strong employee engagement. Bolstered by robust R&D and strategic global partnerships, ASUS maintains its leadership in AI PCs, gaming, and servers, while also providing transformative solutions across smart healthcare, cities, retail, and manufacturing, highlighting its broad global impact and earned confidence.

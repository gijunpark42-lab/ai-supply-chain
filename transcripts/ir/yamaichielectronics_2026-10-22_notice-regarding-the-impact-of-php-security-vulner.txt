Notice Regarding the Impact of PHP Security Vulnerabilities on Our Website

On October 21, 2026, two critical security vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619) were disclosed in PHP, a programming language widely used in website development.

Following this announcement, we reviewed the configuration and operating environment of our corporate website and confirmed that it is not affected by these vulnerabilities.

Our website continues to operate normally and can be used safely as usual.

We will continue to strengthen our security measures and remain committed to maintaining a secure and reliable website.

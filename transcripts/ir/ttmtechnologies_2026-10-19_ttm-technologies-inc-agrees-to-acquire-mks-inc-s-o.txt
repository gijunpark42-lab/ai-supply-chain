TTM Technologies, Inc. Agrees to Acquire MKS Inc.’s Ophir® Optics Business, Adding Advanced Infrared Optics Capabilities

October 19, 2026 8:15am EDT

All-Cash Purchase Price of $650 Million. Expected to be Immediately Accretive to Adjusted EBITDA Margin and Non-GAAP Diluted EPS.

SANTA ANA, Calif., Oct. 19, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TTM Technologies, Inc. (NASDAQ: TTMI) (“TTM”), a leading global manufacturer of technology products, today announced that it has entered into a definitive agreement to acquire the Ophir Optics business (“Ophir Optics”) from MKS Inc. (NASDAQ: MKSI) for approximately $650 million in an all-cash transaction, subject to customary closing adjustments. The Ophir Optics business is an Israel and Romania-based manufacturer of high-precision infrared thermal imaging and laser optics solutions, serving defense, security and industrial applications.

The transaction along with the Company’s previously announced $400M capital and capacity expansion in Asia, are expected to be funded through a combination of cash on hand and expected future cash flow and borrowings under TTM’s existing revolving credit facility. The transaction multiple is estimated to be roughly 10.3x expected FY 2027 Adjusted EBITDA of Ophir Optics, without synergies, and is expected to be immediately accretive to both TTM’s Adjusted EBITDA Margin and Non-GAAP Diluted EPS. This transaction is expected to close in the second quarter of 2027.

“We are excited to announce our planned acquisition of the Ophir Optics business as another important step toward enhancing our long-term growth strategy by expanding our portfolio with mission-critical optical solutions for land, air, maritime, and unmanned platforms and our industrial portfolio,” said Edwin Roks, President and Chief Executive Officer of TTM. “This acquisition adds another important modality to our product offering with advanced optics to provide systems solutions to our defense and industrial customers, which will help us to benefit from key megatrend tailwinds generated by the growing need for surveillance and the increasing use of laser applications by the industrial base.”

This transaction does not include the Ophir Instruments laser characterization business of MKS, which operates separately from Ophir Optics, or the sale of the Ophir brand.

The transaction is expected to close in the second quarter of 2027, and is subject to customary closing conditions, including certain regulatory approvals and certain governmental consents and permits.

Citi is serving as the exclusive financial advisor and Polsinelli PC and Arnon, Tadmor-Levy are serving as co-legal advisers to TTM for the potential acquisition of Ophir Optics.

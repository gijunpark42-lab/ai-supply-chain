JCET Hosts Global Supplier Day 2026 in Jiangyin

Company News

2026-09-17

Share:

JIANGYIN, China, September 17, 2026 – JCET Group held its Global Supplier Day 2026 in Jiangyin, Jiangsu Province. Under the theme “Growing Together, Unlocking Possibilities”, the event brought together 700 participants, including representatives from JCET’s domestic and international supplier partners, industry associations and semiconductor experts. Attendees exchanged insights on emerging trends shaping the AI era, the evolution of advanced packaging technologies, and collaboration across the semiconductor value chain, while exploring opportunities to build a more open and resilient industry ecosystem.

JCET CEO Li Zheng delivered the welcome address and thanked the company’s suppliers for their longstanding support. The event was also attended by Lei Chen, General Manager of the Science and Technology Innovation Department at China Resources Group, Junjie Wang, Executive Secretary-General of the China Semiconductor Industry Association.

Demand generated by AI applications is accelerating innovation across the semiconductor industry. Companies throughout the value chain, including materials, equipment, substrates, packaging and testing providers, are moving beyond conventional supplier relationships toward closer collaboration in joint development, qualification and volume delivery.

JCET Executive Vice President and General Manager of Supply Chain Management Qing Peng, shared the company’s approach to strengthening supply chain efficiency, delivery capabilities and resilience in response to industry changes. Dr. Rebecca Chen, JCET Vice President and General Manager of the Applications Business Unit, outlined the company’s strategy for addressing growing demand from AI, high-performance computing and other rapidly developing applications.

During the conference, JCET recognized suppliers that had demonstrated outstanding performance over the past year in quality, delivery, technical support, sustainability and other areas. Awards across seven categories included the Supplier Excellence Award, ESG Benchmark Supplier Award, Best Delivery Supplier Award, Best Quality Supplier Award, Best Technical Support Supplier Award and Emerging Supplier Award. A total of 84 supplier organizations and 45 individuals from China and overseas received awards.

“ The Global Supplier Conference is an important platform for recognizing our shared achievements and strengthening partnerships,” said Qing Peng, JCET Executive Vice President. “We value the trust of our suppliers and look forward to combining our strengths to address emerging AI opportunities, create greater value and grow together.”

News Release

October 6, 2026

Nittobo

Announcement of Exhibit at TPCA Show TAIPEI 2026

Nittobo Group company Nittobo Taiwan Co., Ltd. will exhibit at the TPCA Show TAIPEI 2026,

which will be held at the Taipei Nangang Exhibition Center (TaiNEX) in Taipei from October 20,

2026 (Tuesday) to October 22, 2026 (Thursday).

The TPCA Show is an international general exhibition organized by the Taiwan Printed Circuit

Association (TPCA) and one of the largest shows in Asia for printed circuit boards (PCBs) and

mounting technology.

Our booth will showcase our sales strategy and future trends in glass cloth development, such

as next-generation special glass (including Low Dk glass and Low CTE glass) and ultra-thin

glass cloth developed with our proprietary technology. We urge visitors to drop by at our

booth (4F , L-704).

■ Outline of TPCA Show TAIPEI 2026

Period: October 20, 2026 (Tuesday) to October 22, 2026 (Thursday), from 9:30 to 17:00 (to

(TaiNEX, Booth number L-704)

Release Date: 2026/09/09

Our research paper has been published in "Chemical Communications" through collaboration between Tokushima University, Kanazawa University and JEOL Ltd. We have reported the new gas-loading tool for NMR observation, designed for JEOL 1 mm MAS NMR systems. The research project was led by Tokushima University.

Gas-loading system compatible with ultrafast magic-angle spinning for solid-state nuclear magnetic resonance in gas atmospheres

DOI:10.1039/D6CC02737G

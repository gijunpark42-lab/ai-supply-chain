News Release

October 5, 2026

Nittobo

Nittobo to Debut at FAKUMA 2026

— Showcasing Specialty Glass Fiber and Sustainable Solutions —

The Composite Materials Business Division of Nittobo will make its first appearance as an

exhibitor at FAKUMA 2026, held from October 12 to 16, 2026, in Friedrichshafen, Germany.

FAKUMA is one of the world's leading trade fairs for plastics processing and molding technology,

highlighting the latest trends in sustainable materials.

Nittobo will showcase high-performance glass fiber products for composite applications,

headlined by Flat Glass Fiber, a world-first innovation developed by Nittobo.

In response to growing environmental demands, Nittobo will also introduce its sustainability

initiatives such as recycled glass fiber via mass-balance approach (ISCC PLUS-certified). Nittobo

aims to deliver unique solutions combining high functionality with reduced environmental impact to

the global market.

During the exhibition, a Nittobo R&D engineer will deliver a technical presentation introducing the

companyʼs latest advancements in sustainable glass fiber products.

Presentation Title: Nittobo's Circular and Sustainable Glass Fiber

■Reference: Sustainable Glass Fiber Product Portfolio

Nittoboʼs Composite Materials Business Division is advancing seamless “drop-in” sustainable

solutions while driving next-generation R&D to support customersʼ circular economy and carbon

neutral goals.

・Mass-Balance Recycled Glass Fiber (ISCC PLUS Certified)

This recycled product offers mechanical properties and quality completely equivalent to standard

products. It enables seamless "drop-in" replacement in existing processes, immediately boosting

recycled content while lowering the carbon footprint (CFP) of composite products.

・Recycled Glass Fiber Sourced from End-of-Life Solar Panel Glass (In Development)

By recycling cover glass from end-of-life solar panels—an emerging environmental challenge—

Nittobo converts this resource into reinforcing glass fibers as well as Flat Glass Fiber. Nittobo is

currently accelerating R&D toward practical application while providing samples for market

development.

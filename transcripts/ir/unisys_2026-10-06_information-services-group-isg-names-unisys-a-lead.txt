Information Services Group (ISG) Names Unisys a Leader in 2026 Private/Hybrid Cloud - Data Center Services Provider Lens®

October 6, 2026 at 10:00 AM EDT

Unisys earns five Leader designations across the U.S ., U.S . Public Sector and U.K . for AI-ready infrastructure, managed cloud hosting and resilient infrastructure services

BLUE BELL, Pa. , Oct. 6, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Unisys (NYSE: UIS) has been named a Leader in the 2026 ISG Provider Lens® Private/Hybrid Cloud – Data Center Services quadrant reports . These reports highlight service providers excelling in private cloud, hybrid cloud and data center infrastructure management. This marks the sixth consecutive year Unisys has been recognized as a Leader with an increase in 2026 to five Leader designations from four in 2025 .

ISG defines Leaders as companies with comprehensive portfolios, strong market presence, established competitive strategies and a demonstrated commitment to innovation.

"Organizations are looking for infrastructure environments that can support growing AI demands while remaining secure, resilient and cost-efficient," said Chris Arrasmith , chief operating officer, Unisys. "Being recognized by ISG as a Leader for the sixth consecutive year reflects the strength of our AI-driven operations, depth of our hybrid cloud expertise and commitment to enabling clients by modernizing their infrastructure and improving their resilience, so they can operate with greater confidence and efficiency."

Unisys was recognized as a Leader in five market quadrants:

U.S . AI-ready Infrastructure Managed Services for Midmarket : for connecting infrastructure transformation with ongoing operations, enabling organizations to modernize data centers and build AI-ready hybrid cloud environments.

U.S . Managed Cloud Hosting and Resilient Infrastructure Services : for delivering integrated cloud hosting, disaster recovery and infrastructure optimization services for complex enterprise environments.

U.S . Public Sector AI-ready Infrastructure Managed Services : for taking a pragmatic approach to modernizing hybrid cloud environments across state, local and education organizations while balancing governance requirements and operational accountability.

U.S . Public Sector Managed Cloud Hosting and Resilient Infrastructure Services : for delivering secure, resilient cloud and data center services that strengthen continuity and control across public sector environments.

U. K. AI -ready Infrastructure Managed Services for Midmarket : for using AI-driven operations and rapid value assessments to identify modernization opportunities and optimize hybrid cloud environments for midmarket organizations.

In the report, Unisys was also recognized as a Product Challenger in several quadrants, including U.S . AI-ready Infrastructure Managed Services — Large Accounts , U. K. AI -ready Infrastructure Managed Services — Large Accounts , and U.K . Managed Cloud Hosting and Resilient Infrastructure Services . Product Challengers are providers that demonstrate strong technology capabilities, broad service offerings and continued investment in expanding their market presence.

"Unisys differentiates itself through its ability to translate hybrid infrastructure transformation into a governed operating model by addressing hosting, recovery and optimization as a single service, making it highly relevant for complex U.S . enterprise environments," said Shashank Rajmane , senior manager and principal analyst, ISG.

Onto Innovation Receives Best Partner Award from SK hynix for Hidden Defect Prevention in High Bandwidth Memory Production

10/12/2026

The award recognizes Onto Innovation’s contribution to HBM process control and advanced memory production for the AI era

WILMINGTON, Mass.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--

Onto Innovation Inc. (NYSE: ONTO) announced today it received the Best Partner Award for technology achievement from SK hynix during the leading memory manufacturer’s 25 th Anniversary Partners Day on September 28, 2026. SK hynix bestowed the honor on Onto, the only company recognized for technology achievement, for developing a defect prevention system with breakthrough measurement technology for high-bandwidth memory (HBM) applications. The award highlights Onto’s contribution to SK hynix's HBM roadmap at a time when advanced memory is central to expanding AI infrastructure.

Onto Innovation receives the Best Partner Award for technology achievement from SK hynix. Ryan Hwang, regional general manager of Onto Innovation Korea, accepts the award from SK hynix CEO Noh-Jung Kwak during the 25th Anniversary Partners Day on September 28, 2026.

“We are proud to be recognized by SK hynix and grateful for the trust they have placed in Onto Innovation. This award is the result of close collaboration between SK hynix and our product and customer success teams to deliver innovations that help identify hidden yield impacting defects critical to reliable HBM manufacturing,” said Mike Plisinski, chief executive officer of Onto Innovation. “HBM presents rapidly evolving application challenges. The inherent flexibility of the Dragonfly ® system enables customers to consistently overcome those challenges—generation after generation.”

Currently, memory suppliers are focused on expanding HBM capacity in order to meet AI-driven demand which, according to TrendForce is expected to swell by greater than 50% by 2027, while rapidly introducing new generations of HBM technology. The move toward 12- and 16-high HBM stacks results in thinner dies and increasingly complex assembly flows, raising the risk of hidden cracks and sub-surface defects during thinning, bonding and assembly. As each HBM generation thins the die, adds more die per stack, and packs in more connections at smaller bump sizes, inspection intensity grows—and with more value concentrated in every device, the cost of finding defects late in production rises accordingly. Onto's Dragonfly G5 inspection system provides the process control insight needed to identify critical defects earlier, helping SK hynix reduce the risk of yield loss in high-value HBM devices.

Partners Day is an annual recognition ceremony of the SK hynix Co-Growth Council, honoring supplier contributions to the company’s technology and business goals. Ryan Hwang, regional general manager of Onto Innovation Korea, accepted the award from SK hynix CEO Noh-Jung Kwak.

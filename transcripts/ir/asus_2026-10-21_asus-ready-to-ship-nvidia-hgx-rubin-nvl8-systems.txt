ASUS Ready to Ship NVIDIA HGX Rubin NVL8 Systems

October 21, 2026

・

Press Release

Showcases AI factory design and simulation using NVIDIA DSX at NVIDIA GTC Berlin 2026

Ready for Q4 2026: ASUS data center based on NVIDIA HGX Rubin NVL8 systems in flexible 2U and 3U configurations for demanding AI workloads

AI factory design & simulation: ASUS systems engineering expertise with NVIDIA DSX Sim Blueprint allows pre-deployment evaluation of tradeoffs

End-to-end AI: Powerful ASUS solutions span infrastructure, model development, edge and agent computing

BERLIN, Germany, October 21, 2026 — ASUS today announced that all-new ASUS systems built on NVIDIA HGX Rubin NVL8 are ready to launch in Q4 2026, with both partial-rack and full-rack configurations available. The portfolio includes the 3U hybrid-cooled XR R1I12B-M1BHFS and 2U 100% liquid-cooled XR R1I12B-M1BLFS , delivering flexible, high-performance platforms built for massive AI reasoning and workloads.

At NVIDIA GTC Berlin 2026, ASUS (Booth #3039) is showcasing these systems alongside a comprehensive portfolio of NVIDIA Grace Blackwell-powered solutions — from the ASUS Ascent GX10 for AI model fine-tuning, prototyping, and inference, to the ExpertCenter Pro ET900N G3 for demanding AI development and large-scale model training. Extending AI beyond the data center, ASUS also presents the ProArt GR1X Mini PC and ProArt P16 , bringing powerful AI capabilities to developers and creators.

Together, these solutions span the full agentic AI journey — from advanced AI infrastructure and model development to edge and agent computing — underscoring the role of ASUS as a total solutions provider that empowers enterprises, developers and creators to unlock the incredible possibilities of AI.

Flexible NVIDIA HGX Rubin platforms for diverse AI requirements

To meet the growing demand for high-performance AI infrastructure, ASUS showcases rigorously validated NVIDIA HGX Rubin server solutions designed to accelerate AI deployment.

Addressing the most rigorous demands of the modern data center, ASUS provides flexible HGX Rubin server options tailored to distinct customer requirements:

XR R1I12B-M1BHFS: Featuring a 3U form factor, it combines D2C liquid cooling for the NVIDIA HGX Rubin 8-GPU baseboard with air cooling for dual Intel® Xeon® 6 processors. The air-cooled CPU sled reduces maintenance time by up to 50%, while flexible U.2/E1.S storage options support diverse deployment requirements.

XR R1I12B-M1BLFS : Featuring a 2U, fully liquid-cooled architecture, it is designed for high thermal efficiency, improved PUE, and high computing density in demanding AI environments. Its NVIDIA-validated design and cable-less board-to-board architecture help simplify system integration and reduce deployment complexity.

Accelerating AI factory design and simulation with NVIDIA DSX Sim

Building an AI factory requires optimization across compute, power, cooling, and facility infrastructure. ASUS combines its system engineering expertise with NVIDIA DSX Sim Blueprint to help enterprises design, simulate, and validate AI factory infrastructure in a digital twin environment, while optimizing for large-scale token generation.

Working with ecosystem partners including Schneider Electric, AVEVA, and IBM, ASUS integrates compute, networking, storage, power, cooling, and facility infrastructure into a unified digital twin environment. This helps customers evaluate infrastructure configurations, identify constraints, and optimize system performance and efficiency before deployment.

October 14, 2026 12:03 pm EDT

Gov. Josh Shapiro joins Merck leadership for official ribbon-cutting ceremony

Merck’s $550 million regional investment reinforces ongoing commitment to U.S. R&D while strengthening its long-standing footprint in Pennsylvania

RAHWAY N.J., Oct. 14, 2026 – Merck (NYSE: MRK), known as MSD outside of the United States and Canada, today inaugurated its state-of-the-art ADVANCE research facility on its West Point, Pennsylvania, campus, signaling a new chapter in global scientific discovery. Pennsylvania Gov. Josh Shapiro joined Merck executive leadership, federal and state government officials and local community partners for a ribbon-cutting ceremony to celebrate the pivotal expansion of the company’s premier research and development capabilities.

By integrating highly automated robotic systems and advanced digital AI capabilities, ADVANCE is designed to streamline the analysis of biological samples from Merck’s ongoing clinical development programs while supporting one of the broadest and most diverse pipelines in the company’s recent history.

“My Administration has made life sciences a key part of our economic development strategy here in Pennsylvania — and Merck’s major investment in Montgomery County is another major win for the Commonwealth,” said Gov. Josh Shapiro. “We are proud to partner with Merck as they expand their operations in Montgomery County, advance cutting-edge research and manufacturing, and create new opportunities for thousands of Pennsylvanians. The life sciences industry is booming in Pennsylvania and I am committed to winning more investments like this and making sure our Commonwealth remains a national leader in innovation, medicine and manufacturing.”

The ribbon-cutting marks another significant milestone for the 400-acre West Point campus, Merck’s largest integrated research and human health manufacturing site. Since 1953, West Point site has been home to a series of important biopharmaceutical breakthroughs. The addition of the ADVANCE research facility ensures Pennsylvania remains a key driver of global scientific innovation.

“The ADVANCE facility represents the future of clinical development at Merck and underscores our continued commitment to addressing the needs of the patients we serve,” said Rob Davis, chairman and chief executive officer, Merck. “This builds on our longstanding presence in Pennsylvania where we’re proud to invest in the people, communities and scientific capabilities that will help drive the next generation of medicines.”

A legacy of scientific progress in Pennsylvania

Merck originally acquired the West Point site as part of the landmark 1953 merger of Merck and Sharp & Dohme. Since then, Merck’s operations in Pennsylvania have grown substantially, transforming the West Point campus into a cornerstone of the company’s research efforts and global manufacturing supply chain. Today, the site is the largest employer in Montgomery County, Pennsylvania, and the seventh largest private employer in the state.

Merck has made significant capital investments to the West Point site totaling more than $15 billion over the past 35 years. As part of the company’s previously announced $70 billion commitment to domestic manufacturing and R&D, Merck is investing approximately $550 million in R&D facilities at ADVANCE and the nearby Spring House Innovation Park (SHIP).

The capital investments at West Point have continuously sustained an on-site workforce of approximately 800 contracting, engineering, and construction jobs during this time. Many of these capital projects have been designed in Pennsylvania and represent more than $1 billion in services provided by locally based architects.

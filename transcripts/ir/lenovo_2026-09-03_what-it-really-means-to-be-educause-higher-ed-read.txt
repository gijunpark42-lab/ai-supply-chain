What it really means to be EDUCAUSE “Higher Ed Ready”

Lenovo achieves EDUCAUSE Higher Ed Ready™ designation, reinforcing our commitment to understanding the unique needs of colleges and universities and building trusted technology partnerships.

By Anthony Pichardo, Executive Director, NA Geo Sales 3 September 2026

For colleges and universities, choosing the right technology has never been simple. Today, that decision is becoming even more complex.

Institutions are navigating rapid AI innovation, growing cybersecurity and privacy risks, accessibility requirements, budget pressures, and evolving expectations from students, faculty, and staff. With no shortage of technology providers promising solutions, the bigger question is not what an organization can offer, but whether it truly understands the distinct realities of higher education—and is committed to providing solutions that help institutions meet their goals.

That is what makes being “Higher Ed Ready” meaningful.

Lenovo has earned the EDUCAUSE Higher Ed Ready™ designation, recognizing its readiness to meet the unique needs and expectations of the higher education community. Representing more than 100,000 professionals and over 2,000 member organizations, EDUCAUSE looks beyond individual products and solutions to evaluate how technology providers engage with and support higher education.

For Lenovo, this distinction reflects a fundamental philosophy that being ready for higher education means understanding that technology is only one part of the equation.

Looking beyond the technology

Technology hardware and services are only part of the equation. Higher education institutions need more from the organizations behind those technologies.

They need partners that understand complex campus environments, provide transparency and responsive support, and consider security, privacy, accessibility, and responsible innovation from the outset. They also need partners prepared to collaborate throughout the relationship.

That philosophy is at the core of the EDUCAUSE Higher Ed Ready™ Program. Applications are evaluated by a panel of higher education CIOs, senior leaders, and technology experts across six areas:

Transparency and communication

Innovation

Customer success

Shared goals and values

Standards and compliance

Collaboration and community engagement

Being EDUCAUSE Higher Ed Ready™ is not simply about having technology that works in higher education. It is about demonstrating that an organization is prepared to partner with higher education.

A commitment that extends across Lenovo

For Lenovo, earning the designation required an extensive review of the company’s approach to serving colleges and universities. The application brought together a cross-functional group spanning sales, global education, legal, operations, marketing, and other teams across the business.

That breadth matters, as higher education institutions do not experience a technology partner through a single team or interaction. Their experience can span everything from early conversations about their needs and procurement to deployment, security, support, and long-term planning.

Lenovo’s EDUCAUSE Higher Ed Ready™ designation reflects more than the work of its education team. It reflects a commitment that reaches across the organization.

“Higher education institutions need technology partners that understand their unique needs, provide solutions built around those needs and are committed to being there for the long term,” says Hernan Londono, Chief Technology and Innovation Strategist, Lenovo. “The EDUCAUSE Higher Ed Ready™ designation provides independent validation of the work Lenovo has done across our organization to be that kind of partner.”

“EDUCAUSE is pleased to recognize Lenovo as part of the first cohort to earn the Higher Ed Ready designation. This recognition reflects Lenovo’s demonstrated commitment to understanding and serving the distinct needs of higher education, and we appreciate their early participation in helping shape the program as it launched,” said John O’Brien, Ph.D., President and CEO, EDUCAUSE.

That organization-wide approach is particularly important as the role of technology on campus expands. Meeting evolving technology needs, workforce expectations, and cybersecurity and privacy challenges requires more than keeping pace with the latest technology. It requires understanding the environment in which that technology will operate.

Built with higher education, not just for it

Understanding higher education also requires listening to the people shaping its future.

Lenovo’s relationship with EDUCAUSE extends beyond the Higher Ed Ready designation. As an EDUCAUSE Mission Sponsor, Lenovo was invited to participate in the program’s launch and apply for the designation. Lenovo has also participated in EDUCAUSE’s Corporate Engagement Advisory Committee (CEAC), which brings together industry professionals and higher education leaders to advise EDUCAUSE on corporate engagement, programming and initiatives that support the evolving higher education technology market.

That engagement reinforces an important distinction in Lenovo’s approach. The goal is to work with the community to understand where institutions are headed and what they need from technology partners to get there.

That can mean understanding the pressures facing IT teams and administrators, considering accessibility and affordability alongside performance, and helping institutions navigate security and privacy requirements while embracing emerging technologies.

What “Higher Ed Ready” means

For an institution evaluating its next technology partner, a designation alone cannot make the decision. But independent validation can help institutions answer an increasingly important question of whether the organization truly understands their needs.

The EDUCAUSE Higher Ed Ready™ designation offers institutions an additional level of confidence that Lenovo has been evaluated against expectations established by the higher education community, not only for what it delivers today, but also for how it communicates, collaborates, supports customers and approaches long-term partnership.

For Lenovo, earning the recognition is an important milestone. More importantly, it reinforces the standard Lenovo aims to meet every day.

Because being EDUCAUSE Higher Ed Ready™ isn’t simply about being ready with the right technology. It’s about being ready to listen, ready to adapt and ready to be a trusted partner for what comes next.

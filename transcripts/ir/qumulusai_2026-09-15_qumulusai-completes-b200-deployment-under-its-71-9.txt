QumulusAI Completes B200 Deployment Under Its $71.9 Million Contracted Blackwell Agreement

September 15, 2026

Customer handoff of B200 capacity under the three-year agreement announced July 28, 2026, was reached Sept. 8, 2026; NVIDIA B300 capacity under the same agreement is in its final stages of deployment.

ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- QumulusAI (Nasdaq: QMLS), a neocloud infrastructure provider purpose-built for the AI computing era, today announced it has completed deployment of NVIDIA B200 GPU capacity and handed off that capacity to the customer under the $71.9 million, three-year agreement QumulusAI announced on July 28, 2026, with an AI inference platform provider. Full handoff of the B200 portion was reached Sept. 8, 2026. The previously announced NVIDIA B300 capacity is in its final stages of deployment.

This deployment ranks among the company's largest customer commitments to reach live production capacity to date.

“Moving from signed agreement to delivered capacity in months, not years, is what this business is built to do. The B200 capacity is live and serving our customer’s inference workloads today, and the B300 portion is close behind,” said Michael Maniscalco, CEO of QumulusAI.

Supermicro Provides First Quarter of Fiscal Year 2027 Preliminary Business Update

October 20, 2026

SAN JOSE, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--

Super Micro Computer, Inc. (NASDAQ: SMCI) (“Supermicro” or the “Company”), an AI, Enterprise, Storage, and 5G/Edge IT Total Solution Provider, featuring Data Center Building Block Solutions® (DCBBS), today is providing a preliminary business update for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 (Q1’27), ended September 30, 2026, and schedules earnings call for November 3rd at 5pm EST.

Revenues for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 are estimated to be in the range of $18.5 billion to $19.5 billion versus guidance of $14.5 billion to $15.5 billion, primarily due to increased demand for AI platforms.

Gross margins are estimated to be in the range of 12.0% to 12.5%, versus guidance of 10.4% to 10.8%, primarily due to customer and product mix.

This unaudited financial information is based on preliminary results and management's estimates and is inherently uncertain and subject to revision in connection with the Company's financial closing procedures and finalization of the Company's financial statements for its first quarter of fiscal year 2027. Actual results for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 may differ materially from these preliminary unaudited financial results.

Conference Call and Webcast Information

Supermicro will present a live audio webcast of our conference call to review its first quarter fiscal 2027 financial results on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. ET / 2:00 p.m. PT. The webcast will be available at https://ir.supermicro.com .

A replay of the webcast will be available shortly after the call at the same website and will remain accessible for one year.

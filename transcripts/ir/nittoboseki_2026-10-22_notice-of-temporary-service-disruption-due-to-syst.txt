News Release

October 22, 2026

Nittobo

Notice of Temporary Service Disruption due to System Maintenance

Thank you very much for using the websites of Nittobo and Nittobo Group.

Please be advised that we will be gradually suspending certain services starting today in order

to conduct system maintenance. We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience this may cause

and appreciate your kind understanding.

Details

1. Affected Services and Pages

Nittobo Official Website

・Inquiry Forms / Contact Pages

Nittobo Group Company Websites

・Inquiry Forms / Contact Pages on each company website

・Nittobo Medical Co., Ltd. English Website (Product Information Pages)

2. Impact

During the maintenance period, the affected pages will be unavailable for viewing, and contact

forms cannot be used.

End

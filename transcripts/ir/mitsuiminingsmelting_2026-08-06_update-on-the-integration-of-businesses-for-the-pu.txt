Update on the Integration of Businesses for the Purchase of Copper Concentrates and the Sale of Related Products

Mitsui Kinzoku Company, Limited (the “Company”; President and Representative Director: IKENOBU Seiji) hereby announces a change to the effective date of the integration stated in the Execution of Final Agreement Concerning Integration of Businesses for the Purchase of Copper Concentrates and the Sale of Related Products, which was published on May 28, 2026.

1.Change of date

Effective date of integration　Original plan October 1, 2026

Revised plan February 1, 2027

2.Reason for change

To execute the integration, it is necessary to complete certain filings and receive approvals from relevant domestic and foreign authorities. The Company

has changed the effective date in light of the time required for the review process.

3.Future outlook

The impact of this change on consolidated business results forecasts for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2027 will be insignificant.

The Company will contribute to the realization of a sustainable society by implementing its vision for 2030, “Building new businesses －and the future－ with our material intelligence,” based on its purpose, “We promote the well-being of the world through a spirit of exploration and diverse technologies.”

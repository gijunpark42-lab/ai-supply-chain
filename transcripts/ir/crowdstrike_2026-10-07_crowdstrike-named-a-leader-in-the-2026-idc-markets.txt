CrowdStrike Named a Leader in the 2026 IDC MarketScape for Modern Endpoint Security for Enterprises

October 7, 2026

PDF Version

AUSTIN, Texas --(BUSINESS WIRE)--Oct. 7, 2026--

CrowdStrike (NASDAQ: CRWD) today announced it has been named a Leader in the IDC MarketScape: Worldwide Modern Endpoint Security for Enterprises 2026 Vendor Assessment . 1

AI is now part of every enterprise, and the endpoint is where agents act autonomously at machine speed with system-level privilege. CrowdStrike pioneered AI-native EDR and is defining what customers need to secure AI with AI Detection and Response (AIDR). CrowdStrike's AIDR business has grown more than 79-fold 2 in three quarters since launch, with Falcon Guardian securing AI agents at the point of execution.

“CrowdStrike pioneered this market, and the world's leading organizations trust the Falcon platform to stop breaches,” said AJ Shipley, chief product officer at CrowdStrike . “Deployed across hundreds of millions of devices and powered by cybersecurity’s richest data set, CrowdStrike has the structural advantage to lead as AI becomes the new attack surface. We secure AI where it executes, on the endpoint at runtime, and we believe this recognition reflects how we continue to define the future of security.”

Giving Defenders the AI Advantage

As adversaries weaponize AI and compress the defender’s window to respond, the report notes “every vendor in this market now markets AI, so the question is no longer whether AI is present but whether it is applied where it changes outcomes.” CrowdStrike’s profile notes some key strengths including “deep AI and agentic investment translating into reported SOC efficiency gains, backed by a very large real-time telemetry pipeline processing trillions of events daily.”

One Unified Platform to Stop Cross-Domain Attacks

AI-driven threats don’t stay on the endpoint; they target every domain at once. CrowdStrike’s unified architecture extends real-time protection across the enterprise. The IDC MarketScape noted another key strength: “broad, natively delivered platform spanning endpoint, identity, cloud, data, exposure management, and increasingly browser and mobile security, reducing reliance on third-party point products.”

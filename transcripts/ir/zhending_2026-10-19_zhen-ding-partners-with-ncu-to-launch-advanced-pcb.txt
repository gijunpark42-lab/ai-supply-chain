Zhen Ding Partners with NCU to Launch “Advanced PCB Talent Development Program” Cultivating Next-Gen PCB Talent to Strengthen Industry Competitiveness in the AI Era

Zhen Ding Technology Holding Limited (TWSE: 4958), the world's leading PCB manufacturer, today announced that it has partnered with National Central University (NCU) and three industry partners, including Elite Material, Ta Liang Technology, and Topoint Technology, to establish the “Advanced PCB Talent Development Program.” A signing ceremony was held on October 19 to mark the launch of this initiative. Zhen Ding and the three industry partners will jointly invest NT$60 million to bring together NCU's interdisciplinary research capabilities and industry resources, establishing a long-term platform for industry-academia collaboration. The initiative will focus on cultivating talent in high-end PCB technologies and laying a strong foundation for the industry's sustainable growth in the AI era.

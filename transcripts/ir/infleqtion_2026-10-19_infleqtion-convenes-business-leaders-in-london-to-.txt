Infleqtion Convenes Business Leaders in London to Chart Quantum Commercial Value

October 19, 2026 12:00am EDT

Quantum Application Forum, 3 November 2026, gives executives a practical view of where quantum can create commercial value

Summary :

Real-world quantum use cases: One-day forum on quantum applications for business and public sector priorities in financial services, healthcare, pharmaceuticals, materials science, energy and telecommunications

A peer forum for business leaders: Executives from leading organizations will compare approaches to shared challenges, including enterprise readiness, technology roadmaps and investment decisions, as quantum converges with next-generation IT and AI infrastructure

OXFORD, England--(BUSINESS WIRE)--

Infleqtion (NYSE: INFQ), a global leader in quantum computing and quantum sensing powered by neutral atom technology, will host the first Quantum Application Forum , an exclusive one-day event convening senior business and technology leaders on 3 November 2026 at IET Savoy Place in London. Executives responsible for technology, innovation and investment decisions will explore how quantum technology can address critical challenges, unlock commercial value and drive national competitiveness and economic growth.

“The UK has an opportunity, alongside its European neighbours, to turn its strength in quantum research into a competitive advantage for its industries,” said Colin Sullivan MBE, Managing Director, at Infleqtion UK. “Business leaders are central to that. They understand which problems are worth solving, and their insight will shape how the technology is put to use. Our aim is that everyone who joins the forum leaves with a clearer view of where quantum could benefit their organisation, and the confidence to take a practical next step.”

The forum’s theme is “Integrating Quantum into National & Economic Growth.” Leaders from financial services and fintech, healthcare and life sciences, pharmaceuticals, materials science, energy, telecommunications, government and the public sector will join.

Morning plenary sessions will lead into afternoon roundtables, with attendees joining discussions on topics including quantum–AI hybrid workloads, software and algorithm optimization, resilient infrastructure and supply chains, security readiness, and the standards and policies shaping adoption. Speakers will be announced ahead of the event.

EVENT DETAILS

Date and time: 3 November 2026, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. GMT, with breakfast and networking beginning at 8 a.m. GMT

Venue: IET Savoy Place, London

Cost: Complimentary for confirmed guests

Format: Plenary sessions and roundtable discussions

Register: To preserve an environment for open dialogue, registration is by approval only and attendance is limited to invited and approved participants. To be considered, visit infleqtion.com/qaf and follow “Register Your Interest.”

Infleqtion has deep roots in Europe and delivered the UK’s first operational 100-physical-qubit quantum computer to the National Quantum Computing Centre at Harwell. Most recently, the company announced a £9 million Innovate UK award to continue development of a deployable quantum radio frequency (QRF) direction finding (DF) system as part of the Quantum Missions Primer programme.

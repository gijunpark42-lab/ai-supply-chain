Press Release 08 October 2026 Digital Industries Erlangen

Siemens to digitalize one of Europe's largest renewable

hydrogen projects

Siemens deploys its software portfolio for hydrogen at industrial scale, shaping the future of digitally optimized renewable hydrogen production

Physics-based digital twin and real-time optimization

100 MW facility to produce up to 15,000 tons of renewable hydrogen per year, targeting 110,000 tons of CO₂ reduction per year

Siemens deploys its software portfolio for hydrogen at industrial scale, shaping the future of digitally optimized renewable hydrogen production

Physics-based digital twin and real-time optimization

100 MW facility to produce up to 15,000 tons of renewable hydrogen per year, targeting 110,000 tons of CO₂ reduction per year

Siemens

and Portugal-based Galp Energia are collaborating to digitalize GalpH2Park in Sines,

Portugal. The plant will be one of Europe's

largest renewable hydrogen plants once it starts operating at year-end. For the 100 MW hydrogen facility of the first

project phase, Siemens is deploying its software portfolio for the hydrogen

industry, enabling smarter operations and optimizing the value of the plant. With

this deployment, Galp and Siemens are shaping the future of digitally optimized

renewable hydrogen production on an industrial scale.

Digital

twin at the heart

Central

to the collaboration is Siemens' Hydrogen Performance Suite (HPS) – an advanced

digital cockpit designed to continuously enhance renewable hydrogen plant

operations. At its core lies a predictive Digital Process Twin: a high-fidelity

model built on physics-based modeling that delivers operational insights via

real-time optimization for faster and data-driven decision-making. To power the

digital twin, Siemens deploys an integrated software stack for process

modelling, data analytics and real-time visualization – translating complex

plant data into clear, actionable insights for operators.

By

seamlessly integrating the digital twin with live plant data and external

sources, such as grid operators and energy markets, the HPS gives Galp’s

operators end-to-end visibility of their facility. The result is smarter

production scheduling, and optimized energy procurement. Siemens estimates that

the HPS can deliver energy savings of up to 15 percent – a significant lever

for both cost competitiveness and sustainability.

"GalpH2Park

is a lighthouse for Europe's energy transition”, said Axel Lorenz, Executive Vice

President Automation Process Industries at Siemens. “We are convinced that

digital innovation is the key to making renewable hydrogen economically viable

and accelerating the decarbonization of industry. Siemens is turning this

ambition into industrial reality – at scale."

Reducing

greenhouse gas emissions

Powered

by renewable electricity sourced through long-term supply agreements and Galp's

own renewable generation capacity, the GalpH2Park plant is set to produce up to 15,000 tons of renewable hydrogen per year.

This is expected to reduce greenhouse gas emissions by approximately 110,000 tons

of CO₂ equivalent per year (Scope

1) – a meaningful contribution to Portugal's and Europe's decarbonization

targets.

Siemens

offers a comprehensive portfolio of digital and automation solutions for the

hydrogen industry – spanning monitoring and control systems, industrial

automation, digital twin, power protection and distribution, and drives. With a

holistic approach that integrates electrification, advanced instrumentation,

and state-of-the-art digital services, Siemens is actively accelerating

industrial decarbonization and the global energy transition.

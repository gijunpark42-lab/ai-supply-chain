September 25, 2026

The KOKUSAI ELECTRIC Group is pleased to announce of our participation in “SEMICON ® West 2026” that will take place at Moscone Center in San Francisco, USA, from Tuesday, October 13 to Thursday, October 15, 2026.

SEMICON ® West 2026 brings together the world’s leading semiconductor companies, engineers, and researchers to cover topics from AI and advanced packaging to smart manufacturing and sustainability and to foster strategic partnerships with the theme being “Transform Tomorrow.”

At our booth, we will propose our semiconductor manufacturing equipment using panels.

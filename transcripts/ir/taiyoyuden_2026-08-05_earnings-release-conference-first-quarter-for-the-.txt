Let me explain the factors behind the operating profit variance compared to the previous quarter.

Please refer to the lower graph.

The main factor for the increase in profit was a ¥1.8 billion effect from capacity utilization. Higher sales

volumes contributed to an increase in operating profit.

On the other hand, inventory, which was initially planned to increase by around ¥1.5 billion, remained

at the same level as in the previous quarter.

This was because capacity utilization for capacitors, planned to be in the high 80% range, fell to

around 85% as there were operational constraints due to logistical disruptions at overseas production

sites.

In addition, there were also constraints due to the impact of labor-management consultations in April.

In the absence of these constraints on operations, it is estimated that net sales would have been

slightly more than ¥ 4.0 billion higher.

It should be noted that these issues have already been resolved, and, as operations are now normal,

no impact on earnings is expected from the second quarter onward.

Other factors contributing to the profit increase were a ¥0.3 billion effect from cost reductions and a

positive impact of ¥0.8 billion from currency fluctuations .

On the other hand, while the ¥0.8 billion decrease in selling prices for the same products negatively

impacted profit, the pace of decline has significantly improved compared to the previous quarter, and

is trending moderately relative to both the same period last year and our initial forecast.

In addition, an increase in fixed costs of ¥0.8 billion also pushed down operating profit.

As a result, operating profit increased by ¥1.3 billion QoQ to ¥4.8 billion.

Background to the Revision of Consolidated Earnings Forecasts for the FYE March 2027

Demand

• AI server demand is expected to surpass initial expectations, driven by the accelerating trends toward higher capacitance

and increased unit counts of multilayer ceramic capacitors.

• Orders received for products for use in information equipment, such as memory modules, were also strong and the

capacitor BB ratio in 1Q reached 1.72, signaling robust demand.

Sales

• Sales of high-value-added, high-end products, particularly those for AI servers, are increasing. The depreciation of the yen

also contributed to the upward revision.

• The pace of price declines when comparing the same products is expected to be more moderate than initially projected.

In addition to the moderation in price decreases in a tight supply and demand environment, we implemented price

revisions for some products, mainly products sold through distributors.

Costs

• In line with the increase in production volume, production-related expenses are expected to be higher than initially

anticipated.

• The expected cost increases resulting from the surge in resource prices due to the situation in the Middle East, along with

the rise in raw material prices and component costs, remain unchanged from the assumptions made at the beginning of

the fiscal year.

10

I will now explain the factors behind the upward revision of the earnings forecasts.

The main factor is the increase in the demand forecasts as of May, which were announced in the initial

projections.

It is forecast that the capacitance and number of MLCCs used in AI servers will increase more than

expected. A high level of orders were also received for products for use in information equipment, such

as memory modules.

In addition, the change in the exchange rate assumption was a factor in pushing up the sales forecast.

In terms of price trends, we anticipate that the pace of price decreases when comparing the same

products will moderate even more than initially expected.

In addition to the moderation in price decreases in a tight supply and demand environment, we

implemented price revisions for some products, mainly products sold through distributors.

In terms of costs, in conjunction with the increase in production volume, the related expenses are

expected to be higher than initially anticipated.

Moreover, the impact of cost increases due to the surge in resource prices, as well as higher prices for

raw materials, including metals, and components, was initially estimated at several billion yen, which

was reflected in the earnings forecast, and there is no change to this figure.

Consolidated Earnings Forecast for Fiscal Year Ending March 2027

11

Consolidated earnings forecast for FYE March 2027 is for net sales of ¥424.0 billion, operating profit of

¥45.0 billion, ordinary profit of ¥42.0 billion, and profit attributable to owners of parent of ¥29.0 billion,

with substantial YoY growth expected in both sales and profit.

Capital investment has increased by ¥2.0 billion from the previous forecast as the timing for capacity

expansion is brought forward considering the strength of AI server demand.

Furthermore, TAIYO YUDEN has decided to commence construction of the new MLCC plant in

Malaysia planned under the medium-term management plan covering the next five years until FY2030

ahead of schedule.

Although it will be the latter half of the medium-term plan when the new plant begins production and

contributes to capacity expansion, this investment decision has been made with an eye on demand

growth over the medium term.

In addition, we have increased our forecast for depreciation by ¥2.0 billion from the previous forecast,

reflecting the impact of the yen's depreciation.

The forecast exchange rate for 2Q onward is US$1:¥160.

Although at present the yen has appreciated since the earnings forecasts were prepared, even if the

yen continues to appreciate from here on, the exchange rate applied by TAIYO YUDEN for July and

August has already been determined at the 160-yen level. Therefore, the impact on earnings in 2Q is

expected to be limited.

If the yen continues to appreciate, the impact on TAIYO YUDEN’s earnings is expected to become

visible from 3Q onward. As a result, in terms of exchange rate sensitivity on a half-year basis, it is

possible that a 1-yen fluctuation could push down TAIYO YUDEN’s second half earnings with an effect

of approximately ¥1.0 billion on net sales and ¥0.5 billion on operating profit.

Furthermore, sensitivity for foreign exchange losses/gains is ¥0.3 to 0.5 billion for each 1-yen

fluctuation, so if the yen continues to appreciate, foreign exchange losses could arise and possibly

push down ordinary profit and profit attributable to owners of parent.

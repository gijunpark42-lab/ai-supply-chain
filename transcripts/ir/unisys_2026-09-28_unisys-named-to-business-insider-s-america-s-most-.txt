Unisys Named to Business Insider's America's Most Innovative Businesses 2027 List

September 28, 2026 at 10:00 AM EDT

Recognition includes a 4.5 out of 5-star rating for performance in reputation, impact and research and development (R&D) investment

BLUE BELL, Pa. , Sept. 28, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Unisys (NYSE: UIS) has been named to Business Insider's 2027 list of 500 America's Most Innovative Businesses , reflecting strong performance in industry reputation, technological impact and R&D investment. The list spotlights organizations across the U.S . shaping the future of innovation.

Companies were also evaluated on peer surveys, patent data and financial filings. America's Most Innovative Businesses 2027 list is presented by Business Insider and Plant-A Insights Group , a research, data and analytics firm.

"We are honored to be included on Business Insider's inaugural list of America's Most Innovative Businesses," said Mike Thomson , chief executive officer and president, Unisys. "Innovation is core to how we operate, from combining AI and deep expertise to solve problems for clients to the investments we make in our company's future. This recognition reflects the talent and commitment of our people across every part of the business and reinforces our focus on delivering meaningful outcomes for clients through innovation."

America's Most Innovative Businesses for 2027 were selected based on a screening of 4,000 publicly traded U.S . companies. After reviewing patent data and financial metrics, 1,600 companies advanced for further evaluation, with the top 500 companies recognized. With a 4.5 out of 5-star rating, Unisys was named one of 15 companies in the IT Services & IT Consulting category.

This recognition follows a series of recent honors for Unisys, including TIME's America's Best Companies 2026 list, The Economist's 2026 Global Top 100 Most Loved Workplaces® list , and the Forbes 2026 list of America's Best Midsize Employers .

Read the full list of America's Most Innovative Businesses at Business Insider .

Zealand Pharma - Completion of share buy-back program and related transactions (week 40 and 41, 2026)

Copenhagen, Denmark, October 5, 2026 – Zealand Pharma A/S ("the Company" or “Zealand Pharma”) (Nasdaq: ZEAL) (CVR-no. 20045078), a biotechnology company transforming the future of metabolic health, today announces that the share buy-back program (the "Program") initiated on May 7, 2026 has been completed. The lead manager, Danske Bank, exercised its option to close the Program ahead of October 31, 2026, after the planned aggregate amount of approximately DKK 1,300 million was reached. The Company also reports transactions under the Program for week 40 (September 28 – October 2, 2026) and week 41 (October 5, 2026).

The Program was carried out in accordance with Article 5 of Regulation (EU) No 596/2014 (the Market Abuse Regulation, "MAR") and Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/1052 (the "Safe Harbour Regulation"). Reference is made to company announcement no. 15 / 2026 dated May 7, 2026, which set out the full terms of the Program. Under the Program, Zealand Pharma was authorized to repurchase shares for a total consideration of up to DKK 1.3 billion. The maximum number of shares that could be acquired under the Program was 7,152,557. The Program commenced on May 7, 2026, and was completed on October 5 2026, ahead of the original deadline of October 31, 2026. The Company appointed Danske Bank as lead manager for the Program. Danske Bank made its own trading decisions independently of, and without influence from, the Company.

During the Program, a total of 4,408,500 shares were repurchased at an average price of DKK 294.88, corresponding to a total gross value of DKK 1,299,996,125.

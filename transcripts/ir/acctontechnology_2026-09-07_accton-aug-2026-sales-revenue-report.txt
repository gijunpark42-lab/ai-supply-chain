Accton’s Monthly Sales Revenue (Consolidated) – Aug

HSINCHU, Taiwan, Sep. 07, 2026 – Accton Technology Corporation (Accton) (TWSE: 2345)

Accton Technology Corporation (Accton) (TWSE: 2345), a premier provider of communication solutions, today announced that the consolidated sales revenue for Aug. 2026 was USD$1,276 million, an increase of 50.14 percent year-over-year.

The cumulative consolidated sales revenue totaled approximately USD$7,741 million, an increase of 57.61 percent year-over-year.

Data Center Cuts Electric Bills Substantially with Tecogen's InVerde

October 14, 2026

BILLERICA, MA / ACCESS Newswire / October 14, 2026 / Tecogen Inc. (NYSE American:TGEN) is pleased share a recent data center customer testimonial .

Robyn Sullivan, CEO and President of Cloudsmart Inc, a Tier 3, SOC 2, Cloud and AI data center in Connecticut said, "For a few months now, we have been creating our own power with Tecogen Inc.'s InVerde and we're glad we did."

"We cut our electric bills substantially, which gives us an edge against other local data centers. Whenever we give potential customers a tour, they are not only intrigued, but love that we can offer lower energy costs and greener power sources. Since starting it up, the unit has run reliably and has been hands-off as their service team has taken care of everything."

Abinand Rangesh, CEO of Tecogen commented "We are thrilled that Cloudsmart chose our InVerde for this data center. As this positive testimonial shows, our products are not only generating tremendous savings but are also able to meet the demanding needs of a data center environment. Such real-world proof points are essential to convert our data center pipeline to orders."

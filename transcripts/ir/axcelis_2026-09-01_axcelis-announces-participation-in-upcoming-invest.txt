Axcelis Announces Participation in Upcoming Investor Conferences

September 1, 2026 at 8:00 AM EDT

BEVERLY, Mass. , Sept. 1, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Axcelis Technologies, Inc. (NASDAQ: ACLS), a leading supplier of enabling ion implantation solutions for the semiconductor industry, announced the company's plans to participate in the following upcoming investor conferences:

The Citi 2026 Global TMT Conference on Tuesday, September 8 th at the New York Hilton Midtown .

The B. Riley Securities Consumer & TMT Conference on Thursday, September 10 th at the InterContinental New York Times Square .

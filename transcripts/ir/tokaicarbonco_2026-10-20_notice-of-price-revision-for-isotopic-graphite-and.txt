October 20, 2026

Notice of Price Revision for Isotopic Graphite and Related Products

Tokai Carbon announces revising the price of Isotopic graphite and

related products as follows.

1. Price increase

(a) Scope:  Isotopic graphite and related products

(b) Effective date:  January 1st, 2027

(c) Price increase:  10% ~ 20%

2. Background

In addition to the recent surge in energy prices and rising logistics costs,

the sharp increase in the prices of coke and coal-tar pitch -our primary raw

materials- has made the business environment surrounding Isotropic

graphite increasingly severe.

We have continually strived to improve production efficiency and reduce

costs in an effort to maintain our current pricing. However, it has become

impossible to absorb these soaring costs through our own efforts alone.

Therefore, to ensure the continuous a nd stable supply of our products in

the future, we have reluctantly decided to request the aforementioned

price revision.

We deeply appreciate your kind understanding and cooperation in this

matter.

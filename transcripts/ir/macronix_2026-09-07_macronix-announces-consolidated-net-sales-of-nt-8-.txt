Hsinchu, Taiwan, September 7, 2026 - Macronix International Co., Ltd. (TSE: 2337) today announced the unaudited consolidated net sales for August 2026 were NT$8.145 billion, an increase of 5.4% from the previous month of NT$7.725 billion. Compared to the same period of NT$2.557 billion in 2025, the consolidated net sales of August increased 218.5% in 2026.

Accumulated consolidated net sales for January to August 2026 were NT$45.463 billion, an increase of 149.1% compared to the same period of NT$18.248 billion in 2025.

Notice Concerning Completion of Payment for Disposal of Treasury Shares

as Restricted Stock Compensation Plan

Sumitomo Bakelite Co., Ltd. hereby announces that the payment procedures for the disposal of its

treasury shares as restricted stock compensation, which were resolved at the meeting of the Board of

Directors held on June 24, 2026, were completed on July 17, 2026 as follows. Please refer to “Notice

Concerning Disposal of Treasury Shares as Restricted Stock Compensation ” announced on June 24,

2026 for more details.

Outline of disposal of treasury shares

(1) Disposal date July 17, 2026

(2) Class and numbers of disposed shares 11,411 shares of the Company’s common shares

(3) Disposal price 7,393 yen per share

(4) Total value of disposed shares 84,361,523 yen

(5) Allottees of disposed shares Directors (excluding outside directors):

number of people - 5   4,795 shares

Executive Officers:

number of people - 12  6,616 shares

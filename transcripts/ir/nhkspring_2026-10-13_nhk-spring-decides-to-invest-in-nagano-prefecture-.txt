NHK SPRING Decides to Invest in Nagano Prefectureʼs “Green Bonds”

NHK SPRING CO., LTD. (Head Office: Yokohama; President & COO: Kazuhisa Uemura) is pleased to

announce its decision to invest in green bonds issued by Nagano Prefecture for the fifth consecutive

year.

Nagano Prefecture is a key business area for the NHK SPRING Group, with approximately 2,000

employees—about 20% of the Groupʼs employees in Japan—working at 14 sites in the Prefecture. In

addition, the Prefecture is advancing decarbonization initiatives and measures to address natural

disasters caused by climate change, with the aim of achieving net-zero carbon dioxide emissions by

2050.

Through this investment, NHK SPRING aims to support these initiatives and contribute to enhancing

the sustainability of the local community, thereby pursuing the continuous development of its business

together with the region.

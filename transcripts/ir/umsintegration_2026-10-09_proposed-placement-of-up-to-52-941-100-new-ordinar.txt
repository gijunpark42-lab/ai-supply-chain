UMS INTEGRATION LIMITED

(Company Registration Number 200100340R)

(Incorporated in the Republic of Singapore)

PROPOSED PLACEMENT OF UP TO 52,941,100 NEW ORDINARY SHARES IN THE CAPITAL OF

THE COMPANY – ALLOTMENT AND ISSUANCE OF NEW SHARES

1. INTRODUCTION

The Board of Directors (the “Board” or “Directors”) of UMS Integration Limited (the “Company”,

and together with its subsidiaries, the “Group”) refers to the Company’s announcements on 29

September 2026 and 5 October 2026 in relation to the Proposed Placement (the “Previous

Announcements”).

Unless otherwise defined, all terms and references used herein shall bear the same meaning as

ascribed to them in the Previous Announcements.

2. ALLOTMENT AND ISSUANCE OF NEW SHARES

Further to the Previous Announcements, the Board is pleased to announce the completion of the

Proposed Placement (“Completion”), pursuant to which all 52,941,100 Placement Shares were

allotted and issued to the respective subscribers at the Placement Price of RM8.50 (equivalent

to approximately S$2.66 as at 28 September 2026, being the last full market day preceding the

date on which the Placement Agreement was signed) per Placement Share in accordance with

the terms and conditions of the Placement Agreement with CGS International Securities Malaysia

Sdn Bhd and AmInvestment Bank Berhad acting as the Placement Agents.

The Placement Shares issued (a) are fully paid up and free and clear of all pledges, liens,

charges, security interest, encumbrances, equities, claims and all other third party rights

whatsoever; (b) have the same rights as, and rank in all respects pari passu with, all the other

Shares in issue; and (c) entitle the persons in whose name the Placement Shares are registered

to all dividends, rights, allotment and distributions declared, paid or made by the Company on or

after the date of issue of the Placement Shares; no person has any pre-emptive, resale or other

rights with respect to the Placement Shares and there are no restrictions on subsequent transfer

of the Placement Shares.

Following the allotment and issuance of the Placement Shares on 9 October 2026, the Closing

Date, the total number of issued shares of the Company increased from 888,167,856 ordinary

shares to 941,108,956 ordinary shares.

The Placement Shares are expected to be listed and quoted on the Main Market of Bursa

Securities and the Mainboard of the SGX-ST on or around 12 October 2026.

The Company will continue to keep Shareholders updated and release announcements relating

to the Proposed Placement (including any material developments and progress made) as may

be appropriate from time to time.

3. INTERESTS OF DIRECTORS AND SUBSTANTIAL SHAREHOLDERS

Save that Xie Xingbei Pearlyn, an independent director of the Company, is a Partner at Shook

Lin & Bok LLP, the Singapore law firm advising the Company on the Proposed Placement, none

of the Directors or Substantial Shareholders has any direct or indirect interest in the Proposed

Placement, other than through their directorships and/or shareholdings in the Company, if any.

For the avoidance of doubt, Wong Gang is the partner-in-charge of the transaction at Shook Lin

& Bok LLP.

Infineon and ASMedia collaborate to expand USB 20Gbps ecosystem for AI and high-bandwidth applications

Press Release

2026/09/30

Infineon Technologies AG (FSE: IFX / OTCQX: IFNNY), a global semiconductor leader in USB connectivity, and ASMedia announce a strategic collaboration to accelerate the adoption of USB 20Gbps technology, targeting the surging demands of machine-learning cameras and physical AI systems. Infineon’s EZ-USB™ FX20 controller, introduced in 2025, helps address the device-side requirements for demands of bandwidth-intensive applications, enabling 20Gbps data throughput over existing USB-C infrastructure.

To support broader deployment, reliable high-bandwidth connectivity requires more than just device-side components. It demands robust cabling and comprehensive host support. By integrating Infineon’s EZ-USB FX20 with ASMedia’s latest USB4 40Gbps host controller, the ASM4242, the two companies are leading the industry in building a cohesive, end-to-end USB 20Gbps ecosystem. This partnership enables developers and manufacturers to implement high-speed USB connectivity in emerging AI and data-intensive applications.

“With the introduction of our EZ-USB FX20 controller in 2025, we’ve empowered next-generation devices, from AI-powered cameras to advanced industrial systems, to unlock the full potential of USB 20Gbps connectivity. However, true ecosystem acceleration requires more than just device-side innovation,” said Ganesh Subramaniam, Senior Vice President of Wired Connectivity Solutions at Infineon. “Partnering with ASMedia allows us to deliver a complete, robust solution that includes critical host support and cabling infrastructure. Together, we are removing the final barriers to widespread USB 20Gbps adoption and enabling developers to harness this high-bandwidth data pipe with confidence.”

“As the industry demands agile breakthroughs in machine learning and physical AI, a seamless connectivity ecosystem is essential,” said Che-Wei Lin, President at ASMedia. “ASMedia drives high-speed transmission leadership with comprehensive USB capabilities spanning 20Gbps to 80Gbps. With this latest integration of our ASM4242 USB4 host controller and Infineon's EZ-USB FX20, we establish a unified pathway that bridges robust host infrastructure with AI peripherals, ensuring a dynamic, future-proof ecosystem.”

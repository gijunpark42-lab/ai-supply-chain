Zhen Ding Technology Holding Limited (4958 TT) September 2026 Monthly Revenue Report

Zhen Ding Technology Holding Limited (Ticker: 4958), a global leading PCB manufacturer, today (October 8) reported September 2026 revenue of NT$26,358 million, up 36.13% YoY and 27.54% MoM, setting a new monthly revenue record. Third-quarter revenue reached NT$64,624 million, up 36.44% YoY, while cumulative revenue for the first nine months totaled NT$153,783 million, up 22.39% YoY. Both figures also reached record highs for their respective periods.

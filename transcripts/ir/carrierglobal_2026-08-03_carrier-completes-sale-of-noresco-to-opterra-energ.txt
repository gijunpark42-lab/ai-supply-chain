Carrier Completes Sale of NORESCO to OPTERRA Energy Services

August 03, 2026

Download (opens in new window)

PALM BEACH GARDENS, Fla. , Aug. 3, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Carrier Global Corporation (NYSE: CARR), global leader in intelligent climate and energy solutions, today announced it has completed the sale of its NORESCO business to OPTERRA Energy Services, a subsidiary of LS Power.

Jefferies LLC served as financial advisor to Carrier. Akerman LLP served as external legal counsel.

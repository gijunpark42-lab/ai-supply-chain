Unisys Named a Leader in NelsonHall's 2026 NEAT Evaluation for AI-Enabled Cloud Infrastructure Management Services

September 14, 2026 at 8:30 AM EDT

Global analyst firm recognizes Unisys for AI-driven cloud management capabilities, intelligent operations and strategic technology investments

BLUE BELL, Pa. , Sept. 14, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Unisys (NYSE: UIS) has been named a Leader in the 2026 AI-Enabled Cloud Infrastructure Management Services report by global analyst firm NelsonHall's Vendor Evaluation & Assessment Tool (NEAT). Unisys was rated highly for its AI-driven approach to cloud infrastructure management, including intelligent operations, self-healing capabilities and agentic AI innovation. The report also highlights Unisys Intelligence Accelerator (UIA), the company's AI enabled delivery platform, which automates workflows and streamlines operations across hybrid infrastructure, applications, data, and security layers of clients' ecosystems.

The NEAT analyzes the performance of vendors offering AI-enabled services for cloud and hybrid environments, allowing strategic sourcing managers to assess vendors' capabilities across various criteria and business situations. The report also enables organizations to identify vendors that drive operational efficiency, strengthen resilience, and support evolving business requirements through AI-powered infrastructure operations.

"AI is reshaping how organizations oversee and optimize their technology environments," said Chris Arrasmith , chief operating officer of Unisys. "Being recognized as a Leader by NelsonHall validates our approach to combining AI, automation and cloud expertise to manage increasingly complex environments. Through continued investments in innovations such as the Unisys Intelligence Accelerator , we are focused on delivering measurable business outcomes that improve business performance, strengthen resilience and accelerate technology transformation."

Additional Unisys strengths cited in the report include:

AI-driven cloud management capabilities for hybrid and multi-cloud environments, including automation, predictive analytics, and self-healing operations.

Differentiated intellectual property and accelerators, including Unisys Intelligent Operations, Cloud Business Office and Multi-Agent Orchestration.

Global delivery scale, supported by approximately 2,200 cloud infrastructure management professionals serving nearly 150 clients worldwide.

"Unisys is investing in its intelligent operations to provide AI and data-driven cloud transformation and management capabilities," said John Lahery, IT services research director at NelsonHall . "This includes AI-centric consulting through its Cloud Business Office, and the expansion of IP and accelerators, including self-healing autonomous agents. Unisys further provides a modular plug-and-play approach that will resonate with clients looking to leverage their existing tooling investments and access a broader partner ecosystem to support their AI initiatives."

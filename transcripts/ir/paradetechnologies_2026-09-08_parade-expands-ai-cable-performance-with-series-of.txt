Parade Expands AI cable performance with series of FIT Ethernet/InfiniBand NDR & XDR cables from 2 to 4 meters

Posted September 7th, 2026

San Jose, Calif.— 09 September, 2026 — Parade Technologies , Ltd. (Taiwan OTC: 4966.TWO), a leading high-speed interface, video display, touch controllers and Linear Redriver supplier would like to announce in cooperation with FIT – a leading cable manufacturer – the successful deployment of Parade Ultra Linear Redrivers (LRDs) in an OSFP (Octal Small Form Factor Pluggable) for inclusion on the June 2026 PF43s InfiniBand Trade Association Integrators List at both XDR (224Gbps) and NDR(112Gbps) data rates.

“Such lists ensure seamless operation with key AI servers, XPUs and switches” says Jimmy Chiu, Executive VP of Marketing at Parade Technologies. “OEMs can now feel confident employing Parade LRDs in copper cable solutions from 2 to 4 meters to reducing power, latency and rack weight, as well as improved rack routing.”

“Parade Ultra Ethernet series of linear redrivers are capable of supporting latest 802.3dj ethernet specification as well as InfiniBand, UALink, NVLink and OIF standards and extends our commitment to enabling the short reach, high-performance, AI scaleup market with low power, low latency solutions”, Jimmy Chiu further added.

Parade offers a family of PAM4 Ethernet/InfiniBand Ultra Linear redrivers at 56G (EDR), 112G (NDR) and 224G( XDR) cable line rates, as well as a line of PCIe linear redriver solutions.

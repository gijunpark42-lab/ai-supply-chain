DNEG scales creative visual effects and animation workflows with HPE servers and financial services

September 8, 2026

Blog Post Compute Customer Win Financial Services Sustainability & Social Impact

In this article

DNEG is using modern HPE infrastructure to support increasingly complex, compute-intensive VFX and animation workflows, including the large-scale environments, water simulations, destruction and storm sequences created for The Last of Us Season 2

DNEG is modernizing its technology environment to enable thousands of artists and technologists across regions and time zones to collaborate more flexibly, with high-performance, secure and scalable infrastructure supporting evolving creative demands

DNEG is using HPE Financial Services to make critical infrastructure investments more flexible and sustainable, combining the HPE 90/9 Advantage with IT asset disposition services to manage cash flow, extend technology lifecycles and support responsible reuse and recycling

DNEG scales creative visual effects and animation workflows with HPE servers and financial services

In the world of visual effects and animation, artists, animators, and technologists collaborate across continents and time zones to bring some of the world's most celebrated films and television series to life.

For DNEG, the award-winning studio behind productions like The Odyssey, Dune , The Last of Us , and The Angry Birds Movie 3 , supporting this global creative ecosystem requires a technology foundation that can deliver performance, security, scalability, and flexibility. With DNEG operating a worldwide production environment, thousands of artists and technology specialists work around the clock, often collaborating on the same productions from different regions. They rely on access to compute-intensive resources capable of handling complex visual effects, high-res olution content, graphics-intensive workloads, and large volumes of project data across globally distributed environments.

For ‘The Last of Us Season 2’, DNEG was tasked with creating significant and complex environmental CG builds, including detailed cityscapes, extensive water simulations, massive building destruction, and large-scale storm sequences. To deliver these high-fidelity shots, DNEG relied on a modernized, high-performance infrastructure designed to meet the demands of complex visual effects workflows. This robust compute environment provided the necessary graphics capabilities and processing power to handle intricate FX simulations, digidouble integration, and vast city builds, enabling seamless collaboration across DNEG’s globally distributed teams.

Modernizing infrastructure to unlock agility

As DNEG's operations expanded globally, traditional desktop environments became increasingly difficult to scale and manage. The company identified virtualization as a key enabler of future growth, allowing artists to access secure, high-performance workspaces from multiple locations while supporting more flexible ways of working. DNEG partnered with HPE to modernize infrastructure, accelerate virtualization initiatives, and create a more agile technology foundation.

Combining high-performance compute infrastructure with flexible financing, DNEG is enabling seamless collaboration across regions for its globally distributed workforce, supporting evolving business demands, and helping position the company for future growth. The modernized environment, provided by HPE ProLiant DL385 Gen11 servers featuring AMD EPYC™ 9475F processors, HPE DDR5 Smart Memory, and NVIDIA RTX PRO™6000 Blackwell Server Edition offers the compute density, memory performance, and graphics capabilities necessary to enable complex visual effects workflows across distributed teams. Engineered for flexible deployment, the platform is optimized for both advanced air cooling and direct liquid cooling configurations to further minimize power consumption in dense data center footprints.

In the age of high-res olution IMAX and 3D cinema and immersive experiences, and with constantly evolving content demands, we needed a technology strategy that could scale with us. HPE has helped us modernize our infrastructure, creating a secure, high-performance environment that provides our artists with the flexibility and high-speed access they need to deliver exceptional creative work.

Vikas Rathee

Chief Financial Officer, DNEG

“We lead on the industry's most demanding creative projects and our success depends on our ability to collaborate and work as one big team across different countries and time zones. In the age of high-res olution IMAX and 3D cinema and immersive experiences, and with constantly evolving content demands, we needed a technology strategy that could scale with us,” said Vikas Rathee, Chief Financial Officer, DNEG. “HPE has helped us modernize our infrastructure, creating a secure, high-performance environment that provides our artists with the flexibility and high-speed access they need to deliver exceptional creative work.”

Aligning technology investment with business strategy

However, like many organizations, DNEG faced competing technology priorities. Significant investments were already underway to support rendering capabilities and core compute infrastructure, making it difficult to accelerate a large-scale virtual desktop initiative through traditional funding approaches. Rather than delaying a project considered essential for future collaboration and operational efficiency, DNEG explored new ways to align infrastructure investment with business outcomes.

Working with HPE Financial Services (HPEFS), the company adopted the recently launched HPE 90/9 Advantage. This offer enabled DNEG to deploy the technology it needed when it’s available, defer payments for 90 days, and begin with ultra-low payments for the following nine months – giving them time to install, migrate, and stabilize their new environment before funding ramps up. This approach provided DNEG with the flexibility needed to move forward with critical modernization efforts, while maintaining financial discipline and protecting investments in other strategic initiatives.

In an era of continuous technological innovation, how and when you invest matters as much as the technology you deploy. Our role is to help customers, like DNEG, move now, with the flexibility to scale and adjust as value materializes and priorities evolve.

Maeve Culloty

President & CEO, HPE Financial Services

“In an era of continuous technological innovation, how and when you invest matters as much as the technology you deploy,” said Maeve Culloty, President & CEO, HPE Financial Services. “Our role is to help customers, like DNEG, move now, with the flexibility to scale and adjust as value materializes and priorities evolve.”

As DNEG continues to transform its technology environment, the company is placing greater emphasis on sustainability and operational efficiency alongside performance. By modernizing its infrastructure with high-performance, energy-efficient technologies and implementing a global IT asset disposition (ITAD) program with HPEFS, DNEG is taking a more holistic approach to sustainable IT. The program supports responsible asset retirement, reuse, and recycling, while helping maximize the value and lifespan of technology investments.

For the company, IT sustainability has become an important consideration alongside performance, helping ensure that future technology investments support both business goals and environmental objectives. This holistic approach into technology lifecycle management reflects the company’s broader strategy of building a resilient, future-ready foundation for global creative production.

UMS INTEGRATION LIMITED

(Company Registration Number 200100340R)

(Incorporated in the Republic of Singapore)

PROPOSED PLACEMENT OF UP TO 52,941,100 NEW ORDINARY SHARES IN THE CAPITAL OF

THE COMPANY – ENTRY INTO PLACEMENT AGREEMENT

1. INTRODUCTION

The Board of Directors (the “Board” or “Directors”) of UMS Integration Limited (the “Company”,

and together with its subsidiaries, the “Group”) wishes to announce that the Company has on 29

September 2026 entered into a placement agreement with CGS International Securities Malaysia

Sdn Bhd and AmInvestment Bank Berhad (collectively referred to as the “Placement Agents”,

and each a “Placement Agent”) (the “Placement Agreement”).

Pursuant to the Placement Agreement, the Company has agreed to offer, and each of the

Placement Agents agreed severally (and not jointly, or jointly and severally), strictly on a best

effort basis and without any obligation on the part of the Placement Agents to underwrite or

subscribe as principal for any Placement Shares that remain unsubscribed for whatever reason,

to (either by itself or through one or more of its affiliates) procure subscriptions for up to

52,941,100 new ordinary shares of the total issued and paid up share capital in the Company

(the “Placement Shares”) at the subscription price of RM8.50 per Placement Share (the

“Placement Price”), amounting to a maximum aggregate consideration of up to RM450 million

(equivalent to approximately S$2.66 per Placement Share, amounting to a maximum aggregate

consideration of approximately up to S$140.83 million, based on the exchange rate referred to in

paragraph 2.1 of Section 2 below) (the "Proposed Placement").

The Placement Shares will only be offered and sold in Malaysia, in an offering for purchase or

an invitation to purchase, under Schedule 6 and Schedule 7 of the Capital Markets and Services

Act 2007 of Malaysia.

For the avoidance of doubt, the Placement Shares are not being offered in Singapore, and the

Proposed Placement does not constitute an offer of capital markets products within the meaning

of the Securities and Futures Act 2001 of Singapore (“SFA”). Accordingly, no prospectus, offer

document or offer information statement will be issued by the Company in connection with the

Proposed Placement.

2. THE PROPOSED PLACEMENT

2.1. Placement Price

For the purposes of Rule 811 of the Listing Manual (Mainboard Rules) of the Singapore Exchange

Securities Trading Limited ("SGX-ST"), the Placement Price of RM8.50 per Placement Share is

equivalent to approximately S$2.66 per Placement Share, based on the closing SGD:RM

exchange rate at the 5.00 p.m. middle rate on 28 September 2026, being the date of volume

weighted average price for trades done in the Shares on SGX-ST for the last full market day

preceding the date on which the Placement Agreement was signed, of SGD1.00:RM3.1953, as

extracted from the website of Bank Negara Malaysia (“BNM”).

This is equivalent to a discount of 0.23% to the volume weighted average price of S$2.6660 for

trades done in the Shares on SGX-ST on 28 September 2026 being the last full market day

preceding the date on which the Placement Agreement was signed, which is within the maximum

discount of 10% permitted under Rule 811(1) of the Listing Manual (Mainboard Rules) of the

SGX-ST.

For information, the Placement Price is at a discount of approximately 1.11% to the volume

weighted average price on 28 September 2026 of RM8.5950 based on the Company’s share

price on Bursa Malaysia Securities Berhad (“Bursa Securities”).

2.2. Placement Shares

The Placement Shares represent approximately 5.96% of the existing issued and paid-up share

capital of the Company comprising 888,167,856 ordinary shares as at the date of this

announcement (the “Shares”) and will represent approximately 5.63%% of the enlarged issued

and paid-up share capital of the Company of 941,108,956 Shares (assuming that the maximum

52,941,100 Placement Shares have been placed).

The Proposed Placement is undertaken pursuant to the general mandate approved by the

shareholders of the Company (the "Shareholders") in its annual general meeting held on 30

April 2026 (the "General Mandate"), and the Placement Shares fall within the aggregate number

of shares permitted to be issued under the General Mandate. More information on the General

Mandate is set out in Section 6 of this announcement. Further, on 1 September 2026, BNM

approved the application submitted by the Company for the issuance of RM securities by a non-

resident with its approval subjected to the following conditions set out in paragraph 4.3 of Section

4 below.

The Placement Shares when issued, allotted and delivered by the Company will be validly allotted

and issued in accordance with the laws and regulations of Malaysia and to the extent applicable,

the laws and regulations of Singapore and will on issuance:

(a) be fully paid up and free and clear of all pledges, liens, charges, security interest,

encumbrances, equities, claims and all other third party rights whatsoever;

(b) have the same rights as, and rank in all respects pari passu with, all the other Shares in

issue; and

(c) entitle the persons in whose name the Placement Shares are registered to all dividends,

rights, allotment and distributions declared, paid or made by the Company on or after the

date of issue of the Placement Shares; no person has any pre-emptive, resale or other rights

with respect to the Placement Shares and there are no restrictions on subsequent transfer

of the Placement Shares.

The Placement Shares will not be placed to any person who is a director or a substantial

shareholder of the Company, or any other person in the categories set out in Rule 812(1) of the

Listing Manual (Mainboard Rules) of the SGX-ST, unless such subscription is otherwise agreed

to by SGX-ST or specific shareholders’ approval is obtained (as applicable).

The purchase and/or subscription of the Placement Shares will not result in the transfer of a

“controlling interest” which would require specific shareholders’ approval pursuant to Rule 803 of

the Listing Manual (Mainboard Rules) of the SGX-ST. The number of Placement Shares to be

placed to any end-placee is not expected to result in the end-placee becoming a substantial

shareholder of the Company (“Substantial Shareholder”). In the event that any end-placee

becomes a Substantial Shareholder as a result of the Proposed Placement, a further

announcement will be released in accordance with the applicable rules and guidelines of the

SGX-ST and Bursa Securities.

3. SALIENT TERMS OF THE PLACEMENT AGREEMENT

3.1. Payment of Fees, Commissions and Expenses

The Company shall pay a fee (the “Placement Fee”) to the Placement Agents amounting to 1.5

per cent of the gross proceeds from the issuance of the Placement Shares at the Placement

Price, discretionary fee (payable by the Company to the Placement Agents solely at the

Company's discretion, which if payable, shall be equal to 0.25 per cent of the gross proceeds

from the Placement Shares ultimately placed out by the Placement Agents pursuant to the

Proposed Placement at the Placement Price) together with any applicable taxes, total amount of

which the Placement Agents are authorised to deduct from the payment to be made by the

Placement Agents to the Company.

In the event the Placement Agents fail to procure subscriptions in accordance with the allocations

agreed between the Company and the Placement Agents, resulting in the number of Placement

Shares ultimately placed out by the Placement Agents deviating from such allocations, the

Placement Agents shall, no later than the Closing Date (as defined below), notify the Company

accordingly by way of a notice and the relevant Placement Fees and discretionary fee (if

applicable) payable to each Placement Agent shall be adjusted accordingly based on the number

of Placement Shares ultimately placed out as set out in such notice.

The Placement Agents shall be entitled to charge and keep for their own account a subscription

fee or brokerage fee of up to 1% of the Placement Price, not comprising any part of the placing

monies that the Placement Agents may receive from their respective placees.

3.2. Completion of the Proposed Placement

Subject to the terms and conditions of the Placement Agreement, completion shall take place on

9 October 2026 and/or such other dates as may be agreed between the parties from time to time

(the “Closing Date”), being the crediting date of the Placement Shares.

3.3. Confirmations by the Placement Agents

The Placement Agents confirm that:

(a) the Placement Fee payable by the Company to the Placement Agents for the Proposed

Placement will not be shared with any of the end-placees of the Proposed Placement;

(b) they will procure representations from the end-placees procured by the Placement Agents

that:

(i) each of the end-placees will not acquire a controlling interest consequent to the

subscription of Placement Shares, and accordingly, the Proposed Placement will not

result in the transfer of a controlling interest of the Company under Rule 803 of the

Listing Manual (Mainboard Rules) of the SGX-ST;

(ii) none of the end-placees is a director or a substantial shareholder or any other person

in the categories set out in Rule 812(1) of the Listing Manual (Mainboard Rules) unless

such subscription is otherwise approved by the SGX-ST, or interested persons as

defined under Chapter 9 of the Listing Manual (Mainboard Rules); and

(c) to the best of their knowledge and belief, there are no share borrowing arrangements

entered into to facilitate the Proposed Placement.

4. RATIONALE FOR AND USE OF PROCEEDS FROM THE PROPOSED PLACEMENT

4.1 Rationale

The Proposed Placement will strengthen the Group's financial position, provide flexibility to

capitalise on growth opportunities, broaden the shareholder base and expand the capital base of

the Group.

4.2 Use of Proceeds

The estimated net proceeds from the Proposed Placement, after deducting estimated fees and

expenses (including listing and application fees, the fees payable to the Placement Agents,

professional fees and other miscellaneous expenses of approximately RM 7.0 million (equivalent

to approximately S$2.19 million) is RM443.0 million (equivalent to approximately S$138.64

million) (the “Net Proceeds”).

The Company intends to apply such Net Proceeds in the following estimated proportions:

(1) The Group intends to utilise proceeds of up to RM332.25 million (equivalent to approximately S$103.98

million) or 75.0% of Net Proceeds to fund the Group’s projects in Penang, Malaysia.

UMS is currently headquartered in Singapore with production facilities in Singapore, Malaysia and

California, USA. In January 2024, the Group has commenced volume production in its Penang

production facilities located In Penang Science North Park. On July 2024, the Company has acquired

an additional industrial land next to the existing production facility to expand the existing production

facility of the Group.

To support the strong and growing demand for its products, and to address ongoing challenges to

manpower shortages, the Company intends to use the proceeds to expand its manufacturing

capabilities by implementing advanced automation in its production processes. This initiative will

involve the integration of automated machinery, robotics, and digital monitoring systems to enhance

production efficiency, improve product consistency, and reduce reliance on manual labour.

(2) The Company intends to utilise proceeds of up to RM110.75 million (equivalent to approximately

S$34.66 million) or 25.0% of Net Proceeds for its business in Singapore and Vietnam. The proceeds

are expected to be use for its business expansion in Vietnam as well ongoing operation in Singapore

which include but not limited to the following: -

(a) In Vietnam, the proceeds is expected to be utilised for acquisition of new factory and/or lease of

new factory as well as to establish a manufacturing operations line (including to buy new

machineries) to support its capacity expansion and to accommodate increasing demand of

products by utilising the higher abundance of skilled labour and lower operational cost in Vietnam.

(b) In Singapore, to address the ongoing challenges related to manpower shortages in Singapore,

the Company also intends to expand its manufacturing capabilities by implementing advanced

automation in its production processes. This initiative will involve integration of automated

machinery, robotics and digital monitoring systems to enhance production efficiency, improve

product consistency, and reduce reliance on manual labour.

The proceeds will be utilised for our implementation of manufacturing automation in Singapore.

The automation expenses include amongst other, the purchases of automation machineries in

Singapore.

The percentages stated above are estimates only and may be adjusted by the Company having

regard to its prevailing business and operational priorities. In the event that the Net Proceeds

raised are less than the maximum amount contemplated under the Proposed Placement, the

Company may allocate the Net Proceeds among the above uses in such proportions as the Board

may deem appropriate.

Pending deployment of the Net Proceeds, such Net Proceeds may be deposited with banks

and/or financial institutions, invested in short-term money market instruments or marketable

securities, and/or used for any other purposes on a short-term basis, as the Company may, in its

absolute discretion, deem fit from time to time.

The Company will make periodic announcements as and when the Net Proceeds are materially

disbursed and whether such a use is in accordance with the stated use and in accordance with

the percentage allocated. Where the Net Proceeds are to be used for working capital purposes,

the Company will disclose a breakdown with specific details on the use of proceeds for working

capital in the Company’s announcements on the use of the Net Proceeds. Where there is any

material deviation from the stated use of proceeds, the Company will announce the reasons for

such deviation. The Company will also provide a status report on the use of the Net Proceeds in

its annual report.

(e) inform BNM of any material change to the information previously given for its application for

such approval, including cancellation of the proposed issuance.

6. MANDATE FOR THE ISSUANCE OF THE PLACEMENT SHARES

The Placement Shares will be allotted and issued pursuant to the General Mandate. The General

Mandate grants authority to the Directors to, inter alia, allot and issue new Shares not exceeding

50% of the total number of issued Shares (excluding treasury shares and subsidiary holdings) as

at the date of the annual general meeting on 30 April 2026, of which the aggregate number of

shares to be issued other than on a pro rata basis to existing Shareholders shall not exceed 20%

of the total number of issued Shares (excluding treasury shares and subsidiary holdings).

As at the date of the annual general meeting on 30 April 2026, the issued and paid-up share

capital of the Company was 888,167,856 Shares. No Shares have been issued under the

General Mandate. Accordingly, the Placement Shares (assuming that the maximum 52,941,100

Placement Shares have been placed) is within the limit of the General Mandate.

The Company will be making an application to the SGX-ST and Bursa Securities for the listing

and quotation of the Placement Shares on the Mainboard of the SGX-ST and Main Market of

Bursa Securities. The Company will make the necessary announcements in due course upon

obtaining the SGX-ST approval.

8. OPINION OF THE DIRECTORS

The Directors are of the opinion that, after taking into consideration:

(a) the Group’s present bank facilities, the working capital available to the Group is sufficient to

meet its present requirements; and

(b) the Group’s present bank facilities and the Net Proceeds of the Proposed Placement, the

working capital available to the Group is sufficient to meet its present requirements.

Notwithstanding the above, the Directors are of the opinion that the Proposed Placement is

beneficial to the interest of the Company and its Shareholders after taking into consideration the

rationale for the Proposed Placement set out in paragraph 4.1. of Section 4 above.

Wesco Named to Fortune's 2026 AIQ75 List

September 29, 2026

PDF Version

PITTSBURGH , Sept. 29, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Wesco International (NYSE:WCC), a leading provider of business-to-business distribution, logistics services and supply chain solutions, has been recognized by Fortune Magazine as a member of the Fortune AIQ 75™, a list that recognizes the top 75 Fortune 500 companies for their readiness and ability to deploy AI at scale.

Wesco earned the #24 spot on the Fortune AIQ 75 list, a ranking that is determined by combining measures of enterprise AI maturity with real-world evidence of how companies are investing in and applying AI. Measurements are based on the result of evaluations from Fortune – in partnership with Enterprise Technology Research (ETR) and leveraging ServiceNow's Enterprise AI Maturity Index- which assess an organization's AI investments, outcomes and reputation among industry peers.

Fortune also states that companies recognized on the AIQ 75 list are both putting governance and visibility at the center of their AI strategy, leveraging the context and control of their agents to deliver impactful results that position their organizations for competitive advantage.

"We prioritize AI as a strategic enabler of growth, productivity and customer value, while maintaining a strong focus on governance and innovation. Our inclusion on Fortune's AIQ 75 list recognizes the progress Wesco has made in translating AI investments into enterprise-wide impact and positioning the company for continued success in an increasingly digital world," said John Engel, Chairman, President and CEO.

The Wesco team is grateful for this recognition of our commitment to building the capabilities, governance and culture needed to leverage AI at scale. We look forward to continuing to advance AI initiatives that improve how we serve customers, empower our teams and drive sustainable business performance.

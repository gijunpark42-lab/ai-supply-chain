Sep 16, 2026

Adeia named Asia Pacific Outstanding Company of the Year 2025-2026

Adeia named Asia Pacific Outstanding Company of the Year 2025-2026

Company named Asia Pacific Outstanding Company of the Year

Steve Kim recognized as Outstanding Executive of the Year

SAN JOSE, Calif., Sept. 16, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Adeia Inc. (Nasdaq: ADEA), the technology company pioneering foundational innovations that shape how the world computes, connects and experiences entertainment, today announced it has received two honors at the Asia Pacific Business Awards 2025–2026.

Adeia was named Asia Pacific Outstanding Company of the Year 2025–2026, while Steve Kim, vice president of licensing, received the Outstanding Executive of the Year 2025–2026 award. Together, the awards recognize Adeia's business performance and commitment to the Asia-Pacific region, as well as Kim’s leadership and contributions to the region.

The recognition reflects Adeia's continued commitment to expanding its licensing business, advancing breakthrough technologies, and strengthening customer relationships throughout the Asia-Pacific region.

"Adeia is honored to be recognized as Asia Pacific Outstanding Company of the Year 2025–2026. This recognition reflects the strength of our Asia-Pacific team, our commitment to customers in the region, and the impact of our technology and licensing business," said Dr. Mark Kokes, chief revenue officer, Adeia. "Steve has been instrumental in expanding Adeia's presence across Asia-Pacific and building trusted relationships with many of the region's leading technology companies. His recognition as Outstanding Executive of the Year is a well-deserved acknowledgment of his leadership and contributions, and I congratulate Steve and the broader Asia-Pacific team on these honors."

"I am honored to receive this recognition, which reflects the dedication of our entire Asia-Pacific team and the trusted relationships we have built with customers across the region," said Steve Kim, vice president of licensing, head of APAC, Adeia. "As demand for next-generation semiconductor and media technologies continues to grow, we remain committed to delivering the foundational innovations and licensing expertise that enable our customers to shape the future."

The Asia Pacific Business Awards recognize organizations and executives operating throughout the Asia-Pacific region for outstanding business performance, visionary leadership and meaningful contributions to their industries. The awards highlight companies and leaders for exceptional performance, regional commitments and leadership.

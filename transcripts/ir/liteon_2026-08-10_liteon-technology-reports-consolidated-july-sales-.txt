LITEON Technology Reports Consolidated July Sales of NT$19 Billion, Up 2% M-o-M and 38% Y-o-Y

Summary

LITEON’s consolidated revenue for July reached NT$19 billion, representing a 2% M-o-M and 38% Y-o-Y increase. Strong demand for AI and cloud computing high-end server power systems, high efficiency backup battery systems (BBU), and opto-electronic semiconductors drove solid revenue growth on both a monthly and yearly basis. The cumulative sales for January to July totaled NT$115.1 billion, up 27% Y-o-Y.

LITEON Technology (2301-tw) today reported its July consolidated revenue of NT$19 billion, up 2% M-o-M and 38% Y-o-Y. Revenue growth was mainly driven by high-end server power systems for cloud and AI applications, high‑efficiency backup battery units (BBU), and opto-electronic semiconductors. The cumulative sales for January to July totaled NT$115.1 billion, up 27% Y-o-Y.

Cloud & AIoT Business contributed a 59% share of total sales, of which, benefiting from the delivery growth for power management systems of high-end datacenter server and cloud computing, Cloud & AIoT business posted a yearly growth about 80%.

Opto-electronics Business contributed a 15% share of sales, of which, LITEON’s photocouplers used in industrial automation, new energy, and AI power applications; infrared applications for invisible LED; advanced visible LED indicator featuring high integration and miniaturization for AI servers and switches continued to see solid demand, contributing to over 20% Y-o-Y revenue growth for the segment.

Information Technology & Consumer Electronics Business accounted for 26% of sales, of which, with the ongoing optimized product mix and diversified application markets, demand for LEO satellite power systems and advanced power supplies continue to grow steadily.

FAQs

Q1: What was LITEON Technology’s revenue in July 2026? What was the growth rate?

LITEON Technology’s global consolidated revenue for July 2026 reached NT$19 billion, representing a 2% increase M-o-M and a 38% increase Y-o-Y.

Q2: What were the key drivers behind LITEON Technology’s revenue growth in July 2026?

LITEON’s revenue growth in July 2026 was driven by continued strong demand for AI and cloud computing applications, including high-end server power supplies, battery backup units (BBUs), and networking power management systems. Growth in the opto-semiconductor business, supported by demand for advanced photocouplers, sensing devices, and visible-light products, also contributed to the company’s strong performance.

Q3: Which products and business segments are the major contributors to LITEON Technology’s revenue?

LITEON’s revenue is primarily generated by three core business segments:

Cloud & AIoT Business (59%): High-end server power systems, BBUs and power management systems.

Opto-electronics Business (15%): Photocouplers, infrared applications for invisible LED and advanced visible LED indicators.

Information Technology & Consumer Electronics Business (26%): LEO satellite power systems and advanced power supplies.

SCREEN to Support Relief Efforts for the 2026 Kumamoto Earthquake

August 03, 2026

Doc. No.: INFO260803-1E

Kyoto, Japan – August 3, 2026 – The SCREEN Group and its employees express deep condolences for those who lost their lives due to the 2026 Kumamoto Earthquake and their bereaved families, and extend heartfelt sympathy to all those affected by the disaster.

The SCREEN Holdings has decided to donate JPY 50 million through the Japanese Red Cross Society, in order to support efforts to provide relief to those affected by the disaster and reconstruct the devastated areas.

We sincerely pray for the swift recovery of the areas affected by the earthquake.

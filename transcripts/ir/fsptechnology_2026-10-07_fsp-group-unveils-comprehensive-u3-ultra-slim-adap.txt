FSP Group Unveils Comprehensive U3 Ultra-Slim Adapter Lineup: 90W to 460W High Power Density Design Enhances Competitive Edge for System OEMs

2026-10-07

[Taipei, Oct. 7 th , 2026] FSP Group, a global leader in power supply units and external power adapters, today announced the complete expansion of its U3 (Ultra Slim) Series adapter portfolio, now spanning an extensive wattage range from 90W to 460W. As a pioneer driving the miniaturization of power solutions for high-performance gaming laptops and edge computing platforms, FSP directly tackles the thermal and spatial bottlenecks inherent in high-power architecture. By delivering high power density paired with superior conversion efficiency, the U3 series empowers System Integrators (SIs) and OEM/ODM partners to optimize chassis layout, cut packaging footprint, and reduce freight costs—substantially boosting terminal market competitiveness.

Accelerating Form Factor Miniaturization: 50% Volume Reduction Slashes Packaging and Freight Overhead

With power requirements escalating alongside advancements in high-end processors and discrete GPUs, conventional high-wattage external adapters have grown increasingly cumbersome. These bulkier legacy units not only encumber end-users on the go, but also occupy substantial packaging space during bulk shipment, driving up ocean and air freight rates and inflating overall operational overhead for device makers.

Engineered with high-integration circuit topologies, the FSP U3 series maintains uncompromised electrical output stability and thermal reliability while compressing casing thickness to approximately 1 inch (25.4 mm). This delivers a volume reduction of up to 50% compared to standard adapters of equivalent wattage. For system manufacturers, halved dimensions translate directly to significantly higher pallet utilization, sharply reducing packaging material usage and international transport costs. The result is a distinct Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) advantage without sacrificing power integrity.

Comprehensive Power Portfolio: 90W to 460W Span, with 460W Slated for Q1 2027 Mass Production

To serve diverse computing workloads across distinct system tiers, FSP delivers an expansive wattage spectrum rarely seen in the industry:

Mass Production Models : 90W, 120W, 135W, 150W, 180W, 250W, 330W, and 420W

Roadmap Addition : 460W flagship model scheduled for mass production in Q1 2027

The introduction of the 460W tier validates the commercial viability of form-factor miniaturization in ultra-high-wattage architectures, extending robust support to next-generation high-throughput compute platforms without forcing compromise on system industrial design.

Versatile Deployments, Top-Tier Efficiency, and Global Safety Certifications

Engineered for low standby power draw and ultra-high efficiency, the U3 series is optimized for diverse target deployments:

Gaming Laptops : Meets heavy workload power spikes while lightening travel carry weight.

Mini-PCs and NUCs : Shrinks external footprints to support cleaner desktop and embedded spaces.

High-Performance Docking Stations : Delivers stable high-wattage delivery across multiple high-speed peripherals.

Point-of-Sale (POS) Terminals : Low-profile form factor enables seamless under-counter routing or direct chassis enclosure.

Across all models, the U3 family achieves over 90% conversion efficiency, complying with U.S. DoE Level VI and European ErP Lot 7 (EU 2025/2052) energy mandates. Built-in multi-layered safety mechanisms include Over Voltage (OVP), Over Current (OCP), Over Temperature (OTP), and Short Circuit Protection (SCP). Full conformance to UL, TUV, CCC, FCC, CE, and CB safety standards streamline international compliance checks and speeds time-to-market. The output DC harness integrates an ergonomic 90-degree right-angle connector, reducing mechanical stress and terminal fatigue.

Advancing "Powering AI Together": Agile Micro-Customization Services

To meet the stringent power integrity requirements of edge AI hardware and bespoke system architectures, FSP executes on its strategic initiative: "Powering AI Together." Beyond an off-the-shelf standard catalog, FSP leverages its vertically integrated design and manufacturing capabilities to deliver high-agility Micro-Customization Services for enterprise clients. FSP’s engineering teams accommodate project-specific modifications—such as fine-tuning output voltage/current rails, altering mechanical enclosures, adjusting cable specifications, and engineering proprietary connectors—helping clients shorten design cycles and de-risk deployment schedules.

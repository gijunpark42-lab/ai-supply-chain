Accton’s Monthly Sales Revenue (Consolidated) – Jun

HSINCHU, Taiwan, Jul. 06, 2026 – Accton Technology Corporation (Accton) (TWSE: 2345)

Accton Technology Corporation (Accton) (TWSE: 2345), a premier provider of communication solutions, today announced that the consolidated sales revenue for Jun. 2026 was USD$1,251 million, an increase of 51.12 percent year-over-year.

The cumulative consolidated sales revenue totaled approximately USD$5,240 million, an increase of 59.87 percent year-over-year.

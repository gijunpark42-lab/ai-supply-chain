MiTAC Computing Fuels the Agentic AI Factory at Data Centre World Asia 2026-MiTAC Holdings Corporation

2026-09-29

MCT

Singapore – September 29 th – MiTAC Computing Technology Corporation, a global leader in high-performance and energy-efficient server solutions and a subsidiary of MiTAC Holdings Corporation (TWSE:3706), today announced its power showcase at Data Centre World Asia 2026 (Booth S110). With the theme "Diversified AI Infra for Agentic AI Factory," MiTAC Computing demonstrates its industry-leading GPU design mastery built to meet massive AI compute demands.

MiTAC Computing is showcasing four featured AI servers and also demonstrating a joint effort with key memory and storage solutions partner, Micron, to address data bandwidth challenges in AI infrastructure, the exhibit features the Micron 9650 NVMe™ Gen 6 SSD , Micron 7600 NVMe™ Gen 5 SSD , Micron DDR5 RDIMM , and Micron DDR5 MRDIMM . Furthermore, MiTAC Computing is participating in the venue’s Live Data Center showcase, featuring our enterprise servers to highlight real-world data center deployment.

AI Acceleration Platforms | Fueling the Agentic AI Factory

MiTAC G4826Z5 (Flagship OAI Showcase): A groundbreaking 4U liquid-cooled GPU server purpose-built for hyperscale AI factories. Architected around 8 AMD Instinct™ MI355X GPUs in OAM specifications and dual AMD EPYC™ 9755 and AMD EPYC™ 9575F processors, the G4826Z5 shatters compute density barriers while setting a new benchmark in thermal efficiency.

MiTAC G8825Z5 : Engineered for high-density AI training, the G8825Z5 supports up to eight AMD Instinct™ MI325X or AMD Instinct™ MI350X GPUs powered by dual AMD EPYC™ 9755 and AMD EPYC™ 9575F processors. This powerhouse maximizes data throughput to drastically compress LLM training cycles in complex AI environments.

MiTAC G4520G6 : Powered by the Intel® Xeon® 6 processors and supporting up to eight NVIDIA RTX PRO™ 4500 Blackwell Server Edition , NVIDIA RTX PRO™ 6000 Blackwell Server Edition , or NVIDIA H200 NVL GPU . It delivers exceptional performance-per-watt efficiency for generative AI, enterprise analytics, and cloud HPC workloads.

MiTAC TN85-B8261 : A dual-socket GPU server supporting AMD Instinct™ MI350P , NVIDIA RTX PRO™ 4500 Blackwell Server Edition GPUs , NVIDIA RTX PRO™ 6000 Blackwell Server Edition GPUs , or NVIDIA H200 NVL GPU . With 24 DDR5-6400 RDIMM slots and tool-less NVMe storage carriers, it delivers the throughput and flexibility deep learning and HPC environments require.

Enterprise Solutions | High-Density Platform for Data-Intensive Applications

MiTAC R2520G6 Enterprise 2U Rack Server Platform

Equipped with dual Intel® Xeon® 6 processors , up to 32 DDR5 6400 RDIMMs, and delivering high scalability with up to 32 E3.S NVMe hot-swap drive bays (PCIe Gen5 x4). This optimized configuration provides the perfect balance of massive data retention and high-speed throughput, ensuring peak efficiency for AI data preprocessing, big data analytics, and enterprise-scale workloads.

MiTAC Computing sincerely welcomes you to visit us at Booth S110 to discover our comprehensive server-to-rack-to-cluster portfolio and ecosystem partnerships powering the future of AI workloads.

Research results from a joint study conducted by Nagoya Institute of Technology and JEOL have been published in the "Journal of Non-Crystalline Solids"

Release Date: 2026/10/07

Research results from a joint study conducted by Nagoya Institute of Technology and JEOL have been published in the "Journal of Non-Crystalline Solids". By combining experimental approaches, including ²⁵Mg solid-state NMR, with molecular dynamics simulations, the study revealed the structural role of Mg in alkali-modified silicate glasses.

Mg coordination-dependent ion release in Al-free SiO 2 −MgO–Na 2 O glass revealed by experiment and molecular dynamics simulations

DOI: 10.1016/j.jnoncrysol.2026.124340

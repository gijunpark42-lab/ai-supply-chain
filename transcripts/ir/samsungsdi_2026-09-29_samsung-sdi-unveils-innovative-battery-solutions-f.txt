SAMSUNG SDI showcases four dedicated zones featuring UPS, BBU, SBB and battery cell lineup

A next-generation cylindrical LFP battery based on proprietary structural technology makes its debut

The company to continue delivering differentiated solutions for AI data center customers

SAMSUNG SDI today announced that the company is showcasing battery solutions and product lineup optimized for AI data centers at Data Centre World Asia (DCWA) 2026, which opened in Singapore on Sept. 29.

[Aerial view of SAMSUNG SDI's exhibition booth at the DCWA 2026]

DCWA is Asia's largest data center exhibition, bringing together the entire data center ecosystem, from design and construction to operations and management, with more than 600 companies participating in this year's event.

At DCWA 2026, SAMSUNG SDI's exhibition booth features four dedicated zones covering Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS), Battery Backup Unit (BBU), Samsung Battery Box (SBB) and Cell Line-up, highlighting a range of battery products powered by the company's differentiated technologies.

At the UPS zone located at the center of the booth, SAMSUNG SDI is showcasing its UPS solution, which recently became the first in the world to satisfy all requirements of the Indoor Large Scale Fire Test (Indoor LSFT) conducted by UL Solutions, a global safety science certifier.

LSFT is a safety evaluation designed to verify the risks of battery explosions and thermal propagation. SAMSUNG SDI's UPS solution satisfied all test requirements, demonstrating industry-leading safety performance.

The BBU zone features SAMSUNG SDI's cylindrical battery lineup optimized for emergency backup power applications in AI data centers, including its high-power, high-capacity 21700 nickel-based battery equipped with a bottom-vent design for enhanced safety.

At the exhibition, SAMSUNG SDI is unveiling its cylindrical LFP battery for the first time. Based on the company's proprietary structural technology, the battery delivers enhanced power output and capacity while further improving safety.

[Employees of SAMSUNG SDI are presenting batteries for UPS and BBU at the DCWA 2026, opened on the 29th]

The SBB zone showcases prismatic lithium iron phosphate (LFP) batteries designed for Samsung Battery Box (SBB) 2.0, SAMSUNG SDI's integrated energy storage system (ESS) solution. By combining SAMSUNG SDI's proprietary prismatic form factor with LFP chemistry, the batteries offer enhanced performance and safety.

During the exhibition, SAMSUNG SDI will meet with global customers to share updates on technology development and conduct marketing activities. The company will also participate in a technical conference to present its battery solutions.

“Building on our battery technologies that deliver enhanced performance and safety, SAMSUNG SDI will continue to introduce differentiated solutions that address the diverse needs of data center customers,” said a SAMSUNG SDI official.

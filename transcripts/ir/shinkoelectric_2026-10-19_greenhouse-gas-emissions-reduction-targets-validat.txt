Greenhouse Gas Emissions Reduction Targets Validated by the SBTi

NAGANO, Japan, October 19, 2026 – SHINKO ELECTRIC INDUSTRIES CO., LTD.  hereby

announces that the greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions reduction targets of the Shinko Group,

with fiscal year 2030 as the target year, have been validated by the Science Based Targets

initiative (SBTi)*1 as science-based targets aligned with the level of ambition required by the

Paris Agreement.

GHG Emissions Reduction Targets Validated by the SBTi

25% reduction by FY2030

The Shinko Group is engaged in a variety of activities to fulfill the responsibility of a

manufacturing company to reduce environmental impact. Among these, the greatest priority is

given to responding to climate change, based on our understanding that it is essential for

realizing a sustainable society, and related activities are conducted throughout the Group.

Against the backdrop of international frameworks such as the Paris Agreement, strengthening

global regulations, and the expanded application of carbon taxes, the trend toward

decarbonization is accelerating further in countries around the world. Under thes e

circumstances, the Shinko Group has been addressing climate change by clarifying its goals

through its “Environmental Policy” and “Environmental Vision 2050” and by setting medium -

to long-term targets. The Shinko Group will further strengthen its initiatives to achieve carbon

neutrality as soon as possible and contribute to the realization of a decarbonized society.

*1 Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) is an international initiative established by CDP, the

United Nations Global Compact (UNGC), the World Resources Institute (WRI), and the

World Wide Fund for Nature (WWF) to evaluate corporate GHG emissions reduction targets.

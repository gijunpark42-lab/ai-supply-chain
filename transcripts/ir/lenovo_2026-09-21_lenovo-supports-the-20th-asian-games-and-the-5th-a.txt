Lenovo Supports the 20th Asian Games and the 5th Asian Para Games as the Official Supplier of PC Equipment and Related Services and Solutions

Through this sponsorship, Lenovo will contribute to building a robust digital environment that supports event organizers, media representatives, and fans, while helping advance digital transformation in the sports industry.

Tokyo, Lenovo Japan G.K., September 21, 2026 – Lenovo Japan (hereinafter referred to as “Lenovo”) today announced that it will support the 20th Asian Games and the 5th Asian Para Games as a Tier 4 Official Supplier, providing PC Equipment and Related Services and Solutions that will help ensure the smooth and efficient operation of both events.

Guided by its vision of “Smarter Technology for All,” Lenovo is committed to expanding human potential through innovative technology. Through this sponsorship, Lenovo will contribute to building a robust digital environment that supports event organizers, media representatives, and fans, while helping advance digital transformation (DX) in the sports industry.

Lenovo Japan is pleased to share the following comment from Ken Wong, EVP & President of Lenovo Solutions & Services Group , “Major sporting events have a unique power to bring people together, and technology plays an essential role in making those experiences possible. As an Official Supplier of the 20th Asian Games Aichi-Nagoya, Lenovo is proud to provide the devices, expertise and support that will help organizers, media and fans stay connected. By combining our global experience with strong local execution, we are helping create a reliable digital experience for the Games and demonstrating how smarter technology can unlock new possibilities for everyone.”

Also Lenovo Japan is pleased to share the following comment from Amar Babu, President Asia Pacific, Lenovo, “Delivering technology for an event of this scale requires much more than devices. It demands seamless deployment, proactive management and dependable support throughout the entire technology lifecycle. By bringing together our Device as a Service capabilities, project management expertise and on-site technical support, Lenovo will help maintain a stable and efficient IT environment, enabling organizers to stay focused on delivering an outstanding Games.”

Throughout the Games, Lenovo will provide more than 6,100 devices , including ThinkPad™ laptops and ThinkCentre™ desktop PCs . These devices will be deployed at the Main Operations Center and competition venues, serving as critical IT infrastructure to support event management, communications, and information sharing and administration.

In addition to supplying hardware, Lenovo will provide comprehensive project management services, including the design and implementation of PC deployment and maintenance services through Plug-and-Play (PnP) support and its Device as a Service (DaaS) solutions. These services will support the full device lifecycle, from deployment and management to maintenance, enabling event organizers to focus on delivering successful competitions while ensuring efficient and stable device operations.

Lenovo will be responsible for designing and operating the on-site support framework across competition venues. During the Games, more than 30 Lenovo team members will be deployed to provide PC technical support, respond to user inquiries, and troubleshoot device-related issues. Through timely and reliable assistance, Lenovo will help maintain a stable and secure IT environment throughout the games.

In addition, Lenovo will provide Lenovo ThinkPad L16 devices (touchscreen model) for use at the Main Media Center (MMC), where journalists from around the world gather to cover the Games. The devices will support news gathering, content creation, and reporting activities, helping create a productive and comfortable working environment for domestic and international media while facilitating the timely dissemination of information about the events.

Lenovo has a long history of supporting major sporting events around the world by delivering reliable IT infrastructure and innovative technologies. At the 20th Asian Games and the 5th Asian Para Games, Lenovo will leverage its global expertise and technical capabilities to provide a strong digital foundation that supports athletes, organizers, media representatives, and spectators alike.

As part of its commitment to “Smarter Technology for All,” Lenovo will continue to contribute to the development of sports through innovative technologies and services, while helping build a smarter future where everyone can unlock new possibilities through the power of technology.

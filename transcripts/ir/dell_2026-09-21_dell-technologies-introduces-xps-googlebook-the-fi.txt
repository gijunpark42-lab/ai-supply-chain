ROUND ROCK, Texas --(BUSINESS WIRE)--Sep. 21, 2026--

Dell Technologies (NYSE: DELL ) announced the XPS Googlebook , a 13-inch premium laptop, powered by the Snapdragon® X Elite processor, and the first XPS device designed for Gemini Intelligence. Built for Android users and supercharged to power their most advanced workflows, the XPS Googlebook brings the premium materials of the XPS portfolio to the Googlebook platform for the first time.

This press release features multimedia. View the full release here: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260921653290/en/

The new XPS Googlebook

Why it matters

The XPS Googlebook is the first Dell laptop built for Gemini Intelligence, marking an expansion of the XPS portfolio into a space where premium options have been limited. It brings together ecosystem integration with the design, materials and performance expected from XPS.

A design that earns its premium

The XPS Googlebook starts with a solid aluminum chassis, with the premium feel that defines XPS. At just 2.2lbs and 12.7mm thin, it is light enough to carry anywhere and solid from every angle, with fewer parting lines and a backlit chiclet keyboard paired with a glass touchpad that is quiet, familiar and precise.

The 2.5K InfinityEdge touch display features edge-to-edge glass, 100% DCI-P3 color coverage and 500 nits of brightness, remaining clear and readable even in direct sunlight. A variable refresh rate adjusts dynamically between 30Hz and 120Hz, preserving battery life when idle and delivering smooth motion when it matters.

Performance and battery life built for the way you work

Snapdragon X Elite processor handles tasks smoothly and efficiently, with up to 32GB of LPDDR5X memory that delivers fast data transfer speeds and an NPU for fast, on-device processing.

A dual-fan cooling system keeps the device cool and quiet during extended sessions. The XPS Googlebook delivers up to 16 hours of Netflix streaming on a single charge, i with the Snapdragon platform and variable refresh rate technology working together to extend runtime without compromising the experience.

Designed for Gemini Intelligence

Gemini Intelligence is woven into the XPS Googlebook. Magic Pointer reimagines the decades-old cursor and allows a simple wiggle cursor to activate Gemini. Gemini also works proactively in the background by connecting with apps to surface proactive suggestions and handling complex to-dos even when the device is closed.

XPS Googlebook and Android Phone Sync

The XPS Googlebook effortlessly syncs with your Android phone. Easily view, search and insert phone files, photos and downloads directly on the laptop without waiting for transfers or duplicating files. Notifications, messages and app context stay aligned across devices, and Android apps are accessible directly on the Googlebook without additional installs.

Meet the Glowbar

It's a statement on your laptop lid that is functional, beautiful and uniquely Google. The Glowbar acts as an easy, at-a-glance indicator of your battery life and charging status, so you know where your battery stands even when your laptop is closed.

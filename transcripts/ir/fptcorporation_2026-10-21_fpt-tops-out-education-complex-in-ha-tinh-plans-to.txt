FPT Tops Out Education Complex in Ha Tinh, Plans to Welcome Students in 2027–2028 Academic Year

10/21/2026

← Back to News

Ha Tinh, October 21, 2026 – FPT Corporation held a topping-out ceremony for the FPT Ha Tinh Education Complex on Nguyen Du Street, Thanh Sen Ward, Ha Tinh Province. The milestone marks significant progress on a project designed to accommodate approximately 4,400 students, supporting digital transformation and the development of a knowledge-based economy in key localities, in line with Resolution No. 57-NQ/TW and Resolution No. 71-NQ/TW.

Topping-out ceremony for the FPT Ha Tinh Education Complex.

The topping-out ceremony reaffirms FPT Corporation's commitment to developing an integrated education and technology ecosystem, expanding access to education and high-quality workforce training in Vietnam's North Central region. FPT aims to create an environment that attracts, nurtures, and develops leading technology talent, contributing to the country's broader development goals.

A key component of the complex is FPT Ha Tinh Primary, Lower Secondary and Upper Secondary School (FPT School Ha Tinh). This will be the first campus of the FPT Schools system in Ha Tinh Province and the eighth in Central Vietnam, marking another important step in expanding FPT's educational network across key provinces and cities.

The FPT Ha Tinh Education Complex is being developed on a 56,855.2-square-meter site, with a planned capacity of approximately 4,400 learners, including around 3,000 students in the integrated primary, lower secondary, and upper secondary school.

Designed as an open campus, the complex will feature academic buildings, communal spaces, sports facilities, and landscaped green areas.

The FPT primary, lower secondary and upper secondary school is a key component of the education complex developed by FPT Corporation.

The integrated school will comprise two five-story academic buildings, Beta and Gamma, with a total floor area of approximately 12,560 square meters.

The buildings are arranged around a central courtyard and connected to gardens, pedestrian walkways, and outdoor learning spaces, providing opportunities for educational activities, interaction, and experiential learning.

In addition to academic facilities, the complex will include a three-story multipurpose building for sports and arts activities, a dedicated Vovinam martial arts training facility, outdoor sports areas, and supporting infrastructure.

These facilities are designed to accommodate academic learning, physical education, skills development, and group activities.

A distinctive feature of the project's architectural design is the inspiration drawn from bamboo, a symbol of resilience, perseverance, and the determination of Ha Tinh's people to overcome challenges.

The bamboo motif is incorporated into the buildings' exterior shading systems, creating a recognizable architectural identity while providing sun protection and adapting to local climatic conditions.

The completion of the topping-out phase marks an important milestone in the project's construction schedule.

In the coming months, work will continue on building finishes, the Vovinam training facility, sports areas, and landscaped green spaces, with the goal of bringing the educational facilities into operation for the 2027–2028 academic year.

Architectural rendering of the FPT integrated school project in Ha Tinh.

The FPT Ha Tinh Education Complex forms part of FPT Corporation's strategy to expand the company's educational network, meet increasingly diverse learning needs, and prepare a skilled workforce amid rapid advances in science, technology, and digital transformation.

Speaking at the topping-out ceremony, Dr. Le Truong Tung, Chairman of the University Council of FPT University, FPT Corporation, said:

“Just over five months after the groundbreaking ceremony, the FPT Ha Tinh Education Complex has officially taken shape. This presence brings with it a significant responsibility for FPT to provide a high-quality learning environment and develop human resources that meet the requirements of digital transformation and local socioeconomic development.

“For general education, FPT Schools is committed to delivering exceptional added value in technology, foreign languages, and life skills, giving parents complete confidence in entrusting their children's education to us.”

Dr. Le Truong Tung, Chairman of the University Council of FPT University, FPT Corporation, speaks at the ceremony.

Alongside the 2018 General Education Curriculum prescribed by the Ministry of Education and Training, FPT School Ha Tinh plans to implement educational programs aligned with FPT Schools' distinctive strengths.

These programs will emphasize English proficiency and global competence, technology, and personal development through academic activities and practical learning experiences.

Students will have opportunities to explore technology-related fields such as STEM, artificial intelligence (AI), and robotics while developing creative thinking, independence, problem-solving skills, and a sense of responsibility toward the community.

The establishment of FPT Schools in Ha Tinh will not only provide additional educational choices for local students and parents but also expand access to modern teaching approaches that integrate knowledge, technology, and practical skills from the general education level.

With construction progressing, FPT School Ha Tinh is expected to be ready for teaching and learning activities from the 2027–2028 academic year, contributing to the expansion of educational facilities and workforce development in Vietnam's North Central region.

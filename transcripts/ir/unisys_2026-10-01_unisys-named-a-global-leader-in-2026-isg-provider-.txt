Unisys Named a Global Leader in 2026 ISG Provider Lens® Future of Work Services Report

October 1, 2026 at 9:00 AM EDT

Unisys earns 17 Leader designations, including recognition in ISG's new AI-Augmented Collaboration and Experience Services quadrant

BLUE BELL, Pa. , Oct. 1, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Unisys (NYSE: UIS) has been named a Leader in the 2026 ISG Provider Lens® Future of Work Services Report by Information Service Group (ISG), earning 17 Leader designations across the U.S ., U.K . and Germany. New to this year's report is the AI-Augmented Collaboration and Experience Services quadrant, where Unisys was designated a Leader in the U.K . and Germany . The recognition reflects the company's leadership in secure, experience-led digital workplace services and continued investment in AI-enabled workplace solutions and responsible AI adoption.

ISG defines Leaders as organizations with a comprehensive portfolio of products and services, a strong market presence and well-established competitive strategies that position them to win business. This is the seventh year Unisys has been recognized as a Leader in the annual report, demonstrating sustained strength across workplace strategy, operations and emerging AI-enabled services.

"Workplace services are being reshaped by AI, evolving stakeholder expectations and increasing demands for security and efficiency," said Patrycja Sobera , senior vice president and general manager, Digital Workplace Solutions, Unisys. "Organizations need partners that can turn those changes into measurable business outcomes. This recognition reflects our focus on delivering workplace experiences that improve productivity, strengthen security and create a practical path for adopting AI at scale."

Unisys received Leader designations in four quadrants in several regions:

AI-Augmented Collaboration and Experience Services (new quadrant): Recognized in the U.K . and Germany for its structured approach to AI governance and adoption, as well as organizational change management.

Workplace Strategy and Enablement Services: Recognized in the U.S ., U.K . and Germany for aligning workplace services with business outcomes and employee experience. ISG highlighted the Unisys Experience Management Office (XMO) and experience level agreement (XLA) model, which enables organizations to measure workplace performance beyond traditional service metrics.

Digital Workplace Operations and Support Services: In the U.S ., U.K . and Germany , ISG cited the Unisys Service Experience Accelerator (SEA), which combines operational, service and experience data to improve outcomes.

Smart and Sustainable Workplace Leadership: Unisys was acknowledged in the U.S . for workplace services that support sustainability and operational efficiency. Unisys stood out for its device lifecycle management capabilities, workplace energy monitoring and ESG reporting.

"In the U.K ., Unisys delivers a comprehensive, experience-led workplace services portfolio that spans the full transformation lifecycle, from strategy and enablement to ongoing operations and support," said Kevin Turner , principal consultant, Future of Work team at ISG. "By connecting the human, digital and physical workplace with XLA 3.0 governance, responsible and intentional AI, along with its execution-ready roadmaps, Unisys helps organizations translate employee experience into operational insight, sustained AI adoption and measurable business value."

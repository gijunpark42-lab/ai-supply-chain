Sumitomo Metal Mining Successfully Develops the World's First 12-inch Bonded SiC Substrate "SiCkrest"

September 18, 2026

Sumitomo Metal Mining Co., Ltd.

Tokyo, Japan - Sumitomo Metal Mining Co., Ltd. (TSE: 5713) has successfully developed the world's first(*) 12-inch (300mm) bonded silicon carbide (SiC) substrate, "SiCkrest®," and has begun sample shipment to a semiconductor manufacturer.

As an important technological achievement that meets the growing demand for larger-diameter SiC substrates in the power semiconductor market, Sumitomo Metal Mining will exhibit the substrate at Booth G-2 at the International Conference on Silicon Carbide and Related Materials 2026 (ICSCRM 2026), to be held at Pacifico Yokohama North from Sunday, September 27 to Friday, October 2.

(*) According to Sumitomo Metal Mining's research. World's first 12-inch SiC substrate achieved through bonding.

Micron Invests in New York Critical Minerals Processing With Indium Corporation

October 20, 2026

Latest investment under Micron's up to $3 billion U.S. supply-chain plan strengthens the domestic semiconductor ecosystem and expands access to critical materials for advanced memory manufacturing BOISE, Idaho, Oct. 20, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Micron Technology, Inc. (Nasdaq: MU) today announced an investment in critical minerals processing and high-purity materials manufacturing that is expected to support the expansion of Indium Corporation’s capabilities in Central New York, restarting domestic processing of critical minerals essential to advanced semiconductor manufacturing. The investment is part of Micron’s plan, announced in July, to invest up to $3 billion to strengthen the U.S. semiconductor supply-chain ecosystem and secure a reliable domestic supply of critical manufacturing materials.

Under the agreement, Micron will provide strategic funding that enables Indium Corporation to expand its refining and manufacturing operations in Rome, New York. The companies will enter into a long-term supply agreement that gives Micron access to these high-purity minerals. The investment is expected to reduce reliance on concentrated global refining capacity, addressing a growing national security priority.

“Advanced memory manufacturing depends on a reliable supply of critical minerals. Enabling that requires engagements well beyond our direct suppliers,” said Ben Tessone, senior vice president and chief procurement officer at Micron Technology. “Securing these inputs is essential to Micron's long-term growth and technology roadmap. Partnering with Indium Corporation to restart this capability in its New York facility expands U.S. capacity for critical minerals production and builds a more resilient supply chain for American semiconductor manufacturing and national security.”

“Micron's decision to partner with Indium Corporation reflects the trust they've placed in our people and our ability to deliver," said Ross Berntson, president and CEO of Indium Corporation. "For more than 90 years, we've called Central New York home, developing materials that support some of the world's most advanced technologies. We're grateful for Micron's confidence in our team and the opportunity to build on that history. Together, we're expanding the supply of semiconductor-grade critical minerals and taking an important step forward for domestic manufacturing."

The agreement with Indium Corporation complements Micron’s plans to accelerate its U.S. fab and technology investments and increase its expected spend to more than $250 billion through 2035. A stronger domestic materials base, including expanded critical minerals capabilities in the same state as its New York fab complex, helps Micron meet growing demand for memory driven by Super Intelligence.

The announcement coincides with Micron's annual Supplier Awards event celebrating excellent performance of the company's top suppliers.

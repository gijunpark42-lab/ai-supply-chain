Realtek Showcases Next-Generation AI Home Entertainment, Smart Streaming, and High-Speed Connectivity Solutions at IBC2026

Advancing AI Home Entertainment and Smart Connectivity Platforms to Enhance Audiovisual, Interactive Sensing Experiences

Hsinchu, Taiwan – September 10, 2026 – At IBC2026 (09/11~09/14), Realtek Semiconductor will showcase innovative smart living applications powered by its next-generation AI home entertainment and AV2 smart streaming platform solutions. These technologies combine cost-effective 100GbE and ultra-low-power 10GbE connectivity with advanced applications including HDT audio, spatial interaction, smart health remote control, intelligent Wi-Fi connectivity, and smart human sensing.

Next-Generation AI Home Entertainment and AV2 Smart Streaming Platforms

Realtek will introduce next-generation home entertainment and intelligent streaming platform solutions featuring its AI Home Media SoC and AV2 Streaming Platform SoC product families, with support for Android, RDK, and Linux platforms. The AI Home Media SoC integrates a transformer-based NPU AI engine for offline Edge Language Model (Edge LM) applications, serving as an intelligent smart home interface and hands-free voice interaction hub. Together with third-generation AIPQ and AISR technologies and a high-performance GPU, the platform enhances 4K picture quality and delivers a smooth user experience. For next-generation streaming services, the AV2 Streaming Platform SoC supports the latest AV2 video decoding technology, helping operators and content providers improve bandwidth efficiency while delivering high-quality video. The platform also strengthens cybersecurity protection against network threats and malicious attacks. In addition, both platforms support multi-stream technology, enabling seamless media switching and simultaneous viewing of multiple content sources. Through advanced platform integration and multimedia technologies, Realtek helps customers accelerate smart home adoption and next-generation streaming service deployment.

Cost-Optimized 100GbE & Exclusive Preview of Ultra-Low Power 10GbE

Realtek’s latest high-speed networking advances are designed to accelerate the evolution of Edge AI and enterprise infrastructure. Now in mass production, Realtek’s 100GbE (25GbE x4) solutions are optimized for Edge AI and internal enterprise servers. Leveraging advanced CMOS technology for high production capacity, these solutions integrate TIA + LA + CDR (RX) and CDR + VCSEL Driver + MCU (TX). The built-in MCU helps reduce overall system costs while ensuring the robust signal integrity critical for local infrastructure. As a special highlight at IBC2026, Realtek will offer an exclusive preview of its next-generation 10GbE solution, slated for release in Q2 2027. Engineered to revolutionize power efficiency and reduce cooling costs in enterprise environments, the upcoming technology targets ultra-low power consumption of under 1W without compromising robust, high-speed performance.

HDT Audio, Spatial Interaction, and Smart Healthcare Remotes

Realtek’s next-generation wireless connectivity and intelligent control portfolio delivers advanced HDT audio, spatial interaction, and health-integrated control for entertainment and smart living. The HDT Audio SoC offers ultra-responsive, low-latency LE Audio with LC3 for high-fidelity immersive gaming. The directional RCU combines Bluetooth, UWB, and IMU for precise, motion-aware spatial control on smart TVs and interactive multimedia systems. The universal RCU adds rich 2.5D graphics, a smooth 30 fps display, and seamless multi-device management to enhance premium smart home and IoT experiences. Completing the portfolio, the Smart Healthcare RCU transforms the TV remote into a health-aware hub by integrating heart rate, blood oxygen, and blood pressure monitoring, along with intelligent features that support a connected digital wellness ecosystem.

Wi-Fi Smart Connection

Realtek Wi-Fi chips fully integrate the latest Wi-Fi Aware standard, bringing a major breakthrough to near-field communication. Without requiring the Internet or an access point, Wi-Fi Aware enables nearby devices to discover one another, pair securely, and establish high-speed direct connections. The latest version further strengthens security with standardized Wi-Fi Aware pairing mechanisms, data path encryption, and beacon protection to effectively prevent man-in-the-middle attacks. This capability enhances zero-configuration smart home onboarding, offline file sharing, and cross-platform interaction, ushering in a brand-new user experience for next-generation wireless connectivity.

Intelligent Human Sensing

Realtek launches the newest IoT solution: a highly integrated SoC combining Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth LE, Thread, and advanced sensing technology. Key features include seamless performance, simplified RF design and BOM, support for Wake-on-Approach contactless control for automatically switching lighting on and off, and privacy-first monitoring. Using Realtek’s in-house radar technology and algorithm, the solution detects human presence and motion without external sensing components. It reduces design complexity and additional costs compared to conventional IoT solutions that require external sensors, while providing an adaptive AI model to enable motion and spatial sensing capabilities.

“AI, video streaming, and high-speed connectivity are transforming home entertainment and smart living," said Yee-Wei Huang, Realtek's vice president and spokesman. “Realtek aims to offer complete technology solutions that help people enjoy sharper video, faster and more reliable connections, and more natural, convenient interactions—supporting customers as they build the next generation of smart home and digital lifestyle services.”

September 2026 | Monthly Revenue Overview

1. The company reported unaudited consolidated revenue of NT$1,158.6 billion in September 2026, representing an increase of 25.70% MoM and an increase of 38.42% YoY (note: in USD, revenue was up 27.2% MoM and 37.4% YoY), reaching highest single-month revenue in the Company's history and the first time monthly revenue surpassing NT$1 trillion. (The previous record for the same month was NT$837.1 billion in September 2025, while the prior all-time monthly high was NT$946.5 billion in July 2026.)

2. Revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was NT$3,026.9 billion, up 20.44% QoQ and 47.12% YoY (note: in USD, revenue was up 19.5% QoQ and 39.7% YoY), reaching the highest for the same period in history. (Third quarter of 2025 ranked the second highest with NT$2,057.4 billion)

3. Accumulated revenue for the first ninth months of 2026 reached NT$7.67 trillion, up 39.53% YoY (note: in USD, revenue was up 38.2% YoY), marking the highest ever for the period. (The second highest was NT$5.50 trillion in the first ninth months of 2025.)

September 2026 | Revenue Performance by Segments

1. In September 2026, revenue was NT$1,158.6 billion, up 25.70% MoM. Smart Consumer Electronics showed strong growth, Components and Other Products, Cloud and Networking Products, and Computing Products demonstrated significant growth. Revenue performance detailed by each product segment as below:

(1) Smart Consumer Electronics: Revenue demonstrated strong growth MoM, benefiting from new product launch in the month.
(2) Components and Other Products: Benefiting from an increase in shipments of core components related to the main business and other income, this category delivered significant growth MoM.
(3) Cloud and Networking Products: Benefiting from strong pull-in momentum for AI products, this category achieved significant growth MoM.
(4) Computing Products: Benefiting from pull-in momentum, this category showed significant growth MoM.

2. September revenue was up 38.42% YoY. Cloud and Networking Products, Components and Other Products and Computing Products showed strong growth, while Smart Consumer Electronics were flattish. Revenue performance detailed by each product segment as below:

(1) Cloud and Networking Products: Benefiting from pull-in momentum for AI products, this category showed strong growth YoY.
(2) Components and Other Products: Benefiting from an increase in components related to the main business and other income, this category showed strong growth YoY.
(3) Computing Products: Benefiting from pull-in momentum, this category delivered strong growth YoY.
(4) Smart Consumer Electronics: With higher shipment prices, this category was flattish YoY.

2026 Q3 | Quarterly Revenue Performance by Segments

Revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was NT$3,026.9 billion, up 20.44% QoQ and 47.12% YoY. Compared to the same period last year, Cloud and Networking Products, Components and Other Products, and Computing Products demonstrated strong growth, while Smart Consumer Electronics show significant growth. Revenue performance detailed by each Product segment as below:

(1) Cloud and Networking Products: Benefiting from pull-in momentum for AI products, this category showed strong growth YoY.
(2) Components and Other Products: Benefiting from an increase in components related to the main business and other income, this category achieved strong growth YoY.
(3) Computing Products: Benefiting from higher shipment prices and pull-in momentum, this category showed strong growth YoY.
Smart Consumer Electronics: Benefiting from pull-in momentum and higher shipment prices, this category showed significant growth YoY.

September 2026 | Cumulative Revenue Performance by Segments

Accumulated revenue for the first ninth months of 2026 reached NT$7.67 trillion, up 39.53% YoY. Cloud and Networking Products and Components and Other Products demonstrated strong growth YoY, Smart Consumer Electronics and Computing Products showed significant growth. Revenue performance detailed by each product segment as below:

(1) Cloud and Networking Products: Benefiting from pull-in momentum for AI products, this category showed strong growth YoY.
(2) Components and Other Products: Benefiting from an increase in components related to the main business and other income, this category achieved strong growth YoY.
(3) Smart Consumer Electronics: Benefiting from pull-in momentum and higher shipment prices, this category achieved significant growth YoY.
(4) Computing Products: Benefiting from higher shipment prices and pull-in momentum, this category showed significant growth YoY.

2026 Q4 | Business Outlook

AI-related operations are expected to continue growing in the fourth quarter. Together with the traditional peak season for ICT products in 2H26, this should support overall performance in line with current market expectations, although it remains necessary to monitor the impact of a volatile global political and economic environment.

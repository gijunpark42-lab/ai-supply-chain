TEL Manufacturing and Engineering of America achieves Platinum Status in RBA VAP

TEL Manufacturing and Engineering of America (Chaska, Minnesota, U.S.A.) has earned Platinum Status, the highest recognition available under the Responsible Business Alliance (RBA)* Validated Assessment Program (VAP), with a perfect score of 200.

Conducted by RBA-approved third-party auditors, the VAP assesses compliance with the RBA Code of Conduct across Labor, Health and Safety, Environment, Ethics, and Management Systems.

TEL Manufacturing and Engineering of America is the first overseas manufacturing site within the Tokyo Electron Group to undergo an RBA VAP assessment, achieving a perfect score across all evaluated categories.

Tokyo Electron Group remains committed to advancing sustainability-focused management practices in alignment with its Corporate Philosophy: “We strive to contribute to the development of a dream-inspiring society through our leading-edge technologies and reliable service and support.”

*RBA (Responsible Business Alliance): The RBA is the world’s largest coalition of electronics and other industries dedicated to responsible and sustainable business practices in global supply chains. Tokyo Electron became a member of the RBA in 2015, and since then we have been working with our suppliers to fully comply with the RBA Code of Conduct.

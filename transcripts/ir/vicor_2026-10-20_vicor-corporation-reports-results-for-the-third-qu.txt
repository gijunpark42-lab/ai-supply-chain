Vicor Corporation Reports Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2026

Oct 20, 2026 at 7:00 AM EDT

PDF Version

ANDOVER, Mass. , Oct. 20, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Vicor Corporation (NASDAQ: VICR) today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 . These results will be discussed at 8:00 a.m. Eastern Time , during management’s quarterly investor conference call. The details for the call are below.

Product and royalty revenues for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 totaled $197.1 million , a 37.5% sequential increase from $143.4 million in the second quarter of 2026, and increased 78.5% from $110.4 million for the corresponding period a year ago.

Gross margin increased sequentially to $134.7 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $83.1 million for the second quarter of 2026, and increased from $63.5 million for the corresponding period a year ago. Gross margin, as a percentage of revenue, increased to 68.3% for the third quarter of 2026, compared to 58.0% for the second quarter of 2026, and increased from 57.5% for the corresponding period a year ago. Operating expenses, including contingency fees to law firms enforcing Vicor IP, increased sequentially to $62.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $48.2 million for the second quarter of 2026, and increased from $42.6 million for the corresponding period a year ago.

Net income for the third quarter was $63.3 million , or $1.33 per diluted share, compared to net income of $49.8 million , or $1.04 per diluted share, for the second quarter of 2026 and net income of $28.3 million or $0.63 per diluted share, for the corresponding period a year ago.

Cash flow from operations totaled $69.7 million for the third quarter, compared to cash flow from operations of $34.0 million in the second quarter of 2026, and cash flow from operations of $38.5 million for the corresponding period a year ago. Capital expenditures for the third quarter totaled $16.1 million , compared to $11.2 million for the second quarter of 2026 and $4.0 million for the corresponding period a year ago. Cash and cash equivalents as of September 30, 2026 increased 13.1% sequentially to approximately $513.1 million compared to approximately $453.6 million as of June 30, 2026 .

Backlog for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 totaled $406 million , a 7% sequential increase from $380 million at the end of the second quarter of 2026, and increased 166% from $153 million for the corresponding period a year ago.

Commenting on recent developments, CEO Dr. Patrizio Vinciarelli stated: “With the purchase of two additional sites within 30 miles of ChiP Fab-1 in Andover , we will be able to provide a nearly 5X increase in capacity from 3 Fabs, relative to Fab-1. Capacity expansion is necessary in view of demand and industry trends.

Open Compute confirmed the challenges of VRs and IVRs. In addition to infringing patents to 1 st GEN VPD, VRs and IVRs face electrical, mechanical and thermal barriers to escalating AI processor requirements. In private presentations and lab sessions with OEMs and Hyperscalers, we demonstrated the distinct advantages of Vicor’s 2 nd GEN VPD. Participants paid attention, providing positive feedback and interest in engaging. The stage is set for broader adoption of high performance ALL-FPA current multiplier modules which enable 2 nd GEN VPD.

On the licensing front, we granted our first VPD license to an AI OEM at Stage-2 unit royalty rates. Unlicensed OEMs and Hyperscalers may not have realized the implications of importing, or planning to import, computing systems infringing Vicor patents to VPD. The ITC decision to institute an investigation into VPD infringement is catching their attention, prompting inquiries about licenses at royalty rates that strongly favor early adopters."

For more information on Vicor and its products, please visit the Company’s website at www.vicorpower.com .

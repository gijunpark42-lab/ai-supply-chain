Samsung Enhances Long-Term Value of Refrigerators and Laundry Appliances With AI-Focused Software Updates

Korea on September 7, 2026

Tizen 10.0 software updates brings latest Bixby, expanded AI features, and refreshed interfaces for select refrigerators and laundry appliances

Samsung Electronics today announced major software updates 1 for select refrigerators and laundry appliances. Beginning in September, the update to Tizen OS 10.0 will bring key services and features to select existing appliances in line with those offered on models launched this year.

“Home appliances are products that consumers rely on for many years. We believe supporting them over time requires not only reliable hardware, but also software that continues to evolve,” said Jeong Seung Moon, Executive Vice President and Head of the R&D Team of the Digital Appliances (DA) Business at Samsung Electronics. “Through this latest regular software update, we aim to help our appliances remain useful and up to date throughout the ownership journey, enhancing the long-term value consumers receive from products they already own.”

Bespoke AI Refrigerator Family Hub’s Newest AI Features Expand Globally

Building on the software update first introduced to select Bespoke AI Refrigerator Family Hubs in the U.S. in May , Samsung is expanding its newest AI-powered refrigerator experiences to additional global markets.

Refrigerators with built-in cameras and AI Vision, including 21.5-inch and 32-inch Family Hub released between 2021 and 2025 — as well as select 9-inch Family Hub models released in 2025 — will receive the updated AI Vision built with Google Gemini. 2 The update expands the refrigerator’s ability to recognize and manage food items, helping users keep track of what is stored inside and make everyday food management easier.

With this update, the refrigerator can recognize a much wider range of fresh and packaged foods, including branded and region-specific items.

In addition, screen-based experiences like Now Brief 3 and What’s for Today? provide users with personalized information and help simplify meal decisions on a daily basis.

Seamless Interaction With Bespoke AI Laundry Appliances

Samsung is also extending its latest software experiences to select laundry appliances released in 2024 and 2025, including Bespoke AI Laundry Combo, Washer, and Dryer models.

A key part of the update is Bixby, 4 which enhances voice recognition and enables more natural interactions with laundry appliances. This allows users to communicate with their appliances more intuitively while carrying out everyday laundry tasks. For example, users can simply say, “When will my laundry be finished?” and Bixby will understand and reply with a phrase such as, “Your laundry is set to finish in two hours and 25 minutes.” 5

The update will introduce a refreshed interface aligned with Samsung’s latest laundry experience, offering more intuitive controls and information delivery on applicable models equipped with 7-inch Smart Screens. This will be achieved by adding Easy Mode, 6 which streamlines the screen layout and simplifies key information in an easy-to-access format.

Availability

The updates will begin rolling out in phases from September 2026. Actual timing, supported features, and availability may vary by product, model year, and market. 7

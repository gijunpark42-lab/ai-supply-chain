Resonac Group Releases Integrated Report “RESONAC REPORT 2026”

~ Phase 2 of Resonac’s Second Founding: Taking on New Growth Challenges ~

Sustainability

September 14, 2026

Resonac Holdings Corporation

Resonac Holdings Corporation (President and CEO: Hidehito Takahashi, hereinafter referred to as “Resonac”) is pleased to announce the release of its integrated report, “RESONAC REPORT 2026,” which is now available on Resonac’s website.

This year’s report marks the fourth year since the business integration that Resonac regards as its “Second Founding” and is centered on the theme, “Phase 2 of Resonac’s Second Founding.” Guided by the concept that “Corporate Value = Strategy × Individual Strengths × Corporate Culture,” Resonac has advanced itsbusiness portfolio transformation while developing a culture of co-creation. The report looks back on the achievements of Phase 1 and introduces our management agenda, strategies, and co-creation initiatives aimed at realizing our Purpose, “Change society through the power of chemistry,” and driving the next phase of growth.

The report also revisits the “Resonac Value Creation Hypothesis,” which was first presented last year. While refining the hypothesis through ongoing dialogue with a wide range of stakeholders, we present examples of how it is being put into practice. The report outlines our approach to maximizing “true corporate value” and introduces perspectives gained through discussions with investors, experts, and other stakeholders. We invite readers to explore these insights and our evolving approach to value creation.

We position the integrated report as a communication tool for dialogue, conveying how we have created value from the past to the present and whether we possess the structure and capabilities to create value into the future. We publish this report annually to share our thinking and hypotheses regarding value creation. Going forward, we will continue to use the report as a starting point for co-creation, fostering dialogue with stakeholders and working together to shape the future.

Cover: Guided by our Purpose, “Change society through the power of chemistry,” the cover illustrates how Resonac’s diverse technologies contribute to society. The employees featured on the cover are the Gold Award winners of AHA! 2025, Resonac’s global award program designed to promote understanding and embodiment of the company’s Purpose and Values.

■Contents:

・Introduction

Provides an overview of the Group’s business, Purpose and Values, as well as the thinking behind the integrated report, our long-term vision, and Resonac’s direction as it enters Phase 2 of its Second Founding.

・1 Next Phase！

Introduces the growth strategy and future vision for Phase 2 of the Second Founding through the CEO Message, long-term vision, management introductions, and financial and capital strategies.

・2 Our Value to Society

Presents Resonac’s Value Creation Hypothesis and explains how technology, people, and corporate culture create value. The chapter also highlights how our products and services generate social and environmental value.

・3 What to Do Together With Whom？

Covers business, human capital, R&D, and sustainability strategies, highlighting value creation and growth through co-creation with diverse internal and external partners.

・4 Resonac's Governance Reform

Introduces governance reform, risk management, and stakeholder engagement initiatives that support sustainable enhancement of corporate value.

・Data Section

Contains financial and non-financial highlights, KPIs related to materiality and management agenda, environmental data, financial statements, and other key information.

Note:The CEO Message section in Chapter 1 includes a video message from the CEO regarding Resonac’s long-term vision and future aspirations. In addition, readers can access post-discussion video messages from dialogue participants via the respective interview and discussion pages.

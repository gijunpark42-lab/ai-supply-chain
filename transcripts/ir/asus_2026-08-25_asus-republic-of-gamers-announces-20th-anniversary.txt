ASUS Republic of Gamers Announces 20th Anniversary Charity Auction

August 25, 2026

・

Press Release

One-of-a-kind ROG 20th Anniversary suitcase signed by Jonney Shih and Jensen Huang will be auctioned on eBay for Charity, with all proceeds going to SpecialEffect

SpecialEffect will host the eBay for Charity auction from August 25 to September 4, 2026.

A signed ROG 20th Anniversary suitcase, VVIP Kit and limited-edition ROG 20th Anniversary gear make up the one-of-a-kind bundle

100% of the auction proceeds will benefit SpecialEffect, with ROG providing the items and campaign support

COLOGNE, Germany, August 25, 2026 — ASUS Republic of Gamers (ROG) today announced at its Gamescom press conference in Cologne that it is sponsoring a global charity auction to benefit SpecialEffect, the UK-based charity that helps people with physical disabilities enjoy video games. SpecialEffect will host and operate the auction through eBay for Charity, and 100% of the proceeds will go to the charity. ROG will provide the auction items and campaign support.

The centerpiece of the auction is a single ROG Slash Hard-Case Luggage Edition 20 signed by Jonney Shih, ASUS Chairman, and Jensen Huang, Founder and CEO, NVIDIA, paired with a signed ROG 20th VVIP Kit that has never been offered for sale. The winning bidder will also receive a full set of limited-edition ROG 20th Anniversary gear created to mark the occasion.

"Gaming has the power to connect people, create joy, and open new possibilities," said Jonney Shih. "As ROG celebrates its 20th anniversary, we are proud to support SpecialEffect in making gaming more accessible — because For Those Who Dare should include everyone who wants to play. Through this charity auction, we hope to help remove barriers and bring the joy of gaming to even more people."

Paige Harvey, Gaming Community Manager at SpecialEffect, added. "SpecialEffect doesn’t charge for any of the support we provide, and we can only continue to provide that support entirely free thanks to the incredible donations and charity campaigns that support our work. The demand for our services is ever increasing, and we’re so thankful that we’re able to accommodate these requests thanks to our fantastic community."

A one-of-a-kind 20th anniversary collection

The winner will receive the following items:

ROG SLASH Hard-Case Luggage Edition 20 — signed by Jonney Shih and Jensen Huang

ROG 20th VVIP Kit — signed and never offered for sale

ROG SLASH Backpack Edition 20

ROG Azoth Extreme Edition 20 Gaming Keyboard

ROG Harpe II Extreme Edition 20 Gaming Mouse

ROG Keycap Mystery Box Edition 20

ROG SAGA OMNI Edition 20 Action Figure

Full details of every item in the bundle will be listed on the official eBay auction page once the listing is live.

Helping more people enjoy gaming

Founded in 2007, SpecialEffect is a gaming charity that works one-to-one with people whose physical disabilities make conventional gaming hardware difficult or impossible to use. Its specialist team assesses each person’s needs and builds a setup around them, drawing on technologies such as modified controllers and eye-gaze systems. SpecialEffect also provides equipment loans and ongoing support as needs change, at no cost to the people it supports.

Gates Grows Its Warehouse Automation Portfolio with IntraLogix™ LRB-100

October 13, 2026

New Live Roller Belt Replaces Conventional Fabric Construction With A More Durable Polyurethane Design to Help Reduce Maintenance and Downtime

DENVER , Oct. 13, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Gates Industrial Corporation Ltd. (NYSE: GTES), a global manufacturer of innovative, highly engineered power transmission and fluid power solutions, today announced the launch of Gates IntraLogix™ LRB-100, a new live roller belt for conveyor systems used in warehouse and distribution center operations. Live roller belts are used in highly automated conveyor solutions and drive the rollers that carry packages, cartons and other goods. Gates LRB-100 replaces conventional fabric belt construction with a homogeneous polyurethane design that helps eliminate V-guide delamination, supporting longer belt life, reduced maintenance and greater uptime for operators.

Warehouse automation is a growing market for engineered belt solutions as e-commerce volumes rise, and operators invest in faster, more reliable fulfillment. Conveyors run at high speed and near-continuous duty in these applications, so a belt issue can slow down an entire operation. LRB-100 reflects a deliberate Gates strategy of applying its materials science and engineering expertise to high-growth, automation-driven end markets.

In testing against conventional belt designs, LRB-100 delivered:

More than five times longer belt life : Reduces replacements, downtime and total ownership costs.

Approximately 75% less elongation : Helps the belt maintain proper tension longer, reducing retensioning downtime and supporting more consistent around-the-clock operations.

Roughly 80% greater abrasion resistance : Extends belt life in demanding warehouse environments.

Approximately 24% greater weld strength than other belts used on accumulating roller pneumatic (ARP) conveyance systems.

On average, 73% lower drive tensions : Reduces system stress while supporting longer equipment life.

"Increased levels of automation are raising the bar for every component on the conveyor line, and that creates meaningful opportunity for Gates to differentiate," said Mike Haen, vice president of global product line management and marketing at Gates. "With LRB-100, we tackled delamination, a common cause of conveyor downtime, with a belt built from a single material. It's a prime example of how we're leveraging materials science know-how with application expertise to create innovation that matters in fast-growing markets."

LRB-100 joins LRB-45 and LRB-77 in the growing Gates IntraLogix portfolio. It is now available in the United States, with international availability to follow. To learn more, visit the IntraLogix LRB-100 Innovations Page and browse the IntraLogix Conveyance Belt Solutions Page .

Huawei Wins Two Network X 2026 Awards for Next Generation FTTR and Next Generation FTTO Innovations

Summary:

Award achievement: Huawei won the "Leading In-Home Wi-Fi Service" and "Most Innovative Enterprise or Smart City PON Use Case" awards at Network X 2026 with its Next Generation FTTR solution and Next Generation FTTO Intelligent All-in-One Security Device, delivering a deterministic whole-home 2000M/3000M experience in the AI era and empowering SME digitalization by addressing chain enterprises' networking, security, and O&M pain points.

[Vienna, Austria, October 15, 2026] At the Network X 2026 Awards, Huawei won the “Leading In-Home Wi-Fi Service” and “Most Innovative Enterprise or Smart City PON Use Case” awards, highlighting its innovation and commercial achievements in optical access in the AI era.

As AI enters homes and enterprises, demands for speed, coverage, and latency rise. Homes face bottlenecks in speed, coverage, and latency, while higher latency pushes up AI interaction costs. SMEs struggle with high storage costs and slow O&M. The industry needs a deterministic, highly reliable all-optical foundation—this is exactly where Huawei's award-winning solutions deliver value.

Huawei won the “Leading In-Home Wi-Fi Service” and “Most Innovative Enterprise or Smart City PON Use Case” awards

Leading In-Home Wi-Fi Service: Next Generation FTTR Redefines Home Network for the AI Era

Huawei Next Generation FTTR integrates Wi-Fi 7 tri-band, AI anti-interference, and intelligent Wi-Fi air-interface scheduling technologies to deliver a deterministic whole-home 2000M/3000M experience.

Broader coverage : Built-in 8 dBi smart antennas ensure stable signals better than -60 dBm, greatly improving Wi-Fi coverage.

More stable speeds : With 100,000+ interference models, it boosts speeds by 20% in complex environments and achieves 100% of contracted bandwidth.

Lower latency : The solution identifies 50+ mainstream applications and proactively accelerates them, guarantees 20 ms latency for VIP applications, and enables tri-band FTTR to achieve 10ms latency through a dedicated 6 GHz band.

The AI Service Home Box, as the star product of Next Generation FTTR, is the industry’s first to integrate FTTR, STB, 3D viewing, and cloud applications, building a home AI service entry. It supports 4K UHD, 3D viewing, AI voice interaction, cross-screen interaction, and AI fitness/health applications, plus network self-inspection and self-repair. Its five-level energy-saving technology reduces power consumption by 60%, and overall power consumption is 20% lower than the CoC v9 standard.

Most Innovative Enterprise PON Use Case: Next Generation FTTO All-in-One Security Device Empowers SMEs

Huawei Next Generation FTTO Intelligent All-in-One Security Device uses an innovative all-in-one architecture to address networking, security, and O&M pain points for chain enterprises. It is the industry’s first to integrate all-optical Wi-Fi 7 with video security, storage, and computing. It connects POS terminals, printers, and vending machines, supporting up to 16 video channels, and could support the employee attendance system. An IoT coffer prevents video hijacking and data leaks.

Its first-of-its-kind Super-Coding technology, with 1.2 TOPS computing power, uses AI pixel-level segmentation to separate foreground from background, enabling lossless foreground and deep background compression. With a 4 TB hard drive, it supports 180-day local storage and playback, saving 75% disk space and 80% storage cost.

Huawei will firmly advance all-optical network evolution and innovation, upholding the philosophy of empowering AI evolution with optics and reshaping the optical future with AI. Huawei will continue working with global operators to help home and enterprise users win new growth in the AI era.

[Information] Rigaku to Be Featured on TV Tokyo’s “The Unknown Gulliver”

{ alert('現在のURLをコピーしました'); });">

Rigaku will be featured on TV Tokyo’s “The Unknown Gulliver: Excellent Company File,” scheduled to air on Saturday, September 12, 2026.

“The Unknown Gulliver” is a business documentary program that highlights outstanding Japanese companies recognized globally by those familiar with their fields, introducing their unique technologies, manufacturing capabilities, and paths of growth.

The program will introduce Rigaku’s X-ray analytical and measurement technologies as the company marks its 75th anniversary this year, along with the R&D and manufacturing sites that support them. Rigaku users also cooperated in the filming.

We hope you will tune in to see Rigaku’s challenge to uncover the unknown that cannot be seen with the naked eye.

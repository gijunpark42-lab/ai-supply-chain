From AI Awareness to Practical Application: Accton Drives Digital Empowerment for the Future Workforce

ESG Spotlight / Social 👪 / Human Resource Dept., AI Team / July 30th, 2026

As AI tools gradually reshape existing workflows and paradigms, mitigating the information security risks of external platforms retaining business data for secondary use and establishing correct usage mindsets among employees have become critical challenges for enterprises adopting new technologies.

Accton recognizes the technical barriers and practical gaps between simply introducing AI tools and truly integrating them into business operations. To bridge this gap, the Human Resources Department, in collaboration with cross-functional teams such as IT and RD, has co-planned and launched a series of internal AI empowerment training courses. The themes range from practical daily applications to professional use, providing employees with a progressive learning experience from basic to advanced levels.

Alongside these digital upskilling efforts, Accton’s IT department has integrated external large language models directly with our internal systems. With data security as the core foundation, we have developed AccEngine as a proprietary enterprise AI platform designed to safely power our employees’ daily workflows.

A Four-Stage Phased Approach to Systematically Build Digital Capabilities

Throughout this training series, Accton adheres to a progressive logic of first building AI awareness, then mastering the tools, advancing to Agent workflows, and ultimately extending to professional work applications. Following this sequence, we are rolling out four stages of practical courses:

Stage 1: Under the theme of “AI Casual Talks and Daily Applications,” we use relatable examples to help colleagues understand AI development trends, usage boundaries, and correct mindsets, thereby bridging the gap with new technologies.

Stage 2: With a foundational understanding of AI, we shift the focus to daily workflows. We guide colleagues in the practical use of enterprise AI tools, such as Accton’s internally developed AccEngine. By applying AI to time-consuming tasks like data organization and information retrieval, we free up valuable time and energy for matters that require human professional judgment.

Stage 3: As familiarity with AI tools grows, we progress from single tasks to teaching colleagues how to transfer their mental standard operating procedures (SOPs) to AI. By understanding Agent workflows and learning how to break down and design process steps, employees can turn AI into a digital assistant capable of automating repetitive daily tasks and significantly boosting work efficiency.

Stage 4: Finally, we move beyond text-based prompts and utilize coding to achieve advanced results. Regardless of their prior programming background, participants can gradually learn basic programming logic during this stage. This not only deepens their application of AI but also cultivates their ability to collaborate with technical teams, clearly articulate requirements, and understand automation logic.

Mastering the tools is only the first step; the true core always remains human critical thinking. Throughout the courses, while helping colleagues become more proficient with this technology, we specifically remind them to maintain an independent thinking mindset and evaluate the accuracy of the information provided by AI. This cultivates the critical judgment essential in the AI era. This learning momentum continues to build. As of the end of June 2026, we have held 9 courses and transformed the materials into online digital courses, providing colleagues with resources for continuous learning and advancement.

Building an Exclusive AI Platform Balancing Information Security and Practical Application

Training is only the first step in empowerment. The core focus of the entire training program is ensuring AI is truly implemented into daily internal workflows. Therefore, Accton’s IT team developed AccEngine, an AI tool that protects corporate information security while allowing employees to practically apply what they have learned.

AccEngine goes beyond handling common information gathering and organization; it precisely addresses specialized internal needs through seamless integration with internal systems and databases. To accommodate diverse work scenarios, the system further provides personal Agent configurations and Vibe Coding capabilities. This empowers employees to build customized automated workflows based on their specific processes, unlocking the substantial value of AI collaboration. Additionally, the IT department continuously develops and expands new features in response to feedback and operational needs from various departments, ensuring AccEngine stays up to date and provides comprehensive support to all employees.

Through progressive and systematic course planning alongside the design and application of enterprise AI tools, we are not only substantially enhancing our employees’ digital skills but also continuously building the digital capabilities and innovative foundation required for future work models.

Looking ahead, Accton will use this four-stage training program as a cornerstone to drive the deep integration of AI tools into the daily operations of all departments. Through solid internal training, we aim to guide our colleagues to confidently master emerging tools and transform technology into practical skills that resolve everyday work challenges.

Achievements

As of the end of June 2026, 9 courses have been conducted with a total of 1,914 participants, and the training materials have been transformed into online digital courses for employees’ continuous learning.

Employee Feedback: The greatest takeaway is mastering the correct mindset for using AI. This includes the understanding that one must possess foundational professional knowledge before engaging with AI. It also requires iteratively discussing and refining directions with the AI, as well as carefully reviewing its responses. While this back-and-forth process can be demanding, it is a necessary experience to ultimately achieve more comprehensive and logical results.

Our collaborative research with IISER Kolkata, IIT Madras, IIT Patna, JEOL Ltd., Nottingham Trent University, IISc Bangalore, and University of Alberta has been published in the "Journal of the American Chemical Society"

Release Date: 2026/09/10

Our collaborative research with Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Kolkata, Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Madras, Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Panta, JEOL Ltd., Nottingham Trent University, Indian Institute of Science(IISc) Bangalore, and University of Alberta has been published in the "Journal of the American Chemical Society". The paper reports the role of chemical structure and COF porosity/structure for selective CO 2 uptake in COF. The research project was led by IISER Kolkata, IIT Panta, University of Alberta, and IIT Madras.

Functionality over Order: Decoupling Chemical and Structural Contributions in CO 2 /CH 4 Separation by Covalent Organic Frameworks

DOI: 10.1021/jacs.6c08177

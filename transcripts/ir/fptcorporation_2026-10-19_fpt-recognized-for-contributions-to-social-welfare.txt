FPT Recognized for Contributions to Social Welfare

10/19/2026

← Back to News

The Vietnam Fatherland Front Central Committee has recognized FPT Corporation for significant contributions to social welfare during the 2024–2026 period, acknowledging the company's practical community initiatives and long-term social impact.

Recently, in Hanoi, the Vietnam Fatherland Front Central Committee held a ceremony to launch the 2026 Peak Month “For the Poor” campaign and honor organizations and individuals who made significant contributions to social welfare between 2024 and 2026.

Held under the theme “Great National Unity – Spreading Solidarity and Compassion,” the event recognized FPT as one of the organizations making meaningful contributions to the community.

Throughout 2024–2026, FPT maintained and implemented a wide range of ongoing, long-term corporate social responsibility and community welfare initiatives, focusing on educational support, assistance for disadvantaged children, and relief for communities affected by natural disasters.

Ms. Bui Thi Minh Hoai, Politburo Member, Secretary of the Party Central Committee, and President of the Vietnam Fatherland Front Central Committee, and Mr. Pham Gia Tuc, Politburo Member, Standing Deputy Secretary of the Government Party Committee, and Standing Deputy Prime Minister, present the recognition to Ms. Tran Thi Thu Ha, Chairwoman of the FPT Trade Union, representing FPT Corporation.

One of FPT's notable initiatives is the FPT People for the Community Fund, sustained through employees' annual contributions of one day's salary. FPT also continues to organize FPT Community Day on March 13, fostering a culture of compassion and social responsibility throughout the corporation.

Another flagship initiative is Hope School, which provides a nurturing learning environment for children who lost one or both parents during the COVID-19 pandemic. In 2025, the school opened a new 6,500-square-meter campus, providing educational facilities for more than 300 students.

In response to natural disasters in 2025, FPT launched the “Together with Communities to Overcome Floods” campaign, mobilizing collective support to rebuild 26 severely damaged school sites in affected localities.

In education and child welfare, FPT has partnered with the Hope Foundation to implement various programs aimed at narrowing educational disparities between regions. These initiatives include constructing durable concrete Hope Bridges in the Mekong Delta and providing scholarships such as the Nguyen Van Dao Scholarship and the FPT Young Talents (FYT) Scholarship.

In community healthcare, FPT has also supported the implementation of a project to deploy automated external defibrillators (AEDs).

These initiatives demonstrate FPT's commitment to social welfare beyond financial contributions and employee participation. By leveraging core strengths in technology, telecommunications, and education, FPT develops practical, sustainable programs and solutions that support communities over the long term.

In 2025 alone, FPT allocated VND 72.8 billion to corporate social responsibility initiatives, providing material and emotional support to approximately 97,300 people nationwide.

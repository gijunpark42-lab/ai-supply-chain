UMS INTEGRATION LIMITED

(Company Registration Number 200100340R)

(Incorporated in the Republic of Singapore)

PROPOSED PLACEMENT OF UP TO 52,941,100 NEW ORDINARY SHARES IN THE CAPITAL OF

THE COMPANY – RECEIPT OF APPROVAL-IN-PRINCIPLE

1. INTRODUCTION

The Board of Directors (the “Board” or “Directors”) of UMS Integration Limited (the “Company”,

and together with its subsidiaries, the “ Group”) refers to the Company’s announcement on 29

September 2026 in relation to the Proposed Placement (the “Previous Announcement”).

Unless otherwise defined, all terms and references used herein shall bear the same meaning as

ascribed to them in the Previous Announcement and the “Listing Rules” stated in this

announcement shall refer to the rules of the Listing Manual (Mainboard Rules) of the SGX -ST.

2. RECEIPT OF LISTING & QUOTATION NOTICE

Further to the Previous Announcement, the Board is pleased to announce that the Company has,

on 2 October 2026, received the listing and quotation notice from the SGX -ST in relation to the

approval in-principal on the application for the listing and quotation of the Placement Shares,

subject to compliance with the SGX-ST’s listing requirements (the “AIP”), including the following:

(a) if there is any significant change affecting any matter contained in the application or a

significant new matter has arisen that would have been required to be included in the

application, the Company must notify SGX-ST immediately pursuant to Listing Rule

864(4);

(b) the Company must comply with Listing Rules 704(30) and 1207(20) in relation to

announcing the use of proceeds; and

(c) the Proposed Placement must not result in a transfer of controlling interest pursuant to

Listing Rule 803, and the Placement Shares will not be placed to any persons specified

in Listing Rule 812.

The AIP states that the Placement Shares have to be placed out within seven (7) market days

from the date of the AIP.

The AIP is not to be taken as an indication of the merits of the  Proposed Placement, the

Placement Shares, the Company, its subsidiaries or its securities.

3. INTERESTS OF DIRECTORS AND SUBSTANTIAL SHAREHOLDERS

Save that Xie Xingbei Pearlyn, an independent director of the Company, is a Partner at Shook

Lin & Bok LLP, the Singapore law firm advising the Company on the Proposed Placement, none

of the Directors or Substantial Shareholders has any direct or indirect interest in the Proposed

Placement, other than through their directorships and/or shareholdings in the Company, if any.

For the avoidance of doubt, Wong Gang is the partner -in-charge of the transaction at Shook Lin

& Bok LLP.

5. FURTHER ANNOUNCEMENTS

The Company will continue to keep Shareholders updated and release announcements relating

to the Proposed Placement (including any material developments and progress made) as may

be appropriate from time to time.

6. CAUTIONARY STATEMENT

Shareholders and potential investors should exercise caution when trading in the shares of the

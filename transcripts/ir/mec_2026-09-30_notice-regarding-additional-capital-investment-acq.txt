Notice Regarding Additional Capital Investment (Acquisition of Noncurrent Assets) to

Increase Production Capacity

MEC COMPANY LTD. (“the Company”) hereby announces th at it has today decided to make capital

investments with the aim of strengthening its Busin ess Continuity Plan (BCP) by addressing rising dema nd,

particularly for products related to semiconductor package substrates, and decentralizing its production bases.

This capital investment is the second phase of construction of the Company’s Kitakyushu Factory, which is

scheduled to begin operations in December 2026.

1. Reason for the acquisition

In recent years, against the backdrop of rapid prog ress in generative AI, demand for electronic

substrates and components has been expanding. As va rious AI-related advancements are expected to

continue in the future, the Company is moving ahead  with the construction of a new factory (the

Kitakyushu Factory), scheduled to begin operations in December 2026, in order to take advantage of the

opportunities for sustainable growth stemming from the expanding demand.

The Company’s policy was to increase the production capacity of the new factory as necessary while

taking into account demand trends. However, against the backdrop of the increased investment related to

AI in recent years, demand for products related to semiconductor package substrates is expected to

greatly increase, particularly for the Company. In order to respond to these demand trends, the Company

has decided to make additional capital investments that go beyond the scope of the original plan in order

to meet a wider range of product demands and ensure compliance with relevant laws and regulations.

2. Outline of the capital investment

(1) Details In order to meet the expected increase in demand for

semiconductor package substrates, the Company will

strengthen facilities at its Kitakyushu Factory, which will

produce chemical products.

(2) Investment amount Approx. ¥2 billion

(3) Start of operation FY2028 (planned)

(4) Financing plan Own funds and borrowings (planne d)

3. Future outlook

The impact on the consolidated financial results for  the fiscal year ending December 2026 is

immaterial.

Systech Launches TraceIQ, Helping Transform Pharmaceutical Traceability from Compliance to Operational Intelligence

DOWNERS GROVE, Ill. , Sept. 2, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Systech , part of Markem-Imaje and Dover and a leading provider of pharmaceutical serialization and traceability solutions, today announced the launch of Systech TraceIQ™, an intelligent and intuitive enterprise traceability platform created for a new operational reality in pharmaceutical supply chains.

Backed by Systech's four decades of packaging execution and serialization expertise, TraceIQ combines core compliance capabilities with an operational intelligence layer enhanced by AI-assisted analytics designed for today's pharmaceutical supply chains.

User-friendly and fast to deploy, TraceIQ transforms traceability data from a passive record of transactions into an active source of insight, enabling teams to identify patterns, investigate root causes and collaborate more effectively with partners.

"Serialization created unprecedented visibility into pharmaceutical supply chains, but visibility alone does not solve operational issues," said Sreedhar Patnala , General Manager, Systech. "Organizations need to quickly understand what happened, why it happened and what action to take next. TraceIQ helps teams move beyond compliance reporting to make proactive, informed decisions that keep products moving throughout the supply chain safely and in a timely manner."

Systech TraceIQ is built around three operational outcomes:

Prevent issues before they impact production: Built-in validation and guardrails help identify bad data and avoidable errors before they create downstream exceptions, rework or shipment delays.

Resolve exceptions before they become disruptions: Centralized traceability data and AI-assisted investigation help teams determine what happened, identify likely causes and take corrective action faster.

Turn traceability data into operational decisions: Intuitive search, analytics and intelligent assistance provide supply chain, quality and compliance teams with the insights needed to make quicker decisions, improve partner collaboration and optimize performance.

From emerging biopharmaceutical companies preparing for commercial launch to global manufacturers and contract organizations managing complex partner ecosystems, TraceIQ helps organizations move beyond compliance and unlock greater value from their serialization and traceability investments through greater operational visibility and increased productivity.

Systech TraceIQ is in production with customers and is being showcased in live demonstrations at the Healthcare Distribution Alliance (HDA) Traceability Seminar in Washington, D.C. , from August 31 to September 2, 2026 . Learn more about TraceIQ here .

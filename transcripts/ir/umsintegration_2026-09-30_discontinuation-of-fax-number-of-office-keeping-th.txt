DISCONTINUATION OF THE FACSIMILE NUMBER OF THE OFFICE AT WHICH THE REGISTER OF SECURITIES OF THE COMPANY IS KEPT IN MALAYSIA

Shareholder Approval No

Announcement Details/Table Section

The Company wishes to announce that the facsimile number of the office at which the Register of Securities of the Company is kept in Malaysia will be discontinued with effect from 1 October 2026.

All correspondence and enquiries previously transmitted by facsimile should therefrom be via telephone or email as follows:

Telephone No.: 03-2084 9000Email Address: info@sshsb.com.my There is no change to the address and telephone number of the office at which the Register of Securities of the Company is kept in Malaysia. The addresses and telephone numbers of the Company's registered office and the office at which its Register of Securities is kept in Singapore also remain unchanged.

This announcement is dated 30 September 2026.

nVent Names Courtney Nelson Wills General Counsel

September 29, 2026

Experienced public company legal executive will lead nVent's global legal, compliance and corporate governance functions LONDON, Sept. 29, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- nVent Electric plc (nVent), a global leader in electrical connection and protection solutions, today announced the appointment of Courtney Nelson Wills as Executive Vice President, General Counsel and Secretary, effective October 12. Wills will report directly to nVent Chair and CEO Beth Wozniak and will lead nVent's global legal organization, including the compliance, corporate governance and corporate secretary functions. She will be based out of nVent's management offices in St. Louis Park, MN.

"Courtney is a strategic public company general counsel with decades of legal, business and governance experience across global organizations," said Beth Wozniak, nVent Chair and CEO. "Her passion for business, technology and people makes her a great fit for nVent, and she will provide strong leadership as we continue to execute our growth strategy."

Nelson Wills joins nVent with more than 25 years of experience spanning global commercial operations, public-company governance, M&A, intellectual property and regulatory and compliance matters. Most recently, she served as Senior Vice President, General Counsel and Corporate Secretary of MiniMed Group, Inc., a multibillion-dollar spin-off from Medtronic which completed its IPO in early 2026.

"I'm honored to join nVent and excited about the opportunities ahead," said Courtney Nelson Wills. "nVent is a company with a strong track record, exciting growth strategy and a great culture focused on people and performance. I look forward to working with the leadership team and employees around the world to support the company's continued success."

Nelson Wills earned a Juris Doctor from the University of Minnesota Law School and a Bachelor of Science degree in Chemical Engineering from the University of Minnesota.

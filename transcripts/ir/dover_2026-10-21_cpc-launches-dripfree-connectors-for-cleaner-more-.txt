CPC Launches DripFree Connectors for Cleaner, More Efficient Printing Processes

DOWNERS GROVE, Ill. , Oct. 21, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- CPC ( Colder Products Company ), part of PSG , a Dover company (NYSE: DOV), and a leading manufacturer of connection technologies, today announced the launch of the DripFree™ Series, a line of non-spill connectors designed to support fast, clean, and efficient servicing of ink delivery systems in commercial inkjet printers.

"The ink spills, waste, and contamination that the printing industry has tolerated during equipment start-up and maintenance can be a thing of the past with the DripFree Series," said Mat Stahl , Senior Product Manager, CPC. "DripFree Series connectors enable quick material fills and easier equipment servicing, and what really sets them apart is their new lubrication-free design."

Quick disconnects typically rely on lubricated O-rings. The DripFree Series' lubrication-free valve avoids the risk of contaminants that can enter the flow path and affect print quality while enabling thousands of non-spill disconnect cycles.

The DripFree Series also features non-spill valves that help prevent messy ink spills after disconnection and minimize air inclusion during connection, reducing or eliminating foaming within the ink line. Together, these capabilities support print quality, reduce ink waste, and keep printing environments clean.

The DripFree product line is manufactured in an ISO Class 8 cleanroom, minimizing particulates generated during assembly. The connectors are chemically compatible with a wide range of inks and solvents, and ergonomic thumb latches make both connections and disconnections seamless.

The DripFree™ DF2 Series*, the first size in the DripFree Series, is a compact 1/8-inch flow quick disconnect that fits conveniently into tightly configured printing systems. For more information on the DripFree DF2 Series, visit https://www.cpcworldwide.com/General-Purpose/Products/Valved/DripFree-DF2 .

The DripFree Series was unveiled at this year's PRINTING United Expo in Las Vegas, which is recognized as the premier printing event in North America , featuring nearly 850 exhibitors and more than 30,000 registered attendees from 104 countries.

For more information about CPC's portfolio of connectors for print and ink management applications, visit cpcworldwide.com/print-and-ink .

*DripFree™ DF2 Series spillage per disconnect is <0.05mL, less than a drop of water.

Tokyo Electron is chosen for "MSCI Selection Indexes"

Tokyo Electron (TEL) continues to be elected as a component of the world-renowned ESG (Environmental, Social and Governance) indexes, "MSCI Selection Indexes".

MSCI ESG Index series were developed by Morgan Stanley Capital International (MSCI, U.S.) and selects companies with excellent ESG performance in each industry. We also continue to be selected as a component of “MSCI Nihonkabu ESG Select Leaders Index”, “MSCI Japan ESG Select Leaders Index” and “MSCI Japan Empowering Women Index (WIN)”.

We will continue to promote sustainability-focused management and pursue its corporate philosophy of "We strive to contribute to the development of a dream-inspiring society through our leading-edge technologies and reliable service and support."

Please refer to the URL below for further details.

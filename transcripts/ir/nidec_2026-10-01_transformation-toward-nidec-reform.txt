Major Impairment Losses

AMEC (Automotive Motor & Electronic Control Business Unit) ٻ204.2 billion yen (FY22)

• AMEC primarily focuses on automotive motors, inclu ding those for electric power steering (EPS), elect ric brakes, and

pumps . Anticipating electrification, the business expanded its operational foundation through M&A and capital

expenditures.

• In addition to shifts in the EV market, revised el ectrification strategies by Western OEMs, and falling vehicle prices driven

by the rise of Chinese OEMs, price reduction demands from automakers and Tier 1 suppliers intensified. For products

with diminished differentiation, adoption of Chinese suppliers expanded, intensifying price competition .

• Securing orders and market share failed to yield s tandard profitability, widening the mismatch with production capacity

originally built on expectations of demand growth. As the financial corrections for prior years made c lear that

profitability was continuously declining , impairment losses were recognized .

NDTC-G (Nidec Drive Technology Group) ٻ95.3 billion yen (FY24)

• NDTC-G is a machinery segment encompassing speed r educers, machine tools, and press machines. The speed reducer

business operates bases in Japan and Europe (e.g., G ermany), offering a line of products ranging from s mall to large

sizes.

• The speed reducer business has suffered from persis tent low profitability due to intensifying competition with Chinese

and other manufacturers , centered on small-sized products, as well as cost burdens at European sites . Necessitated

transitioning production of small-sized products to low-cost regions and shifting toward promising large-sized products,

such as those for data center generators.

• The speed reducer business in Japan and certain ove rseas operations also stagnated, while in the press  business,

excess inventory in the plate fabrication segment p ressured operating performance . Acquired businesses have also

failed to fully deliver projected growth and integration synergies.

• As the financial corrections made clear that certain businesses remained persistently unprofitable , it was

determined that businesses previously evaluated as a single unit should be assessed individually, leading to the

recognition of impairment losses.

Major Impairment Losses

ACIM (Appliance, commercial and industrial products) -298.8 billion yen

Business overview

Motors and compressors for home appliances and HVAC, commercial and industrial motors for agriculture, water

treatment, power generation, commercial HVAC and refrigeration equipment

Background of impairment

1. Home appliances business:  Excess supply capacity

Following the post-COVID slowdown in market growth, 40-50% of our supply capacity -prepared on the premise

of expanding demands- became surplus. This excess capacity weighed on earnings as a fixed-cost burden.

2. Home appliances business: Excessive competition i n the Chinese market

In the Chinese market, while our market share rivaled that of domestic competitors, we faced escalating price

reduction pressure from home appliance manufacturer customers.

Excessive price competition in the Chinese market remained as local competitors prioritized maintaining their

market share over profitability, accepting operating losses.

Thus, our business in this area, including compressors and motors for home appliances, fell into an operating

loss, and it became difficult to expect a recovery to the break-even point in the medium term.

3. General industrial motor business: Business structure with heavy fixed-cost burden

General industrial motor accounts for around 75% of this line of business. With numerous operational/

production sites concentrated in Western Europe (mainly in France), this business has been facing heavy fixed-

cost burden, resulting in sustained low-profitability.

To avoid short-term impacts on financial performance, structural reforms, including those of production sites,

had not sufficiently progressed. As a result, even after factoring in structural reforms and additional investments,

profitability recovery was expected to require a prolonged period.

Based on the above, we reviewed future potential and profitability of the business and decided to pursue radical

structural reforms including those in production systems, resulting in the record of impairment losses.

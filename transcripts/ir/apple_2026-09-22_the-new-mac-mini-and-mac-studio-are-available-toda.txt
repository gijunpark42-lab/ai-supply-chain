September 22, 2026

The new Mac mini and Mac Studio are available today

Apple’s latest desktops, Mac mini with the blazing-fast M6 and M5 Pro, and Mac Studio with M5 Max and the powerful M5 Ultra, are now available at Apple Store locations, the Apple Store online, and in the Apple Store app

Customers can now shop and discover the new Mac mini, Apple’s most versatile Mac, and Mac Studio, Apple’s most powerful Mac, both powered by the latest Apple silicon.

Starting today, the new Mac mini and Mac Studio, both powered by the latest Apple silicon, are now available. Customers can shop and discover the new Mac mini, Apple’s most versatile Mac, which features the all-new M6 and M5 Pro. The ultracompact do-it-all desktop is a powerhouse for a wide range of users — from everyday consumers and students to aspiring creatives and small business owners. With M6, Mac mini delivers up to 4x faster AI performance, 1 2x faster graphics 2 and storage, 1 and 40 percent faster CPU performance. 1 Users will feel this performance in everything they do, whether it’s multitasking across productivity apps, running agentic AI workflows, or ripping through creative projects like music production and compiling code. Mac mini with M5 Pro offers even greater pro-level performance for complex projects like video production and game development. The new Mac Studio, Apple’s most powerful Mac, features M5 Max and the all-new M5 Ultra for pros tackling the world’s most demanding workloads. The ultimate pro desktop raises the bar with up to 4.3x faster AI performance, 3 up to 1.8x faster graphics, 4 and support for up to 512GB of unified memory, all in a quiet, compact design that sits right on a user’s desk.

Mac mini and Mac Studio are also powerful platforms for on-device AI, featuring next-generation GPUs with Neural Accelerators in every core, along with advanced CPUs, and Neural Engines. Combined with the power of macOS 27 and the latest Apple Intelligence features, including Siri AI, the new Mac mini and Mac Studio are a tremendous upgrade for existing and new-to-Mac users. 5 Both are available now at Apple Store locations, on apple.com , and in the Apple Store app. Mac mini with M6 starts at $899, while Mac mini with M5 Pro is available at $1,699. Mac Studio with M5 Max starts at $2,499, while Mac Studio with M5 Ultra starts at $5,499.

Say Hello to the New Mac mini: Apple’s Most Versatile Mac

For everyday users, students, developers, creative enthusiasts, and businesses, Mac mini delivers a dramatic boost in performance and remarkable versatility, all in a super small design. Featuring a 12-core CPU with the world’s fastest single-threaded performance, a 12-core GPU with Neural Accelerators built into each core, and the all-new Dual 16-core Neural Engine, it blazes through everything from everyday productivity to advanced agentic AI workflows. 6 Starting with 16GB of unified memory, configurable up to 32GB, and higher memory bandwidth up to 170GB/s, multitasking is faster than ever. With M6, Mac mini brings up to 4x faster AI performance, 1 a supercharge to the leading desktop for always-on agentic computing. Mac mini with M5 Pro delivers even more pro-level performance with an up-to-18-core CPU and an up-to-20-core GPU, along with support for up to 64GB of unified memory with 307GB/s of memory bandwidth, empowering users to take on demanding creative and technical projects. Both models include Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, and upgraded 2.5Gb Ethernet, with a 10Gb option available. College students and educators can save on the new Mac mini with year-round education pricing starting at $799 for Mac mini with M6.

Mac mini is more versatile than ever, whether it’s multitasking across productivity apps, running agentic AI workflows, or ripping through creative projects like music production and compiling code.

The New Mac Studio: Apple’s Most Powerful Mac

Engineered for professionals who tackle the most intense workloads, Mac Studio is the ultimate desktop for pro workflows and on-device AI. Mac Studio with M5 Max features an 18-core CPU, an up-to-40-core GPU with Neural Accelerators built into each core, and up to 128GB of unified memory, enabling musicians, photographers, software engineers, and designers to push the limits of real-time 3D, motion graphics, and AI workflows. Mac Studio with M5 Ultra takes performance to an entirely new level with an up-to-36-core CPU, an up-to-80-core GPU, and a staggering up to 512GB of unified memory with 1.2TB/s of memory bandwidth, empowering filmmakers to color-grade uncompressed 8K footage in real time, VFX artists to render complex scenes, and AI researchers to run enormous LLMs entirely on device. Both models feature Thunderbolt 5 for blazing-fast transfer speeds up to 120Gb/s, so users can take advantage of superfast external storage, a PCIe expansion chassis, and powerful hub solutions. For the most advanced users pushing the limits of compute, Thunderbolt 5 with Remote Direct Memory Access (RDMA) also enables multiple Mac Studio systems to be clustered, now bringing up to 3x faster performance for distributed AI inference when compared to a single system. 3 Mac Studio supports up to eight displays, or up to four Studio Display XDR at full 5K resolution and 120Hz, providing an expansive canvas for the most demanding projects.

Mac Studio is engineered for professionals who tackle the most demanding workloads, including 3D, motion graphics, and AI. Advanced users pushing the limits of compute can gain even faster performance by clustering multiple Mac Studio systems.

macOS 27 Golden Gate

The new Mac mini and Mac Studio come to life with the recently released macOS 27 , which includes Siri AI , a profoundly more capable and personal assistant; helpful Apple Intelligence features across everyday apps; and improvements that make the Mac even more responsive and reliable. Siri AI can draw on personal context to help users find what they need in the moment across their messages, emails, and photos; answer questions about virtually any topic; and get things done across apps. Siri AI is integrated into Spotlight and systemwide context menus, and users can ask Siri about what’s on their display with Visual Intelligence using a dedicated keyboard shortcut. Users can also write and edit with Siri almost anywhere they type.

Additionally, Apple Intelligence makes apps smarter and more useful. In Safari, it powers new tools that automatically organize a user’s tabs into topics; notify them of website changes, like product restocks or price drops; and enable the creation of custom extensions based on a description. Users can also easily create an automation in Shortcuts by simply describing it, or tap into advanced photo editing in the Photos app. Features users already rely on get even better with improvements to performance and search. Refinements to Liquid Glass deliver an even more focused and approachable experience. A new slider in Settings gives users the option to personalize Liquid Glass, adjusting it anywhere from ultraclear to fully tinted to match their preference, and app icons have been updated to be sharper and more defined.

With Visual Intelligence, users can now ask Siri about what they’re looking at onscreen.

Write with Siri allows users to simply describe what they need to generate a draft and help refine what they’ve written.

In Safari, Apple Intelligence powers new tools that automatically organize a user’s tabs into topics.

Personalized Service and Support

Apple Specialists are available to help with customers’ shopping needs in-store, online, and in the Apple Store app. Whether they’re looking for assistance in choosing the right product, learning about monthly leasing and financing options, Apple Trade In, or setting up and going further with a new device, customers receive best-in-class support from Apple’s knowledgeable retail team members.

Apple offers great ways to save and upgrade to the latest Mac desktop this fall, along with personalized service and support:

Apple Trade In : Customers can trade in their current computer and get credit toward a new Mac. Customers can visit apple.com/shop/trade-in to see what their device is worth. Customers in the U.S. who shop at Apple using Apple Card 7 can also take advantage of financing options through Apple Card Monthly Installments. 8

Savings for students : Available exclusively from Apple — students can save with year-round education pricing starting at $799 (U.S.) for Mac mini and $2,299 (U.S.) for Mac Studio. Students can learn more by visiting their nearest Apple Store or at apple.com/us-edu/store .

Apple Upgrade : ‡ Eligible customers in the U.S. can explore 24- and 36-month leasing options for Mac Studio, with lease prices starting as low as $48.99 (U.S.) per month for Mac Studio with M5 Max for a 36-month lease (excluding taxes and any trade-in credit). Users can also lease a Mac Studio with M5 Ultra with Apple Upgrade from $110.10 (U.S.) per month (excluding taxes and any trade-in credit) for a 36-month lease. Additional configure-to-order options are available at apple.com/mac-studio .^ At the end of their lease term, eligible customers can upgrade their device to the latest generation, purchase it with a one-time payment, or simply return it and exit the program.

Shopping and setup support : On apple.com and in the Apple Store app, customers can receive personalized shopping assistance via chat and phone to find the best products that suit their needs. And for an even more immersive shopping experience, Shop with a Specialist over Video offers a secure, one-way video call to browse the latest Apple products with an Apple Specialist online. After receiving their order, customers can connect with an Apple Specialist online or in-store and take advantage of the Personal Setup service to ensure a safe, secure, and seamless data transfer experience to their new device, and learn how to use key product and software features.

Build to order : For customers shopping Mac mini, Mac Studio, or the latest Studio Display models, Apple offers configure-to-order options, allowing them to select and customize the device to their specific requirements. This includes size, chip, memory, storage, and display height.

Dedicated business support : In-store and online, Apple’s dedicated Business Team members are available to help small- and medium-sized businesses with their technology needs, from personalized product recommendations and bulk purchasing, to seamless setup and ongoing support for their Mac, iPad, and iPhone devices.

Today at Apple : Customers can learn how to get the most out of their new devices through Today at Apple sessions — free daily education experiences led by Apple Creatives that inspire possibilities across photography, art, music, coding, productivity, and so much more. These sessions make it easy for customers to get set up and learn new skills to help them go further with their devices. Participants can register for a Today at Apple session at apple.com/today or drop in to a session at their local Apple Store.

Pricing and Availability

The new Mac mini and Mac Studio will begin arriving to customers, and in Apple Store locations and Apple Authorized Resellers, starting today, September 22. Mac Studio with 512GB of unified memory is coming in late October.

Mac mini with M6 starts at $899 (U.S.) and $799 (U.S.) for education . Mac mini with M5 Pro starts at $1,699 (U.S.) and $1,599 (U.S.) for education . Additional technical specifications are available at apple.com/mac-mini .

Mac Studio with M5 Max starts at $2,499 (U.S.) and $2,299 (U.S.) for education . Mac Studio with M5 Ultra starts at $5,499 (U.S.) and $5,099 (U.S.) for education . Additional configure-to-order options are available at apple.com/mac-studio .

With Apple Upgrade, ‡ eligible customers in the U.S. can lease a new Mac with low monthly payments and easily upgrade at the end of their lease: apple.com/shop/apple-upgrade . Lease Mac Studio with M5 Max with Apple Upgrade from $48.99 (U.S.) per month (excluding taxes and any trade-in credit) for a 36-month lease. Lease Mac Studio with M5 Ultra with Apple Upgrade from $110.10 (U.S.) per month (excluding taxes and any trade-in credit) for a 36-month lease. Additional configure-to-order options are available at apple.com/mac-studio .^

Additional technical specifications, configure-to-order options, and information on Studio Display, Studio Display XDR, and Magic accessories are available at apple.com/mac .

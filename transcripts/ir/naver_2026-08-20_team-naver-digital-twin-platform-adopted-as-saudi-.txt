TEAM NAVER Digital Twin Platform Adopted as Saudi Arabia’s National Standard

- Digital twin platform project that began with a contract for major Saudi cities becomes a national standard in just three years

- Saudi Ministry of Municipalities and Housing formally recommends prioritizing TEAM NAVER’s platform for national and municipal digital twin projects

- TEAM NAVER solidifies its position as a key partner in Saudi Arabia’s nationwide digital transformation

August 20, 2026

[Photo] In May 2025, NAVER Cloud signed an agreement with Saudi Arabia’s National Housing Company (NHC) to establish NAVER Innovation, a strategic joint venture.

(Clockwise from back left) Chae Seon-ju, Head of Strategic Business at NAVER; Majed Al-Hogail, Saudi Minister of Municipalities and Housing; Rayan Al-Aqeel, CEO of NHC Innovation; and Han Sangyoung, Executive Director of Global DX & Innovation at NAVER Cloud.

​

TEAM NAVER announced on August 20 that its cloud-based digital twin platform has received official approval from Saudi Arabia’s Digital Government Authority (DGA) for use by municipalities and has been adopted as the Saudi government’s “national standard digital twin platform.” TEAM NAVER said the milestone marks the formal certification of a Korean IT company’s technology by a foreign government agency and gives the company a foothold to expand its technology across Saudi Arabia just three years after winning the project in 2023.

Following the DGA’s approval, Saudi Arabia’s Ministry of Municipalities and Housing recently issued an official notice to municipalities across the country recommending preferential adoption of TEAM NAVER’s platform for smart city projects.

The notice calls on municipalities to give priority to TEAM NAVER’s platform when undertaking digital twin projects in order to prevent duplicative investments among local governments and harmonize technical standards and methodologies nationwide. The aim is to integrate digital twin and digital urban planning initiatives undertaken by local governments across Saudi Arabia within a unified national framework.

The Saudi government plans to use TEAM NAVER’s digital twin platform as the national standard to make a wide range of municipal administrative functions — including urban planning, city operations, asset and infrastructure management, and urban safety — data-driven, while enabling municipalities to share data and operational experience.

TEAM NAVER previously won a contract from Saudi Arabia’s Ministry of Municipalities and Housing in October 2023 to build a digital twin platform for major cities. In 2025, the company completed deployments in Makkah, Madinah, and Jeddah, creating digital twins of more than 920,000 buildings across 6,800 square kilometers — an area more than 11 times the size of Seoul — and laying the groundwork for a nationwide rollout.

With its platform now adopted as a national standard, TEAM NAVER plans to further strengthen cooperation with the Saudi government and local partners. In particular, the company aims to accelerate the refinement of technologies tailored to local needs, drawing on the expertise and data it has accumulated through large-scale urban digital twin deployments.

“The adoption of our platform as a national standard is especially meaningful because it validates not only TEAM NAVER’s technological capabilities but also the track record and trust we have built in Saudi Arabia,” said Chae Seon-ju, Head of Strategic Business at NAVER and Chair of NAVER Innovation. “Building on this milestone, we will apply TEAM NAVER’s capabilities in digital twin technology, AI, and cloud to urban infrastructure development across Saudi Arabia, while further solidifying our position as a key technology partner supporting digital transformation across the Middle East.” (End)

Delta Electronics’ Consolidated Sales Revenues

for September 2026 Totaled NT$65,400 Million

TAIPEI, October 12, 2026 – Delta Electronics, Inc. today announced its consolidated sales revenues for September 2026 totaled NT$65,400 million, a 14.6 percent increase as compared to NT$57,061 million for September 2025 and a 1.3 percent increase as compared to NT$64,586 million for August 2026. The Company's cumulative consolidated sales revenues from January to September in 2026 were NT$539,668 million, a 37.2 percent increase as compared to NT$393,272 million for the same period in 2025.

Percentage of sales for Delta’s four main business categories for September 2026:

Power Electronics

51%

Mobility

6%

Automation

8%

Infrastructure

35%

Others

0%

The figures have not been audited.

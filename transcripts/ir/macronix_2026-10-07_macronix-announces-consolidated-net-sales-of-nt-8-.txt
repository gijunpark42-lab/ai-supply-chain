Hsinchu, Taiwan, October 7, 2026 - Macronix International Co., Ltd. (TSE: 2337) today announced the unaudited consolidated net sales for September 2026 were NT$8.367 billion, an increase of 2.7% from the previous month of NT$8.145 billion. Compared to the same period of NT$2.903 billion in 2025, the consolidated net sales of September increased 188.2% in 2026.

Accumulated consolidated net sales for January to September 2026 were NT$53.831 billion, an increase of 154.5% compared to the same period of NT$21.151 billion in 2025.

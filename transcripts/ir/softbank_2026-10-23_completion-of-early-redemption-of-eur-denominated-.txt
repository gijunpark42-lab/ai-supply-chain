Completion of Early Redemption of EUR-denominated Senior Notes Issued in 2021

October 23, 2026

SoftBank Group Corp.

SoftBank Group Corp. announces today that it has completed the early redemption of its EUR-denominated 2.875% Senior Notes due January 6, 2027, which were issued in 2021 (the “Notes”), as of yesterday. The Notes have been fully redeemed and canceled, and there are no Notes outstanding.

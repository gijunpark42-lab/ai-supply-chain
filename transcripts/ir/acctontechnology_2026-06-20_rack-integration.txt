Why integrate at the rack

The rack is the new unit of compute.

AI workloads spill past the boundary of any single server. The rack — its fabric, its silicon, its watts and its coolant — is now the smallest piece that meaningfully scales. So it has to be engineered as a whole.

100 kW+

Power per rack

AI racks concentrate power at a density air cannot serve — integrated liquid cooling becomes mandatory, not optional.

1.6 T

Fabric per port

Top-of-rack switching scales 800G → 1.6T on open hardware, stitching every tray into one low-latency fabric.

5 → 1

Disciplines to one machine

Power, cooling, compute, optics, and networking — co-validated so the rack behaves identically from unit one to one thousand.

Built with an ecosystem

We don't build alone.

Accton sits at the center of a deep partner ecosystem — silicon and accelerators, optical innovators, cooling specialists, power systems, and open software. We integrate their best work into one validated rack, accountable end to end.

Accton is the integrator. We co-design with partners and own the assembly, validation, and manufacturing of the complete rack.

Open by default. SONiC and ONiE keep the fabric free of lock-in, so partner technology drops in cleanly.

One accountable partner. A single point of ownership for the whole rack — from first thermal model to the data-center floor.

October 9, 2026

Notice Regarding Changes in Specified Subsidiaries

in Prior Fiscal Years

It has been determined that changes in two specified subsidiaries in prior fiscal years were not

disclosed in a timely manner as required under the stock exchange regulations. We sincerely

apologize for the delayed disclosure and hereby announce the details as set forth below.

1. Overview of Specified Subsidiary No. 1

Item Details

（1）Reason for the Change

The Company resolved at a meeting of its Board of

Directors held on February 18, 2020 to establish a

subsidiary in India, and the subsidiary was incorporated in

April 2020. As the capital of the subsidiary amounted to

10% or more of the Company's capital, the subsidiary

became a specified subsidiary of the Company.

（2）Company Name Nissan Bharat Rasayan PVT. LTD.

（3）Location  5th Floor, Tower B, Spazedge Commercial Complex,

Sector-47, Sohna Road,Gurgaon-122002, Haryana, India

（4）Representative   NIKI Toshio

（5）Business Manufacture of Agrochemical Active Ingredients

（6）Registered Capital INR 1.5 billion (approximately JPY 2.2 billion)

（7）Date of Establishment April 1, 2020

（8）Fiscal Year-End March 31

（9）Shareholding Ratio The Company 70%; Bharat Rasayan Limited 30%

（10）Financial Position and Operating Results for the Most Recent Three Fiscal Years

(Units: JPY million)

FY 2023 FY 2024 FY 2025

Net Assets 3,189 3,990 4,061

Total Assets 13,538 12,125 12,017

2. Overview of Specified Subsidiary No. 2

Item Details

（1）Reason for the Change

The Company resolved at a meeting of its Management

Committee held on December 12, 2022 to acquire additional

shares of the subsidiary and make it a wholly owned

subsidiary, and acquired such shares in April 2023. As the

capital of the subsidiary amounted to 10% or more of the

Company's capital, the subsidiary became a specified

subsidiary of the Company.

（2）Company Name NC Tokyo Bay Corporation *As of April 2023, the company

name was Nippon Phosphoric Acid Co., Ltd.

（5）Business Manufacture of Sulfuric Acid

（6）Registered Capital JPY 1,946 million

（7）Date of Establishment December 25, 1967

（8）Fiscal Year-End March 31

（9）Shareholding Ratio 100% owned by the Company

3．Impact on Financial Results

The financial results of the two subsidiaries concerned have already been incorporated into

the consolidated earnings forecast announced on May 15, 2026. Accordingly, this matter will

have no impact on the Company's financial results for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2027.

4. Future Measures

The Company takes this matter very seriously and will review its internal verification

procedures related to timely disclosure, strengthen information sharing and coordination

among relevant departments, and further enhance employee education regarding tim ely

disclosure requirements, with the aim of preventing any recurrence.

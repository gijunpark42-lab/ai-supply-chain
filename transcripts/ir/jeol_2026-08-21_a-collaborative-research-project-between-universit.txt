A collaborative research project between University of California, Riverside, National Institute for Materials Science (NIMS), Shinshu University, University of Tsukuba, and JEOL Ltd. has been published in Angewandte Chemie International Edition.

Release Date: 2026/08/21

A collaborative research project between University of California, Riverside, National Institute for Materials Science (NIMS), Shinshu University, University of Tsukuba, and JEOL Ltd. has been published in Angewandte Chemie International Edition. The article, entitled “Anisotropy-Driven Discovery of Rare-Earth-Free Magnets in Mo₂FeB₂-Type Borides,” reports the discovery of the new rare-earth-free ferromagnetic materials MoMn₂B₂ and WMn₂B₂ in the Mo₂FeB₂-type boride system, a class of compounds traditionally known for its outstanding mechanical properties. The newly discovered materials exhibit ferromagnetism above room temperature, highlighting their potential as rare-earth-free permanent magnet candidates. In recognition of its novelty and impact, the article was selected as a Hot Paper by Angewandte Chemie.

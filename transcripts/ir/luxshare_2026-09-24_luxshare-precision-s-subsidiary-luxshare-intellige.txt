Luxshare Precision's Subsidiary Luxshare Intelligent Computing Unveils NEXTiX LEGION at Intel Connection 2026

Intel Connection 2026, Intel’s technology innovation and industry ecosystem conference, took place on September 22–23 at the Suzhou International Expo Center. Luxshare Precision showcased NEXTiX LEGION, its AI infrastructure product portfolio, featuring a range of AI computing systems and comprehensive solutions spanning core components, prefabricated data centers, and computing services. Building on its strengths in R&D, precision manufacturing, and vertical integration, Luxshare Precision continues to expand its AI computing infrastructure portfolio and strengthen its ability to deliver standardized solutions at scale.

As AI models grow in scale and applications become more diverse, computing infrastructure is evolving from standalone machines toward integrated systems and clusters. In response to this trend, NEXTiX LEGION embraces the product philosophy of “Evolving with models. Built for every scenario.” Built on the unified iX Architecture, NEXTiX LEGION delivers four core capabilities: intelligent orchestration (Intelligence), efficient computing (eXecution), high-speed interconnects (eXchange), and extreme scalability (eXtreme). It scales flexibly from a single server to GPU clusters with tens of thousands of GPUs, providing efficient, scalable computing power for a wide range of AI applications.

LEGION Server Portfolio for Diverse AI Computing Needs

Six NEXTiX LEGION products—TITAN, APEX, NOVA, IRON, VORTEX, and FALCON—were showcased at the conference, supporting a wide range of applications, including large-scale computing centers, core AI training workloads, open heterogeneous computing, enterprise AI, high-density computing, and high-density liquid-cooled deployments.

Built on a unified architecture, the product portfolio shares coordinated firmware, thermal design, operations and maintenance frameworks, and cluster networking capabilities. This reduces the complexity of large-scale deployment and ongoing operations, providing flexible, efficient computing options for applications of varying scales and requirements.

Prefabricated Data Centers Accelerate AI Infrastructure Deployment

To meet rapidly growing demand for computing capacity, Luxshare Precision continues to advance prefabricated, modular data center construction. Power, cooling, computing, networking, and storage systems are integrated and tested before being transported to the project site for modular deployment, significantly reducing construction timelines. Individual functional modules can be flexibly expanded to meet business needs, bringing new computing resources online faster and further improving the efficiency and flexibility of infrastructure development.

Extending from Product Delivery to Computing Services

While continuously improving hardware products and infrastructure solutions, Luxshare Precision is actively expanding its computing service model. By building a GPU computing resource pool and combining it with remote monitoring, on-site O&M, and other service capabilities, the company flexibly allocates computing resources according to actual business needs, driving the extension of computing capabilities from product delivery to continuous services. This exhibition further presented Luxshare Precision's layout in AI computing servers, liquid cooling infrastructure, prefabricated data centers, and computing services, reflecting the Group's comprehensive capabilities in continuously extending from core components to complete machines, systems, and services.

Looking ahead, Luxshare Precision will continue to leverage its comprehensive advantages in technology R&D, precision manufacturing, vertical integration, and large-scale delivery. Working hand in hand with industry chain partners, the company will continuously improve its AI computing infrastructure products and solutions, providing reliable and efficient computing support for the intelligent transformation of various industries.

Socionext Participated in a Panel Discussion at Open Source Summit Europe 2026

Socionext Inc. participated in a panel discussion at Open Source Summit Europe 2026, which was held from October 7 to 9, 2026, in the Czech Republic.

Open Source Summit Europe 2026 was one of the leading conferences in the open source software (OSS*) community, organized by The Linux Foundation.

The event brought together developers, engineers, and community leaders to share knowledge, foster collaboration in addressing industry challenges, and discuss the future development of open source projects.

Socionext supports its employees' involvement in OSS community activities. As part of this initiative, a Socionext employee participated as a panelist in the panel discussion, where topics such as SBOM (Software Bill of Materials) quality improvement and effective information sharing across organizations were discussed.

*: Open Source Software, Software with publicly available source code that can be collaboratively developed and enhanced by a community of developers.

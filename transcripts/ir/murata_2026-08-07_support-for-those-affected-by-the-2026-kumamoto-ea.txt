Support for Those Affected by the 2026 Kumamoto Earthquake

Go to the bottom of the page

8/7/2026

Murata Manufacturing Co., Ltd.

President: Norio Nakajima

The Murata Manufacturing Group extends its deepest condolences to the families and loved ones of those who lost their lives in the 2026 Kumamoto Earthquake and expresses its sincere sympathy to all those affected.

To support those affected and contribute to the recovery and reconstruction of the affected areas, the Group will make a donation of JPY 10 million. The Group will also provide towels, clothing, and other essential supplies to the affected areas.

The Group sincerely hopes for the swift recovery and reconstruction of the affected areas.

Murata in Brief

Murata Manufacturing Co., Ltd. is a worldwide leader in the design, manufacture and sale of ceramic-based passive electronic components & solutions, communication modules and power supply modules. Murata is committed to the development of advanced electronic materials and leading edge, multi-functional, high-density modules. The company has employees and manufacturing facilities throughout the world.

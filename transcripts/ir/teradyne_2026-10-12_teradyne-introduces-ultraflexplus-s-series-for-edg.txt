Teradyne Introduces UltraFLEXplus S Series for Edge AI Applications

October 12, 2026 9:00am EDT

New Series of Testers Extend the UltraFLEXplus Architecture to Deliver Superior Floor Space Economics for High-Mix, Variable-Volume Manufacturing

NORTH READING, Mass.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--

Teradyne, Inc . (NASDAQ: TER), a leading provider of automated test equipment and advanced robotics, today announced the launch of the UltraFLEXplus S series , an extension of its UltraFLEXplus platform engineered to maximize output per square meter and operational efficiency for high-mix, variable-volume semiconductor manufacturing for edge AI applications. The first model, UltraFLEXplus S24, is a 24-slot system built for both engineering and production use, with particular emphasis on the economics that matter most to integrated device manufacturers (IDMs) and outsourced semiconductor assembly and test (OSAT) providers.

This press release features multimedia. View the full release here: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261012117050/en/

Teradyne UltraFLEXplus S series for edge AI applications extends the UltraFLEXplus architecture to deliver superior floor space economics for high-mix, variable-volume manufacturing.

The single-module UltraFLEXplus S series is positioned alongside the quad-module UltraFLEXplus Q series as part of Teradyne’s scalable UltraFLEXplus platform strategy. As demand grows at the edge, manufacturers face economic constraints, supply chain dynamics, device volumes, and product mixes that differ from those in data center and cloud applications, making cost effectiveness across both capital and operating expenses critical to success. The UltraFLEXplus S delivers an efficient option for these devices built on the same architecture and IG-XL software foundation.

"Floor space is one of the biggest hidden costs in high-mix manufacturing, and UltraFLEXplus S is built to optimize it," said Roy Chorev, vice president and general manager of the Compute Test division at Teradyne. "As AI moves from the data center to the edge, customers need a platform that fits their facilities today and scales with them tomorrow, without giving up the rigor and reliability they already trust from UltraFLEXplus."

Key Features and Benefits

Right-Sized Footprint: A compact system that reduces floor space and facilities overhead without compromising test capability.

Workflow Continuity: Utilizes the same instrument module ecosystem, IG-XL software environment, and test program library as the UltraFLEXplus Q series, allowing customers to reuse existing engineering investment.

Optimized Economics: Engineered to maximize utilization, overall equipment effectiveness (OEE), and output per unit of floor space, the metrics that matter most in high-mix, variable-volume production environments.

Scalable Platform: UltraFLEXplus S24 is the first member of the S series, offering customers an entry point into the UltraFLEXplus platform architecture.

Broad Application Coverage: Supports both engineering and production test insertions, including tri-temperature testing, for microcontrollers (MCUs), multimedia, and edge AI devices across automotive, industrial, and consumer end markets.

With the introduction of UltraFLEXplus S series, Teradyne extends the reach of its proven UltraFLEXplus architecture to tackle emerging edge applications. By leveraging existing test development infrastructure, such as test program libraries, and the IG-XL software environment, UltraFLEXplus S supports Teradyne’s continued investment in helping customers scale test operations while improving engineering efficiency and quality to meet the evolving market demand.

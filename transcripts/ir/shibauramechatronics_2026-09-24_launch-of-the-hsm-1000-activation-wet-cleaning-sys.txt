SHIBAURA MECHATRONICS CORPORATION

Launch of the HSM-1000, Activation & Wet Cleaning System for Hybrid & Fusion

Process in Advanced Packaging

Shibaura Mechatronics Corporation has launched the HSM-1000, activation & wet cleaning system

for hybrid & fusion process in advanced packaging.

This product is a system for performing the pre-bond surface treatment processes required for

hybrid bonding and fusion bonding. Adoption of hybrid bonding is expanding in advanced

semiconductor packages for logic semiconductors and memory devices used in high-performance

servers, generative AI, and high-performance PCs. Adoption is also expanding in image sensors.

In the field of Advanced Semiconductor Packages, hybrid bonding, a bumpless bonding

technology, is attracting increasing attention against the backdrop of the miniaturization limits of

microbumps. To ensure bonding quality, multiple pre-bonding processes, including plasma

activation, cleaning, and rinsing, are required, and there is growing demand for equipment that

integrates these processes as well as for one-stop solutions that cover bonding processes.

The HSM-1000 is a system that combines the vacuum plasma technology, cleaning technology, and

automation technology that we have cultivated over many years, and enables the pre-bond surface

treatment processes required for hybrid bonding to be carried out on a single system.

Combined with our hybrid bonders, the HSM-1000 provides a total solution covering the entire

process from pre-bond surface treatment to bonding. Through this solution, we aim to further

expand our business in the advanced semiconductor packaging market.

Nidec Corporation

Released on September 28, 2026, in Kyoto, Japan

Nidec to Establish a Software Technology Development Center

- To consolidate the entire Nidec Group’s supervisory function and reorganize its Indian business base to shift

toward software-oriented solution business -

Nidec Corporation (the “Company” or “we”)  has announced today that it will establish a Software Technology

Development Center (the “Center”), a facility to be under direct control of the Company’s Chief Technology Officer

(“CTO”), on October 01, 2026.

Background and purpose of launching the Center

As products become more sophisticated, software development is becoming increasingly important in recent years .

Nidec is establishing this Center, which will integrate the entire Nidec Group’s software, to further add value to the

Group’s products in the areas of focus and elsewhere, and to  accelerate its structural shift from the conventional

component-supplying business to a module and solution enterprise.

In addition, as part of the aforementioned strategy, NIDEC ADV ANCE TECHNOLOGY INDIA PRIV A TE LIMITED,

a business base of Nidec Advance Technology, a subsidiary of the Company, in Bangalore, Karnataka, India, will be

transferred to be under the Company’s direct control.  After being renamed NIDEC INDIA SOFTW ARE CENTER

PRIV A TE LIMITED on November 01, 2026 (plan), the Indian facility will, with the Center ’s promotion, have its

function enhanced as a global site to lead the entire Nidec Group’s software development.

By building this unified management system, Nidec will optimize the allocation of the Nidec Group’s internal software

personnel and intellectual properties, prevent technology leakage, enable efficient development, and secure excellent

quality.

Outlook of the Center

- Address: 2-8, Shin-Kawasaki, Saiwai-ku, Kawasaki-shi, Kanagawa (in Nidec Shin-Kawasaki Technology Center)

- Director: Hironori Wada

- The Center’s main businesses will inclide:

1. Create high-value-added solutions: Utilize software technology to sophisticate products and offer solutions.

2. Governance and consolidated resource management: Intregated management of the Nidec Group’s internal

software personnel and intellectual propertires and the cross-sectional supervision of quality - and security-

related risks

3. Plan and develop development platforms : Build a group -wide common software platform and a next -

generation development process to support Nidec’s next-geenration product portfolio.

4. Optimum use of Nidec’s global business bases: Strengthen ties with NIDEC INDIA SOFTW ARE CENTER

PRIV A TE LIMITED to be less dependent on external resources, prevent technology leakage, and optimize

development cost.

ROG celebrates 20 years at Gamescom 2026, showcasing its evolution from a gaming hardware brand into a broader gaming ecosystem.

Edition 20 collection featuring the ROG Xbox Ally X20, ROG G1000 Edition 20, and ROG SLASH Edition 20 products highlights two decades of gaming innovation.

Esports innovation with the ROG Gladius IV Ace and ROG Ace OLED esports monitors reinforces ROG’s focus on competitive gaming.

The gaming community is bringing together fans, creators, esports teams, and partners through competitions, live experiences, and special appearances.

Gamescom 2026 in Cologne has wrapped up, capping off a week of gaming, innovation, and community. During the event, ASUS Republic of Gamers (ROG) continued the celebration of its 20th anniversary by inviting gamers, creators, and fans together to experience the latest in gear and tech and discover what’s next for the world of gaming.

Celebrating 20 Years of ROG at Gamescom 2026

The 20th anniversary of ROG was woven throughout the entire Gamescom experience. Visitors were welcomed by larger-than-life installations of the ROG XBOX Ally X20 and the ROG Zephyrus Duo, anniversary-themed installations, interactive experiences, and showcases that highlighted both the brand's heritage and its future ambitions.

Additionally, ROG showcased its Edition 20 collection, a series of special products featuring a distinctive black-and-gold design language inspired by two decades of gaming innovation. Alongside these limited-edition releases, visitors explored new gaming technologies, hands-on demonstrations, esports experiences, and community activities that reflected how ROG has grown far beyond its roots as a PC hardware brand.

The ROG XBOX Ally X20 Bundle

Event highlights

Among the highlights at the booth was the Edition 20 collection , created to commemorate ROG's 20th anniversary. The collection demonstrates how ROG has evolved from a gaming hardware brand into a broader ecosystem encompassing technology, lifestyle products, collectibles, and community experiences.

Leading the lineup was the ROG XBOX Ally X20 , a special-edition handheld that combined a translucent black chassis with gold-accented internal detailing to celebrate the anniversary. The device quickly became a focal point of the booth, drawing visitors eager to experience the latest evolution of portable gaming.

The Edition 20 collection extends far beyond handheld gaming to include the ROG G1000 Edition 20 desktop , ROG SLASH Hard-Case Luggage Edition 20 , ROG SLASH Backpack Edition 20 , the ROG SAGA OMNI Edition 20 Action Figure , and the new ROG SAGA: In Search of Lapuntu Edition 20 board game.

01 ROG Zephyrus Duo GX651

Another major attraction at the booth was the latest ROG Zephyrus Duo , which offered visitors a glimpse into the future of gaming laptops.

As one of the flagship products showcased during the anniversary celebration, the Zephyrus Duo highlights ROG's continued commitment to innovation in gaming form factors and user experiences as well as reinforcing its drive to continually redefine what's possible in gaming hardware.

Team Vitality CS2 Academy KV

Esports has been an important part of the ROG story since the very beginning, and Gamescom 2026 showcased how that commitment continues to evolve.

Visitors explored the latest esports-focused hardware, including the new ROG Gladius IV Ace gaming mouse and the growing family of ROG Ace OLED esports monitors . Developed with feedback from professional players, these products reflect ROG's philosophy of building competitive gaming gear informed by real-world esports experience.

ROG has also strengthened its esports ecosystem through expanded partnerships, including a new collaboration with GamerLegion , a professional esports organization known for its Counter-Strike competition teams, further reinforcing its commitment to supporting professional players and organizations competing at the highest levels. In addition to new hardware announcements and esports partnerships, ROG brought competitive gaming directly to attendees through dedicated gaming competitions headlined by Team Vitality and livestreamed to fans around the world, demonstrating the brand's continued investment in the esports community and fan engagement.

ROG Swift OLED PG259QWS Ace x Official Monitor of PGL CS2 Major

One of the most significant esports announcements at Gamescom 2026 was the selection of the ROG Esports OLED 720Hz monitor as the official display for the PGL CS2 Major Singapore 2026 , one of Counter-Strike 2’s premier world championship tournaments. The announcement marked an important milestone for competitive gaming. The tournament will become the first Counter-Strike Major ever played on OLED displays, highlighting how display technology continues to advance alongside the needs of professional players . OLED technology delivers exceptional motion clarity, ultra-fast response times, and stronger contrast, qualities that can help competitors react more quickly and maintain visual accuracy in fast-paced matches.

The achievement also reflected ROG's collaborative approach to innovation. Developed with input from esports professionals and tournament organizers, the ROG Esports OLED 720Hz represents the latest example of how feedback from elite competition continues to influence gaming technology for players everywhere.

Gamescom is ultimately about the games, and visitors had no shortage of opportunities to experience upcoming titles on the latest ROG hardware. The hands-on demos brought the excitement of new releases to life while showing how ROG hardware helps deliver immersive gaming experiences.

Across the booth, attendees went hands-on with highly anticipated releases including Minecraft Dungeons II, PRAGMATA, Resident Evil Requiem, and Monster Hunter Stories 3: Twisted Reflection. Other featured titles, including Cosmo Tales, Ascend to Zero, and TANUKI: Pon's Summer, gave visitors the opportunity to discover exciting new experiences before their official launches.

While innovative technology remained a major draw, Gamescom 2026 also demonstrated the strength of the ROG community.

Throughout the week, the booth served as a gathering place for fans from around the world. Visitors participated in activities, giveaways, competitions, and interactive experiences designed to celebrate twenty years of ROG.

Special appearances added even more excitement to the festivities. World-renowned DJ Alan Walker met with fans before delivering a live performance, while actor Ned Luke, known to many gamers for his portrayal of Michael De Santa in Grand Theft Auto V, connected with attendees through special appearances and meet-and-greet sessions.

Meanwhile, OMNI, the iconic ROG mascot, remained one of the booth's most popular attractions as visitors stopped for photos and joined in the anniversary celebration.

ROG also partnered with SpecialEffect on a charity initiative featuring collectible bundles to help support gamers with disabilities, adding a meaningful community component to the Gamescom celebration.

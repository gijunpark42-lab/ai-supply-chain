Impact of Typhoon No. 25 on Our Operations

We would like to express our deepest sympathies to all those affected by Typhoon No. 25, which approached the Kanto region on September 21.

At our Sakura Plant in Sakura City, Chiba Prefecture, we have confirmed the safety of our employees and assessed the condition of our buildings, production facilities, and other equipment.

As of now, no injuries to our employees or damage to the buildings or production facilities at the Sakura Plant have been reported. There has been no impact on production activities, and the plant is operating as usual.

However, as flooding and road closures have occurred in Sakura City and surrounding areas, we are taking necessary measures, including the use of remote work, with the safety of our employees as our highest priority.

We will continue to closely monitor the situation and conduct our business operations while giving the highest priority to ensuring the safety of our employees.

Lenovo’s expanded services portfolio helps organizations realize value from Hybrid AI Factories in as little as 90 days

With the launch of Lenovo AI Express, organizations have a faster path to validated, right-sized AI infrastructure.

30 September 2026

With the launch of Lenovo AI Express, organizations have a faster path to validated, right-sized AI infrastructure. But the customer journey to AI outcomes doesn’t start or end with capacity alone. Lenovo’s advisory, implementation and support services help organizations realize more value from their hybrid AI factories — from identifying high-value use cases and accelerating deployment to validating ROI and supporting ongoing optimization as demands evolve.

Organizations are increasingly focused on moving AI initiatives from experimentation to scaled execution. Yet long lead times, integration complexity and readiness barriers can delay operational deployment and business outcomes.

The newly launched Lenovo AI Express was designed to help customers overcome these challenges by providing an accelerated onramp to the Lenovo Hybrid AI Factory . The quick-start program gives customers rapid access to validated, right-sized infrastructure that can be configured along with a choice of enterprise AI software and services to align with customer workloads and budgets. With accelerated order-to-ship delivery in as little as 15 business days 1 , customers can scale as models, agents, users, data and inference demands grow.

But infrastructure is part of the much broader customer journey to putting AI to work successfully. Organizations must identify the right use-case opportunities, establish a path to ROI, reduce deployment complexity, implement and utilize GPU resources effectively, optimize performance, and maintain continuity as AI becomes more deeply embedded across the business.

Lenovo services address these requirements across the AI lifecycle.

Start with a clear path to value

A successful AI deployment begins with identifying the use cases worth pursuing and defining how results will be measured. That clarity is critical to optimizing infrastructure to transform workflows in ways that directly influence business outcomes.

Lenovo AI Discover helps customers identify and evaluate expected value of AI use cases, define how results will be measured, and build a practical implementation roadmap aligned with business needs.

Lenovo AI Fast Start helps customers develop and remotely deploy AI solutions and agents using their own data, for a fully working pilot in as little as 90 days . This gives teams a concrete way to advance prioritized use cases and evaluate them in the context of their own information and requirements.

Match deployment expertise to the customer’s environment

Deploying AI successfully extends beyond infrastructure delivery. Systems must be configured, integrated, validated and prepared for production while minimizing disruption to ongoing operations. Customers need a path from purchase to production that balances speed, precision, and confidence.

Lenovo Factory Integrated Services can prepare eligible infrastructure before it leaves the factory. Depending on the selected configuration, services can include rack integration, RAID setup, firmware and software settings, custom image loading, asset tagging, and documentation. Completing this work in a controlled factory environment helps reduce onsite effort, improve consistency, and identify configuration issues before equipment arrives on site.

After delivery, Hardware Installation Services provide certified Lenovo technicians for onsite physical installation and verification. Technicians can rack and cable eligible systems and complete defined installation checks, helping reduce the burden on internal IT teams and confirm that infrastructure has been installed correctly.

When customers need more than physical installation, Premier Deploy for Infrastructure provides a defined path from readiness to production for supported ThinkSystem servers, ThinkSystem storage arrays, and ThinkAgile solutions. With Standard Deploy , Premier Deploy , and Premier Deploy Plus , customers can match the right level of deployment support for their environment based on project complexity, internal IT capabilities, and needs after go-live. Depending on the selected approach and platform, support can extend through installation, integration, validation, documentation, knowledge transfer, and post-deployment assistance.

Turn AI capacity into operational performance

Once the environment is live, the priority shifts to using that capacity efficiently and optimizing performance for AI, HPC and other GPU-intensive workloads.

Lenovo GPU Advanced Services provide specialized expertise to help customers plan, configure and manage GPU environments, aligning infrastructure with evolving workload requirements. Depending on the selected service, Lenovo can provide ongoing optimization to help customers continue to realize value from their infrastructure investment after deployment, improve resource utilization and efficiency, accelerate workload performance, and scale effectively as demand grows.

Protect continuity as AI becomes operational

As AI environments move into regular operation, infrastructure reliability and responsive technical support become critical. Issues can interrupt workloads, consume internal resources, and cause delays.

Lenovo Premier Support for Infrastructure gives customers direct access to Lenovo experts, prioritized response and end-to-end case ownership across supported server and storage environments. Coordinated hardware and software troubleshooting reduces unnecessary handoffs and simplifies support through one accountable partner.

Premier Support Plus for Infrastructure extends this model with proactive and predictive capabilities designed to identify potential issues earlier across eligible server and storage environments. AI-driven analytics, automated case creation, proactive parts dispatch, and designated Service Engagement Manager oversight help customers identify and address potential issues earlier to reduce the risk of operational disruption and maintain greater visibility into ongoing service needs.

Technical Account Manager services provide ongoing technical guidance and service planning beyond individual support incidents as requirements change.

Realize value and scale as requirements grow

As organizations reinvent their workflows with AI, they need a full-stack approach to realize value from their AI investments. With Lenovo AI Express as the starting point, customers have an accelerated path to the Lenovo Hybrid AI Factory and Lenovo Hybrid AI Advantage™ , Lenovo’s full-stack approach and services for putting AI to work across the business.

Lenovo Hybrid AI Services enables customers to move faster from AI ambition to impact with a portfolio of advisory, implementation and managed services, supporting customers across the AI lifecycle with everything from planning and deployment to ongoing operations.

Lenovo AI Library provides a curated catalogue of enterprise-ready, validated AI use cases and agents that are customizable and governed by design, helping organizations realize up to 24x faster time to value 2 .

Support for the complete AI journey

The launch of Lenovo AI Express gives organizations a faster, more predictable way to acquire validated AI infrastructure and begin building the capacity their workloads demand. But the complete customer journey doesn’t end there. With Lenovo’s expanded services portfolio, customers have a structured, supported path for planning, deploying, operating, and scaling AI to meet business needs and realize more value from their investments.

1 For eligible Lenovo AI Express configurations and selected parts in non-restrictive markets. Clock starts after completion of order validation, payment/credit clearance, End User Certification (EUC), and applicable due diligence/export approvals.

2 Based on Signal65’s independent assessment of custom enterprise AI agent development, informed by industry estimates that place custom build timelines at 3–6 months for business-grade solutions, with enterprise deployments often extending beyond that range.

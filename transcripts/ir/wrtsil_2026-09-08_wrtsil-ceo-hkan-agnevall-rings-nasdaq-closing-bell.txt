Wärtsilä CEO Håkan Agnevall rings Nasdaq Closing Bell to celebrate growth in the U.S.

Wärtsilä Corporation,

News

8 September 2026 at 15:00 UTC+2

Wärtsilä's President and CEO, Håkan Agnevall, will ring the Nasdaq Closing Bell in New York today, 8 September 2026, marking the company's continued growth in the U.S. market.

The continued electrification of industries and the growth of AI and data centres are driving an unprecedented power demand. Wärtsilä is helping customers secure reliable, efficient and affordable energy while supporting long-term growth and competitiveness. “After half a century of serving the U.S., customers continue to trust us, and we see tremendous opportunities ahead. Our U.S. workforce is twice the size it was at the end of the last decade and will grow significantly in the coming years. We are committed to being a long-term partner in America’s energy future”, Håkan Agnevall states.

The Nasdaq Closing Bell ceremony can be viewed live via Nasdaq's website and selected financial media channels from 4:00 p.m. EDT (UTC-4) / 11:00 p.m. EEST.

A live stream of the ceremony is available here: https://www.nasdaq.com/marketsite/bell-ringing-ceremony

A recording of the event will be available after the ceremony in Nasdaq's archive: https://www.nasdaq.com/news-and-insights/nasdaq-stock-market-bell-ceremonies

Wärtsilä in brief

Wärtsilä is a global leader in innovative technologies and lifecycle solutions for the marine and energy markets. We emphasise innovation in sustainable technology and services to help our customers continuously improve environmental and economic performance. Our dedicated and passionate team of 17,900 professionals in 199 locations in 78 countries shape the decarbonisation transformation of our industries across the globe. In 2025, Wärtsilä’s net sales totalled EUR 6.2 billion. Wärtsilä is listed on Nasdaq Helsinki. www.wartsila.com

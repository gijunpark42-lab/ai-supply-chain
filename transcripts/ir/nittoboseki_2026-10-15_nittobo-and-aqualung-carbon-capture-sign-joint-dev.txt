Nittobo and Aqualung Carbon Capture sign joint development agreement on

advanced materials for CO₂ separation membranes

―Partnership pairs Nittobo’s polymer chemistry and manufacturing

expertise with Aqualung’s membrane technology to support the scale-up of

membrane-based carbon capture―

Nitto Boseki Co., Ltd. (“Nittobo”) and Aqualung Carbon Capture AS (“Aqualung”), a Norwegian

developer of membrane-based carbon dioxide (CO₂) capture technology, and today announced

that they have signed a joint research and development agreement to develop advanced

polymer materials for CO₂ separation membranes.

With global warming making the development of efficient CO₂ separation and capture

technologies from air and flue gas an urgent priority, both companies are uniting in a strong

partnership to drive innovative technology development aimed at resolving this pressing

societal challenge.

Under the agreement, the two companies will jointly develop and manufacture new polymer

materials for use in CO₂ separation membranes and assess their suitability for industrial-scale

production. Nittobo will contribute its experience in polymer chemistry and specialty chemical

manufacturing. Aqualung will contribute its expertise in membrane development and CO₂

separation performance testing.

The collaboration supports Aqualung’s work to scale membrane-based capture for industrial

emitters, and Nittobo’s focus on materials for environment and energy applications.

Saravanan Janakiram, Chief Technology Officer at Aqualung, said: “Materials are at the

heart of membrane performance. Nittobo’s depth in polymer chemistry, and its long

experience making specialty chemicals at industrial scale, make it an excellent partner as we

develop the next generation of our membranes. We look forward to working closely with the

Nittobo team.”

Shotaro Yasuda, Executive Officer, Division General Manager of the Material Solution

Business Division at Nittobo, said: “We are delighted to partner with Aqualung—a specialist

group possessing deep expertise in CO₂ separation membranes—to apply our proprietary

polymer synthesis technology to the field of CO₂ capture. This collaboration reflects our vision

and commitment to the environment and energy sectors, and we look forward to supporting

the development efforts of Aqualung, a true innovator in the field.”

The companies will share further updates as the collaboration progresses.

About Aqualung Carbon Capture

Aqualung Carbon Capture AS is a Norwegian technology company developing membrane-

based solutions for capturing and separating CO₂ from industrial gas streams. Headquartered

in Høvik, near Oslo, Aqualung works with industrial partners to demonstrate and deploy its

technology in hard-to-abate sectors. For more information, visit www.aqualung-cc.com.

About Nittobo

Founded in 1923 and headquartered in Fukushima, Japan, Nitto Boseki Co., Ltd. (Nittobo) is a

specialty materials company operating globally across glass fiber materials, functional and

specialty chemicals, and in vitro diagnostics. The company is listed on the Prime Market of the

Tokyo Stock Exchange (securities code: 3110). For more information, please visit

https://www.nittobo.co.jp/eng/.

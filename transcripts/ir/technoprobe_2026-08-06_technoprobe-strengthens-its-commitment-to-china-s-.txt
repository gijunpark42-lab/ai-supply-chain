Technoprobe strengthens its commitment to China’s semiconductor market: introducing Nexprobe

We are pleased to announce that, in response to the strong and rapidly growing demand for high-performance probe cards in the Chinese market, Technoprobe Group has decided to officially launch Nexprobe ( 鼎探芯 ) , a dedicated sub-brand for China, in July 2026. At the same time, we are establishing our second probe card manufacturing facility in Suzhou, further strengthening our local presence and enabling us to respond more effectively to customers’ requirements for shorter lead times, advanced technological performance and reliable local support.

Nexprobe will build on Technoprobe’s decades of experience, engineering excellence and extensive portfolio of proprietary technologies and patents. These intellectual-property assets underpin probe card solutions adopted by many of the world’s leading semiconductor companies and represent a key source of Technoprobe’s technological differentiation and competitive advantage.

By combining this globally proven technology platform with local manufacturing capabilities, application expertise and close customer collaboration, Nexprobe will bring advanced and highly reliable testing solutions closer to the Chinese market.

We are confident that Nexprobe will deliver highly competitive solutions tailored to the evolving requirements of China’s semiconductor ecosystem, while maintaining the high standards of innovation, quality and performance for which Technoprobe is recognized worldwide. Through this initiative, we aim not only to serve our customers more rapidly and effectively, but also to contribute actively to the technological advancement and sustainable growth of China’s semiconductor industry.

We sincerely appreciate your continued trust, support and partnership as we embark on this important new chapter.

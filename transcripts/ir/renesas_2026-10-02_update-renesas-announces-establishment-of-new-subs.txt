October 2, 2026

TOKYO, Japan — Renesas Electronics Corporation (TSE: 6723), a premier supplier of advanced semiconductor solutions, today finalized certain matters that were left undecided in its disclosure " Renesas Announces Establishment of New Subsidiary and Changes as Specified Subsidiary " dated July 25, 2025. Details of the specified subsidiary (the “New Subsidiary”) are as follows, with the updated items underlined.

1. Overview of the New Subsidiary

(1)

Company name

Re-Alt Software Holdings Inc.

(2)

Address

Delaware, United States

(3)

Representative

Director and President: Aris Bolisay

(4)

Type of business

Holding company related to Software & Digitalization business

(5)

Capital

1 US$

(6)

Date of establishment

Mid-October 2026

(7)

Major shareholder and ownership ratio

Renesas Electronics Corporation

100%

(8)

Relationship between Renesas and the New Subsidiary

Capital relationship

The New Subsidiary is a wholly owned subsidiary of Renesas.

Personal relationship

One Renesas employee will be appointed as a Director and President of the New Subsidiary.

Transactional relationship

There is currently no relevant transactional relationship.

2. Future Outlook

There is no material impact on Renesas' consolidated financial results for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2026.

BizLink Completes Acquisition of Interplex Datacom, Expanding Global Market Presence and Deepening Customer Partnerships

September 23, 2026

Table of contents

BizLink Holding Inc. (TWSE: 3665) (“BizLink”), a global leader in interconnect solutions, today announced the successful completion of its acquisition of Interplex Datacom, previously a division of Ennovi, a portfolio company managed by Blackstone Inc. (NYSE: BX) and its affiliates (“Blackstone”). The all-cash transaction was completed at an enterprise value of US$850 million, plus up to US$50 million in contingent consideration.

Combining Complementary Technologies for AI Infrastructure

Interplex Datacom’s expertise in the mechanical and structural components that form the physical architecture of AI server racks complements BizLink’s strengths in the electrical and connectivity dimensions. Bringing these capabilities together further strengthens BizLink’s position as a “one-stop-shop” provider of integrated data center solutions, with a more comprehensive offering to address increasingly complex AI infrastructure requirements.

The combination of the two businesses’ manufacturing depth and R&D expertise is expected to strengthen BizLink’s ability to anticipate customer needs, shorten development cycles and co-create solutions at speed. Interplex Datacom’s manufacturing footprint and established customer relationships across Asia further support BizLink’s ability to scale alongside its customers as demand for AI infrastructure continues to grow.

The acquisition also strengthens BizLink’s precision metal fabrication capabilities, creating opportunities to develop more complex, higher-value-added products for the businesses’ shared customer base. These expanded capabilities will support BizLink’s existing semiconductor enclosure, medical equipment and system integration assembly businesses.

The completion marks another strategic milestone in BizLink’s 30-year history, reinforcing its data center connectivity and infrastructure business while creating new opportunities for long-term growth and customer collaboration.

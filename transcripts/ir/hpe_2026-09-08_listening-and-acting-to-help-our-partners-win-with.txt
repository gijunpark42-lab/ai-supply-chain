Listening and acting to help our partners win with HPE

September 8, 2026 | Simon Ewington, Senior Vice President, Worldwide Channel and Partner Ecosystem, HPE

Blog Post Partner Focus Exec Authored

HPE is listening and acting to help partners move faster, compete confidently and maximise margins

In this article

HPE enhances competitiveness across partner-led business in Q3

Pricing and program changes are helping partners pursue opportunities with greater confidence

Expanded channel-only offerings are helping partners build expertise, differentiate and unlock more profitable growth

Partners are at the centre of HPE’s growth strategy. They bring our innovation closer to customers, help organisations modernise with confidence, and create the reach, expertise and trusted relationships needed to turn technology investments into outcomes.

That is why Q3 was about action. In a dynamic supply environment, partners told us they needed greater speed, simplicity and predictability. We listened, and we moved quickly to enhance competitiveness across our partner-led business. HPE announced Q3 earnings [last week], and the continued momentum in our partner business is impressive.

Innovation: Positioning HPE in high-growth markets

Innovation only matters when partners can turn it into customer value. In Q3, our focus was to help partners respond quickly to customer demand, compete with confidence and bring more compelling proposals to market.

A clear example is the removal of repricing terms on transactions below $1 million for both direct and indirect business which simplified a significant portion of partner-led opportunities and gave our partners more confidence in the pricing they take to customers.

That change builds on the actions we had already introduced following HPE Discover Las Vegas 2026, which included setting a 30-day quote validity for new compute, storage and GreenLake Flex quotes.

Customers are moving fast, and partners need the ability to shape opportunities without uncertainty slowing them down.

This is what innovation must look like in practice: a strong portfolio and a route for partners to position that portfolio, create relevance and win.

We will continue to communicate openly with our partner community as market conditions evolve. Supply and cost dynamics remain fluid across the industry, and future actions may still be required. But our direction is clear: we will act responsibly, provide as much stability as possible, and keep partners in a strong position to support customers.

Expertise: Helping partners move faster and differentiate

Competitiveness is not only about price, but also comes from expertise. When our partners understand customer priorities, they can design the right solution and move quickly from opportunity to outcome.

Through HPE Partner Ready Vantage, we are helping partners build the skills and confidence to lead in high-growth areas. This has been on full display in Q3 as partners take advantage of our expanded channel-only routes to market in HPE Private Cloud PC3000, HPE SimpliVity PC1000, and HPE Zerto Software.

Our VM Essentials for partner IT offering also enables partners to run the software in their own IT environments and build hands-on expertise with VM Essentials software licenses provided free of charge for three years with partners paying only support costs.

Our actions mean that no matter the opportunity, our partners can move beyond transactional selling and become strategic advisors to customers.

Margin: Creating more predictable paths to profitable growth

The final priority is margin. Partners invest in HPE because they see the opportunity to build a profitable business around our portfolio, our programs and our experience. In Q3, our actions were designed to protect momentum, help partners pursue time-sensitive opportunities and create stronger economics for their business.

Margin also comes from the ability to sell across the full portfolio, attach services and expand into new areas of customer demand. When innovation, expertise and margin come together, partners can build more relevant solutions, differentiate more clearly and grow more profitably with HPE.

My message to partners is simple: we are listening, we are acting and we are continuing to invest in making HPE easy to work with and more competitive. We will keep focusing on the actions that help partners move faster, build expertise and unlock profitable growth for our mutual customers.

OpenAI and Google Cloud ⁠ (opens in a new window) are partnering to bring OpenAI’s frontier models and Codex ⁠ (opens in a new window) agentic workflows into Google Cloud’s Model Garden. The integration will give customers native access to these models to power complex agentic workflows at scale, backed by Google Cloud’s data governance and high-performance infrastructure.

Google Cloud’s longstanding investment in machine learning and AI has made it a destination for AI workloads and the developers and data scientists building them. Bringing OpenAI models to its model garden gives these teams access to OpenAI’s frontier models within the environment where they already work, enabling enterprises to modernize their software development lifecycle, build agentic workflows, and change how people work.

For organizations in regulated industries, deploying AI requires control over who can access sensitive data and how it is used. The integration will pair OpenAI’s frontier models with Google Cloud’s built-in access controls, centralized data governance, and high-performance infrastructure to support demanding enterprise workloads.

Google Cloud customers will be able to purchase OpenAI frontier models via model garden, transacted through the Google Cloud Marketplace. Enterprises with Google Cloud spend commitments will be able to draw down their GCP commitments for these purchases.

Additionally, OpenAI models will soon be available in Google Antigravity and the Gemini Agent, providing enterprise customers with model choice flexibility and the best model for each task. By expanding access through Google Cloud, we are giving enterprises greater choice in how they discover and use frontier models, with a path from evaluation to broader adoption.

“ Google Cloud is a leading platform for developers and businesses building AI agents, writing software, and putting AI to work. This partnership is an important step in making our most capable models and Codex available where customers are already building, giving them even more choice in how they use frontier AI as we grow the agentic ecosystem together. ” —Greg Brockman, President and co-founder, OpenAI

“ We are focused on giving customers a broad selection of models, including those from OpenAI, so they can choose the right model for any given job, helping to deliver optimal quality and lower costs. ” —Thomas Kurian, Chief Executive Officer, Google Cloud

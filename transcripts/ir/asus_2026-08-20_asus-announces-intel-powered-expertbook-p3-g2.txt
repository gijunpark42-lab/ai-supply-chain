ASUS Announces Intel-Powered ExpertBook P3 G2

August 20, 2026

・

Press Release

Exceptional performance, dependable efficiency, and business-grade security in a sleek, portable 14" or 16" laptop design

Crafted for productivity: Productivity is boosted with Intel® Core™ 7 Series 3 processor for faster and more responsive performance

On-the-go workflow: Built for the workday, combining long battery life, fast charging, and durable design for portable productivity

ASUS ExpertGuardian: Protected by ASUS ExpertGuardian, including a NIST SP 800-155-compliant BIOS, Windows 11 Secured-core technologies

TAIPEI, Taiwan, August 20, 2026 — ASUS today announced ExpertBook P3 G2, a laptop designed for modern professionals who demand both performance and day-long comfort. Powered by up to an Intel® Core™ 7 Series 3 processor, ExpertBook P3 G2 delivers fast, responsive performance to help users stay productive throughout the workday, whether tackling everyday business tasks in the office or on the move.

Built for all-day productivity, ExpertBook P3 G2 combines long battery life, fast charging, and durable design to support seamless workflows wherever work takes users. Protected by ASUS ExpertGuardian NIST SP 800-155 compliant BIOS and Windows 11 Secured-core technologies, it safeguards privacy and business data while delivering dependable protection for today’s modern workstyles.

Available in two sizes — 14-inch (P3406CMA) and 16-inch (P3606CMA) — the latest ExpertBook P3 G2 combines advanced connectivity and business-ready security in a sleek, professional design. Its Misty Grey chassis meets MIL-STD-810H military-grade durability standards, ensuring reliability in demanding work environments. ExpertBook P3 G2 also features an enlarged touchpad for improved workflow efficiency and a refined keyboard with 1.5mm key travel for a more comfortable typing experience, making it an ideal choice for professionals, educators, and hybrid workers seeking dependable performance.

Crafted for productivity

ExpertBook P3 G2 is built to keep pace with demanding workflows. Powered by up to an Intel Core 7 Series 3 processor and equipped with high-speed DDR5 memory and a PCIe® 4.0 SSD, it delivers fast, responsive performance for multitasking, data-intensive tasks, and daily business operations.

Advanced thermal architecture ensures efficient heat dissipation, maintaining stable performance even during extended use. Whether handling large files, running multiple applications or switching between tasks, ExpertBook P3 G2 offers a consistently smooth and reliable experience.

Smooth workflows, maximum efficiency

ExpertBook P3 G2 is thoughtfully engineered for smooth workflows and maximum efficiency, with user-centric features such as a spacious touchpad for precise control and a comfortable keyboard layout optimized for extended use. Dedicated shortcut keys simplify common tasks such as video conferencing, helping users stay focused and productive throughout the day.

With support for multiple high-resolution external displays, users can easily expand their workspace and tailor their setup to match their workflow — whether for multitasking, content creation or data analysis.

ASUS ExpertGuardian for robust business defense

ASUS ExpertGuardian delivers comprehensive, commercial-grade security to protect businesses from all angles. With a NIST SP 800-155-compliant BIOS, five years of ASUS BIOS and driver updates, and TPM 2.0 protection, ExpertGuardian ensures hardware and software protection that shields both privacy and safeguards critical data.

Additional features like a nano Kensington lock slot and a free one-year McAfee+ Premium membership enhance data security, allowing businesses to focus on growth without worry. With ASUS ExpertGuardian, organizations of all sizes are assured seamless, best-in-class security at every level, empowering them to focus on what matters most.

ASUS ExpertBook P3 G2 is engineered with MIL-STD-810H durability, and is backed by flexible ASUS Business Support services, providing the assurance of worry-free long-term operation and rapid assistance — should it ever be needed.

ASUS Business Support

Understanding the critical needs of modern professionals, ASUS Business Support is not merely a warranty — it’s a comprehensive service package that includes on-site repairs, dedicated technical assistance and 24/7 customer support. This robust support framework ensures that every ExpertBook user experience minimal downtime and receives personalized solutions to their technical issues.

Amkor Technology and NVIDIA Advance Strategic Partnership for AI Infrastructure

New $1.3 billion agreement supports further expansion in South Korea , building on the companies’ recent Arizona collaboration

TEMPE, Ariz. --(BUSINESS WIRE)--Oct. 21, 2026--

Amkor Technology, Inc. (Nasdaq: AMKR), a leading provider of outsourced semiconductor packaging and test services, today announced additional investments in South Korea for capacity expansion initiatives with NVIDIA. With this latest commitment, Amkor’s multi-year agreements with NVIDIA in Arizona and South Korea total $3 billion . The initiatives support growing demand for next-generation AI and accelerated computing platforms.

Advanced packaging technologies are essential to enabling the performance, power efficiency and integration required for next-generation computing systems. The prepayments align Amkor's manufacturing capacity with NVIDIA’s long-term growth plans and support increasing demand for advanced packaging solutions.

“Our partnership with NVIDIA underscores the critical role advanced packaging plays in enabling next-generation AI platforms,” said Kevin Engel , president and chief executive officer of Amkor Technology . “These investments build on our global manufacturing footprint and strengthen our ability to support customers through our operations in both the United States and South Korea.”

“Growing demand for accelerated computing is increasing the need for advanced packaging capacity,” said Debora Shoquist , executive vice president, operations of NVIDIA. “Our work with Amkor helps us scale production of current and next-generation platforms while strengthening the resilience of our global supply chain.”

This collaboration further strengthens Amkor’s role as a strategic manufacturing partner for NVIDIA and reinforces the companies’ shared commitment to supporting the continued expansion of AI infrastructure.

Amkor will also exhibit at NVIDIA GTC Washington, D.C. from Nov. 30 - Dec. 3 , where the company will showcase its advanced packaging and test capabilities and engage with customers, policymakers and industry leaders.

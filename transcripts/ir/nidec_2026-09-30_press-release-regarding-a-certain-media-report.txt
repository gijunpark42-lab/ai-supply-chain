Press Release Regarding A Certain Media Report

With respect to yesterday ’s report by the Nihon Keizai Shimbun  digital edition newspaper on Nidec Corporation

(TSE: 6594; OTC US: NJDCY) (“Nidec” or the “Company”) planning to divest Nidec Components Corporation, a

wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, to The Carlyle Group for more than 100 billion yen, while it is true that

Nidec is contemplating the divestiture, it has not been decided by the Company at this point.

The Company will promptly announce any matter that should be disclosed when it occurs.

Invited Presentation on Seed-Layer Deposition Technology for TGV Formation in Advanced Packaging at IMPACT-IAAC 2026

ULVAC, Inc. will deliver an invited presentation at the 21st International Microsystems, Packaging, Assembly and Circuits Technology Conference (IMPACT-IAAC 2026), one of the largest international conferences in the field of semiconductor packaging in Taiwan. In addition, ULVAC will serve as a session chair in the ICEP Session, organized in collaboration with ICEP, an international conference on advanced packaging technologies in Japan.

IMPACT-IAAC 2026 is an international conference on advanced packaging technologies organized by IEEE-EPS Taipei, iMAPS Taiwan, ITRI, and TPCA. This year's conference will be co-hosted with the iMAPS All Asia Conference (IAAC), bringing together key leaders and experts in advanced packaging from across Asia.

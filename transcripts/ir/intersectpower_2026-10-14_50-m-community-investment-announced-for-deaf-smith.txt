$ 50 M Community Investment Announced for Deaf Smith, Roberts, and Gray Counties in Texas

Published

14 Oct 2026

Type

Press releases

Media Alert

Establishes permanent legacy funds and sustained annual support for local organizations in the Meitner and Roman Energy Center communities.

Intersect and Google have committed $ 50 million in long-term community investment across Deaf Smith, Gray, and Roberts Counties, home of the Meitner and Roman Energy Centers. The investment ensures benefits to the local communities for generations.

This $ 50 million commitment establishes permanent endowed funds intended for sustained, long-term community development and civic programs across the three counties. Specific funds are also allocated for grants associated with community priorities and sustained support of local events, sponsorships, and programs. This additional investment builds on Intersect and Google’s longstanding history of supporting local communities where they operate.

The funds will be overseen in partnership with the Amarillo Area Foundation. A dedicated community advisory council comprising local residents and civic leaders will be formed to direct the grants, disbursements, and ensure transparent governance and local alignment to community priorities.

A Community Needs Assessment will be conducted throughout the fall and winter to guide the council’s funding strategy ahead of the first official grant cycle in early 2027 .

​ “ Google is committed to being a supportive partner in the communities where we build, and that means investing in their long term future,” said Austin Hutchinson, Community Development Manager at Google. ​ “ These funds put decision-making directly into our communities and the organizations doing the important work.”

BizLink Receives Lam Research 2026 Supplier Excellence Award

August 21, 2026

Table of contents

FREMONT, Calif., August 21, 2026—BizLink today announced it has been awarded the 2026 Supplier Excellence Award winner by Lam Research, a global provider of innovative wafer fabrication equipment and services to the semiconductor industry. One of 14 suppliers recognized this year, BizLink was selected for its outstanding operational standards, consistent performance, and close collaboration.

Lam’s award-winning suppliers were selected for leadership and excellence across a range of performance categories:

Leadership in Growth Enablement

Leadership in Operational Execution

Leadership in Quality

Leadership in Responsible Supply Chain

Leadership in Value Creation

New Product Enablement

BizLink received the New Product Enablement award for its strategic foresight and exceptional velocity across its value chain. By anticipating future requirements and working seamlessly within Lam's dynamic development ecosystem, BizLink enabled faster technology transitions to support advanced chip manufacturing.

“We are deeply honored to receive this recognition from Lam Research,” said Felix Teng, Chief Executive Officer of BizLink. “This award reinforces our commitment to driving continuous innovation, engineering agility, and strong strategic alignment with Lam Research as we advance semiconductor manufacturing technology together.”

“We congratulate BizLink on winning Lam’s Supplier Excellence Award for their leading performance in the New Product Enablement category,” said Karthik Rammohan, senior vice president and head of global operations and enterprise solutions, Lam Research. “As AI accelerates innovation and increases demand throughout the semiconductor ecosystem, close collaboration with our suppliers is essential to maintaining the agility, resilience, and scale our customers depend on.”

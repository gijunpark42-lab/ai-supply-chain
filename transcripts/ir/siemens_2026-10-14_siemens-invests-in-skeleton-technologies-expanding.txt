Press Release 14 October 2026 Smart Infrastructure Munich

Siemens invests in Skeleton Technologies

expanding its data center portfolio

Strategic investment and exclusive partnership expand Siemens’ position in the fast-growing AI factories space

Siemens will integrate Skeleton’s supercapacitor technology into its end-to-end electrical infrastructure portfolio

Global partnership gives Siemens exclusivity to sell and scale Skeleton’s products in the data center market. The new product range expands Siemens’ offering from the grid connection to the AI rack

Siemens is expanding its data center

portfolio through a strategic investment and exclusive partnership with

Skeleton Technologies. Skeleton Technologies is a developer and manufacturer of

high-power energy storage systems, specializing in supercapacitors to manage

transient power spikes and peak shaving solutions. The partnership strengthens

Siemens’ position in the fast-growing data centers space by adding

high-performance supercapacitor technology to its electrical infrastructure

portfolio. As a leading technology company, Siemens supports customers across

the entire data center ecosystem from the utility connection to the white space

and is a trusted partner relied upon by nine of the top ten global data center

providers.

Siemens has been supporting Skeleton Technologies as a technology partner

since 2022 and has also been involved as an investor since 2023. With this,

Siemens is continuously strengthening its role in the AI infrastructure

ecosystem.

People from left to right: Barry Powell, EVP and Managing Director,

Electrical Products North America, Siemens Industry Inc.; Taavi Madiberk, CEO

and co-founder of Skeleton Technologies; Markus Grabmeier, President of

Electrical Products at Siemens AG

The new offering addresses a central

constraint on the expansion of data centers: massive, rapid and recurring

swings in electricity demand. AI workloads synchronize thousands of powerful

chips, whose power demand can rise or fall within milliseconds and aggregate

into swings at megawatt scale in seconds.

Skeleton’s supercapacitors solve this

industry challenge by acting as an electrical shock absorber, absorbing these

rapid power swings, protecting valuable equipment and the grid while helping

operators increase compute density and limiting the need for costly and

time-consuming upgrades to existing facilities. The offering is aimed at the

expansive ecosystem, building out hyperscale data centers and AI Factories.

It is designed to enable higher-density AI

deployments, reduce utility-side power disturbances and accelerate the

deployment of new capacity without a complete facility redesign. The solution

can be deployed in existing AC architectures and retrofit projects, while

providing a pathway toward future 800V DC standards.

“The race to scale AI is increasingly becoming a race for power. Data

center operators need ways to expand capacity faster without waiting years for

new grid and facility infrastructure. Our strategic investment into Skeleton

addresses exactly this challenge. By combining Siemens’ electrical

infrastructure expertise with Skeleton’s breakthrough supercapacitor

technology, we can help customers unlock more AI capacity, improve resilience

and accelerate growth in a rapidly expanding market,” said Peter Koerte, Member

of the Managing Board of Siemens AG and President Smart Infrastructure.

“Our partnership with Siemens brings

together Skeleton’s innovation and Siemens’ global industrial scale to

accelerate the deployment of AI infrastructure, unlock more computing power and

improve energy efficiency. This winning combination gives us the strongest

value proposition in the AI power infrastructure market, and we plan to become

the world’s largest provider of white-space power systems for AI data centers,”

said Taavi Madiberk, CEO and co-founder of Skeleton Technologies.

“Skeleton's portfolio perfectly complements

Siemens' comprehensive data center offering. Our combined innovation roadmap will

deliver benchmark solutions for the white space of next-generation data

centers, delivered by a strong manufacturing network across Europe and the

USA,” said Markus Grabmeier, President of Electrical Products at Siemens AG.

The transactions will be completed following

the fulfilment of customary completion formalities.

Invitation to Munters Capital Markets Day 2026

Wed, Sep 23, 2026 07:30 CET

Report this content

Munters invites analysts, investors, media and other stakeholders to its Capital Markets Day on November 11, 2026. The event will be held in Stockholm, Sweden, with a live webcast available globally.

The event will take place at At Six, Brunkebergstorg 6 in Stockholm, starting at 1:00 p.m. CET with a light lunch. The presentations and webcast will begin at 2:00 p.m. CET (1:00 p.m. GMT in London and 8:00 a.m. EST in New York) and are expected to conclude at approximately 5:30 p.m. CET. During the day, participants attending in person will also have the opportunity to explore a selection of Munters products and solutions and meet and engage with our experts.

Please confirm your attendance, either in person or virtually, via our registration portal: https://munters.events.inderes.com/cmd-2026/register . Munters kindly asks participants attending in person to register no later than November 6.

The Capital Markets Day will provide further insight into Munters strategic direction and ambitions. The event will feature presentations and discussions with representatives from our business areas and Group Management, including Stefan Aspman, CEO.

Participants attending virtually will have the opportunity to submit written questions during the webcast. A recording of the webcast will also be available on Munters website after the event.

Link to registration and webcast: https://munters.events.inderes.com/cmd-2026/register

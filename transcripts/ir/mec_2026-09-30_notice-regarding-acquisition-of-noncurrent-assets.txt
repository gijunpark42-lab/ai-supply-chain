Notice Regarding Acquisition of Noncurrent Assets

MEC COMPANY LTD. (“the Company”) hereby announces th at, at a meeting of the Board of Directors

held today, it resolved to commence procedures for the acquisition of noncurrent assets. Details are a s

follows.

1. Reason for the acquisition

In recent years, against the backdrop of rapid prog ress in generative AI, demand for electronic

substrates and components has been expanding. The C ompany supplies chemicals used in the

manufacturing process for electronic substrates and  components, and has a particularly high market

share in the manufacturing process for package subs trates on which semiconductors are mounted. As

various AI-related advancements are expected to con tinue in the future, the Company is working to

further strengthen its supply structure throughout the Group to take advantage of the opportunities fo r

sustainable growth stemming from the expanding demand.

The Company’s Nagaoka Factory, a mass-production factory in Japan, will mark 40 years of operation in

2033, and is expected to undergo major upgrades. With this in mind, the Company has been considering the

acquisition of land near the Nagaoka Factory, and h as decided to proceed with the acquisition process after

receiving information regarding sale of the adjacent land. The Company believes that the acquisition of this

land will expand its options for renovation of the current factory.

2. Outline of the assets to be acquired

(1) Location Nagaoka City, Niigata Prefecture

(2) Investment amount Approx. ¥2.3 billion (includi ng land and buildings)

(3) Financing plan Own funds (planned)

3. Outline of the counterparty

The counterparty is a general business corporation i n Japan. In accordance with the agreement with

the counterparty, an outline of the counterparty wi ll not be disclosed. There are no capital, personal , or

business relationships between the Company and the counterparty that require disclosure, and the

counterparty is not a related party.

Our research paper has been published in "Advanced Materials" through collaboration between A*STAR, Nanyang Technological University, JEOL Ltd., and Kyoto University

Release Date: 2026/09/08

Our research paper has been published in "Advanced Materials" through collaboration between A*STAR, Nanyang Technological University, JEOL Ltd., and Kyoto University. A new class of MOF-Vitrimer Composites which enables thermal healing and mechanical reprocessing was proposed. The research project was led by A*STAR and Nanyang Technological University.

Healable and Post-Programmable Metal–Organic Framework—Vitrimer Composites

DOI: 10.1002/adma.74248

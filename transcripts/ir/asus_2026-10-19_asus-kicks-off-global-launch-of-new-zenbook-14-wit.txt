ASUS Kicks Off Global Launch of New Zenbook 14 With Design-Led European Debut

October 19, 2026

・

Press Release

New Arctic Blue and Komodo Coral Ceraluminum finishes, immersive European experiences inspired by Zenbook’s design philosophy and sensory craftsmanship

Premium Zenbook evolution: Zenbook 14 combines refined design, innovative materials, and AI-powered experiences

Signature material innovation: Ceraluminum™ takes center stage in new Arctic Blue and Komodo Coral finishes

European debut: Zenbook 14 marks the first chapter of its global launch against a backdrop of design, craftsmanship, and lifestyle culture

Evolving brand platform: Immersive new Design You Can Feel consumer experiences advance Zenbook as a premium lifestyle technology brand

TAIPEI, Taiwan, October 19, 2028 — ASUS today announced the debut of Zenbook 14 (UX3480), the newest evolution of its premium Zenbook portfolio. The new laptop, the latest expression of the Zenbook philosophy, combines premium craftsmanship, innovative materials, AI-powered experiences, and a renewed focus on sensory design.

As part of its debut, ASUS has chosen Europe as the first stage for a collection of limited-time brand experiences inspired by the product's design language, transforming the launch from a traditional technology announcement into a celebration of design, materiality, and lifestyle.

At the heart of the new ASUS Zenbook 14 is our signature Ceraluminum ™ material, combining the durability of ceramic with the lightweight characteristics of aluminum. The new generation introduces two nature-inspired finishes, Arctic Blue and Komodo Coral, bringing a softer and more emotional design expression to personal technology.

Inspired by the natural world, each finish tells a distinctive story. Komodo Coral captures the organic essence of Indonesia’s famous Pink Beach, one of the world’s rare coastal wonders. Its unique muted tone draws from the surreal blend of pristine white sands and vibrant, pulverized crimson fragments of microscopic foraminifera and coral reefs.

Arctic Blue is rooted in the untouched majesty of glacial landscapes, with a sophisticated palette inspired by deep-winter lakes. Its pale, frosted-blue light blends with rich, dense undertones of compressed ice, evoking a tactile, multidimensional surface reminiscent of frozen water mapping fine geometric fractures in natural forms.

The new colorways – designed to be seen, touched, and experienced – reflect our belief that technology should not only perform exceptionally, but also create a meaningful connection with the people who use it.

Additionally, AI-powered experiences are integrated into Zenbook 14 to support more intuitive, responsive and personalized everyday use, complementing the product’s emphasis on craftsmanship and sensory design.

Bringing the product to life

To celebrate the launch, ASUS extended the design language of Zenbook 14 beyond the device itself.

Inspired by the Arctic Blue and Komodo Coral finishes, selected destinations across London, Milan, Madrid, Lisbon, Paris, Munich, Berlin, Stockholm, and Amsterdam have been transformed into immersive Zenbook environments that translate color, materiality, and craftsmanship into physical experiences. Rather than presenting the product in a conventional retail setting, ASUS invites consumers to step into the world of Zenbook and experience the philosophy behind the product firsthand.

Lifestyle venues including cafés, bookstores, boutique hotels, and curated retail destinations have been reimagined through a Zenbook lens, creating unique environments where visitors can engage with the product through sight, touch, interaction, and discovery.

Design You Can Feel

The launch also represents the next evolution of the ASUS Design You Can Feel platform.

Following successful showcases at major international design events, including the London Design Festival and Milan Design Week , ASUS is now extending the initiative beyond exhibitions and into everyday life. Design You Can Feel is no longer limited to a display environment; it has become something consumers can encounter in the places they visit, the experiences they share, and the rituals that shape modern living.

Rather than beginning with specifications, ASUS chose to begin with feeling, allowing consumers to discover Zenbook through color, texture, craftsmanship, and sensory experiences before exploring the technology itself.

With the launch of the new Zenbook 14, ASUS continues its journey to redefine the relationship between technology and design. By combining material innovation, craftsmanship, and immersive brand experiences, Zenbook is evolving beyond a premium laptop into a design-driven lifestyle brand that consumers can genuinely connect with.

The European debut marks the first chapter of this new journey, transforming the launch of ASUS Zenbook 14 into an experience that can be seen, touched, and felt.

Release Date: 2026/09/29

Our collaborative research with the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Kolkata, JEOL Ltd., and Rigaku Asia Pacific Pte. Ltd. has been published in the "Journal of the American Chemical Society".

The paper reports the self-assembly of [6+12] octahedral porous organic cages.

Our contribution focused on elucidating the reaction mechanism by solid-state NMR spectroscopy using the JNM-ECZ600R and confirming the formation of octahedral cage-structured molecules by MALDI-TOFMS using the NewSpiralTOF™.

The research project was led by IISER Kolkata and Rigaku Asia Pacific Pte. Ltd.

Symmetry-Encoded [6 + 12] Octahedral Organic Cages Enabled by Retrospective Design

DOI: 10.1021/jacs.6c10786

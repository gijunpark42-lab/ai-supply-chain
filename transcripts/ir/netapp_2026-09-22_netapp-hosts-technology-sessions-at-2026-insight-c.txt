NetApp Hosts Technology Sessions at 2026 INSIGHT Conference in Las Vegas, Nevada

September 22, 2026

SUNNYVALE, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--

NetApp (NASDAQ: NTAP) will hold their 2026 INSIGHT™ customer conference in Las Vegas, Nevada on September 29 – October 1, 2026.

On Tuesday, September 29, 2026, NetApp will host a technology session for financial analysts and investors from 11:00 a.m. – 3:00 p.m. Pacific Time. The event will include George Kurian, Chief Executive Officer; Syam Nair, Chief Product Officer; Wissam Jabre, Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer and several tech specialists discussing NetApp’s differentiation and value proposition in the areas of enterprise storage, AI, cloud transformation, data infrastructure modernization, and cyber resilience. Kris Newton, Vice President, Investor Relations, will moderate a customer panel.

Furthermore, NetApp will also host a keynote session of mainstage headliners from 9:00 – 10:15 a.m. Pacific Time which will include special tech luminaries, industry leaders and entertainment icons.

How to Participate

Live video webcasts of both events will be available at http://investors.netapp.com . A webcast replay of the events will be available at http://investors.netapp.com approximately 24 hours after the event.

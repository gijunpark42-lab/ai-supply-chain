CreeLED Inc. and Promier Products, Inc. (d/b/a LitezAll) Resolve Patent Dispute Through Limited License Agreement

August 31, 2026

DURHAM, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--

Cree LED ®, a Penguin Solutions brand (Nasdaq: PENG ), and Promier Products, Inc. today announced that they have reached a mutually beneficial settlement resolving a patent infringement dispute involving Cree LED’s patents related to LED components used in portable lighting products. As part of the settlement, Cree LED has granted Promier Products a limited license to certain Cree LED patents covering LED components.

Cree LED is firmly committed to protecting its intellectual property.

Cree LED has established itself as a trusted brand in portable lighting applications, known for consistently high performance and reliable product quality. Cree LED is firmly committed to protecting its intellectual property and actively monitors the entire value chain of the global market to identify and address unauthorized use of its patented technologies. This vigilance ensures that Cree LED’s innovations remain protected and that customers can rely on the integrity and performance of the company’s industry-leading solutions.

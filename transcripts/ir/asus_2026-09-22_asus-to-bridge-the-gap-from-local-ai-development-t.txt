ASUS to Bridge the Gap from Local AI Development to Hyperscale AI Factories at DCD Connect London

September 22, 2026

・

Press Release

At DCD Connect London Booth 35, ASUS showcases NVIDIA-Certified Systems for scaling from AI experimentation to high-volume token generation

Scalable AI infrastructure: Seamless progression from local platforms to hyperscale AI factories built on NVIDIA-accelerated computing

End-to-end AI deployment: ASUS solutions span deskside development, enterprise adoption, data center expansion and hyperscale AI processing

Unified full-stack advantage: Integrated compute, networking, cooling, storage and support simplify deployment to reduce time-to-revenue and TCO

LONDON, UK, September 16, 2026 — ASUS , a global leader in accelerated computing solutions, today announced its presence at DCD Connect London , taking place from 16-17 September at Booth 35. Displaying exclusive ASUS server solutions built with NVIDIA®-accelerated computing, designed to help enterprises navigate the complex operational shift from initial algorithmic development to industrial-scale data center deployment.

The AI scaling challenge: From deskside to data center

As artificial intelligence transitions from early testing phases into business-critical operations, organizations face the hurdle of infrastructure fragmentation. Moving models out of localized developer labs into enterprise data centers often requires massive, costly re-architecting of hardware configurations.

To overcome this roadblock, enterprises need a continuous, adaptive hardware foundation. Rather than presenting a disjointed list of standalone products, ASUS is demonstrating its capabilities as a singular partner for full-stack AI deployment, providing structural consistency as an organization’s computational needs expand.

A progressive journey across the AI lifecycle

At Booth 35, attendees can learn how the ASUS portfolio maps directly to every tier of the corporate AI journey, guiding deployments smoothly across four key phases:

ASUS Ascent GX10 : Developer: The ASUS Ascent GX10 platform offers accessible, secure AI engineering environments optimised for rapid model iteration and localised software training.

Enterprise : The ASUS ExpertCenter Pro ET900N delivers a more powerful deskside supercomputing foundation. When paired with server-class ASUS ET900N / NVIDIA-Certified Systems™, businesses gain a validated, highly dependable framework for mainstream IT environment adoption.

ASUS Data Center built on NVIDIA HGX Rubin NVL8 : These liquid-cooled servers provide high-density, multi-GPU accelerated computing systems engineered to scale inside traditional data centre envelopes without exceeding thermal or space limitations.

ASU S AI Fac tory built on NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 : For massive, hyperscale processing hubs. This rack-scale supercomputer is a co-designed integrated system and is built specifically for continuous, multi-trillion parameter model training and high-volume low-cost token generation.

Deepening the collaboration with NVIDIA

The long-standing relationship between ASUS and NVIDIA focuses on the collaboration on engineering hardware, software, and physical layer management tools to transition traditional enterprise setups into production-ready platforms.

Through this tight technology integration, ASUS systems blend seamlessly into advanced accelerated computing architectures. This gives data center architects the exact structural foundation required to scale their infrastructure — bridging the gap smoothly from localized deskside developer tools to massive, high-volume token generation factories.

Why ASUS: The full-stack advantage

The ultimate value ASUS brings to modern enterprises is a unified operational ecosystem. True scalability encompasses more than raw GPU nodes; it demands synchronized management across compute, networking, cooling architectures, storage subsystems, physical deployment frameworks, and global support services. By managing these intricate layers under a single operational banner, ASUS significantly shortens time-to-revenue and minimizes total cost of ownership (TCO).

Engage with ASUS at DCD Connect London

DCD Connect London attendees are invited to visit the ASUS team at Booth 35 to discuss how to construct flexible, future-proof data center solutions that easily absorb the next generation of accelerated computing tech.

To book an on-site consultation or request a customized layout assessment, please visit the ASUS AI Server Solutions Portal .

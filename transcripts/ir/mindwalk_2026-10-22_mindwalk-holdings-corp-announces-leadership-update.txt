MindWalk Holdings Corp. Announces Leadership Update

October 22, 2026

Commercial Priorities Unchanged; LensAI™ Remains in Contracted, Recurring Deployments with Life Sciences Customers

AUSTIN, Texas--(BUSINESS WIRE)--

MindWalk Holdings Corp. ("MindWalk") (NASDAQ: HYFT), a Bio-Native AI company, today announced that Thomas Lynch, PhD, has departed the company and ceased to serve as Chief Business Officer, effective October 16, 2026.

"We thank Thomas for his contributions to MindWalk and wish him well," said Jennifer Bath, PhD, Chief Executive Officer and President of MindWalk. "Our commercial priorities do not change. We have moved from building the technology to deploying it, and the work in front of the team is the same today as it was last week: delivering on the platform deployments already under contract and advancing the pharma and partner engagements in progress."

MindWalk's commercial activity continues on plan. LensAI™, the company's reasoning and application layer, is in contracted, recurring arrangements with life sciences customers today, running on ReefIQ™, the biological data substrate powered by HYFT® Technology. The commercial and business development team continues to report to the Chief Executive Officer.

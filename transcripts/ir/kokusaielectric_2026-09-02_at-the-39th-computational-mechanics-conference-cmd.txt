September 2, 2026

KOKUSAI ELECTRIC CORPORATION (President and CEO: Kazunori Tsukada, Head Office: Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo, Japan) will give a poster presentation coauthored with Shinshu University at the Japan Society of Mechanical Engineers' 39th Computational Mechanics Conference (CMD2026), to be held from Monday, September 14 to Wednesday, September 16 at Osaka Institute of Technology Umeda Campus (OIT Umeda Tower).

The society is a group of about 30,000 engineers engaged in machinery, mechanical systems, and related fields, and it provides venues for engineers to present their research and improve their standing. In addition, this symposium is a forum where researchers and engineers related to mechanical engineering gather together to discuss.

Notice Regarding Certain Media Reports | KIOXIA Holdings Corporation

Notice Regarding Certain Media Reports

September 15, 2026

Kioxia Holdings Corporation

There have been media reports regarding the listing of the Company's American Depositary Shares on a U.S. stock exchange. These reports are not announcements made by the Company.

While the Company is preparing to list American Depositary Shares representing its common shares on a U.S. stock exchange to steadily and sustainably increase corporate value, details of the listing such as the schedule, listing market, and listing method have not yet been decided, and the Company may decide not to continue pursuing the listing depending on the circumstances over the course of the preparation.

The Company will promptly make an announcement if any matters requiring notification arise.

Timely disclosure made on May 15, 2026

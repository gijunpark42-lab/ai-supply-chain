A collaborative research team led by Hanyang University, with participation from JEOL, has published its findings in "Advanced Functional Materials". The study demonstrated, through ⁷Li solid-state NMR and other characterization techniques, that particle-structure control via stacking-fault topology improves the reversibility of anion redox reactions in Li-rich Mn-based cathode materials.

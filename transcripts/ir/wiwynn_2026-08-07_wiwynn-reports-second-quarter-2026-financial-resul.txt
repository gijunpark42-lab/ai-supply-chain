Wiwynn Reports Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results

2 : 35

Wiwynn held a Board of Directors meeting today (August 7) and approved its financial statements for the second quarter of 2026.

In the second quarter of 2026, consolidated revenue reached NT$278.153 billion, representing a year-over-year increase of 26.0%. Profit after tax (PAT) was NT$14.969 billion, increased by 23.5% from the same period in 2025. Beginning in April, memory procurement for certain customers shifted to a procurement agency model, with the related amounts excluded from revenue and cost of goods sold. Combined with higher NRE income from a new product introduction during the quarter, gross margin improved to 9.3%. Operating margin was 7.3%, and PAT margin was 5.4%. Basic earnings per share (EPS) was NT$80.43, compared with NT$65.23 in the same period of 2025. In response to changes in the AI product mix and rising component costs, the Company has continued to work with customers to adjust its business model.

In the first half of 2026, consolidated revenue achieved NT$554.660 billion, increased by 41.7% year-over-year. PAT was NT$29.084 billion, increased by 32.7% year-over-year. Gross margin was 8.4%, operating margin was 6.8%, and PAT margin was 5.2%. EPS increased to NT$156.38 from NT$117.93 in the same period of 2025, setting a record high for the period.

The Company is optimistic about the long-term growth of the AI and data center markets, and continues to advance computing, thermal and power solutions, and related technologies and product development. To support business growth and global capacity expansion, the Board approved a consolidated capital expenditure budget of US$942 million for the second half of 2026, primarily for investments in power infrastructure, equipment, land, and buildings. In addition, to support working capital and long-term investment needs, the Board approved a proposal to issue the Company's first domestic unsecured convertible bonds of up to NT$15 billion and to arrange a US$1.5 billion syndicated loan.

The Board also approved September 2 as the ex-rights trading date for the stock dividend distribution from 2025 earnings. For details of other relevant dates, please refer to the Company’s announcement.

BizLink Partners with ams OSRAM to Advance Photonics for AI Data Center Interconnects

FREMONT, Calif., September 21, 2026—BizLink today announced an ecosystem partnership with innovative light and sensor solutions leader ams OSRAM to co-develop next-generation optical interconnect solutions for AI scale-up domains. Leveraging its core strengths in fiber array assembly, optical packaging, optical testing, and high-volume manufacturing, BizLink is collaborating with ams OSRAM to demonstrate a revolutionary low-power, low-latency "wide-and-slow" optical link technology at the upcoming ECOC 2026 conference and exhibition.

The rise of artificial intelligence and the required training infrastructure is redefining the requirements for intra-datacenter connectivity. Bigger AI scale-up domains require interconnects designed for power, latency, density, and reliability together. To address this bottleneck, this innovative optical architecture directly targets these critical requirements. Rather than pushing ever more data through a few complex, power-hungry high-speed channels, this approach distributes data across numerous parallel, lower-speed channels, achieving extended reach and ultra-low latency while preserving cost and power efficiency.

‍

ECOC 2026 Live Demonstration

At the upcoming ECOC 2026 conference and exhibition in Spain (September 21–23), BizLink and ams OSRAM will present a connectorized multi-core fiber solution. Shown in a private room, the optical link demonstrator features a 2D addressable microVCSEL array with an 850 nm wavelength paired with BizLink's specialized fiber-to-fiber connector and optical packaging.

“This demonstrator validates a fundamentally different optical interconnect for low power and low latency wide-and-slow interconnects. By combining ams OSRAM's addressable microVCSEL technology with BizLink's expertise in fiber-array integration, optical packaging, and manufacturing we have pushed the boundaries in optical interconnect technology,” said Hendrik Coldenstrodt, CTO of the Computing and Transportation Business Group at BizLink.

“We’re very excited to partner with BizLink to co-develop and demonstrate a high-performance optical data link based on our wide-and-slow interconnect architecture and our photonics engine. Together we’re creating a powerful ecosystem with the goal to industrialize this new architecture at scale.” said Ashkan Seyedi, Vice President and General Manager of the Optical Interconnects Business Line at ams OSRAM.

Long committed to HPC and data centers, BizLink continues to advance its leadership in high-speed and optical interconnects. This collaboration with ams OSRAM highlights BizLink’s expertise in AI optical packaging and optoelectronic manufacturing. Moving forward, BizLink remains dedicated to collaborating with global partners to empower the CPO industry for next-generation AI.

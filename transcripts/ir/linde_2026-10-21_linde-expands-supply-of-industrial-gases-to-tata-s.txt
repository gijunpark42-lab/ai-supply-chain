Linde Expands Supply of Industrial Gases to Tata Steel in India

Woking,

UK, October 21, 2026 – Linde (Nasdaq:

LIN) today announced it has expanded its long-term supply of industrial gases

to Tata Steel Limited at its Kalinganagar Industrial Complex in Odisha, India.

Under a long-term supply agreement, Linde has doubled

its on-site capacity for Tata Steel through the de-captivation of two air

separation units (ASUs) at Kalinganagar. The newly acquired units are now

providing oxygen, nitrogen and argon for Tata Steel’s iron and steelmaking

operations. The additional capacity will help enable Tata Steel to increase

annual steel production at Kalinganagar from three million tons to eight

million tons per year.

The new ASUs bring Linde’s total capacity in the

Kalinganagar region to more than seven thousand tons of oxygen per day. In

addition to supporting Tata Steel’s growth, the additional ASUs will help Linde

meet increasing demand for industrial gases across the regional merchant

market.

“This expansion with Tata Steel underscores the

strength of our long-standing relationship and highlights the critical role of industrial

gases in steelmaking,” said Moloy Banerjee, President ASEAN & South Asia,

Linde. “The project further deepens our network density in an important

industrial region and positions Linde to capture further growth opportunities

in India.”

Recursion Announces Pricing of $200 Million Underwritten Offering of Class A Common Stock

October 15, 2026 at 8:30 AM EDT

PDF Version

Salt Lake City, UT , Oct. 15, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Recursion Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (“ Recursion ”) (NASDAQ: RXRX), a leading clinical-stage company pioneering AI-enabled drug discovery and development decoding biology to radically improve lives, today announced the pricing of an underwritten offering of 61,540,000 shares of its Class A common stock at a price of $3.25 per share. All of the shares are to be sold by Recursion. Before deducting the underwriting discounts and commissions and estimated offering expenses, Recursion expects to receive total gross proceeds of approximately $200 million . The offering is expected to close on or about October 16, 2026 , subject to satisfaction of customary closing conditions.

Leerink Partners , Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC , Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC and Evercore Group L.L.C. are acting as joint bookrunning managers for the offering. Needham & Company, LLC is acting as lead manager for the offering.

The financing included participation from leading healthcare and institutional investors, including Janus Henderson Investors , Invus , Longaeva Partners , ARK Invest, Driehaus Capital Management and other prominent healthcare specialists and mutual funds.

The shares are being offered by Recursion pursuant to a registration statement on Form S-3, which became automatically effective upon filing with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC”) on February 12, 2025 . The offering is being made solely by means of a written prospectus and a prospectus supplement that form a part of the registration statement. The preliminary prospectus supplement and accompanying prospectus relating to the offering was filed on October 15, 2026 . A final prospectus supplement and accompanying prospectus relating to the offering will also be filed with the SEC . These documents can be accessed for free through the SEC’s website at www.sec.gov .

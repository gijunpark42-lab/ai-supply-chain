Press Release 24 September 2026 Siemens Mobility Berlin

Siemens and Infrabel expand

strategic partnership to strengthen digital rail operations in Belgium

11-year framework to modernize, maintain and future-proof Infrabel’s rail Operational Control System (OCS) environment

Agile, collaborative partnership for continuous signaling software evolution and hardware renewal

Enhanced digital resilience through dedicated cybersecurity and next-generation signaling architecture

11-year framework to modernize, maintain and future-proof Infrabel’s rail Operational Control System (OCS) environment

Agile, collaborative partnership for continuous signaling software evolution and hardware renewal

Enhanced digital resilience through dedicated cybersecurity and next-generation signaling architecture

Michael Peter, CEO of Siemens Mobility, and Benoît Gilson, CEO of Infrabel (from left to right), Photo source: Siemens Mobility

Siemens

Mobility, a leading provider of intelligent and sustainable rail solutions, and

Infrabel, Belgium's national railway infrastructure manager, have signed a

long-term framework agreement to modernize and maintain the country’s

Operational Control System (OCS) environment; these are the digital systems used to manage

rail traffic across Belgium. Over the next 11 years, the partners will ensure the

continuous evolution of signaling software and hardware for one of Europe’s

densest rail networks. This digital transformation will significantly enhance

network resilience and safety, ensuring a more reliable and efficient rail

service for passengers and freight while preparing the rail operational control

centers for the future. The framework agreement has a total order value of

approximately €215 million.

“Our long-standing

partnership with Infrabel is built on trust and a shared commitment to

advancing rail operations in Belgium. With this new 11-year framework agreement,

we are taking our collaboration to the next level by jointly modernizing the

digital foundation of Belgium´s rail operation control centers. Our digital

technology thrives to 100% system availability and introduces open interfaces

according to the newest standards,”

said Michael Peter, CEO of Siemens

Mobility . “By combining our global digitalization, software and

cybersecurity expertise with strong local teams in Brussels, we are helping Infrabel

to ensure that Belgium’s railway remains innovative, secure and operationally resilient

for decades to come.”

“This

contract goes far beyond software and technology. It is an investment in the

future of our railway, but also in Belgium’s industrial and economic ecosystem.

By partnering with Siemens, we are not only strengthening the safety,

reliability and performance of our network; we are also supporting economic

activity and jobs in Belgium, while contributing to the development of local

expertise in a highly strategic sector. This is how investment in rail

infrastructure creates value well beyond the tracks themselves,”

said

Benoit

Gilson, CEO of Infrabel.

The

long-term partnership will rely on local Siemens Mobility expertise in Belgium,

supporting highly skilled employment and helping to further develop the

country’s capabilities in digital rail systems, software engineering and

cybersecurity. This creates economic value that extends well beyond the railway

sector itself.

The framework agreement introduces a dynamic governance structure

centered around a four-year rolling roadmap. Through an annual alignment

process, Infrabel and Siemens Mobility can continuously synchronize strategic

priorities with resource allocation. This ensures that the partnership remains

flexible and can rapidly respond to evolving requirements over the 11-year

term.

A key pillar of the modernization is a rigorous focus on cybersecurity.

This includes the appointment of a dedicated security officer and a continuous

commitment to the fast-evolving landscape of rail-specific cyber security

standards like the IEC62443 standard and the ISO 27001 certification standard –

the internationally recognized standard for organizational information security

management. This ensures that the rail infrastructure is protected against

cyber threats.

The modernized OCS environment will be future-proof through an intuitive

user interface that enhances operational efficiency for control center

operators. This will enable continuous modernization of mission-critical

signaling software and operational hardware, while providing enhanced digital

resilience through next-generation signaling architecture.

The OCS environment supports the management of one of Europe's most

intensively used rail networks, handling thousands of passenger and freight

services every day. Reliable and secure operations control is essential for

maintaining punctuality, increasing capacity and network resilience across

Belgium's rail system.

Siemens Mobility

is a separately managed company of Siemens AG. As a leader in intelligent transport solutions for more than 175 years, Siemens Mobility is constantly innovating its portfolio. Its core areas include rolling stock, rail automation and electrification, a comprehensive software portfolio, turnkey systems as well as related services. With digital products and solutions, and the use of industrial AI, Siemens Mobility is enabling mobility operators worldwide to make their infrastructure intelligent, increase value sustainably over the entire lifecycle, enhance passenger experience, and guarantee availability. In fiscal year 2025, which ended on September 30, 2025, Siemens Mobility posted revenue of €12.4 billion and employed around 43,400 people worldwide. Further information is available at:

www.siemens.com/mobility

Infrabel is Belgium’s national railway infrastructure manager. The company manages one of Europe’s busiest rail networks and employs around 9,600 people. Every day, Infrabel enables the safe and efficient movement of passenger and freight trains across Belgium. Through continuous investment in infrastructure, digitalisation and innovation, Infrabel supports sustainable mobility, economic growth and the competitiveness of Belgium.

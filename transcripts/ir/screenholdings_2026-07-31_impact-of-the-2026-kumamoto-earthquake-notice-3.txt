Impact of the 2026 Kumamoto Earthquake (Notice #3)

July 31, 2026

Doc. No.: INFO260731-1E

The SCREEN Group and its employees express deep sympathy for those who were affected by the earthquake that hit Kumamoto Prefecture, Japan, and surrounding regions on July 28, 2026 at 4:27 PM (JST).

SCREEN Holdings has established an emergency headquarters within its head office immediately after the earthquake. Under the Company’s Representative Director, President and CEO Masato Goto, we are striving to confirm the safety and whereabouts of our employees and related information.

Here are the updates of what have been confirmed, as of July 31, 2026 at 4:00 PM (JST).

Safety and whereabouts of our employees

Safety of all employees working in the Kumamoto area has been confirmed.

Damage to our facilities, etc.

Some damage has been confirmed with our facilities located in the Kumamoto area. While recovery efforts are currently underway with the highest priority placed on safety, some operations have been impacted.

Status of our clients and partner companies

Some damage has been confirmed on the side of certain clients and partner companies. We are still gathering information in this regard.

Impact on business operations

We are currently assessing the impact.

We will post further updates on this website as soon as new information becomes available.​​​

Advanced Energy Showcases Power Delivery with Unmatched Control for Advanced IC Manufacturing at SEM

Advanced Energy Showcases Power Delivery with Unmatched Control for Advanced IC Manufacturing at SEMICON West

Posted

October 09, 2026

Showcasing Power Control Solutions for Sub-2nm Etch and Deposition Profiles

DENVER — Oct. 9, 2026 — Advanced Energy Industries Inc. (Nasdaq: AEIS) will showcase plasma power, sensing, and control products designed to deliver the precision and performance demanded by the Angstrom-era and the latest advanced integrated circuit (IC) manufacturing techniques at SEMICON West 2026, from Oct. 13 to Oct. 15 in San Francisco.

The company is a global leader in highly engineered, precision power conversion, measurement, and control solutions. Highlighted technologies include the eVerest® RF generator platform , enabling process development and volume manufacturing for sub-2 nm deposition and etch processes. The eVoS® ME bias supply enables the most advanced etch profiles for 3D chip structures through precise Ion energy control. At SEMICON West, Advanced Energy will unveil a new product designed to deliver more accurate, repeatable measurements, and deeper process insight.

“Advanced Energy develops high-precision power delivery systems that are based on modular architecture, which makes them highly configurable,” said Juergen Braun, Executive Vice President, Plasma Power, Advanced Energy. “As a result of our engineering work, we deliver tailored waveforms that ensure the most accurate power delivery and control.”

Featured Solutions:

Plasma Power: eVerest generator, NavX™ impedance matching network, eVoS ME bias supplies and MAXstream® 300 plasma source. Advanced Energy will showcase PowerInsight, a platform for data acquisition, monitoring, and analytics.

Support Power: NeoPower™ series, Evergreen Vento AC-DC enclosed supplies, and iHP air-cooled modular platforms.

Critical Sensing and Control: 401M pyrometers, M-1200 fiber optic temperature measurement system, Thyro-XD SCR power controller and 5540A RF power meter.

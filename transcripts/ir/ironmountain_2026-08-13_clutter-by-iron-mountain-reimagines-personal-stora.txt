Clutter by Iron Mountain Reimagines Personal Storage, Delivering a Premium Managed Storage Solution

August 13, 2026

Combining enterprise-standard logistics, digital access, and Iron Mountain’s trusted legacy, Clutter offers a complete, end-to-end personal storage experience designed around safekeeping

LOS ANGELES--(BUSINESS WIRE)--

Clutter by Iron Mountain today announced the relaunch of its managed personal storage service, introducing a premium alternative to traditional self-storage. Designed for consumers seeking a more secure and convenient way to store their belongings, the service combines enterprise-level logistics, secure facility storage, and AI enhanced digital access all backed by Iron Mountain’s 75-year track record of protecting the world’s most valuable assets.

For decades, consumers have viewed personal storage as a self-managed hassle and a basic commodity. Rather than asking customers to rent trucks, haul heavy boxes, and pack their belongings themselves, Clutter by Iron Mountain provides an end-to-end managed storage experience. From pickup and transportation to secure storage and return delivery, our managed solution allows customers to ensure their items receive the same standard of care that institutions have enjoyed for decades.

Beyond handling the physical heavy lifting, Clutter by Iron Mountain integrates modern technology directly into the experience. Via a digital inventory platform, customers can view photos of their stored items online and schedule on-demand return delivery for items with just a few clicks. Clutter by Iron Mountain is the only seamless, end-to-end, managed storage provider to deliver packing, transportation, digital inventory and the assurance of Iron Mountain’s time-tested protection all in one service.

"When people entrust us with their belongings, they're placing their memories, milestones, and the things that matter most in our care," said Amy Sheaves, VP of Consumer Storage at Clutter by Iron Mountain. "For too long, storage has felt like a burdensome chore. Clutter by Iron Mountain is changing that conversation by demonstrating that managed storage delivers more than convenience. We deliver asset stewardship. Whether protecting priceless business records or treasured family keepsakes, the standard of care should never change. Consumers deserve the same level of protection, stewardship, and operational excellence that organizations around the world have relied on from Iron Mountain for decades."

Clutter by Iron Mountain serves a broad range of consumers who value security, convenience, and peace of mind. Whether they’re busy urbanites who prefer not to visit self-storage units alone, homeowners renovating, older adults rightsizing, or growing families making space for a new chapter, these "Asset Protectors" share the same priority: trusting their memories and investments to the highest standard of care.

Clutter by Iron Mountain’s premium managed storage experience is available in more than 25 key U.S. and Canadian metropolitan markets, including New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Dallas, Miami, and Toronto among many others.

To learn more or schedule a reservation, visit www.clutter.com .

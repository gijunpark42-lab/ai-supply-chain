The thinnest and lightest Galaxy Z Flip yet, Galaxy Z Flip8 delivers greater convenience anytime, anywhere in a compact form factor that fits comfortably in one hand.

A standout feature of Galaxy Z Flip8 is its upgraded cover display, FlexWindow. Users can take photos, access frequently used apps, and experience agentic AI — all without unfolding the device.

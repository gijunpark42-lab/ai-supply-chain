Infineon Wins Integrated Communication Award 2026 for Integrated Communication Management

Business & Financial Press

Sep 23, 2026

Munich, Germany – 23 September 2026 – Infineon Technologies AG has been honored with the Integrated Communication Award 2026 in the category “Integrated Communication Management.” The award was presented on 22 September during the 19th German Brand Summit at the F.A.Z. Tower in Frankfurt am Main. Through this recognition, the F.A.Z. Institute and an expert jury acknowledge companies that consistently organize their communications strategically, from alignment with overarching business objectives and clear governance structures to technology-enabled processes and a collaborative communication culture.

“Strategic and integrated communication makes a decisive contribution to business success. We are pleased to receive this recognition,” said Florian Martens, Executive Vice President & Chief Communications Officer at Infineon. “In an increasingly complex communications landscape, we can only create impact by breaking down silos and addressing topics holistically. This award recognizes the progress our team has achieved together over the past several years. My sincere thanks go to all colleagues who have helped shape this journey.”

Infineon’s communications function operates within an integrated organization that brings together all communication disciplines, ranging from public affairs and corporate communications to marketing communications, regional communications, and communication services. Content is planned and managed across functions and channels, topic ownership is bundled, and communication activities are aligned with strategic objectives. Clear performance indicators are used to measure success. In this way, communications makes a significant and measurable contribution to Infineon’s corporate strategy and business performance.

The jury particularly recognized the holistic approach with which Infineon manages communications across the company: “What impressed us was the consistent way communication has been transformed into an integrated management system. Planning, processes, topic management, and measurement are systematically interconnected. Particularly noteworthy are the clear governance framework and the transparent management architecture, which create a traceable link between communication activities and business objectives.”

The Integrated Communication Award, presented by the F.A.Z. Institute, recognizes outstanding approaches to integrated communications. In the category “Integrated Communication Management,” Allianz, Bayer, GEA, and Merck were also nominated alongside Infineon. The jury comprises communications experts from both industry and academia and is chaired by Prof. Dr. Ansgar Zerfass of Leipzig University.

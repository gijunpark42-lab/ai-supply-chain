October 7, 2026

Notice Regarding the Establishment of a Subsidiary

Nissan Chemical Corporation (Head Office: Chuo- ku, Tokyo; President: Shinsuke Yagi;

hereinafter “the Company”) hereby announce that we have established a subsidiary in China, as

described below.

In addition, since the amount of capital of the subsidiary exceeds 10% of the amount of the

Company's capital, the subsidiary qualifies as a specified subsidiary of the Company.

1. Purpose of the Establishment of the Company

The Company has established a subsidiary in Zhangjiagang City, Jiangsu Province, the

People's Republic of China, with the aim of strengthening its supply framework  in the growing

Chinese semiconductor market.

In recent years, demand for semiconductors has continued to expand, driven by ongoing

digitalization and other factors. At the same time, increasing customer demand for supply chain

resilience has heightened the need for a more stable supply framework for semiconductor

materials.

The new company will manufacture and sell Anti-Reflective Coatings (BARC) for photoresists

and multilayer materials. By strengthening its supply base in China and establishing a supply

framework closer to customers, the Company aims to respond more promptly to changes in

demand and further enhance customer service.

Through these initiatives, the Company seeks to achieve sustainable growth in its

semiconductor materials business and enhance its corporate value.

2. Overview of the Subsidiary

Item Details

(1) Company Name Nissan Chemical Semiconductor materials Zhangjiagang Co., Ltd.

(2) Location  Zhangjiagang City, Jiangsu Province, People's Republic of China

(3) Representative   NAKADA Takakazu

(4) Business Manufacture and sale of semiconductor materials

(5) Registered Capital RMB 210 million (approximately JPY 5.0 billion)

(6) Date of Establishment September 1, 2026

(7) Fiscal Year-End December 31

(8) Equity Ownership 100% owned by the Company

3. Progress to Date and Future Schedule

The establishment of the subsidiary was resolved at a meeting of the Board of Directors held

on April 28, 2026. Thereafter, the Company entered into an investment agreement with

Zhangjiagang City, Jiangsu Province, and the subsidiary was registered on Sept ember 1,

2026.

The first capital contribution of RMB 89 million (approximately JPY 2.1 billion) is scheduled

to be made on October 15, 2026.

4. Impact on Financial Results

The impact of this matter on the Company's financial results for the fiscal year ending March

31, 2027 is expected to be immaterial. The Company will promptly disclose any matters that

require timely disclosure.

Samsung Opens Built-In Appliance Showroom in Germany To Advance European Market Expansion

Korea on September 22, 2026

Permanent showroom in Löhne gives kitchen-industry partners year-round access to Samsung’s built-in appliances and connected kitchen experiences

Samsung Electronics today announced the opening of a new built-in appliance showroom in Löhne, Germany.

Located on the ground floor of the Kitchen Center Löhne — an exhibition center for kitchen industry leaders — the permanent showroom provides kitchen retailers, planners, buyers, and other industry partners with year-round access to Samsung’s built-in appliance portfolio and connected kitchen solutions.

“The opening of this permanent showroom marks an important step for our commitment to the built-in appliance business in Europe,” said Sang Jik Lee, Executive Vice President and Head of Sales & Marketing Team of Digital Appliances (DA) Business at Samsung Electronics. “We aim to deepen industry collaboration, strengthen Samsung’s premium positioning, and better capture opportunities in the European built-in market.”

Bringing the AI Connected Kitchen to Life

Designed around Samsung’s connected appliances, the showroom moves beyond conventional product displays by placing built-in appliances in realistic kitchen and residential environments. Trade visitors can experience how Samsung appliances, AI-powered features, and SmartThings connectivity work together to create more intuitive and connected experiences throughout the home. They can also see how seamlessly Samsung’s built-in appliances fit into kitchens in different price segments, from entry to premium price levels.

The showroom includes various areas designed to present Samsung’s built-in appliances.

Near the entrance, kitchen and residential environments demonstrate how Samsung appliances and SmartThings-connected devices can work together throughout the home in realistic settings. In particular, three distinct built-in kitchen packages allow visitors to easily identify and compare differences across Samsung’s product lineup. The displays also highlight how the company’s built-in kitchen appliances complement a variety of kitchen furniture concepts, including wood, white, and navy finishes.

Next, a dedicated product zone presents the company’s refrigeration, cooking, and dishwashing portfolios alongside lifestyle displays and select new products. Key products on display include the Extractor Induction Hob — Samsung’s induction cooktop with an integrated extractor which launched this year — the Bespoke AI Dishwasher featuring AI Wash , 1 Samsung’s built-in and freestanding refrigerators, and the Bespoke AI Oven featuring AI Pro Cooking. 2

At the center of the showroom, an open consultation and communication space supports product demonstrations, partner meetings, briefings, and training programs. Visitors can also receive an introduction to Samsung’s B2B platform SPACE, including information on ordering processes, product availability, and product specifications. Together, the areas allow industry professionals to explore Samsung’s built-in portfolio while experiencing the value of its connected-home vision firsthand.

A Strategic Hub for Samsung’s European Built-In Appliance Business

Samsung’s new showroom is also serving as the company’s exhibition space for its participation in Küchenmeile A30 , a kitchen trade fair that is one of Europe’s leading B2B platforms for the kitchen, furniture, and interior design industries.

Held every September in and around Löhne — a region in which approximately two-thirds of Germany’s kitchen industry is concentrated — the event brings together more than 300 major kitchen furniture and appliance brands from around the world. It attracts more than 25,000 visitors annually.

Samsung has participated in Küchenmeile A30 since 2013, consistently using the platform to introduce its latest innovation in built-in products. This year, the company’s new showroom provides a novel experience for the wide range of stakeholders visiting the event.

Initially focused on Germany, the showroom is positioned to become a regional demonstration, training, and collaboration hub for Samsung teams and kitchen-industry partners across Europe.

Coherent Corp. Announces Timing of FY2027 First Quarter Earnings Release

October 14, 2026

PDF Version

SAXONBURG, Pa., Oct. 14, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Coherent Corp. (NYSE: COHR) (“Coherent,” “We,” or the “Company”), a global leader in photonics, announced today that it will release its financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, on Wednesday, November 4, after the New York Stock Exchange closes. The release will be followed by a live audio webcast at 4:30 p.m. ET to discuss the results.

The Company invites investors to join the live audio webcast at ir.coherent.com . The webcast will be recorded and a replay will be available within 24 hours after the live audio webcast on the company’s website.

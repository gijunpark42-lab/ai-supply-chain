Enplas Joins the ST Partner Program to Advance 60GHz Wireless Connectivity | enplas corporation

Enplas Corporation is pleased to announce its participation in the STMicroelectronics (ST) Partner Program as an ST Authorized Partner, further strengthening its RF lens solutions for customers developing products based on the ST60 60GHz wireless platform.

The ST Partner Program is a global ecosystem that brings together ST’s semiconductor technologies, microcontrollers, wireless modules, MEMS sensors, and the specialized expertise of Authorized Partners. Through this collaboration, customers can accelerate product development, address design challenges more efficiently, and shorten time-to-market.

Today, more than 320 Authorized Partners worldwide contribute to the ST ecosystem, helping customers develop complete end-to-end solutions across a wide range of applications.

The Importance of Antenna Design in Wireless Systems and Enplas’ Value Proposition

In wireless systems used in IoT devices, industrial equipment, smart devices, automotive applications, and robotics, antenna design plays a critical role in maximizing communication performance and overall product quality.

Enplas provides antenna optimization solutions for customers adopting ST’s 60 GHz millimeter-wave wireless connectivity solution, ST60, leveraging its extensive expertise in RF Lens technology.

By combining the ST60 platform with Enplas RF lens, customers can benefit from several technical advantages, including:

• Higher antenna gain extends communication range while providing greater flexibility in the placement and orientation of transmitting and receiving devices. This contributes to simpler system design and more efficient manufacturing processes.

• Compact RF lens solutions that support space-efficient product designs.

These advantages help accelerate the adoption of millimeter-wave wireless communication in applications where deployment has traditionally been challenging, while expanding the potential use cases of ST60-based solutions.

Driving New Use Cases Through Technical Collaboration

This technology collaboration aims at creating new use cases and expanding market opportunities for ST60-based wireless connectivity solutions. It initially focuses on the following application areas:

(1)Firmware Over-The-Air (FOTA):

Enabling wireless software and firmware updates throughout both manufacturing and field deployment environments.

This capability contributes to improved production efficiency, reduced rework, streamlined maintenance operations, and lower total operating costs.

(2)Communication Solutions for Robotics:

Extending communication range and reducing alignment constraints between the robot and its contactless docking station.

Enabling stable high-speed wireless communication across robotic joints and other rotating mechanisms, even in full-duplex communication environments.

The solution helps reduce dependence on mechanical components such as pogo pins and slip rings while improving design flexibility, maintainability, and overall system reliability.

Looking Ahead

Through its collaboration with STMicroelectronics, Enplas will continue to expand the application possibilities of millimeter-wave wireless communication technologies and promote the development of innovative wireless connectivity solutions.

Looking ahead, Enplas sees significant potential for further expansion into a wide range of applications, including:

• IoT Devices

• Industrial Equipment

• Smart Devices

• Aerospace

• Semiconductor Manufacturing Equipment

• Medical Devices

• Next-Generation Wireless Systems

Together with STMicroelectronics, Enplas will continue to explore new opportunities for ST60-based solutions, helping customers unlock the full potential of millimeter-wave wireless connectivity in emerging markets and next-generation applications.

Mycronic receives order for an SLX mask writer

2026-10-20 14:00:00

Mycronic AB (publ) has received an order for an SLX mask writer from an existing customer, for delivery to Europe. The order value is in the range of USD 5-7 million. Delivery of the system is planned for the third quarter of 2027.

The SLX laser mask writer meets rising demand for photomasks for the semiconductor industry and a need for replacement and modernization. Photomasks manufactured by laser mask writers are of high importance and account for a majority of all photomasks produced for semiconductor manufacturing. SLX is a modern and energy efficient mask writer based on the same technology as Mycronic’s mask writers for displays.

“We are pleased that one of our existing customers has chosen the cost-efficient and reliable SLX mask writer to phase out an older mask writer, enabling us to contribute to the development and modernization of the European semiconductor industry”, says Charlott Samuelsson, Senior Vice President Pattern Generators at Mycronic.

Mycronic’s Pattern Generators division provides mask writers and metrology, inspection and repair systems for display manufacturing and production of semiconductors.

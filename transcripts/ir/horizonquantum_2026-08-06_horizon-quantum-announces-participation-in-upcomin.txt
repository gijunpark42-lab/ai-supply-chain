Horizon Quantum Announces Participation in Upcoming Investor Conferences

August 6, 2026

PDF Version

SINGAPORE --(BUSINESS WIRE)--Aug. 6, 2026--

Horizon Quantum Holdings Ltd. (Nasdaq: HQ) (“Horizon Quantum,” “Horizon,” or “the Company”), a pioneer of software infrastructure for quantum applications, today announced that Dr. Joe Fitzsimons , Founder & Chief Executive Officer, and Greg Gould , Chief Financial Officer, will participate in the following upcoming investor conferences.

46th Annual Canaccord Genuity Growth Conference in Boston : On Tuesday, August 11 , Dr. Fitzsimons will participate in a fireside chat hosted by Kingsley Crane of Canaccord Genuity. Horizon’s discussion will begin at 8:30 AM ET , and the webcast link will be available on the Company’s investor website .

7th Annual Needham Virtual Semiconductor & Semicap 1x1 Conference: Horizon Quantum’s management team will host one-on-one meetings on Wednesday, August 19 .

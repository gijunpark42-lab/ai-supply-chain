Atsushi Horiba OBE, Chairman & Group CEO of HORIBA, Ltd., Named Honorary Fellow of University College London

09/29/2026

|

Press Release

UCL Honorary Fellowship awarded in recognition of partnership working toward the realisation of a sustainable society, including decarbonisation

HORIBA Group is proud to announce that Atsushi Horiba OBE, Chairman & Group CEO, has been named an Honorary Fellow of University College London (hereafter UCL), United Kingdom.

The title of Honorary Fellow is awarded to individuals who have made distinguished achievements in fields such as the arts, science, and business, or who have made outstanding contributions to UCL through service and other activities. Atsushi Horiba OBE was selected in recognition of the contributions made through the partnership between UCL and the HORIBA Group toward the realization of a sustainable society, including decarbonization.

The title was conferred at the UCL Fellowship Award ceremony held on September 10, 2026, at Royal Festival Hall, a prestigious cultural venue in London, UK.

Photo from the UCL Fellowship Award Ceremony. From left to right: Dr Michael Spence, President and Provost of UCL, and Atsushi Horiba OBE, Chairman & Group CEO of HORIBA, Ltd.

Photo credit: Marston Events.

Partnership with UCL

HORIBA UK Limited (Head Office: Northampton, United Kingdom; Managing Executive: Richard Carter), a HORIBA Group company, and UCL signed a partnership agreement in 2021 and have since advanced a broad range of collaborations, including personnel exchanges and joint research for new product development.

In September 2026, UCL established the HORIBA Chair of Advanced Propulsion Technologies, appointing Professor Thomas S. Miller of the UCL Faculty of Engineering as the inaugural post-holder. Together, UCL and HORIBA UK Limited are advancing efforts to help realize a decarbonized society through research into advanced propulsion technologies for highly efficient, low-emission transportation using next-generation mobility, including electric vehicles and fuel cell vehicles. In addition to the newly established Chair, HORIBA UK Limited supports two doctoral research students based in UCL’s Advanced Propulsion Lab, contributing to cutting-edge research and helping to develop the next generation of engineering talent.

Comments from Atsushi Horiba OBE, Chairman & Group CEO, HORIBA, Ltd.

“I am deeply honored to receive the title of Honorary Fellow from UCL. Since its founding, HORIBA Group has placed great importance on industry-academia collaboration and has worked with many universities and research institutions to address social challenges. Going forward, we will further strengthen our collaboration with UCL and devote even greater effort to advancing technologies that help realize a sustainable society, as well as to developing diverse talent that will support those technologies.

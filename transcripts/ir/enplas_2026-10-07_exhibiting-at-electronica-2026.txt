Exhibiting at electronica 2026 | enplas corporation

electronica is one of the world’s leading trade fairs for the electronics industry, covering a broad range of technologies including semiconductors, communications, automotive electronics, and industrial systems. Bringing together companies, research institutions, and technology experts from around the world, electronica serves as a platform for showcasing the latest innovations and solutions. Held every two years, the 2024 event welcomed more than 80,000 visitors and over 3,400 exhibitors.

Following the relocation of Enplas Corporation’s headquarters to Saitama City, Japan, Enplas will participate in the Saitama City Pavilion as a local company based in the city.

At electronica 2026, Enplas will showcase its Lens Antenna solutions for millimeter-wave radar and next-generation wireless communications. Demonstrations will include:

(a) Robotics Solution (wireless communication for robotic joints)

(b) FOTA Solution (Firmware Over-the-Air)

(c) Gesture Recognition (Touchless HMI)

Enplas Lens Antennas are compact, high-gain antenna solutions designed for millimeter-wave communications and radar applications in the 60 GHz to 300 GHz frequency range. They can be applied to a wide variety of applications, including automotive radar, 5G/6G communications, satellite communications, and industrial wireless systems.

We look forward to welcoming you to our booth.

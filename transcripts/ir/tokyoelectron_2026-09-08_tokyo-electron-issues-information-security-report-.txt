Tokyo Electron Issues Information Security Report 2026

Tokyo Electron (TEL; Head Office: Minato-ku, Tokyo; President: Toshiki Kawai) considers information security a material issue requiring focused and prioritized responses. Accordingly, TEL has issued the Information Security Report 2026, which systematically outlines the relevant activities of the TEL Group. In light of the ever-changing business environment and technological trends, the TEL Group is implementing various information security initiatives to both ensure business continuity and increase corporate value, as detailed in the report.

The report covers the following main topics:

(1) The governance and risk management structures related to information security and cybersecurity, as well as how they are coordinated with TEL's management strategies.

(2) Measures to strengthen the TEL Group's global business security, including protection against cyberattacks and physical security risks, as well as securing manufacturing plants and products.

(3) External security engagements and human resource development programs that aim to improve the information security level of the semiconductor industry and society as a whole.

To learn more, please visit:

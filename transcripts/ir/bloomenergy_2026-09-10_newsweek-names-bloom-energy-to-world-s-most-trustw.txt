Newsweek Names Bloom Energy to World’s Most Trustworthy Companies List in 2026

September 10, 2026

Bloom recognized as a trusted global leader among customers, investors, and employees

Ranking reflects 25 years of delivering reliable onsite power for businesses and communities worldwide

SAN JOSE, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--

Bloom Energy (NYSE: BE), a global leader in power solutions, today announced it has been named to Newsweek’s World’s Most Trustworthy Companies 2026 list in the Energy & Utilities category. The list, developed with Statista, recognizes 1,000 companies across 20 countries.

"Organizations and communities around the world rely on Bloom to power their most critical operations,” said Natalie Sunderland, CMO of Bloom Energy. “For 25 years, we've helped hospitals, colleges, manufacturers, retailers and data centers access clean, reliable power when and where they need it. We're honored to be recognized for the confidence our customers, employees and investors place in us."

Newsweek’s World’s Most Trustworthy Companies 2026 ranking, developed in partnership with Statista, recognizes 1,000 companies across 20 countries that have demonstrated trust among customers, investors and employees. The ranking is based on an independent survey of more than 65,000 participants who submitted approximately 200,000 company evaluations across three dimensions of trust, as well as a social listening analysis of more than 500,000 company mentions.

The World’s Most Trustworthy Companies 2026 list can be viewed on Newsweek’s website.

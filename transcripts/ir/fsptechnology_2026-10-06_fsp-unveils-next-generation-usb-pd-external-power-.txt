FSP Unveils Next-Generation USB PD External Power Supplies Compliant with EU (EU) 2025/2052 and PS2 Standards

2026-10-06

[Taipei, Oct. 6 th , 2026] FSP Group, a global leading power supply manufacturer, today officially introduced its latest series of high-power USB Type-C PD (Power Delivery) external power supply products to the market. All four newly launched external power adapters comply with the European Union's latest common charger and external power supply regulation (EU) 2025/2052, demonstrating FSP's solid R&D strength in green energy conversion and international regulatory compliance. Notably, the flagship model FSP100-A2BR3 concurrently complies with the IEC 62368-1 PS2 safety standard, delivering external power solutions that integrate top-tier energy efficiency, superior compatibility, and ultimate safety for global customers.

Compliance with EU (EU) 2025/2052: Combining Energy Efficiency, Interoperability, and Global Market Competitiveness

The European Union's latest Common Charger Regulation (EU) 2025/2052 establishes stringent technical thresholds for external power supplies (EPS), aiming to reduce electronic waste and promote the standardization of charging interfaces. FSP's newly released products fully comply with this regulation, delivering multiple key advantages to end customers:

Outstanding Energy Efficiency and Low Standby Power : The products meet the regulation's rigorous requirements for active mode average efficiency and no-load standby power consumption, significantly reducing waste heat generation and power loss during operation to achieve green energy-saving benefits.

High Interoperability with USB-C and PD Protocols : Fully adopting the standardized Type-C interface and universal fast-charging protocols, the products feature extensive cross-brand and cross-device compatibility, substantially simplifying users' charging accessory requirements and reducing redundant purchases.

Accelerated Global Compliance Certification and Time-to-Market : Achieving early compliance with the latest EU regulations helps brand customers and system integrators eliminate technical barriers to entering European and international markets, effectively mitigating redesign risks arising from regulatory updates and dramatically shortening end-product time-to-market.

Pioneering the IEC 62368-1 PS2 Safety Standard to Unleash Mechanical Design Flexibility

Among the products complying with (EU) 2025/2052, the flagship model FSP100-A2BR3 has further achieved IEC/EN 62368-1 PS2 (Power Source Class 2) safety certification. According to international electrical safety regulations, the PS2 category strictly limits output energy within a threshold unlikely to initiate an electrical fire. Compared to standard PS3 systems, external power supplies adhering to PS2 specifications precisely limit energy release during abnormal overload or equipment faults, substantially minimizing overheating and fire hazards. This advantage provides system integrators with enhanced mechanical design flexibility, allowing the use of lighter, more cost-effective flame-retardant enclosure materials, thereby enabling extreme slimness and lightweight form factors for end products while ensuring the highest safety standards.

Comprehensive Debut of Four New PD External Power Supplies: Covering 100W to 240W Diverse Applications

All four models introduced conform to the EU (EU) 2025/2052 regulation, feature universal wide-range AC input (90–264 VAC), have passed authoritative international CB certification and EN55032 / FCC Class B electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) testing, and integrate multiple protection mechanisms including Short Circuit Protection (SCP), Over Temperature Protection (OTP), Over Power Protection (OPP), and Over Voltage Protection (OVP):

FSP100-A2BR3 (100W)

Key Specifications : Complies with EU (EU) 2025/2052 regulation and is uniquely certified under CB 62368-1 (PS2 standard).

Features and Applications : Combines high conversion efficiency with an ultra-compact form factor, significantly suppressing standby power consumption and surface temperature rise. Tailor-made for high-end business laptops, smart office devices, and high-reliability ITE equipment, delivering both ultimate portability and top-tier safety protection.

FSP140-A3BR3 (140W)

Key Specifications : Complies with EU (EU) 2025/2052 regulation, supports the latest USB PD 3.2 protocol, and is certified under CB 62368-1.

Features and Applications : Features excellent high power density and dynamic load response capability, stably providing efficient fast charging via a single Type-C interface. Highly suited for professional creator laptops, premium audiovisual multimedia equipment, multifunctional docking stations, and compact workstations.

FSP240-A3BR3 (with C6 inlet) / FSP240-A3CR3 (with C8 inlet) (240W)

Key Specifications : Complies with EU (EU) 2025/2052 regulation, supports USB PD 3.2 (Extended Power Range - EPR) protocol, with output power reaching up to 240W.

Features and Applications : Breaks through conventional PD power limits, adopting an advanced topology architecture to achieve ultra-high conversion efficiency and superior thermal management. Satisfies extreme high-power fast-charging demands for high-end gaming laptops, mobile workstations, professional digital studio equipment, as well as next-generation micromobility vehicles (such as e-Bikes) and industrial handheld instruments.

FSP240-A4BR3 (240W)

Key Specifications : Complies with EU (EU) 2025/2052 regulation, supports USB PD 3.2 standard, delivers up to 240W output power, and features Peak Load capability.

Features and Applications : Specifically engineered for heavy computing workloads and industrial-grade stability demands, offering outstanding anti-interference capability and extended service life. Broadly applicable to various high-end ITE computing devices, industrial automation controllers, and specialized tech products.

Fulfilling "Powering AI Together": Empowering Intelligent Ecosystems with Micro-Customization Flexibility

FSP Group has consistently upheld its core corporate spirit of "Powering AI Together", committed to building a stable and reliable power foundation for partners across diverse domains. Facing rapidly evolving technology markets and increasingly stringent international standards, FSP not only continues to launch standard products meeting the latest EU (EU) 2025/2052 and PS2 safety specifications, but also leverages its exceptional R&D team to provide highly flexible micro-customization services.

From output cable length, connector specification fine-tuning, and flexible mechanical enclosure configuration, to granular firmware profile tuning of charging communication protocols and specific electrical parameter optimization, FSP can swiftly respond to clients' unique requirements with custom support, empowering partners to build terminal solutions that combine high compliance with market competitiveness.

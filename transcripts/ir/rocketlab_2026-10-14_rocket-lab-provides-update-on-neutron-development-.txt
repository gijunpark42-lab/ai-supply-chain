Rocket Lab Provides Update on Neutron Development and Adjusts Target Launch Timing

October 14, 2026

PDF Version

LONG BEACH, Calif., Oct. 14, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Rocket Lab Corporation (Nasdaq: RKLB) (“Rocket Lab” or “the Company”), a global leader in launch services and space systems, today shared an update on the Neutron launch vehicle development program, including an adjusted schedule for Neutron’s inaugural launch, which is now expected to occur in 2027.

A presentation discussing further details of this Neutron update is hosted on Rocket Lab’s investor relations website at https://investors.rocketlabcorp.com/events-presentations/presentations

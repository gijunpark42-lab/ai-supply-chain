ASUS Wins Ten 2026 European Hardware Association Reader Awards

September 18, 2026

・

Press Release

ASUS is EHA readers’ top choice for motherboards, graphics cards, gaming & portable monitors, desktop gaming PCs, gaming laptops, overclocking products, and routers

The EHA readers’ recognition validates the ASUS commitment to delivering user-centric products that meet cutting-edge design excellence

ASUS scoops ten awards across multiple categories, highlighting the company’s Design Thinking philosophy across a broad range of products

Market-leading ASUS and ROG gaming hardware helps secure wins across seven different product categories

TAIPEI, Taiwan, September 18, 2026 — ASUS today announced it has won ten 2026 European Hardware Association (EHA) Reader Awards in the categories of motherboards, graphics cards, gaming and portable monitors, desktop gaming PCs, gaming laptops, overclocking products, and routers.

The EHA readers' recognition is a testament to the ASUS commitment to delivering user-centric, feature-rich components, peripherals, and PCs. The nine distinguished European technology publications that comprise the EHA serve a combined audience of more than 20 million enthusiasts across 22 countries. The ASUS dedication to design excellence places it at the cutting edge of enthusiast computing.

The world's top-selling ASUS motherboards

ASUS won the Best Intel Motherboard and Best AMD Motherboard categories. For over 20 years, ASUS motherboards have set the global standard — with more than 600 million units sold and countless industry awards. Trusted for their stability, intuitive design, and ease of use, they are the preferred choice of gamers, creators, and professionals. With AI-powered features like AI Cache Boost, AI Advisor, and NPU Boost, plus DIY-friendly Q-Design, ASUS motherboards deliver powerful performance and a seamless building experience.

People First: Accton’s Caring Across Generations

ESG Spotlight / Social 👪 / Accton Human Resource Department, Accton Health Management Division, Accton E-Charity / August 27th, 2026

Accton believes that sustainability stems not only from operational growth but also from long-term support for employees and their families across all ages. With “Caring Across Generations” as an important principle of talent development, Accton responds to employees’ needs at different life stages, including marriage, childbirth, parenting, and health promotion, by providing appropriate resources to help them balance family responsibilities with career development. Accton also extends its care to retired employees and seniors in society. By connecting corporate resources with families and communities, Accton responds to emerging needs in a super-aged society and puts its people-first approach into practice as part of its commitment to sustainability.

Supporting Family Formation and Parenting

First, to support employees in starting a family, Accton launches a marriage allowance. Employees who marry after joining Accton and have each completed at least one year of service are eligible to receive a combined NT$6,000 monthly allowance. This aims to help ease the financial burden associated with starting a family and enables employees to plan their future with greater peace of mind.

In addition, to support maternal protection, Accton provides a comprehensive range of maternal care services. In addition to reserving dedicated parking spaces for expectant mothers, professional nurses also provide health consultations and education to expectant mothers before, during, and after pregnancy. A special maternity gift is also offered upon pregnancy notification to encourage early reporting, enabling timely support and helping employees welcome the arrival of a new family member.

Furthermore, Accton places strong emphasis on parenting support. It was the first Company in the Hsinchu Science Park to establish both a kindergarten and a childcare center, providing childcare services for employees’ children from zero to six years of age and helping to ease the childcare pressure of working parents. From marriage and childbirth to parenting, Accton has gradually developed a comprehensive family support system that addresses employees’ needs at different life stages and provides practical support for employees and their families.

Completing Family Support, Promoting Work-life Balance

Additionally, Accton continues to promote diverse initiatives to foster a supportive workplace that enables employees to maintain a healthy work-life balance. In addition to providing statutory leave entitlements, Accton has introduced flexible working arrangements and provides one day of flexible leave each quarter, allowing employees to manage their work and personal commitments according to their individual needs. For employees requiring extended recovery due to a serious illness or injury, Accton also provides up to one year of paid medical leave with job protection, as well as shuttle services for follow-up visits, enabling employees to focus on their recovery.

Accton also recognizes the importance of spending time with family and provides employees with two days of paid family care leave per year to encourage them to spend time with their parents and grandparents. Whether for family trips, holiday reunions, or medical appointments, the leave provides greater flexibility for employees to manage their work and family responsibilities. In addition, Accton provides two days of fully paid volunteer leave each year, enabling employees to participate in internal and external charitable activities according to their personal interests and schedules. These initiatives encourage employees to contribute to causes they care about while maintaining a balance between work and personal life.

Through flexible leave arrangements and a range of employee benefits, Accton integrates employee care into its daily management and continues to foster a supportive and resilient workplace that promotes employee well-being and work-life balance.

Strengthening Health Across All Ages, Supporting Employee Well-being

In addition to fostering a culture that supports work-life balance, Accton continues to strengthen its health and well-being programs for employees of all ages. By bringing together a professional team of occupational nurses and occupational medicine specialists, the Company promotes health promotion, risk stratification, and early intervention in accordance with the three-stage, five-level framework of preventive medicine, providing employee health services, reducing disease risks, and improving the efficiency of medical resource utilization. Accton also provides annual health examinations and cancer screenings that exceed statutory requirements, enabling employees to better understand their health status at an early stage and maintain their ability to work and quality of life through comprehensive health management.

Accton also places a strong emphasis on employees’ mental health and well-being. The Company regularly organizes a range of health and well-being seminars covering mental health and illness prevention, emotional well-being, and workplace adaptation, as well as topics relevant to different stages of life, including long-term care, family financial planning, and parenting. These seminars help employees develop sound health awareness and self-care skills, while extending health awareness and practices into family life.

Accton continues to focus on the health and well-being of senior employees as well. Employees aged 45 and above are offered work aptitude assessments to identify potential health and occupational risks. Based on the assessment results, external occupational medicine specialists provide individual health consultations to help employees identify health risks at an early stage and maintain their ability to work. Through these measures, Accton supports employees of different ages in maintaining their health and work ability, putting its commitment to health and well-being across all stages of life into practice.

Extending Care to Elders and Advancing Well-being Across Generations

Accton’s caring across generations is not only for current employees and their families, but also extends to retired employees and the elders in society to respond to the new needs of Taiwan’s super-aged society.

For retired employees, Accton provides an Employee Transition Assistance Program to help them adapt to life after retirement. In addition to offering opportunities for retirees to return to work and continue contributing their professional expertise and experience, Accton provides hybrid work models and flexible hours, allowing retirees to choose how and when to participate in projects. The Company also provides a range of life support services, including legal, medical and family counseling, referrals to long-term care and social welfare resources, and financial assistance. These measures help retired employees navigate this new stage of life while extending Accton’s commitment to supporting its people beyond retirement.

At the same time, Accton E-Charity, established by Accton Technology, has also devoted itself to seniors in society. Since 2022, Accton E-Charity has teamed up with the Chiayi City Government Health Bureau to launch the “Grandpa and Grandma Sharing is Not NG” project, fostering elders’ interview and program-editing skills through the “Stylish Seniors on Air” podcast, encouraging them to learn continuously, and fostering communication across generations.

Besides the Chiayi City Government Health Bureau, Accton E-Charity also partnered with the Accton Cultural and Educational Foundation and the artists supported by the Foundation to launch “The Time Capsule in the Living Room” project. It also cooperated with Yunlin Chang Tai Day Care Center with the “sī-tuā-lâng, tàu-tīnuē ― Walking into Taiwanese Art History with the Elders” program, leading elders to have artful thinking, communicate and create, and rediscover their value. This year, the result was also exhibited at the Hsinchu Railway Art Village, inviting the public to explore the daily lives and personal stories of elders and experience the intergenerational connections fostered through artistic creation.

Moreover, to promote self-reliance support, Accton E-Charity has collaborated with Chang Tai Day Care System to operate the self-media channel “Jin Li Wang Sheng – Accton E-Charity Accompanies You Through 50+” on mainstream social platforms Facebook, podcasts, YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram, raising the awareness of healthy ageing and everyday care, helping to raise public awareness and understanding of the principles of independent living and support for older adults.

In the future, Accton will continuously connect corporate, family, and social resources to build a more comprehensive caring system for cross-generational support, ensuring each generation has appropriate support at different life stages and creating a more resilient, sustainable future.

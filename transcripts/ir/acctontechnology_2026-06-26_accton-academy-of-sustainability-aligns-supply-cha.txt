Accton Academy of Sustainability Aligns Supply Chain as Zero-Carbon Exemplar

ESG Spotlight / Environmental 🌄 / Accton Academy of Sustainability / June 26th, 2026

As the global supply chain faces the challenges of net-zero transformation, corporate sustainability is no longer just about reducing a single company’s own carbon footprint, but about driving the entire value chain forward. As a leading global provider of network communications equipment, Accton well understands that the goal of net-zero emissions by 2050 cannot be achieved by any single enterprise alone. The true challenge lies in equipping every partner within the supply chain with carbon reduction capabilities, transforming sustainability concepts into concrete actions.

To address this, Accton launched the “Zero Carbon Chain Project,” centering its efforts around the “Accton Academy of Sustainability.” Partnering with ten key supply chain members, the initiative drives collaborative efforts in energy management, product carbon footprint tracking, and process optimization. Together, they are building a low-carbon supply chain ecosystem, extending sustainability from internal corporate practices out into the wider value chain.

Accton Academy of Sustainability: Resolving Carbon Reduction Pain Points for SMEs

During the initial phases of the initiative, the Accton team discovered that while many small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) were willing to reduce carbon emissions, they faced three common hurdles:

A lack of methodology and starting points for carbon accounting.

Difficulty identifying high-energy-consuming equipment and processes.

An inability to effectively evaluate the actual performance of mitigation measures.

To resolve these pain points, Accton established the “Accton Academy of Sustainability.” Through systematic coursework and practical on-site guidance, the academy helps suppliers build independent management capabilities. Within a single year, Accton organized multiple joint forums and educational training sessions covering greenhouse gas inventory, product carbon footprinting, Life Cycle Assessment (LCA), internal carbon pricing mechanisms, ISO 20400 sustainable procurement guidelines, carbon reduction methodologies, and circular economy strategies. Concurrently, expert teams were dispatched to conduct on-site factory diagnostics. By analyzing production workflows, energy usage, and improvement opportunities for each supplier, they provided tailored, actionable recommendations, enabling supply chain partners to truly develop autonomous carbon management capabilities.

Data-Driven Tech and Platform Collaboration: Creating a Common Language for Low-Carbon Manufacturing

Beyond talent cultivation, Accton has integrated digital technology into its carbon reduction workflows. Utilizing Energy Management Systems (EMS) and Internet of Things (IoT) technologies, the team can monitor energy hotspots during manufacturing in real time. In the SMT production lines at the Hsinchu HSR (Zhubei) AI Park, Accton redesigned production workflows and equipment configurations, successfully reducing unit power consumption from 0.92 kWh to 0.57 kWh per unit, representing a 38% increase in energy efficiency. Expanding this optimization across 15 production lines saves over 3.6 million kWh of electricity annually, equivalent to a reduction of 1,826 metric tons of CO₂ emissions, achieving low-carbon manufacturing characterized by “increased capacity, decreased emissions.”

To meet international clients’ demands for carbon information disclosure, Accton further developed a “Product Carbon Footprint Collaborative Platform.” Once fully deployed, this platform will integrate process energy consumption, product life cycles, and supply chain data, allowing product carbon emissions to be quantified, tracked, and managed. Through real-time data and standardized methodologies, suppliers and customers can collaborate to identify emission sources and pinpoint areas for improvement, making the platform a common language for supply chain carbon reduction dialogues.

Supplier-Led Transformation: Shifting from Added Costs to Enhanced Competitiveness

As the Accton Academy of Sustainability continues to deepen its engagement, partner enterprises have successfully merged carbon reduction with lean production principles, actively driving autonomous improvement initiatives. Tangible outcomes include:

Win-Laser Technology: Introduced nano-coating technology to lower steel plate cleaning frequency, boosting production line efficiency by approximately 10% while reducing material and energy waste.

All Best Electronics: Deployed automated assembly and CCD inspection equipment, slashing machinery power consumption by 44% while simultaneously improving production quality and efficiency.

Max Echo Technology: Simplified manufacturing processes using triaxial mixing equipment, cutting down the use of high-energy-consuming machinery and significantly increasing energy-saving benefits.

Scaling from One to Ten: Driving Collective Industry Prosperity

Through the Zero Carbon Chain Project, the overall energy-saving rate reached 20.5%, resulting in an annual carbon reduction of 3,836 metric tons of CO₂e, which surpassed original targets. However, for Accton, the more meaningful achievement is the establishment of a sustainable, self-sustaining management mechanism.

Spanning education, on-site diagnostics, energy management, carbon footprint platforms, and sustainable procurement frameworks, Accton is progressively translating the carbon reduction expertise of a large corporation into a driving force for collective supply chain growth. Moving forward, the Accton Academy of Sustainability will continue to expand its influence, helping more partners upgrade their carbon management capabilities. True sustainability is not about sprinting ahead alone; it is about moving forward together with your partners

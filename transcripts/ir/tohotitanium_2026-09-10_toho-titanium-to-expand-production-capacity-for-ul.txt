Toho Titanium to Expand Production Capacity for Ultra-Fine Nickel Powder Used in MLCCs for AI Data Centers

Toho Titanium Co., Ltd. (“the Company” President: Hyakuno Manabu) has decided to expand its production capacity for smaller-particle-size ultra-fine nickel powder used in multilayer ceramic capacitors (MLCCs). The Company plans to phase the new equipment into operation at its Chigasaki Plant (Chigasaki City, Kanagawa Prefecture) beginning in 2027, doubling its production capacity for smaller-particle-size products. The company will subsequently expand its supply capacity in stages in line with demand trends.

In recent years, the rapid adoption of generative AI has driven the construction of AI data centers worldwide, resulting in growing demand for the electronic components that high-performance servers and peripheral equipment require. High-end MLCCs, in particular, must combine smaller size and higher capacitance with a high degree of reliability. Accordingly, demand is also increasing for finer and more consistent nickel powder used as an internal electrode material.

The Company manufactures ultra-fine nickel powder at its Chigasaki Plant and Wakamatsu Plant (Kitakyushu City, Fukuoka Prefecture). By applying the proprietary production technology it has derived from its titanium smelting expertise, The Company is able to manufacture ultra-fine nickel powder with a narrow particle size distribution. Its ultra-fine nickel powder is used in the internal electrodes of high-end MLCCs.

To meet the anticipated growth in demand from AI data centers and other applications, the Company will expand the facilities at its Chigasaki Plant and strengthen its mass-production capabilities for smaller-particle-size ultra-fine nickel powder.

Through this capacity expansion, the Company will further strengthen its ability to supply ultra-fine nickel powder for high-end MLCCs, which are becoming increasingly compact and higher in capacitance. The Company will also continue working to establish a stable supply system capable of meeting future growth in the MLCCs market.

# Belimo Strengthens Its Hinwil, Zurich Site with New Production and Logistics Building 

With the official opening of its new production and logistics building, Belimo is making a strategic 

investment in the future of its Hinwil site. The new building creates additional capacity for further 

growth, strengthens global delivery capability, and d emonstrates how innovative building technology 

and sustainability can successfully work together. 

Hinwil (Switzerland), August 25, 2026  – Together with representatives from government and industry, as 

well as project and construction partners, Belimo officially inaugurated the new production and logistics 

building at its headquarters in Hinwil. With this new building, the company is making a  strategic investment in 

additional production and logistics capacity, thereby strengthening its ability to continue providing customers 

worldwide with the highest product availability, quality, and short delivery times. 

The investment in Hinwil is part of Belimo’s long -term growth strategy and is based on forward -looking 

capacity planning enabling production and logistics capacities to be expanded in a timely manner. This 

foresight is proving to be an important advantage  as in the first half of 2026, Belimo increased sales in local 

currencies by 29.6 percent to CHF 676.4 million. This investment ensures that Belimo is able to reliably meet 

both the current very high demand as well as the expected growth in the future. 

At the same time, Belimo is continuously expanding its capacity in other key markets, including the USA, 

Germany, and China. The headquarters in Hinwil remains one of the central production, logistics, and 

innovation sites within the global network. “With  this investment , we are creating the conditions for further 

global growth, increased delivery capability, and sustainable production at our headquarters in Hinwil. The 

opening is a joint success and an important milestone for Belimo,” says Lars van der Haegen, CEO Belimo .

State -of -the -art logistics for sustainable growth 

As one of the most advanced production and logistics buildings in the Belimo network, the new building relies 

on fully automated material flow and forward -looking logistics technolog ies. An automated high -bay 

warehouse with 3,000 pallet spaces and an automated small -parts warehouse with 80,000 container spaces 

ensure maximum efficiency, transparency, and supply reliability. The concept is complemented by the use of 

automated guided ve hicles (AGVs), which autonomously manage internal material supply based on demand. 

The high level of automation in production, storage, and transport provides the foundation  for sustainable 

growth, increases productivity, and strengthens the long -term competitiveness of the Hinwil site. 

Sustainability consistently implemente d

The new production and logistics building meets the highest standards for energy efficiency and sustainability. 

Belimo products control the building’s heating, ventilation, and air  conditioning systems and ensure energy -

efficient operation. A large part of the façades as well as the available roof surfaces have been equipped with 

photovoltaic modules. This increases the installed capacity at the headquarters from 200 kWp to around 70 0

kWp and covers a significant portion of the energy required for building cooling in summer. For heating in 

winter, the building is connected to the district heating network of KEZO (waste recycling plant in the area of 

Zürcher Oberland) and uses the residual heat generated during waste incineration. 

“The consistent use of our own technologies demonstrates how sustainable buildings with the highest energy 

efficiency can be achieved,” says Lars van der Haegen. “It combines innovation, productivity, and 

responsibility, thereby reflecting Belimo’s values. ”

The building was constructed on an approximately 8,500 -square -meter (91,500 sq ft) plot at Wässeristrasse 

21 in Hinwil and is directly connected to the existing production and logistics center via a skybridge. The 

existing production area of 12,600 square meters was expanded by an additional 6,200 square meters. A fully 

automated storage system ensures effici ent material flow and supports high product availability. The Belimo Group is a global market leader in the development, production, and sales of field devices for the energy 

efficient control of heating, ventilation, and air -conditioning systems. The focus of its core business is on damper actuators, 

control valves, sensors and meters. In 2025, the Company reported sales of CHF 1 121 million and over 2 800 employees. 

Informat ion about the Company and its products is available at www.belimo.com. The shares of BELIMO Holding AG have 

been traded on the SIX Swiss Exchange since 1995 (BEAN).

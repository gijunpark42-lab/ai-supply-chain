AIXTRON Q3 2026: Order intake and operating profitability significantly exceed market expectations

Herzogenrath, October 15, 2026 – AIXTRON SE (FSE: AIXA, ISIN DE000A0WMPJ6) has determined preliminary results for the third quarter of 2026. Order intake, operating result (EBIT) and EBIT margin significantly exceed current market expectations.

Preliminary Q3 2026 order intake amounted to approximately EUR 275 million, vs. EUR 124.0 million in the prior-year quarter. This was significantly above the median analyst estimate of EUR 214.6 million based on the company's current consensus, and also well above the EUR 214.5 million reported in Q2 2026.

Preliminary revenue in Q3 2026 amounted to approximately EUR 184 million (Q3 2025: EUR 119.6 million) well within the forecast range for the quarter of EUR 180 million plus or minus EUR 20 million.

Preliminary operating profitability exceeded market expectations, in particular due to a favorable product mix. Q3 2026 gross profit reached approximately EUR 87 million, corresponding to a gross margin of approximately 48% (Q3 2025: EUR 46.4 million; 39%). Preliminary operating result (EBIT) amounted to approximately EUR 57 million, with an EBIT margin of approximately 31% (Q3 2025: EUR 15.4 million; 13%).

The guidance for fiscal year 2026 remains unchanged.

The full quarterly group statement for the third quarter of 2026, including the results for the first nine months of 2026, will be published as scheduled on October 29, 2026.

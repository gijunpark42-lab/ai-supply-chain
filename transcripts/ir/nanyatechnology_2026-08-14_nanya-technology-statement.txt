Nanya Technology strictly enforces a zero-tolerance policy against any infringement or unauthorized use of the Company's intellectual property, as well as any theft, disclosure, misappropriation, or improper use of the Company's trade secrets, and will pursue all appropriate legal remedies against responsible parties.

FUJIFILM Business Innovation Announces Optimization of Production and Sales Structure for Printing Plate Materials to Strengthen Graphic Communications Business

Tokyo, September 9, 2026 – FUJIFILM Business Innovation Corp. (President, Representative Director: Naoki Hama) today announced that it will optimize and reorganize its production and sales structure for printing plate materials as part of its ongoing efforts to strengthen its Graphic Communications business. As part of this initiative, FUJIFILM Business Innovation will transfer its production, sales, and related business functions for printing plate materials (excluding Japan, the United States, Canada and Mexico) to Huangshan Jinruitai Technology Co., Ltd. and its affiliates, a leading producer for printing plate materials based in China and a long-standing business partner of Fujifilm in this field.

FUJIFILM Business Innovation has contributed to the growth of the printing industry as the only comprehensive vendor offering a one-stop portfolio spanning offset printing materials – such as printing plate materials – to digital printing equipment, as well as DX solutions that improve the efficiency of printing workflows.

In recent years, the printing industry has undergone significant transformation, driven by the advancement of digital transformation (DX), increasing demand for automation and labor-saving solutions, and the diversified customer needs. These changes have accelerated the shift from offset to digital printing, alongside growing demand for higher value-added digital presses.

In response to this evolving market environment, FUJIFILM Business Innovation has been strengthening its business structure in the offset printing domain. Key initiatives include the development and provision of processless plates as next-generation printing plates, as well as the optimization of its global production structure. The business transfer forms part of these ongoing structural initiatives. Going forward, in this domain, FUJIFILM Business Innovation will concentrate on the production of printing plate materials in Japan and sales for markets in Japan, the U.S., Canada and Mexico.

Looking ahead, in its Graphic Communications business FUJIFILM Business Innovation will further accelerate global growth in the digital printing domain, where continued market expansion is expected. The Company will enhance the development of digital presses and related solutions, centered on the Revoria Press™ series. By delivering solutions that leverage AI technologies to automate and streamline printing operations and enable high value-added print expressions, FUJIFILM Business Innovation will continue to support customer value creation.

Naoki Hama, President and Representative Director, FUJIFILM Business Innovation Corp.

"FUJIFILM Business Innovation is accelerating its transformation toward a solutions-driven business model and strengthening its business foundation to support the next stage of growth.

As part of our ongoing efforts to achieve sustainable growth and enhance corporate value, today's announced optimization of our production and sales structure for printing plate materials will further strengthen our competitiveness. We will continue striving to be a company of choice for our customers and the market."

Summary of Business Transfer

Overview

The transfer includes all equity interests held by FUJIFILM (China) Investment Co., Ltd. in FUJIFILM Printing Plate (China) Co., Ltd., a manufacturer and seller of printing plate materials

Excluding Japan, the United States, Canada and Mexico, the transfer of the sales and related business functions for printing plate materials, and the grant of license to use product brands and certain intellectual property rights, including patents.

Business transfer to

Huangshan Jinruitai Technology Co., Ltd. and its affiliates

Transfer Price

Undisclosed

Date of Sign

August 28, 2026

Effective Date of the Business Transfer

The transfer of all equity interests in FUJIFILM Printing Plate (China) Co., Ltd. is scheduled to be completed by the end of 2026, while the transfer of the sales and related business functions for printing plate materials in regions excluding Japan, the United States, Canada and Mexico is scheduled to be completed in the first half of 2027.

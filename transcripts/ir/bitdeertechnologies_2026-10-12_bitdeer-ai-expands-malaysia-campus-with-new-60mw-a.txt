Bitdeer AI Expands Malaysia Campus with New 60MW A103 Facility, Closing in on Up-to-350MW Global Capacity Target

October 12, 2026

PDF Version

New 8-year data center services agreement for the A103 facility at the Cyberjaya campus brings secured AI Cloud data center facility to approximately 333.5MW, designed for next-generation NVIDIA Vera Rubin deployment

SINGAPORE, Oct. 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Bitdeer AI, part of Bitdeer Technologies Group (NASDAQ: BTDR) (“Bitdeer AI”), an AI neocloud service provider and preferred NVIDIA Cloud Partner, today announced that it has entered into an 8-year data center services agreement for A103, a 60MW AI Cloud data center facility on the same Cyberjaya, Malaysia campus as its A101 and A102 facilities.

A103 is designed for liquid-cooled, rack-scale NVIDIA systems, including the next-generation NVIDIA Vera Rubin platform, with energization expected in the first quarter of 2028. A103 is located on the existing Cyberjaya campus allowing Bitdeer AI to leverage established power, liquid cooling and networking to shorten the path to energization. Together with the A101 and A102 facilities, the Cyberjaya campus will represent approximately 71.5MW of AI Cloud data center capacity.

With the addition of the A103 facility, Bitdeer AI’s secured AI Cloud data center capacity — capacity owned or under an executed data center services agreement — totals approximately 333.5MW across facilities in Malaysia, Norway and the United States, representing approximately 95% of its target of up to 350MW to be delivered by the first quarter of 2028. All megawatt figures in this release refer to critical IT load.

Demand for uncontracted capacity across Bitdeer AI’s AI Cloud sites remains strong. Bitdeer AI’s active pipeline for AI Cloud capacity is estimated to exceed $10 billion.

“We have secured approximately 95% of our up-to-350MW target with the addition of the A103 facility,” said Michael G. Potter, Chief Financial Officer of Bitdeer Technologies Group. “Adding 60MW alongside the A101 and A102 facilities means we benefit from the power, the cooling and the network infrastructure already in place, improving our time-to-market. A103 is also built for the Vera Rubin generation, which is the capacity customers are asking us about for 2028.”

Pasqal Achieves ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Certification for Its Information Security Management System Spanning R&D to Customer Support

15 October 2026

See in French

As enterprises and governments scale their use of quantum computing, Pasqal’s information security management system is independently certified against the internationally recognized standard

PARIS, October 15, 2026 – Pasqal (Nasdaq: PSQL), a global leader in neutral-atom quantum computing, today announced that its Information Security Management System (ISMS) has achieved ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification, the internationally recognized standard for information security, cybersecurity and privacy protection. The certification provides customers, particularly in regulated industries, with independent assurance that Pasqal systematically identifies, assesses, and manages information security risks across its certified activities, as they transition quantum computing from pilots to production.

As quantum computing expands into increasingly sensitive and business-critical applications, from financial risk modeling to energy systems and advanced materials research, organizations expect their technology partners to demonstrate effective risk management and compliance, with internationally recognized security standards.

“In quantum computing, trust is not a feature you add once the technology works. It has to be built in from the first qubit,” said Wasiq Bokhari, CEO of Pasqal. “Our customers are preparing to run some of their most sensitive workloads on quantum computers. That is why we have built information security into the way we design, develop, produce and support our solutions, and this certification recognizes that work.”

Certified by SGS under COFRAC accreditation after an independent audit, Pasqal’s Information Security Management System (ISMS) covers activities related to the design, development, industrialization, deployment, maintenance and support of quantum computing solutions and associated services across its sites in France, Canada and Saudi Arabia. This scope includes hardware R&D, QPU production, software development, product management, sales, procurement, operations and corporate functions, including legal and intellectual property.

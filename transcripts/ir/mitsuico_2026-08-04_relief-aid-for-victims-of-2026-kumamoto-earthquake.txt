Relief Aid for Victims of 2026 Kumamoto Earthquake

Aug. 4, 2026

Facebook

Main Contents

We would like to express our sincere condolences and sympathies for those who have perished in, and all who have been impacted by, the recent tragic earthquake in the Kumamoto region.

In consideration of the serious damage caused by the earthquake that struck the Kumamoto region on July 28, 2026, as well as the many victims who are subsequently suffering from the effects of the disaster, Mitsui & Co., Ltd. ("Mitsui", Head Office: Tokyo, President and CEO: Kenichi Hori) announced today that Mitsui will make a JPY 10 million donation to provide aid for the affected areas and people.

Officers and employees of Mitsui will also make donations to aid relief efforts.

We sincerely wish for a swift recovery from this tragedy.

The information contained in this release is true and accurate at the time of publication; however, it may be subject to change without prior notice.

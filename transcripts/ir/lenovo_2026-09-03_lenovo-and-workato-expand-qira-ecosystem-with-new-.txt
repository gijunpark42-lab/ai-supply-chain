Lenovo and Workato Expand Qira Ecosystem with New Cross-App AI Experiences

New integrations help Lenovo™ and Motorola® Qira users move from intent to action across everyday apps and services using natural language.

3 September 2026

September 3, 2026 – Lenovo today announced a strategic collaboration with Workato to expand the connected ecosystem behind Lenovo and Motorola Qira, with Workato serving as the connectivity layer powering Lenovo Qira’s underlying Model Context Protocol (MCP) server infrastructure.

Through the collaboration, Lenovo Qira will be able to connect with many of the tools people already use to work, plan, communicate, and stay organized, helping users move more seamlessly from intent to action across apps and services through natural language. Initial integrations include Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Trello, Asana, and Discord, allowing users to accomplish tasks without constantly switching between services. Additional integrations are planned as Lenovo continues to expand the Lenovo Qira ecosystem.

For example, a user could ask, “Hey Lenovo Qira, create a Trello task for my website redesign project,” or “Hey Lenovo Qira, share today’s meeting notes with my team in Discord.” Instead of manually moving between applications, Lenovo Qira helps users continue from a single natural language request across connected services.

“ Lenovo Qira is designed to understand your context across devices and help you move naturally from intent to action. It knows you, it acts in your world and is private to only you ,” said Dan Dery, Vice President of AI Ecosystem, Lenovo . “ Working with Workato brings together Lenovo’s global device reach with an enterprise-grade integration platform to deliver a more ambient AI experience that works across the apps and services users already rely on. It is the first phase of a multi-stage agreement, beginning with Lenovo Qira for consumers and extending toward a broader Lenovo Qira marketplace .”

“ Agentic AI becomes significantly more valuable when it can securely interact with the tools and services people already use ,” said Bhaskar Roy, Chief of AI, Workato . “ We’re excited to support Lenovo Qira with AI control and execution capabilities that help connect AI experiences to the applications, data, and workflows that matter most. “

For consumers, the collaboration further advances Lenovo Qira’s vision as a built-in, cross-device intelligence that helps people interact more naturally with their devices, applications, and digital services. By combining Lenovo’s AI experience with Workato’s integration capabilities, Lenovo Qira helps users move from intent to action across connected services through natural language, spending less time switching between apps and more time getting things done.

Looking ahead, Lenovo and Workato plan to explore additional integrations, agent experiences, marketplace capabilities, and opportunities for developer participation, helping expand the Lenovo Qira ecosystem and create greater value for users over time.

Learn more about Lenovo Qira here: http://www.lenovo.com/qira .

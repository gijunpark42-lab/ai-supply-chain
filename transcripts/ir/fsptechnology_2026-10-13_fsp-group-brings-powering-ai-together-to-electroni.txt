FSP Group Brings Powering AI Together to electronica 2026

2026-10-13

Summary

FSP Group will showcase power supply solutions for AI servers, data centers, networking, edge computing and terminal devices at electronica 2026. Highlights include 19-inch and 21-inch OCP ORv3 Power Shelves up to 33 kW, CRPS solutions up to 6,000 W, redundant and ATX 3.1 power supplies, PoE open-frame solutions, USB PD adapters and medical power supplies.

---

[Taipei, Oct. 13 rd , 2026] FSP Group, a global leading power supply manufacturer, will exhibit at electronica 2026, taking place from November 10–13, 2026 in Munich, Germany. Under the theme "Powering AI Together", FSP Group will present its comprehensive power supply solutions for AI computing, data centers, networking, edge computing, industrial systems, and terminal devices at Hall A5, Booth 236.

As AI drives higher computing performance and power demand is increasing, system developers face a broader set of power supply requirements. Beyond efficiency and power density, factors such as redundancy, form factor, power distribution, and system integration can influence the choice of power architecture.

At electronica 2026, FSP Group will demonstrate how different power supply architectures can meet these requirements. The exhibit will bring together standardized platforms and application-specific approaches across a broad range of power levels and form factors.

Power Supply Solutions from AI Infrastructure to the Edge

The FSP Group exhibit will include solutions for high-power computing platforms, network infrastructure, industrial equipment, and end devices. Key technologies on display will include:

OCP ORv3 Power Shelf – covering both 19-inch and 21-inch formats, with output up to 33 kW for high-power data center and AI computing infrastructure.

CRPS solutions up to 6,000 W – spanning 12 V models up to 4,000 W for servers and AI computing, 54 V models up to 6,000 W for high-power AI.

CRPS PDB solutions – adapting standardized CRPS output to ATX power distribution, with 1U 1+1, 2U 1+1 and 1U 1+0 configurations for different server architectures, redundancy requirements and space constraints.

1U redundant power supplies up to 1,400 W – developed for applications that require compact form factors and continuous power availability.

ATX 3.1 power supplies up to 2,000 W – providing reliable power support for industrial PCs, workstations and high-performance computing systems.

PoE open-frame power supplies up to 950 W – developed for high-power networking applications, including designs with isolated 54 V and 12 V outputs in a compact 1U-compatible form factor.

USB PD adapters up to 240 W – covering a broad USB Power Delivery portfolio for laptops, mobile devices and other USB-C-powered equipment.

Slim adapters up to 240 W – delivering higher output power in a compact, slim form factor for edge AI and other space-constrained applications.

Power supplies for medical equipment – spanning ATX, adapter and open-frame solutions for applications from diagnostic and treatment equipment to rehabilitation and home healthcare.

Together, the portfolio gives system designers a range of power architectures and form factors to address different electrical, mechanical, and platform integration requirements.

Flexible Power Supply Integration Through Micro Customization

Alongside its standard power supply portfolio, FSP Group offers "Micro Customization" to meet application requirements that off-the-shelf products cannot fully address. The approach combines established power supply platforms with targeted modifications, allowing selected aspects of a standard design to be adapted to specific system requirements.

This approach can help customers shorten development cycles while building on an established product platform. At electronica 2026, FSP Group will present Micro Customization as part of its "Powering AI Together" concept and engage with system developers and equipment manufacturers on adapting standard power supply platforms to specific electrical, mechanical and integration requirements.

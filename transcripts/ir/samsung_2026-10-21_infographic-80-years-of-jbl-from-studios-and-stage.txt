on October 21, 2026

Share open

The devices people use to listen to music and the ways they enjoy it have changed over time. How has JBL, the professional audio brand of HARMAN, a Samsung subsidiary, maintained its appeal amid these shifts?

As JBL celebrates its 80th anniversary in 2026, the company’s history is closely intertwined with how people create and enjoy music. Audio technologies refined in recording studios and live venues have made their way into everyday life, while JBL’s products have evolved alongside changing listening habits. Decades of innovation and experience have helped define JBL’s signature sound.

Samsung Newsroom traces JBL’s 80-year journey through its iconic products and defining moments in the infographic below.

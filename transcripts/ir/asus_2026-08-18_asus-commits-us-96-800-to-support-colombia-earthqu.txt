ASUS Commits US$96,800 to Support Colombia Earthquake Recovery Efforts

August 18, 2026

・

Press Release

ASUS today announced a donation of US$96,800 to support relief and recovery efforts following the devastating earthquake that recently impacted communities across Colombia.

The funds will be distributed through World Vision Taiwan and Sociedad de Mejoras de Pereira to support emergency assistance, recovery programs, and resources for individuals and families affected by the disaster.

ASUS extends its deepest sympathies to everyone affected by the earthquake and stands in solidarity with the people of Colombia during this challenging time.

The company is grateful to report that all ASUS employees in Colombia and their immediate family members are safe. ASUS has remained in close contact with local teams while monitoring developments and assessing community needs.

For over the past 15 years, ASUS has maintained a presence in Colombia, working closely with customers, partners, retailers, and local communities across the country. As recovery efforts continue, ASUS is committed to helping support those impacted by the disaster and contributing to the rebuilding of affected communities.

ASUS believes that businesses have a responsibility to support communities in times of need. Through this contribution and ongoing cooperation with local partners, ASUS hopes to assist relief efforts and help affected families begin the process of recovery and rebuilding.

Rigaku provides local students with work experience opportunities as part of its efforts to introduce the appeal of manufacturing to the younger generation. In July 2026, we welcomed two junior high school students to Yamanashi Factory (Hokuto City, Yamanashi Prefecture) for a work experience program.

Through a factory tour and hands-on activities on the factory floor, they experienced Rigaku’s manufacturing.

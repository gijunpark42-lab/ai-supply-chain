Fujikura Ltd. (Director, President and CEO: Naoki Okada) has been selected for the first time as a constituent of the JPX Prime 150 Index, announced by JPX Market Innovation & Research, Inc. on August 24, 2026.

The JPX Prime 150 Index is a stock index developed by JPX Market Innovation & Research, Inc. to identify leading Japanese companies that are considered to be creating value. Constituents are selected from among the largest companies listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange Prime Market based on two criteria: capital profitability derived from financial performance and market valuation reflecting future prospects and non-financial information.

The index consists of 150 constituents, and the selection period will run from August 31, 2026 to August 30, 2027.

In light of this selection, Fujikura remains committed to sustainable growth and enhancing corporate value over the medium to long term while meeting the expectations of its stakeholders.

For more information on the JPX Prime 150 Index, please visit:

https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/markets/indices/jpx-prime150/index.html

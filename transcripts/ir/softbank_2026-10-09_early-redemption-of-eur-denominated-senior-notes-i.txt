Early Redemption of EUR-denominated Senior Notes Issued in 2021

October 9, 2026

SoftBank Group Corp.

SoftBank Group Corp. announced today that it will redeem all of its outstanding EUR-denominated 2.875% Senior Notes due January 6, 2027, issued on July 6, 2021, at par on October 22, 2026, in accordance with its terms and conditions. The details of the redemption are as follows.

1. Total outstanding principal amount EUR405,103,000

2. Date of issuance July 6, 2021

3. Maturity date January 6, 2027

4. Date of redemption October 22, 2026

5. Interest rate 2.875% per annum

6. Total principal amount of redemption EUR405,103,000

7. Redemption price 100.00%

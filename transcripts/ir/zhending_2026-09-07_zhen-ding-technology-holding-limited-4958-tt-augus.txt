Zhen Ding Technology Holding Limited (4958 TT) August 2026 Monthly Revenue Report

Zhen Ding Technology Holding Limited (Ticker: 4958), a global leading PCB manufacturer, today (September 7) reported August 2026 revenue of NT$20,666 million, up 41.05% YoY and 17.42% MoM, again setting a record high for the same period in the company’s history. For January through August 2026, cumulative revenue reached NT$127,426 million, up 19.89% YoY, also marking a record high for the same period.

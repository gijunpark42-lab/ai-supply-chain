Samsung Expands Language Support Across AI Experiences on Galaxy

on October 14, 2026

AUDIO Play

Share open

Samsung is expanding language support for Galaxy AI and Bixby, bringing these experiences to more users in their own language.

Galaxy AI is expanding language support across more of its features, starting with those that translate, transcribe, and summarize. Call Assist, Note Assist, Interpreter, and Transcript Assist now include Hungarian and Malay, bringing features such as Live Translate to 24 languages. 1

▲ Live Translate of a phone call in Malay-English (Image simulated for illustrative purposes. Actual UX/UI may differ.)

Elsewhere in Galaxy AI, Now Nudge, which suggests a relevant next step based on what is on screen, adds eight languages 2 to reach 24 as well.

Bixby is a device agent that offers intuitive device control through natural conversation. Users can ask for what they need in their own words, without having to know the exact name of a setting or where to find it in a menu. With the addition of Polish, Thai, and Vietnamese, Bixby now supports 12 languages, 3 allowing more Galaxy users to control their devices more effortlessly.

Bixby’s new languages will begin rolling out on October 14, 4 with the new Galaxy AI languages to follow in the coming weeks. 5

Galaxy AI supports 24 languages: Arabic, Chinese (China mainland, Hong Kong, Taiwan), Dutch, English (Australia, India, UK, U.S.), Filipino, French (Canada, France), German, Gujarati, Hindi, Hungarian, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil, Europe), Romanian, Russian, Spanish (Mexico, Spain, U.S.), Swedish, Thai, Turkish, and Vietnamese. Regions in parentheses do not represent separate languages. ↩︎

Now Nudge adds Dutch, Filipino, Gujarati, Hungarian, Malay, Romanian, Swedish, and Turkish. ↩︎

Bixby service availability requires Samsung Account login and a data connection. Available functions and features may vary by country, region, and language. Bixby supports 12 languages: Chinese (China mainland), English (India, UK, U.S.), French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish (Latin America, Spain), Thai, and Vietnamese. Regions in parentheses do not represent separate languages. Other supported languages may be added in the future. Not all accents, dialects, and expressions are recognized, and actual performance may vary depending on pronunciation, voice level, and the surrounding environment. Use of Bixby may be limited in certain situations such as when recording media (Video/Game/Voice), during calls, and when using Maximum Power Saving mode, Emergency mode, and Samsung Kids. ↩︎

Detailed schedules and features may vary depending on the device or country. ↩︎

Some of the new Galaxy AI languages can be downloaded as language packs from the Settings app. ↩︎

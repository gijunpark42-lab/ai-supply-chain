Adeia Renews IP License Agreement with Spectrum

Oct 05, 2026

|

PDF Version

Multi-year renewal extends Adeia’s long-standing relationship with the nation’s leading video provider

SAN JOSE, Calif., Oct. 05, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Adeia Inc. (Nasdaq: ADEA) , the technology company pioneering foundational innovations that shape how the world computes, connects and experiences entertainment, today announced that it has entered into a multi-year renewal of its intellectual property (IP) license agreement with Charter Communications, Inc., through its Spectrum brand. The agreement renews Spectrum's license to Adeia's media IP portfolio across its pay-TV and other video services and also includes coverage for Spectrum's broadband and mobility businesses.

This agreement marks the latest in a series of renewals with Spectrum, the nation’s leading video provider, whose relationship with Adeia and its predecessor companies spans more than two decades.

“This key renewal with Spectrum underscores the enduring strength and relevance of our media IP portfolio in the pay-TV market,” said Dr. Mark Kokes, chief revenue officer of Adeia. “The agreement also extends coverage to Spectrum's broadband and mobility businesses, reflecting the growing relevance of our innovations as video, broadband and mobile services increasingly converge. We are proud to continue our long-standing relationship with Spectrum and to support the entertainment experiences it delivers to millions of customers across the country.”

Adeia's media IP portfolio has been developed through decades of research and development and is broadly licensed across the global media, entertainment and connectivity ecosystems. The company's innovations are embedded in billions of devices worldwide, enabling advanced functionality across pay-TV, streaming, connected TV, broadband, and wireless platforms.

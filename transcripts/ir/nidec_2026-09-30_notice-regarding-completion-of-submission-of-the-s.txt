Nidec Corporation

Tokyo Stock Exchange code: 6594

Contact:  Teruaki Urago General Manager Investor Relations +81-75-935-6140 ir@nidec.com

Released on September 30, 2026, in Kyoto, Japan

Notice Regarding Completion of Submission of the Securities Report for the Fiscal Year Ended March

31, 2026 (53rd Fiscal Year)

As announced in the “Notice Regarding Approval of an Extension of the Filing Deadline of Annual Securities

Report for the 53rd Fiscal Year (Ended March 31, 2026)” dated June 30, 2026, Nidec Corporation (TSE: 6594;

OTC US: NJDCY) (the “Company”) received approval of deadline extension for submission of the said

securities report to September 30, 2026 from the Kanto Local Finance Bureau, and the Company, hereby

announces that the Company has submitted the said securities report to the Kanto Local Finance Bureau today.

The Company has also disclosed today the Financial Statements for the nine months ended December 31, 2025,

and the fiscal year ended March 31, 2026, both of which had been postponed.

We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience and concern caused to our shareholders, investors, and other

stakeholders.

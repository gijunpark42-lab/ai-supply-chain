Belimo strengthens global data centre strategy with new experience centre in the Americas

Danbury (Connecticut), October 20, 2026 - Belimo, a global leader in HVAC field devices, announced today the opening of a Data Centre Experience Centre at its Belimo Americas regional headquarters in Danbury, Connecticut.

The Experience Centre represents the next step in Belimo's growing global network, following the opening of a similar space at its Singapore location and ahead of a planned Data Centre Experience Centre in the United Kingdom. Together, these centres enable Belimo to support customers globally through hands-on learning, collaboration, and the sharing of application expertise.

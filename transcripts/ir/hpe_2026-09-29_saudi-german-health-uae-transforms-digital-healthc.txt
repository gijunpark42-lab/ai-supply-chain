Saudi German Health UAE transforms digital healthcare in the UAE with the HPE Self-Driving Network

September 29, 2026

Press Release Networking AI

Secure, AI-native wired and wireless operations enable always-on clinical care reliable telemedicine and scalable infrastructure to support continued growth and expansion

In this article

Saudi German Health UAE is strengthening the digital foundation for 24/7 healthcare, supporting reliable access to critical clinical applications, electronic medical records and telemedicine

AI-powered network operations and centralized visibility will help IT teams proactively identify and resolve issues across hospitals, clinics and medical education facilities

The secure and scalable network will support Saudi German Health UAE’s continued growth, including its new medical college and future smart hospital and AI-enabled healthcare initiatives

Dubai, United Arab Emirates – September 29, 2026 – HPE (NYSE: HPE) today announced that S audi German Health UAE, one of the leading private healthcare providers across the Middle East and North Africa, has selected HPE ’s wired, wireless NAC solution managed via HPE Aruba Central to modernize its network infrastructure and accelerate the next phase of its digital healthcare transformation in the UAE. HPE and Saudi German Health UAE have partnered for 14 years, supporting the organization’s continued growth and transformation.

Saudi German Health UAE selected the HPE Self-Driving Network portfolio to modernize its wired, wireless and security infrastructure and support the continued expansion of its hospitals, clinics, medical education facilities, and new medical college in the UAE. As digital services, connected devices, bandwidth-intensive applications such as diagnostic imaging, and mobile users across multiple campuses grew, the legacy network could no longer provide the scale, visibility, and reliability required. The transformation forms part of a broader digital transformation program spanning infrastructure, networks, applications, digitalization and AI implementation across the group.

With industry-leading agentic AIOps and unified network operations, HPE simplifies the deployment of new wired, wireless, and NAC services, accelerates issue detection and resolution, and optimizes digital experiences for patients, healthcare professionals, and students while supporting future growth. AI-powered insights and centralized management provide greater visibility across the group, helping the IT team proactively identify issues and plan for expansion.

We operate in a 24/7 acute-care environment, so reliability, resilience and security are non-negotiable. The centralized visibility and AI-driven insights will help our teams manage the network more effectively, scale with the organization and ultimately enhance the patient experience.

Abid Shah

Chief information officer, Saudi German Health UAE

“HPE’s secure, AI-native networking provides the resilient foundation we need to support our growing healthcare and academic operations,” said Abid Shah, chief information officer, Saudi German Health UAE. “We operate in a 24/7 acute-care environment, so reliability, resilience and security are non-negotiable. The centralized visibility and AI-driven insights will help our teams manage the network more effectively, scale with the organization and ultimately enhance the patient experience.”

“With healthcare organizations under constant pressure to deliver secure, always-on digital services, networks must become more intelligent, automated, and easier to operate,” said Zeeshan Hadi, Business Head, UAE, Oman, Bahrain & Africa, Networking, HPE. “Healthcare providers must support large numbers of users, connected devices, and latency-sensitive applications across multiple locations without any disruption. Saudi German Health is building a modern digital foundation with a self-driving network powered by HPE by combining AI-native operations, strong security, and cloud-native management to support exceptional experiences, and future innovations including smart hospital initiatives, and AI-enabled healthcare applications.”

Saudi German Health is building a modern digital foundation with a self-driving network powered by HPE by combining AI-native operations, strong security, and cloud-native management to support exceptional experiences, and future innovations including smart hospital initiatives, and AI-enabled healthcare applications.

Zeeshan Hadi

Business Head, UAE, Oman, Bahrain & Africa, Networking, HPE

The new environment is designed to improve reliability and performance of mission-critical healthcare applications while enabling uninterrupted access to electronic medical records (EMR) and clinical systems . High-density wireless connectivity powered by HPE Aruba Networking Access Points and resilient switching infrastructure built on the HPE Networking CX Switches will support clinicians, staff, patients, and students across multiple sites. To strengthen security and simplify operations, the deployment integrates HPE Networking Gateway Series and HPE Networking ClearPass Policy Manager enabling role-based access for users and connected medical devices while helping protect sensitive patient data. In parallel, HPE Networking User Experience Insight provides proactive experience monitoring to identify and resolve issues before they affect users, while HPE Networking manages both production systems and air-gapped CCTV infrastructure.

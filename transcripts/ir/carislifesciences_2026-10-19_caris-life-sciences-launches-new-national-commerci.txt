Caris Life Sciences Launches New National Commercial Featuring Jordan Spieth Highlighting the Power of Artificial Intelligence in Early Stage Cancer Detection

October 19, 2026

PDF Version

New campaign connects AI-powered performance insights linking advanced molecular intelligence to help detect cancer in earlier stages

IRVING, Texas , Oct. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Caris Life Sciences ® (NASDAQ: CAI), a leading TechBio company, today announced the launch of the second commercial in a series promoting Caris Detect™, Caris' multi-cancer early detection (MCED) test, featuring three-time major champion professional golfer, Jordan Spieth .

The campaign emphasizes that while modern technology can measure nearly every aspect of athletic performance, the same level of intelligence can be applied to helping people find early stage cancer – when treatment is most effective. The new commercial explores how artificial intelligence has transformed golf, analyzing millions of swings and countless data points to help players improve performance. The commercial highlights how Caris is applying decades of expertise in molecular science and artificial intelligence to help identify cancer-associated molecular signals through a simple blood draw.

"Artificial intelligence is transforming nearly every aspect of our lives, from how we work and communicate to how athletes refine their performance," said David Dean Halbert , Founder, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Caris Life Sciences . "At Caris, we believe one of the most important applications of AI is helping advance early stage cancer detection. Caris Detect combines comprehensive molecular analysis with advanced artificial intelligence to help create a future where cancer may be detected in early stage, when there is the best chance for cure."

"Every golfer is looking for an edge. We use technology to measure everything from ball speed to spin rate because better information leads to better decisions. That's what resonated with me about Caris Detect. If advanced artificial intelligence can help us understand our golf game, it should also help us better understand our health. Raising awareness about the importance of early detection is something I'm proud to support," said Jordan Spieth , three-time major champion professional golfer.

Unlike approaches that evaluate only a limited subset of biomarkers, Caris Detect™ leverages ultra-deep leverages Whole Genome Sequencing (WGS) for all 23,000 genes, Whole Transcriptome Sequencing (WTS) and advanced artificial intelligence to analyze the full breadth of cancer-associated molecular signals in both DNA and RNA. This comprehensive approach is designed to provide a more complete picture of early stage cancer biology, enabling the identification of molecular patterns associated with cancer through a single blood draw and helping support earlier stage detection when cancers are most treatable.

Individuals interested in learning more about Caris Detect may visit CarisDetect.com .

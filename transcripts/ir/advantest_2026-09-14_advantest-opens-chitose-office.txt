TOKYO, Japan—Sept. 14, 2026—Leading semiconductor test equipment supplier Advantest Corporation (TSE: 6857) today opened a new sales and support office in Chitose, Hokkaido.

By establishing a presence in Chitose, which is expected to emerge as a new hub for the semiconductor industry, Advantest will further enhance its support for customers in the surrounding area.

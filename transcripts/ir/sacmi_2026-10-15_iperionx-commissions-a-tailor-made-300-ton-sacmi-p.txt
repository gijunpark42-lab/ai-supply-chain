IperionX commissions a tailor-made 300-ton SACMI press, equipped with innovative “Die Wall Lubrication System” for Advanced Titanium Manufacturing

The installation of a 300-ton powder metal press featuring SACMI’s innovative Die Wall Lubrication technology marks a key milestone in the expansion of high-performance titanium component production in the United States.

SACMI is pleased to highlight the successful commissioning of a highly customized 300-ton powder metal press at IperionX’s Titanium Manufacturing Campus in South Boston, Virginia (USA).

Advanced Forming Capabilities for Complex Components

The SACMI press introduces multi-axis compaction with enhanced force and position controls, repeatability and geometry precision, offering significant advantages over conventional systems.

These capabilities enable the efficient production of complex components for demanding industrial applications and advanced manufacturing sectors.

A Technology Partnership for Advanced Materials

This project confirms SACMI’s role as a technology partner in advanced materials processing, contributing to the development of innovative manufacturing solutions for strategic industries.

SACMI thanks IperionX for its trust and collaboration and is proud to be chosen as a partner for this revolutionary and globally unique approach to titanium manufacturing.

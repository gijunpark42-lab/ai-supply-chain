TSMC August 2026 Revenue Report

HSINCHU, Taiwan, R.O.C. – September 10, 2026 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenue for August 2026: On a consolidated basis, revenue for August 2026 was approximately NT$514.81 billion, an increase of 10.1 percent from July 2026 and an increase of 53.3 percent from August 2025. Revenue for January through August 2026 totaled NT$3,386.87 billion, an increase of 39.3 percent compared to the same period in 2025.

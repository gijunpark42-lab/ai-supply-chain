Nikon Announces Reorganization of Corporate Structure

October 1, 2026

TOKYO - Nikon Corporation announced today the reorganization of its corporate structure as outlined

below, starting October 1, 2026.

Industry Business Group

Nikon is newly establishing the Industry Business Group as the parent organization of the Industrial

Solutions Business Unit, Customized Products Business Unit, and Glass Business Unit.

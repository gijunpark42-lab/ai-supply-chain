Conclusion of a Memorandum of Understanding

Regarding Business Alliance with TDK Corporation

TAIYO YUDEN CO., LTD. (hereinafter “the Company”) hereby announces that on September 29, 2026, the

Board of Directors of the Company resolved on concluding a memorandum of understanding (hereinafter, the

“Memorandum”) with TDK Corporation (hereinafter, “TDK”) regarding a business alliance focused primarily

on the joint development of electronic components, including multilayer ceramic capacitors (“MLCCs”) and

inductors and entered into the Memorandum on the same date. Details are as follows.

1. Reason for the Business Alliance

The TAIYO YUDEN Group operates in the electronic components business, including MLCCs and inductors,

and provides high-value-added products that integrate advanced core technologies such as materials

technology, multilayer technology, and design technology. In recent years, the rapid growth of artificial

intelligence technologies, including generative AI, has driven global demand for semiconductors and chipsets

used in AI data centers, AI servers, and physical AI applications. As the performance of these systems continues

to improve, demand is also rising rapidly for product groups that the TAIYO YUDEN Group focuses on,

namely, high-capacitance, highly reliable MLCCs, inductors, and other electronic components essential for

power supply stabilization and noise suppression.

In response to these market trends, and in order to respond swiftly to the rapid expansion in demand for

cutting-edge products and market growth, the Company and TDK have agreed to begin exploring a

comprehensive business alliance centered on the joint development of advanced electronic components,

including cutting-edge MLCCs and inductors for AI data centers and physical AI, and encompassing

cooperation in manufacturing and procurement. The Company has concluded that pursuing such an alliance

will contribute to its corporate value and, consequently, the common interests of its shareholders, and has

therefore decided to enter into this memorandum of understanding.

Through the alliance, the companies aim to respond quickly and reliably to demand for advanced products

from customers worldwide, enhance their competitiveness in the electronic components market, and

consistently capitalize on growth opportunities while expanding revenue and enhancing profit margins. As key

Japanese players in the manufacture of advanced MLCCs, inductors, and other electronic components, the

companies also intend to contribute to strengthening Japan’s industrial base and enhancing supply chain

resilience.

2

Furthermore, advanced electronic components, including MLCCs, have been designated as Specified

Critical Products subject to measures to ensure a stable supply under the Act on the Promotion of Ensuring

National Security through Integrated Implementation of Economic Measures (the Economic Security

Promotion Act). Moving forward, the two companies will consider formulating a joint supply assurance plan

and submitting a joint application to the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry. Through efforts by the

companies to secure and strengthen their technological advantages in AI-related fields, including physical AI,

this business alliance will contribute to strengthening Japan’s industrial competitiveness.

2. Contents of the Business Alliance

Going forward, the Company and TDK will proceed with detailed discussions regarding the specific areas of

collaboration and the allocation of roles and responsibilities under a comprehensive business alliance centered

on the joint development of advanced electronic components, including cutting-edge MLCCs and inductors,

and encompassing cooperation in manufacturing and procurement, while also considering the possibility of a

future capital alliance.

Announcing the New ASUS ExpertBook B3 G2

August 20, 2026

・

Press Release

Configurable Intel Core Series 3 business laptop for the modern workplace

Designed for business productivity: Powered by up to an Intel® Core™ 7 Series 3 with integrated GPU and up to 17 TOPS NPU for AI acceleration

ASUS ExpertGuardian: All-around protection with a holistic hardware and software security architecture

All-day productivity : Up to a 70Wh battery delivers long-lasting power for all-day efficiency

TAIPEI, Taiwan, August 20, 2026 — ASUS today announced the new ASUS ExpertBook B3 G2, a configurable laptop designed for business, available in 14-inch (B3406CMA) and 16-inch (B3606CMA) models and featuring a durable, lightweight design starting from just 1.45kg that offers a 180° lay-flat hinge, AI capabilities. enterprise-grade security, and seamless collaboration.

Powered by up to the latest Intel® Core™ 7 Series 3 processors with integrated GPU and NPU acceleration, ASUS ExpertBook B3 G2's advanced AI features — including Microsoft Copilot, and AI noise-canceling technology — enhance business productivity. The lightweight, easy-maintenance design offers extensive I/O options and support for high-speed WiFi 6E connectivity [i] . Security is strengthened by ASUS ExpertGuardian, delivering NIST SP 800-193 compliance, dual ROM recovery, and TPM 2.0 protection for robust data resilience.

Built to military-grade durability standards, ASUS ExpertBook B3 G2 combines performance, security, and reliability to meet the demands of today’s fast-paced business environments.

Designed for business productivity

ASUS ExpertBook B3 G2 is powered by up to Intel Core 7 Series 3 processors with integrated Intel graphics, up to 64GB DDR5 memory [ii] , and up to 4TB SSD storage [iii] , enabling scalable configuration for diverse enterprise requirements. Integrated NPU acceleration of up to 17 TOPS enhances system efficiency and responsiveness, supporting AI-assisted features while ensuring smooth multitasking across demanding business workloads.

A high-capacity battery of up to 70Wh supports productivity throughout workdays, from back-to-back meetings to hybrid collaboration. An up to 2.5K anti-glare display with a 144Hz refresh rate delivers sharp visuals and smooth scrolling, while an up to FHD IR camera enables high-quality video conferencing. Enhanced by AI noise-canceling technology and the advanced ExpertCool thermal design, ASUS ExpertBook B3 G2 sustains stable performance and clear communication under sustained workloads.

ASUS ExpertGuardian

ASUS ExpertGuardian delivers robust, multi-layered protection engineered for today’s business and enterprise environments. Built with a holistic, hardware-to-software security architecture, it ensures comprehensive defense at every level. With NIST SP 800-193 compliance, five years of ASUS BIOS and driver updates, and TPM 2.0 protection, ExpertGuardian safeguards system integrity, protects privacy, and secures mission-critical data from emerging threats.

This end-to-end security is further reinforced by advanced features including dual ROM recovery, a dedicated Security Processor Unit, chassis-intrusion detection, and cloud recovery. Together, these capabilities provide a resilient security foundation that enables organizations to operate, scale, and innovate with confidence — supported by a seamless and reliable IT management experience.

ASUS ExpertBook B3 G2 complements this with MIL-STD-810H military-grade durability, passing 24 rigorous procedures, ensuring reliable performance in even the most demanding environments. Backed by flexible ASUS Business Support services, it offers enterprises the assurance of long-term stability and rapid assistance whenever needed.

ASUS solutions for every business

ASUS provides scalable, customized solutions for business of all sizes. Our comprehensive lifecycle services include seamless device deployment and management, enterprise-grade security, all-around IT support, and simplified device retirements. ASUS business laptops empower your IT team to enjoy enhanced productivity and efficiency.

[i] WiFi 6E is an optional feature.

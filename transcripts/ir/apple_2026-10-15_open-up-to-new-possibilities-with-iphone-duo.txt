October 15, 2026

Open up to new possibilities with iPhone Duo

Pre-orders begin Friday, October 16

Starting this Friday, customers can pre-order iPhone Duo — featuring a foldable design — along with beautiful new accessories.

Beginning Friday, October 16, at 5 a.m. PT, customers can pre-order iPhone Duo on apple.com and in the Apple Store app.

iPhone Duo delivers Apple’s largest iPhone display ever in a foldable design. Opened, it is the thinnest iPhone ever, with an inner display that is 50 percent larger than the display on iPhone 18 Pro Max — providing a large canvas for viewing content, gaming, and multitasking. Closed, the 5.4-inch outer display delivers 90 percent of the screen area of iPhone 18 Pro in a compact, pocketable design. 1 iPhone Duo is also built to last and features a precision-engineered hinge that offers a smooth, satisfying feel with every use. Powered by A20 Pro, iPhone Duo delivers pro performance along with a dual-battery architecture for impressive all-day battery life. iOS 27 is reimagined, remaining familiar and intuitive while adapting to the versatile ways users can interact with iPhone Duo. And with Siri AI, 2 powered by Apple Intelligence , 3 iPhone Duo also brings powerful AI capabilities together with a user’s personal context to become an intelligent personal hub with privacy and security at its core.

Customers shopping in-store or online for iPhone Duo can connect with Apple Specialists for personalized support and get assistance with ways to save and pay, including leasing with Apple Upgrade, 4 financing options, and carrier deals, as well as learn how to protect their products with AppleCare+ or AppleCare One. Customers can also take advantage of convenient home delivery or arrange for pickup at their local Apple Store at a time most suitable for them.

Closed, iPhone Duo delivers 90 percent of the screen area of iPhone 18 Pro in a pocketable design. Opened, it showcases Apple’s largest-ever iPhone display.

Ways to Save Directly with Apple

Upgrading to iPhone Duo is incredibly simple with a variety of flexible payment options. Customers can choose from great leasing and financing options or select one of the exceptional carrier deals — and since iPhone retains its value longer than other smartphones, they can maximize their savings through Apple Trade In. 5

Apple Trade In

Trading in a current device is a great way to save on iPhone Duo. With Apple Trade In, customers can receive an instant credit ranging from $175 to $885 when trading in an eligible iPhone 13 or higher. That credit can then be used to lower their monthly payments across Apple Upgrade, Apple Card Monthly Installments, 6 or one of the incredible carrier deals.

With great financing options, customers can save by shopping directly with Apple. They can also trade in an eligible device and receive instant credit.

Apple Upgrade

With Apple Upgrade, provided by Klarna, it’s easy for customers to get their hands on iPhone Duo with a lease term that’s best for them. Apple Upgrade offers 12- and 24-month leasing options for iPhone, with prices starting as low as $57.99 (excluding taxes and any trade-in credit) per month for a 24-month lease for iPhone Duo. At the end of their lease term, eligible customers can upgrade their device to the latest generation, purchase it with a one-time payment, or simply return it and exit the program.

More Value with Carrier Deals Directly with Apple

Apple partners with the major wireless carriers to offer incredible deals, including up to $1,200 in credit toward iPhone Duo when trading in iPhone 14 Pro Max or higher. 7 Customers can activate their new phone with AT&T, T-Mobile, or Verizon — all in one place.

Apple Card Monthly Installments

In the U.S., Apple Card customers can pay for their new devices over time at 0 percent APR when they choose to check out with Apple Card Monthly Installments at Apple and earn 3 percent Daily Cash back all up front. It takes as little as a minute for customers to apply for Apple Card and start using it right away.† Tracking payments is just as simple, with installments automatically grouped into one easy-to-manage balance in the Wallet app.

Explore iPhone Duo

iPhone Duo features an advanced camera system that enables all-new experiences made possible by its breakthrough design. The 48MP Fusion Main camera shoots sharp photos with zero shutter lag, and users can get closer to the subject with an integrated optical-quality 2x Telephoto. The 48MP Fusion Ultra Wide camera captures wider fields of view and enables macro photography, while the Center Stage front camera can automatically take photos and videos in any orientation, so users don’t have to rotate iPhone for landscape selfies. A new FaceTime camera placed underneath the inner display helps users connect with friends and family, whether iPhone Duo is held open or propped on a surface for hands-free calls.

The expansive display on iPhone Duo supercharges productivity with Split View multitasking and seamless app switching, and a reimagined yet familiar iOS experience introduces entirely new ways to interact with iPhone. iOS also adapts to content on iPhone Duo by reacting as it folds, reorienting when turned to the side, and switching to the appropriate display when flipped over. iPhone Duo can also enter StandBy on either the outer or inner display — even when it’s not charging — turning the device into a photo frame, a hub for widgets, a clock, and more.

Siri AI is an entirely new version of Siri powered by Apple Intelligence — profoundly more personal, capable, and conversational, with personal context understanding, broad world knowledge, and onscreen awareness to help users find what they need and get things done. Rolling out in beta in English with iOS 27, Siri AI is even more useful on the large inner display, where iPhone Duo users can keep Siri open alongside an app and ask about what’s onscreen. And with Siri mode in Camera, users can get information and take action on what’s in front of them.

iPhone Duo is splash-, water-, and dust-resistant with an IP68 rating, 8 and is available in two refined colors: star white and night sky.

iPhone Duo customers can choose between two refined colors: star white and night sky.

Get Ready to Upgrade with Apple

Getting ready early is the best way to secure the new iPhone Duo. Right now, customers can configure their device, choose accessories, secure leasing or financing pre-approval, and arrange their trade-in ahead of the availability-day rush. By saving these choices to their shopping bag before 9 p.m. PT on Thursday, October 15, they are able to check out with a single click the moment pre-orders go live at 5 a.m. PT on Friday, October 16.

Customers get the very best of Apple when shopping directly online or in-store. Knowledgeable Specialists can provide personalized guidance from start to finish on everything from discovering the latest trade-in offers and additional ways to save, to safely and securely transferring data with Personal Setup. Customers can also discover how to go further with their new devices through free Today at Apple sessions. With updated offerings like “Say Hello to iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Duo” and “Tips: What’s New on iOS 27,” these hands-on classes are the ultimate way to explore the capabilities of the latest products.

Designed for iPhone Duo

To complement iPhone Duo, Apple is introducing beautiful new accessories. The iPhone Duo Folio — available in navy blue and taupe — features an integrated kickstand that supports a wide range of angles for reading, viewing content, or FaceTime, and folds away neatly when not in use. The iPhone Duo Case — available in sand and navy blue — is thin and light, with a protective two-part design that wraps around both sides of the device. Both cases are compatible with MagSafe, Qi2 25W, and Qi-certified chargers. With built-in magnets that align perfectly with iPhone Duo, they offer a magical attach experience and fast wireless charging.

Protection for Every Product

AppleCare plans protect a user’s new iPhone Duo from accidents like drops and spills and, in many regions, theft and loss too — all at a great value. 9 In available markets, 10 customers can choose AppleCare+ or AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss for their new iPhone Duo. AppleCare One Individual, where offered, provides the same benefits as AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss, at the same monthly price — but extends coverage to two more devices. Customers in the U.S. can also choose AppleCare One Family to cover every eligible Apple product across their Family Sharing group, with new purchases, upgrades, and devices the family already owns covered automatically at no additional cost. 11 Learn more at apple.com/applecare or in Settings on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

With AppleCare One Family, customers in the U.S. can protect their new iPhone Duo as well as every eligible Apple product in their Family Sharing group.

Try Apple One

New and qualified returning subscribers who purchase iPhone Duo can unlock three months of Apple One for free. Apple One bundles up to six Apple services — including Apple TV, Apple Music, up to 2TB of iCloud+ storage, and more — into one convenient plan across multiple Apple devices.‡

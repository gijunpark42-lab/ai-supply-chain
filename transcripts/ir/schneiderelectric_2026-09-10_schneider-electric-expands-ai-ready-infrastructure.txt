p]:my-4 [&_a:active]:text-[#00374D] [&_a:disabled]:opacity-40 [&_a:hover]:text-[#005C80] [&_a:visited:active]:text-[#351056] [&_a:visited:hover]:text-[#5C1C96] [&_a:visited]:text-[#7524BF] [&_a]:text-[#0075A3] [&_a]:no-underline"> New range of prefabricated power modules and prefabricated power skids deliver scalable, resilient, and quick-to-deploy power capacities from 2MW to 2.5MW for hyperscale, neocloud and colocation environments

Standardized reference designs, created to support NVIDIA’s latest GPUs, reduce deployment timelines to as little as six months, accelerating AI infrastructure rollouts

p]:my-4 [&_a:active]:text-[#00374D] [&_a:disabled]:opacity-40 [&_a:hover]:text-[#005C80] [&_a:visited:active]:text-[#351056] [&_a:visited:hover]:text-[#5C1C96] [&_a:visited]:text-[#7524BF] [&_a]:text-[#0075A3] [&_a]:no-underline svelte-1ars9v5"> Schneider Electric , a global energy technology leader, today introduced a new range of standardized Prefabricated Power Modules and Power Skids, expanding its AI-ready infrastructure portfolio with scalable, high-density power solutions designed for hyperscale, neocloud and colocation data center environments.

Available immediately in Europe, and powered by Schneider Electric’s Galaxy VXL (GVXL) UPS technology, the new solutions provide up to 2.5MW of power capacity per module, creating the largest, single-container prefabricated power solution Schneider Electric has brought to market.

Designed as scalable “power blocks” based on Schneider Electric’s latest reference designs with NVIDIA, which include integrated power management and liquid cooling controls for its GB300 NVL72 platform, the solutions enable customers to rapidly deploy AI-ready power infrastructure to support high-performance computing (HPC), AI training clusters and GPU-intensive workloads in as little as six months.

Available in 2MW, 2.25MW and 2.5MW configurations, the new Power Modules and Power Skids are engineered, manufactured and tested at Schneider Electric’s Sant Boi de Llobregat, Barcelona , which was recently expanded to support growing global demand for modular data center infrastructure.

Initially launched in Europe, the new power solutions will become available in North America later this year, followed by additional international markets and APAC.

For more information – visit SE.com

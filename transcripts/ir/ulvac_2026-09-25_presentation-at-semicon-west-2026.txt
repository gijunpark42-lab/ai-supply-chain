Presentation at SEMICON West 2026

ULVAC will participate in SEMICON West 2026, where an employee serving as Chair of SEMI Smart Sustainability will deliver a keynote presentation on a customizable fab model developed by the task force, which leverages Industry 4.0/5.0, including machine learning and AI, to improve productivity, sustainability metrics, and resource efficiency in device manufacturing fabs.

New Production Building Completed at Murata Manufacturing Vietnam’s Ho Chi Minh Plant

Go to the bottom of the page

10/8/2026

Murata Manufacturing Co., Ltd.

President: Norio Nakajima

Murata Manufacturing Vietnam Co., Ltd. (Da Nang City, Vietnam), a manufacturing subsidiary of Murata Manufacturing Co., Ltd., has invested approximately 3 billion yen *1 in the construction of a new production building that began in May 2025 on the premises of the branch of Murata Manufacturing Vietnam Co., Ltd., in Ho Chi Minh (Dong Nai City, Vietnam). The new production building is now completed, with a completion ceremony held on Thursday, October 8, 2026. Going forward, a production system will be put in place, and operations will begin sequentially. With the completion of the new production building, Murata will work toward stable supply of coil products *2 for consumer equipment and automotive use.

*1 Building only

*2 Electronic components used to stabilize electric currents and suppress noise

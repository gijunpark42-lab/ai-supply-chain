Zhen Ding Brings Its “Benefiting Others” Spirit into Action by Partnering with the Children Are Us Foundation Empowering Individuals with Intellectual Disabilities Through Arts Education and Self-Realization

To promote cultural participation and arts education for individuals with intellectual disabilities, Zhen Ding Technology Group has partnered with the Children Are Us Foundation to support its annual theater and musical training and performance program. Through structured drama and music courses as well as public performances, the program helps over a hundred participants develop communication, self-expression, and teamwork skills while building confidence, discovering their potential, and expanding opportunities for social participation through continuous learning and stage practice.

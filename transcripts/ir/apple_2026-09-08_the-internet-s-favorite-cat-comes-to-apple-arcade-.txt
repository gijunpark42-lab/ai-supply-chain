September 8, 2026

The internet’s favorite cat gets her first game with Pusheen’s Place, only on Apple Arcade

River City Survivors, Terraria+, Touchgrind BMX 2+, and 4 Pics 1 Word+ are also joining the service next month

Pusheen’s Place, launching exclusively on Apple Arcade on October 1, is the first officially licensed game starring Pusheen the Cat.

On October 1, Apple Arcade is rolling out a lineup of fun new games for players of every style, from cozy cat collecting to zombie-blasting mayhem to beloved App Store hits. Whether players want to unwind in Pusheen’s Place , dive into the adrenaline-pumping chaos of River City Survivors , or revisit App Store favorites like Terraria+ , Touchgrind BMX 2+ , and 4 Pics 1 Word+ , there’s plenty to discover, download, and play.

Pusheen’s Place is the first officially licensed game starring Pusheen the Cat, the global icon of cuteness and whimsy. Available exclusively on Apple Arcade, the game lets players build a cozy Pusheen paradise and collect more than 100 adorable kitties. They can play, craft, and decorate dreamy rooms for popular characters like Pusheenicorn and Pancake Pusheen. Then, for an extra dose of fun, unwind with sweet mini-games like Snack Stack, Parfait Pop, and Batch Match. This game adds to Arcade’s thriving catalog of cozy games, joining fan favorites like Hello Kitty Island Adventure , Tamagotchi Adventure Kingdom , and Cozy Caravan .

Cat lovers and cozy gamers say hello to their purr-fect new hangout in Pusheen’s Place, developed by Lively.

From the long-running River City series comes River City Survivors, an explosive survivors-like action game that throws players into wave after wave of zombie chaos. Blast through hordes, unleash flashy special moves, and grab randomized upgrades to build an unstoppable run every time. Players can choose from more than 20 playable characters, including franchise icons Kunio and Riki, and recruit up to two companions to build their dream team.

Blast through hordes and fight for survival in River City Survivors, developed by Arc System Works.

And the hits keep coming, with three beloved App Store favorites also joining the service on October 1, completely free of ads and in-app purchases. Terraria+ is a sandbox action-adventure where players fight, explore, and build their way through an ever-expanding world. This version includes the Bigger & Boulder update 1.4.5, which brings a Dead Cells crossover, iconic Pals from Palworld, and much more to discover. Players can also join forces with up to seven friends via Game Center or local Wi-Fi and explore more than 20 biomes in a boundless adventure of survival, discovery, and creativity.

Terraria+ developed by 505 Games.

Rounding out the trio are Touchgrind BMX 2+, a physics-driven BMX stunt game with unique two-finger controls that make every trick feel real, and 4 Pics 1 Word+, the Arcade edition of the popular puzzle game that’s stumped and delighted more than 400 million players worldwide.

Touchgrind BMX 2+ developed by Illusion Labs.

4 Pics 1 Word+ developed by Lotum.

Pricing and Availability

Apple Arcade is available for $6.99 (U.S.) per month with a one-month free trial. Customers who purchase a new iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Apple TV receive three months of Apple Arcade for free. 1

Apple Arcade is part of Apple One ’s Individual ( $21.95 U.S.), Family ( $27.95 U.S.), and Premier ( $39.95 U.S.) plans, with a one-month free trial. 2

Arcade Originals are playable across iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, and Apple Vision Pro. App Store Greats are available on iPhone, iPad, and Apple Vision Pro.

An Apple Arcade subscription gives a family of up to six unlimited access to all the games in its catalog.

Availability for the 200+ games across devices varies based on hardware and software compatibility. Some content may not be available in all areas.

MediaTek Dimensity CX C10 Max Offers Flagship Performance to a New Era of AI-First Laptops

HSINCHU, Taiwan – Sept. 21, 2026 – MediaTek, a global leader in computing and mobile technologies, today introduced the MediaTek Dimensity CX C10 Max, the inaugural SoC for the company’s new Dimensity Compute Experiences (CX) family. As the flagship of the lineup, Dimensity CX C10 Max combines exceptional performance, advanced functionality, and impressive power efficiency to enable an upcoming premium, ultra-portable Googlebook device.

Built on TSMC’s advanced 3nm process, Dimensity CX C10 Max utilizes an All Big Core design with octa-core CPU, 11-core GPU, and MediaTek’s 8th-generation NPU in a highly efficient platform engineered to deliver stellar performance across multitasking, productivity, entertainment, intelligent assistance, and more. Its power-efficient architecture enables manufacturers to design thinner, lighter, and fanless devices with all-day1 battery life.

The MediaTek NPU 890 delivers up to 55 TOPS of performance, helping enable upcoming Googlebook devices to better deliver an experience that’s a step ahead. Further expanding MediaTek’s AI leadership spanning from the Edge to the cloud, MediaTek NPU 890 is designed to power Gemini Intelligence features, delivering faster responses and offering additional privacy benefits.

“Dimensity CX C10 Max is designed for the next wave of intelligent computing, combining flagship performance and power-efficient processing to ensure an incredible user experience paired with amazing battery life,” said Vince Hu, Senior Vice President & General Manager of Data Center & Computing Business at MediaTek. “Bringing these capabilities to Googlebook builds on our leadership in Android computing and continues our long history of collaboration with Google.”

The MediaTek Imagiq 1090 ISP integrated into the Dimensity CX C10 Max delivers best-in-class professional camera quality. Supporting camera sensors of up to 32MP, the AI-ISP enables high-quality HDR video capture, smooth video zoom, cinematic video, and sustained focus; resulting in clearer, more immersive video calls, livestreams and content creation.

The platform also supports Wi-Fi 7 connectivity with speeds of up to 7.3Gbps, providing fast, reliable connections for streaming, downloads, and video conferencing in busy wireless environments. Dual-engine Bluetooth 6.0 support provides efficient connectivity for wireless peripherals and audio devices2.

By bringing together All Big Core CPU, powerful GPU and MediaTek’s industry-leading NPU with accelerated agentic AI, Dimensity CX C10 Max is designed to crunch through the most demanding tasks and deliver ultra-fast multitasking to achieve maximum productivity.

For more information about the Dimensity CX C10 Max, visit https://www.mediatek.com/products/laptops-and-tablets/dimensity-cx/c10-max .

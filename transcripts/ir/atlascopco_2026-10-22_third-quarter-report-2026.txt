Strong orders, record revenues and solid cash flow

The comparison figures presented in this report refer to previous year unless otherwise stated.

Third quarter

Orders received increased 29% to MSEK 52 338 (40 517), organic increase of 25%

Revenues reached MSEK 47 920 (41 621), organic increase of 11%

Operating profit reached MSEK 9 950 (8 546), corresponding to a margin of 20.8% (20.5)

Adjusted operating profit, excluding items affecting comparability, was MSEK 10 195 (8 862),

corresponding to a margin of 21.3% (21.3)

Profit before tax amounted to MSEK 9 789 (8 456)

Basic earnings per share were SEK 1.58 (1.37)

Operating cash flow reached MSEK 8 728 (7 330)

Return on capital employed was 24% (25)

Near-term outlook

Atlas Copco Group expects the customer activity to remain at the current level.

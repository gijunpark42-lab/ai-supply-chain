INNIO and Aggreko Extend Strategic Partnership Through 2031, Securing Long-Term Engine Capacity to Meet Growing Global Power Demand

October 8, 2026

PDF Version

MUNICH --(BUSINESS WIRE)--Oct. 8, 2026--

INNIO N.V. (Nasdaq: INIO) today announced a substantial extension of its long-standing strategic partnership with Aggreko , a global leader in engineered energy and temperature solutions. The extended agreement, now running through 2031, secures substantial long-term engine capacity for Aggreko while providing INNIO with additional order visibility for medium-power range engines.

“Global power demand is rising rapidly, while grid constraints are growing. Together with Aggreko , we are delivering reliable, efficient, and sustainable power solutions that help bridge these gaps and keep customers moving,” said Dr. Olaf Berlien , President and CEO of INNIO.

“This agreement secures proven technology and capacity to deliver reliable power wherever and whenever our customers need it,” said Sunny Thakrar , Commercial Director of Aggreko .

Under the agreement, INNIO has agreed to provide a minimum of 450 MW of engine capacity in the medium-power range (Jenbacher Type 4) in 2027 and access to agreed capacity levels for the remainder of the agreement. The partnership also includes a multi-year service agreement covering Aggreko’s existing INNIO fleet.

At the center of the partnership is INNIO's Jenbacher Type 4 platform, including the J420 and J424 engines. The partnership has now expanded to include the supply of the J620 engine from 2028. Combining high power density, robust operational performance, and fuel flexibility with a modular design, these engines are particularly well suited to Aggreko’s mobile equipment fleet. They enable fast deployment and reliable operation across data centers and a wide range of industrial and critical power applications, where rapidly growing power demand and grid constraints are increasing the need for flexible, scalable power solutions.

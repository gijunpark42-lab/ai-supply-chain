TAIPEI, Taiwan, August 25, 2026 — ASUS Republic of Gamers (ROG) today announced its growing commitment to esports through new product launches, expanded partnerships with elite teams and pro players, and a dedicated social media channel. As esports continues to shape the expectations of modern gamers and esports fans worldwide, ROG creates high-performance Ace Collection gaming gear made with insights from professional teams and athletes. The brand is also strengthening its role within the community with dedicated esports communication platforms to keep fans and enthusiasts up to date

ROG Gladius IV Ace: Refined for competitive play

Developed through extensive sports-science testing and iterative evaluations with esports players, the ergonomic shape of the ROG Gladius IV Ace helps reduce muscle strain as players grip, lift and move the mouse during competitive play. This approach will also help shape further Ace esports mouse aesthetics, helping ROG develop designs for different grip types, hand sizes, and mouse movement. During tracking movements, the Gladius remained stable in velocity and acceleration after fatigue, and users also spent less average time off target, even after fatigue. In 180-degree flick scenarios, users also displayed higher hit accuracy.

The Gladius IV Ace has a center-balanced design and a default weight of 56 grams. It includes 3g, 5g, and 7g stackable weights to give players the flexibility to create seven additional configurations to fine-tune the mouse’s handling. Each mouse is individually machine-tested as a complete unit to verify left- and right-click consistency within 5gf for a closely matched actuation feel.

ROG SpeedNova 8K Gen II technology features an enhanced receiver with a large antenna designed to strengthen wireless signal quality. The receiver detects surrounding RF conditions and indicates connection quality in real time, while Active Dynamic Hopping scans for optimal channels to help reduce interference. Dual LEDs provide quick RF status feedback, and players can personalize the indicators via Gear Link.

The ROG AimPoint Pro 65K optical sensor delivers up to 65,000dpi, 800ips and 70g maximum acceleration, with 11 selectable lift-off-distance levels for fine-tuned tracking. With up to 20,000 frames-per-second scanning and 8000Hz polling, the sensor captures movement quickly for responsive tracking during flicks, wide swipes, and precise adjustments. Zone Mode provides competitive-gaming hardware settings, while Dynamic TX Power Control offers an option for maximum transmission strength and stronger anti-interference performance.

The Gladius IV Ace includes a dedicated carry case for safe storage of the mouse and accessories, giving gamers a travel-ready setup for scrims, bootcamps, LANs, and tournament days. Gamers can use Gear Link to customize key mouse settings through a browser. With no software downloads required, Gear Link supports faster setups across practice rooms, tournament PCs, and shared systems.

ROG OLED esports monitors

ROG brings OLED technology to the competitive arena with exceptional color and contrast, consistent color reproduction across the panel. Self-lit OLED pixels respond almost instantly, enabling sharp motion clarity. Unlike conventional LCD esports monitors relying on traditional LCD backlight strobing, OLED needs no backlight — reducing eye strain and keeping gamers comfortable through marathon sessions. The result is a fast, consistent image for high-stakes play.

As the world’s No.1 OLED gaming monitor brand [i] , ROG is proud to introduce the ROG Swift OLED PG259QWS Ace, ROG Strix OLED XG259QDPG Ace and ROG Strix OLED XG259QDNS Ace. These 24.5-inch monitors are developed with direct input from professional players, and they expand the ROG OLED esports monitor lineup alongside the ROG Strix OLED XG259QWPG Ace, which debuted at Computex 2026.

Every Ace monitor includes integrated measurement scales on the stand to help players reproduce their preferred setup, a Quick OSD menu for fast setting adjustments, and three Esports Color modes tuned for different FPS gaming scenarios. Gallium Nitride Field-Effect Transistor (GaNFET) technology in the power-supply design helps optimize heat dissipation for extended OLED lifespan.

ROG Swift OLED PG259QWS Ace

With a 720Hz refresh rate, the ROG Swift OLED PG259QWS Ace is the fastest model in the series. Its TrueBlack Glossy™ Tandem WOLED panel produces supersharp imagery with deep blacks, even under bright arena lighting. It also features up to 1700-nits peak brightness and Dolby Vision® support for realistic HDR visuals. The integrated Position Sensor measures the gamer’s preferred angles and displays them on-screen, helping players reproduce precise setups at home, during practice sessions, or live competition. This particular model is the official esports monitor of the 2026 PGL Major tournament.

“A stage this historic deserves a display to match. The first OLED Major on the fastest OLED ever made — it’s a new generation of competitive Counter-Strike,” said Silviu Stroie, CEO, PGL.

The new ROG Swift OLED PG259QWS Ace and ROG Strix OLED XG259QWPG Ace will be available in early Q4 with MSRPs of US$1099 and US$699 respectively.

ROG Strix OLED XG259QDPG Ace

The ROG Strix OLED XG259QDPG Ace is a 560Hz RGB QD-OLED panel featuring BlackShield™ Film and an anti-glare low-reflection coating that boosts perceived black levels by up to 40% and increases panel hardness by 2.5 times. The monitor delivers vivid, true-to-life colors and glare-free visuals for fast-paced competitive play. Connectivity options include one DisplayPort™ 1.4 (DSC), two HDMI® 2.1, and three USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-A ports for maximum compatibility.

ROG Strix OLED XG259QDNS Ace

The ROG Strix OLED XG259QDNS Ace combines the benefits of an RGB QD-OLED panel featuring BlackShield Film and an anti-glare low-reflection coating with an accessible 360Hz refresh rate, enabling a wider base of players to enjoy Ace-grade esports visuals and pro-tuned setup tools. Connectivity options include DisplayPort™ 1.4 (DSC) and HDMI 2.1.

ROG expands its esports partnerships

ROG today announced its partnership with GamerLegion , serving as an official peripherals sponsor and supporting GamerLegion players with ROG gaming mice, keyboards, mouse pads, and other esports-focused gear. This announcement reinforces ROG's growing global esports network alongside existing partnerships with leading teams, and strengthens the connection between ROG Ace products and professional competitive play

“We are super happy to welcome ROG as a new partner of GamerLegion. We both cherish dedication and prowess, which is why we see great fits for both our brands. In light of our path to solidify ourselves in the top ranks, for the journey that we are on there are few partners we could wish on our side that carry as much endemic value and power as ROG. Excited to become ‘those who dare’ and break limits, together!” said GamerLegion’s CEO, Nicolas Reber.

“At ROG, the goal is to support competitive players with gear built for the realities of professional play, while working closely with teams to understand what players need at the highest level. We look forward to supporting GamerLegion with performance-focused peripherals as the team continues to compete on the global stage,” said Kris Huang, General Manager of the ASUS Gaming and Workspace Gear Business Unit.

In addition, ROG announced that it has teamed up with Timothy ‘iiTzTimmy’ An , acclaimed FPS content creator and former professional Apex Legends player, adding him to its growing roster of esports partners across gaming gear and displays.

With his combination of exceptional mechanics, entertaining content, and strong connection with the global gaming community, iiTzTimmy has become one of the most recognizable creators in competitive gaming. Through the partnership, iiTzTimmy will work closely with ROG across upcoming content, product showcases, and community activations, bringing his perspective as a high-level FPS player to the latest ROG gaming gear. With a shared focus on performance and continuous improvement, the collaboration further strengthens ROG’s commitment to supporting competitive players and the broader esports community.

Dedicated ROG Ace esports channel

The new ROG Ace Esports Instagram provides competitive players and esports fans with a dedicated destination for the latest ROG esports content. Product news, professional player insights, team collaborations, tournament updates, and setup inspiration can come together in one channel, helping fans stay close to the gear, players, and stories that shape the ROG esports ecosystem.

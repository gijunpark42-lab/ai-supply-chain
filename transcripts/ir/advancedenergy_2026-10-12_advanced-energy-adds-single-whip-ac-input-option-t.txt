Advanced Energy Adds Single-Whip AC Input Option to 33 kW ORv3 HPR Power Shelf

Advanced Energy Adds Single-Whip AC Input Option to 33 kW ORv3 HPR Power Shelf

Posted

October 12, 2026

New connection option enables rack designs optimized for a single AC feed to each power shelf, with multiple shelves operating in parallel to deliver 100 kW or more for AI data center and high-performance computing racks

DENVER, Oct. 12, 2026 — Advanced Energy Industries Inc. (Nasdaq: AEIS), a global leader in highly engineered, precision power conversion, measurement, and control solutions, today launched a single-whip option for its 33 kW ORv3 High-Power Rack (HPR) power shelf at the 2026 OCP Global Summit. The option provides one AC power connection per shelf, with separate left- and right-side input configurations to accommodate different data center rack layouts.

“Power delivery into IT racks has been planned around a facility’s AC distribution and its computing load,” said Conor Quinn, Vice President, Marketing, High Volume Products, Advanced Energy. “The position of the AC feed affects cable routing and rack layout. Offering engineers a choice of input location enables them to account for these design constraints in their system design work.”

The shelf accommodates six 5.5 kW power supply modules in one Open Rack Unit (1OU) form factor. A fully populated shelf delivers 33 kW without redundancy or 27.5 kW with N+1 power-module redundancy. The modules are hot-swappable.

Multiple shelves can operate in parallel to power racks requiring 100 kW or more, supporting AI and high-performance computing applications. Customers can select from multiple monitoring and control options, including an Ethernet-based controller compatible with DMTF Redfish®. As always, Advanced Energy’s global field application engineering team provides responsive technical support.

Product specifications and more information are available here. Advanced Energy’s ORv3 HPR Power Shelf family has been supporting a range of needs since 2023.

Press Release 25 September 2026 Siemens Mobility Berlin

BeNEX awards Siemens contract for 56 regional trains for the network Mainfranken

Delivery of 56 regional trains in total: 26 four-car Desiro High Capacity and 30 three-car Mireo trains

Deployment on the Frankfurt–Würzburg–Nürnberg/Bamberg routes in Hesse and Bavaria starting in late 2030

Modern vehicle equipment featuring future-proof signaling and control technology, accessibility, environmentally friendly air conditioning, USB ports, and bicycle spaces

Delivery of 56 regional trains in total: 26 four-car Desiro High Capacity and 30 three-car Mireo trains

Deployment on the Frankfurt–Würzburg–Nürnberg/Bamberg routes in Hesse and Bavaria starting in late 2030

Modern vehicle equipment featuring future-proof signaling and control technology, accessibility, environmentally friendly air conditioning, USB ports, and bicycle spaces

Markus Frank, Head of Finance Commuter Rail, Siemens

Mobility; Thomas Graetz Head

Director at BeNEX and Michael von Mallinckrodt, Managing Director at BeNEX

Mobility

BeNEX commissioned

Siemens Mobility at InnoTrans in Berlin – the international trade fair for the

transport technology industry – to deliver a total of 56 regional trains for

the Mainfranken regional network. The order comprises 26 four-car Desiro HC and

30 three-car Mireo trains equipped with the European Train Control System

(ETCS), intermittent automatic train control (PZB), and Onboard Units. The

trains are barrier-free and equipped with eco-friendly air conditioning, USB

ports, and bicycle spaces. The new vehicles are scheduled to enter service on

the Frankfurt–Würzburg–Nürnberg/Bamberg routes at the end of 2030.

“Awarding the vehicle

contract to Siemens is a significant step for the successful development of the

Mainfranken regional network by agilis, the BeNEX Group's regional transport

company in Bavaria. In Siemens Mobility, we have chosen a partner that stands

for high quality and reliability," says

Johann von Georg, Managing

Director at BeNEX. Michael von Mallinckrodt, Managing Director at BeNEX ,

adds: "The vehicles will set new standards in comfort and capacity for

passengers in the region and offers a significantly improved travel experience.

With this project, we are continuing our successful collaboration with

Siemens."

“With the new trains

for the Mainfranken regional network, an even more attractive rail offering is

being created for millions of passengers in Hesse and Bavaria. Our Desiro High

Capacity and Mireo trains combine state-of-the-art rail technology with high

capacity, accessibility, and travel comfort. That’s how we are creating

conditions for more capacity for passengers on the network and making an

important contribution to sustainable mobility in one of Germany's strongest

economic regions," says

Thomas Graetz, Head

of Commuter and Regional Trains, Siemens Mobility.

Starting in December

2030, passengers in the Mainfranken regional network will benefit from modern

regional trains. All 56 vehicles are equipped with ETCS and PZB train

protection systems and offer high travel comfort with environmentally friendly

air conditioning, USB charging ports, barrier-free entry, wheelchair spaces,

and generous areas for bicycles. As such, the new trains combine modern rail

technology with a comfortable and inclusive travel experience.

Michael

von Mallinckrodt, Managing Director at BeNEX; Thomas Graetz Head of Commuter and

Regional Trains, Siemens Mobility and Johann von Georg, Managing Director at

BeNEX (from left to right), Photo source: Siemens

Mobility

The 30 three-car Mireo

trains impress with their high flexibility in regional transport. With 210

seats, two toilets, and 18 bicycle spaces, they offer a balanced train design

for both commuters and leisure travelers. The vehicles are specifically

designed for comfortable daily rail journeys, combining energy efficiency and

high performance with a modern interior design.

The 26 four-car Desiro

High Capacity trains will be deployed on routes with particularly high demand.

With 424 seats, these double-decker trains provide significantly more capacity

for growing passenger numbers. 39 bicycle spaces, three toilets, and the spacious

double-decker concept make these vehicles true "space miracles" in

regional transport, ensuring a relaxed travel experience even during peak

times.

For efficient operation

and predictive maintenance, the vehicles will be connected to the Siemens

Mobility Railigent X platform. This digital interface will be used to exchange

and evaluate diagnostic data, passenger counting data, and information for the

passenger information system and other functions. Furthermore, Siemens will

supply spare parts packages and special tools for the Mireo and Desiro HC

vehicle platforms. This establishes the foundation for high vehicle

availability and reliable operation throughout the entire lifecycle of the

fleet.

The vehicles will be

used across a network approximately 330 kilometers long in Hesse and Bavaria. The

authorities responsible for the network are the Bavarian Railway Company (BEG)

and the Rhine-Main Transport Association (RMV). The network was officially

awarded to BeNEX on July 16, 2026.

Siemens Mobility

is a separately managed company of Siemens AG. As a leader in intelligent transport solutions for more than 175 years, Siemens Mobility is constantly innovating its portfolio. Its core areas include rolling stock, rail automation and electrification, a comprehensive software portfolio, turnkey systems as well as related services. With digital products and solutions, and the use of industrial AI, Siemens Mobility is enabling mobility operators worldwide to make their infrastructure intelligent, increase value sustainably over the entire lifecycle, enhance passenger experience, and guarantee availability. In fiscal year 2025, which ended on September 30, 2025, Siemens Mobility posted revenue of €12.4 billion and employed around 43,400 people worldwide. Further information is available at:

www.siemens.com/mobility

BeNEX is a leading investment holding company for rail transport in Germany. It holds stakes in seven established regional rail companies (agilis, abellio, cantus, nordbahn, metronom, ODEG, and WestfalenBahn), as well as the BeNEX Academy—a training and further education provider for train drivers—and PTS, a service company specializing in cleaning and security. The Group employs over 3,500 people and, with approximately 240 rail vehicles, delivers an annual transport volume of 72 million train kilometers. With the recently awarded Mainfranken network, this figure is set to grow to 79 million train kilometers per year. For further information, please visit:

www.benex.de

ASUS Makes TIME’s World’s Best Companies List for the Third Time, Ranked 48th Globally

September 10, 2026

・

Press Release

The prestigious ranking celebrates ASUS for outstanding performance in employee satisfaction, revenue growth, and sustainable transparency

TIME recognition: Ranked 48th globally, ASUS secures third inclusion on TIME’s World’s Best Companies that set the standard in employee satisfaction, growth, sustainability

A commitment to innovation: ASUS advances its Ubiquitous AI. Incredible Possibilities strategy to become the world’s most comprehensive AI company

Worldwide acclaim: ASUS continues to receive global distinction from esteemed organizations such as Fortune, Forbes, and Newsweek

TAIPEI, Taiwan, September 10, 2026 — ASUS today announced its third appearance in TIME magazine’s esteemed list of the World’s Best Companies, ranking 48th globally. This accolade recognizes organizations that set the standard in employee satisfaction, revenue growth, and sustainability transparency. The win highlights ASUS’s commitment to excellence in the IT, Electronics, Hardware, and Equipment industry.

TIME’s World’s Best Companies 2026 ranking offers valuable insight into what defines corporate success today — guiding consumers, investors, and policymakers to support the world’s most forward-thinking businesses. The inclusion of ASUS reflects these standards and reinforces the company’s continued focus on people, innovation, and sustainability.

“We are honored that ASUS has again been recognized by TIME as one of the World’s Best Companies,” said S.Y. Hsu, ASUS Co-CEO. “This reflects the progress of our Ubiquitous AI. Incredible Possibilities strategy as we build a comprehensive AI ecosystem — from infrastructure and accelerators to AI PCs, edge applications, and real-world solutions. By uniting our innovation capabilities across the company, ASUS is turning AI into meaningful experiences that create value for customers, industries, and society.”

“We are proud to receive this recognition from TIME, which underscores our commitment to sustainable growth and responsible innovation,” said Samson Hu, ASUS Co-CEO. “Through stronger sustainability governance and initiatives such as our AI x ESG Platform, ASUS is making sustainability data more transparent, proactive, and actionable. As we advance toward our 2050 net-zero goal, we remain focused on creating long-term value for people, partners, and the planet.”

Global Recognition and Excellence

ASUS continues to drive innovation and excellence in technology, earning global recognition for its commitment to enhancing lives and delivering incredible experiences. The company was recently recognized on Fortune’s 2026 World’s Most Admired Companies for the 11th time, listed on Forbes’ 2026 America’s Best Employers , and it topped Interbrand’s 2025 Best Taiwan Global Brands for the 12th time. These achievements validate the company’s vision of becoming the world’s most admired, innovative, and leading technology enterprise in the new digital era.

TIME’s World’s Best Companies 2026

Recognized globally, the World’s Best Companies 2026 list by TIME Magazine spotlights 1,000 organizations setting the standard in innovation, growth, and employee satisfaction. Companies were selected through a three-stage screening process evaluating employee satisfaction, based on respondents’ willingness to recommend their employer and satisfaction with work-related factors; revenue growth, where both relative and absolute growth were considered, as it underlines companies’ financial performance; and ESG practices, including carbon emissions and other sustainability transparency metrics. The ranking highlights organizations that deliver strong results while fostering positive workplaces and promoting responsible, sustainable practices.

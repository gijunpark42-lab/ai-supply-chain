TAIPEI, Taiwan, August 25, 2026 — ASUS Republic of Gamers (ROG) today at Gamescom 2026 announced an arsenal of new products including three new esports-grade OLED gaming monitors, fully-customizable gaming mice and keyboards, high-performance gaming routers, and special edition graphics cards.

These latest offerings give gamers more ways to build setups that match their play styles, spaces, and performance needs. As part of the Gamescom celebration, ROG is also celebrating its 20th anniversary, honoring two decades of innovation, passion, and the spirit of gaming.

Visitors can expect a full schedule of onstage and community events throughout Gamescom 2026 with live giveaways, musical performances, and gaming competitions featuring Team Vitality. Additionally, ROG is also marking its 20th anniversary with support for SpecialEffect: a one-of-a-kind ROG 20th Anniversary suitcase signed by Jonney Shih, ASUS Chairman, and Jensen Huang, NVIDIA founder and CEO, together with limited-edition ROG gear, goes to a charity auction hosted by SpecialEffect on eBay for Charity from August 25 to September 4 , with 100% of the proceeds going to the charity. Full details and bidding are available on the official eBay listing.

ROG OLED Esports Monitors

ROG OLED Esports monitors

As the world's No.1 OLED gaming monitor brand , ROG further sharpens its competitive edge with the launch of the ROG Swift OLED PG259QWS Ace, the ROG Strix OLED XG259QDPG Ace, and the XG259QDNS Ace. These three 24.5-inch Ace esports monitors are developed with direct input from professional players and expand the ROG OLED Esports monitor lineup, joining the ROG Strix OLED XG259QWPG Ace, which recently made its debut at Computex 2026.

Every Ace monitor shares the tools pros rely on to perform at their best, including integrated measurement scales [i] on the tilt (-5°–+33°), swivel (±45°), and height (0–155mm) adjustments to help players consistently reproduce their preferred setup; a Quick OSD menu that lets players easily tweak core settings before every game; and three Esports Color modes that provide tailored presets optimized for three different FPS gaming scenarios. Every Ace monitor also incorporates Gallium Nitride Field-Effect Transistor (GaNFET) technology in its power-supply design to optimize heat dissipation to protect the panel and help extend the OLED lifespan.

ROG Swift OLED PG259QWS Ace monitor

With a blistering 720Hz refresh rate, the ROG Swift OLED PG259QWS Ace is the new hero of the series and the fastest OLED monitor ever made. Its TrueBlack Glossy™ Tandem WOLED panel features a 0.02ms response time for exceptional motion clarity and delivers supersharp imagery with deep, inky blacks, even under bright arena lights. The exclusive Position Sensor feature takes precision setups to the next level: a built-in sensor measures the monitor's tilt, height, and swivel angles and displays them on-screen, so pro players can quickly and precisely dial in their exact preferred setup — even in the high-pressure environment of a live competition.

ROG is also proud to announce that the PG259QWS Ace has been selected as the official esports monitor of the 2026 PGL CS2 Major tournament, bringing the world's fastest OLED to the sport's biggest stage. “A stage this historic deserves a display to match,” says Silviu Stroie, CEO of PGL. “The first OLED Major on the fastest OLED ever made — it’s a new generation of competitive Counter-Strike." The new ROG Swift OLED PG259QWS Ace and ROG Strix OLED XG259QWPG Ace will be available in early Q4 with MSRPs of US$1,099 and US$699, respectively.

ROG Strix OLED XG259QDPG Ace monitor

The ROG Strix OLED XG259QDPG Ace is a 560Hz RGB QD-OLED panel featuring BlackShield™ Film and an anti-glare low-reflection coating that boosts perceived black levels by up to 40% and increases panel hardness by 2.5X. It delivers vivid, true-to-life color and glare-free visuals for fast-paced competitive play. Connectivity options include one DisplayPort™ 1.4 (DSC), two HDMI® 2.1, and two USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-A ports for maximum compatibility.

ROG Strix OLED XG259QDNS Ace monitor

The ROG Strix OLED XG259QDNS Ace combines the benefits of an RGB QD-OLED panel featuring BlackShield Film and an anti-glare low-reflection coating with an accessible 360Hz refresh rate, enabling a wider base of players to enjoy Ace-grade esports visuals and pro-tuned setup tools. Connectivity options include DisplayPort 1.4 (DSC) and two HDMI 2.1 ports.

ROG Strix XG27UCMG and XG27ACNS Triple Mode monitors

The ROG Strix XG27UCMG is the world's first 4K triple-mode gaming monitor. ROG Triple Mode technology allows gamers to enjoy different game genres with a single monitor. The 27-inch 4K (3840 x 2160) display enables 4K@240Hz, QHD@400Hz (OC), or FHD@488Hz (OC) visuals — so players can prioritize pristine fidelity or blistering speed on demand.

A 95% DCI-P3 wide color gamut and VESA® DisplayHDR™ 400 bring vibrant, lifelike colors and deep contrast, while ASUS Smart Pixel technology enhances every detail for sharper, more vibrant visuals — even at lower resolutions. In addition, a built-in KVM switch with hotkey enables effortless switching and management between two connected systems. The XG27UCMG offers a host of connectivity options, including DisplayPort 1.4 (DSC), HDMI 2.1, USB-C with 15-watt Power Delivery, and two USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-A ports. It includes ASUS DisplayWidget Center for auto firmware updates and easy export/import of monitor settings to any device, plus ROG Gaming AI technology with AI Visual, Dynamic Shadow Boost, and Dynamic Crosshair.

The ROG Strix XG27ACNS is a 27-inch QHD (2560 x 1440) triple-mode gaming monitor that offers QHD@475Hz (OC), FHD@620Hz, or HD@920Hz for extreme high-refresh competitive play. Its Fast IPS panel with 0.3ms (min) GTG response time further reduces ghosting for fluid gaming experiences, while a 95% DCI-P3 wide color gamut and VESA DisplayHDR 400 deliver vibrant, lifelike colors and deep contrast. The XG27ACNS features DisplayPort 1.4 (DSC), HDMI 2.1, and USB-C with 15-watt Power Delivery. Like the aforementioned XG27UCMG, it features ASUS DisplayWidget Center and ROG Gaming AI technology with AI Visual, Dynamic Shadow Boost, and Dynamic Crosshair.

ROG Gladius IV Ace mouse

ROG Gladius IV Ace is an ergonomic esports gaming mouse built through sports-science research and refined for competitive play. With customizable weight, ROG SpeedNova 8K Gen II wireless technology, ROG AimPoint Pro 65K sensor, Zone Mode, and browser-based Gear Link customization, it delivers precision and performance tuned for every clutch moment. Its e rgonomic design is tested by esports pros and validated by sports science to help minimize muscle strain during repetitive, high-intensity movements.

The Gladius IV Ace weighs just 56 grams and includes a set of modular weights for players to adjust mouse feel with seven different weight configurations. Each button features ROG optical micro switches that ensure consistent click-force, instant actuation, and a 100-million-click lifespan. Tracking is incredibly precise and consistent with the ROG Aimpoint Pro 65K sensor, providing up to 65,000dpi sensitivity and 11-level lift-off distance (LOD) customization.

ROG SpeedNova 8K Gen II technology ensures stable true 8K wireless performance for competitive play, improved battery efficiency, and features an enhanced receiver with dual LED indicators to show RF status, battery level, and active profile. Zone Mode gives gamers a competition-ready profile with optimized polling, sensor responsiveness, frame-rate tuning, and TX power control. Gamers also benefit from easy customization directly via a browser, using ROG Gear Link, with no additional software downloads required. The Gladius IV Ace features wide, low-friction 100% PTFE mouse feet with rounded edges that facilitate smooth and fast movements.

ROG also plans to release the ROG Gladius IV Ace Max – a slightly larger version for players with bigger hands. This ties in with ROG’s goal of creating ‘a shape for every champion’.

ROG Spatha X 65K

ROG Spatha X 65K mouse

The ROG Spatha X 65K is a customizable wireless MMO gaming mouse with 12 programmable buttons, five weight-and-balance configurations, and interchangeable switches. It features the ROG AimPoint Pro 65K sensor and an advanced charging dock that doubles as a wireless receiver for fast, stable ROG SpeedNova connectivity. The programmable buttons can be configured for skills and macros to keep essential in-game commands at one’s fingertips, and gamers can fit modular weights into the chassis to adjust the overall feel of the mouse.

Complementing the ROG Push-Fit Switch Socket III design that enables fast and easy switch installation, the mouse includes two switch options: the ROG Micro Switch II offers a crisp feel with a 100-million-click durability, while the ROG Silent Micro Switch provides a muted, 'thocky' feel and 50-million-click lifespan. For flexibility, the socket also supports a wide range of third-party switches.

The ROG AimPoint Pro 65K sensor provides up to 65,000dpi sensitivity and 11-level lift-off distance (LOD) customization, ensuring incredibly precise and consistent tracking. In addition, ROG SpeedNova wireless technology offers ultra-low-latency performance with stable, reliable connectivity. Gamers can easily customize various mouse settings via a browser, using ROG Gear Link.

Tri-mode connectivity allows gamers to connect via 2.4GHz RF, Bluetooth, or wired USB. The Spatha X 65K includes a wireless magnetic charging dock that also serves as a display stand when the mouse is not in use.

ROG Rapture GT-BN98 gaming router

The ROG Rapture GT-BN98 is the world's first WiFi 8 gaming router, engineered to deliver next-generation performance with greater stability, lower latency, and enhanced multi-device connectivity. Featuring AI Game Boost, intelligent network optimization, dual 10G and four 2.5G ports, and advanced thermal design, it provides ultrafast wired and wireless performance for demanding gaming and home network environments. Built with ASUS-exclusive networking technologies and WiFi 8 innovations, the GT-BN98 redefines premium gaming connectivity.

WiFi 8 offers higher mid-to-long range throughput and better IoT coverage, boosting quad band throughput up to 25,000Mbps. Adaptive QoE dynamically allocates bandwidth based on live network conditions and application demands, ensuring smooth performance even in high-congestion, multi-tasking environments. WiFi Insight runs in the background and monitors the entire network across all bands in real time, with continuous detection of both WiFi and non-WiFi interference. In addition, ASUS GTNet analyzes real-time network conditions to dynamically route traffic through the most reliable path to game servers.

The GT-BN98 also features advanced protection that blocks ads, trackers, malicious sites, and infected devices to safeguard the network. Plus, Guest Network Pro software offers effortless network segmentation with up to five SSIDs for easy IoT device setup and management, instant VPN connections and convenient parental controls.

ASUS ZenWiFi BN12

ASUS ZenWiFi BN12 mesh system

ZenWiFi BN12 is a next-generation WiFi 8 mesh router, designed to deliver fast, stable connections for streaming, gaming, remote work, and every connected device throughout the home. ZenWiFi BN12 delivers up to 19000Mbps tri-band speeds with higher mid-to-long range throughput for reliable connections. ASUS Smart AiMesh with WiFi 8 Multi-AP Coordination provides up to 7000 sq. ft. of coverage and enhanced IoT coverage. The router’s eight internal antennas and high-power front-end modules eliminate dead zones. ZenWiFi BN12 also enables flexible WAN setups with its dual 10G Ethernet ports, auto WAN detection, and 4G/5G mobile tethering for always-on connectivity. Advanced triple-level network security with AiProtection includes parental controls, VPN support, and built-in blocking of ads, trackers, and malicious sites. Meanwhile, adaptive QoE prioritizes the most important applications for smoother gaming, streaming, and low-latency performance.

Vistra Announces Redemption of All Outstanding Series A Preferred Stock

IRVING, Texas , Oct. 15, 2026 / PRNewswire / -- Vistra Corp. (NYSE: VST) today announced its redemption of all outstanding shares of its 8.0% Fixed-Rate Reset Cumulative Redeemable Perpetual Preferred Stock, Series A, $0.01 par value per share (the "Series A Preferred Stock") in accordance with the terms and conditions set forth in the governing documents for the Series A Preferred Stock.

All one million shares of the Series A Preferred Stock (CUSIP: 92840M AB8 (Rule 144A) and U92266 AA0 (Regulation S)) are being redeemed at a price of $1,000 per share of preferred stock representing $1.0 billion in aggregate liquidation preference. Vistra had previously declared distributions on the Series A Preferred Shares payable on Oct. 15, 2026, thus there were no accrued and unpaid dividends as of the redemption date.

A formal notice of redemption was delivered to holders of record in accordance with the terms of the Series A Preferred Stock and applicable procedures of Cede & Co., the nominee of DTC. Additional information regarding the redemption, including payment procedures, the treatment of accrued dividends, and any required holder actions, was provided in the official notice of redemption and related materials distributed by Equiniti Trust Company, LLC, as redemption and paying agent.

On and after the redemption date, the Series A Preferred Stock ceased to be outstanding, dividends ceased to accrue on such shares, and all rights of holders were terminated other than the right to receive the applicable redemption price and any accrued and unpaid dividends payable in accordance with the terms of the Series A Preferred Stock.

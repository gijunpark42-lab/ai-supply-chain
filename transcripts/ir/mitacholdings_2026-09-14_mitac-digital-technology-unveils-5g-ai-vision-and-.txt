MiTAC Digital Technology Unveils 5G AI Vision and Integrated Fleet Solutions at IAA 2026-MiTAC Holdings Corporation

2026-09-14

MDT

Empowering Telematics Service Providers with a Comprehensive 360° Safety Ecosystem

HANNOVER, Germany & TAIPEI, Taiwan – September 14, 2026 — MiTAC Digital Technology Corp. (MDT), a leading provider of smart telematics solutions, today announced its participation in IAA TRANSPORTATION 2026 . Meeting the growing safety and operational demands of commercial fleets, MDT will showcase its end-to-end telematics lineup—seamlessly connecting 5G dashcams, rugged vehicle tablets, and cloud management platforms. MDT also underscores its commitment as a trusted technology partner for global Telematics Service Providers (TSPs), accelerating commercial fleet intelligence.

End-to-End Synergy: 360° Zero-Blind-Spot Fleet Protection

Designed for demanding logistics environments, MDT deeply integrates its latest 5G K Series connected dashcams , the MioWORK™ F840 rugged vehicle tablet , and the VisionMax fleet platform into an uninterrupted safety ecosystem:

In-Cabin Display & Blind-Spot Removal: The in-vehicle MioWORK™ F840 serves as a navigation and dispatch terminal with multi-camera integration. Triggered by turn signals or reverse gear, it automatically displays corresponding exterior camera angles, eliminating blind spots and mitigating collision risks.

5G Connectivity & 7-Channel Panoramic Coverage: Featuring high-speed 5G connectivity , the latest K Series dashcam delivers high-bandwidth, ultra-low-latency video streaming and instant cloud incident backups. With support for up to seven camera channels covering vehicle exteriors and the cabin, it guarantees complete 360° visibility.

Cloud AI Driver Monitoring System (DMS): Leveraging advanced cloud AI algorithms with high detection accuracy, the DMS performs secondary analytics on critical risk behaviors—such as fatigue, distracted driving (phone use or smoking), and unfastened seatbelts. This minimizes manual review times and translates raw data into targeted coaching insights.

RouteComplete Platform: Dynamic Dispatching: The RouteComplete 2.0 system integrates vehicle routing with video telematics, offering continuous time-lapse recording and instant event reporting. In emergencies, dispatchers can view real-time location and video feeds to execute immediate rerouting, maximizing operational agility.

Enabling TSPs: Open, Modular, and Built for Seamless Integration

Fleet architectures are increasingly complex, often burdening TSPs with hardware fragmentation, video latency, and algorithm adaptation. MDT stands out with 100% in-house development across core hardware, edge computing, and cloud platforms—delivering an industrial-grade foundation for partners.

With an open, modular architecture, MDT supports diverse TSP business models:

Deploying ready-to-use, complete solutions to fast-track commercial rollout.

Utilizing comprehensive APIs and SDKs to embed MDT’s imaging and AI analytics into existing fleet portals.

This eliminates the cost of building low-level infrastructure from scratch, significantly accelerating time-to-market.

"The future of commercial telematics lies in openness and collaboration," stated MDT. "We provide not only robust automotive hardware and AI vision, but also dedicated technical backing. Through modular flexibility, we help TSPs lower R&D overhead, quickly address market demands, and capture global mobility opportunities."

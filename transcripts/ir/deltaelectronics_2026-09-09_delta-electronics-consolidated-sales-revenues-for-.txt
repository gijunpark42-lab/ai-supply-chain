Delta Electronics’ Consolidated Sales Revenues

for August 2026 Totaled NT$64,586 Million

TAIPEI, September 9, 2026 – Delta Electronics, Inc. today announced its consolidated sales revenues for August 2026 totaled NT$64,586 million, a 34.9 percent increase as compared to NT$47,860 million for August 2025 and a 3.7 percent decrease as compared to NT$67,073 million for July 2026. The Company's cumulative consolidated sales revenues from January to August in 2026 were NT$474,268 million, a 41.1 percent increase as compared to NT$336,211 million for the same period in 2025.

Percentage of sales for Delta’s four main business categories for August 2026:

Power Electronics

54%

Mobility

4%

Automation

8%

Infrastructure

34%

Others

0%

The figures have not been audited.

Socionext Presented at ELIV Congress 2026

Socionext Inc. presented at the ELIV Congress 2026 which was held from October 14 to 15, 2026, at Kongresshaus Baden-Baden in Germany.

ELIV (Electronics in Vehicles) was one of the world's largest international conferences focused on automotive electronics, software, and applications.

The event brought together automotive manufacturers, suppliers, and research institutions from around the globe to discuss the latest technologies and industry trends.

At this conference, Socionext presented its approach to addressing the reliability, safety, and design challenges associated with automotive chiplets.

Accton’s Monthly Sales Revenue (Consolidated) – Jul

HSINCHU, Taiwan, Agu. 06, 2026 – Accton Technology Corporation (Accton) (TWSE: 2345)

Accton Technology Corporation (Accton) (TWSE: 2345), a premier provider of communication solutions, today announced that the consolidated sales revenue for Jul. 2026 was USD$1,225 million, an increase of 56.23 percent year-over-year.

The cumulative consolidated sales revenue totaled approximately USD$6,465 million, an increase of 59.17 percent year-over-year.

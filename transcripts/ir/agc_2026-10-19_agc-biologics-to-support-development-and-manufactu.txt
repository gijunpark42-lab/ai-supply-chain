AGC Biologics to Support Development and Manufacturing of Flen Health’s Next-Generation Enzyme Therapy for Diabetic Foot Ulcers ｜ News ｜ AGC

AGC Biologics, a global biopharmaceutical contract development and manufacturing organization (CDMO) within the AGC Group, has entered into an agreement with Belgian biopharmaceutical company Flen Health to provide process development and GMP* manufacturing services for recombinant enzymes for use in a next-generation therapy for diabetic foot ulcers.

At its Heidelberg site in Germany, AGC Biologics will carry out process development and manufacturing activities for lactoperoxidase and glucose oxidase. This work will support Flen Health's planned Investigational New Drug (IND) application and Phase II clinical trial in the United States.

Advancing a Next-Generation Enzyme Therapy for Diabetic Foot Ulcers

In 2024, approximately 589 million adults worldwide, or about one in nine, were living with diabetes. Its prevalence is projected to rise further.

Diabetic foot ulcers are chronic wounds that develop on the feet of people with diabetes. More than one in five people with diabetes are estimated to be at risk of developing a diabetic foot ulcer during their lifetime. Effective treatment options remain limited, creating a strong need for new therapeutic approaches.

To address this unmet medical need, Flen Health is developing a next-generation therapy using recombinant enzymes. The therapy is based on Flaminal ® , Flen Health’s wound treatment gel that combines enzymes and alginate and is marketed primarily in Europe.

Expanding the Collaboration from a Feasibility Program to GMP Manufacturing

AGC Biologics and Flen Health began a feasibility program in July 2025. At its Heidelberg site, AGC Biologics has since undertaken technology transfer for the manufacturing processes of lactoperoxidase and glucose oxidase. It has also developed cultivation and purification processes and implemented analytical methods.

Under the new agreement, the collaboration will advance to the next stage, covering process development, scale-up and GMP manufacturing. AGC Biologics plans to manufacture the recombinant enzymes at the 100-liter and 1,000-liter scales.

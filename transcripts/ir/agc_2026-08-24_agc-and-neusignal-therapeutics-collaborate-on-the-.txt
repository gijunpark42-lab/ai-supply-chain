AGC and Neusignal Therapeutics Collaborate on the Development of Alzheimer’s Disease Drug Candidate “NTX-083” ｜ News ｜ AGC

AGC Inc. (Headquarters: Tokyo; President: Yoshinori Hirai; hereinafter “AGC”) highlights its end-to-end support capabilities as a small-molecule pharmaceutical CDMO as demonstrated by its collaboration with Neusignal Therapeutics, Inc. (Headquarters: Tokyo; CEO: Itaru Arimoto; hereinafter “NTX”).

In this collaboration, AGC provided integrated support for the drug substance of “NTX-083,” an Alzheimer’s disease drug candidate being developed by NTX, covering process development, GMP *1 manufacturing, and regulatory support from the early stages of development. In addition to establishing the manufacturing process for the drug substance, AGC completed the preparation of documentation required for an IND application *2 in the United States. NTX-083 is scheduled to enter clinical trials in the United States in 2026.

Process Development

Pharma Chemicals Chiba Site

Alzheimer’s disease is one of the disorders with significant unmet medical needs *3 , and the number of patients continues to increase worldwide. NTX is advancing the development of NTX-083 with the goal of providing a new treatment option for patients facing this challenge. In the process development and manufacturing of the NTX-083 drug substance, AGC supported a smooth transition from the early stages of development to the clinical stage through collaboration between its Yokohama Technical Center, a process development site, and its Chiba Site, a GMP manufacturing site. AGC also supported the preparation of CMC *4 -related documentation required for the IND application.

Under its medium-term management plan, AGC plus-2026 , the AGC Group has identified three social values it aims to create through its products and technologies. One of these values, “Well-being” aims to contribute to safe, secure, and healthy lives through the stable supply of products and services essential to healthcare and everyday life. Through its pharmaceutical and agrochemical CDMO services, the Life Science business supports drug discovery and pharmaceutical development and contributes to improving people’s health and quality of life.

〈Notes〉

*1

GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice):

A quality standard that ensures the quality and safety of pharmaceutical products by controlling the entire manufacturing process, from raw material receipt through production and product release.

*2

IND (Investigational New Drug) Application:

An application submitted to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) to obtain authorization to initiate clinical trials using an investigational drug.

*3

Unmet Medical Needs:

Medical needs that remain insufficiently addressed due to the absence of effective treatments or because existing therapies do not provide adequate outcomes.

*4

CMC (Chemistry, Manufacturing and Controls):

Information and documentation related to manufacturing processes and quality controls required to ensure the quality and safety of pharmaceutical products.

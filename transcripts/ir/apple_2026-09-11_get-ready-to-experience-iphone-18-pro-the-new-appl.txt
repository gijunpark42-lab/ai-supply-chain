Get ready to experience iPhone 18 Pro, the next generation of Apple Watch, and AirPods 5

Pre-orders for iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max begin Saturday, September 12

Customers can soon pre-order the iPhone 18 Pro lineup, the next generation of Apple Watch, and AirPods 5, along with all-new accessories.

It’s almost iPhone Pro time. Starting Saturday, September 12, at 5 a.m. PT, customers can pre-order iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max on apple.com and in the Apple Store app.

iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max deliver massive advancements across camera, performance, battery, and intelligence. The new 48MP Fusion Main camera with variable aperture unlocks creative control that wasn’t possible before, and new Pro controls let users customize their experience in the Camera app for even easier access to their most used settings. Both models are powered by A20 Pro and feature a next-generation vapor chamber for the highest sustained performance yet, while updated battery designs leverage powerful silicon efficiencies to deliver incredible battery life. Featuring iOS 27 with the next generation of Apple Intelligence and Siri AI, an entirely new version of Siri, iPhone 18 Pro models bring powerful AI capabilities together with personal context to become an intelligent personal hub with privacy and security at its core. 1

Whether shopping in stores or online, customers can connect with Apple Specialists for personalized support to help choose the right device for them and learn how to protect their products with AppleCare+ or AppleCare One, as well as for assistance with ways to save and pay, including leasing, financing options, and carrier deals. Customers can then take advantage of convenient home delivery or arrange for pickup at their local Apple Store at a time most suitable for them.

Apple Watch Series 12, Apple Watch Ultra 4, AirPods 5, Mac mini, and Mac Studio are all available to pre-order now on apple.com and in the Apple Store app. Along with iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max, Apple Watch and AirPods 5 will be available in stores and online worldwide beginning Friday, September 18. Mac mini and Mac Studio will be available in stores on Tuesday, September 22.

Apple Specialists are ready to help customers explore the latest products, choose from a variety of financing options and carrier deals, and select convenient home delivery options or in-store pickup.

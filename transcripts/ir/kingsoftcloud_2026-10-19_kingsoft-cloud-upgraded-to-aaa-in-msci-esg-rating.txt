Kingsoft Cloud Upgraded to "AAA" in MSCI ESG Rating

10/19/2026

PDF Version

BEIJING , Oct. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Kingsoft Cloud Holdings Limited (" Kingsoft Cloud " or the " Company ") (NASDAQ: KC and HKEX: 3896), a leading cloud service provider in China , is pleased to announce that its Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) rating by Morgan Stanley Capital International (" MSCI ") recently has been upgraded from "AA" to " AAA " (the highest tier), marking its third rating upgrade from 2022 to 2026. This achievement highlights Kingsoft Cloud's continuous commitment to excellence in ESG practices.

Kingsoft Cloud introduced its "CLOUD" sustainability strategy in its 2025 ESG Report, encompassing Corporate Governance, Labor Cultivating, Operational Excellence, Unified Eco-Creation and Digital Innovation. Under this framework, the Company continues to enhance its practices across key areas including technological innovation, privacy and data security, customer service, talent development, business ethics and compliance, while promoting the sustainable development of its cloud business in the AI era.

MSCI ESG Ratings are designed to measure a company's resilience to financially relevant, industry-specific sustainability risks and opportunities relative to its peers. Ratings range from AAA to CCC, with AAA being the highest level. The ratings serve as an essential benchmark for institutional investors in risk management and capital allocation.

Looking ahead, Kingsoft Cloud will focus on delivering the strategy of " High-Quality Sustainable Development ", and continue to integrate sustainability into its technological innovation and business operations, strengthen its ESG governance, and create long-term value for customers, employees, shareholders and the broader community.

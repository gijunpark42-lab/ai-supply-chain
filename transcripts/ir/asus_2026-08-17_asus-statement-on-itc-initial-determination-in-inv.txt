ASUS Statement on ITC Initial Determination in Investigation No. 337-TA-1454

A notice of an Initial Determination ha s been issued by the Administrative Law Judge of the U.S. International Trade Commission (ITC) in Investigation No. 337-TA-1454 , regarding a complaint filed by Estelgia, LLC.

T he present Initial Determination does not currently impose any restrictions on the sale of ASUS WiFi and/or router products in the United States, nor does it affect software updates, warranty support, or repair services. The case will now proceed to review by the full ITC Commission.

ASUS respects all intellectual property rights and remains confident in the compliance of its products and technologies. We will continue to vigorously protect our interests through the appropriate legal processes.

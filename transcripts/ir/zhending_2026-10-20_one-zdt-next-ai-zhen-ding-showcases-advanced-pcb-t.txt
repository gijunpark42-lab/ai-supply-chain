One ZDT, Next AI! Zhen Ding Showcases Advanced PCB Technologies for the AI Era at TPCA Show 2026

Zhen Ding Technology Holding Limited (TWSE: 4958), the world's largest PCB manufacturer for nine consecutive years, announced its participation in TPCA Show 2026, taking place from October 20 to 22 at Taipei Nangang Exhibition Center, Hall 1. Under the theme “One ZDT, Next AI,” Zhen Ding will showcase its latest technologies and R&D achievements across AI servers, high-speed optical communications, edge AI devices, and other advanced applications at Booth M-617. The exhibition will highlight the company’s comprehensive PCB solutions spanning AI infrastructure to intelligent end devices. In addition, Zhen Ding’s General Manager Jeff Chien will join the “TPCA Show Trends Forum” on October 21 to discuss technological innovation, global manufacturing strategies, and supply chain resilience in the AI era alongside industry leaders.

3. Reason for not counting a portion of the number of voting rights of shareholders who

attended the meeting in the voting results

Conditions for approval for each proposal were satisfied by t he total number of all the

voting rights exercised beforehand up until the day prior to the day of the General Meeting

of Shareholders and those of shareholders who attended on the day whose indicati on of

approval or disapproval for each proposal had been able to be  verified, and the resolutions

of each proposal were passed pursuant to the Companies Act. Accordingly, the Company

discontinued counting the portion of voting rights for which approval, disapproval or

abstention had not yet been able to be verified.

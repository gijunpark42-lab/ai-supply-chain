Nuvoton Introduces NSP2340BA24E Smart Buzzer , Redefining the Buzzer Sound Experience

Beyond the Beep — Extending Conventional Buzzer Alerts to Voice & Melody for More Intuitive Audio Interaction in Smart Devices

Hsinchu, Taiwan– October 13 , 2026 – Nuvoton Technology introduces the NSP2340BA24E Smart Buzzer, integrating voice capabilities and buzzer driving functions into a single IC. Designed to extend the capabilities of conventional piezo buzzers beyond simple beep sounds, the NSP2340BA24E enables direct playback of voice and sound effects, bringing more intuitive and distinctive audio interaction to home appliances, security systems, healthcare devices, industrial equipment, and other smart applications.

Conventional buzzers have long been widely used in electronic products for audible alerts and operation feedback due to their simple architecture and proven reliability. However, as smart devices become increasingly sophisticated, simple beep patterns alone may not provide the intuitive and informative user experience required by modern applications.

Built around the concept of “Beyond the Beep,” the NSP2340BA24E expands the role of the conventional buzzer by enabling richer and more expressive audio feedback.

From Simple Beeps to Voice & Melody

The NSP2340BA24E Smart Buzzer integrates embedded Flash and a buzzer driver, together with a voltage booster and Class-AB amplifier, enabling direct drive of a piezo buzzer for voice and sound-effect playback. This transforms the buzzer from a simple audible alert component into a more informative audio interface.

With voice and diverse audio content, products can communicate operating status, notifications, and warning messages more clearly. Instead of relying solely on different beep patterns to indicate different conditions, the NSP2340BA24E enables more recognizable voice or sound feedback, helping users understand device status more intuitively. This approach allows developers to build upon familiar piezo buzzer designs while creating greater product differentiation and a more engaging audio experience.

Highly Integrated Design for Easier Implementation

The NSP2340BA24E integrates audio content storage, voltage boosting, and buzzer driving capabilities into a highly integrated solution. The NSP2340BA24E supports booster output of up to 24V, along with UART, I²C, and Two-Wire interfaces for host MCU control. It also supports In-System Programming (ISP), allowing audio content to be updated through the host MCU.

With this integrated architecture, developers can more easily introduce Voice & Melody capabilities into products traditionally designed around buzzers, while maintaining flexibility to customize audio content for different products and application requirements.

Expanding the Value of Buzzers Across Smart Applications

The NSP2340BA24E can be applied across a broad range of products requiring audible feedback and status notification. From operation and completion notifications in home appliances to warning alerts in security systems and status feedback in industrial equipment, the NSP2340BA24E allows sound to do more than simply attract attention—it can help users better understand what a device is communicating.

With the NSP2340BA24E Smart Buzzer , Nuvoton extends the conventional piezo buzzer beyond simple beep sounds to richer Voice & Melody experiences, providing developers with greater flexibility in audio design and enabling more intuitive audio interaction for the next generation of smart devices.

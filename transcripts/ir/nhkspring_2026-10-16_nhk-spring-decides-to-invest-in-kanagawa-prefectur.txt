NHK SPRING Decides to Invest in Kanagawa Prefectureʼs “Green Bonds”

NHK SPRING CO., LTD. (Head Office: Yokohama; President & COO: Kazuhisa Uemura) is pleased to

announce its decision to invest in green bonds issued by Kanagawa Prefecture for the fifth consecutive

year.

Kanagawa Prefecture is a key business area for the NHK SPRING Group, with approximately 5,000

employees—about half of the Groupʼs employees in Japan—working at 26 sites in the Prefecture. In

addition to initiatives aimed at achieving a decarbonized society by 2050, the Prefecture is advancing

disaster prevention and mitigation measures for rivers, erosion control, forests, coastlines, roads, and

other infrastructure in preparation for increasingly severe and frequent natural disasters caused by

climate change, including torrential rain and typhoons.

Through this investment, NHK SPRING aims to support these initiatives and contribute to enhancing

the sustainability of the local community, thereby pursuing the continuous development of its business

together with the region.

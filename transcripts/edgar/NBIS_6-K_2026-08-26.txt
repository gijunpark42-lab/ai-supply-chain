## Exhibit EX-99.1 (tm2624077d1_ex99-1.htm)
EXHIBIT 99.1 Nebius Group N.V. announces results of its Annual General Meeting Amsterdam, the Netherlands — August 26, 2026 — Nebius Group N.V. (the “Company”; NASDAQ: NBIS), the AI cloud company, today announced that all resolutions proposed at the Annual General Meeting of the Company (the “AGM”), held on August 25, 2026, have been adopted. The total number of Class A shares eligible to vote at the AGM was 238,402,543, with a total of 238,402,543 voting rights; the total number of Class B shares eligible to vote at the AGM was 33,455,053, with a total of 334,550,530 voting rights. Each Class A share carries one vote; each Class B share carries ten votes. The Class A shares and Class B shares voted together as a single class on all matters at the AGM.

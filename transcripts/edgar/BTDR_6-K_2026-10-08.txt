US$100 MILLION SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM

The Company’s board of directors has authorized a new share repurchase program to repurchase up to US$100 million worth of its Class A ordinary shares (“Shares”), effective from October 8, 2026 through October 7, 2028. The Company has fully utilized the US$40 million capacity under its previous share repurchase program adopted in May 2025.

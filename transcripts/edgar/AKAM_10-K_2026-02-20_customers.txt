Credit Risk Concentrations of credit risk with respect to accounts receivable are limited to certain customers to which we make substantial sales. Our customer base consists of a large number of geographically dispersed customers diversified across numerous industries. We believe that our accounts receivable credit risk exposure is limited. As of December 31, 2025, there was one customer with an accounts receivable balance greater than 10% of total accounts receivable. As of December 31, 2024, no customer had an accounts receivable balance greater than 10% of total accounts receivable. We believe that at December 31, 2025, the concentration of credit risk related to accounts receivable was insignificant.

Concentrations of credit risk with respect to accounts receivable are primarily limited to certain customers to which the Company makes substantial sales. The Company’s customer base consists of a large number of geographically-dispersed customers diversified across several industries. To reduce risk, the Company routinely assesses the financial strength of its customers. Based on such assessments, the Company believes that its accounts receivable credit risk exposure is limited. The Company believes that, as of December 31, 2025 and 2024, its concentration of credit risk related to accounts receivable was not significant.

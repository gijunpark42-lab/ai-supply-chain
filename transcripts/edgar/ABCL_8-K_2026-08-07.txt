About ABCL635 ABCL635 is a potential rst-in-class antibody drug for the non-hormonal treatment of moderate-to-severe VMS, commonly known as hot ashes, due to menopause. ABCL635 specically targets NK3R, a clinically validated G protein-coupled receptor (GPCR) expressed on KNDy neurons in the infundibular nucleus of the hypothalamus. 1 [image: abcellera-toxannouncexto002.jpg — not text] ABCL635 is the rst program from AbCellera’s GPCR and ion channel platform to advance into the pipeline, entering the clinic in July 2025. Additional details are available at www.abcellera.com/pipeline. The Phase 2 trial of ABCL635 (NCT07118891) is a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial with approximately 80 postmenopausal women designed to evaluate the ecacy of ABCL635 in reducing the frequency and severity of moderate-to-severe VMS.

Space Exploration Technologies Corp. and its wholly owned subsidiaries, collectively referred to as the “Company” or “SpaceX,” operate three segments – (i) the Space segment designs, manufactures, and launches reusable rockets to provide high cadence, reliable, and affordable access to space at unprecedented scale, (ii) the Connectivity segment operates a worldwide high-speed, low-latency broadband network powered by thousands of Starlink satellites in Low-Earth Orbit, delivering connectivity to millions of consumer, enterprise, and government customers through our Starlink offering, and (iii) the Super Intelligence (“SI”) segment (formerly known as the AI segment) operates a vertically integrated SI platform spanning SI infrastructure solutions for enterprise customers, as well as a frontier LLM Grok, SI-assisted software engineering platform Cursor, and real-time information, entertainment, and free speech platform X for consumer and enterprise customers.

On June 16, 2026, the Company entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger ("Cursor Merger Agreement") with Anysphere, Inc. ("Cursor") and X67 Inc., a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company ("Merger Sub"). On August 14, 2026 ("Closing Date"), the Company completed the merger with Cursor ("Cursor Merger"). Pursuant to the Cursor Merger Agreement, Merger Sub merged with and into Cursor, with Cursor surviving the merger as a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company. At the Closing Date, each share of Cursor's common stock and preferred stock outstanding immediately prior to the Closing Date was automatically converted into the right to receive shares of the Company's Class A common stock, plus cash in lieu of fractional shares. The conversion was based on an implied equity value of Cursor of $60 billion and the volume-weighted average closing price (“VWAP”) of the Company's Class A common stock over the seven consecutive trading days immediately preceding the closing of the Cursor Merger (“Exchange Ratio”). The results of operations of Cursor are included in the Company’s consolidated financial statements from the Closing Date and are reported within the SI segment. See Note 20 - Acquisitions for additional details.

The Company’s backlog represents the transaction price of performance obligations to customers for which work remains to be performed. The amount of backlog increases with new contracts or additions to existing contracts and decreases as revenue is recognized on existing contracts. Contracts are included in backlog when an enforceable agreement has been reached. Backlog does not include amounts related to performance obligations that are billed and recognized as they are delivered, optional purchases that do not represent material rights and any estimated amounts of variable consideration that are subject to constraint. Backlog totaled $62,959 million as of September 30, 2026, of which $17,520 million was recognized as deferred revenue at September 30, 2026. Approximately 67% is expected to be recognized within one year, and approximately 27% is expected to be recognized between one and three years, with the remaining 6% to be recognized thereafter.

Principal Repayments. On March 2, 2026, the Company repaid the full outstanding principal balance and accrued interest, including a prepayment penalty of $425 million, resulting in the extinguishment of the X B-1 Term Loan and X B-3 Term Loan. The X B-1 Term Loan was repayable at any time, in whole or in part, without premium or penalty, subject to mandatory quarterly prepayments of principal beginning on the last day of the fiscal quarter ended March 31, 2023, in amounts equal to 0.25% of the original principal amount of borrowings thereunder, with the unpaid balance being payable on the final maturity date thereof. The X B-1 Term Loan was also subject to additional customary mandatory prepayment provisions from the proceeds of certain debt issuances and asset sales, as well as sweeps of a portion of excess cash flow, subject to certain leverage-based step-downs and exceptions. The X B-3 Term Loan had prepayment penalties of 107.13% of the outstanding principal before October 27, 2026, 104.75% of the outstanding principal before October 27, 2027, and 102.38% of the outstanding principal before October 27, 2028.

Principal Repayments. Principal Repayments. In April 2026, the Company entered into an option agreement (“Cursor Option Agreement”) providing the right, but not the obligation, to acquire Cursor. In June 2026, the Company exercised the call option and entered into the Cursor Merger Agreement to acquire Cursor. On August 14, 2026, the Cursor Merger was completed. The Cursor Merger is an extension of the Company’s strategy to vertically integrate compute infrastructure, SI models, and applications.

Concurrently with the Cursor Option Agreement, the Company entered into a compute agreement (“Cursor Compute Agreement”) where the Company would provide Cursor with certain GPU cluster compute capacity and the parties would collaborate to improve existing models, including Grok, and to jointly develop SI models and related model-specific deliverables or products. The Cursor Compute Agreement was terminated at the Closing Date of the Cursor Merger. The net amount attributable to this collaboration arrangement through the Closing Date was not material.

Pursuant to the Cursor Merger Agreement, each share of Cursor's common stock and preferred stock outstanding immediately prior to the Closing Date was automatically converted into the right to receive shares of the Company's Class A common stock, plus cash in lieu of fractional shares, based on the Exchange Ratio of 9.7142. The Exchange Ratio was calculated based on an implied equity value of Cursor of $60 billion and VWAP of the Company's Class A common stock over the seven consecutive trading days immediately preceding the closing of the Cursor Merger of $128.65.

As part of the Cursor Merger Agreement, certain outstanding shares of Cursor common stock were converted to SpaceX equity awards with time-based vesting conditions in order to retain key talent, which are considered share-based compensation rather than purchase consideration under ASC 805, Business Combinations (“ASC 805”). In addition, any outstanding, unvested equity awards held by Cursor employees prior to the Cursor Merger were assumed and converted into equivalent SpaceX awards based on the Exchange Ratio. Refer to Note 14 - Share-based Compensation for additional details. The total value of these equity awards attributable to post-acquisition service as of the Closing Date is $23,376 million, subject to future forfeitures.

TSMC July 2026 Revenue Report

HSINCHU, Taiwan, R.O.C. – August 10, 2026 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenue for July 2026: On a consolidated basis, revenue for July 2026 was approximately NT$467.58 billion, an increase of 5.6 percent from June 2026 and an increase of 44.7 percent from July 2025. Revenue for January through July 2026 totaled NT$2,872.06 billion, an increase of 37.0 percent compared to the same period in 2025.

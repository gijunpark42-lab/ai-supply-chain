## Item 1.01
Item 1.01 | Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement. | 



 On October 9, 2026 (the “Bridge Closing Date”), Flex Ltd., a company organized under the laws of Singapore (the “Company” or “Flex”) entered into a Bridge Credit Agreement (the “Bridge Credit Agreement”), by and among the Company, as borrower, the lenders party thereto and Citibank, N.A., as administrative agent, which provides a senior bridge term loan credit facility (the “Bridge Credit Facility”) in an aggregate committed amount of $1.1 billion. In connection with the consummation of the Transaction (as defined below), on October 14, 2026, the Company borrowed $1.1 billion under the Bridge Credit Facility in a single advance. The Bridge Credit Facility will mature on October 13, 2027, the date that is 364 days after the date on which it was funded. 

 Loans under the Bridge Credit Agreement bear interest at a floating rate, which can be, at the Company’s option, either (i) Term SOFR (as defined in the Bridge Credit Agreement) plus an applicable margin or (ii) the Base Rate (as defined in the Bridge Credit Agreement) plus an applicable margin, in each case, with such margin determined based on the Company’s senior unsecured long-term debt ratings. 

 The Bridge Credit Agreement contains various customary covenants, including, but not limited to, restrictions on the ability of the Company and its subsidiaries to incur indebtedness, grant liens, dispose of material assets, merge or consolidate with or into other companies, materially change the nature of their business and make certain accounting changes, in each case, subject to various exceptions. The Bridge Credit Agreement requires the maintenance of (i) a Debt/EBITDA Ratio (as defined in the Bridge Credit Agreement) not to exceed 4.50 to 1.00 as of the last day of any fiscal quarter of the Company and (ii) an Interest Coverage Ratio (as defined in the Bridge Credit Agreement) of not less than 3.00 to 1.00 as of the last day of any fiscal quarter of the Company. 

 The Bridge Credit Agreement also includes various customary events of default. Upon an event of default, commitments under the Bridge Credit Agreement may be terminated and outstanding borrowings may be accelerated. 

 The obligations under the Bridge Credit Agreement are not guaranteed by any subsidiary of the Company. The Company may, at any time after the Bridge Closing Date and upon prior written notice to the administrative agent, cause any of its subsidiaries to become a subsidiary guarantor. The information set forth in Item 2.01 and Item 2.03 of this Current Report on Form 8-K regarding the use of proceeds under the Bridge Credit Facility is hereby incorporated by reference into this Item 1.01. 

 A copy of the Bridge Credit Agreement is filed with this Current Report on Form 8-K as Exhibit 10.1 and is incorporated by reference into this Item 1.01 as though fully set forth herein. The foregoing summary description of the Bridge Credit Agreement is not intended to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by the complete text of the Bridge Credit Agreement.

EX-99.1 Exhibit 99.1 Flex Completes Acquisition of EPC Power, Expanding Power Conversion Capabilities for AI Data Centers and Grid Applications Acquisition strengthens Flex’s Cloud and Power Infrastructure portfolio with differentiated grid-forming and power conversion technology AUSTIN, Texas, October 14, 2026 /PRNewswire/ — Flex (NASDAQ: FLEX) today announced that it has completed its acquisition of EPC Power, a leading provider of intelligent power conversion solutions for data center and grid applications. EPC Power is now part of Flex’s Cloud and Power Infrastructure segment, expanding the company’s capabilities across power, cooling and compute to address growing demand for higher-density AI infrastructure and next-generation 800V data center power architectures. “We are pleased to welcome the EPC Power team to Flex,” said Revathi Advaithi, Chief Executive Officer of Flex. “EPC Power adds differentiated power conversion and grid-forming technology to our capabilities across power, cooling and compute. Together, we can help customers address the growing power demands of AI infrastructure with more efficient, resilient and integrated solutions.” EPC Power combines proprietary hardware, software and controls with U.S.-based engineering and manufacturing. With more than 15 GW deployed, its technology supports grid stabilization and backup power, and its platform is engineered to support next generation 800V data center power architectures. Flex plans to separate its Cloud and Power Infrastructure segment into an independent publicly traded company, Axiom Solutions International, Inc., in the first calendar quarter of 2027. About Flex Flex (Reg. No. 199002645H) is the manufacturing partner of choice that helps leading brands design, build, and manage products that improve the world. With a global footprint spanning 30 countries, Flex delivers advanced manufacturing and supply chain solutions, innovative products and technology, and lifecycle services that support customers from concept to scale. In the AI era, Flex is helping customers accelerate data center deployment by solving power, heat, and scale challenges through cutting-edge power and cooling technology and scalable IT infrastructure solutions. For information about Flex’s intent to spin off its Cloud and Power Infrastructure portfolio, visit: https://flex.com/transaction-resources.

## Item 7.01
| Item 7.01. | Regulation FD Disclosure. | 



 

 SolarEdge Technologies, Inc. (Nasdaq: SEDG), a global leader in smart energy technology, is webcasting its 2026 Investor Day today, Thursday, September 10, 2026. Management will share details about the Company’s strategy, market trends, product and technology roadmap as well as operational and financial plans.

 

 A live webcast will begin today at 10:00 AM ET and can be accessed in the Investor Relations section of SolarEdge’s website at: https://investors.solaredge.com/events. Presentations will conclude at approximately 12:30 PM ET. Following the conclusion of the event, the Investor Day presentation materials will be posted on the same page of SolarEdge’s Investor Relations website at https://investors.solaredge.com/events. A replay of the webcast will also be available approximately two hours after the conclusion of the event and will remain available for approximately 30 calendar days.

Exhibit 99.1 [image: ex99-1_001.jpg — not text] ChronoScale Reports Fiscal First Quarter 2027 Results MENLO PARK, Calif. – October 15, 2026 — ChronoScale Holdings Corporation (NASDAQ: CHRN) (“ChronoScale” or the “Company”), an accelerated compute platform purpose-built to support demanding artificial intelligence workloads, reported financial results for the fiscal first quarter ended August 31, 2026. Following the business combination completed on May 5, 2026, the Company classified its legacy Ekso Bionics business (“Legacy Ekso Business”) as held for sale and reported its results in discontinued operations in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”). Accordingly, Legacy Ekso Business’ results are excluded from the Company’s continuing operations and presented separately in its consolidated financial statements. Subsequent to quarter end, the Company completed the sale of the Legacy Ekso Business on September 30, 2026. Unless otherwise specified, disclosures in this earnings release, including the below, reflect continuing operations only. With our customer agreements and current deployment schedule supporting an expected $1 billion in annualized revenue run rate by calendar Q3 2027, our priority is disciplined execution and delivering the infrastructure our customers need at scale.” Financial Results from Operations for Fiscal First Quarter 2027 Revenue in the fiscal first quarter of 2027 was $41.5 million, up 148% from $16.7 million in the fiscal first quarter of 2026. The increase was primarily attributable to revenue generated from a new customer during the three months ended August 31, 2026, including revenue recognized from the sale of AI infrastructure hardware and related support and maintenance services pursuant to resale arrangements with the customer. Cost of revenues were $45.6 million for the three months ended August 31, 2026, up 91% from $23.9 million in the prior-year period. The increase was driven primarily by higher AI infrastructure hardware purchases under resale arrangements with a customer, along with higher depreciation and amortization from additional in-service compute equipment and leases, higher energy costs from increased rates and usage, and higher personnel expenses. These increases were partially offset by lower lease and lease-related expenses resulting from prior-period lease renegotiations. Selling, general and administrative expense increased approximately $20.4 million, to $20.7 million for the three months ended August 31, 2026, from $0.4 million in the prior-year period. The increase was driven primarily by higher stock-based compensation from restricted stock unit and restricted stock award grants to eligible employees and non-employee directors, respectively during the period, higher professional service expenses for legal services related to discrete transactions and projects, and higher personnel expenses from increased headcount. Lease and lease-related expenses not directly attributable to generating revenue also increased, as did other general operating costs, including filing fees, insurance, and supplies. Net loss from continuing operations for the fiscal first quarter 2027 was $26.4 million, or $0.18 per basic and diluted share. This compares to a net loss from continuing operations of $11.2 million, or $0.08 per basic and diluted share for the fiscal first quarter of 2026. *Annualized run-rate revenue is an operating metric calculated by multiplying revenue expected to be recognized under the Company’s customer contracts in a quarterly period by four.

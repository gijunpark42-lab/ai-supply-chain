Item 2.02. Results of Operations and Financial Condition.

Document EXHIBIT 99.1 [image: elementlogo_regxtm-01002a.jpg — not text] Element Solutions Inc Reports Record Results for Third Quarter 2026 •Net sales of $995 million, representing 52% growth on a reported basis and 13% organic growth from the third quarter of 2025 •Reported net income of $66 million, compared to $39 million in the same period last year •Adjusted EBITDA of $191 million, representing 29% constant currency growth •Third quarter 2026 Cash flows from operating activities of $159 million and free cash flow of $135 million •Increasing 2026 Adjusted EBITDA guidance to approximately $720 million Miami, Fla., October 21, 2026 -- Element Solutions Inc (NYSE:ESI) (“Element Solutions” or the “Company”), a global and diversified specialty chemicals technology company, today announced its financial results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026. Executive Commentary Chief Executive Officer Benjamin Gliklich commented, “Our team continued to execute exceptionally well and delivered another quarter of record results. We are translating the tailwinds in the Electronics market to profitable growth, and seizing the opportunities created by the pace of technological change to gain market share. Demand across AI infrastructure and other high-performance electronics applications drove broad-based double-digit organic growth in each of our Electronics verticals. This growth is the product of several years of focused investment in technology, customer engagement, capacity expansion and operational excellence, which have strengthened our competitive position in the fastest-growing and highest-value areas of the electronics ecosystem. As our customers continue to build capacity and our supply chain continues to bring forward advanced technologies, we are investing alongside them. We are accelerating our own innovation, adding capacity in high-growth product lines and deepening strategic supply chain relationships to continue delivering for our customers and shareholders." Mr Gliklich continued, "I am particularly proud of this quarter's results considering the risk of distraction from the proposed and subsequently terminated Solstice transaction. Our team did not miss a step. Reinforced by yet another consecutive record quarter, we are moving forward with greater focus, urgency and confidence. Our standalone, independent trajectory has only improved, and, as a result, we are raising our 2026 adjusted EBITDA guidance to approximately $720 million. This should translate to adjusted EPS growth in excess of 20% for the full year, driven by sustained momentum in Electronics and meaningful contributions from our recent acquisitions. Our formula of operational excellence and prudent capital allocation continues to deliver.” Third Quarter 2026 Highlights (compared with third quarter 2025) •Net sales on a reported basis for the third quarter of 2026 were $995 million, an increase of 52% over the third quarter of 2025. Organic net sales increased 13%. ◦Electronics: Net sales increased 68% to $780 million, including 30% from acquisitions. Organic net sales increased 18%. ◦Specialties: Net sales increased 13% to $216 million, including 12% from acquisitions net of divestitures. Organic net sales increased 1%. •Third quarter of 2026 earnings per share (EPS) performance: ◦GAAP diluted EPS was $0.27, as compared to $0.16 for the same period last year. ◦Adjusted EPS was $0.50, as compared to $0.41 for the same period last year. •Reported net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $66 million, as compared to $39 million for the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 67%. ◦Net income margin increased by 60 basis points to 6.6%. •Adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter of 2026 was $191 million, as compared to $147 million for the third quarter of 2025. On a constant currency basis, adjusted EBITDA increased 29%. ◦Electronics: Adjusted EBITDA was $148 million, an increase of 46%. On a constant currency basis, adjusted EBITDA increased 45%. The Micromax business had a positive impact of 19% on constant currency adjusted EBITDA growth. ◦Specialties: Adjusted EBITDA was $43 million, a decrease of 6%. On a constant currency basis, adjusted EBITDA decreased 6%. The EFC Gases & Advanced Materials business had a positive impact of 16% on constant currency adjusted EBITDA growth. ◦Adjusted EBITDA margin increased by 50 basis points to 28.2%. Updated 2026 Guidance The Company now expects full year 2026 adjusted EBITDA to be approximately $720 million, inclusive of full year contributions from the Micromax and EFC acquisitions and assuming stable exchange rates and metal prices, and an adjusted EPS growth rate of over 20% for the full year. Recent Developments Solstice Transaction - On July 6, 2026, the Company entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger (the “Merger Agreement”) with Solstice. On August 27, 2026, the Company and Solstice entered into a Termination Agreement pursuant to which the parties mutually agreed to terminate the Merger Agreement, which, subject to customary exceptions, mutually released the parties from any claims of liability to one another relating to the contemplated merger transaction. Constant Currency: The Company discloses net sales and adjusted EBITDA on a constant currency basis by adjusting results to exclude the impact of changes due to the translation of foreign currencies of its international locations into U.S. dollars. Management believes this non-GAAP financial information facilitates period-to-period comparison in the analysis of trends in business performance, thereby providing valuable supplemental information regarding its results of operations, consistent with how the Company internally evaluates its financial results. The impact of foreign currency translation is calculated by converting the Company's current-period local currency financial results into U.S. dollars using the prior period's exchange rates and comparing these adjusted amounts to its prior period reported results. The difference between actual growth rates and constant currency growth rates represents the estimated impact of foreign currency translation.

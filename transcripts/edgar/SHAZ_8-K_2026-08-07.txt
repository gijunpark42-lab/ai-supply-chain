## Item 2.02
Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.

 

 On August 6, 2026, the Company issued a press release and will hold a conference call regarding its financial results for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026. A copy of the press release is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 hereto and is incorporated herein by reference. The Company also posted its Second Quarter 2026 Results Presentation (the “Presentation”) to the “Investor Relations” section of its website at https://sharonai.com/investors/. A copy of the Presentation is furnished as Exhibit 99.2 hereto and is incorporated herein by reference.

 

 The information in this Item 2.02, including Exhibits 99.1 and 99.2, is being furnished and shall not be deemed “filed” for the purpose of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”), or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that section, nor shall such information be deemed incorporated by reference into any filing under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “Securities Act”), or the Exchange Act, except as expressly set forth by specific reference in such a filing.

## Exhibit EX-99.1 (hyft-ex99_1.htm)
EX-99.1 99.1 FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE MindWalk Holdings Corp. Announces Leadership Update Commercial Priorities Unchanged; LensAI Remains in Contracted, Recurring Deployments with Life Sciences Customers AUSTIN, Texas--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MindWalk Holdings Corp. ("MindWalk") (NASDAQ: HYFT), a Bio-Native AI company, today announced that Thomas Lynch, PhD, has departed the company and ceased to serve as Chief Business Officer, effective October 16, 2026. "We thank Thomas for his contributions to MindWalk and wish him well," said Jennifer Bath, PhD, Chief Executive Officer and President of MindWalk. "Our commercial priorities do not change. We have moved from building the technology to deploying it, and the work in front of the team is the same today as it was last week: delivering on the platform deployments already under contract and advancing the pharma and partner engagements in progress." MindWalk's commercial activity continues on plan. LensAI, the company's reasoning and application layer, is in contracted, recurring arrangements with life sciences customers today, running on ReefIQ, the biological data substrate powered by HYFT® Technology. The commercial and business development team continues to report to the Chief Executive Officer. About MindWalk Holdings Corp. MindWalk Holdings Corp. (NASDAQ: HYFT) is a Bio-Native AI company building the BioIntelligence infrastructure that life sciences AI and agentic AI require, integrating AI, data, and advanced wet lab capabilities into one connected discovery ecosystem. At its core is HYFT® Technology, a proprietary, function-aware representation of biology. Its HYFT fingerprints span sequence and structural biology and, refined over 20 years of curation, form a continuously evolving biological representation of 660 million patterns and 25 billion relationships. This enriched biological representation is the architecture behind ReefIQ, the biological data substrate that provides context for life sciences, enriching data at ingestion and growing more valuable with each program run on it, and LensAI, the reasoning and application layer for target discovery, candidate diligence, portfolio decision support, and the agentic AI workflows pharma is racing to deploy. By design, value compounds in this HYFT representation layer, not in any individual AI model that runs on top of it.

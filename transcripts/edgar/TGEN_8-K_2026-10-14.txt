## Item 8.01
Item 8.01. Other Events. 




On October 14, 2026, Tecogen Inc., a Delaware corporation (the “Company”), issued the attached press release regarding a customer testimonial from Cloudsmart, Inc., a Cloud and AI data center in Connecticut. The press release describes Cloudsmart’s use of the Company’s InVerde cogeneration system at Cloudsmart’s data center to reduce energy costs. The press release highlights Cloudsmart’s satisfaction with the service provided by the Company and the performance of the Company’s cogeneration equipment in a demanding data center environment. 




 The Company’s press release dated October 14, 2026, and Cloudsmart’s customer testimonial are attached hereto as Exhibits 99.1 and 99.2.

## Exhibit EX-99.1 (tecogenpressreleaserecus.htm)
tecogenpressreleaserecus [image: tecogenpressreleaserecus001.jpg — not text] On Wednesday, October 14, 2026 at 10:00AM ET Data Center Cuts Electric Bills Substantially with Tecogen's InVerde BILLERICA, MA / ACCESS Newswire / October 14, 2026 / Tecogen Inc. (NYSE American:TGEN) is pleased share a recent data center customer testimonial. Robyn Sullivan, CEO and President of Cloudsmart Inc, a Tier 3, SOC 2, Cloud and AI data center in Connecticut said, "For a few months now, we have been creating our own power with Tecogen Inc.'s InVerde and we're glad we did." "We cut our electric bills substantially, which gives us an edge against other local data centers. Whenever we give potential customers a tour, they are not only intrigued, but love that we can offer lower energy costs and greener power sources. Since starting it up, the unit has run reliably and has been hands-off as their service team has taken care of everything." Abinand Rangesh, CEO of Tecogen commented "We are thrilled that Cloudsmart chose our InVerde for this data center. As this positive testimonial shows, our products are not only generating tremendous savings but are also able to meet the demanding needs of a data center environment. Such real-world proof points are essential to convert our data center pipeline to orders." About Tecogen Tecogen designs, manufactures, sells, installs, and maintains high efficiency, ultra-clean, cogeneration products including engine- driven combined heat and power, air conditioning systems, and high-efficiency water heaters for residential, commercial, recreational and industrial use. The company provides cost effective, environmentally friendly and reliable products for energy production that nearly eliminate criteria pollutants and significantly reduce a customer's carbon footprint. In business for over 35 years, Tecogen has shipped more than 3,200 units, supported by an established network of engineering, sales, and service personnel in key markets in North America. For more information, please visit www.tecogen.com or contact us for a free Site Assessment.

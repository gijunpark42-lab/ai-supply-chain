CREDIT FACILITY

On October 17, 2026, Nebius Group N.V., a Dutch public company with limited liability (the “Company”), Nebius Inc., a Delaware limited liability company (the “US Borrower”), and Nebius B.V., a Dutch private limited liability company (the “Dutch Borrower” and together with the Company and US Borrower, the “Borrowers”), entered into a Bridge Facility Agreement (the “Facility Agreement”) with Citibank, N.A., as mandated lead arranger and lender and certain of its affiliates as agent and security agent. The Facility Agreement provides for a senior secured term loan facility in an aggregate principal amount of $3.5 billion for the purpose of financing the buildout of the Borrowers’ GPU infrastructure capacity and for other general corporate purposes.

Loans under the Facility Agreement bear interest at Term SOFR plus an applicable margin commencing at 2.75% per annum and increasing by 0.25% for each 90-day period after October 17, 2026 up to 3.50% per annum (subject to other adjustments). The Borrowers may voluntarily prepay loans under the Facility Agreement without premium or penalty. The maturity date of the Facility Agreement is October 16, 2027.

The Facility Agreement contains a minimum liquidity financial covenant, other affirmative and negative covenants subject to exceptions and customary events of default subject to grace periods and thresholds.

The obligations under the Facility Agreement are secured by a security interest on substantially all of each Borrower’s assets, including all of the shares of the US Borrower and Dutch Borrower held by the Company, subject to exceptions for excluded assets and permitted liens.

A press release announcing the signing of the Facility Agreement is attached as Exhibit 99.1.

## Exhibit EX-99.1 (tm2628078d1_ex99-1.htm)
Exhibit 99.1 Nebius secures $3.5 billion financing facility with Citi | ● | Marks next step in Nebius’s strategy to fund global capacity build-out on attractive terms | | ● | Advances diversified capital structure built on a disciplined balance sheet | | ● | Increases flexibility over timing and structure of longer-term financing to fuel growth in 2027 and beyond | Amsterdam, 22 October 2026 — Nebius Group N.V. (Nasdaq: NBIS), the AI cloud company, today announced that it has entered into a $3.5 billion senior secured facility provided by Citi. The facility provides access to capital at attractive rates to support Nebius’s global capacity build-out, and advances the company’s diversified and sequenced funding strategy. It complements a funding base that includes customer prepayments and operating cash flows with other financing mechanisms. This provides flexibility over the timing and structure of further external capital-raising as the company continues to execute on its global build-out plans. Ophir Nave, Chief Operating Officer of Nebius, said: “We continue to execute our funding strategy as planned, and are pleased to have secured this financing from a leading global financial institution. This is a result of our disciplined approach to managing our balance sheet to fund our portfolio of projects, and once again demonstrates our ability to choose when and how we raise capital to support our growth. “Sequencing our financing is how we optimize our long-term cost of capital. This facility provides additional financing to support our near-term capacity build-out. We still have a range of capital sources available to us to finance the next phase of growth, including asset-backed financing based on investment-grade customer commitments of more than $40 billion of long-term contracts.” The facility has a 364-day maturity and initially bears interest at Term SOFR plus 2.75% per annum, increasing up to a maximum of 3.50% per annum, and subject to other adjustments. The proceeds will be used for data center development and GPU procurement, as well as for general corporate purposes. Citi acted as mandated lead arranger, agent and security agent.

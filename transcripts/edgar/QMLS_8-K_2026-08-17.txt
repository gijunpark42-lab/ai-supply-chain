## Item 2.02
Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition. 

 

 QumulusAI, Inc. (the “Company”) expects to report cash as of June 30, 2026 of approximately $19.97 million, gross profit for the three months ended June 30, 2026 of approximately $4.47 million and gross profit for the six months ended June 30, 2026 of approximately $5.75 million. These amounts are preliminary and subject to completion of the Company’s financial closing procedures, including the completion of management’s reviews.

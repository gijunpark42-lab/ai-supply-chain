## Item 8.01
Item 8.01 Other Events 

 On September 12, 2026, Oracle issued a press release announcing that Larry Ellison, Executive Chair of the Board and Chief Technology Officer of Oracle Corporation (“Oracle”), has cancelled his 10b5-1 Plan to sell Oracle stock. No Oracle stock was sold under the plan prior to termination. A copy of the press release is attached as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.

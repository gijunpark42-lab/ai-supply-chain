## Exhibit EX-99.1 (a3q26e_withguidancexfinal.htm)
Document | | | [image: image_1.jpg — not text] | TSMC Reports Third Quarter EPS of NT$29.43 HSINCHU, Taiwan, R.O.C., Oct. 15, 2026 -- TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced consolidated revenue of NT$1,494.24 billion, net income of NT$763.06 billion, and diluted earnings per share of NT$29.43 (US$4.60 per ADR unit) for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. Year-over-year, third quarter revenue increased 50.9%, while net income and diluted EPS both increased 68.7%. Compared to second quarter 2026, third quarter results represented a 17.6% increase in revenue and an 8.0% increase in net income. All figures were prepared in accordance with TIFRS on a consolidated basis. In US dollars, third quarter revenue was $46.72 billion, which increased 41.2% year-over-year and increased 16.2% from the previous quarter. Gross margin for the quarter was 66.4%, operating margin was 58.7%, and net profit margin was 51.1%. In the third quarter, shipments of 2-nanometer accounted for 8% of total wafer revenue; 3-nanometer accounted for 33%; 5-nanometer accounted for 28%; and 7-nanometer accounted for 10%. Advanced technologies, defined as 7-nanometer and more advanced technologies, accounted for 79% of total wafer revenue. “Our business in the third quarter was supported by strong demand for our leading-edge process technologies,” said Wendell Huang, Senior VP and Chief Financial Officer of TSMC. “Moving into fourth quarter 2026, we expect our business to be supported by continued strong demand for our leading-edge process technologies, including the steep ramp-up of our 2-nanometer technology.” Based on the Company’s current business outlook, management expects the overall performance for fourth quarter 2026 to be as follows: •Revenue is expected to be between US$52.3 billion and US$54.3 billion; And, based on the exchange rate assumption of 1 US dollar to 31.8 NT dollars, •Gross profit margin is expected to be between 65% and 67%; •Operating profit margin is expected to be between 56.5% and 58.5%. | | | [image: image_1.jpg — not text] | TSMC’s 2026 third quarter consolidated results: (Unit: NT$ million, except for EPS) | | | | | | | | | | | | | | | | | | | 3Q26 Amounta | 3Q25 Amount | YoY Inc.

Non - GAAP Financial Measures This presentation includes expected Project NOI and expected Project NOI margin for the TensorWave phase one lease, which are non - GAAP financial measures. The Company defines expected Project NOI as expected fixed lease payments under the lease (base rent and fixed capacity charges, including fixed contractu al escalators) less expected non - reimbursable operating expenses of the data center and the power generation assets that serve it, including operations and maintenance, long - term service agreement costs, insurance, property taxes, ground rent, an allocation of campus common area costs and an operating cost contingency. Expected Project NOI margin is expected Project NOI divided by ex pec ted fixed lease payments. These measures exclude variable power charges passed through to the tenant and the associated variable power costs, and exclude depreciation and amortization , s elling, general and administrative expenses, interest expense and capital expenditures. The Company believes these measures are useful to investors in evaluating the expected operating perfor man ce of the lease, and management uses them for that purpose. They have limitations as analytical tools and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for measures prepared in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”). Operating income is the GAAP measure most directly comparable to expected Project NOI. The Company has not provided a reconciliation of these measures to operating income because the facility has not commenced operations and the Company cannot predict without unreasonable effort certain items re qui red to calculate operating income, principally depreciation, which depends on final capitalized project costs and placed - in - service dates, and the allocation of selling, general and adminis trative expenses. These items could be significant. Other companies, including real estate investment trusts, may calculate similarly titled measures differently.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures 

Adjusted gross margin, adjusted operating margin, adjusted gross margin, excluding business held for sale, adjusted diluted earnings per share, adjusted normalized tax rate and free cashflow as a percentage of net sales are non-GAAP financial measures. They supplement, and should not be considered a substitute for, measures prepared in accordance with GAAP, and they may not be comparable to similarly titled measures used by other companies. Definitions of these measures, the items they exclude, and reconciliations to the most comparable GAAP measures are included in Semtech's Earnings Release on Form 8-K for the second quarter of fiscal year 2027 filed with the SEC on August 25, 2026. 




The Company is unable to include a reconciliation of forward-looking non-GAAP results to the corresponding GAAP measures as they are not available without unreasonable efforts due to the high variability and low visibility with respect to the impact of transaction, integration and restructuring expenses, share-based awards, amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets and other items that are excluded from these non-GAAP measures. The Company expects the variability of the above charges to have a potentially significant impact on its GAAP financial results.

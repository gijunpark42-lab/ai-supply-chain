Information regarding the interim consolidated statement of income and comprehensive income
*Impairment losses
During the current interim consolidated accounting period, the Group recognized
impairment losses on the following assets.
The Group groups its assets by affiliated company, treating each as the smallest unit that generates cash flow.
In addition, idle assets and assets scheduled for disposal are grouped individually.
DISCO HI-TEC EUROPE GmbH, a consolidated subsidiary of the Company,
decided to demolish existing buildings and construct a new building.
Consequently, the carrying amount of the buildings scheduled for demolition was reduced
to the recoverable amount, and the resulting decrease was recognized as an impairment loss.
The recoverable amount was measured based on value in use.
However, since no future cash flow is expected, the recoverable amount was determined to be zero.

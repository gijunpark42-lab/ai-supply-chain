Basel, 22 October 2026
Roche continues strong momentum in the third quarter with sales
growth of +7% at constant exchange rates (CER) and +8% in CHF;
Group sales increase +6% (CER), +1% in CHF, in the first nine months
of 2026
● Group sales were +6% at constant exchange rates (CER)1, +1% when reported in CHF
and +7% in USD2, in the first nine months of 2026, driven by high demand for our
innovative medicines and diagnostics. In the third quarter, sales grew +7% (CER) and
+8% in CHF (+7% in USD), as exchange rates stabilised.
● Pharmaceuticals Division sales were +7% at CER, +2% when reported in CHF and +8%
in USD, in the first nine months, due to continued high demand for medicines to treat
severe diseases; Xolair (chronic hives, food allergies), Hemlibra (haemophilia A),
Ocrevus (multiple sclerosis), Phesgo (breast cancer) and Vabysmo (severe eye
diseases) were the top growth drivers.
● Diagnostics Division sales were +4% at CER, stable when reported in CHF and +6% in
USD, in the first nine months, as demand for core lab and pathology solutions more
than offset the impact of healthcare pricing reforms in China.
● Highlights:
○ US approval for Tecentriq for the adjuvant treatment of stage-III DNA
mismatch repair colon cancer, Gazyva/Gazyvaro for reducing the risk of
relapse in patients 2 years of age and older with frequently relapsing or
steroid-dependent childhood-onset idiopathic nephrotic syndrome and
Enspryng in thyroid eye disease
○ US Priority Reviews for fenebrutinib in both relapsing and primary progressive
multiple sclerosis and Enspryng in MOGAD, an autoimmune disease;
vamikibart filing acceptance in uveitic macular edema, a leading cause of
vision loss
○ EU approval for Susvimo in neovascular age-related macular degeneration
(nAMD), a severe eye disease, and Ocrevus in paediatric relapsing multiple
sclerosis; EMA validation of application for Enspryng in MOGAD in Europe

Group sales
In the first nine months of 2026, Roche sales were +6% at CER, +1% when reported in CHF, to
CHF 46.5 billion due to strong demand for pharmaceutical products and diagnostic solutions.
The appreciation of the Swiss franc against most currencies, notably the US dollar, had a
significant impact on the results reported in Swiss francs compared to constant exchange
rates. This currency impact reversed in the third quarter.
Sales growth in the Pharmaceuticals Division was +7% at CER, +2% when reported in CHF, to
CHF 36.2 billion, with medicines for severe diseases continuing their strong growth.
The top five growth drivers – Xolair, Hemlibra, Ocrevus, Phesgo and Vabysmo – achieved total
sales of CHF 16.8 billion, an increase of +12% at CER, or +6% in CHF, compared to the first
nine months of 2025.
Sales of products with expired patents (Avastin, Herceptin, MabThera/Rituxan, Lucentis and
Actemra/RoActemra) decreased by a combined CHF 0.4 billion at CER, or CHF 0.6 billion in
CHF.

In the United States, sales were +5% at CER, stable when reported in CHF, due to continued
growth of Xolair, Hemlibra, Ocrevus, Polivy (blood cancer) and Tecentriq (cancer
immunotherapy). This growth more than compensated for the lower sales of Kadcyla (breast
cancer) and Perjeta (breast cancer).
Sales in Europe were +2% at CER, stable when reported in CHF, as the growing demand for
Ocrevus, Phesgo and Evrysdi (spinal muscular atrophy) more than offset the impact of
biosimilar competition on Actemra/RoActemra and the conversion to Phesgo on Perjeta.
In Japan, sales were +25% at CER, +10% when reported in CHF, mainly due to product supply
to third parties and continued growth of Lunsumio (blood cancer), Hemlibra, Polivy, Elevidys
(Duchenne muscular dystrophy) and Vabysmo. There was also a positive impact of timing
effects related to the implementation of a new enterprise resource planning (ERP) system.
Sales in the International region were +10% at CER, +7% when reported in CHF, led by higher
sales of Phesgo, Vabysmo, Hemlibra, Ocrevus and Evrysdi. In China, sales were stable at CER
and when reported in CHF, driven by continued growth of Phesgo, Vabysmo, Alecensa and
Polivy. The increase was partially offset by lower sales of Perjeta due to the conversion to
Phesgo and Xofluza due to a milder flu season in 2025.
The Diagnostics Division’s sales were +4% at CER, stable when reported in CHF, to CHF 10.3
billion as growth in demand for core lab and pathology solutions more than offset the impact
of healthcare pricing reforms in China.
Sales in the Europe, Middle East and Africa (EMEA) region were +3% at CER, unchanged
when reported in CHF, driven by higher sales of clinical chemistry and immunodiagnostic
products. In North America, sales were +9% at CER, +3% when reported in CHF, with growth
across the Core Lab, Pathology Lab and Molecular Lab customer areas. Sales in Asia-Pacific
were -4% at CER, or -8% when reported in CHF, due to the healthcare pricing reforms in China.
In Latin America, sales growth was +12% at CER, or +11% when reported in CHF.

Pharmaceuticals: key developments
Compound Milestone
Regulatory
Enspryng
Thyroid eye disease
FDA approves Enspryng, a first-in-class IL-6R inhibitor, as the first and only
at-home subcutaneous treatment option for thyroid eye disease (TED)
● FDA approval based on phase III SatraGO-1 and -2 studies, with SatraGO-2
demonstrating a significant reduction in eye bulging (proptosis) and
improvement in diplopia (double vision), with a favourable safety profile,
regardless of TED activity or duration.
● Enspryng (satralizumab) represents an important advance for people with
TED, offering the first at-home subcutaneous treatment option every four
weeks during maintenance phase, allowing for greater flexibility in how
treatment is administered.
● Enspryng is an anti-IL-6R monoclonal antibody using novel recycling
antibody technology designed to inhibit IL-6 signalling upstream of
autoantibody-driven pathology.
● TED is a rare autoimmune disease affecting approximately 155 out of every
100,000 people in the US that can lead to facial disfigurement and vision-
threatening complications if left untreated.
Tecentriq
Different types of
cancer
FDA approves Tecentriq in combination with a fluoropyrimidine and
oxaliplatin for the adjuvant treatment of a certain type of stage III colon
cancer
● The approval is based on results from the landmark phase III ATOMIC study,
published in The New England Journal of Medicine, which showed
Tecentriq plus chemotherapy reduced the risk of disease recurrence or
death by 50% compared to chemotherapy alone.
● It is the first approved adjuvant immunotherapy regimen for stage III dMMR
colon cancer, positioning Tecentriq as a new post-surgery standard of
care.
● Nearly 25% of all colon cancers are diagnosed at stage III, and almost one
in three patients with stage III colon cancer experience a recurrence within
five years.
● Approval marks the twelfth indication for Tecentriq in the US.
Fenebrutinib
Multiple sclerosis
Fenebrutinib is the first BTK inhibitor to receive US FDA filing acceptance in
both relapsing and primary progressive multiple sclerosis
● Fenebrutinib cut relapses by 51% and 58% vs teriflunomide in phase III RMS
trials (FENhance 1 and 2) while showing a consistent, positive trend in
delaying disability.
● Fenebrutinib became the first investigational medicine in more than a
decade to slow disability progression in a phase III PPMS trial (FENtrepid),
with numerical benefit vs Ocrevus.
● Fenebrutinib is designed to address two drivers of MS – acute inflammation
causing relapses and chronic brain inflammation driving disability
progression.

● If approved, fenebrutinib would become the first BTK inhibitor and first
high-efficacy oral treatment for both RMS and PPMS, offering a new option
for the nearly 1 million people living with MS in the US.
Susvimo
Severe eye diseases
European Commission approves Susvimo for the treatment of neovascular
age-related macular degeneration
● By continuously delivering medicine into the eye through the Contivue
refillable implant, Susvimo is the first and only continuous delivery
treatment that offers an alternative to standard-of-care eye injections for
nAMD.
● Susvimo helps people with nAMD maintain their vision with as few as two
treatments per year.
● Data from the phase III Archway study showed that Susvimo maintained
vision equivalent to monthly intravitreal ranibizumab injections.
● nAMD is a leading cause of vision loss in adults over 60 and affects
approximately 1.7 million people across the European Union.
Enspryng
MOGAD
FDA grants Priority Review for Enspryng for MOGAD, an autoimmune
disease with no approved treatments
● If approved, Enspryng would be the first and only disease-modifying
therapy for MOGAD (myelin oligodendrocyte glycoprotein antibody-
associated disease).
● The filing acceptance is based on the phase III METEOROID study showing
that Enspryng reduced the risk of relapses by 68%.
● The European Medicines Agency (EMA) has validated the application for
Enspryng for MOGAD in Europe, with a decision expected in Q3 2027.
Phase III, pivotal and other key read-outs
Sefaxersen
IgA nephropathy
Positive interim data shows sefaxersen significantly reduces proteinuria in
people with IgA nephropathy (IgAN)
● In the phase III IMAgINATION study, sefaxersen demonstrated statistically
significant and clinically meaningful reductions in proteinuria versus
placebo at 37 weeks, showing best-in-class potential.
● Sefaxersen inhibits complement factor B production at its primary source
in the liver, thus blocking a key driver of IgAN-related kidney damage.
● Interim data will be presented at an upcoming medical meeting and shared
with health authorities.
Enicepatide
Obesity
Roche announces positive phase II results for dual GLP-1/GIP receptor
agonist enicepatide in people living with type 2 diabetes and overweight or
obesity
● At the highest dose (24 mg), enicepatide achieved a mean HbA1c reduction
of 2.65% at 48 weeks, highlighting its potential for best-in-disease
glycaemic control.
● By week 48, 90% of patients in the 24 mg enicepatide arm met the type-2-
diabetes glycaemic target of ≤6.5% HbA1c, while 62% achieved
normoglycaemia (HbA1c <5.7%).
● At the highest 24 mg dose, enicepatide achieved a mean weight loss of
15.5% at 48 weeks, without a weight loss plateau.

Lunsumio
Blood cancer
Regimen based on Lunsumio significantly improves progression-free
survival in follicular lymphoma in phase III CELESTIMO study
● Phase III CELESTIMO study met its primary endpoint at the interim analysis
demonstrating a statistically significant and clinically meaningful
reduction in the risk of disease progression in people with follicular
lymphoma who have received at least one prior line of treatment.
● Data reinforce the potential of this regimen based on Lunsumio to improve
clinical outcomes, while offering a tolerable safety profile, a simple dosing
schedule and outpatient administration.
● CELESTIMO is the confirmatory study required to convert the accelerated
approval / conditional marketing authorisation of Lunsumio monotherapy
for third-line or later (3L+) follicular lymphoma to full approval, as well as to
secure an indication in second-line or later (2L+) follicular lymphoma.
● Data will be submitted to health authorities and presented at an upcoming
medical meeting.
Tam-Peli
Lung cancer
Roche’s collaborator MediLink announces phase III data for Tam-Peli
showing significantly improved overall survival in Chinese patient
population with relapsed small-cell lung cancer
● Tambotatug pelitecan (Tam-Peli) demonstrated statistically significant
efficacy over topotecan in a phase III study conducted in China, reducing
the risk of death by 54% and disease progression by 71%, while achieving
an objective response rate of 59.1% vs 9.7%.
● The novel B7-H3-targeted antibody-drug conjugate with a dual release
mechanism of the payload is based on MediLink’s proprietary TMALIN
platform, designed to maximise antitumour activity while minimising off-
target toxicity.
● Roche is developing and commercialising Tam-Peli outside mainland China,
Hong Kong and Macau across multiple tumour types, with plans to rapidly
initiate global trials. Tam-Peli has received US FDA and China CDE
Breakthrough Therapy Designations for relapsed small-cell lung cancer.
Vabysmo
Severe eye diseases
Vabysmo demonstrates sustained two-year results in a difficult-to-treat
form of nAMD
● The SALWEEN study showed significant improvements in vision and retinal
health.
● More than 60% of Asian patients with polypoidal choroidal vasculopathy
(PCV), an aggressive subtype of nAMD, treated with Vabysmo showed no
signs of polypoidal lesions.
● By the end of year two, more than 60% of patients were on an extended 20-
week treatment interval, reducing treatment burden.

Vamikibart
Uveitic maclar edema
Roche presents new phase III one-year data for vamikibart in uveitic

● In the MEERKAT and SANDCAT studies, vamikibart demonstrated sustained

UME at 52 weeks.
● UME is the leading cause of moderate and severe vision loss in patients
with uveitis, posing a significant risk of blindness if left untreated.
● The FDA has accepted Roche’s Biologics License Application (BLA), with an

Vabysmo
Severe eye diseases
Roche presents new data at EURETINA, highlighting the real-world
effectiveness and safety of Vabysmo across major retinal conditions
● New real-world results for Vabysmo in nAMD, diabetic macular edema and
retinal vein occlusion reinforce robust effectiveness and durability across
previously untreated and treated eyes, with no new safety signals.
● Advanced AI-enabled analysis of imaging confirms rapid and meaningful
anatomical drying supporting Vabysmo as a standard-of-care therapy in
routine clinical care.
● With more than 5,000 patients enrolled, VOYAGER is the largest,
prospective, non-interventional real-world dataset evaluating Vabysmo
across diverse clinical practice settings worldwide.
Giredestrant
Breast cancer
Giredestrant combination significantly improved progression-free survival
in ER-positive advanced breast cancer in phase III evERA data published in
The New England Journal of Medicine
● Giredestrant plus everolimus reduced the risk of disease progression or
death by 44% and 62% in ITT and ESR1-mutated populations, respectively,
versus standard endocrine therapy plus everolimus.
● By targeting resistance to endocrine treatments, the giredestrant
combination seeks to delay disease progression and improve overall
patient outcomes.
● The FDA has set a PDUFA goal date for giredestrant in early-stage breast
cancer of 30 November 2026 and in advanced disease of 18 December
2026.

Alzheimer’s disease

disease assessment across primary and specialty care

existing laboratory infrastructure.

About Roche
Roche (SIX: RO, ROP; OTCQX: RHHBY) is a healthcare company uniquely placed to prevent,
stop and cure diseases by uniting leading science and technology across diagnostics,
medicines and digital solutions.
Roche was founded in Basel, Switzerland in 1896 and today is a leading provider of
transformative medicines and diagnostics for millions of people in over 150 countries around
the world. It is dedicated to tackling healthcare challenges that place the greatest strain on
patients, families, communities and healthcare systems. Across its Diagnostics and
Pharmaceuticals Divisions, Roche focuses on areas including oncology, neurology,
cardiovascular and metabolic diseases, ophthalmology, infectious diseases and immunology
with the aim of providing real and positive change for patients, the people they love and the
professionals who care for them.
Genentech in the United States is a fully owned subsidiary in the Roche Group. Roche is the
majority shareholder in Chugai Pharmaceutical, a major innovator in the Japanese
therapeutic antibody market.

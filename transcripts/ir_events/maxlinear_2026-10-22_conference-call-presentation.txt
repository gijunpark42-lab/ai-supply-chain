Q3'26 Financial Summary
• Revenue of $218.1 million, up 29% from Q2'26 and up 72% from Q3'25
• GAAP and non-GAAP gross margin was 58.7% and 60.0%
• GAAP and non-GAAP operating margin was 13.9% and 29.5%
• GAAP and non-GAAP diluted earnings per share was $0.30 and $0.62

--- page 5
Q3'26 Business Highlights
• Infrastructure grew 47% sequentially and 210% over Q3'25 continuing as MaxLinear’s
largest revenue category. This was led by strong growth in high-speed optical
interconnects.
• Keystone, our 100G-per-lane PAM4 DSP platform, continues to ramp into high-volume
production. Rushmore, our 1.6T, 200G-per-lane PAM4 DSP , is now in customers' hands
and progressing through initial qualification.
• Panther Storage Accelerator won FMS 2026 Best in Show award in servers & networking
• Launched Puma 9, enabling operators to accelerate AI-ready DOCSIS 4.0/3.1+ multi-gig
services
• Introduced Carmel high-speed USB-to-UART for next-gen AI data center console access
• Introduced RackCommander control plane portfolio for next-gen AI infrastructure

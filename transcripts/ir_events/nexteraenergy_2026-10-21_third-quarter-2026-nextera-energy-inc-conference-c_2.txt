(4) HURRICANE ISAIAS
Before I turn to the progress we have made executing against our
broader strategy, I want to spend a moment on Hurricane Isaias. FPL
continues to stand shoulder to shoulder with our communities throughout
Northwest Florida devastated by this high-end, Category 2 storm. While we
have restored power to all customers able to safely accept service, it will
undoubtedly take months for life to truly recover from the destructive wind,
torrential rain and toppled trees that ravaged many homes and businesses.
In preparation for the storm, FPL assembled a restoration workforce
of approximately 17,000 men and women from 29 states, pre-positioning
crews, equipment and supplies ahead of the storm so that restoration could
begin as soon as conditions were safe. I want to recognize our employees
and the thousands of contractors, mutual-assistance crews, first
responders and community partners who worked around the clock for our
customers. Their preparation, dedication, commitment and selflessness
allowed us to restore power safely and quickly. The hurricane exited our

4
service area early Saturday morning, and by Tuesday we restored power to
more than 97% of customers affected.
Early forensic analysis shows investing in a stronger, smarter and
more storm resilient grid benefited our customers. Not a single hardened
transmission or distribution pole failed during Isaias. Underground
neighborhood powerlines performed approximately fourteen times better
than overhead lines. And, smart grid technology avoided approximately
140,000 outages. To be clear, these investments do not make the grid
hurricane-proof, but together with our preparation and coordinated

enable us to get our customers’ lives back to normal faster. But it is our
people who make the biggest difference, and I again want to say thank you
to our teams and the thousands of mutual assistance crews who worked
tirelessly to support our communities when they needed us most.

At FPL, we continue to support Florida’s strong and growing
economy. Florida remains one of the fastest-growing states in the nation,
supported by continued net in-migration, job creation and business

Customers also continue to benefit from top-decile reliability that’s more
than 60% better than the national average. This performance is the direct
result of a proven long-term strategy centered on sustained, disciplined
capital investment, scale, a strong balance sheet and credit rating, a highly
efficient operating model, and a diverse generation fleet. FPL’s customer
service is also ranked top decile nationally for residential customer

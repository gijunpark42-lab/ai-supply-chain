# source label: FPT Corporation IR presentation: Content of Information disclosure: Public release on FPT's (10-21-2026)

--- page 1 (scan: irregular information disclosure No. 323/FPT-FAF, Hanoi, October 21st, 2026)
FPT Corporation (stock code FPT) discloses to the State Securities Commission and the Hochiminh Stock Exchange its Public release on FPT's financial performance in 9M2026, published on the company's website on 21/10/2026.

--- page 2 (scan: Financial performance highlights in 9M2026, No. 322/FPT-FAF)
From 2026, the Group will account for FPT Telecom using the equity method (applicable to associates); the 2025 figures have been restated using the same accounting method.
In the first nine months of 2026, FPT posted revenue of VND 40,023 billion and profit before tax (PBT) of VND 8,770 billion, up 12.4% and 17.0% year on year, respectively. Profit after tax attributable to shareholders of the parent company reached VND 7,778 billion, increasing 13.3% year on year, while EPS rose 12.8% to VND 4,142/share.
FPT secures 16 projects worth over $10 million each in the first nine months of 2026; new contract value from Global IT Services grows 23.1% year on year.
The Technology segment (Global IT Services and Domestic IT Services) contributed 89% of total revenue and 58% of profit before tax; revenue and profit before tax reached VND35,515 billion and VND5,052 billion, up 14.8% and 16.6% year on year.
Revenue from Global IT Services grew 11.6% year on year to VND28,538 billion. Japan posted revenue growth of 17.2% year on year, equivalent to 25.2% in JPY terms. Signed revenue from Global IT Services reached VND36,137 billion in the first nine months of 2026, up 23.1% year on year.
Digital Transformation revenue from overseas markets reached VND14,411 billion, up 16.3%; revenue from AI and Data Analytics services reached VND2,915 billion, increasing 55% year on year.

--- page 3 (scan)
Domestic IT Services generated revenue of VND6,977 billion, up 29.8% year on year, while profit before tax reached VND542 billion, up 108.5%. FPT was granted a Certificate of Eligibility to provide Data Intermediary Products and Services.
The Made-by-FPT product ecosystem generated revenue of VND2,284 billion, up 19.9% year on year.
Revenue from the Education, Investment and Other businesses declined 3.1% year on year to VND4,508 billion; profit before tax increased 17.6% to VND3,719 billion.
In the third quarter of 2026 FPT became a Global Partner (Select Partner) of OpenAI. FPT introduced MediSight, an AI transformation framework tailored to healthcare. In Vietnam, FPT entered into a partnership with the National Data Center in data, AI and high-performance computing.
Also in the third quarter of 2026, FPT and NVIDIA expanded the Nemotron-Personas-Vietnam dataset from 100,000 to one million synthetic user profiles, a tenfold increase from the initial version launched in June 2026.

--- page 4 (scan)
FPT deployed Flezi Metis, its proprietary AI platform, for a leading global automotive technology and services group headquartered in the United States. Initial pilot results on a project comprising approximately one million lines of code showed a 50% reduction in source code search time and a 30% reduction in the time required to complete technical tasks. FPT is expanding Flezi Metis into banking, insurance and the public sector.
